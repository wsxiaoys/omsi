## Billing basis (verbatim)

> HappyHorse-Text-to-video — Only output is billed. For pricing rules, see Video generation.

> The following models offer a free quota only in Singapore. No free quota is available in other
> regions.

> Failed requests incur no cost and do not consume your free quota.

For the editing model the basis is different:

> HappyHorse-Video editing — Pricing rule: both input and output videos are billed by video duration
> (seconds). Failed requests are not billed and do not consume the free quota.

text-to-video table: 1.1 is $0.07 / $0.14 / $0.18 per second at 480P / 720P / 1080P; 1.0 is
$0.14 / $0.24 per second at 720P / 1080P.

## Other regions (verbatim rows, `happyhorse-1.1-*`)

China (Beijing), Germany (Frankfurt), US (Virginia) and Japan (Tokyo) tables all give the same
1.1 prices:

| Output resolution | Output price |
| --- | --- |
| 480P | $0.0618845/second |
| 720P | $0.123769/second |
| 1080P | $0.165026/second |

For 1.0 in those regions: 720P $0.123769/second, 1080P $0.220034/second; editing
`happyhorse-1.0-video-edit` 720P $0.123769/second, 1080P $0.220034/second.

## Summary

- Billing is **per second of output video**, by resolution tier, with failed requests free. Editing
  additionally bills input seconds.
- 1.1 is cheaper than 1.0 at the shared 1080P tier ($0.18 vs $0.24 per second) and adds a 480P tier
  that 1.0 does not have.
- Non-Singapore regions are priced roughly 8 % below Singapore ($0.165026 vs $0.18 at 1080P) and carry
  no free quota.
- The 1080P $0.18/second rate is $10.80 per minute, which is the per-minute figure Artificial
  Analysis prints for HappyHorse-1.1 — the aggregator is quoting this vendor rate, not a measured cost.

## Gaps

- No volume tiers, batch discounts, or committed-use pricing on this page.
- No statement of how 480P output maps to pixel dimensions.
- Prices are as published on the capture date; the page is edited frequently (it lists model releases
  through 2026-10-22).

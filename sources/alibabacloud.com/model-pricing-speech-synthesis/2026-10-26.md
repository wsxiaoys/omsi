## Character counting rule (verbatim)

> **Character counting rules**: For the models in this section that are billed by character count
> (input prices are listed per 10,000 characters), the number of characters in the input text is
> counted as follows:
> Each Chinese character (including simplified Chinese characters, traditional Chinese characters,
> Japanese kanji, and Korean hanja) counts as 2 characters.
> Each other character (such as an English letter, a digit, a punctuation mark, a space, a Japanese
> kana, or a Korean letter) counts as 1 character.
> When SSML is used, the SSML tags themselves are not counted.

Examples given: `你好` = 4 characters; `中A文123` = 8; `中文。` = 5; `中 文。` = 6.

## Voice cloning

Pricing page, "Qwen-TTS Voice cloning", verbatim: "Pricing rule: billed by the number of new voice
clones created." `qwen-voice-enrollment` is **$0.01 per voice clone** in both Singapore and China
(Beijing), with the free-quota column reading "1,000 voices/account".

The voice-cloning guide states a different rule for the newer families, verbatim:

> **Qwen-Audio-TTS / Qwen-Audio-Realtime / CosyVoice**: Voice creation is free. Speech synthesis and
> real-time conversations using cloned voices are billed according to the target model.

and a global cap: "Each Alibaba Cloud Model Studio account can have up to 1,000 custom voices in
total. Voice cloning and voice design share this account quota across models."

Because the Model Studio capability matrix lists voice cloning as **unsupported** for
`qwen3-tts-flash` and `qwen3-tts-flash-realtime`, the `qwen-voice-enrollment` charge applies to the
Qwen-Audio-TTS / CosyVoice cloning route, not to Qwen3-TTS on this platform. Whether the "1,000
voices/account" figure in the price table is a free quota or the account cap is not resolved by the
page.

## Notes

- Qwen3-TTS on Model Studio is billed on **input characters only**; generated audio is free. This is
  the same basis fal and Replicate use for the open weights, so the three routes are directly
  comparable per character.
- All prices are list prices in USD as published; no discounts, committed-use tiers, or regional
  tax treatment were captured.

## Gaps

- No historical price data, so the "after November 13" realtime free-quota note cannot be read as a
  price change rather than a quota change.
- No pricing for Qwen3-TTS voice cloning exists because the platform does not offer it.
- No first-party statement of what a character of Qwen3-TTS output costs in audio seconds, so
  per-minute comparisons against GPU-time hosts are not possible from published data.

## Billing rule and formula (verbatim)

> Pricing rule: both input and output videos are billed by video duration (seconds). Failed requests
> are not billed and do not consume the free quota.

> Billing formula: billable duration = input video duration + output video duration.

> The billable duration of the input video is the actual input video duration in seconds.

> The billable duration of the output video is the duration (in seconds) of successfully generated
> videos.

> The free quota is 30 seconds combined for input and output video duration.

> The following models offer a free quota only in Singapore. No free quota is available in other
> regions.

> Free quota: valid for 90 days from the date of Model Studio activation, model release, or
> application approval, whichever is later.

Billing is therefore **per second of video duration, input plus output**, not per request and not per
token. A 30-second output that also consumes 15 seconds of reference video is billed for 45 seconds.
Audio on or off does not change the price (the API reference states this explicitly).

## Two pricing asymmetries the vendor does not explain

1. **Only the standard model carries a promotion label.** Every `wan3.0-video` row reads "List price …
   (Limited-time 30% off)"; every `wan3.0-video-prime` row states a bare price with no discount
   marker. If the promotion is live, the standard tier's effective price is up to 30% below list,
   widening the gap beyond the 1.36–1.54× computed from list prices. The page does not say whether the
   promotion is currently active, and fal charges the list price for both.
2. **The regional spread is not a flat multiplier.** Prime's "Global" rows are 6.47% below its
   International row at 480P (0.0636 vs 0.068) but 9.14% below at 720P (0.127199 vs 0.14) and 9.14%
   below at 1080P. The standard model's spread is a flat 17.49% at all three resolutions. No page
   explains either structure.

## Free quota

30 seconds of combined input+output video duration, Singapore only, valid 90 days from whichever is
later of activation, model release, or approval. At Singapore list prices that is worth $1.50 of
standard 1080P output, or $2.10 of Prime 1080P output — enough for roughly one 30-second clip or six
5-second clips.

## Gaps

- No volume-discount, committed-use, or batch/offline pricing for Wan 3.0 was found; the API reference
  marks batch inference unsupported.
- The page does not state the CNY prices behind the Beijing USD figures, nor the exchange rate used.
- "Limited-time" has no end date anywhere captured.

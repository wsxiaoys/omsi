## Model description (verbatim)

> Qwen-Audio-3.1-TTS-Plus is a high-quality, professional text-to-speech model for premium voices and
> content creation. It supports multiple languages and dialects, streaming speech synthesis,
> free-style instruction following, and fine-grained tags to control emotion, tone, persona, speaking
> rate, and volume. For voice cloning, it is more robust to noise and reverberation in reference
> audio, improving audio quality, clarity, and expressiveness. The Plus version emphasizes audio
> quality and resolution for professional scenarios such as brand voices, audio content creation,
> film and television dubbing, and character performance.

> The inference service provider for qwen-audio-3.1-tts-plus is Alibaba Cloud Model Studio.

The Flash page's description, for contrast, positions that model for "real-time interactions …
voice assistants, real-time conversations, and customer service".

## Summary

This is the canonical first-party page for the model: it confirms the model ID, that Alibaba Cloud
Model Studio is the sole inference provider named, the Plus tier's quality/content-production
positioning against the Flash tier's latency positioning, a per-character price that is 50% above
`qwen-audio-3.0-tts-plus` in Singapore ($0.3 vs $0.2 per 10,000 characters) and 3.85% above it in
Beijing ($0.2 vs $0.19253), and a 180 RPM cap.

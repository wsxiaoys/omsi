## What this establishes

- The vendor treats Qwen Image 3 as **two model IDs**, not one: a `-pro` flagship and a standard
  sibling. The standard sibling shipped 15 days after the pro.
- Both releases fall inside the one-year window (on or after 2025-10-26).
- "Qwen-Image-3.0 Standard" is the vendor's own name for the non-pro model.

## Gaps

- The table is region-tabbed (Singapore / US Virginia / China Beijing / Hong Kong / Germany /
  Japan). The captured rows are the **International** deployment; regional availability and dates
  for the other tabs were not captured.
- The page dates are model-release dates as published by Model Studio. They are not necessarily
  the first public announcement date (a Qwen blog post could precede or follow them), and no Qwen
  blog post for Qwen Image 3 was captured.
- Descriptions are vendor marketing; no benchmark numbers, harness, or methodology accompany them.

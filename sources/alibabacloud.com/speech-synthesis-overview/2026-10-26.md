## Model selection (verbatim)

| Requirement | Recommended model | Key capabilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice assistants, real-time conversations, and customer service | `qwen-audio-3.1-tts-flash` | "Designed for low-latency interaction. Supports system voices, voice cloning, voice design, natural-language instructions, and emotion and paralinguistic tags." |
| Audiobooks, dubbing, brand voices, and content production | `qwen-audio-3.1-tts-plus` | "Focuses on audio quality, naturalness, and expressive detail. Supports system voices, voice cloning, voice design, and expressive controls." |

## Synthesis modes (verbatim)

| Mode | Text input | Audio output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Non-streaming | Send the complete text once. | Retrieve the complete audio after synthesis finishes. |
| Unidirectional streaming | Send the complete text once. | Receive audio in chunks during synthesis. |
| Bidirectional streaming | Send text segments as they become available. | Receive audio in chunks during synthesis. |

## Qwen-Audio-TTS languages and dialects (verbatim)

| Voice type | Speech output languages and dialects |
| --- | --- |
| System voice | "Support languages such as Chinese and English. Availability varies by model and voice. See the Qwen-Audio-TTS voice list." |
| Cloned voice | Chinese (Mandarin, Cantonese, Chongqing, Northeastern, Gansu, Guizhou, Zhejiang, Hebei, Henan, Hubei, Hunan, Jiangxi, Ningbo, Ningxia, Qingdao, Shaanxi, Shanxi, Shandong, Shanghainese, Sichuan, Yunnan), English, Japanese, Korean, Russian, French, German, Portuguese, Thai, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Spanish, Italian, Malaysian, Filipino, and Arabic (dialects are configured through instruction control) |
| Designed voice | Chinese (Mandarin), English |

This table is **family-level**: it is not stated per model ID. Qwen3-TTS and CosyVoice have their own
tables on the same page, so the grouping is deliberate, but a reader cannot tell from it which of the
four Qwen-Audio-TTS IDs honour which row.

## Recommended-models page (verbatim)

Under "Text-to-speech — For audiobook reading, voice broadcasting, virtual avatars, and more":
"`qwen-audio-3.1-tts-plus` Real-time and non-real-time" and "`qwen-audio-3.1-tts-flash` Real-time and
non-real-time". The same page lists `qwen-audio-3.1-tts-next` separately under "Audio generation —
Complete sound scene generation" and `qwen-audio-3.1-realtime-plus` under "Speech-to-speech".

## Contradiction recorded, not resolved

Under "Synthesis controls" the overview offers "Mathematical formula reading | Express formulas in
LaTeX" as a general TTS control, while the SSML/LaTeX page restricts LaTeX to the CosyVoice models
([SSML and LaTeX](../ssml-latex-user-guide/2026-10-26.md)). The overview's control list is not scoped
per family, so it reads as a Qwen-Audio-TTS capability that the feature page does not grant.

## Gaps

- No release dates, no per-model language matrix, no per-model voice counts, no input-length limits.
- "Real-time and non-real-time" is asserted for `qwen-audio-3.1-tts-plus` on the recommended-models
  page, but the model page itself says the model playground is unsupported and publishes no realtime
  endpoint; the realtime user guide is the only place the plus ID appears in a streaming context.

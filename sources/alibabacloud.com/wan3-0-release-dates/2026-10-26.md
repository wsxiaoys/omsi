## Gaps

- The changelog gives no "new release" versus "update" distinction on these rows (other Model Studio
  changelogs have used such markers), so a staged rollout before 2026-08-06 cannot be ruled out.
- No deprecation or lifecycle row for either model was found in the captured page.
- The page header date (2026-10-27) is later than the capture date (2026-10-26), presumably a
  time-zone artifact of the vendor's rendering; treat the row dates as reliable and the header as not.

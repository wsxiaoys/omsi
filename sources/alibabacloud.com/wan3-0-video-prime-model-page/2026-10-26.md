## Description (verbatim)

> Wan3.0-Video-Prime is the accelerated version of the Wan video generation model, delivering
> significantly faster generation speed while maintaining high-quality output. It supports
> multi-modal inputs including text, image, video, and audio, enabling text-to-video, image-to-video
> (first frame/first-last frame), and reference-based video generation within a single model. It
> supports 480P, 720P, and 1080P resolution output, with a maximum video duration of 30 seconds.

> The inference service for the `wan3.0-video-prime` model is provided by Alibaba Cloud Model Studio.

This is the strongest first-party identity statement captured: Prime is **the same Wan video
generation model, accelerated**. It is not described as a larger, better, or differently trained
model. The page's own capability list is character-for-character the same as the standard model's.

## Gaps

- The page never quantifies "significantly faster": no latency, TPS, or wall-clock figure appears.
- No statement of what, if anything, is traded away for the speed. "while maintaining high-quality
  output" is the only quality claim and carries no measurement.
- No release date, version string, or checkpoint identifier on the page itself; those come from the
  Model Studio changelog (see `sources/alibabacloud.com/wan3-0-release-dates/2026-10-26.md`).
- The scope/region price split is undocumented; no page explains "International" vs "Global".

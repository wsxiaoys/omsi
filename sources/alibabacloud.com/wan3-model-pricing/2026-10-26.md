## Billing rule (verbatim)

> Pricing rule: both input and output videos are billed by video duration (seconds). Failed requests
> are not billed and do not consume the free quota.

> Billing formula: billable duration = input video duration + output video duration.
> The billable duration of the input video is the actual input video duration in seconds.
> The billable duration of the output video is the duration (in seconds) of successfully generated
> videos.

> The free quota is 30 seconds combined for input and output video duration.

> The following models offer a free quota only in Singapore. No free quota is available in other
> regions.

The free-quota footnote reads: "Valid for 90 days from the date of Model Studio activation, model
release, or application approval, whichever is later".

Input video duration being billable means a reference-video or editing request costs the source clip
length again on top of the output length. The API reference's `usage` object reports
`input_video_duration` and `output_video_duration` separately, consistent with this formula.

## Reading the two schedules

- The 30% discount applies to the **standard** tier only; `wan3.0-video-prime` has no discount line.
  The discounted standard rates ($0.035 / $0.07 / $0.14 per second in Singapore) are therefore *below*
  the prime tier's $0.068 / $0.14 / $0.28 by exactly the same 2× ratio the list prices show.
- Non-Singapore rates are ~0.909× the Singapore list price ($0.041256 / $0.082513 / $0.165025 vs
  $0.05 / $0.10 / $0.20) — a regional price difference the page does not explain.
- The page states it "only lists standard prices" and that promotions live in the console, so the
  "Limited-time 30% off" annotation is itself a promotion recorded in the standard list.

## Summary

Per-second, per-resolution billing for both Wan 3.0 IDs, with input video duration added to output
duration. Standard tier is half the prime tier's price at every resolution, in every region. A 30-second
combined free quota exists only in Singapore. Failed generations are not billed.

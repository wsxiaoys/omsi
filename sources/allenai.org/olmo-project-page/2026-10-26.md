- Origin: Ai2, "Olmo" project page, <https://allenai.org/olmo>
- Evidence kind: official product/project page (first-party)

- Relevant models: Olmo 3 family; Olmo 3.1 32B Think and 32B Instruct (as the current 32B downloads)

## What the page shows

Six variant cards: "32B-Base", "32B-Think", "32B-Instruct", "7B-Base", "7B-Think", "7B-Instruct",
described as "The Olmo 3 model family — Pick a variant to explore weights, code and reports."

Card blurbs, verbatim:

> 32B-Base — Achieves strong results in programming, reading comprehension, and math problem solving,
> maintains performance at extended context lengths, and works well with RL setups.

> 32B-Think — Capable of reasoning through complex problems step by step. A strong platform for RL
> research and other advanced experiments that need serious horsepower.

> 32B-Instruct — Our most capable fully open chat model to date. An instruction-tuned model built for
> chat, tool use, and multi-turn dialogue.

> 7B-Base — A smaller, lighter-weight base model able to run on a wider range of hardware while
> delivering competitive performance.

> 7B-Think — Delivers strong reasoning capabilities at 7B scale, surfacing intermediate thinking steps
> for complex prompts at high efficiency.

> 7B-Instruct — Model for efficient inference that handles multi-turn chat, tool use, and more.

## The 32B links resolve to 3.1 weights

The "Download weights" and "Chat with model" links attached to the 32B-Think and 32B-Instruct cards
point at 3.1 artifacts, not at Olmo 3 32B artifacts:

- `https://huggingface.co/allenai/Olmo-3.1-32B-Think` and
  `https://playground.allenai.org/?model=Olmo-3.1-32B-Think`
- `https://huggingface.co/allenai/Olmo-3.1-32B-Instruct` and
  `https://playground.allenai.org/?model=Olmo-3.1-32B-Instruct`

The 7B cards link to `Olmo-3-1025-7B`, `Olmo-3-7B-Think`, `Olmo-3-7B-Instruct`; the 32B-Base card links
to `Olmo-3-1125-32B`. The page never uses the strings "3.1" or "Olmo 3.1" in visible text.

The page also lists the model flow (Pretraining → Midtraining → Long context → Instruct SFT/DPO/RL,
Thinking SFT/DPO/RL, RL Zero), the open data pools ("Standard Pool", "Long Context Mix"), the
post-training corpora, and the open tools (OlmoCore, Duplodocus, Datamap-rs, Open Instruct, OLMES,
Decon), plus OlmoTrace and FlexOlmo.

## Summary

Ai2's product page still labels the family "Olmo 3", but its 32B Instruct and 32B Think cards now
download and serve the **3.1** checkpoints. Read together with the blog's "Update 12/12", this is the
clearest first-party statement that 3.1 *replaces* the 32B Think checkpoint and *introduces* a 32B
Instruct: a reader who follows the product page gets 3.1 weights without the page ever naming 3.1.

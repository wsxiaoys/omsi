## What this establishes

- The vendor operates its own hosted, account-gated, credit-billed playground for ABot-World-0, and it
  is the only first-party *API-free* route — there is no Alibaba Cloud Model Studio/DashScope model
  entry for ABot-World captured here.
- The only required creative input is **one image**; text and actions are optional refinements. That
  matches the repo's "first-frame reference image + prompt" and the model card's `image-to-video`
  classification.
- The vendor's own product page claims <1.5 s render latency, looser than the paper's 1.2 s
  action-to-first-frame figure and measured on vendor infrastructure rather than a desktop GPU.
- The Studio is China-facing (Chinese UI, Beijing ICP filing) and requires login; it is not a
  documented public API.

## Gaps

- Credit prices, free allowance, output resolution/quality tiers, and session-length limits are not
  visible without an account and were not captured.
- No API documentation, no rate limits, no terms of service text (the 服务条款 and 隐私政策 links were not
  fetched).
- The 120+ worlds / 3,800 h figures are unlabelled aggregates with no stated measurement method.

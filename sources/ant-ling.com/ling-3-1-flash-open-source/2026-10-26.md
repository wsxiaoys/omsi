## Summary

This record supplies the facts the launch post omitted: the open-weights date (**2026-10-15**),
the vendor's own promotional and list prices in both USD (via OpenRouter) and RMB (via Ling
Studio), the 75%-off promotion, and the vendor's local-deployment story (MXFP4/block-FP8,
534GB→292GB, 8→4 Hopper cards, TTFT 604 ms, ≈1.36 QPS). It is also the only vendor page that
prints a price for the model.

Two things to keep separate: the AA figures here (index 41, ≈210 tok/s, ≈$0.19 blended) are the
vendor **restating an independent evaluator**, and the deployment numbers are vendor
measurements on vendor-chosen configurations.

## Gaps

- The 292GB MXFP4 artifact described here is not what the Hugging Face repository publishes: that
  repository carries one `fp8` build in 54 shards and lists no MXFP4 files
  ([model card](../../huggingface.co/ling-3.1-flash-model-card/2026-10-26.md)). Whether the
  MXFP4 build is published elsewhere is not established.
- "2.5 折" is not given an end date here or on the docs price page.
- The QPS/TTFT figures are single vendor test configurations; no run-to-run variance, no
  comparison model on the same rig.
- No benchmark table. As with the launch post, scores are shown in images that were not captured.
- No checkpoint identifier, so it cannot be attributed to the pre- or post-`1019` weights.

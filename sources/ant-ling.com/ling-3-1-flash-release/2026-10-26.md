# Ant Ling blog: "Ling-3.1-flash：面向真实世界任务的模型升级" (release post)

- Origin: "Ling-3.1-flash：面向真实世界任务的模型升级" (Ling-3.1-flash: a model upgrade for
  real-world tasks), 百灵大模型 (Ant Ling), Ant Group,
  <https://developer.ant-ling.com/zh-CN/blogs/ling-3.1-flash>. Retrieved 2026-10-26.
- Evidence kind: first-party release announcement (vendor blog). Marketing framing; contains
  vendor-run anecdotes and links to vendor charts.
- Publication date: 2026-09-30, printed on the page as "2026年9月30日 · 11 分钟阅读".
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Ling-3.1-flash (the pre-`1019` checkpoint; the post names no checkpoint).
- Capture type: rendered page text, Chinese. Quotations below are verbatim; translations are
  mine and marked.

## Identity and scale, verbatim

> Ling-3.1-flash 拥有约 560B 总参数，每个 Token 激活约 25B 参数；模型上下文窗口上限为 1M，可容纳更长的文档、代码和任务历史。

("Ling-3.1-flash has about 560B total parameters, activating about 25B per token; the model's
context window ceiling is 1M.")

> 在 Attention 结构上，Ling-3.1-flash 按照 7 层 KDA 配 1 层 Gated MLA 的方式组成混合结构。

("In attention structure, Ling-3.1-flash is a hybrid of 7 KDA layers to 1 Gated MLA layer.")

> 模型设置 512 个路由专家。每个 Token 从中选择 8 个路由专家，并同时调用 1 个共享专家。

("The model has 512 routed experts. Each token selects 8 routed experts and also calls 1 shared
expert.")

The post calls the architecture an extension of Ling-3.0-flash's hybrid-linear design with a
higher proportion of linear-attention layers.

## Availability and date, verbatim

> Ling-3.1-flash 将开放为期两周的免费体验。考虑到综合服务成本，免费体验期间模型服务长度为 256K；免费体验期结束并转为付费服务后，计划开放 1M 上下文。届时，团队也计划同步开源，并继续更新模型性能。

("Ling-3.1-flash will open a two-week free trial. Given overall serving cost, service length
during the free trial is 256K; after the free trial ends and the model moves to paid service,
the plan is to open 1M context. At that point the team also plans to open-source it in step and
keep updating model performance.")

This is the announcement date (2026-09-30) and the origin of the 256K-served / 1M-model-window
split that the docs later formalise. It also states that open-sourcing was planned, not yet
done, on 2026-09-30.

## Benchmarks: named, not numbered

The post names the evaluations it is discussing — **GDPVal-AA V2.1**, **HealthBench
Professional**, **FrontierSWE**, **Terminal-Bench 4.0**, plus "automation, security analysis,
codebase QA, financial agents and multi-turn challenge" evaluations — but **prints no numeric
score for any of them**. Results are presented in images ("🎬 视频演示（见公众号原文）" and figure
captions) that are not in the text capture. The only numbers in the post are the long-horizon
task anecdotes below.

The one evaluation claim with an explicit epistemic caveat, verbatim:

> HealthBench Professional 结果为这一方向提供了评测参考。它反映的是模型整体在特定评测口径下的表现，不等同于临床安全认证，也不能代替实际医疗流程中的专业审核。

("The HealthBench Professional result provides an evaluation reference for this direction. It
reflects the model's overall performance under a specific evaluation setup; it is not equivalent
to clinical safety certification and cannot replace professional review in real medical
workflows.")

## Vendor anecdotes, verbatim figures

Unverified vendor runs, presented with comparison models:

- **Lua compiler from scratch** — "经过约 17 小时的持续执行…最终独立测试通过 178 项，共 182 项，通过率约 97.8%。文档中的对比结果为 Claude Fable 5 的 99.0% 和 GLM-5.3-Flash 的 95.9%。"
  (≈17 h of continuous execution; 178 of 182 independent tests pass, ≈97.8%; the same document
  lists Claude Fable 5 at 99.0% and GLM-5.3-Flash at 95.9%.)
- **Pyright 1.1.400 optimisation** — "约 10 小时后…在 2279 项基准和回归测试全部通过的条件下达到 1.432 倍加速。文档所列 Claude Fable 5 为 1.502 倍，GLM-5.3-Flash 为 1.290 倍。"
- **C image library reimplemented in Rust** — "任务持续约 20 小时，最终 30 项验证全部通过，并达到 8.015 倍加速…高于文档所列 GLM-5.3-Flash 的 1.396 倍，但低于 Claude Fable 5 的 27.310 倍。"
- **Security analysis** — the model is said to have found a stack-buffer-overflow in
  OceanBase's open-source parser component
  (`ObIOCalibration::parse_calibration_string()`, unbounded `%s` into three 64-byte stack
  buffers) and produced a correct patch; "截止目前已经修复" (fixed as of writing).

- **Design Arena** — with filters Open Weights / Recent (last 30 days) / Elo, the model is
  "在 Mobile App 类别和 SVG 类别分别位列开放权重模型的第二和第四" (second in Mobile App,
  fourth in SVG among open-weights models).

## Deployment and product context

- Ant Group's internal desktop co-work agent "小虎" runs on Ling-3.1-flash.
- The post concedes a cost trade-off, verbatim: "随着强化学习带来任务效果提升，模型的思考链长度和交互轮数可能增加。这会影响响应时间、Token 消耗和任务总成本。"
  ("as RL improves task results, chain-of-thought length and interaction rounds may grow, which
  affects response time, token consumption and total task cost.")

## Summary

This is the first-party launch record: it fixes the announcement date (2026-09-30), the
parameter counts (≈560B total / ≈25B active), the 1M model window with a 256K free-trial
service length, and the benchmark names the vendor wants read as strengths. It supplies **no
numeric benchmark table**, so every number the model is credited with in third-party sources is
either a chart in an image or an Artificial Analysis measurement restated later
([open-source post](../ling-3-1-flash-open-source/2026-10-26.md)).

## Gaps

- No numeric scores. The charts referenced (GDPVal-AA V2.1, HealthBench Professional,
  FrontierSWE, Terminal-Bench 4.0, Design Arena) are images and were not captured.
- No checkpoint identifier. The post predates the `1019` naming, so it cannot say which
  checkpoint its anecdotes describe.
- The long-horizon anecdotes (Lua compiler, Pyright, Rust port) are single vendor runs with no
  harness, seed, or repetition details, and the comparison figures for Claude Fable 5 and
  GLM-5.3-Flash are unattributed vendor-quoted numbers.
- No licensing statement. Weights licence is only established by the Hugging Face repositories.
- No API pricing; pricing appears in the 2026-10-15 open-source post and the docs price page.

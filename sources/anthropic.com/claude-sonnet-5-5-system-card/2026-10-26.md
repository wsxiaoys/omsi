# Claude Sonnet 5.5 — system card

- **Origin:** "System Card: Claude Sonnet 5.5", Anthropic, dated September 28, 2026,
  <https://www.anthropic.com/document/claude-sonnet-5-5-system-card> (resolves to a 148-page
  PDF on `assets.claude.com`). Retrieved 2026-10-26.
- **Evidence kind:** first-party pre-deployment evaluation report (vendor-run capability and
  safety evaluations, with a few third-party-run benchmarks noted inline).
- **Publication date:** 2026-09-28. Capture date 2026-10-26.
- **Relevant models:** Claude Sonnet 5.5, compared with Claude Sonnet 5, Claude Opus 5.5,
  Claude Fable 5.1, Claude Mythos 5.1, Claude Opus 5, GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6 Astra.
- **Capture type:** full text extraction of the PDF. Charts and figures are images and were
  not read; only figures stated in prose or in tables are recorded here.

## Snapshot provenance, verbatim

> "Different 'snapshots' of our models are taken at various points during the training
> process. Except where otherwise noted, the evaluations in this system card were run on the
> final released snapshot of Claude Sonnet 5.5."

The RSP section (§2.2.1) instead says its automated evaluations "were run on the launch
candidate snapshot". The card does not publish a dated snapshot ID.

## Terminal-Bench 4.0, verbatim (§8.5)

> "Claude Sonnet 5.5 scored 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 with safeguards enabled; requests
> flagged by the safeguards were answered by a fallback model following the default
> server-side fallback policy (1.2% of requests, affecting 1.5% of trials, with one attempt
> stopping instead of replying)."

> "We ran the evaluation with no internet egress allowed, preemptively caching resources
> needed for the model to operate based on historical run logs, and rebuilding the images
> accordingly."

> "For comparison, Claude Opus 5.5 scored 66.4% (with 2.5% of requests flagged by safeguards,
> affecting 10% of trials), Claude Mythos 5.1 scored 60.9%, Claude Fable 5.1 scored 55.8%,
> and Claude Opus 5 scored 52.3%."

> "Scores are averaged over five trials per task (330 trials) for Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 …
> Treating trials as independent, the standard error is ±2.5 points for Sonnet 5.5, ±2.6
> points for Opus 5.5"

Harness: "The numbers reported use Claude Code in `--bare` mode at max thinking effort, except
Opus 5.5, which is reported at xhigh effort." The benchmark "comprises a set of 66 tasks".

## Other effort-sensitive results, verbatim

- **FrontierCode v1.1** (§8.4, run by Cognition): "At xhigh effort, Claude Sonnet 5.5 scores
  52.1% on Main and 64.4% on Extended. At max effort, Sonnet 5.5 scores 46.2% on Main and
  59.1% on Extended." Scores are lower at max effort than at xhigh, which the announcement
  explains as the code-review skill splitting work across subagents and producing
  out-of-scope edits.
- **CursorBench 4.0** (§8.8, "All scores were measured and reported independently by
  Cursor"): "Sonnet 5.5 scored 55.5% on CursorBench at max effort, 53.1% at xhigh, 47.8% at
  high, and 39.2% at medium."
- **OSWorld 2.1** (§8.13.3): "Claude Sonnet 5.5 achieved a partial score of 80.1% and a
  strict pass rate of 43.5%." Opus 5.5: 81.8% partial / 48.7% strict. Sonnet 5: 57.0% / 25.6%.
- **Chartography** (§8.13.1, Surge AI benchmark): "Claude Sonnet 5.5 achieved a score of 61.6%
  without tools and 90.2% with tools. Claude Opus 5.5 scored 64.4% and 89.0%."
- **GDPval-AA v2.1** (§8.14.3): "Claude Sonnet 5.5 scores an Elo of 1844 at max effort, only 2
  points below Claude Opus 5.5 at max effort (1846). … At xhigh effort, Sonnet 5.5 scores
  1725 while using about 67% fewer output tokens than at max."
- **AA-Briefcase v1.1** (§8.14.4): "Claude Sonnet 5.5 scores an Elo of 1811 at max effort, only
  11 points below Claude Opus 5.5 at max (1822), and 1746 at xhigh effort. … At xhigh effort,
  Sonnet 5.5 uses about 61% fewer output tokens than at max."
- **ProgramBench** (§8.10.1, long context): "Claude Sonnet 5.5 scored 79.7%, whereas Claude
  Sonnet 5 scored 77.3% and Claude Opus 5.5 scored 91.2%." "Sonnet 5.5 episodes cover a range
  of context lengths up to the full 1M token window."
- **DeepSWE v1.1** (§8.3): "Sonnet 5.5 scored an average of 71.0% over five trials."
- **Toolathlon-Verified** (§8.14.5): "Claude Sonnet 5.5 achieved 77.8% Pass@1, matching Claude
  Opus 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.1 (both 77.8%) and ahead of Claude Sonnet 5 (74.7%)."

**Who ran what.** Anthropic ran SWE-Bench, HLE, OSWorld, Terminal-Bench, ProgramBench,
Toolathlon and the safety evaluations. Cognition ran FrontierCode. Cursor ran CursorBench and
reported the scores to Anthropic. Artificial Analysis ran GDPval-AA v2.1 and AA-Briefcase
v1.1. Surge AI authored Chartography; the grading harness was Anthropic's. Competitor rows
are quoted from the competitors' own publications, not re-run.

## Long context and modality

- HLE runs used "a 980k token task budget" with "Context compaction was not used"; thinking
  set to auto. Multimodal: "Humanity's Last Exam (HLE) is a multimodal benchmark".
- OSWorld runs used "1080p resolution and a maximum of 500 action steps per task at max
  effort", with server-side context management compacting "once it exceeds 100k tokens".
- No explicit statement of input/output modalities in the card; those come from the model
  page (text and images → text).

## Safeguards, verbatim (§1.5)

> "For chemical and biological (CB) risks, we have deployed the same harmful CB misuse
> classifiers as for our deployment of Claude Opus 5. These classifiers have no fallback
> model."

> "For cyber misuse, we have deployed similar blocking classifiers to those on Opus 5. Blocks
> on these classifiers will fall back to Claude Sonnet 5."

> "…safeguards on Sonnet 5.5 for a narrow set of capabilities related to developing frontier
> LLMs, such as kernel development on certain ML accelerators… Blocks on these classifiers
> will fall back to Sonnet 5."

> "Our classifiers to prevent distillation of our models (for example, by attempting to
> extract a model's hidden reasoning) will block on Sonnet 5.5 with no fallback model."

> "The fallback behavior described above applies to our first-party products and to developers
> who are opted in to such fallbacks on our API; traffic on our models via other platforms and
> providers may experience different behavior."

Cyber safeguard mechanics (§3.3): "a probe that looks at Claude's internal activations, a
lightweight classifier that runs on Claude Sonnet 5.5 itself, and a trained LLM classifier".
"users should expect increased refusals with Sonnet 5.5, even on benign cybersecurity-related
tasks." Policy: "our policy allows vulnerability discovery in source code … but it blocks
vulnerability discovery in compiled binaries."

## RSP and safety findings, verbatim

> "Our Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP) evaluations found that Sonnet 5.5 is broadly less
> capable than Opus 5.5 across domains and does not cross any new RSP thresholds."

> "We do treat Sonnet 5.5 as meeting our CB-1 and Autonomy-1 thresholds and apply the
> corresponding mitigations from our RSP."

> "Our agentic safety evaluations find Sonnet 5.5 to be our most robust Sonnet-class model yet
> to prompt injections, particularly in coding environments and browser use. Its refusal rate
> on harmful tasks in computer use environments is comparable to Opus 5.5 but a regression
> relative to some earlier models."

> "Across the models we tested, it has the lowest propensity for unprompted boundary probing
> and unsanctioned third-party contact, and the lowest rate of attempted sandbox escapes in our
> dedicated evaluation. On the other hand, its thinking is more illegible than many previous
> models."

> "Safeguards and harmlessness performance for Sonnet 5.5 is largely comparable to that of
> Sonnet 5. Among other changes, we see regressions in some areas on multi-turn testing, such
> as tracking and surveillance"

Single-turn harmless response rate (§4.1.1): Sonnet 5.5 95.61% (±0.18) on the API without a
system prompt, 99.40% (±0.09) on claude.ai — against Sonnet 5's 96.65% / 99.20%. Over-refusal
rate (§4.1.2): 0.02% on the API, 0.20% on claude.ai, against Sonnet 5's 0.59% / 1.54%.

> "The majority of evaluations of Claude Sonnet 5.5 were run in-house at Anthropic."

Training data (§1.1): "a proprietary mix of publicly available information from the internet,
public and private datasets, and other sources, such as synthetic data generated by other
models"; crawler ClaudeBot; "Claude is multilingual and typically responds in the same
language as the user's input. Output quality varies by language."

## Gaps

- No dated snapshot ID for the released model.
- Figures were not read; per-effort score/cost curves for most benchmarks exist only as
  images in this capture.
- The 70.6% Terminal-Bench 4.0 figure has no public-leaderboard counterpart captured here;
  Artificial Analysis's independent Terminal-Bench 4.0 number for the model is 63.6% at max
  effort (see the [OpenRouter record](../../openrouter.ai/claude-sonnet-5-5/2026-10-26.md),
  which restates AA's per-effort figures).
- The card does not quantify how often the biology/`frontier_llm` safeguards fire in
  real traffic, only in evaluation runs.
- No statement on pricing, licensing, or host differences; those are in other documents.

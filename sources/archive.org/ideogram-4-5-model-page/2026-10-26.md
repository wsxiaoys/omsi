## Interpretation

- The 4.5 product page existed by **2026-09-30 19:07 UTC**. Combined with Replicate's
  `ideogram-ai/ideogram-4-5` model creation at 2026-09-30 17:42 UTC
  ([Replicate](../../replicate.com/ideogram-4-5/2026-10-26.md)), 2026-09-30 is the best-supported
  release date, but it is **inferred**: no first-party dated announcement was captured.
- The marketing claim moved from "reduces this drift" to "eliminates artifact buildup" between
  2026-09-30 and 2026-10-26 with no new published evidence. Treat the stronger wording as a later
  marketing revision, not as a measured improvement.

## Known gaps

- A first capture is not proof of non-existence before that timestamp; the page could have been
  live earlier without being archived.
- Only the two endpoints above were inspected; intermediate revisions between 2026-09-30 and
  2026-10-26 were not diffed.

## What this establishes

No capture of the Flash-Next post exists before 2026-08-26T12:52:55Z, while the
Qwen3.8-Max post was already being captured on 2026-08-04. A page published on
2026-08-03 that announced weights "now available" would have had three weeks of exposure
to the crawler, and the Max post from the same date did get captured. The archive
therefore supports **2026-08-26** as the publication date and makes the reader proxy's
`Published Time: 2026-08-03T10:00:00+08:00` look like reused metadata.

  with a single coordinated release but does not by itself establish ordering.

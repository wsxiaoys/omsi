## Interpretation

- The Topaz Labs **image upscaling API was publicly documented and sold with a published price list
  by 2024-09-07**, roughly 14 months before the roster's 2025-10-26 window opens. "Tokens" at
  $0.158 each became "credits" at $0.08–$0.12 each, but the unit was already 24 MP of output.
- The vendor's claim that the API runs "the exact same models in Photo AI and Gigapixel" is unchanged
  between 2024 and 2026
  (`../../topazlabs.com/api-product-page/2026-10-26.md`).
- The video API was announced as future work on that page ("coming by Dec 2024"), which is consistent
  with the image API being the older product.
- The 24 MP unit and the 5x rollover cap are identical in 2024 and 2026, so the pricing architecture
  predates the window even though the rates changed.

## Gaps

- This dates the **page**, not necessarily the launch. The API may have launched weeks or months
  earlier; no dated launch announcement was found in the news index or the docs.
- No archived capture of the 2024 developer documentation body was fetched, so the 2024 endpoint
  paths and model IDs are not recorded here.
- The snapshot's plan prices are list prices; whether they were honoured is not observable.

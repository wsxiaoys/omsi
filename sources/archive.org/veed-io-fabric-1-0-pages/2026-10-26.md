## Summary

The archive dates the product's public life more precisely than any VEED page does. On 2025-09-09
`veed.io/ai/fabric-1-0` was a **waitlist** page; fal listed the model ten days later on 2025-09-19; the
API landing page existed by 2025-09-28; and by 2025-10-08 the editor product page carried the credit and
length FAQ it still carries today (3 free generations at 10 s, paid up to 30 s, 240 credits per
generation). The model page, first archived 2025-10-09, stated a 30-second ceiling and said "longer
durations coming soon"; it now claims up to 5 minutes. That makes the 5-minute claim a later rewrite
consistent with an announced expansion, not merely inflation — but the editor landing page and the
2026-08-19 help article still describe 30 seconds per generation.

## Gaps

- A waitlist page does not prove the model existed publicly; it proves the marketing page existed. The
  fal listing on 2025-09-19 is the earliest evidence of a callable endpoint.
- No capture pins the waitlist-to-general-availability transition: 2025-09-09 is a waitlist page, the
  2025-09-19 rows are two redirects followed by a 200, and by 2025-10-08 the page is a live product page
  with the credit FAQ. Whether the redirect marks the switch is not established.
- No archived snapshot of VEED's announcement or launch post was found; the sitemap lists no blog entry
  for Fabric.
- The archive cannot show whether the "5 minutes" wording replaced an earlier "30 seconds" statement or
  was added alongside it — the 2025-10-09 model page said "longer durations coming soon", but no capture
  shows the change landing, and the editor-side pages never adopted it.

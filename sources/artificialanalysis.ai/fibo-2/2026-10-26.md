## What this establishes

- Independent, blind-vote Elo for Fibo 2.0 on both boards, on ~5k samples each, captured 8 days
  after release: mid-pack overall (22nd of 76 editing entries, 23rd of 106 T2I entries), and far
  from the frontier — 111 Elo below the editing leader (GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst, 1199) and 183 below
  the T2I leader (1222).
- A large, independently measured gap between Fibo 2.0 and its predecessors on the same harness:
  **+173 Elo editing** over FIBO Edit (915 → 1088) and **+190 Elo T2I** over FIBO (849 → 1039). That
  is well outside the ±19 CIs and is the strongest available evidence that Fibo 2 is a new
  generation rather than a relabel.
- AA's `releaseDate` for Fibo 2.0 is **2026-10-20**, and its `introducedAt` is 2026-10-15T23:06Z.
- AA prices Fibo 2.0 at $32 per 1k images, matching BRIA's own $0.032/image list price and below
  fal's $0.04.
- AA records Fibo 2.0's open weights as `briaai/Fibo-2.0-dev` with a note that the editing weights
  are `briaai/Fibo-2.0-edit-turbo`.

## Contradiction with the vendor's ranking claim

Bria's release page claims "#1 open-weights image editing model … with an Elo of 1087" and "a top-2
open-weights text-to-image model" with Elo 1038 (see
`sources/bria.ai/fibo-2-release-notes/2026-10-26.md`).

- The editing claim **holds up** on this capture: 1088 Elo and no open-weights model ranked above
  it, though it is tied with Qwen-Image-2.1 within the CI.
- The T2I claim does **not** hold as stated at capture: Fibo 2.0 is third among open-weights entries
  (FLUX 3 Image Klein 1043, Qwen-Image-2.1 1042, Fibo 2.0 1039), all inside overlapping CIs. Either
  Bria's chart was taken before those two entries were scored, or it uses a different open-weights
  filter. The one-point Elo difference (1038 claimed vs 1039 measured) suggests a very close read,
  not a different model.

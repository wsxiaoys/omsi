## Deltas that matter

- **Klein vs its own parent:** −74.5 Elo text-to-image (1117.15 → 1042.68) and −50.5 Elo editing
  (1122.44 → 1071.97). Both gaps exceed the ±12/±14 confidence intervals, so Klein is measurably
  weaker than FLUX 3 Image on preference votes, at roughly one fifth the price.
- **Klein vs FLUX.2 [klein] 9B** — the comparison BFL's launch post quantifies: AA measures
  **+113.2** text-to-image (1042.68 vs 929.45) and **+57.1** editing (1071.97 vs 1014.91). BFL
  claimed **+91** and **+70**. The direction is corroborated and both deltas are far outside the
  intervals, but the magnitudes do not match: AA's text-to-image delta is larger than BFL's claim
  and its editing delta is smaller. Neither figure can be checked against BFL's method, which is
  unpublished.
- **Klein vs Ideogram 4.5 (High)**, which BFL claims to beat: AA has Klein clearly ahead on
  text-to-image (1042.68 vs 1015.07, non-overlapping intervals) but only 2.4 Elo ahead on editing
  (1071.97 vs 1069.61) with heavily overlapping intervals. BFL's claim holds for text-to-image and
  is not distinguishable from noise for editing.
- **Klein vs FLUX.2 [pro]**, the other model BFL claims to beat: ahead by 39.9 text-to-image and
  60.5 editing, both outside the intervals.

## Notes on row identity

- AA lists **one** FLUX 3 Image Klein row. It does not separate the distilled and base
  checkpoints, and it does not name the host or endpoint that served the images.
- FLUX.2 [klein] is split into 9B and 4B rows; Klein 3 is not split at all, despite having two
  checkpoints with different sampling settings.
- The `flux-3-image` and `flux-3-image-klein` slugs carry no vendor or host suffix, unlike other
  rows on the same board (`fal_flux-2-dev-turbo`, `black_forest_labs_flux-2-max`), which hints at
  a first-party serving path but does not establish one.

## Discrepancy with an existing repository record

`sources/artificialanalysis.ai/ideogram-4-5/2026-10-26.md` captured this same page and recorded
Ideogram 4.5 (High) at 1069.61 as a **text-to-image** row. The payload separates the arenas:
1069.61 is the **imageEditing** value, and Ideogram 4.5 (High) scores 1015.07 in `textToImage`.
That record's comparison table (Sunburst 1198.81, FLUX 3 Image 1122.44, FLUX.2 [pro] 1011.50) is
likewise the editing arena. Its conclusion that Ideogram 4.5 is "statistically indistinguishable
from a broad band running from FLUX 3 Image Klein (1072) to Qwen-Image-2.1-Pro (1069)" is
therefore an *editing-arena* statement and remains valid there. This record is not a correction of
that one — both describe the same page, and the earlier record's arena attribution is noted here
rather than edited there.

## Gaps

- No host, endpoint, prompt set, vote count, or snapshot date per row; the Klein row's serving
  configuration is unknown, so its score cannot be tied to distilled vs base, or to 12 vs 16 steps.
- The "Image Editing" arena's prompt set and edit taxonomy are not described on the captured page.
- Elo is preference, not instruction-following accuracy, typography fidelity, or layout compliance
  — the three things Klein's bounding boxes are sold on. No independent bounding-box measurement
  was captured.
- Intervals are ±12 (text-to-image) and ±14 (editing), so differences under ~20 points on this
  board are not signal.

## What these fields establish

- **AA credits FLUX 3 Max to "Fal", not to Black Forest Labs**, and sets `isDerived: true` with
  `baseModel` "Based on FLUX 3". AA's board explains the flag as: "Derived models are post-trained
  from a listed base model."
- **Release date 2026-10-19** — the same day fal published its endpoints. AA's own `introducedAt`
  is 2026-10-13, i.e. the row existed on the board before the public launch date it prints.
- **The measurement is of the fal API at `reasoning_level: "high"`**, so this Elo is
  configuration-specific; a `low` run is not covered.
- The comparison with base FLUX 3 (1086.34) is the independent version of fal's "+50 Elo" claim:
  AA shows **+51.68**, and the two intervals do not overlap (1070–1102 vs 1123–1153).

## What this board adds

- On **text-to-video** the post-training gain is **+12.98 Elo** (1142.19 → 1155.17), and the two
  95% intervals overlap (1128–1156 vs 1140–1170). Unlike image-to-video, this board does not
  separate Max from base FLUX 3.
- fal's public claim — "Post-training lifted FLUX 3 by 50 Elo points" — is an image-to-video
  figure. Read as a general quality claim it overstates the text-to-video result.
- Same attribution as the i2v board: creator "Fal", `isDerived: true`, based on FLUX 3, released
  2026-10-19, measured at `reasoning_level: "high"`.

## Gaps

- Prompt set, per-prompt sample counts and rater pool are not published on the page.
- "Overall / With Audio" is the view captured; the silent-board and category views were not.
- Pricing of `$2.55 /min` is AA's normalisation of fal's promotional rate (75% off until November
  7, 2026). After that date AA's figure will be wrong unless updated — a reminder that AA's price
  column is a snapshot, not a schedule.

## Summary

Independent measurement puts the model at **41 on the AA Intelligence Index v4.3.2** — above the
comparable-price median of 28 but far from the frontier tier that the roster's other text rows
occupy. The distinguishing measured facts are throughput (134 tok/s, fast) and **TTFT of 28.67 s**,
which AA calls "at the higher end"; combined with 170M tokens of index output against an 81M median,
the cost of the model in practice is driven by verbosity and reasoning time, not by the $0.75/$3.75
sticker.

## What this establishes and what it does not

- Establishes an independent absolute score, an independent release date (September 2, 2026), an
  independent modality and context read, and a measured latency/verbosity profile.
- Does **not** establish a delta over 3.7 Flash: AA's captured page shows only the `high` variant of
  3.8 Flash, and no 3.7 Flash row appears in the captured text.
- Does not give per-benchmark numbers. Terminal-Bench 4.0 appears only as an index component; the
  vendor's 19.1% claim cannot be checked against this capture.
- The 41 here is the `high` variant; AA lists 40.9 as the same model's index in its API-facing
  summary (see the OpenRouter capture), which is rounding, not a disagreement.

## Gaps

- No confidence intervals, task counts or per-evaluation scores in the text capture.

## Summary

AA treats thinking-on and thinking-off as **separate leaderboard entries with separate
comparison classes** (74 non-reasoning models here, 141 in the reasoning class). The 31B moves
from **15** (reasoning) to **14** (non-reasoning) — a one-point delta, which is not evidence of a
real quality gap. What does change is speed: 35.3 → 54.3 tok/s. AA's quoted price also shifts
($0.00 for the reasoning row against $0.16/$0.40 here), which reflects a different measured
route rather than a different product.

## Gaps

- The one-point index delta sits inside AA's own "Estimated" marking; it cannot be read as a
  capability difference.
- No cost-per-task or verbosity measurement.
- The price difference against the reasoning row is unexplained and is probably a provider
  selection artefact.

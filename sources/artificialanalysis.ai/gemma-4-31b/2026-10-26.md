## Summary

AA's independent composite puts the 31B at **15** (rank 19 of 141 open-weight models in its
size class), i.e. mid-pack among open models and far below the frontier tier on the same
board (Claude Opus 5.5 = 58, Gemini 4 Argon = 53 per the shared leaderboard record). It is
slow (35.3 tok/s) but concise (49M tokens, against an 82M median), which is why its cost per
task is negligible. AA measures 11 providers, whose cost per task spans $0.00–$0.54 and whose
median output speed spans 9–501 tok/s.

## Gaps

- No per-benchmark sub-scores were extractable; the index is composite and the individual
  evaluation charts are client-rendered.
- AA's price of $0.00 reflects a free route (its own provider table shows $0.00–$0.54 cost per
  task across 11 providers); it cannot be used as a market price.
- AA's "#19 / 141" rank is within a class it defines (open weights, 4B–40B, reasoning
  compared across both), not a global rank.
- The captured page shows only the reasoning variant; no delta against the non-reasoning
  variant is given.

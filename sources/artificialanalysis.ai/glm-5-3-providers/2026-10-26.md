## What this establishes

- A **third, independent view of the provider market**, measuring 26 providers against
  OpenRouter's 37 named providers / 48 routes. AA's count is of providers it benchmarked, not
  of routes listed.
- That the model's serving cost and speed are dominated by **who serves it, not what it is**:
  1110% speed spread and 21.6× price spread for the same weights.
- That AA's own headline speed figure (90.1 t/s on the model page) is roughly **8× slower than
  the fastest provider it measured** (708.5 t/s). Both are AA numbers, on different pages; the
  model-page figure is presumably a default or aggregate endpoint, but AA does not say.
- That the cheapest providers are all fp4 or otherwise quantized builds (FP4 appears in three
  of the five fastest names), so speed and price rankings partly measure quantization choices.

## Gaps

- No per-provider table in the text capture: only the top five in each category, so Z.AI's own
  endpoint has no captured rank.
- No quantization, context or uptime data per provider.
- "Blended price" is not defined on this page beyond the label; the blend ratio is not stated.
- 26 providers here versus 37 on OpenRouter; the overlap is not resolvable from either capture.

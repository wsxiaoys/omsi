Artificial Analysis is the only independent measurement in this evidence set, and it is thin:
one composite score (12 on Intelligence Index v4.3.2), one speed figure (241 tok/s median across
providers), one TTFT (0.75 s), one cost-per-task ($0.11), and a verbosity count (84M tokens). It
confirms the vendor's headline shape — cheap, fast, concise, strong for its size class but far from
the frontier — and it is the source of the $0.15/$0.60 price that other aggregators repeat. It does
not independently confirm any of OpenAI's benchmark numbers.

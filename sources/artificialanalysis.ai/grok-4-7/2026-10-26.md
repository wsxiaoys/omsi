## Vendor-claim versus independent measurement

Both xAI and Artificial Analysis ran Terminal-Bench 4.0, GDPval, and AA-Briefcase. Comparing them
is the most useful thing this record does.

| Benchmark | xAI's claim (Grok 4.7, xhigh) | Artificial Analysis, measured | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Terminal-Bench 4.0 | 37.6% | **25.76%** | −11.8 points |
| GDPval | 1695 Elo | **1721.59 Elo** (GDPval-AA v2.1, CI 1700–1743) | +26.6, and xAI's figure falls outside AA's CI |
| AA Briefcase | 1657 Elo | **1637.84 Elo** | −19.2 |

The Terminal-Bench gap is the material one: xAI's own table shows Grok 4.7 within 0.3 points of
GPT-5.6 Sol (37.6% vs 37.3%) and 20.3 points off Fable 5.1; on Artificial Analysis's harness the
same model scores 25.76%, roughly two thirds of xAI's figure. The direction of the 4.6→4.7
improvement survives in both (xAI: +17.3; AA measures 4.7 but publishes no 4.6 row here, so the
delta itself is not independently checkable). The GDPval and AA-Briefcase gaps are much smaller
and in opposite directions, which is what a harness-version difference looks like rather than a
claim-level discrepancy — AA runs "GDPval-AA v2.1", xAI reports plain "GDPval".

## What this establishes

- An **independent absolute score**: 46 on Intelligence Index v4.3.2, rank 32 of 221.
- An **independent confirmation of the release date**, 2026-09-21.
- That the model is **mid-frontier, not leading**: on the rendered board 23 of 205 rows score
  above 46 — Claude Opus 5.5 (max with fallback) 58, Claude Sonnet 5.5 (max with fallback) 56,
  Claude Opus 5.5 (xhigh with fallback) 56, Claude Opus 5.5 (high with fallback) 54, Claude
  Fable 5.1 (max with fallback) 53, GPT-6 Astra (max) 53, GPT-6.1 Sol (max) 52 — and Grok 4.7
  (xhigh) sits level with MiMo-V2.6-Pro (46) and GPT-6 Astra (low) (46), above GLM-5.3 (max) (45)
  and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (max) (45). Artificial Analysis's own ranking puts it at 32 of 221
  on the index; the rendered table here shows only 205 rows.
- That **effort level moves the composite by up to 4 points** (xhigh 46, high 46, low 42) while
  moving cost per task by 3.1x ($3.24 → $1.03) and TTFT by 14x (75.96 s → 5.43 s). `xhigh` and
  `high` score identically on the Index in this capture, so paying for `xhigh` is not justified by
  the composite — though `high` is cheaper and much faster.
- That the model is **very verbose** (240M output tokens on the Index vs an 81M median) and has a
  **long tail on TTFT** (p95 267.8 s), both of which raise effective cost and hurt interactive use
  in ways the $2/$6 sticker price does not show.
- That Artificial Analysis **flags `grok-4.6` and `grok-4.5` as deprecated** while xAI still
  serves both — including `grok-4.6` on the US regional endpoint
  ([regions](../../x.ai/regional-endpoints/2026-10-26.md)). The aggregator's deprecation flag is
  its own editorial call, not a vendor statement.

## What this does not establish

- No confidence intervals for the Index itself, and no task counts per evaluation.
- No CursorBench 4.0, DeepSWE v1.1, EEBench, Harvey Legal Agent, HealthBench Professional, or
  LatchBio/HackerBench scores, so the vendor's headline rows other than Terminal-Bench, GDPval,
  and AA-Briefcase cannot be cross-checked at all.
- No 4.6 row, so the claimed generation-over-generation gain is not independently verified.
- No image-input evaluation: the model is marked `inputModalityImage: true` but no multimodal
  benchmark appears in the payload (`mmmuPro` is `null`).

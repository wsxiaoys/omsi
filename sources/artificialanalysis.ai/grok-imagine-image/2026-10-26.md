## What this establishes

- Artificial Analysis **does** carry a distinct Elo for the base tier: **1022.23**
  (95% CI 1011.23–1033.23). This is the only independent measurement found for the
  `grok-imagine-image` model ID.
- The quality tier measures **1048.86** (CI 1039.86–1057.86). The two confidence intervals do not
  overlap; the gap is about **27 Elo**. On this evidence the tiers are behaviourally distinct, not
  a repackaging of one model.
- `pricePer1kImages: 50` for the quality tier corroborates xAI's $0.05/image for that tier, and the
  ~5.1 s median generation time (p05 4.09 s, p95 6.46 s) is an independent latency measurement for
  the quality tier — **not** for the base tier.
- Artificial Analysis lists no host endpoint for either Grok Imagine image tier
  (`providersUrl: null`), so its latency and price figures are not tied to a specific host.

## Coverage gaps in this aggregator view

- The family page captured on 2026-10-26 lists `grok-imagine-image-quality` but **not**
  `grok-imagine-image-2.0`, and its model table does not list `grok-imagine-image` either — the base
  tier's Elo is only visible on its own model page. Aggregator absence is not evidence that 2.0
  does not exist or was not measured; it means this capture cannot say.
- No prompt set, sample size, or vote count is published on either page, so the Elo cannot be
  re-derived.
- The creator is labelled "SpaceXAI", matching the self-branding on docs.x.ai during this capture
  rather than the "xAI" name used elsewhere.

## What this does not establish

- Nothing about editing quality for either tier (the Elo is a text-to-image image-quality score).
- Nothing about the base tier's speed or price, which this aggregator does not carry.

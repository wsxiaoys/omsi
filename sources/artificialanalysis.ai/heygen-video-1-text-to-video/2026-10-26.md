## What this establishes

- **AA treats HeyGen Video 1 as derived from MiniMax H3** (`isDerived: true`, `baseModel: {name:
  "MiniMax H3", label: "Based on MiniMax H3"}`), which independently corroborates the vendor's own
  "built on MiniMax H3 and post-trained by HeyGen" statement.
- On the main board it is **4th of 30**, statistically tied with the two rows on either side
  (1157 vs 1158 and 1156, with ±15), i.e. in the leading group but not first.
- On the **silent** board it is **1st of 32** (1143, range 1-2). The vendor's page claim "Ranked first
  on Artificial Analysis' video leaderboard" is true only of this sub-board.
- Its weakest measured categories are **live-action film, architecture/real estate,
  audio-synchronization and dialogue/lip-sync** (9th–10th), which matches the vendor's own caveats
  about soft organic motion and close hand work, and suggests the generated audio track is a relative
  weakness rather than a strength.
- **AA benchmarked it with `prompt_enhancement: "ultraquality"`**, a setting that caps duration at
  15 s in the vendor API and is not the default (`quality`) nor fal's default (`turbo`). Scores
  therefore describe the slowest, most-expanded prompt path.
- AA's `releaseDate` **2026-10-13** and `introducedAt` **2026-10-14** are the earliest dates any
  captured source gives for the model; the vendor's changelog only says September 2026 for the API
  reference.
- AA's `pricePerMinute` **$2.40** matches no published rate (vendor: $0.01/s promo, $0.02/s regular at
  768p; fal: $0.01–$0.03/s promo). The basis is undocumented.

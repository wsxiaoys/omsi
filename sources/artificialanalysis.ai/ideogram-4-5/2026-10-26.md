### Corroboration of price

The $100 per 1k images figure is $0.10 per image, matching the first-party High prompt-only price
for Generate ([Ideogram model page](../../ideogram.ai/ideogram-4-5-model-page/2026-10-26.md)) and

([fal text-to-image](../../fal.ai/ideogram-v4-5/2026-10-26.md)), which supports reading fal's
number as the outlier rather than a 2K tier.

## Interpretation, with limits

- Ideogram 4.5 (High) sits mid-pack on text-to-image preference: ~129 Elo below the arena leader
  (GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst max) with non-overlapping 95% intervals, and statistically
  indistinguishable from a broad band running from FLUX 3 Image Klein (1072) to Qwen-Image-2.1-Pro
  (1069). It is the strongest Ideogram model listed (4.0 Quality is 1026, 3.0 is 911).
- **This does not measure what Ideogram claims.** The arena measures text-to-image preference from
  blind pairwise votes; it says nothing about multi-turn edit fidelity, artifact buildup, or
  typography, which are 4.5's advertised strengths. No independent edit-quality measurement was
  captured.
- Elo deltas are relative within one board; the intervals above are the appropriate equivalence
  band, so differences smaller than ~20 points on this board are not signal.

## Known gaps

- The arena's per-model row does not state which host or endpoint served the images, the prompt
  set, or the number of votes behind the Ideogram 4.5 row.
- The "Image Editing" tab was not captured, so independent edit-quality data may exist but is not
  in evidence here.
- No typography-specific metric is published on the captured page.

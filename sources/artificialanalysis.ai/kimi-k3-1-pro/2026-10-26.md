## Three classification conflicts with first-party material

1. **"Proprietary model."** The vendor calls K3.1 Pro its "most capable 3T-class open-weight
   model" and promises weights "by Nov 4". AA labels it proprietary, which on AA's own
   definition ("Indicates whether the model weights are available") is the correct label at
   capture: no weights were published. The label is a statement about availability, not about
   the vendor's intent.
2. **Input modality.** AA: "text and image". Vendor docs and OpenRouter: text, image **and
   video**. AA's narrower reading is consistent with the model having been measured on a
   text+image harness, but the page does not say so.
3. **"A non-reasoning variant may also exist."** This is AA's generic caveat on every
   reasoning model page. The vendor states the opposite in plain terms: thinking cannot be
   turned off, only effort can be lowered.

## Notes on the capture

- The string "No publicly available API" appears eight times on the page, each time as a
  **chart legend entry** for a hatched data series (models without a public API). It is not a
  statement about this model, which AA clearly measured. Recorded here so it is not misread.
- AA's cost figure ($2.11 per index task) and the vendor's list price ($2/$10) are consistent
  in the sense that AA states the price it used; AA's own "cache discount 90%" matches the
  vendor's $0.20 cached-input rate against $2.00 input.

  ([AA leaderboard, 2026-10-26](../../artificialanalysis.ai/leaderboards-models/2026-10-26.md))

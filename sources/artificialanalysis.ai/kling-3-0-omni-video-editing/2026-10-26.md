  fixed anchor of AA-Video-Editing v1.1, and every one of the other nine ranked models scores above
  1000. Rank 10 of 10, with 19,250 pairwise samples — the largest sample count on the board after
  HappyHorse and Wan 2.7.
- **A release date**: AA records `releaseDate: 2026-02-04` for both the 1080p and 720p Omni rows,
  which matches fal's `publishedAt` for the O3 endpoints exactly.
- **The canonical name**: AA calls the model "Kling 3.0 Omni", never "O3". Its creator field is

- **A price disagreement**: AA quotes $10.91/min for 1080p, i.e. $0.1818/s. The vendor's own
  1080p-with-video-input list price is $0.168/s ($10.08/min) and its 1080p-with-audio generation price
  is $0.14/s ($8.40/min). No captured configuration produces $0.1818/s exactly. The same row on the
  *image-to-video* board is quoted at $8.40/min, which **is** the vendor's $0.14/s audio-on 1080p rate,
  so AA's prices do map onto vendor rows elsewhere — which makes the editing figure the anomaly.

## Caveats

- **An anchor is not a measurement.** Elo 1000 for this row is definitional; the interval is zero
  because the scale is pinned to it. What the board measures is the *gap* between this model and the
  others, not an absolute quality level.
- The board evaluates editing from a text instruction with "audio kept". That is close to, but not
  identical with, the fal `keep_audio: true` default; it is not a test of reference-image or element
  editing, multi-shot editing, or 4K output.
- No direct tests were run for this knowledge base; this record transcribes AA's published numbers.

## Gaps

- AA does not publish the prompt set, the rater pool, or per-category ranks for this row in the

- No indication of which endpoint (vendor API, fal, Replicate) the ratings were generated through.
- The 720p Omni row appears on the image-to-video board but was not located in this board's All list;

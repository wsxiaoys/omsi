## What this establishes

- An independent blind-vote leaderboard exists for Krea 3, with two separately rated modes.
- **Ultra is the competitive mode; Fast is materially lower.** Ultra's point Elo is 25.4 above Fast
  and its rank is 4 vs 11. The mode difference is therefore not just price: the board rates them
  differently.
- Ultra is in the top group but is not the leader. It trails GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst (max) by ~48 Elo
  and Flare (max) by ~31 Elo on the point estimates, and its interval overlaps the models below it.
- Krea 3 is listed as `openWeightsUrl: null`, i.e. AA does not record public weights.

## Gaps and cautions

- **Live board, no snapshot.** AA publishes no page date. The values moved between Krea's 2026-10-20
  reading (Ultra #5, 1150.58, 5,378 samples; Fast #11, 1125.11, 5,087) and this 2026-10-26 reading
  (Ultra #4, 1151.11, 5,505; Fast #11, 1125.67, 5,203). Cite the capture date with any number.
- **AA's price column does not reconcile with Krea's list price.** AA shows $270/1k for Ultra and
  $70/1k for Fast, i.e. $0.27 and $0.07 per image. Krea's API pricing gives $0.06 for a 2K fast
  image, $0.08 for 2K high, and $0.26 for 2K ultra with no references. The $0.01 differences may be
  an AA estimate or a different configuration; do not use AA's price column as a cost source without
  checking.
- AA rates "Ultra" and "Fast" only. There is no `high` row, so the middle mode is unmeasured here.
- The category boards (use cases and editing actions) were not captured directly; Krea's post
  transcribes them. See the [Krea benchmark record](../../krea.ai/krea-3-benchmarks/2026-10-26.md).
- No raw votes, prompts, or per-category sample exports were captured.

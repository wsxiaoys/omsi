## The row that matters

Verbatim row text:

> `DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (max) 1M DeepSeek 45 $0.26 169 1.17 15.96`

Readable with confidence: name, 1M context, vendor DeepSeek, **Intelligence Index 45**,
**$0.26** blended price per 1M tokens. The final three numbers (`169`, `1.17`, `15.96`)
are not labelled in the capture; by analogy with other rows they are speed and latency
figures, but this record does not assign them meanings.

**The entry is named after the `1017` checkpoint.** Artificial Analysis does not list a
separate un-suffixed "DeepSeek V4.1 Flash" row on this capture. The model page at
`/models/deepseek-v4-1-flash` is titled "DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (max)", which is the same
model under a shorter label — the `(max)` suffix denotes maximum reasoning effort, not a
variant.

## What this establishes

- An **independent absolute score** for the `1017` checkpoint: 45 on the AA Intelligence
  Index v4.3.2.
- That the model is **mid-pack, not frontier**: 13 points below Claude Opus 5.5 and level
  with GLM-5.3. DeepSeek's own agentic benchmark rows make the model look closer to the
  frontier than a composite index does.
- That the model is the **best-scoring DeepSeek entry** on the board, above V4 Pro (36).

## What it does not establish

- **No delta.** There is no un-suffixed V4.1 Flash row, so this capture cannot measure the
  improvement over the September checkpoint. The delta figures attributed to Artificial
  Analysis elsewhere in the wiki come from secondary reporting, not from this page.
- No Terminal-Bench 4.0 sub-score appears in the text capture; the index is composite.
- No confidence interval or task count in the captured text.

## Gaps

- Column meanings unresolved (see above).
- Index composition is AA's own (v4.3.2: AA-Briefcase, GDPval-AA, AutomationBench-AA,
  Terminal-Bench 4.0, SciCode, HLE, GDP.pdf, CritPt, AA-Omniscience, AA-LCR). A composite
  score is not comparable to any single vendor benchmark row.
- AA index values drift as the index version changes, so a score is only meaningful with
  the version and date attached.

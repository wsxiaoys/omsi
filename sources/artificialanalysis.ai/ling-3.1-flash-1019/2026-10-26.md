## Summary

This is the only independent measurement of the `1019` checkpoint, and it is the evidence that
makes the checkpoint question answerable. It says three things at once:

1. **The gain is real and concentrated.** Briefcase +124 Elo with non-overlapping confidence
   intervals, CritPt +8, AutomationBench +5.8, HLE +4, GDP.pdf +5.6, SciCode +2.8, GDPval +36.
2. **It is not uniform.** Terminal-Bench 4.0 and AA-LCR move slightly *down*, presentation Elo
   moves down, and AA-Omniscience goes from +2.2 to −0.3 — the model answers more and hallucinates
   slightly more.
3. **It is paid for in tokens.** Output tokens rise 59% and cost per index task rises 48% at an
   unchanged unit price. Ranked by intelligence it moves #8 → #4 of 122; ranked by cost per task
   it moves the wrong way at #40 → #44 of 47, and it is last of 48 on verbosity.

Speed is unmeasured for this entry, so no latency comparison is possible.

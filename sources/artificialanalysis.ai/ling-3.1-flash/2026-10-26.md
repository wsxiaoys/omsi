## Summary

AA's independent measurement of the model the OpenRouter route serves: **index 41, rank 8 of 122,
218 t/s, $1.01 per index task, 220M index tokens**. The profile is "fast but very verbose" —
top-five speed, bottom-decile cost-efficiency among compared models — and the capability shape is
strong on knowledge/work benchmarks (GDPval 1617 Elo, HLE 39.4%, LCR 83%) and weak on
terminal-agent work (Terminal-Bench 33.3%).

Two points of friction with other sources: AA classifies it "Open weights" with an MIT licence
(matching the repository) but its Openness sub-scores are low because post-training and
pre-training data are not disclosed; and AA prices it at the **undiscounted** $0.30/$0.90, so the
$1.01 cost per task overstates what a reader pays during the 75% promotion.

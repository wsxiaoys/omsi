## Summary

This is the only independent measurement of LTX-2.5 located for this page. On both audio-enabled boards
it sits mid-table: 19th of ~23 on image-to-video at Elo 984, and 24th of ~30 on text-to-video at 949,
roughly 150–225 Elo below the board leaders. Against its own predecessor it is a clear improvement —
+81 Elo over LTX-2.3 Pro on i2v and +63 on t2v — and it is AA's second-ranked open-weights family on
both boards, behind MiniMax H3 by a wide and unambiguous margin (984 vs 1140; 949 vs 1158).

Three cautions:

1. **Fast outscores Pro on both boards** (984 vs 958 i2v; 949 vs 944 t2v), with overlapping intervals.
   AA offers no explanation, and it is the opposite of the vendor's tier positioning.
2. **AA's own note says LTX-2.5 Fast was generated at 3840×2160 with a 1080p rendition served to the
   arena**, so the Elo measures a 4K generation downscaled for comparison, not a 1080p request.
3. The board is being revised ("AA-Video-I2V v2.1 is coming soon, with a new prompt set"), so these
   numbers have a short shelf life.

## Gaps

- AA's `pricePerMinute` ($7.80 Fast, $10.20 Pro) corresponds to the vendor's 1080p rates ($0.13/s and

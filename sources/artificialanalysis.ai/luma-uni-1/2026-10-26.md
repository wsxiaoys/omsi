### Methodology as stated by AA (verbatim FAQ, Text-to-Image board)

> Models are ranked using an Elo rating system derived from blind comparisons judged by our validated
> private evaluators. Voters compare two images generated from the same prompt without knowing which
> model created each image. Higher Elo scores indicate a model is preferred more often.

Editing board FAQ:

> Voters compare edited outputs from the same input image and editing instruction, choosing the
> result they prefer.

## What this establishes

- An independent blind-vote board exists for both tiers on both tasks, with large sample counts
  (5.5k–9.5k per row).
- **Luma UNI 1 is mid-board, not a leader.** T2I: 992 Elo, rank 51 (range 45–60), 230 Elo below the
  top row. Editing: 1043, rank 40 (range 36–42), 156 Elo below the top row.
- **Max is materially better than base on this board**: +31 Elo on T2I and +36 on editing, for 2.5×
  the price. The T2I rank gap (33 vs 51) is well outside the ±12 intervals.
- AA's price column ($40.4 and $100 per 1k images) matches Luma's current list prices exactly
  ($0.0404 / $0.10), unlike the case with some other vendors.
- AA records no open weights (`openWeightsUrl: null`) for either row.
- AA's `releaseDate` for both rows is **2026-05-05** — the Uni-1.1 API date, not the March 2026
  launch of Uni-1 itself. The `introducedAt` values (2026-05-11 / 2026-05-15) are when AA began
  collecting votes. So AA's "Luma UNI 1" row is best read as the model served by the Uni-1.1 API.

## Gaps and cautions

- **Live board, no snapshot.** AA publishes no page date; cite the capture date with any number.
- AA's label "Luma UNI 1" does not exist as a vendor identifier — Luma's IDs are `uni-1` and
  `uni-1-max`, and its marketing says "Uni-1.1". The mapping from AA rows to vendor IDs is inferred
  from price and date, not stated by AA.
- The board is not dated to Luma's launch. Luma's own claim of "#1 in human preference Elo" on
  2026-05-05 ([Uni-1.1 post](../../lumalabs.ai/uni-1-1-api/2026-10-26.md)) is not reproducible on this
  board at capture; the two are not reconcilable from the captured material (different arena,
  different date, or both).
- No category breakdown (reference-based generation, style) was captured; Luma's claim covers a
  "Reference-Based Generation" category that these two boards do not show.
- No raw votes, prompts, or per-category exports.

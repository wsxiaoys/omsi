## Summary

AA gives the only **independent measurement** of MAI-Image-2.6 captured for this page: Elo 1166 on
text-to-image (5th, band 4–6, 8,342 comparisons) and 1151 on image editing (4th at capture, band
4–7, 16,644 comparisons). Both boards place it clearly above MAI-Image-2.5 (1118 / 1127) and above
2.5-Pro (1114 / 1123) — a consistent ~48 Elo text-to-image and ~24 Elo editing gap, well outside the
±11–13 intervals — and both place it below the GPT Image 2.5 pair and Nano Banana 2.1.

Two contradictions with vendor copy are preserved here rather than resolved:

1. Microsoft's launch post claims "#2 text-to-image and #1 image editing on Artificial Analysis (as
   of Sep 4, 2026)". On this capture 2.6 is 5th and 4th. Either the boards moved after September 4
   (they did: newer GPT Image 2.5 entries, Nano Banana 2.1, Grok Imagine Image 2.0 and 2.7-Preview
   all sit at or above it), or Microsoft was citing a different slice. The claim is stale, not
   necessarily false.
2. AA's per-1,000-image prices ($38.9, $19.5) are Microsoft's own chart figures to the cent, which
   suggests AA is reporting Azure list prices rather than measured spend. The price axis is
   therefore not independent evidence.

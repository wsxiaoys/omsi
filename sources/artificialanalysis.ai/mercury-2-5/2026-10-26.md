## Reading

- **This is a measurement of Mercury 2.5, not Mercury 3, and it does not transfer.** The wiki
  skill's rule applies: a new version does not inherit the predecessor's findings.
- **AA's measured 714 t/s is 36% below the vendor's claimed 1,107 t/s** for Mercury 2.5
  (`../../inceptionlabs.ai/mercury-2-5-launch/2026-10-26.md`). The gap is the single most useful
  caution when reading Mercury 3's "1,068 t/s" claim, which no independent source has yet
  measured.
- **Throughput and time-to-first-token diverge:** AA's median TTFT is **3.4 s** and end-to-end
  4.1 s for a model sold on conversational latency. High tokens/s does not imply a low first-token
  wait.
- **Terminal-Bench 4.0 = 0** on the predecessor, matching Mercury 3's 3.03% at Vals
  (`../../../vals.ai/mercury-3/2026-10-26.md`). The near-floor agentic-terminal result is
  consistent across two models and two evaluators.
- **AA's price ($0.25/$0.75) does not match Inception's docs ($0.20/$0.75)** or OpenRouter
  ($0.04/$0.15). AA's cache-hit price of $0.025 is exactly 10% of $0.25, so AA is internally
  consistent; the input price is simply different from the vendor's current listing.
- **No independent provider spread**: `hostModelCount: 1` and `performanceDataSource:
  firstParty` mean AA measured Inception's own endpoint, not a third-party host.

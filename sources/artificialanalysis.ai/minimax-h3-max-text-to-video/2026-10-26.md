## Attribution shown on the H3 Max row (verbatim page structure)

The row carries the **Fal** logo next to the model name "MiniMax H3 Max", and a tooltip button
labelled **"Based on MiniMax H3"** whose icon is the MiniMax logo. The same treatment is used for
FLUX 3 Max (720p), which carries a `creator: Fal` field and `isDerived: true` in the board's
structured data.

## What this establishes

- **An independent evaluator treats H3 Max as a fal-derived model, not a MiniMax model**: Fal logo
  for authorship, MiniMax logo in a "Based on MiniMax H3" tooltip. This is third-party corroboration
  of the identity fal and MiniMax both state.
- **H3 Max is measured, not assumed**: 6,320 pairwise samples, Elo 1152 ±15. The sample count is
  larger than FLUX 3 Max's (5,650) and close to the parent H3's (6,669), so the row is not thin.
- **H3 Max scores *below* the model it was post-trained from.** MiniMax H3 (768p) is Elo 1158
  (1144–1172); H3 Max is 1152 (1137–1167). The intervals overlap heavily, so this is *not* evidence
  that H3 Max is worse — it is evidence that the two are **statistically indistinguishable** on
  overall preference, which directly undercuts fal's "ranks #1 … including the original H3" claim.
- **H3 Max is rank 7, not rank 1.** Four models sit above the parent H3 as well (Wan 3.0, Utopai X,
  HeyGen Video 1, Dreamina Seedance 2.5). fal's blog asserts "In independent benchmarks from
  Artificial Analysis and Design Arena, H3 Max also ranks #1 against other video models"; on this
  board it does not.
- **AA prices both H3 and H3 Max at $4.80/min** — $0.08/s at 768P, matching MiniMax's own price list
  for both models. AA does not credit H3 Max with a speed or price advantage.
- AA's "Hide derived models" filter exists specifically for rows like this one, which means a reader
  who filters derivatives out will not see H3 Max at all.

## Gaps

- The board ranks **overall preference only**; the per-dimension breakdowns (prompt understanding,
  aesthetics) that fal reports are not on this page.
- No latency or throughput column, so AA's board cannot corroborate or refute the speed claims.
- The board does not say which resolution, duration, or endpoint configuration each generation used
  beyond the parenthetical in the model name ("MiniMax H3 (768p)"; H3 Max carries no resolution
  suffix).
- The structured `isDerived` / `baseModel` fields were read directly only for FLUX 3 Max; for H3 Max
  the same relationship is visible in the page markup (tooltip "Based on MiniMax H3") but the JSON
  record was not extracted.
- Design Arena's video board was not capturable with an H3 Max row (client-rendered; no H3 Max string
  in the returned HTML on 2026-10-26), so the "more than 50× the speed" claim attributed to Design
  Arena remains second-hand via fal's blog.

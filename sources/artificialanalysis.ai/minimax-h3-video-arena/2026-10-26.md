## Summary

This is the only independent measurement of H3 located for this page, and it is a strong one for
image-to-video: **H3 (768p) ranks first on AA-Video-I2V v1.1 at 1140 ±16** (4,209 samples), above FLUX 3
Max (1138) and Seedance 2.5 (1138), though the intervals overlap all three. On text-to-video it is
**third at 1158 ±14** (6,669 samples), behind Wan 3.0 (1174) and Utopai X (1168). On both boards AA
names it the **leading open-weights model**, with a gap large enough to matter (i2v: 1140 versus 1046 for
the next open model).

Three things about the measurement matter for reading H3:

1. **AA runs H3 at 768p, not 2K.** Its own note: "MiniMax H3 is available in 768p or 2K only. We run it
   at 768p because our benchmark standard is the highest resolution at or below 1080p." The 2K
   regeneration path that MiniMax markets as the default is therefore not what these Elo scores measure.
2. **AA files H3 under the model family `minimax-hailuo`** (`url: "/video/model-families/minimax-hailuo"`),
   independent corroboration of the Hailuo lineage that MiniMax implies and fal encodes as
   `hailuo-03-new`.
3. **AA lists H3 Max as a derived model credited to Fal**, `isDerived: true`, `baseModel` "MiniMax H3",
   release date 2026-08-26 — the same attribution MiniMax and fal both make, from a third party.

A minor date discrepancy: AA prints H3's release date as **2026-07-30**, one day before MiniMax's own
blog post and release-notes entry (2026-07-31).

## Gaps

- No per-prompt results, per-prompt sample counts, rater demographics, or prompt-set contents.
- The board is stated to be revised ("AA-Video-I2V v2.1 is coming soon, with a new prompt set … 1080p
  videos on the silent board"), so these numbers will be superseded.
- AA's price column ($4.80/min for H3, $5.76/min for H3 Max on i2v) is a normalisation that does not
  correspond to any published first-party schedule; MiniMax charges $0.08/s at 768P, which is
  $4.80/min, so the i2v figure is consistent with 768P billing — the $5.76/min for H3 Max is not
  explained.
- H3's 2K output, reference-conditioned generation, and audio quality are not separately evaluated;
  the boards measure a single configuration per model.
- Only the "Overall / With Audio" views were captured, plus the silent-board FAQ text; per-category
  genre views were not transcribed.

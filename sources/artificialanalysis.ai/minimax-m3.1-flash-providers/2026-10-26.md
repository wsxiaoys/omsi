## Summary

- Third-party serving is **widely distributed**: 13 providers, with blended prices from $0.10/M
  (below MiniMax's own $0.15/$0.60 list) up to roughly $0.23/M.
- **Speed varies by 7.5× across providers** (764 t/s at Inco vs the slowest), and latency by a
  similar order. Provider choice matters more for this model than for models with one or two
  hosts.
- The 764 t/s top end is far above both AA's model-page figure (117.9 t/s) and MiniMax's own
  "200+ TPS" claim — three different numbers measuring three different things (provider
  configuration, AA's default provider, vendor marketing).
- The provider list here (13) does not match OpenRouter's stated 10 providers
  ([OpenRouter](../../openrouter.ai/minimax-m3.1-flash/2026-10-26.md)); AA measures providers
  directly, OpenRouter counts only those it routes to.

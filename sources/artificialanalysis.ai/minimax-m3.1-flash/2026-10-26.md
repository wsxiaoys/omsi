## Summary

- **Independent composite score: 43, rank 5 of 173** — top-five on AA's index at capture, at a
  list price AA calls "competitively priced".
- **Cost per task $0.22** against a $0.15/$0.60 token price; the gap is driven by verbosity
  (**180M** index tokens against a 100M median). AA's own verdict is "very verbose".
- **Speed 117.9 tok/s**, "faster than average (113)" — but well below the vendor's "200+ TPS,
  P50 170+" claim ([launch post](../../minimax.io/minimax-m3.1-flash-launch/2026-10-26.md)).
  Different workloads and different measurement points are the likely explanation; neither
  source states enough to reconcile them.
- **AA classifies the model as "Proprietary"** in the page header even though the weights are
  public under a community licence. That is a classification disagreement with the vendor and
  with the Hugging Face repository, preserved rather than resolved.
- AA lists **text and image** input only, not video — a third modality position, against vendor
  and OpenRouter's text+image+video.

## Gaps

- Per-benchmark sub-scores (Terminal-Bench 4.0, HLE, SciCode, etc.) are behind client-rendered
  charts and were not extractable from this capture; they reach the wiki only through
  OpenRouter's restatement ([OpenRouter](../../openrouter.ai/minimax-m3.1-flash/2026-10-26.md)).
- No effort setting is stated for the measured configuration. AA shows a single row for this
  model, unlike effort-split models elsewhere on the site.
- "Released October 2026" only; AA does not give a day.
- No confidence intervals, task counts, or per-provider breakdown on the model page (the
  provider breakdown is a separate page, captured separately).
- AA's "Proprietary" label is not explained; the page's own legend distinguishes "Open Weights",
  "Open Weights (Commercial Use Restricted)" and "Proprietary", so the label appears to be a
  deliberate classification rather than a rendering artefact.

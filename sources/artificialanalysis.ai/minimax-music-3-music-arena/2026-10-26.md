## Method (verbatim, FAQ on both pages)

> Models are ranked using an Elo rating system derived from blind comparisons judged by our validated
> private evaluators. Evaluators listen to pairs of music samples generated from the same prompt and
> choose their preferred track without knowing which model created it.

> AA-Music-Instrumental v1.1 ranks models generating music without vocals, while AA-Music-Vocal v1.1
> ranks models that generate music with singing or vocal elements.

## What this establishes

- **Music 3.0 is the anchor model on both boards, and its predecessor is statistically
  indistinguishable from it.** On vocals: 3.0 = 1000 (fixed), 2.6 = 997 ±18. On instrumentals:
  3.0 = 1000 (fixed), 2.6 = 1001 ±21, 2.5+ = 1007 ±21. Every MiniMax entry on both boards falls
  inside one ±18/±21 confidence interval of the anchor.
- Because the anchor is *fixed* rather than measured, AA publishes no confidence interval for
  Music 3.0 itself; the ±18/±21 bands apply to the models compared against it. The comparison
  "2.6 vs 3.0" is therefore a comparison against a fixed reference, and the ±18 on the 2.6 row is
  the only uncertainty estimate available.
- Music 3.0 sits **well below the leaders**: 144 Elo behind Suno v6 on vocals and 148 behind on
  instrumentals — roughly eight times the interval width, so the gap is not noise.
- The two boards disagree about the MiniMax line's internal ordering (2.5+ ranks above 3.0 on
  instrumentals, below it on vocals), and every difference is inside the interval. No MiniMax
  music version is measurably better than another on AA's data.
- Sample counts (≈2.2k–3.3k votes per entry) are comparable across the three MiniMax rows, so the
  null result is not an artefact of a thinly-voted entry.

## Gaps

- No per-prompt breakdown, no audio-quality sub-scores, no latency or price columns on these boards.
- The board does not state which endpoint, checkpoint, duration, or output format produced the
  samples — so the measurement cannot be attributed to a specific host or to the open weights.
- No confidence interval is published for the anchor (Music 3.0) itself.
- The page does not say when each MiniMax entry was introduced or when the boards were last refreshed.

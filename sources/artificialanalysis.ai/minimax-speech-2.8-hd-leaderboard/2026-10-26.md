## What this establishes

- **The first independent quality number for this model.** Elo 1174.05 with a ±10 CI, from an arena
  that states its votes come from users and that its measurements are run independently.
- **The tier ladder is real and measurable within the family:** 2.8 HD 1174.05 > 2.8 Turbo 1152.5
  (Δ 21.6, outside the CIs) > 2.6 HD 1125.98 (Δ 48.1 vs 2.8 HD) > 2.6 Turbo 1120.05 > 02 HD 1108.25
  > 02 Turbo 1088.64. Every step in the vendor's generational story survives independent
  measurement, and the HD/Turbo premium buys ~20–22 Elo at 1.67× the price.
- **"Ranked first" is false as of capture.** Speech 2.8 HD sits 24th of 98, ~159 Elo below the
  leader (Eleven v4 Turbo). This directly contradicts the Replicate README's "ranked first on the
  Artificial Analysis Speech Arena"
  ([Replicate record](../../replicate.com/minimax-speech-2.8-hd/2026-10-26.md)). No captured
  snapshot shows it first; whether it ever held first place between 2026-02 and 2026-10 is unknown
  and unfalsifiable from this capture.
- **Price aggregation agrees with everyone else:** $100/M chars for HD, $60/M for Turbo — the same
  numbers MiniMax and fal publish. The one exception is the Telnyx provider row for Turbo at $34/M,
  which is the only sub-list price found anywhere in this corpus.
- **The board's `releaseDate` is a fourth date for this model: 2026-02-14.** MiniMax's changelog says
  2026-01-23, fal listed it 2026-02-04, Replicate shows ≈2026-02-05. AA's date is 22 days after the
  vendor announcement and is not explained.
- MiniMax's own latency is not exceptional on this board: 6.99 s median generation time at 107
  chars/s for 2.8 HD, versus the vendor's positioning of Turbo as the fast tier (7.43 s here — the
  board's generation-time fields are noisy and should not be read as a Turbo/HD ranking).

## Gaps

- The page does not state vote counts per model, arena prompt sets, or when each Elo was last
  updated, so drift between February and October 2026 cannot be reconstructed.
- Elo is a preference measure, not a WER or speaker-similarity measure; no accuracy metric is
  published here.
- The generation-time fields show identical 5th/25th/75th/95th percentiles on some entries, which
  suggests sparse sampling — treat latency columns as indicative only.
- No per-language or per-accent breakdown.

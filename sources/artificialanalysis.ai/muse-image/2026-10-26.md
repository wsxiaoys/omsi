## Notes

- **AA's model slug is `muse-image-1.0`**, the same identifier Meta publishes, and AA lists a single
  endpoint, hosted by **Meta**, marked `isRepresentative`. AA does not list a fal-hosted variant of
  the model.
- `releaseDate` is 2026-07-07 (matching the launch blog), but `introducedAt` — when the entry
  entered the board — is 2026-08-27T00:08:23Z, one day after fal published its endpoints and the
  same day OpenRouter dates the model.
- `openWeightsUrl` is `null`: AA records no public weights.
- The page states its price column is "Price per 1k images generated by the model" and its
  generation time is "Median time the provider takes to generate an image over the past 3 days of
  measurements". Elo is "as determined by millions of responses from users in Artificial Analysis'
  Image Arena" — blind pairwise preference, not a benchmark score.
- The board renders "16 of 26 models" in its comparison charts, i.e. Muse Image is one of 16 image
  models AA plots on the quality/price/generation-time charts.

## Gaps

- **No per-task breakdown.** AA's single entry aggregates generation and editing; the editing board
  row reuses the same model entry, so an Elo difference between boards is a board effect, not a
  second model.
- **Generation time is provider-observed**, not vendor-published, and is a 3-day rolling median with
  no prompt-level detail; the p05–p95 spread (9.4 s to 27.0 s) is nearly 3×.
- **No resolution or output-size column** on either board, so AA cannot corroborate fal's "roughly
  2.5 megapixels".
- AA does not publish the prompt set, sampling procedure, or per-battle counts for this model beyond
  the appearance totals.

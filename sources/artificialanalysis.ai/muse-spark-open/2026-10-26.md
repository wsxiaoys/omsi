## Summary

The only independent measurement in this capture set. AA puts Muse Spark Open at **39 on Intelligence
Index v4.3.2, #12 of 122 in the open-weights Large class**, with 189 tok/s median output speed,
0.74 s TTFT, and $0.84 cost per Index task at a $1.25/$4.25 provider price — i.e. strong
intelligence and speed for an open-weight model of this size, but priced at the top of its class.
AA independently corroborates the 816B/42B parameter split, 1M context, Muse Model License, and the
2026-10-15 release date, and its modality list (text, image, speech, video) matches Meta's.

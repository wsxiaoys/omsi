## Methodology (verbatim from the page FAQ, shared with the vocal board)

> Models are ranked using an Elo rating system derived from blind comparisons judged by our validated
> private evaluators. Evaluators listen to pairs of music samples generated from the same prompt and
> choose their preferred track without knowing which model created it. Higher Elo scores indicate a
> model is preferred more often.

The page distinguishes the two boards: `AA-Music-Instrumental v1.1` ranks models on instrumental
output, `AA-Music-Vocal v1.1` on output with vocals.

## Summary

On instrumental material **Lyria 3 Pro outranks Lyria 3.5 by 1.5 Elo** (1079.9 vs 1078.4) — the
reverse of the vocal board's 3.8-point gap for 3.5, and again far inside the ±21 interval. Read with
the vocal board, the two Google models are separated by preference noise, not by a measurable
quality step. Both sit ~65 Elo below Suno v6. Lyria 2 is 18th (926), i.e. ~150 Elo behind its
successors, which is the one gap on these boards that is clearly larger than the intervals.

Presence of Lyria 3.5 and Lyria 3 Pro on an instrumental board indicates AA produced instrumental
samples from both; it is not a vendor statement that either model has an instrumental mode.

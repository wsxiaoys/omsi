## Method, verbatim from the page

> Models are ranked using an Elo rating system derived from blind comparisons judged by our validated
> private evaluators. Evaluators listen to pairs of music samples generated from the same prompt and
> choose their preferred track without knowing which model created it. Higher Elo scores indicate a
> model is preferred more often.

> AA-Music-Instrumental v1.1 ranks models generating music without vocals, while AA-Music-Vocal v1.1
> ranks models that generate music with singing or vocal elements.

## What the numbers say

- **Large is the best Stable Audio 3 tier on independent preference** (1013.86), Medium second (995.88),
  Small third (945.91) — the ordering the paper's own MOS reports for music (large 4.30 MUS, medium 4.15,
  small 3.15).
- **Medium does not clearly beat Stable Audio 2.5.** Medium 995.88 versus 2.5's 983.54 is a 12.3-point
  gap with ±21-point intervals on both sides; the board's own rank bands overlap (Medium's `rankLower` 9
  to `rankUpper` 16). The vendor paper claims medium "substantially improves musicality" over 2.5
  (OVL 4.20 vs 3.90, MUS 4.15 vs 3.70), and its own FAD/CLAP rows are close (0.107/0.390 vs
  0.106/0.395). **The independent preference measurement does not confirm the vendor's
  "substantially improves" framing** — it is consistent with a small, within-noise improvement.
- **All four Stable Audio entries sit below the MiniMax Music 3.0 anchor (1000)** except Large, which is
  14 points above it. On this board Stable Audio 3 is a mid-table instrumental model, not a leader.
- **AA's `releaseDate` for all three is 2026-05-20**, matching the vendor launch post.

## Absence on the vocals board

The AA-Music-Vocal v1.1 page contains **no Stable Audio entry at all** — consistent with the vendor's
own scope statement that Stable Audio 3.0 "is designed primarily for instrumental work … not intended to
generate intelligible vocals". AA therefore treats the family as instrumental-only, which is the
practical consequence of that limitation.

## Gaps

- No per-model AA page (`/models/stable-audio-3-medium` returns 404), so there is no AA-side price,
  latency, or generation-duration metadata for these entries.
- The board does not state which endpoint, checkpoint, or duration was sampled for each entry, and the
  three Stable Audio 3 tiers carry identical `releaseDate` and near-identical `appearances`, so the
  sampling protocol per tier is not visible.
- Elo is a preference measure, not a fidelity measure; it cannot be compared to the paper's FAD/CLAP.
- No capture of the Music Arena's raw vote data.

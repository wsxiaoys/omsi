## Methodology (verbatim from the page FAQ)

> Models are ranked using an Elo rating system derived from blind comparisons judged by our validated
> private evaluators. Evaluators listen to pairs of music samples generated from the same prompt and
> choose their preferred track without knowing which model created it. Higher Elo scores indicate a
> model is preferred more often.

The board name carries a version (`AA-Music-Vocal v1.1`), so scores are comparable within v1.1 only.

## Summary

Lyria 3.5 is Google's highest-ranked music model with vocals on this board and sits **5th of 13**
(1051 Elo, ±18). Lyria 3 Pro is 6th (1047, ±18). The gap is **+3.8 Elo against a ±18 confidence
interval**, i.e. the two are statistically indistinguishable on this measurement; the same is true
of the gap to Mureka V9 (1062) above them. Both Google models sit well below the Suno pair (1144 /
1132) and roughly 50 Elo below Mureka V9.5.

The `releaseDate` field (2026-07-29 for 3.5) is AA's own metadata, not a vendor announcement, and
`introducedAt` (2026-08-25) records when AA added the model to the arena. Nothing on the page states
which endpoint, host, or prompt set produced the samples, or how many samples per model.

## Gaps

- No sample-per-model count, prompt set, language, or generation-parameter disclosure; "appearances"
  is a vote count, not a sample count.
- No indication of which surface (Flow Music, Gemini, fal) supplied the Lyria 3.5 audio.
- No history: a single capture cannot show whether the 3.5 row moved after introduction.
- Elo intervals are wide (±18) relative to the differences between adjacent rows, so adjacent
  rankings are not separable.

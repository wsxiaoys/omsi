**Context contradiction.** AA records 131,072 tokens; the model card says "Input: up to 64K

suffix in the name marks this entry); the pair was not both captured, so the non-reasoning
variant's numbers are unknown here.

## Summary

AA's two Bolt records show the edge tier with two very different levels of evidence: Bolt 74B-A9B
has a full Intelligence Index (21.41) — above Lightning's 12.86, below Super VL's 26.49 — plus
a 43.7% hallucination rate and a hard zero on Terminal-Bench 4.0, but **zero hosting**; Bolt
E4B has essentially nothing measured except IFBench 61.1%. Neither has a price anywhere. AA
confirms both are vision-capable per its flags (E4B's card says text-only, so even that is
contested) and confirms 262K and 131K context respectively.

## Gaps

- `hostModelCount: 0` for both: AA measured models that no one was serving at capture. How the
  requests were routed is not stated.
- No speed, latency or price data at all for either Bolt model.
- E4B's context window and modality both conflict with its model card; unresolved.
- The Bolt E4B non-reasoning variant on AA was not captured.
- No independent confirmation of the Bolt 74B card's own benchmark table (which shows it
  beating Qwen3.6-35B-A3B and Gemma4-26B-A4B on 11 of 13 rows).

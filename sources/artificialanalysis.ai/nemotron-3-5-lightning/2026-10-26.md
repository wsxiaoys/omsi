## Summary

AA puts Lightning at Intelligence Index 12.86 — a small-model score, roughly half the
flagship's — with GPQA 74.3% as its standout result and near-zero agentic scores
(AutomationBench 0.8%, Terminal-Bench 4.0 0.5%, CritPt 0). It is the cheapest 3.5 tier at
$0.06/$0.20 per million tokens, and its classic-benchmark numbers corroborate NVIDIA's card
closely while its AA-specific numbers do not. AA's 31.6B/3.6B parameter count and 1M context
both conflict with the card or with every hosted route.

## Gaps

- AA's price is an aggregate across six hosts with no published rule.
- No per-host speed or price breakdown, so the 308 tok/s median cannot be attributed.
- AA did not run IFBench or FrontierCode on this model, leaving the card's strongest
  instruction-following claims unchecked.
- The 1M context figure has no corroboration from any host.

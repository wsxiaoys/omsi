## Summary

AA measures Super VL at Intelligence Index 26.49 — a mid-tier score, not a frontier one — with
a distinctive profile: fast (454 tok/s), cheap per task ($0.19), strong on GDPval (1162) and
SciCode (47.8%), and completely unable to do Terminal-Bench 4.0 (0%) or long-context retrieval
at the level the vendor implies (AA-LCR 69%). It confirms open weights under OpenMDW-1.1 and a
1M context ceiling, denies video input (contradicting the card and OpenRouter), and aggregates
price at $0.225/$0.575 per million tokens.

## Gaps

- AA's chart-only metrics (GPQA, MMLU-Pro, LiveCodeBench) are `null` for this model, so the

- No capture of AA's comparison table, so no peer-relative ranking is recorded here.

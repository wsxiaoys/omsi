## Summary

AA lists the Think checkpoint as an open-weights, text-only reasoning model, 32.2B parameters, Apache 2.0,
66k context, with an Intelligence Index of **7 — also marked "Estimated"** — described as below average
for open-weight reasoning models of similar size (class median 8). Its release date (2025-12-12) matches
Ai2's "Update 12/12" announcement and predates the date AA gives for the Instruct sibling.

## Gaps

- The index is an estimate, not a measured run; it cannot be compared against measured rows.
- No speed, cost or verbosity figures: AA had no public API to run.
- **Sibling date inconsistency inside AA:** Think is dated 2025-12-12, Instruct "January 13, 2026", though
  Ai2 announced both in the same 12/12 update and uploaded both weight sets on 2025-12-10. Neither AA
  page explains the difference.

- No per-benchmark numbers are printed; charts were not extractable.

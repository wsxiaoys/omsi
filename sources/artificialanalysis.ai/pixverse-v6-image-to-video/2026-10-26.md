## What this establishes

- **An independent absolute score**: Elo 1024 on AA-Video-I2V v1.1, rank 14 of the board with a
  rank range of 13–16, on 3,451 pairwise samples. That is mid-pack: 114 Elo below the board leader
  and 24 above the fixed anchor.
- **A release date of 2026-03-23**, one week *before* PixVerse's own launch post (2026-03-30) and six
  days before fal published V6 endpoints (2026-03-29). AA's `introducedAt` (2026-03-19) is earlier
  still. The same page pattern appears on other AA rows, where `introducedAt` predates the printed
  `releaseDate`; AA does not explain the difference.
- **AA's price for the row is $6.90/min**, i.e. $0.115/s. That matches no single published vendor or
  host rate exactly (vendor 720p+audio is $0.053/s; fal 1080p+audio is $0.115/s; Replicate
  720p+audio is $0.12/s), so the basis is unstated. Treat it as AA's own normalised estimate, not a
  quote.
- `openWeightsUrl: null` and `isDerived: false` — AA treats V6 as a first-party model with no
  published weights.
- Only **one** PixVerse row is on this board (V6). V6 Max (2026-10-22) and C1 (2026-04-07) are not
  listed, so this is the highest-scoring PixVerse entry on AA's I2V board by default, not by
  measurement.

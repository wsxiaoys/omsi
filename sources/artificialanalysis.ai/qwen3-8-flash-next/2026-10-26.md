## Contradictions and gaps

- **Entity naming vs. serving.** AA names the entity `Qwen3.8-Flash-Next`, labels it open
  weights with a 256k context, but measures it "based on Alibaba's API", which sells the
  1M-context `qwen3.8-flash` SKU. AA does not say which artifact its 256k refers to.
- **No separate Flash row.** AA has no entry for `Qwen3.8 Flash`; a reader cannot compare
  the open artifact with the hosted SKU on this board.
- **Latency disagreement.** 2.00 s TTFT (FAQ) against 39.89 s "time to first answer token"
  (provider page); the provider page attributes the difference to a 10k-input workload.
- **Verbosity rank vs. score.** Verbosity #45 of 47 — near the bottom of its class — while
  the index score is #9 of 121.
- No per-benchmark sub-scores, confidence interval or task count in the captured text.
  Index values drift with index version; 40 is only meaningful with v4.3.2 and 2026-10-26
  attached.

## What this establishes

- An independent composite score for the 0902 snapshot: **45** on AA Intelligence Index
  v4.3.2, level with DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 and GLM-5.3, one point below Grok 4.7 and
  MiMo-V2.6-Pro.
- An independent cost-per-task figure: **$5.41**, the most expensive of the index-45 group
  and 20× DeepSeek V4.1 Flash's $0.26 for the same index score.
- Independently measured speed: **40.5 t/s** output, **2.92 s** TTFT on Alibaba's API —
  slow for the price tier (median 88.7 t/s).
- Extreme verbosity: 190M output tokens to run the index, versus a median of 81M. Verbosity
  is why cost-per-task is high despite a mid-tier per-token price.

## Contradictions and gaps

- **Modalities:** AA says "text and image input". Alibaba's Model Studio and QwenCloud pages
  list **Image, Text and Video** input. AA's own OpenRouter-derived metadata elsewhere in the
  wiki also lists video. Either AA did not evaluate video or its metadata omits it; the
  vendor claim is broader than the independent record.
- **Weights:** AA labels the 0902 SKU "proprietary. The model weights are not publicly
  available", while Qwen publishes `Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B` weights under a "Qwen3.8-Max
  License". Both can be true — the Max serving configuration is not the open artifact — but
  a reader who takes AA's label at face value will miss that a Max-class open release exists.
  AA's separate 262k row for `Qwen3.8 2.4T A95B` is the evidence that it treats them as
  different artifacts.
- **Context:** AA reports 984k (its own measurement of usable context); the vendor states a
  1,000,000-token window with a 991,808-token max input.
- No per-benchmark sub-scores appear in this capture beyond the composite. The coding (76.2)
  and agentic (56) sub-indices are known only through OpenRouter's re-publication of AA data.
- No confidence interval or task count in the captured text.
- Index values drift with index version; 45 is only meaningful with v4.3.2 and 2026-10-26
  attached.

## Interpretation, with limits

- The three rated variants span a wide range: Utility (1026) and Utility Pro (1017) are close,
  with overlapping 95% intervals; Flash (949) is ~77 Elo below Utility with non-overlapping
  intervals, so the Flash gap is signal while the Utility/Utility Pro difference is not.
- Utility is the highest-rated Recraft entry on this board, but it is a predictability variant,
  not the base V4.1. **The base V4.1 and V4.1 Pro are unrated here**, so their quality relative to
  the rated variants — or to competitors — is not established by this evidence.
- AA measures blind pairwise preference on text-to-image prompts; it says nothing about vector
  output, typography control, image-to-image, or resolution-specific quality.
- Elo deltas are relative within one board; the 95% intervals are the appropriate equivalence
  band.

## Gaps

- **No base V4.1 or V4.1 Pro rows.** The roster's "AA Image Arena" lead resolves to the Utility,
  Utility Pro, and Flash variants only; the base model is not rated.
- Live board, no snapshot date. Values moved between boards/reads; cite the 2026-10-26 capture.
- The board does not state which host or endpoint served the images, the prompt set, or the
  number of votes behind each row beyond the appearances count.
- The "Image Editing" board was checked on 2026-10-26 and contains no Recraft rows; no independent
  edit-quality measurement for V4.1 was captured.
- AA's price column matches Recraft's list for the rated variants; it is not an independent cost
  measurement.

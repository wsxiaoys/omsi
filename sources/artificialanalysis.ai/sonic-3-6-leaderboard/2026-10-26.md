## Summary

Three facts this board supports:

1. **Sonic 3.6 is 4th, not 1st.** Elo 1292 ± 14, rank interval 4-5. Cartesia's marketing page claims
   "Ranked #1 in Speech Arena" (undated) — the same evaluator's board on the same day does not show
   that, on the Provider Voice Leaderboard at least. The claim may refer to another tab or an earlier
   date; nothing captured reconciles them.
2. **The generations separate materially on the same harness.** Sonic 3 1054 → Sonic 3.5 1190
   (+136) → Sonic 3.6 1292 (+102), all on the same board, same 8-voice protocol. This is independent
   support for treating 3.6 as a distinct model rather than a repackaging, and for the vendor's
   "quality update" framing.
3. **Price is flat across generations.** AA normalises all three Sonic rows to $49.0/1M characters,
   consistent with Cartesia billing every Sonic model at ~1 credit/character.

## Gaps

- The page does not state its prompt set, rater pool, sample selection or price normalisation method.
- No per-language or per-accent breakdown was captured; Cartesia's strongest claim ("native speaker
  quality" in 44 languages) cannot be checked from the aggregate row.
- One capture only, so the 3.6 row's stability over time is unknown.
- AA's $49.0/1M is a third-party normalisation; Cartesia publishes credits, not a USD-per-1M rate.

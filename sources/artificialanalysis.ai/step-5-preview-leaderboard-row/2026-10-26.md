## What the numbers add beyond the model page

- **Index precision.** 43.7343, not the rounded 44 or the 43.7 that OpenRouter prints. AA's own
  page rounds to 44; the underlying value is 43.73.
- **Cost per task** $1.0264 and **cache write** `null` — consistent with the vendor's statement
  that the cache-miss input price includes cache writes, i.e. AA has no separate write line to
  charge.
- **Latency decomposition.** Median TTFT 1.238 s, median time to first *answer* token 11.82 s,
  median reasoning time 10.58 s, median end-to-end 14.47 s. The model thinks for roughly ten
  seconds before the first visible answer token on AA's workload; the 1.24 s figure alone would
  misrepresent perceived responsiveness by an order of magnitude.
- **Speed spread.** Median 189.0 output tokens/s, 5th percentile 158.4, 95th percentile 258.9 —
  the spread is narrow, so the headline speed is not an artefact of lucky runs.
- **`deprecated: false`** on both the measurement and catalogue rows: AA records no deprecation
  for Step 5 Preview, while it marks all three earlier Step text models deprecated.

## Summary

AA's leaderboard payload confirms the model-page figures at full precision, adds the latency
decomposition (1.24 s to first token, 10.58 s reasoning, 11.82 s to first answer token), records
`releaseDate` 2026-09-18, `deprecated: false`, and `isOpenWeights: true`, and marks the earlier
Step text models deprecated.

## Gaps

- `releaseDate` has no cited first-party source; it disagrees with OpenRouter's 2026-10-08.
- The leaderboard does not say which reasoning-effort setting was measured, nor which provider
  endpoint served the requests (the provider page attributes all runs to StepFun).
- `isOpenWeights: true` is AA's availability judgement; the licence conditions sit behind an
  unread `LICENSE.txt`.

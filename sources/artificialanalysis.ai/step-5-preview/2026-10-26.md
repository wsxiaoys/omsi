## Two AA-internal conflicts worth recording

1. **Latency.** The model page FAQ says TTFT 1.24 s. The providers page says time to first
   *answer* token 11.82 s. These are different metrics — first token includes the first reasoning
   token; first answer token comes after thinking. A 10× gap between them is the model's
   reasoning length showing up in the second number, and neither figure alone describes
   perceived latency.
2. **Modality.** AA says "text and image" on both the model page and the FAQ, while the vendor
   documents text, image **and video** input, and OpenRouter's catalogue says
   `text+image+video->text`. AA's narrower reading is consistent with a text+image harness, but
   AA does not say what it tested.

AA independently measures Step 5 Preview at **44** on Intelligence Index v4.3.2 (#5 of 122,
median 19), 189 tok/s (#10 of 78), 160M index tokens (somewhat verbose), $1.03 per index task,

StepFun's own API.

## Summary

This is the only independent measurement of an Inworld TTS model captured for this page. On the
2026-10-26 board, Inworld's current flagship ranks **7th** (score 1267, interval 5-8) and TTS-2 Flash
**13th** (1218). **No TTS-1.5 row exists on this capture** — neither "Inworld TTS-1.5", "Realtime
TTS-1.5 Max" nor "Realtime TTS-1.5 Mini" is listed, which means the vendor's and Replicate's "#1 on
Artificial Analysis" claim is no longer visible on the board, and this record cannot verify it as a
past state.

Artificial Analysis's own normalised prices ($20.8/M for TTS-2, $10.4/M for TTS-2 Flash) are third
party estimates, not Inworld's list prices ($25/M and $15/M).

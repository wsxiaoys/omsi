## What this changes

1. **The "#1" claim is stale, not current.** On the captured boards HappyHorse-1.1 sits at #13 of 30
   (T2V) and #10 of 23 (I2V), with non-overlapping intervals below the leaders. fal's landing page
   prints a #1 rank "April 2026" for 1.0 with the caveat that scores "reflect early vote counts".
2. **1.1 measurably beats 1.0** on both boards (+17 Elo T2V, +19 Elo I2V) with overlapping intervals —
   a real ordering, not a separated tier.
3. **Alibaba's own Wan 3.0 outranks HappyHorse 1.1 by ~124 Elo on T2V and ~66 on I2V.** The vendor's
   own guide recommends Wan 3.0 for longer and more controllable work, consistent with HappyHorse being
   positioned as the cheaper prompt-to-clip model.
4. **The "$10.80 /min" figure equals the vendor's $0.18/s 1080P rate** ($0.18 × 60), and 1.0's $14.40/min
   equals the vendor's $0.24/s — so the aggregator is quoting vendor list price, and the vendor 1.0 rate,
   not fal's $0.28/s.

## Caveats

- Elo from blind human preference is a preference measure, not a capability test; ±15–17 intervals at
  these sample counts mean ranks 12–17 on the T2V board are effectively tied.
- The capture does not record which resolution/aspect/prompt set the board used for HappyHorse, nor
  which host served the clips.
- Artificial Analysis labels the lab **Alibaba-ATH** (Alibaba Token Hub), a finer attribution than the
  vendor documentation gives; the board does not cite its source for that attribution.

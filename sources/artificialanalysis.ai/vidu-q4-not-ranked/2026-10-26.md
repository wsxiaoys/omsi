## Summary

Artificial Analysis gives an independent quality anchor for Q3 and **none for Q4**. Any statement about
Q4's quality relative to Q3, Seedance, Kling or Wan is therefore vendor marketing plus host copy; there
is no blind-preference measurement of Q4 in this capture. The Q3 numbers are useful as a baseline: Q3
Pro ranks 16/≈30 on image-to-video at Elo 1016 and 26 on text-to-video at 937, i.e. mid-table on both.

AA's provider split also corroborates that Q3 is served by at least five routes (fal.ai, MachGen,
Replicate, Runware, Vidu, WaveSpeed — five per tier, with MachGen only on Turbo and WaveSpeed only on
Pro) — a breadth Q4 does not yet have.

## Gaps

- AA does not date its rows or say when a model was added; the "Release" column is the model's own
  release month, not the date AA started ranking it.
- No Q4 page, no "coming soon" note for Q4, and no statement of an inclusion criterion that would
  explain the omission (recency, access, provider submission).
- Price-per-minute figures ($7.20 and $3.90) do not state the resolution or host they are normalised to,
  so they cannot be reconciled with the vendor's credit table without an assumption.
- The I2V board carries both v1.1 and a "AA-Video-I2V v2.1 is coming soon" notice; the rows above are
  v1.1 (with-audio). The silent board (v1.0) was not transcribed.

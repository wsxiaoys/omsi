## Summary

The only independent measurement of the model in this corpus. On the default board Voxtral 3 TTS sits
**12th of 98 with 1221 ±14**, which is 59 points below Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS (1280 ±14), 112 below
Eleven v4 Turbo (1333 ±16), and — the material comparison — **+133 over its own predecessor**
(1088 ±12), against non-overlapping confidence intervals.

Footnote 1 settles the price question: the board's price column is "the cost to generate 1M
characters on the model creator's API", so $13.0/1M chars is Mistral's own list price restated, not
an independent estimate. Footnote 2 explains why the predecessor is `openWeights: true` but
`openWeightsCommercial: false`: CC BY-NC 4.0 does not permit commercial use.

The per-slice spread (1139.8–1358.8) is larger than the model's confidence interval and larger than
the gap to the leaders on some slices, so the headline rank is filter-dependent. This record quotes
the default board and treats the other values as context, not as evidence of a better or worse model.

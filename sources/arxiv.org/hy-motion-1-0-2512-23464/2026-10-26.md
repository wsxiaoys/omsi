## Abstract (verbatim)

> We present HY-Motion 1.0, a series of state-of-the-art, large-scale, motion generation models
> capable of generating 3D human motions from textual descriptions. HY-Motion 1.0 represents the first
> successful attempt to scale up Diffusion Transformer (DiT)-based flow matching models to the
> billion-parameter scale within the motion generation domain, delivering instruction-following
> capabilities that significantly outperform current open-source benchmarks. Uniquely, we introduce a
> comprehensive, full-stage training paradigm -- including large-scale pretraining on over 3,000 hours
> of motion data, high-quality fine-tuning on 400 hours of curated data, and reinforcement learning
> from both human feedback and reward models -- to ensure precise alignment with the text instruction
> and high motion quality. This framework is supported by our meticulous data processing pipeline,
> which performs rigorous motion cleaning and captioning. Consequently, our model achieves the most
> extensive coverage, spanning over 200 motion categories across 6 major classes. We release HY-Motion
> 1.0 to the open-source community to foster future research and accelerate the transition of 3D human
> motion generation models towards commercial maturity.

## Method details relevant to use

- **Skeleton and representation.** All training motion is retargeted to "a neutral SMPL-H skeleton".
  Each frame is a 201-dimension vector: global root translation (3), global body orientation (6), 21
  local joint rotations in 6D continuous representation (126), and 22 local joint positions (66).
  Velocities and foot-contact labels are dropped. The paper states the representation is "similar to
  DART, where the rotation representation is compatible with typical animation workflows".
- **Frame rate and clip length (verbatim).** "All motions were resampled to 30 fps, and sequences
  longer than 12 seconds were segmented into multiple clips." Canonicalisation: "Y-axis up, the
  starting position centered at the origin, the lowest body point aligned on the ground plane, and
  initial facing direction along the positive [axis]".
- **Inputs.** "The core is the HY-Motion DiT model, which accepts a text prompt and an expected
  duration of the motion as inputs and generates a clip of 3D human motion as outputs."
- **Text conditioning.** Qwen3-8B token-wise embeddings with bidirectional attention (following
  HunyuanVideo), plus CLIP; a separate duration-prediction and prompt-rewrite module is fine-tuned
  from Qwen3-30B-A3B.
- **Data scale.** Over 3,000 hours of pretraining motion data, 400 hours of curated fine-tuning data,
  and roughly 500 hours of mocap/animation assets acquired directly; real video clips were processed
  with GVHMR to recover SMPL-X tracks.
- **Scale finding (verbatim).** "while instruction-following capability consistently improves with
  larger models, motion quality reaches a saturation point beyond the 0.46B parameter size… we plan to
  release 'DiT-1B' as the primary HY-Motion 1.0 model".

## Notes

- This page names four variants and gives [max] the "top-tier" slot, but it does not repeat the
  docs' grounding-search-exclusive-to-[max] table; it attributes grounded generation to [max]
  only in the "At a glance" copy above.
- The performance list is presented for the family, not per variant. "32K text input tokens"
  and "Sub-10-second generation speeds" are therefore family claims; no source states them for
  [max] specifically, and the API reference documents no prompt-length limit at all.
- "400px²" and "Sub-10-second" have no conditions attached (resolution, steps, hardware,
  endpoint). Treat as marketing figures.

# BFL: "FLUX 3 Image Klein: open weights, 5.6B parameters" (launch post)

- Origin: Black Forest Labs, "FLUX 3 Image Klein: open weights, 5.6B parameters",
  https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-image-klein
- Evidence kind: official announcement (first-party marketing + vendor benchmarks)
- Publication date: October 15, 2026 (stated on the page)
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: FLUX 3 Image Klein (distilled and base), FLUX 3 Image, FLUX 3 Action,
  FLUX.2 [klein] 9B, Qwen-Image 2.1, Krea 2, Ideogram V4.5, FLUX.2 [pro]

## Captured content — verbatim

Launch paragraph:

> Today we are releasing FLUX 3 Image Klein, a 5.6B parameter open weights model for
> text-to-image generation, image editing and multi-reference generation with up to 10
> reference images. It shares core capabilities with the full FLUX 3 Image model: native 4K
> generation, pixel-perfect editing, and bounding boxes that control where elements are
> placed or confine edits to a defined area.

On lineage:

> FLUX 3 Image Klein builds on FLUX 3's shared learnings across images, video and audio.
> Images contribute spatial detail, video adds motion and physical dynamics, and audio
> provides information about events and interactions.

> The release includes a base model for fine-tuning and a step distilled model for
> low-latency generation, along with FP8, INT8 and NVFP4 builds developed with NVIDIA
> support, under the FLUX Kommunity License.

> FLUX 3 Image Klein is the second open weights release from the FLUX 3 family, following
> FLUX 3 Action. More will follow.

### Vendor benchmark claims (section "Leading open weights quality with fewer parameters")

> In internal evaluations, FLUX 3 Image Klein ranks first in text-to-image, multi-reference
> generation and image editing, ahead of larger open weights models. It also rates above the
> closed models in the comparison, Ideogram V4.5 and FLUX.2 [pro]. At 5.6B parameters, it is
> the smallest open model in the comparison, against 7B for Qwen-Image 2.1 and 12.9B for
> Krea 2. Compared with FLUX.2 [klein] 9B, it gains 91 Elo in text-to-image and 70 in image
> editing, with 38% fewer parameters.

> Parameter counts are for the image generator, as each publisher reports them; text encoders
> are not counted. Krea 2 does not support image editing and is not included in the image
> editing comparison.

No method, prompt set, vote count, or rater pool is given for these internal evaluations.

### Hardware and size claims

> The generator weights require 11.2 GB in BF16 and 5.6 GB in FP8. With the INT8 build,
> FLUX 3 Image Klein runs on NVIDIA GeForce RTX GPUs with as little as 8GB of VRAM, through
> ComfyUI or Diffusers, extending what FLUX.2 [klein] started to even smaller hardware. With
> the distilled model and the INT8 build, it generates a 1MP image in about 4 seconds on an
> NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 and under 9 seconds on an RTX 4070 through ComfyUI.

> On NVIDIA GB300, the distilled model generates a 1MP image in under 0.6 seconds in NVFP4.
> Serving a LoRA adds around 5% to generation time.

Timing caveat stated by BFL for the API latency charts:

> Latency is measured end to end, from request submission to the finished 1 MP image,
> including queueing. FLUX 3 Image Klein was timed on our servers. Other models were timed
> from the client, which includes polling and network time.

### Access, pricing, licence

> BFL API: the distilled model is available as flux-3-image-klein, with prompt upsampling and
> pixel restoration, which preserves unedited regions of an image exactly. Pricing starts at
> $0.009 per 1K image.

> Day-one support in ComfyUI, Hugging Face Diffusers, TensorRT-LLM, SGLang Diffusion and
> vLLM-Omni.

> LoRA training with Ostris's AI Toolkit and recipes in NVIDIA NeMo AutoModel.

> The weights are available under the FLUX Kommunity License, which allows free commercial
> use for individuals and companies with annual revenue under $5M, alongside research and
> non-commercial use. Larger organizations can deploy commercially through our self-hosted
> licensing options or API.

Safety paragraph (summary): dataset filters during pre-training, targeted fine-tuning during
post-training, internal red-teaming plus evaluation by external partner Cinder, claiming
"substantially fewer vulnerabilities than other popular open weights models".

## What this establishes

- Launch date 2026-10-15 and the canonical product name **FLUX 3 Image Klein**.
- 5.6B parameters, open weights, two checkpoints (base + step-distilled), three quantized
  builds, FLUX Kommunity License.
- Klein is positioned as the open-weights member of the FLUX 3 Image family that keeps 4K,
  editing, multi-reference (10 images) and bounding boxes — i.e. a smaller sibling, not a
  different modality.
- First-party BFL API price floor: **$0.009 per 1K image**.
- A vendor claim of +91 Elo (text-to-image) and +70 Elo (editing) over FLUX.2 [klein] 9B.

## Gaps

- Internal evaluation methodology, prompt sets, rater counts and confidence intervals are not
  published; the Elo deltas are not reproducible from this page.
- "Pixel restoration" is described in prose only; no parameter name, default, or
  documentation link is given, and the API reference (separate record) has no such field.
- The licence sentence here ("allows free commercial use for individuals and companies with
  annual revenue under $5M") is broader than the Hugging Face model card's wording, which
  limits the exception to commercial *output generation* and excludes commercial fine-tuning
  or distribution. See the model-card and ComfyUI records for the narrower readings.
- The 8 GB VRAM / RTX 3090-class claims are not reconciled with the model card's measured
  peak-memory figures (25–31 GB in BF16/FP8/NVFP4 with the reference code). Different
  quantization builds and frameworks are the likely explanation, but no source states which
  configuration the small-VRAM claim was measured on.

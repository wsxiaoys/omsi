# BFL: Open Weights Licensing page — tiers that include FLUX 3 Image Klein

- Origin: Black Forest Labs, "Open Weights Licensing", https://bfl.ai/licensing
- Evidence kind: official documentation (commercial licensing terms, marketing page)
- Publication date: not stated; page live at capture
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: FLUX 3 Image Klein, FLUX.2 [klein] (4B/9B Base), FLUX.2 [dev]

## Captured content — verbatim

Page framing: "Frontier models you can customize. License FLUX model weights. Fine-tune on your
data, self-host on your infrastructure."

Four tiers, each listing which models it covers:

| Tier | Models named | Volume | Domains / users |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Builder | "FLUX 3 Image Klein and FLUX.2 [klein] models" | 10K images/month | 1 domain, 10 licensed users |
| Platform | "FLUX 3 Image Klein + FLUX.2 [klein] Base 9B + FLUX.2 [dev]" | 100K images/month | 1 domain, 10 licensed users |
| Professional | "FLUX 3 Image Klein + FLUX.2 [dev]" | 100K images/month | up to 3 domains, 10 licensed users |
| Enterprise | "All models + new releases" | custom | custom |

Tier descriptions, verbatim excerpts:

> **Builder** — For developers and early-stage teams. Fine-tuning and LoRA rights, 10K
> images/month, and commercial use within a single domain [...] Not meant for client use or
> downstream applications.

> **Platform** — For product teams shipping at volume. Our FLUX 3 Image Klein, FLUX.2 [klein] Base
> 9B and FLUX.2 [dev] models with 100K images/month, built for SaaS companies and product teams
> integrating image generation as a feature for their end users.

> **Professional** — For agencies and service providers. Our FLUX 3 Image Klein and FLUX.2 [dev]
> models alongside 100K images/month [...] The first 3 clients are included. Beyond this, additional
> per-client fees apply.

> **Enterprise** — All models, every new release. Fully bespoke terms [...] Permissive commercial
> use. API + Weights combo pricing.

Every tier lists "Fine-tuning & LoRA rights". A separate "Need Synthetic Data?" block offers
"FLUX 3 Image Klein or commercial models" with "Training rights on outputs (scoped to use case)".

## What this establishes

- FLUX 3 Image Klein is the **entry model of BFL's paid self-hosting ladder** — it appears in all
  four tiers, including the cheapest (Builder), which is the only tier that names no other FLUX 3
  model.
- Commercial fine-tuning and distribution rights exist, but as a **paid licence**, which is
  consistent with the FLUX Kommunity License's exclusion of commercial fine-tuning (see the
  Hugging Face card record).
- This page does not restate the Kommunity Licence terms, does not give prices, and does not
  describe what "Qualifying User" means.

## Gaps

- No dollar figures, no contract text, no definition of "commercial use within a single domain".
- The page is a sales page; it does not link the actual licence documents for each tier, so the
  binding terms were not retrievable from this capture.

## What is absent

**No entry mentions FLUX 3 Max.** The strings "FLUX 3 Max" and "flux-3-max" do not occur in the
captured page. Every occurrence of "max" is one of: FLUX.2 \[max] (the December 16, 2025 image
model), the phrase "All FLUX.2 models — Pro, Max, Klein 4B/9B, and Flex", or ordinary words
("maximum control", "maximum duration", "Maximum quality"). BFL's changelog therefore records no
launch, endpoint, price, or capability change for FLUX 3 Max.

Caveat that limits what the absence proves: the newest entry is October 15, 2026, one day before
fal's FLUX 3 Max endpoints were created (2026-10-16) and four days before they were published
(2026-10-19). The changelog may simply not have been updated yet at capture time, so this is
**not** evidence that BFL declined to announce Max.

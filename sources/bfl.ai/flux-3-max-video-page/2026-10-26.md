## Captured content

The page is the FLUX 3 **Video** product page. FLUX 3 Max appears only as one marketing block
between the logo ticker and the "FLUX 3 Video's core capabilities" console; there is no separate
`/models/flux-3-max` route (it is absent from `https://bfl.ai/sitemap.xml`).

>

The block's only call to action is a link to fal:

### Site-wide placement

So BFL treats "FLUX 3 Max" as a named member of the FLUX 3 lineup while pointing its only
first-party link at fal.

### FLUX 3 Video facts stated on the same page (base model context)

- "FLUX 3 is Black Forest Labs' multimodal foundation model for generating and understanding
  video, audio, images, and actions within one unified architecture."
- "FLUX 3 can generate clips up to 20 seconds long in a single generation, in HD (up to
  1 megapixel per frame), FHD (up to 2), QHD/2K (up to 4), or UHD/4K (up to 8). Resolution bands
  are set by total pixels per frame, not aspect ratio. Draft mode is HD only."
- Video pricing, verbatim: "Text or image to video costs $0.06/sec for Draft HD, then $0.17/sec
  for standard HD, $0.29/sec for FHD, $0.40/sec for QHD, or $0.80/sec for UHD. Video to video
  costs $0.12/sec for Draft HD, then $0.41/sec for standard HD, $0.53/sec for FHD, $0.65/sec for
  QHD, or $0.95/sec for UHD. Five seconds of standard text-to-video in HD costs $0.85."
- "Audio is included at no extra charge."
- On scope of the rollout: "FLUX 3 Image, FLUX 3 Action, and the FLUX 3 Dev open-weight backbone
  are separate parts of the rollout."

## Notes

- The Max block is **undated**. Nothing on the page ties it to a launch date; the dates in this
  KB come from fal and Artificial Analysis instead.
- BFL describes FLUX 3 Video as one model whose higher resolution classes (`fhd`/`qhd`/`uhd`) are
  "the same generation finished by the video upsampler at a larger canvas". That is a resolution
  tier inside one endpoint. Max is presented differently — as a separately named model with its
  own fal endpoint — but the page never says what changed.

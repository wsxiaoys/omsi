## Notes and contradictions

- **[dev] is marketed as a variant but is absent from the comparison table.** The "at a glance"
  block and the side-by-side comparison cover [max], [pro], [flex] and [klein] only. A reader
  using this page to choose a model gets one sentence about [dev] and no comparison row.
- The capability bullets ("32K text input tokens", "Sub-10-second generation speeds", "4MP",
  "JSON-based control") are stated for FLUX.2 as a whole, not per variant. None of them is
  attributed to [dev] specifically, and "sub-10-second" is unqualified by hardware or endpoint —
  which matters for [dev], where latency depends entirely on the hardware the user runs.
- The page states no parameter count and no licence for [dev]; the "Open Weights" link is the
  only pointer to the weights.

## Gaps

- No [dev]-specific pricing, VRAM, hardware or licence information on this page.
- The destination of the "Open Weights" button was not captured.
- Publication date is not stated, so it cannot be established from this page whether the [dev]
  variant line predates the 2025-11-25 launch blog.

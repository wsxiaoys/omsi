The licence covers weights, parameters and inference code. Note the text says the company "may
also make available certain elements ... under a separate license, such as the inference code",
so parts of the release can carry different terms (the FLUX.2 autoencoder is Apache 2.0 per the
[GitHub README](../../github.com/flux2-dev-readme/2026-10-26.md)).

## Notes

- The licence is not FLUX.2 [dev]-specific. It is a family template whose "Last Revised" date
  (2025-11-25) coincides with the FLUX.2 launch, so a reader cannot tell from the licence alone
  which revision applied before that date.
- "Commercial use of hosted [dev] endpoints" (fal, Replicate) is a host-side claim; this licence
  governs weights you run yourself. The two are recorded separately on the model page because
  they answer different questions.

## Gaps

- No price, royalty rate or turnaround is published for the commercial self-host licence.
- Whether fine-tuning a [dev] derivative and distributing it commercially is covered by the
  self-hosted commercial terms, rather than this licence, is not stated here.
- No capture was made of the separate Self-Hosted Commercial License Terms
  (https://bfl.ai/legal/self-hosted-commercial-license-terms) referenced by the model card's

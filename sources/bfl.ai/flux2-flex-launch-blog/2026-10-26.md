## What this establishes

- What [flex] actually changes, in BFL's own words: exposed **steps** and **guidance scale**
  ("full control over quality, prompt adherence and speed"). Nothing in the post claims a
  different architecture, a different base checkpoint, or better fidelity than [pro] — the
  differentiator is control plus typography/detail specialisation.
- BFL ties the steps parameter to **typography accuracy** and to **image detail**, i.e. the
  control surface and the specialisation are presented as connected: fewer steps cost
  typographic accuracy.
- The post names four launch-time members ([pro], [flex], [dev], [klein]); [max] followed on
  2025-12-16 per the release notes. It treats [flex] as a family member in its own right
  ("FLUX.2 [flex]: ... This model excels at ..."), not as a preset of [pro].
- Architecture is described only at family level (Mistral-3 24B VLM + rectified flow
  transformer), so no [flex]-specific parameter count exists.
- Open weights are attributed to [dev] and [klein] only; the post never offers [flex] weights.

## Known gaps

- No statement of whether [flex] shares [pro]'s weights, is a fine-tune, or is a separately
  trained checkpoint.
- No benchmark numbers, no latency figures, no pricing in this post.
- The "overview graph" of performance vs. control is an image; its axes and placements were not
  machine-readable in this capture.

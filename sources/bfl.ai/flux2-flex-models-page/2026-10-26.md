## What this establishes

- BFL's consumer-facing one-liner for [flex] is "Maximum precision. Text that reads. Complete
  creative control." — again positioning on typography and control, not on overall fidelity.
- The page sells [flex] alongside [pro] in the hero ("FLUX.2 [pro] | [flex]"), while the
  "Four variants" block lists [max], [pro], [flex], [dev] and omits [klein].
- The "10 images", "4MP", "32K text input tokens" and "sub-10-second" figures are family-level
  marketing statements on this page. They are not attributed to [flex] alone and no source or
  measurement is given; treat them as claims, not findings.

## Known gaps

- No pricing, no parameter documentation, no per-variant speed numbers.
- "Sub-10-second generation speeds" does not say at which resolution, step count, or endpoint —
  the step count matters most for [flex], so this figure cannot be applied to it.
- No statement on weights or licensing for [flex] on this page.
- There is no per-model product page for [flex]: `https://bfl.ai/sitemap.xml` (read 2026-10-26)
  lists `/models/flux-2` (this family page), `/models/flux-2-max` and `/models/flux-2-klein`, and
  no `flux-2-flex` path.

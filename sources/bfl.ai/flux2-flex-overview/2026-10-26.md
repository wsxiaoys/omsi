## What this establishes

- BFL lists [flex] as its own entry in the family comparison, with its own "Best for", its own
  control surface, and no grounding search. It is not described as a mode of [pro].
- **[flex] has no pinned snapshot and no preview endpoint.** The endpoint table names a fixed
  and a preview endpoint for [pro] and for [klein] 9B, and none for [flex] — so `flux-2-flex`
  is the only addressable [flex] target, and BFL publishes no reproducibility guarantee for it.
  That matters because the same endpoint later received a performance update (see
  [release-notes record](../flux2-flex-release-notes/2026-10-26.md)).
- Multi-reference is documented at "Up to 8 (API), 10 (playground)" for [flex], the same as
  [pro] and [max].

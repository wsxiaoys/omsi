## November 25, 2025 — FLUX.2 [pro] and [flex] Launch

Section heading (verbatim): "Introducing the next generation of FLUX models — **FLUX.2 [pro]**
and **FLUX.2 [flex]**."

FLUX.2 [flex] section, subtitle "Fine-grained control for precise results." (verbatim bullets):

> * **Specialized for typography** — Best-in-class text rendering
> * **Adjustable parameters** — Control inference steps (up to 50) and guidance scale (1.5–10)
> * **Detail preservation** — Maintains small details in complex scenes
> * **Production workflows** — Ideal for designs requiring exact specifications

Shared "Key Capabilities" for both models (verbatim):

> * **Exact color control** — Specify brand colors via hex codes with precision matching
> * **Structured prompting** — JSON-style prompts for automated workflows
> * **Multi-reference editing** — Character consistency, style transfer, and pose guidance
> * **Up to 4MP output** — High-resolution generation at any aspect ratio

## January 29, 2026 — FLUX.2 [flex] Performance Update

Entire entry (verbatim):

## May 11, 2026 — FLUX MCP Server (flex listed among supported models)

> * **All FLUX.2 models** — Pro, Max, Klein 4B/9B, and Flex, plus the FLUX Tools

The MCP integration page separately names `flux2_flex` as the "best for typography" choice on the
MCP `generate_image` tool (see
[bfl.ai/flux2-flex-mcp-reference/2026-10-26.md](../flux2-flex-mcp-reference/2026-10-26.md)).

## What this establishes

- Launch date is **2025-11-25**, shared with [pro].
- The published control range is steps ≤ 50 and guidance 1.5–10, matching the API schema.
- The single `flux-2-flex` endpoint received a **speed change on 2026-01-29** with no endpoint
  fork: the entry does not announce a new endpoint, a preview endpoint, or a pinned snapshot.
  Combined with the overview page's endpoint table, which lists fixed and preview endpoints only
  for [pro] and [klein] 9B, this means [flex] outputs are not reproducible across that date by
  choosing an endpoint.
- "up to 3x faster" and "same quality" are BFL's own claims; no latency numbers, benchmark
  names, or methodology are published.

## Known gaps

- The entry does not say which day's weights the pre-update endpoint served, whether partner
  hosts picked the update up, or whether the update is retroactive.
- No [flex] entries appear between 2026-01-29 and the capture date other than the MCP listing.

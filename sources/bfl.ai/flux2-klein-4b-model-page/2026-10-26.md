## Contradiction with the launch post and model card

This page gives the 4B a **VRAM figure of 8.4 GB** (4B Base 9.2 GB), while the launch post and both
Hugging Face model cards say the 4B "fits in **~13GB VRAM**"
([blog](../flux2-klein-4b-blog/2026-10-26.md),
[Hugging Face](../../huggingface.co/flux-2-klein-4b/2026-10-26.md)). The two numbers are not
reconciled anywhere. A plausible reading — unconfirmed — is that 8.4 GB is the transformer's bf16
weights while ~13 GB is end-to-end inference including the text encoder, but BFL does not say so.
Do not treat either figure as a measured floor.

## What this establishes

- BFL publishes per-variant latency on two named GPUs, so the 4B and 4B Base are distinguishable by
  speed as well as by distillation: ~0.3 s vs ~3 s on GB200 (10x), ~1.2 s vs ~17 s on RTX 5090.
- The 4B is faster than the 9B on both GPUs (~0.3 vs ~0.5 s GB200; ~1.2 vs ~2 s RTX 5090) and uses
  less VRAM (8.4 vs 19.6 GB on this page's accounting).
- License split confirmed: 4B/4B Base Apache 2.0; 9B/9B Base FLUX NCL.

## Gaps

- No resolution, step count, or batch conditions for the latency numbers; "GB200" is given as the
  hardware, and the blog says "in bf16".
- No parameter counts beyond the "4B"/"9B" labels, and no statement of whether the label covers the
  text encoder.
- "30%+ faster than any competing model" names no competitor and no measurement.

## What this establishes

- BFL's own multi-reference ceiling for **[klein] is 4** images — lower than [pro]/[flex]/[max]
  (8 via API, 10 in playground) and lower than [dev]'s recommended 6.
- Both API-served klein models are **4-step distilled**; the Base variants are undistilled and are
  **not on the public API** — they are weights-only.
- 4B and 9B share the same step count and "sub-second" description; the differentiators BFL states
  are license and "balanced quality and speed" vs "high volume".
- **[klein] has no prompt upsampling**, unlike [pro], where upsampling is on by default.
- There is a preview/non-preview endpoint pair for [pro] and for [klein] 9B, but **no 4B pair is
  listed** — the docs neither name a `flux-2-klein-4b-preview` nor say the 4B endpoint is frozen.

## January 15, 2026 — "FLUX.2 [klein] Launch" (verbatim)

> We're excited to announce **FLUX.2 [klein]** — our fastest model family with **sub-second
> inference**.

> ### Key Features
> * **Sub-second generation** — Real-time image generation for interactive applications
> * **Two variants** — 4B from ($0.014 / image) and 9B from ($0.015 / image) models
> * **Runs on consumer hardware** — As little as 13GB VRAM required
> * **Open weights available** — 4B under Apache 2.0, 9B under FLUX Non-Commercial License

> ### API Endpoints
> * `flux-2-klein-4b` — Fastest, ideal for high-volume and local deployment
> * `flux-2-klein-9b` — Balanced quality and speed with 8B Qwen3 text embedder

This is the **first-party launch date: 2026-01-15**, which is on or after the roster's 2025-10-26
cutoff.

## April 23, 2026 — "FLUX.2 LoRA Inference — Public Beta" (verbatim, abridged)

> Run your own trained LoRAs on FLUX.2 [klein] through the BFL API. Train with the tools of your
> choice (AI-Toolkit, Diffusers, …), upload once in the Dashboard, then serve inference through a
> managed `-finetuned` endpoint — no GPUs to provision.

> * **Six endpoints** — Covering FLUX.2 [klein] 4B, 9B, and Base variants in FP8, plus a BF16 option
> * **Base-endpoint pricing during beta** — Fine-tuned requests are billed at the same rate as the
>   underlying base endpoint

> `POST /v1/{base_model}-finetuned`, e.g. `/v1/flux-2-klein-9b-kv-finetuned`

So the 4B has a first-party LoRA-serving path, and it is billed at the base endpoint's rate while in
beta.

## October 15, 2026 — "FLUX 3 Image Klein" (verbatim, abridged)

> FLUX 3 Image Klein is a 5.6B parameter version of FLUX 3 Image with open weights. It supports the
> same features: text to image, image editing, multi reference, and bounding boxes.

> * **Two variants:** Klein (Distilled) runs 16 steps at CFG 1 for fast inference. Klein Base runs
>   50 steps at CFG 3.5 and is the model for fine-tuning and LoRA training
> * **Open weights:** both variants are on Hugging Face under the FLUX Kommunity License and run on
>   consumer GPUs, down to an RTX 3090
> * **Up to 4K output:** `resolution` from `768sq` to `4k`, with up to ten reference images per
>   request

> | `resolution` | Price |
> | 768sq | $0.008 |
> | 1k | $0.009 |
> | 1.5k | $0.011 |
> | 2k | $0.014 |
> | 4k | $0.036 |

## What this establishes

- FLUX.2 [klein] launched **2026-01-15** with both 4B and 9B; the roster's one-year filter passes.
- The launch price for the 4B ("from $0.014 / image") is the same first-MP rate the pricing data
  still reports on 2026-10-26, i.e. no price change captured in either direction.
- A **later, smaller-and-cheaper "Klein" exists in the FLUX 3 generation** (FLUX 3 Image Klein,
  5.6B, 16-step distilled, $0.009 at 1k, up to 4K and 10 references), released 2026-10-15 under the
  FLUX Kommunity License rather than Apache 2.0. Anyone choosing FLUX.2 [klein] 4B today is choosing
  it against a newer sibling with a different license, not against an empty field.
- No entry in this changelog deprecates `flux-2-klein-4b` or announces a 4B preview endpoint.

## Gaps

- The changelog is a single rolling page; there is no per-entry permalink captured, so these quotes
  are pinned only by this capture date and the entry dates.
- The "$0.014 / image" and "$0.015 / image" figures are stated per image without stating the
  resolution they assume. The pricing cards imply 1 MP.
- No FLUX 3 Image Klein quality or benchmark claims are recorded here; its page is out of scope for
  this record.

## What this establishes

- BFL's **marketing** page positions [klein] as the speed/efficiency tier for "rapid iteration
  and prototyping", a weaker claim than the docs' "state-of-the-art quality" phrasing in the
  model card.
- The page is **internally inconsistent about family membership**: the "Four variants" block
  omits [klein] while the comparison block includes it. That is a marketing-page structure
  artefact, not evidence that [klein] belongs to a different family; the docs and release notes
  are unambiguous that it is FLUX.2.
- The hero's "4MP photorealistic output" belongs to the [pro]/[flex] framing at the top of the
  page; it is **not** a [klein]-specific resolution claim and should not be read as one.

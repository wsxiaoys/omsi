## Basis (verbatim)

> **Simple credit-based pricing:** 1 credit = $0.01 USD • Pay per image, or per second for
> video • Same price for API and Playground

> FLUX.2 uses **megapixel-based pricing** — cost scales with output resolution.

> **Klein pricing explained:** The first megapixel costs a flat rate, then each additional MP
> adds to the total. For example, a 2MP image with Klein 4B costs [firstMpPrice] + [mpPrice] =
> [firstMpPrice at 2MP].

## Fine-tuned endpoints (verbatim)

> During public beta, fine-tuned FLUX.2 endpoints are billed at the **same rate as their base
> endpoints**. For example, a request to `flux-2-klein-9b-kv-finetuned` costs the same as the
> equivalent `flux-2-klein-9b` request at the same resolution.

> Pricing for fine-tuned endpoints may change once out of public beta.

## What this establishes

- BFL bills [klein] **per megapixel**, with a first-megapixel rate plus a per-additional-MP
  rate — the same structure as [pro]. It is not a flat per-image price.
- **Text-to-image and image editing are separately listed columns** for [klein], i.e. the
  first-party price for an edit is a distinct (higher) rate, as it is for [pro].
- **LoRA inference is billed at the base endpoint rate** while in public beta.
- [dev] is explicitly "Local only" with "no hosted API", which is a contrast worth keeping in
  mind: [klein] is the family's *hosted plus open-weights* member, [dev] is weights-only.

## Gaps and contradiction to preserve

- **The dollar values are client-rendered and were not captured.** The page's numeric cells
  render through a client-side `Pricing` component, so both the HTML and the markdown capture
  came back with the figures missing. The only first-party numbers captured for [klein] are the
  release-note figures: "4B from ($0.014 / image) and 9B from ($0.015 / image)"
  (`../../bfl.ai/flux2-klein-9b-release-notes/2026-10-26.md`).
- Those release-note figures are phrased **per image**, while this page describes a
  **per-megapixel** schedule. For an image above 1MP the two cannot both be literal, and no
  captured page reconciles them. Host prices differ as well (fal $0.006/MP, Replicate
  $0.015/output MP) — see the host records.
- The page points at a calculator (https://bfl.ai/pricing) for exact costs; that calculator was
  not captured.

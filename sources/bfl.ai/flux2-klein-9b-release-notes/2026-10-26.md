## January 15, 2026 — FLUX.2 [klein] Launch (verbatim)

> We're excited to announce **FLUX.2 [klein]** — our fastest model family with **sub-second
> inference**.
>
> ### Key Features
>
> * **Sub-second generation** — Real-time image generation for interactive applications
> * **Two variants** — 4B from ($0.014 / image) and 9B from ($0.015 / image) models
> * **Runs on consumer hardware** — As little as 13GB VRAM required
> * **Open weights available** — 4B under Apache 2.0, 9B under FLUX Non-Commercial License
>
> ### API Endpoints
>
> * `flux-2-klein-4b` — Fastest, ideal for high-volume and local deployment
> * `flux-2-klein-9b` — Balanced quality and speed with 8B Qwen3 text embedder
>
> Download open weights from [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs) or get
> started with the [API documentation](https://docs.bfl.ml/flux_2/flux2_text_to_image.md).

## April 23, 2026 — FLUX.2 LoRA Inference, Public Beta (verbatim, klein-relevant parts)

> Run your own trained LoRAs on FLUX.2 [klein] through the BFL API. Train with the tools of
> your choice (AI-Toolkit, Diffusers, …), upload once in the Dashboard, then serve inference
> through a managed `-finetuned` endpoint — no GPUs to provision. This is a new self-serve
> path, separate from the FLUX.1-era Finetuning API deprecated in October 2025.
>
> * **Six endpoints** — Covering FLUX.2 [klein] 4B, 9B, and Base variants in FP8, plus a BF16
>   option
> * **Base-endpoint pricing during beta** — Fine-tuned requests are billed at the same rate as
>   the underlying base endpoint
>
> ### Endpoints
>
> `POST /v1/{base_model}-finetuned`, e.g. `/v1/flux-2-klein-9b-kv-finetuned`

## What this establishes

- **Launch date: 2026-01-15**, inside the curation window (on or after 2025-10-26).
- FLUX.2 [klein] is a **two-model family** (4B and 9B) with **different licences**: Apache 2.0
  for 4B, FLUX Non-Commercial License for 9B. The 9B is therefore open-weights but not
  open-licence for commercial self-hosting.
- The "13GB VRAM" figure is stated for the family in one line; BFL's family overview attaches
  it specifically to the 4B (see `../../bfl.ai/flux2-klein-9b-overview/2026-10-26.md`). It is
  not a 9B figure, and the 9B model card gives ~29GB.
- BFL distinguishes the 9B by its **8B Qwen3 text embedder**; the "9B" label counts the flow
  model, not the text encoder.
- LoRA inference is a **first-party** path (`flux-2-klein-9b-kv-finetuned`) as well as a
  host-side one on fal; it exists only for [klein], not for [pro]/[max]/[flex].

## Gaps

- No benchmark figures, latency measurements, or resolution ceiling for [klein] appear in
  these entries.
- The pricing figures ("$0.014 / image", "$0.015 / image") are stated per image; the separate
  pricing page describes a first-megapixel + additional-megapixel schedule without rendering
  the numbers (see `../../bfl.ai/flux2-klein-9b-pricing/2026-10-26.md`). Which basis applies
  to a non-1MP image is not stated in the release notes.
- The page's FLUX 3 Image Klein entry (2026-10-01, 5.6B, open weights under the FLUX Kommunity
  License) is a later, separate family and is not evidence about FLUX.2 [klein].

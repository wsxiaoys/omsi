# BFL: "FLUX 3 — Real World Models" announcement

- Origin: Black Forest Labs, "FLUX 3 - Real World Models: Towards Multimodal Flow Models as the
  Backbone of Visual Intelligence", https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor research announcement)
- Publication date: July 23, 2026 (stated on page: "July 23, 2026", "5 min read")
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: FLUX 3 (family); the image section is the pre-release description of FLUX 3 Image

## Captured content

> FLUX 3 is our new multimodal foundation model. It jointly learns from images, videos, and audio
> within a unified architecture …

Architecture (verbatim):

> FLUX 3 builds on **Self-Flow,** our approach for efficiently aligning multimodal generation and
> understanding within the same underlying architecture. Based on this approach, we significantly
> scaled up compute and data resources to train FLUX 3 across video, images, and audio at the same
> time.

Image section (verbatim):

> FLUX 3 can synthesize and edit images in a wide variety of styles, aspect ratios, and
> resolutions. In preliminary evaluations conducted during midtraining, FLUX 3 already shows a
> significant improvement over earlier versions of FLUX: its ability to handle complex prompts and
> text generation has improved significantly. The model produces a wide range of output styles …
> and is able to render high-accuracy text in multiple languages.

> As with video evaluations, these are preliminary results, and we expect further improvements
> before release. We will open up an early access phase for FLUX 3 Image in the following weeks.

Launch plan (verbatim), the clearest first-party statement of what each tier is:

> Over the next few weeks and months, we will make the following capabilities available … All
> capabilities are built from the same underlying multimodal flow matching model.
>
> - Video and audio generation and editing through APIs and private weight access. ("FLUX 3 Video")
> - Action prediction through selected research and commercial partners, beginning with mimic
>   robotics ("FLUX-mimic and FLUX 3 Action")
> - Image synthesis and editing through APIs and private weight access. ("FLUX 3 Image")
> - Open-weight access to a multimodal backbone, for content creation (video, audio and image) and
>   action prediction. ("FLUX 3 Dev")

Video claims are quantified and comparative; image claims are not: the video section reports
preference rates against Grok Imagine Video (69%), Kling v3 Pro (60%), Happy Horse v1/v1.1 (59%,
57%), Seedance 2.0 and Gemini Omni Flash (52%), Runway Gen-4.5 (77%), Luma Ray 3.2 (93%), from
10-second 720p text-to-video clips.

## What this establishes

- **FLUX 3 Image is a mode of a single jointly-trained multimodal model, not a standalone image
  model.** BFL says so three ways: the announcement, the launch plan, and the product-page FAQ.
- Weight access for FLUX 3 Image is "private" by design; the open-weight route is a separate
  "FLUX 3 Dev" multimodal backbone and (in practice) FLUX 3 Image Klein.
- The image quality claim is explicitly "preliminary evaluations conducted during midtraining" —
  i.e. pre-release, unquantified, and not a statement about the shipped model.

## Gaps

- No image-side numbers of any kind: no benchmark, no human preference rate, no comparison with
  FLUX.2 [pro]/[max]/[flex].
- "FLUX 3 Dev" is named as a plan; this record does not establish whether it shipped. No such
  first-party model page was found at capture, and the Hugging Face search captured separately
  (`sources/huggingface.co/flux3-image-weights-search/2026-10-26.md`) returns only Klein and Action
  weights.
- The announcement does not use the name "FLUX 3 Max"; the video product page does.

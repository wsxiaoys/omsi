### FAQ (verbatim, selected)

> **What is FLUX 3 Image?** FLUX 3 is Black Forest Labs' multimodal model for video, audio, images
> and actions. FLUX 3 Image is the part that generates and edits images.

> **Does FLUX 3 change the boxes I draw?** No. A prompt upsampler turns your short request into the
> dense caption FLUX 3 was trained on. It may write the caption around your boxes and suggest more
> elements, but every box you drew reaches the model verbatim, with the same id and the same
> coordinates.

> **Can I edit an image I already made?** Yes, and you can make several targeted edits at once.

> **What kinds of images suit bounding boxes?** Compositions with many parts in strict
> relationships. Type set around a photograph, collages and panel grids, editorial spreads, or a
> crowded scene where every face has its place.

## What this establishes

- **Weights are not public for FLUX 3 Image.** The strongest available route is a commercial
  weights licence by negotiation ("Talk to us about commercial weights"). Nothing on this page
  offers a download.
- BFL's own FAQ frames FLUX 3 Image as a capability of one multimodal model, not as a
  separately-trained image model.
- Prompt upsampling is unconditional on the first-party route (a "prompt upsampler turns your short
  request into the dense caption"), and boxes survive it verbatim — a specific, checkable claim.
- FLUX 3 Max is presented as a *video* tier ("10-second video in 12 seconds", "Try FLUX 3 Max on
  fal") and has no first-party model page. It is therefore not an image sibling of this page.

## Gaps

- Every quality statement here is marketing; no benchmark, no side-by-side against FLUX.2, no
  numbers of any kind.
- The commercial weights licence terms (price, redistribution, output rights) are not published.
- "16.8 MP" is a demo label; the API reference gives 16,822,272 px as the hard cap.
- No statement about how upsampling interacts with `grounding`, or whether boxes are honoured
  identically through third-party hosts.

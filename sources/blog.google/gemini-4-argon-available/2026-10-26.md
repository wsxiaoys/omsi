## Availability (verbatim)

> Gemini 4 Argon is now globally available today for developers and enterprises via the API
> in AI Studio and Gemini platform, for Google AI Ultra subscribers in Google Antigravity,
> and Google AI Ultra and Pro subscribers in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search.

> For cybersecurity defenders: Apply to join the Fairwind Program to get access to Argon
> without cyber guardrails on a rolling basis.

> Gemini 4 Argon is just the beginning. We're looking forward to bringing you the rest of the
> Gemini 4 family.

## Price (verbatim)

> The model launches today at an introductory price of $2 per million input tokens and $10
> per million output tokens (with cached input tokens at 95% off).

The footnote again states the post-introductory price of $4 input / $20 output per 1M tokens
and does not date the change.

## Safety and security claims (vendor-internal)

- "external bio red teaming could only unlock less than 1% of bio sub-tasks we test for";
  violations on an adversarial set "reduced nearly 2-fold".
- Shortcut attempts on coding benchmarks "dropped to just 2%".
- Gray Swan Indirect Prompt Injection: "cutting attack success across 15 adaptive attempts to
  0.7%".
- An automated misalignment monitor reads step-by-step reasoning and actions and blocks
  unintended workarounds.

## Internal deployment anecdotes (marketing; unverifiable)

Chrome refactor across "hundreds of files"; ADK Python session-prep time cut 40% and cold
imports 21%; xTB "up to 2.3x faster" at predicting molecular structures; 2.85x vLLM inference
speedup on Gemma 4 E2B; "agentic code submissions by more than 35%" with "code rollbacks by
nearly a third". These are Google-internal productivity claims with no published protocol.

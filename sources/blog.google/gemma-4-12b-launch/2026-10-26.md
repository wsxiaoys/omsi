# blog.google: "Introducing Gemma 4 12B" (second Gemma 4 release)

- Origin: Google, "Introducing Gemma 4 12B: a unified, encoder-free multimodal model",
  The Keyword, Olivier Lacombe and Gus Martins (Product Management, Google DeepMind),
  <https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemma-4-12B/>
- Evidence kind: official announcement (vendor marketing)
- Publication date: **Jun 03, 2026** (as printed on the page)
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Gemma 4 12B (Unified)

## Captured content

### Positioning (verbatim)

> Today, we are introducing Gemma 4 12B, our latest model designed to bring agentic
> multimodal intelligence directly to laptops. Bridging the gap between our edge-friendly E4B
> and our more advanced 26B Mixture of Experts (MoE), Gemma 4 12B packages powerful
> capabilities inside a reduced memory footprint. It is also our first mid-sized model to
> feature native audio inputs.

> Thanks to the developer community, Gemma 4 models have now crossed 150 million downloads.

### Four claims about the 12B (verbatim bullets)

- "Novel unified architecture: No multimodal encoders. The vision and audio inputs flow
  directly into the LLM backbone."
- "Advanced reasoning: Benchmark performance nearing our 26B model, unlocking powerful
  multi-step reasoning and agentic workflows."
- "Laptop ready: Small enough to run locally with just 16GB of VRAM or unified memory."
- "Open and accessible: Released under an Apache 2.0 license with support across the
  developer ecosystem."
- "Drafter-ready: Gemma 4 12B comes equipped with Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) drafters to
  reduce latency."

### Encoder-free detail (verbatim)

> Vision: We replaced Gemma 4's vision encoder with a lightweight embedding module consisting
> of a single matrix multiplication, positional embedding and normalizations.

> Audio: We simplified audio processing even further. We removed the audio encoder entirely
> and projected the raw audio signal into the same dimensional space as text tokens.

### Distribution (verbatim, abridged)

Local: "LM Studio, Ollama, Google AI Edge Gallery App, the Google AI Edge Eloquent app and the
LiteRT-LM CLI". Weights: "Hugging Face and Kaggle". Tools: "Hugging Face Transformers,
llama.cpp, MLX, SGLang, and vLLM, or fine-tune with efficiency using Unsloth". Production:
"Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform Model Garden, Cloud Run and GKE".

## Summary

The 12B is a second, separately announced release (2026-06-03), not part of the 2026-04-02
launch. It is the only encoder-free member of the family and the only mid-size model with
native audio input; Google states a 16GB VRAM floor. No benchmark numbers appear in the blog
itself — "nearing our 26B model" is qualitative.

## Gaps

- "Benchmark performance nearing our 26B model" is unquantified here; the numbers are only in
  the model-card table.
- No pricing, no context length, no per-token availability statement.
- "150 million downloads" is cumulative across the family and not independently verifiable.

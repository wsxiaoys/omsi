# blog.google: "Gemma 4: Byte for byte, the most capable open models" (launch)

- Origin: Google, "Gemma 4: Byte for byte, the most capable open models", The Keyword,
  Clement Farabet (VP of Research, Google DeepMind) and Olivier Lacombe (Director Product
  Management, Google DeepMind),
  <https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemma-4/>
- Evidence kind: official announcement (vendor marketing; claims separated below)
- Publication date: **Apr 02, 2026** (as printed on the page)
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Gemma 4 E2B, E4B, 26B MoE (A4B), 31B Dense

## Captured content

### Announcement and licensing (verbatim)

> Today, we are introducing Gemma 4 — our most intelligent open models to date. Purpose-built
> for advanced reasoning and agentic workflows, Gemma 4 delivers an unprecedented level of
> intelligence-per-parameter.

> ... Gemma 4 is our answer: breakthrough capabilities made widely accessible under an
> Apache 2.0 license .

> You gave us feedback, and we listened. ... That's why Gemma 4 is released under a
> commercially permissive Apache 2.0 license.

### Sizes announced on launch day (verbatim)

> We are releasing Gemma 4 in four versatile sizes: Effective 2B (E2B ), Effective 4B (E4B) ,
> 26B Mixture of Experts (MoE) and 31B Dense .

No 12B is mentioned. The 12B was announced separately on 2026-06-03 (separate record).

### Leaderboard claims (verbatim, vendor claim)

> Our larger models deliver state-of-the-art performance for their sizes, with the 31B model
> currently ranking as the #3 open model in the world on the industry-standard Arena AI text
> leaderboard , and the 26B model securing the #6 spot. There, Gemma 4 outcompetes models 20x
> its size.

Caption on the accompanying chart: "Open model performance vs size on Arena.ai 's chat arena
as of 4/1." These are Google's own claims about a third-party arena, not an independent
capture.

### Capability and hardware claims (verbatim, abridged)

- "Built from the same world-class research and technology as Gemini 3".
- "The edge models feature a 128K context window, while the larger models offer up to 256K".
- "Natively trained on over 140 languages".
- "our unquantized bfloat16 weights fit efficiently on a single 80GB NVIDIA H100 GPU".
- "26B Mixture of Experts (MoE) focus on latency, activating only 3.8 billion of its total
  parameters during inference".
- E2B/E4B "run completely offline with near-zero latency across edge devices like phones,
  Raspberry Pi, and NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano".
- "developers have downloaded Gemma over 400 million times, building a vibrant Gemmaverse of
  more than 100,000 variants."

### Distribution claims (verbatim, abridged)

Day-one tool support named: "Hugging Face (Transformers, TRL, Transformers.js, Candle),
LiteRT-LM, vLLM, llama.cpp, MLX, Ollama, NVIDIA NIM and NeMo, LM Studio, Unsloth, SGLang,
Cactus, Baseten, Docker, MaxText, Tunix, Keras". Weights from "Hugging Face, Kaggle or
Ollama". Hosted: "Google AI Studio (31B and 26B MoE)" and "Google AI Edge Gallery (E4B and
E2B)".

## Summary

The launch announcement, dated 2026-04-02, for four sizes under Apache 2.0. It is the source
of the "#3 open model on Arena AI" claim and of the 80GB-H100 bfloat16 fit claim. It
contradicts the release-history page by two days (that page says the release was 2026-03-31).

## Gaps

- No benchmark table on the blog; it defers to the model card ("See additional benchmarks in
  our model card").
- The Arena AI rank is asserted without a snapshot, query, or date beyond "as of 4/1".
- No pricing for any hosted route.
- The two-day discrepancy against the release-history page is not acknowledged.

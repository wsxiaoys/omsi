# Google blog: "Build with Nano Banana 2, our best image generation and editing model"

- Origin: Google, "Build with Nano Banana 2, our best image generation and editing model",
  Alisa Fortin (Product Manager, Google DeepMind) and Bea Alessio (Group Product Manager,
  Google DeepMind), https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/build-with-nano-banana-2/
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party launch announcement / marketing)
- Publication date: **February 26, 2026** (date printed on the post)
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant model: Nano Banana 2 = Gemini 3.1 Flash Image

## Captured content

### Identity and date (verbatim)

> Today, we're introducing Nano Banana 2 (Gemini 3.1 Flash Image), which brings high-fidelity
> image generation and faster, advanced editing to the new Flash model. It allows you to
> deploy sophisticated visual creation at scale with an amazing price-performance ratio.

> Our latest update to Nano Banana brings higher fidelity, faster advanced editing, with
> improved world knowledge.

Availability stated at launch (verbatim, abridged): "The model is available now via the
Gemini API in Google AI Studio, for enterprise deployment on Vertex AI. It is available for
use in Google Antigravity and Firebase." A paid API key is required.

### Capability claims (verbatim bullets)

> - **Native aspect ratios:** Seamlessly generate or edit images to fit your project
>   requirement, with native support for all existing aspect ratios we have now added 4:1,
>   1:4, 8:1 and 1:8.
> - **New 512px resolution:** Optimize for efficiency. Joining our 1K, 2K and 4K offerings,
>   this new resolution tier minimizes latency for rapid-fire iterations and heavy-duty
>   pipelines.
> - **Improved instruction following:** The model adheres much more strictly to complex,
>   multi-layered developer prompts, helping to ensure the image your application requests is
>   what is generated.
> - **Configurable thinking levels:** You now have more control over the model's reasoning.
>   Adjust thinking levels (Minimal (default) vs. High/Dynamic) to allow the model to reason
>   through complex prompts before rendering, significantly improving output quality and
>   prompt adherence.

Narrative claims, all unquantified: "Improved world knowledge" via web search and image
grounding ("Window Seat" demo); "Advanced text rendering and localization", including
in-image translation; "speed and visual fidelity, with vibrant lighting, richer textures and
sharper details"; character/identity maintenance across scenes ("Pet Passport" demo).

## Known gaps

- Every quality claim on this post is unquantified and unsourced: no benchmark, dataset,
  sample size, or human-preference study. "Significantly improving output quality" has no
  measured delta and no harness.
- The post names thinking levels as "Minimal (default) vs. High/Dynamic"; the developer docs
  later list exactly two levels for this model, `minimal` and `high`. "Dynamic" appears in the
  marketing copy only and is not a documented parameter value on the docs page captured here.
- The post does not state a GA date, a shutdown date, or a price.
- The "what developers are saying" partner section renders as a heading with no visible quotes
  in the captured text; it could not be read and is not summarised here.
- Marketing framing ("our best image generation and editing model") was true on 2026-02-26; it
  is superseded by Nano Banana 2.1 (2026-10-06) and is not a current claim.

## What this establishes

- BRIA's marketing site uses **"Fibo"** as the family name and, on `/models`, labels the newest
  foundation model **"Fibo - Bria 4.0"**. That "Bria 4.0" label appears only on the catalogue card;
  the `/fibo` page it links to still describes the 8B first-generation FIBO (JSON-native T2I, 100M+
  training images, VLM expansion). The site never writes "Fibo 2".
- So the vendor has three overlapping labels in circulation for the same family line: "Fibo" (family
  and, on the site, the 1.x product page), "Bria 4.0" (catalogue card), and "Fibo 2" (developer
  docs, release notes, Hugging Face). Which of them the `/models` card refers to cannot be settled
  from the captured text.
- Positioning is consistent across pages: structured JSON/VGL control, licensed training data, C2PA
  provenance, commercial indemnification, and a smaller on-prem tier (Fibo Lite).
- "8B Parameters" is a first-party figure for the `/fibo` page's model. It is not stated to be
  Fibo 2's parameter count (the Fibo 2 weights report 8.13B in the Hugging Face metadata; see the
  huggingface.co record).

## Gaps

- No page-level date, no changelog, and no version history on any of the three pages.
- The `/models` page's model list is rendered as cards; only the two cards quoted above produced
  readable text in the capture, so the full catalogue could not be enumerated.
- No benchmark tables on these pages, only downloadable reports that were not captured.

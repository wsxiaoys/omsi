## What this establishes

- First-party list price for generation and for editing is **$0.032 per image**, with the
  structured-prompt translation step priced separately at **$0.02 per call** when it is billed
  separately. A prompt-based generation that runs the bridge is therefore a $0.032 image plus,
  potentially, a $0.02 translation call — the page does not say whether `/v2/image/generate`
  already includes the bridge call in the $0.032.
- Fibo Lite is about 10% cheaper per image ($0.0288).
- fal charges $0.04 per image for both Fibo 2 endpoints (see the fal record), roughly 25% above
  Bria's direct list price.

## Identity caveat

The price list names the rows **"Fibo"** and **"Fibo Edit"**, not "Fibo 2". Bria's docs say the
`/v2/image/generate` and `/v2/image/edit` endpoints now run Fibo 2 ("Same API, nothing to change in
your code"), and this page has no version column. Read literally, the list does not distinguish
Fibo 2 from the earlier Fibo/Fibo Edit. The 2026-10-19 `lastmod` and the unchanged $0.032 rate
suggest the price carried over rather than being re-versioned — but that is an inference from the
date, not a statement on the page.

## Gaps

- No statement of what counts as a billable image at 4MP versus 1MP; the generate endpoint offers a
  4MP tier and the price list has one flat per-image rate.
- No statement of whether a `structured_prompt` request (bridge bypassed) still costs the $0.02
  structured-prompt call.
- No enterprise or self-hosted pricing; the page defers to "Enterprise" for larger images.
- Commercial licensing terms are referenced but not stated here (see the terms-of-use page, not
  captured; the weights licence is in the huggingface.co record).

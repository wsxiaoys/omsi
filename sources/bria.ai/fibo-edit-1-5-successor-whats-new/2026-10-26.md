```bash
  -H "api_token: $BRIA_API_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
  "images": [ … ],
  "instruction": "luxury fragrance poster. …",
  "aspect_ratio": "1:1",
  "seed": 202
}'
```

## What this record establishes about FIBO-Edit 1.5

- The `/v2/image/edit` contract (endpoint path, `images`/`instruction`/`mask`/`seed` parameters,
  `structured_instruction` return value) predates Fibo 2: Bria says "Same API, nothing to change in
  your code", so 1.5 was served through the same endpoint shape.
- The nine aspect ratios and the four-image cap are carried over from the 1.5 generation; Fibo 2 adds
  12 ratios rather than replacing the set.
- Bria's own successor messaging contains no acknowledgement, deprecation note, or price change for
  FIBO-Edit 1.5. The page does not say whether 1.5 remains served anywhere first-party.

## Gaps

- No release date, no version list, no changelog, no deprecation schedule.
- The Artificial Analysis claim is vendor transcription; the board itself is captured separately.
- The post does not name FIBO-Edit 1.5 anywhere, so it is evidence about succession, not about 1.5's
  own capabilities.

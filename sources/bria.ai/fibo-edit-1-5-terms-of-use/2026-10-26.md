## What this record does and does not establish

- It establishes that the default website grant is **personal, non-commercial**, and that commercial
  rights require a paid subscription or a separate licensing agreement.
- It does **not** contain an output-ownership clause. The only mention of "Outputs" is the
  prohibition on stripping Bria's attribution/watermark metadata. Ownership of generated images is
  therefore not established by the public terms.
- It does not reference the open-weights licences at all; those are stated on the Hugging Face model
  cards (CC BY-NC 4.0 with a separate commercial agreement).

## Gaps

- No effective date, no version number, no archive of prior versions.
- No clause on training use of outputs, no clause on redistribution of weights, and no definition of
  "commercial use rights" as sold on the pricing page.

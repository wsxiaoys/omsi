## Section text (verbatim)

### "Unified orchestration of multiple audio elements with fine-grained timing control"

> Seed Audio 1.0 takes a unified approach, rather than encoding each audio type as a separate task:
> A single acoustic encoder captures various audio elements as parts of one coherent sound scene,
> mapping them into a shared representation.
>
> This way, the model weaves together character tone, ambient atmosphere, and rhythm and timing into
> a single, complete piece — not a set of stitched-together clips.
>
> Seed Audio 1.0 supports prompt-level timing control for character dialogue, with current timing
> precision at 100 ms intervals. It turns a creative idea into a structured timeline, pinpointing
> when each character, line, emotion, and sound effect should enter.

### "Controllable voice identity with long-form stability"

> Seed Audio 1.0 supports zero-shot audio generation. Creators can shape a target voice through text
> description alone, or pair it with a reference sample for finer control — no separate model
> training is required for each individual voice.
>
> For longer content, Seed Audio 1.0 can generate up to two minutes of audio in a single pass and
> supports further continuation, helping a character stay recognizable across longer scenes and
> repeated extensions.

### "Create multilingual audio with natural expression"

> Seed Audio 1.0 supports natural transfer of the same voice across languages — its rhythm, stress,
> pauses, and emotion all follow the expressive styles of the target language. It now covers 20+
> languages, including Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Indonesian, German, French,
> Thai, and Vietnamese.

### Evaluation section (vendor claims, no tables captured)

> In A/B subjective evaluations, Seed Audio 1.0 showed clear preference gains in generating voices
> from text descriptions. It also improved both Availability Rate and High-quality Rate.

> For multi-scene audio generation, we further evaluated use cases including film and television,
> TV shows, short dramas, animation, podcast dialogue, LIVE commerce, online content creation, stage
> plays, and text-to-speech. Results show that the audio Availability Rate exceeds 90% in most
> scenarios.

> Seed Audio 1.0 also delivers strong performance in multilingual audio generation. Results show
> that on Naturalness MOS, most languages scored above 4.0; on Instruction Following MOS for complex
> audio generation tasks, all evaluated languages except Vietnamese scored above 3.5.

### "What comes next" (verbatim)

> Building on Seed Audio 1.0, we will push the boundaries of model capabilities within real creative

## Summary

- Modality: **full-scene audio creation** — speech (multi-character, directed), sound effects,
  ambience, and "other audio elements". The page never claims a music model, and it lists **video
  references, long-form, multitrack, and controllable translation as future work**.
- Controls claimed: prompt-level timing at 100 ms granularity (dialogue only), voice from text
  description and/or a reference sample (zero-shot, no per-voice training), voice-identity
  transfer across languages.
- Length: up to 2 minutes per pass, plus "further continuation".
- Languages: "20+", ten named (zh, en, ja, ko, es, id, de, fr, th, vi).
- Quality: A/B preference and MOS claims, all vendor-run, numbers only in images.

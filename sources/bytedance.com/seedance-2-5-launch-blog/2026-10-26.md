## Launch statement and access (verbatim)

> Today, we are officially launching Seedance 2.5, the new-generation video creation model.

> Building on the unified multimodal audio-video joint-generation architecture of Seedance 2.0,
> Seedance 2.5 centers on foundational generation and reference-based generation, delivering major
> breakthroughs in long-form storytelling, multimodal reference, and editing.

> Today, Seedance 2.5 is rolling out on Jimeng AI, Doubao Pro, and other platforms, with API access
> coming soon via BytePlus ModelArk.

The Chinese version says the API "也将在近期上线火山方舟" (will also go live on Volcengine Ark
shortly). That is a **contradiction with the Volcengine changelog**, which files
`doubao-seedance-2-5-260628` as 新发布 in its 2026-06 section (see the release-announcement record),
i.e. roughly a month before this post. The post dates the consumer-product rollout, not the API.

## The three headline changes (verbatim, condensed)

> **Up to 30 seconds per generation, with multi-round extensions:** Seedance 2.5 can generate
> high-quality, 30-second audio-video clips in a single pass and supports multiple rounds of
> extension. It also improves shot transitions and scene changes for stronger continuity in longer
> videos … users can produce high-quality multi-minute content with a consistent audiovisual
> language, bringing a complete story to life in one take.

> **Fully upgraded multimodal referencing:** Users can now input up to 30 images, 10 video clips, and
> 10 audio clips as reference materials in a single pass. The model also strengthens a range of
> reference capabilities, including clay render, motion, and creative references …

> **More precise and stable editing capabilities:** Seedance 2.5 offers timestamp-level control for
> targeted editing of audio and video content … The model also enhances advanced editing features,
> such as green screen, camera perspective, and reference-based editing, to meet the rigorous demands
> of professional, complex fields like film and advertising.

Note the arithmetic: 30 + 10 + 10 = **50** reference assets, the figure both vendor clouds quote as
the per-request cap.

## Detail the vendor adds beyond the model page

- **Extension is the mechanism for length beyond 30 s.** "Thanks to the model's multi-round extension
  capability, users can smoothly append subsequent shots to existing video outputs … This allows users
  to output videos lasting several minutes at once." One captured R2V prompt is explicitly an
  extension: "Extend the video. Continue from the visuals and subjects in @Video 1 and generate
  another 30-second clip".
- **Clay-render / blockout referencing.** "with clay render referencing, users can build a scene's
  spatial structure, character poses, motion paths, and camera angles using textureless 3D models.
  The model then uses this structure to generate the video, ensuring that the composition and
  blocking of complex shots closely match the creator's expectations." The post also claims the model
  derives physically consistent lighting from the blockout's spatial information.
- **Timestamp control is generation-time *and* edit-time.** "During the generation phase, users can
  use prompts to control the narrative, camera perspective, movement, and overall rhythm for a
  specific time frame … After generation, users can make targeted modifications to characters,
  actions, or plot elements within specific clips".
- **Green-screen editing** replaces the background while keeping the performance, and re-renders the
  subject's physical response (cloth direction, hair, gait, lighting) for the new scene.
- **Image-quality claims.** The post says 2.5 "systematically optimizes details such as object
  textures, skin and eye features, lighting, and color saturation" to reduce the "overly artificial
  look often seen in AI-generated videos", and "minimizes uncontrolled occurrences in subtitles and
  background music". These are unquantified marketing claims.
- **Industry use claims** for education (Doubao Learning app "Doubao Classroom"), industrial
  manufacturing, embodied-intelligence training data, and autonomous-driving long-tail simulation.
  No numbers, customers or evaluations are given.

## Vendor's own stated weaknesses (verbatim)

> we recognize there is still room for improvement, particularly regarding the physical plausibility
> of complex motions and the stability of scenes involving interactions among multiple subjects.

This is the vendor's own list of known gaps and is more specific than most launch copy. Note that the
Seedance 2.0 launch post made a comparable self-criticism, so the presence of a caveat does not by
itself distinguish 2.5 from 2.0.

## What is absent

- No model ID, price, resolution, frame rate, region, latency, rate limit or licence.
- No benchmark table or chart, unlike the Seedance 2.0 launch materials (SeedVideoBench-2.0 radar
  charts) and the 2.0 arXiv report.
- No comparison against Seedance 2.0 on any measured axis; the only 2.0 reference is architectural
  continuity ("Building on the unified multimodal audio-video joint-generation architecture of
  Seedance 2.0").

## Gaps

- "Multi-round extension" has no stated maximum number of rounds; the model page says "extend twice".
- "Several minutes" is not given as a hard limit.
- No statement of what 2.5 changed in the weights versus 2.0 beyond capability descriptions.

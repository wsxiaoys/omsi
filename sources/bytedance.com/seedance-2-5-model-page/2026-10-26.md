## What this page does and does not establish

- It names the model "Seedance 2.5" and positions it as a successor generation to Seedance 2.0
  ("next-generation"), but states no model ID, price, resolution, frame rate, region, release date or
  availability date.
- "white-model control" is the page's term for what the launch post calls 白模参考 ("clay render" /
  blockout reference) — an untextured 3D model supplied as a structural reference.
- "the option to extend twice" is narrower than the launch post's "multiple rounds of extension"
  (see the launch-blog record). The two are not reconciled on this page.

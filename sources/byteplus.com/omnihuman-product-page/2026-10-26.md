## Summary

BytePlus has replaced the OmniHuman 1.5 marketing page with an OmniHuman 2.0 page. 1.5 survives as a
cheaper, slower, shorter-duration tile ($0.12/s, "SOTA Lip Sync") next to 2.0 ($0.07/s, 30–300 s
output). The site navigation still says "Omnihuman 1.5", so the label and the page content no longer
agree.

- Origin: BytePlus (ByteDance) ModelArk documentation, "Pricing",
  <https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/modelark/model-pricing> (document code `model-pricing`)
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor price list)
- Publication date: none stated; living document, capture date 2026-10-26
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Dreamina Seedance 2.0, 2.0 fast, 2.0 mini (Seedance 2.5 and 1.0 rows are also on
  the page and are not transcribed here)
- Capture type: **reconstructed table.** The page renders its tables client-side from an embedded

## Billing basis (verbatim)

> Video pricing is determined by both the token rate and token consumption. … A video generated at a
> higher resolution costs more than one generated at a lower resolution. A video generated with video
> input costs more than one generated without video input.

> You are only charged for successfully generated videos. No fee is charged if generation fails due to
> reasons such as content moderation.

> Estimated video price: Token unit price × token consumption
>
> Estimated token consumption: (Input video duration + output video duration) × output video width ×
> output video height × output video frame rate / 1024

> For the Dreamina Seedance 2.0 series and Dreamina Seedance 2.5, minimum token consumption applies
> when the input includes video: if estimated token consumption is below the minimum, the video is
> billed at the minimum token consumption.

## Estimated price examples — 16:9, 5-second output, no video input (USD)

| Resolution | Dreamina Seedance 2.0 | Seedance 2.0 fast | Seedance 2.0 mini |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 480p | $0.35 per video / $0.07 per second | $0.28 / $0.06 | $0.18 / $0.04 |
| 720p | $0.76 per video / $0.15 per second | $0.60 / $0.12 | $0.38 / $0.08 |
| 1080p | $1.87 per video / $0.37 per second | not supported | not supported |
| 4K | $3.89 per video / $0.78 per second | not supported | not supported |

With video input the page gives ranges rather than single values, because billing uses the larger of
the estimate and a minimum token consumption: e.g. Seedance 2.0 480p $0.39–$0.86 per video (lowest at
2–4 s input, highest at 15 s input), 720p $0.84–$1.86, 1080p $2.06–$4.57, 4K $4.20–$9.33; fast 480p
$0.30–$0.66 and 720p $0.64–$1.43; mini 480p $0.19–$0.42 and 720p $0.41–$0.91.

## Extraction caveat

The pricing tables are not in the page's server-rendered HTML. Values were recovered from the embedded
Slate document payload, where table cells are stored out of order, then assigned to columns using the
page's own header order (the resolution column order on this table is 480p, 4K, 720p, 1080p). Two
independent checks support the mapping:

1. Internal consistency: the fast and mini rows each carry exactly two prices and two "Not supported"
   cells, matching their documented 480p/720p-only support.
2. The USD figures sit at a near-constant ratio to Volcengine's CNY example prices for the same
   configurations (e.g. ¥2.31 → $0.35; ¥4.97 → $0.76; ¥12.39 → $1.87; ¥25.27 → $3.89).

Treat the table as a faithful reconstruction with a small residual risk of column mis-assignment,
not as a verbatim transcription.

## Gaps

- The page's token unit prices (USD per million tokens, split by resolution and by whether video input
  is present) could not be mapped reliably from the payload and are not recorded here. The per-video
  examples above are the usable summary.
- No date on the page, so this is a price as of capture only.
- Seedance 2.0's rate limits are on the Model list page, not here.

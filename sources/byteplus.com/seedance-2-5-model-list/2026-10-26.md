## Region availability (verbatim)

> ModelArk currently supports the following regions:

> Region availability:
>
> - Currently, all the models listed in Model list are supported in the ap-southeast-1 region.
> - The seed-2-0 and seedream-5-0-lite models are also supported in the eu-west-1 region.
> - Base URL by region:
>   - ap-southeast-1: `https://ark.ap-southeast.bytepluses.com/api/v3`
>   - eu-west-1: `https://ark.eu-west.bytepluses.com/api/v3`

Read as a statement about Seedance 2.5: it is served from **`ap-southeast-1` (Johor) only**. The EU
region carries one text model and one image model, no video model — the same conclusion the Seedance
2.0 region record reaches for 2.0. International users outside APAC therefore cross regions, and the
Seedance 2.0 tutorial's warning that requests "may be routed to inference resources in other regions"
applies here too by platform design (not restated on this page).

## Billing basis (verbatim)

> Video pricing is determined by both the token rate and token consumption.

> Estimated video price: Token unit price × token consumption
>
> Estimated token consumption: (Input video duration + output video duration) × output video width ×
> output video height × output video frame rate / 1024

> You are only charged for successfully generated videos. No fee is charged if generation fails due to
> reasons such as content moderation.

> Dreamina Seedance 2.5 generates Draft videos at a fixed 480p resolution. The token consumption and
> token rate are the same as normal 480p videos.

> For the Dreamina Seedance 2.0 series and Dreamina Seedance 2.5, minimum token consumption applies
> when the input includes video: if estimated token consumption is below the minimum, the video is
> billed at the minimum token consumption.

## Extraction caveat

Three independent checks support the mapping:

1. **Internal consistency of shape.** 2.5 and 2.0 rows each carry two "Not supported" cells (4K and
   offline) plus four prices, matching the documented 480p/720p/1080p-only support for 2.5 and the
   480p–4K support for 2.0.
2. **Cross-check against the published examples.** 2.0 480p 16:9 5 s = 48,600 tokens; × $7.00/M =
   $0.340, and the page publishes $0.35. The lower set ($4.30) reproduces the *with-video* examples
   instead, which is how the two tables were told apart.
3. **Ratio to the China price list.** $10.70 × 6.57 ≈ 70 CNY/M and $6.40 × 6.57 ≈ 42 CNY/M, matching
   Volcengine's published unit prices for the same configurations; the 2.0 rows give the same ~6.57
   CNY/USD factor.

Treat the table as a faithful reconstruction with a residual risk of column mis-assignment, not as a
verbatim transcription.

## Cross-platform comparison

The international list is the China list converted at ~6.57 CNY/USD with no regional discount: 2.5 is
priced ~1.5× 2.0 on both platforms at every comparable resolution, and the with-video discount is
0.6× on both. Neither platform offers 4K on 2.5 or offline inference on any Seedance model.

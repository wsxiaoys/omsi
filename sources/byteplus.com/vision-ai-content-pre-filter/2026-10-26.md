- Origin: BytePlus (ByteDance), Vision AI documentation, "Security Trust — Vision AI Content
  Pre-filter", <https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-vision/vision-ai-content-pre-filter>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor safety policy)
- Publication date: none stated; page footer reads "Last updated: December 26, 2025 01:25:54"
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: the whole Vision AI platform, including OmniHuman 1.5 and 2.0
- Capture type: rendered text of a client-side documentation page; policy prose quoted verbatim.

## Scope

This is the only safety document in the Vision AI tree that applies to OmniHuman. It is platform-wide,
not OmniHuman-specific, and it says nothing about likeness, voice cloning, or consent.

## Verbatim

> For enhanced safety performance, BytePlus offers the Content Pre-filter System, a robust protection
> mechanism adaptable for diverse application scenarios. This content filter can detect specific
> categories of potentially risky content in input prompts and output completions, and will implement
> corresponding measures, such as refusing to answer or returning a more benign response.

> Activate API: When users activate an API service, they can control the content filtering function via
> the Content Pre-filter button. To ensure the stability of content security, this feature is enabled
> by default.

> The implementation of Content Pre-filter shall not be construed as a warranty by BytePlus regarding
> the appropriateness, or compliance of AI generated content. The user remains solely responsible for
> the usage of any generated content and shall bear all liability and damages arising from such use.

> Regardless of whether the user enables or disables the Content Pre-filter, they must comply with the
> customer agreement and BytePlus GenAI Acceptable Use Policy.

> Besides filtering risky content, BytePlus also identifies content and/or behavior that may violate
> applicable product terms when using the service. Please note that even if you disable this feature,
> our services still maintain baseline content safety policies.

Refusal surface, verbatim: "when the system identifies risky content, the error 'Algovproxy Risk
Error' will be returned", with the example body:

```json
{ "code": 50510, "data": None, "message": "Algovproxy Risk Error",
  "request_id": "xxxxx", "status": 50510, "time_elapsed": "2.156702557s" }
```

Filtered categories, verbatim list headings: "Minor Safety", "Hate Speech and Hateful Content",
"Nudity, Sexual, and Graphic Content", "Misinformation".

Data handling, verbatim: "we will ... retain logs from the use of the Content Pre-filter feature and
the risk-related content data filtered by it for a limited period. This data is stored on servers in
Malaysia or Singapore belonging to BytePlus or its affiliates."

## Summary

BytePlus applies a default-on input/output content filter to Vision AI APIs, returns `Algovproxy Risk
Error` (code 50510) on refusal, and disclaims responsibility for generated content. Liability for the
output sits with the caller.

## Gaps

- No likeness, deepfake, voice-cloning, or consent clause anywhere in this document. A user
  animating a real person's photo with their cloned voice is not addressed by the captured policy.
- The BytePlus GenAI Acceptable Use Policy is referenced but was not retrievable at capture (the legal
  page renders empty without JavaScript and returned no policy text).
- No threshold, no appeal path, and no statement of whether OmniHuman's image input is filtered at all
  versus only the prompt text.

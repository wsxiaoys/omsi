- Relevant models: Mirage Avatar X as shipped in the Captions app (AI Creator / Prompt to Video)

## Captured content (verbatim, selected)

> **Are these videos available for commercial use?** Yes, all videos made using AI Creator can be used for
> commercial use. Please read more in our Terms and Conditions.

> **What is the minimum and maximum length of the script?** AI Creator scripts are limited to 1 minute and
> your script must be between 10 and 800 characters. Each space and line break counts as one character.

Supported languages (two lists on the page, differing): Arabic, Chinese (simplified), Chinese (traditional),
Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hebrew, Hindi, Hinglish, Indonesian, Italian,
Japanese, Korean, Malay, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Spanish, Swedish, Tamil, Turkish,
Ukrainian — plus Filipino (Tagalog) in the second list only.

AI Echo: "record your script how you like, and the avatar you choose will have the exact tone, intonation,
and pronunciation as your original recording."

## Summary

The app route states its own limits — script 10–800 characters and 1 minute maximum — and an explicit
commercial-use answer for AI Creator output. These are app-level constraints layered on Avatar X, not API
limits; the page does not name the underlying model.

## Gaps

- No statement of the underlying model, resolution, or render time.
- The two language lists on the same page disagree (Filipino/Tagalog appears in one).
- The commercial-use answer is a FAQ statement; the operative licence is the Terms.

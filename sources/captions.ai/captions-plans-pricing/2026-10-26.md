# Captions (Mirage) app pricing and plans

- Origin: Captions (Mirage) pricing page, <https://captions.ai/pricing>
- Evidence kind: official pricing page (consumer/app plans)
- Publication date: none stated; capture date 2026-10-26
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Captions AI avatars / AI Twin / AI Creator, which the 2026-07-28 launch post says are
  powered by Mirage Avatar X

## Captured content (verbatim, selected)

> Max — Use generative AI to make new footage, AI actors and more — **$24.99/mo**

> Create digital twins or custom AI actors … Access 500 credits per month

> Frontier / Frontier 2x / Frontier 4x — $69.99/mo / $139.99/mo / $279.99/mo — 1x usage / 2x usage / 4x
> usage … 1,400 credits per month / 2,800 credits per month / 5,600 credits per month

> Access our most sophisticated tier of generative AI models

> \*All prices displayed in USD. Features and prices reflect iOS plans only.

> Enterprise — Build a custom package … Bulk credit discounts … Training data exclusion

The feature comparison matrix lists `AI Creator`, `AI Ads`, `AI Skits`, `AI Twin` and `AI Video` as
available on Free, Max and Frontier, without per-feature credit costs.

## Summary

The in-app route to Avatar X is a **credit-based subscription**: a Max plan at $24.99/mo with 500 credits,
and Frontier tiers at $69.99–$279.99/mo with 1,400–5,600 credits. No page captured states how many credits
one avatar generation costs, so the app route cannot be converted to a per-second price.

## Gaps

- No credit cost per avatar video, per minute, or per AI Twin, so the app and API prices are not comparable.
- "Prices reflect iOS plans only" — web pricing may differ; no web price list was captured.
- Enterprise pricing is "custom"; no terms were captured.
- The page does not mention Avatar X by name.

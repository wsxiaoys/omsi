## Captured content

### August 2026 — "Sonic 3.6"

> Sonic 3.6 is an update to Sonic 3.5 — generally available from August 27 on `sonic-3.6`.

What's new (verbatim):

- **More natural speech, pacing, and emotional expression** — "the model adapts intonation, pacing,
  and emotiveness to the context of the transcript, with no SSML tags or explicit instructions. In
  blind head-to-head testing, listeners preferred Sonic 3.6 to Sonic 3.5 by nearly two to one on
  English transcripts."
- **Step-change multilingual naturalness** — "native-sounding pronunciation and accents across 44
  languages / 61 locales."
- **Two new languages** — Odia (`or`) and Urdu (`ur`), including voices and IVC support.
- **Hindi and Hinglish transcript following** — romanized/Latin-script Hindi follows the transcript;
  new `normalization` field.
- **Non-English alphanumerics** — "spelled-out letters, codes, and IDs use native letter names and
  accent, in all supported languages."
- **In-transcript disfluencies** — written disfluencies shift pacing and intonation to sound like
  natural hesitation.
- **English heteronyms** — higher accuracy on "read", "bass", "live" in context.

Migration notes (verbatim):

> For customers currently using `sonic-3.5`, you will have to manually repoint production traffic to
> `sonic-3.6-2026-08-27` or `sonic-3.6`.

> Your voice clones carry over. Instant and Professional Voice Clones work on Sonic 3.6 with the same
> voice IDs — nothing to recreate. Human evals confirm voices sound the same as on Sonic 3.5.

> No changes to the feature surface. Prompting guidance, SSML tags, timestamps, codec, and speed and
> volume controls all behave as they do on Sonic 3.5.

The same entry announces three new TTS API fields — `locale` ("`en-GB`, `es-MX`, `hi-IN`, and 58
more"), `accent` (multilingual voices only), and `normalization` (`auto`, `off`, or a locale code) —
and API version **2026-08-14**.

### September 2026 — later Sonic 3.6 changes

- Pronunciation dictionaries on Sonic 3.6 now ignore case by default (opt-in case sensitivity).
- Spacing around Latin-script words inside Japanese and Chinese transcripts improved.
- **"Sonic 3.6 for self-hosted deployments"** — "The September 17 release brings self-hosted customers
  Sonic 3.6's quality improvements, Odia and Urdu support, and locale-aware normalization."
- Voices: "**85 new public voices** — Across 24 languages, including Hindi, Arabic, Indonesian, and
  Swedish"; "**58 voices** in the voice library now speak up to 27 languages and accents natively."

## Summary

This is the strongest first-party date evidence: **GA from 2026-08-27**, which matches the snapshot
name and the leaderboard's "Aug 2026". It also supplies the only quantitative quality claim the vendor
makes — a "nearly two to one" blind preference over Sonic 3.5 on English transcripts — with no sample
size, rater pool, text set or date, so it cannot be audited. It establishes that Sonic 3.6 shipped as a
quality update to Sonic 3.5 with an unchanged feature surface, and that self-hosted customers got it
on September 17, 2026.

## Gaps

- No number behind "nearly two to one": no n, no interval, no evaluator named.
- "44 languages / 61 locales" — the locale count is not enumerated on this page.
- The changelog does not say whether `sonic-3.6` (the moving alias) changed after 2026-08-27, or
  whether the September items shipped to the same snapshot.

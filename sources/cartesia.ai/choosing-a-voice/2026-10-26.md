- Evidence kind: official documentation (voice inventory guidance)
- Publication date: not stated.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Sonic TTS family, including `sonic-3.6`

## Captured content

> Cartesia offers 500+ voices out-of-the-box, as well as the ability to clone your own voices.

> Featured voices are a handpicked set of Voices that our Voices team believes to be great voices. We
> make this determination through a combination of usage, performance, robust internal evaluations,
> and customer feedback.

Recommended for production voice agents (verbatim):

- Male: `Daniel`, `Archie`, `Parker`, `Jameson`, `Corey`
- Female: `Skylar`, `Gemma`, `Jacqueline`, `Emma`, `Lauren`

> For voice agents in production, we've found that more stable, realistic voices perform better than
> studio quality, emotive voices.

Recommended for AI characters / emotive use: Male `Brandon`, `Cory`, `Alec`; Female `Lucy`, `Ariana`,
`Dorothy`. "Sonic is expressive with some voices like `Lucy` and `Ariana` labeled as emotive in the
playground."

## Summary

The only first-party voice-count figure captured: **500+ voices out of the box**, plus cloning. The
page also splits the inventory by intended use — "stable" voices for agents, "emotive" voices for
characters — which matters because Cartesia's own emotion guidance says emotion control works best with
voices tagged Emotive.

## Gaps

- No exact voice count, no per-language counts, and no list of which voices support which accents.
- "500+" is undated; the September 2026 changelog adds "85 new public voices", so the figure may lag.

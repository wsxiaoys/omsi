# Cartesia: docs pricing page (credits, billing basis, error handling)

- Origin: Cartesia, "Pricing — How Cartesia charges for usage",
  <https://docs.cartesia.ai/pricing> (read as Markdown at `…/pricing.md`)
- Evidence kind: official documentation (billing basis)
- Publication date: not stated.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Sonic TTS family (`sonic-3.6`, `sonic-3.5`, `sonic-3`); Ink 2 STT

## Captured content

> Cartesia meters model usage in **credits** and agent usage in **agent dollars**.

> Credits are only used by successful requests; errors will not consume credits.

| Capability | Endpoint | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TTS | `/tts/bytes`, `/tts/sse`, `/tts/websocket` | ~1 credit per character |
| STT | `/stt`, `/stt/websocket`, `/stt/turns/websocket` | Depends on endpoint, model, and audio duration |
| Infill | `/infill/bytes` | 300 credits + ~1 credit per character |
| Managed Agents | agent endpoints | Billed per minute in USD, not credits |

> Standard TTS costs approximately 1 credit per character. The exact number of credits can vary
> slightly due to transcript pre-processing.

> This applies to every TTS endpoint: `/tts/bytes`, `/tts/sse`, and `/tts/websocket`.

Managed Agents are billed **per minute in USD**: "$0.06" base per agent call minute, "+$0.014"
telephony add-on for a Cartesia-provided number. Those per-minute rates exclude LLM usage.

Overages: with overages enabled, "requests continue to succeed past your monthly allotment and the
extra usage is billed as an overage"; with overages disabled, "requests that would exceed your
allotment fail until the next renewal".

## Summary

Confirms the billing basis is **characters, not seconds or requests**, and that it is uniform across
the three TTS endpoints and across Sonic model versions. The "~1 credit per character" hedge is the
only first-party acknowledgement that the exact charge varies with pre-processing — which is why the
pricing FAQ quotes 750–800 credits per minute rather than a fixed 60-second ratio.

## Gaps

- No USD figure on this page at all; it defers to `cartesia.ai/pricing`.
- "Vary slightly due to transcript pre-processing" is not quantified, so a long SSML-tag-heavy
  transcript cannot be costed exactly in advance.
- No statement about whether a request that streams partially and then fails is billed.

## Summary

Sonic 3.6 is the **only current default**, with 3.5 and `sonic-3-2026-01-12` stable alongside it and
`sonic-preview` explicitly beta. The whole Sonic 2 / Sonic Turbo line and one Sonic 3 snapshot are
scheduled to disappear, which makes 3.6 the migration target the vendor names.

Two things in this document do not line up with the capture date. The "upcoming" sunset of
2026-10-20 is six days in the past at capture, yet still listed as upcoming; and the raw-audio Voice
Changer endpoints were retired on 2026-08-20, three weeks after Sonic 3.6 shipped.

## Gaps

- No sunset date for `sonic-3.5` or for `sonic-3-2026-01-12`.
- No explanation of the Oct 20, 2026 date being both past and "upcoming".
- Voice Changer (raw audio) has no documented replacement, so the capability appears withdrawn.

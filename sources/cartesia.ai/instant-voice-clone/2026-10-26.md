# Cartesia: Instant Voice Clone

- Origin: Cartesia, "Instant Voice Clone — Create a custom voice in seconds from a short recording",
  <https://docs.cartesia.ai/build-with-cartesia/capability-guides/clone-voices>
  (read as Markdown at `…/clone-voices.md`)
- Evidence kind: official documentation
- Publication date: not stated.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Sonic 3.6 (`sonic-3.6`) and newer; behaviour differs on older models

## Captured content

> You can create a custom voice from a short recording in seconds using Cartesia's instant voice
> clone. 10 seconds of audio is enough to get started, and up to 60 seconds to better retain the
> speaker's accent.

> The 60-second limit applies to Sonic 3.6 and newer. Older models only learn from the first 10
> seconds of your clip, so a longer sample won't improve results there.

> Uploaded audio files must be no larger than 16 MB.

Recording guidance (verbatim excerpts):

- "The clone picks up the mood of your clip. A cheerful take gives you a voice that sounds cheerful in
  everything it says."
- "One speaker, no background noise."
- "Each clone is built from one language. To make the clone speak other languages natively, clone it
  in its own language first, then add languages with the Add Voice Accents API."

Two routes: the Playground (`play.cartesia.ai/voices/create/clone`) and `POST /voices/clone` with "your
clip, a name, and the language you recorded in".

> An instant voice clone works well for most voices. If the speaker has a rare accent, is a character
> voice, or carries distinctive qualities you need to preserve, use a Pro Voice Clone instead.

Related: the deprecated-models page says the old voice-embedding endpoint `POST /voices/clone/clip`
was sunset on 2026-06-01 in favour of `POST /voices/clone`, and that `POST /voices` (Create Voice) is
also gone; TTS requests using `"voice": {"mode": "embedding"}` stopped being accepted on 2026-06-01.

## Summary

IVC is a **10-second minimum, 60-second maximum** (Sonic 3.6+), 16 MB upload, one language per clone,
dashboard or API. The 60-second ceiling is a genuine Sonic 3.6 behaviour change over older models,
which only learn from the first 10 seconds — one of the concrete differences between 3.6 and Sonic 3.

Consent is required by policy (see the marketing page: "All voice clones require verified consent from
the speaker"), but the consent *mechanism* is not described on this page.

## Gaps

- No documentation of how consent is verified, what the attestation looks like, or what happens to an
  existing clone if a complaint is filed.
- No quality metric for IVC (speaker similarity, WER on cloned speech).
- No statement of how many instant clones a plan may hold.

## Summary

The generational record: **Sonic 3** (snapshots 2025-10-27 and 2026-01-12), **Sonic 3.5** (2026-05-04)
and **Sonic 3.6** (2026-08-27), each with 42 → 42 → 44 languages. Per the changelog, 3.6 changes the
model without changing the feature surface (see `sources/cartesia.ai/changelog-2026/2026-10-26.md`).

Two inconsistencies are visible in this one page. First, `sonic-3-2025-10-27` is still listed with a
future sunset ("Sunsets Oct 20, 2026") at a capture date of 2026-10-26 — six days *after* that date.
Second, `sonic-latest` is called a deprecated alias here while the TTS bytes schema still accepts it as
an enum value. Neither page addresses the other.

## Gaps

- No statement of whether Sonic 3.5 or Sonic 3 has its own sunset date.

### FAQ — the credit arithmetic

> Sonic text-to-speech: One minute of audio generation requires 750-800 credits. 1 credit equals 1
> character.

> Training a Professional Voice Clone is free. Speech generated with a PVC costs 1 credit per
> character, unless your custom Enterprise plan specifies otherwise.

> At pro tier, it's $65 per 1M credits. At startup tier, it's $45 per 1M credits. At scale tier, it's
> $38 per 1M credits.

The overage sentence is the only place on the page that converts credits to USD per 1M. Dividing plan
price by included credits gives a different, lower figure: Pro $5/100K = **$50.00/1M**, Startup
$49/1.25M = **$39.20/1M**, Scale $299/8M = **$37.38/1M**.

> Do TTS, STT, and Agent concurrency limits affect each other? No. Each service has its own dedicated
> concurrency pool.

## Summary

Billing basis is **per character, metered as credits at ~1 credit/character**, with one minute of
speech costing 750–800 credits. The self-serve effective rate is therefore roughly **$37–$50 per 1M
characters** depending on tier, and roughly **$0.028–$0.040 per minute of audio** at 750–800
credits/min. There is no per-minute TTS price and no separate Sonic-3.6 price: every Sonic model is
billed at the same credit rate.

Limits are plan-scoped and **concurrency-based** (2/3/5/15 concurrent TTS requests), not
request-length-based. Commercial use requires a paid plan ("Commercial use license" appears on Pro and
above). Pro Voice Cloning is gated at Startup and above, with 2 or 4 slots.

## Gaps

- No published USD-per-1M-character list price; the conversion above is arithmetic from plan price and
  credit allotment, not a stated rate.
- No statement of how a partial credit or a failed/empty generation is rounded, beyond the docs'
  "credits are only used by successful requests".
- No rate for self-hosted/OEM licensing.

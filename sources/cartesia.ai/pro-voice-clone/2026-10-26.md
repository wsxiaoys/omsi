# Cartesia: Pro Voice Clone (PVC)

- Origin: Cartesia, "Pro Voice Clone — Create a near-exact voice replica, trained on 30+ minutes of
  your audio", <https://docs.cartesia.ai/build-with-cartesia/capability-guides/clone-voices-pro>
  (read as Markdown at `…/clone-voices-pro.md`)
- Evidence kind: official documentation
- Publication date: not stated.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `sonic-3.6-2026-08-27`, `sonic-3.5-2026-05-04` (the listed compatible snapshots)

## Captured content

> We recommend starting with an Instant Voice Clone … Use a PVC when the IVC isn't a close enough
> match and you can supply 30+ minutes of studio-quality audio of a single speaker.

| | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Pro Voice Clone | 30 minutes of audio |
| Plan | Startup plan or above |

> A PVC closely matches the audio you train it on. … The more you provide, the better: 30 minutes is
> the minimum. Training then takes up to 3 hours.

Compatible snapshots (verbatim list):

- `sonic-3.5-2026-05-04`
- `sonic-3.6-2026-08-27`

> A PVC is trained on one language at a time, so record a separate dataset for each language you need.

> A PVC can speak every language the model supports, but it sounds native only in the language it was
> trained on. … Add Voice Accents doesn't apply to PVCs.

> You can't use a PVC with `sonic-preview`. … Passing `sonic-preview` or an unsupported `model_id`
> returns HTTP `400` with a `voice_model_mismatch` error: "The requested voice is not compatible with
> the requested model. Switch to one of these compatible models: sonic-3.5-2026-05-04,
> sonic-3.6-2026-08-27."

Request-time speed control: PVCs "take their pacing from their source recordings and don't support
request-time speed adjustments" (stated on the Volume/Speed/Emotion page).

## Summary

PVC is the enterprise-tier clone: **≥30 minutes**, **up to 3 hours of training**, **Startup plan or
above**, per-language datasets, one voice ID per language, incompatible with `sonic-preview`. The
compatible-model list is the clearest first-party statement that the pinned 3.6 snapshot is
`sonic-3.6-2026-08-27`.

The marketing page gives the PVC minimum as "15–30 minutes"; the docs say 30 minutes is the minimum.
The docs are the more specific statement.

## Gaps

- No price: training is free per the pricing FAQ, generation billed at 1 credit/character.
- No quality metric, no statement on how a PVC ages across future snapshots beyond "trained
  automatically on all eligible models" (September 2026 changelog).
- Consent verification for PVC datasets is not described here.

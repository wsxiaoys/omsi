### "What's new in Sonic 3.6?"

- **Human-like naturalness** — "the most natural and human-sounding TTS model… pause length adapts
  to the context of the sentence, and in-transcript disfluencies (e.g. 'uhm', 'hmm') produce a
  natural 'thinking' pace."
- **Native speaker quality** — "Sonic 3.6 speaks 44 languages and pronounces words and phrases the
  way a local would."
- **Hinglish support** — expanded support for Hindi transcripts written in Latin script, plus
  "improved pronunciation of Indian names and places".
- **Two new languages** — Odia (`or`) and Urdu (`ur`), "with instant voice cloning support".

> PVCs created on older models will work on Sonic 3.6.

### Language handling

- `language` and `locale` "accept language codes such as `en` and regional locale codes such as
  `en-GB`. Set one, never both."
- "By default, text normalization follows that value."
- Odia and Urdu: "Curated voices for both are in the Voice Library, or clone a voice in either
  language."

### Relationship to the previous model

> Note that Sonic 3.6 is fully backwards compatible with Sonic 3.5.

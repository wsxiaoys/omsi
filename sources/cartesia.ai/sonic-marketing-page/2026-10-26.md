## Summary

Two claims on this page matter for the wiki page and neither is measured here:

1. **Latency.** "Sub-90ms" is stated three ways (first audio under 90 ms; sub-90 ms model latency;
   lowest latency in the market) with no region, endpoint, text set or percentile attached. A
   competitor's chart puts Sonic 3.6 at 262 ms median time to first speech (see
   `sources/elevenlabs.com/eleven-v4-landing-page/2026-10-26.md`), so the two numbers are not the same
   quantity and cannot both be read as TTFS.
2. **Leaderboard position.** "Ranked #1 in Speech Arena" is contradicted by the Artificial Analysis
   text-to-speech board captured the same day, where Sonic 3.6 is 4th with Elo 1292
   (see `sources/artificialanalysis.ai/sonic-3-6-leaderboard/2026-10-26.md`). The claim is undated.

The page also establishes that Sonic 3.6 is available **self-hosted** (on-prem, VPC, air-gapped, OEM)
under enterprise contract — unusual for this knowledge base's TTS set, where every other model is
hosted-only — and that commercial use is gated on a paid plan.

## Gaps

- No date, methodology or category filter for the "#1 in Speech Arena" claim.
- No definition of "model latency" vs "first audio" vs network overhead.
- The PVC minimum here ("15–30 minutes") disagrees with the docs' "30 minutes" (see
  `sources/cartesia.ai/pro-voice-clone/2026-10-26.md`).
- No per-character or per-minute USD price on this page; it defers to the pricing page.

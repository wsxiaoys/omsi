## Overview, verbatim

> "Claude Fable 5.1 extends Claude Fable 5 at the same input and output prices, with cache
> reads at a quarter of the cost, and brings stronger long-running agentic coding, multistep
> research, and document, spreadsheet, and slide work. For most workloads, start with Claude
> Opus 5.5 (see Choosing a model). Use Claude Fable 5.1 for demanding reasoning and
> long-horizon agentic work, or when your evals on Claude Opus 5.5 at higher effort still
> don't meet your requirements. Claude Mythos 5.1 offers the same capabilities only to
> organizations verified through Anthropic's verification programs, such as the Cyber
> Verification Program."

This is the first-party statement of the tier ordering the roster assumes: Opus 5.5 is the
default, Fable 5.1 is the escalation above it.

## Behaviour statements, verbatim

> "If you already call Claude Fable 5, four changes are breaking: Forced tool use returns an
> error. Only Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1 can read Claude Fable 5.1's thinking
> blocks. Editing earlier turns invalidates thinking blocks. Some advisor tool pairings are
> not supported."

> "Five changes are additive: Mid-conversation effort changes (beta). Turn-scoped system
> messages (beta). Readable progress updates between tool calls (`display: "updates"`, beta).
> A lower cache read price. Content provenance."

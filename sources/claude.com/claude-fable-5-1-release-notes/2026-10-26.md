## September 1, 2026 — launch, verbatim

> "We've launched Claude Fable 5.1 (`claude-fable-5-1`), the successor to Claude Fable 5 for
> long-running agentic coding, knowledge work, and research, alongside Claude Mythos 5.1
> (`claude-mythos-5-1`) for Project Glasswing participants. Both models support a 1M token
> context window by default, 128k max output tokens, and always-on adaptive thinking, at $10
> / $50 USD per MTok, the same as Claude Fable 5, with cache reads cut to $0.25 USD per MTok.
> Claude Fable 5.1 is available on the Claude API, Claude in Amazon Bedrock, Claude Platform
> on AWS, Claude on Google Cloud, and Claude in Microsoft Foundry."

> "Prompt cache reads on Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1 cost $0.25 USD per million
> tokens: 0.025x the base input price, compared with 0.1x on other models. Cache writes are
> unchanged."

> "On Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1, `tool_choice` types `any` and `tool` aren't
> supported and return a 400 error."

> "Thinking blocks produced by Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1 are preserved only for
> the model that produced them or a newer one."

> "Like Claude Fable 5, both models require 30-day data retention and aren't available under
> zero data retention unless expressly authorized by Anthropic. See Model-specific data
> retention requirements."

Also on 2026-09-01: per-message effort changes (beta), turn-scoped system messages (beta),
`thinking.display: "updates"` (beta), and the text watermark plus C2PA Content Credentials
for supported files.

## Contradiction with the launch post on data retention

This record says the models "require 30-day data retention and aren't available under zero
data retention unless expressly authorized by Anthropic". The
[launch post](../../anthropic.com/claude-fable-5-1-announcement/2026-10-26.md) says "Until
EFS is available, eligible customers will be able to use Fable 5.1 with zero data retention".
The two are reconcilable only if "eligible customers" means the expressly-authorized case;
neither document says so. Both readings are recorded on the model page.

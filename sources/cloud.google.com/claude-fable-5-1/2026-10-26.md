## Safety, retention and regions, verbatim

> "Advanced AI Safety Notice: Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 on Google Cloud is within the scope
> of the Advanced AI Safety Addendum."

> "All prompts and responses are stored for up to 30 days for the sole purpose of monitoring
> for potential abuse of relevant terms of service."

Supported regions: "United States — Multi-region", "Europe — Multi-region", "Global — global
endpoint", with ML processing in "United States — Multi-region", "Europe — Multi-region" and
"Asia Pacific — asia-southeast1".

The safety notice names "Claude Fable 5", not 5.1, on a page otherwise about 5.1 — either a
typo or a sign that the addendum scope was inherited from the predecessor.

Enablement requires consenting to the Advanced AI Safety Addendum in Model Garden and, for
Private Offer customers, enabling prompt-response sharing with Anthropic per model location.

## Contradiction with first-party lifecycle

This page says retirement is "No sooner than March 1, 2027". Anthropic's own model page,
deprecations table and Bedrock card all say 2027-09-01. Google's floor is six months earlier
and its wording is the same kind of commitment, so this is a real discrepancy about the
serving window on that platform, not a rounding difference. Neither page explains it.

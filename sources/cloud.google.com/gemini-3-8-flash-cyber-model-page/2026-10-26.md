## Summary

`gemini-3.8-flash-cyber` is a **separate model ID**: a post-trained cybersecurity variant of the same
base, GA but allowlisted, priced at the undiscounted standard rate, and stripped of every tool except
structured output and caching. It is not a cheaper or faster tier of `gemini-3.8-flash`, and its
benchmark behaviour is not covered by the 3.8 Flash model card.

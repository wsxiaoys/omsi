## Captured content

> Gemini 3.8 Flash, Gemini 3.7 Flash, Gemini 3.6 Flash, and CodeMender using these models are offered

> For non-global endpoints, pricing will go into effect for the Generally available Gemini 3 and later

## Summary

The platform ladder matches the Gemini API's shape once the three mode tables are read separately:
Standard $0.75/$3.75, Flex/Batch at half ($0.375/$1.875), Priority at 1.8× ($1.35/$6.75), all through
2026 and doubled from 2027-01-01. Non-global endpoints add exactly 10%. The model carries **no
long-context surcharge** — the `>200K` columns repeat the `≤200K` figures — unlike models where the
footnote's long-context rule does bite.

## Gaps

- The asterisk on `Gemini 3.8 Flash*` is not defined in the captured text (the page's asterisk notes
  cover other models); presumably it marks the introductory-price footnote.
- Provisioned Throughput rates for this model are on a separate page and were not captured.
- "Long context rates" are not defined as a formula; this model happens to have none.
- Non-USD billing uses Cloud Platform SKUs, not captured.
- Two Google price pages (Gemini API and this one) agree on the ladder but differ in presentation, and
  the Gemini API page additionally lists a Contributor tier that has no counterpart here.

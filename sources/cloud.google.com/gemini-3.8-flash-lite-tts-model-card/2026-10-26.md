## Summary

A second Google platform serves the same model ID with a different launch stage and date:
**Preview, released 2026-09-28, `global` region only**, against the Gemini API's **GA on 2026-09-22**.
This is the clearest contradiction in the corpus and it is not resolvable from the pages: two Google
surfaces disagree on whether `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts` is GA, and give launch dates six days apart.
What they agree on is the spec: 8,192 / 16,384 token limits, text-in/audio-out, 101 languages, and
the same Voice design / Voice replication support.

It also resolves the voice-count ambiguity partially: **30 prebuilt + "more than 2,000 curated voices
in the Extended Voice Library"**, which is a concrete number where the Gemini API guide says only
"hundreds" and the changelog says "150+".

## Gaps

- No price on this page, and no statement whether Vertex prices differ from the Gemini API's
  $6.00/1M audio tokens.
- No regional breakdown beyond "global"; no statement on data residency.
- No watermarking or SynthID statement.
- No statement of how the Preview launch stage interacts with the Gemini API's GA status for the same
  ID.

### Description (verbatim)

> Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS (`gemini-3.8-flash-tts`) is Google's flagship text-to-speech model, available
> through Gemini Enterprise. It's built for studio-grade voice fidelity, expressive acting,
> authentic regional accents, and stable long-form, multi-turn audio.

### Capability bullets (verbatim)

> **Acoustic fidelity and acting nuance**: Delivers a wide emotional range, natural cadence, and
> close adherence to turn-level style directions and inline vocal events such as `<laugh>`, `<sigh>`,
> and `<short pause>`.
> **Long-form, multi-turn stability**: Keeps voice identity, timbre, volume, and room tone consistent
> across long dialogues and multi-minute narration.
> **Regional accents and pronunciation**: Supports regional accents and minority dialects in 130
> languages.
> **Voice options**: Works with 30 prebuilt voices, more than 2,000 curated voices in the Extended
> Voice Library, voices that you create with Voice design, and voices that you replicate with Voice
> replication.

## Summary

A second Google platform serves the same model ID with a different launch stage and date: **Preview,
released 2026-09-28, `global` region only**, against the Gemini API's **GA on 2026-09-22**
(`sources/google.dev/gemini-3.8-flash-tts-release-notes/2026-10-26.md`). Two Google surfaces disagree
on whether `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` is GA, and their dates are six days apart; neither page addresses
the other.

What the surfaces agree on is the spec: 8,192 / 16,384 token limits, text-in/audio-out, 130
languages, and Voice design plus Voice replication supported. Vertex adds a **concrete** Extended
Voice Library figure — "more than 2,000 curated voices" — where the Gemini API guide says only
"hundreds" and the changelog says "150+".

## Gaps

- No price on this page, and no statement whether the Vertex route is billed differently from the
  Gemini API's $9.00/1M audio tokens.
- No regional breakdown beyond `global`; no data-residency statement.
- No watermarking or SynthID statement (contrast the Replicate record, which asserts SynthID).
- No statement of how a Preview launch stage on this platform interacts with the Gemini API's GA
  status for the same ID, or which support window applies.
- The card gives no per-request character or duration ceiling beyond the token limits.

## Reading of the promotional structure

Three first-party documents describe the same discount differently, and all three are
consistent if the introductory price *is* the 50% credit:

- Launch posts (2026-09-30 and 2026-10-13): "introductory price of $2 per million input
  tokens and $10 per million output tokens", with $4/$20 applying "after the introductory
  period expires".
- This page: $2/$10 "through November 30, 2026", $4/$20 from December 1, 2026, with the
  discount implemented as "50% credits back on net spend".
- OpenRouter: lists $4.00 / $20.00 with a "50% off" badge, i.e. the same $2/$10.

So the *list* price of `gemini-4-argon` is $4 / $20 per 1M input / output tokens, and
$2 / $10 is a dated promotion running through 2026-11-30, not a permanent tier price. The
cyber-permissive variant is excluded from the promotion and is priced at list from day one.

## Gaps

- No cache-write price on this page; OpenRouter's metadata carries one
  (`input_cache_write` = $0.1875 per 1M), which has no first-party counterpart in this
  capture.
- No statement of what happens to in-flight long decodes when the price changes on
  2026-12-01.
- The Gemini API (AI Studio) price list was not captured; whether it carries the same promo
  end date is unverified here.
- Token-based billing for image/audio/video input is not broken out by modality; all are
  priced as input tokens.

excludes a Preview model ID regardless of family.

## What this means for a buyer

- Lyria 3 output is **not** covered by Google's generative-AI IP indemnity at the capture date,
  because Lyria is absent from the list and the model is Preview. A customer generating music with
  `lyria-3-pro-preview` carries the copyright risk of the output themselves.
- Google's own Preview terms (see the Lyria 3 model card record) permit commercial use of Preview
  output — "Customers may elect to use it for production or commercial purposes" — so the gap is
  indemnity, not permission.
- Clause (j) shifts responsibility for filter use, non-use, and modification onto the customer. The
  Lyria 3 model card lists filtering (input, recitation, vocal likeness) as a supported capability;
  neither document says which Lyria filters a caller may configure or disable.

## Gaps

- No Lyria-specific output-ownership or commercial-use statement was found on Google Cloud terms
  pages; the model card's Preview paragraph is the only first-party commercial-use language captured.
- Weights are not published for Lyria 3 under any licence; no weights licence text exists to check.

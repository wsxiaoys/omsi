## What this establishes about the relationship to Lyria 3

Lyria 2 is GA, instrumental-only, English-prompt-only, WAV/48 kHz, four clips per request, and its
card lists "Audio watermarking: Not supported" and "Filtering: Not supported". Lyria 3 is Preview,
vocal-capable, eight languages, MP3/44.1 kHz, one clip per request, with watermarking, filtering,
prompt rewriting and C2PA all listed as supported.

Those are not increments of the same surface — every row that matters for a caller differs. The card
does not describe Lyria 2 as deprecated or as having a retirement date.

## Gaps

- No retirement or deprecation date for `lyria-002`.
- The card's "Audio watermarking: Not supported" and "Filtering: Not supported" rows conflict with
  the [Lyria API reference](../../cloud.google.com/lyria-api-reference/2026-10-26.md) for the same
  model, which says "Content safety filters, recitation checking, artist intent checks, and SynthID
  watermarking are applied" to `lyria-002`. Two first-party pages disagree about the same model ID;
  neither is dated relative to the other, so this page records the contradiction rather than picking a
  side.
- The [API reference](../../cloud.google.com/lyria-api-reference/2026-10-26.md) says each clip is
  "30 seconds long" where this card and the generate-music guide both say **32.8 s**.

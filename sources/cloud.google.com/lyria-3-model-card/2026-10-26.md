## Notes

- **"Maximum number of clips per prompt: 1"** for both variants. This is the sharpest break from
  Lyria 2, whose card allows four clips per request.
- **`sample_count` and `seed` are absent** from the Lyria 3 capability list, although Lyria 2's API
  reference documents both. The card does not say whether they are unsupported or simply undocumented.
- The card's structure controls ("Duration, BPM, Intensity") are **not request parameters** — the
  generate-music page's request body carries only `model` and `input` (text and image parts). They
  are prompt-level directions, per the prompt guide.
- 184 seconds = 3 min 04 s, consistent with DeepMind's marketing "up to 3 minutes".
- The card says nothing about weights, licensing of output, or a deprecation window.

## Gaps

- No statement on whether `lyria-3-pro-preview`/`lyria-3-clip-preview` are the only IDs, or whether a
  GA ID is planned.
- No PDF input documented here, although the prompt guide claims PDFs are accepted (see the prompt
  guide record).
- No per-request prompt length limit.

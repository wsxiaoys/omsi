## Reading the "count" unit

The table prices per "1 count" and never defines the unit. For this section the unit is not
ambiguous in practice:

- Lyria 3 is $0.04 per clip. fal charges exactly `$0.04 per audios` for `fal-ai/lyria3` and Replicate
  charges `$0.04` "per output audio file" for `google/lyria-3` — the same number, per clip.
- Lyria 3 Pro is $0.08 per song. fal charges exactly `$0.08 per audios` for `fal-ai/lyria3/pro`.
- Lyria 2 is $0.06 per 30 s of output. The
  [Lyria API reference](../lyria-api-reference/2026-10-26.md) states this explicitly ("$0.06 per 30
  seconds of output music generated"), while fal's `fal-ai/lyria2` charges `$0.1 per 30 seconds` —
  a host markup.

So "1 count" = one generated clip/song for Lyria 3 and Pro; for Lyria 2 the count is a 30-second
unit. No batch or priority tier is offered for Lyria, unlike the Veo/Gemini rows on the same page.

## Price comparison that matters

| Surface | Lyria 3 (30 s) | Lyria 3 Pro (184 s) | Lyria 2 (32.8 s) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Cloud | $0.04 | $0.08 | $0.06 / 30 s |
| fal | $0.04 (`fal-ai/lyria3`) | $0.08 (`fal-ai/lyria3/pro`) | $0.10 / 30 s (`fal-ai/lyria2`) |
| Replicate | $0.04 per output file, "or 25 files for $1" | $0.08 per output file, "or around 12 files for $1" | not captured |

Google Cloud and both hosts agree on Lyria 3 and Lyria 3 Pro to the cent, which is why "1 count"
reads as one clip/song. The only markup in the family is fal's Lyria 2 rate, 67% above Google's.

Replicate's model JSON also carries a top-level `"price": "$0.0001 per second"` alongside its
per-file tier. That field is a template placeholder — it disagrees with the per-file tier on the
same page and with every other source — so the per-file tier is the figure to trust.

## Gaps

- No per-second or per-minute rate, no free tier, no Provisioned Throughput option for Lyria.
- The page does not say whether a blocked/filtered request is billed.

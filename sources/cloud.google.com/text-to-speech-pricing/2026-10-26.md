# Google Cloud: Text-to-Speech pricing, Gemini-TTS table

- Origin: Google Cloud, "Text-to-Speech pricing", <https://cloud.google.com/text-to-speech/pricing>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party price list)
- Publication date: not stated; continuously edited. Prices are list prices on the capture date.
  Capture date 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` (primary subject), plus the 3.8 and 2.5 TTS rows for
  comparison

## Billing basis (verbatim)

> Text-to-Speech is priced based on the number of characters sent to the service to be synthesized
> into audio each month.

> The total number of characters in the input string are counted for billing purposes, including
> spaces and newline characters.

The Gemini-TTS block below, however, prices in tokens:

> \* Audio tokens correspond to 25 tokens per second of audio

## Gemini-TTS table (verbatim)

| Model | Free usage limit | Price after free usage limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS (Preview) | Not available | Input tokens: **$1.00 per 1 million text tokens** (sku `EB3F-1051-05D7`). Output tokens: **$20.00 per 1 million audio tokens** (sku `7976-330B-8F17`) |
| Gemini 2.5 Flash TTS / Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite Preview TTS | Not available | $0.50 per 1M text tokens; $10.00 per 1M audio tokens |
| Gemini 2.5 Pro TTS | Not available | $1.00 per 1M text tokens; $20.00 per 1M audio tokens |
| Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS (Preview) through December 31, 2026 | Not available | $0.50 per 1M text tokens; $9.00 per 1M audio tokens |
| Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS (Preview) starting January 1, 2027 | Not available | $1.00 per 1M text tokens; $18.00 per 1M audio tokens |
| Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS (Preview) through December 31, 2026 | Not available | $0.50 per 1M text tokens; $6.00 per 1M audio tokens |

## Summary

Google Cloud bills `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` at **$1.00 per 1M input text tokens and $20.00 per
1M output audio tokens**, identical to the Gemini API list price
([Gemini API pricing](../../google.dev/gemini-api-pricing-tts/2026-10-26.md)) and to Gemini 2.5 Pro
TTS. There is no free usage allowance. 3.8 Flash TTS is cheaper on the same platform ($9.00 output)
through 2026.

## Gaps

- No per-character rate is given for Gemini-TTS despite the page's character-billing preamble, so the
  two bases coexist on one page without reconciliation.
- No rounding, minimum-charge or truncation-billing rule.

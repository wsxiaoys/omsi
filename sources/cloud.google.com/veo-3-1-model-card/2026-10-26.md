Two readings matter and are easy to get wrong:

- The "Audio — Not supported" row sits in the **modalities** block (text in, image in, video out), so
  it describes audio *input*. Sound *generation* is listed as "Supported" for all three tiers.
- "Pay-as-you-go — Not supported" and "Fixed quota — Supported" are the platform's consumption
  options, not statements that the model is free or unavailable. Pricing is per generated video on
  the pricing page.

## Summary

The authoritative first-party specification of the Veo 3.1 family as served on Google Cloud: three
model IDs, two GA (standard and fast, both released 2025-11-17) and one preview (lite, 2026-04-02),
all in `us-central1`, all 24 FPS mp4, 4/6/8-second clips, 9:16 or 16:9, English prompts, up to four
videos per request, 20 MB image inputs. Only the standard tier advertises 4K output; standard and
fast both accept image and reference-image input; Lite takes text only. The page does not name the preview-era IDs
(`veo-3.1-generate-preview`, `veo-3.1-fast-generate-preview`) — those appear in the release notes.

## Gaps

- No per-second or per-video price; it links to the pricing page instead.
- No latency, throughput, concurrency or queue figures beyond the odd quota line ("50 tokens per
  minute" per base model per region), which is not explained.
- No statement of what "extend videos" yields in total length (the extend guide gives 37 s).
- No statement about audio sample rate, channel count or codec inside the mp4.
- Region list is a single zone; the page does not say whether other regions are planned.

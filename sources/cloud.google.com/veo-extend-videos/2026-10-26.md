# Google Cloud: Extend videos (Veo)

- Origin: Google Cloud, "Extend videos", Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform video generation guide,
  <https://cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/video/extend-videos>
- Evidence kind: official API guide (first-party)
- Publication date: continuously edited; no date captured on the Veo section.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `veo-3.1-generate-001`, `veo-3.1-fast-generate-001`, `veo-3.1-lite-generate-001`
  (plus `gemini-omni-*`, out of scope)

## What the guide says (verbatim)

> You can use Gemini Omni Flash or Veo on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform to extend videos. The input
> video is limited between 1 and 30 seconds in length. Videos can be extended up to 40 seconds for
> Gemini Omni Flash or **37 seconds for Veo** on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform.

Supported models for the Veo path:

> `veo-3.1-lite-generate-001`
> `veo-3.1-generate-001`
> `veo-3.1-fast-generate-001`

Veo limitations on input (verbatim):

> When using Veo to extend videos, input videos are subject to the following limitations:
> The length must be 1 to 30 seconds.
> The frame rate must be 24 frames per second.

On output (verbatim):

> When using Veo to extend videos, output videos are subject to the following limitations
> The extended length is 7 seconds.
> The frame rate is 24 frames per second.

Accepted model IDs for the extension call, verbatim: "Veo 3.1: `veo-3.1-generate-001`", "Veo 3.1:
`veo-3.1-fast-generate-001`". One Python sample in the page still uses
`model = "veo-3.1-generate-preview"`, a preview ID the changelog retired on 2026-03-03 — the sample
is stale.

The call takes a Cloud Storage URI for the video to extend, and a prompt; the response is the
extended segment.

## Summary

Extension is a chained, 7-second-per-step operation on the Veo 3.1 family (including Lite), applied to
a 1–30-second, 24 fps input, capped at 37 seconds total on Google Cloud. That cap conflicts with fal's
documented "up to 20 steps … roughly 148 seconds" (see the fal landing record); the two hosts either
enforce different limits or fal's figure describes a chain the Vertex API does not permit.

## Gaps

- No statement of what happens at the 37-second cap, or whether the chain is one call or several.
- No audio-specific guidance: the guide does not say whether the extended segment carries audio, even
  though the marketing page claims "maintaining visual and audio consistency".
- No pricing note for extension; the pricing page has no extension row.

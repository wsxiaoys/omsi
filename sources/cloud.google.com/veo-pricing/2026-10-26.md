## The "count" ambiguity

The page writes every Veo price as "$X / 1 count" and never defines the unit. It does not say whether
one count is one second of output or one generated video. This matters by a factor of 4–8:

- Read as **per second**, Veo 3.1 at $0.40/s with audio matches both hosts exactly — fal charges
  $0.40/s and Replicate $0.40/s for 720p/1080p with audio (see the fal and Replicate records).
- Read as **per video**, an 8-second 1080p clip with audio would cost $0.40 on Google Cloud against
  $3.20 on fal and $3.20 on Replicate.

The identical unit prices for Veo 3.1 and Veo 3 ($0.40 with audio, $0.20 without) and the presence of
a separate, higher 4K row are consistent with the per-second reading, and both hosts' figures match
it. But the page does not state it, so this record does not resolve it.

Note also that the price list sells Fast and Lite at **4k** even though the model card caps Fast at
1080p and Lite at 1080p output; either the price rows are template rows or the model card is stale.

## Summary

Google Cloud sells Veo 3.1 per output unit with a with-audio and a without-audio rate and a 4K
surcharge: $0.40/$0.20 (720p–1080p) and $0.60/$0.40 (4K) for standard, roughly 3–4× cheaper for Fast
and 6–8× cheaper for Lite. The unit ("count") is undefined on the page, and the Fast/Lite 4K rows
contradict the model card's output-resolution caps.

## Gaps

- Unit of "count" undefined; no worked example, no duration dependence stated.
- No region-specific pricing (the model card lists `us-central1` only).
- No statement about billing failed or filtered generations.
- No Provisioned Throughput rates, although the model card lists PT as supported.

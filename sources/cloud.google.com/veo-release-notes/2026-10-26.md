- Origin: Google Cloud, "Vertex AI generative AI release notes",
  <https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/release-notes>
- Evidence kind: official changelog (first-party, dated entries)
- Publication date: rolling; the captured render's newest entry is "April 17, 2026" and the page is
  continuously edited.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Veo 3.1 (`veo-3.1-generate-preview`, `veo-3.1-fast-generate-preview`,

## Veo timeline (verbatim entries, chronological)

**October 15, 2025 — Feature**

> Veo video generation
> Veo 3.1 is available in Preview, and introduces the following models:
> Veo 3.1 preview
> Veo 3.1 Fast preview.
> … Generate Veo videos from text prompts / Generate Veo videos from an image / Generate Veo videos
> using first and last frames / **Direct Veo using a reference image** / Veo video generation API

The same date carries a second Veo entry: "Veo 2 supports adding and removing objects from videos in
Preview."

**November 17, 2025 — Feature**

> Veo video generation
> Veo 3.1 is Generally Available, and introduces the following models:
> Veo 3.1
> Veo 3.1 Fast

**December 8, 2025 — Feature**

> Veo 3.1 video extension
> Veo 3.1 supports video extension in Preview.

**January 13, 2026 — Feature**

> Veo 3.1 reference-to-video update
> Veo 3.1 Preview models now support the following features:
> 9:16 aspect ratio for reference-to-video.
> Upsampling for videos generated at 1080p and 4k resolutions.

**March 3, 2026 — Deprecated**

> Video generation preview endpoints deprecation
> The following table describes video generation endpoints that are deprecated and their
> replacements. We recommend updating your model endpoints before April 2, 2026, to avoid service
> disruption.
> `veo-3.1-generate-preview` → `veo-3.1-generate-001`
> `veo-3.1-fast-generate-preview` → `veo-3.1-fast-generate-001`

The same table retires the Veo 2 and Veo 3 preview IDs (`veo-3.0-generate-preview`,
`veo-3.0-fast-generate-preview`, `veo-2.0-generate-preview`, `veo-2.0-generate-exp`,
`veo-001-preview-0815`, `veo-001-preview`) into their GA successors.

**March 24, 2026 — Deprecated**

> Video generation GA endpoints deprecation
> … We recommend updating your model endpoints before June 30, 2026, to avoid service disruption.
> `veo-3.0-generate-001` → `veo-3.1-generate-001`
> `veo-3.0-fast-generate-001` → `veo-3.1-fast-generate-001`
> `veo-2.0-generate-001` → `veo-3.1-generate-001`

**April 2, 2026 — Feature**

> Veo 3.1 Lite
> Veo 3.1 Lite is available in public preview. This release is our most cost-efficient Veo on Vertex
> AI model.

## Summary

This is the only captured first-party document that dates Veo 3.1: **preview on 2025-10-15** (both
standard and fast preview IDs), **GA on 2025-11-17**, video extension in preview 2025-12-08,
reference-to-video 9:16 and 1080p/4K upsampling on 2026-01-13, preview IDs retired 2026-03-03, Veo 3
and Veo 2 GA IDs deprecated in favour of 3.1 on 2026-03-24, and Veo 3.1 Lite in public preview on
2026-04-02.

The 2025-10-15 entry also states that reference-image ("Direct Veo using a reference image") support
was part of the preview launch, while video *extension* only arrived on 2025-12-08 — extension is
therefore a later addition to the family, not a launch capability, on this platform.

## Gaps

- No entry dates the 4K output tier or the shift of Fast from 1080p to 4K (the pricing page sells
  Fast 4K; the model card does not).
- No entry covers a Gemini API-side launch, so the Gemini API dates for Veo 3.1 are not established
  by this record.
- Preview→GA gaps (Oct 15 → Nov 17) are inferred from the two entries; the changelog never states a
  duration.

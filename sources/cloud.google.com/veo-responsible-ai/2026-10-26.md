# Google Cloud: Responsible AI for Veo video generation

- Origin: Google Cloud, "Responsible AI for Veo on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform video generation",
  <https://cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/video/responsible-ai-and-usage-guidelines>
- Evidence kind: official policy and safety documentation (first-party)
- Publication date: continuously edited; page footer says "Last updated 2026-10-22 UTC".
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Veo family on Google Cloud (page is family-level, not per-tier)

## Safety filters (verbatim)

> Veo on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform offers several ways to input prompts to generate videos,
> including text, video, and images. Prompts that are provided to Veo are assessed against a list of
> safety filters, which include harmful categories (for example, violence, sexual, derogatory, and
> toxic). These safety filters aim to filter out input images and videos that violate the Google Cloud
> Platform Acceptable Use Policy, Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy, or Our AI Principles.

> If the model responds to a request with an error message, such as The prompt couldn't be submitted
> or it might violate our policies, then the input is triggering a safety filter. If fewer videos than
> requested are returned, then some generated output is being blocked for not meeting safety
> requirements.

## Summary

Google Cloud documents a support-code taxonomy rather than a prose policy for Veo: twelve categories,
a `personGeneration` switch and per-project allowlists for depictions of children and photorealistic
prominent people, and partial-result behaviour (fewer videos returned than requested) when some
outputs are filtered. This is the closest first-party statement to a likeness restriction on the API.

The page does **not** mention SynthID or C2PA; the DeepMind page claims SynthID watermarking, and the
model card lists "Content Credentials (C2PA) — Supported". Watermark behaviour is therefore documented
on three pages that never reference each other.

## Gaps

- No moderation thresholds, false-positive data, appeal process or region variation.
- No statement about audio-only harms (voice cloning, impersonation) even though Veo generates speech.
- No statement about output ownership, licensing or disclosure obligations.

- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party dated changelog)

- Relevant models: Lyria 3 (`lyria-3-pro-preview`, `lyria-3-clip-preview`), Lyria 2 (`lyria-002`)

## Lyria 3 entry (verbatim, under the March 25, 2026 date header)

> **March 25, 2026** — Feature — **Lyria 3** — Lyria is available in public preview. You can use
> `lyria-3-pro-preview` to generate 184 seconds of audio, or `lyria-3-clip-preview` to generate 30
> seconds of audio. For more information, see the following: Lyria 3 Pro Preview · Lyria 3 Clip
> Preview

This is the only dated first-party launch record found for Lyria 3. It fixes the canonical date at
**2026-03-25** and confirms both model IDs shipped together. The entries immediately above it are
dated 2026-04-02 and 2026-03-24, so the page is in descending date order here and the label is
self-consistent.

## Lyria 2 entry (verbatim, under the May 20, 2025 date header)

> Announcement — **Lyria 2**, our latest music generation model, is now generally available. See our
> music generation prompt guide and our user guide for more information. The model is available in
> the API and Vertex AI Studio.

The May 20, 2025 header governs a block of entries (Imagen 4 previews, Lyria 2 GA, MedGemma, Veo 3
preview); no per-entry day is printed. This conflicts with the Lyria 2 model card, which gives the
`lyria-002` version a release date of **2025-10-27**. Preserve both: the changelog dates the GA
announcement to 2025-05-20, the model card dates the version to 2025-10-27. Neither source explains
the five-month gap (a plausible reading is a 2025-05 announcement followed by a 2025-10-27 version
refresh, but no source says so).

## Why this matters for the roster

- Lyria 3 falls inside the roster's one-year window (on or after 2025-10-26) with five months to
  spare.
- Lyria 2's dating is ambiguous, but it is a predecessor, not the row subject.

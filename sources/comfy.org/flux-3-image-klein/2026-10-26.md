# Comfy.org: FLUX 3 Image Klein model page and FAQ

- Origin: Comfy Org, "FLUX 3 Image Klein Launch", https://comfy.org/flux-3-image-klein
  ("Updated October 2026")
- Evidence kind: third-party ecosystem vendor documentation (host/integration, restating BFL
  material plus its own licence reading)
- Publication date: "Updated October 2026" on the page; no day given
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: FLUX 3 Image Klein (base and distilled), FLUX 3 Image (via the Partner Node)

## Captured content — verbatim

Header framing:

> Black Forest Labs' newest open-weight model comes in two variants: standard and step-distilled
> turbo. Both generate and edit images. Create an image with standard, or iterate fast with turbo.

Variant naming (FAQ):

> FLUX 3 Image Klein ships as an undistilled base checkpoint for fine-tuning, further training,
> and research, and as a distilled checkpoint for faster generation. Both come with INT8, FP8 and
> NVFP4 quantized builds that use less memory. All of them run on the same ComfyUI loaders.

Identity vs the hosted parent (FAQ):

> No. The FLUX 3 Image Partner Node calls Black Forest Labs' hosted API and runs the larger FLUX 3
> Image model. FLUX 3 Image Klein is the open-weights model you download and run yourself, locally
> or on Comfy Cloud. Both are available in ComfyUI.

Licence reading (FAQ):

> FLUX 3 Image Klein is released under the FLUX Kommunity License, which allows free non-commercial
> use. If you and your affiliates have under US$5M in annual revenue, you can also use the images
> you generate commercially, provided you filter or review outputs and disclose AI generation where
> the law requires it. Any other commercial use, **including commercial fine-tunes**, needs a
> license from Black Forest Labs at bfl.ai/licensing.

> Yes. The undistilled base checkpoint of FLUX 3 Image Klein is built for fine-tuning and further
> training, and LoRAs trained on it load through ComfyUI's standard LoRA loader. Under the FLUX
> Kommunity License, fine-tuning for commercial use requires a separate license from Black Forest
> Labs.

Comparison to FLUX.2 [klein] (FAQ):

> FLUX 3 Image Klein improves on FLUX.2 [klein] 9B in Black Forest Labs' internal text-to-image
> evaluations while using fewer parameters, 5.6B against 9B. It also brings over control features
> from the larger FLUX 3 Image model, including bounding boxes, and generates natively at 4K.

4K claim (FAQ):

> Yes. FLUX 3 Image Klein generates natively at 4K rather than upscaling to it. Fine detail holds
> up when you zoom in, crop, or print.

Prompt upsampler in ComfyUI:

> The prompt upsampler is a separate model that expands a short prompt into a detailed one before
> FLUX 3 Image Klein generates. In ComfyUI it loads through Load CLIP with type set to flux3.

## What this establishes

- A third party documents the same two-checkpoint structure and the same three quantized builds,
  and distinguishes the open-weights model from the hosted FLUX 3 Image that the "Partner Node"
  calls — an independent statement of the parent/child relationship.
- **Variant naming varies by ecosystem**: BFL says "Base" and "Distilled"; Comfy says "standard"
  and "turbo". Neither is wrong; the same two checkpoints are meant.
- Comfy's licence reading matches the Hugging Face model card (commercial fine-tunes need a BFL
  licence) and **contradicts the looser "free commercial use" phrasing in BFL's launch post**.
  Two independent restatements of the narrow reading against one vendor sentence of the broad one.
- "Natively at 4K rather than upscaling" is asserted here and in BFL's launch post, but neither
  source gives the generation pipeline for the 4K tier.

## Gaps

- This is a host/integration page restating BFL material; it is a host record, not a measurement.
  It publishes no benchmark, no VRAM figure, and no latency number.
- "Free" badges on the gallery examples refer to Comfy Cloud run cost, not to licence terms.
- The claim that ComfyUI's `Load CLIP` with type `flux3` loads the upsampler is integration
  detail, not evidence about model behaviour.

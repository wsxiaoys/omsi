# Creatify API: AI Avatar (v1/v2) and Custom Avatar — how they differ from Aurora

- Origin: Creatify API docs, three pages:
  - "Overview (v1)" — AI Avatar, <https://docs.creatify.ai/api-documentation/ai-avatar/lipsync>
  - "Overview (v2)" — AI Avatar v2, <https://docs.creatify.ai/api-documentation/ai-avatar/lipsync-v2>
  - "Custom Avatar API", <https://docs.creatify.ai/api-documentation/custom-avatar/byoa>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor API docs, first-party)
- Publication date: not stated per page; AI Avatar pages carry Mintlify `lastModified` 2026-02-05
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: AI Avatar v1/v2 (library personas), Custom Avatar (BYOA), Aurora as a selectable
  `model_version`

## Verbatim (selected)

AI Avatar v1:

> The **AI Avatar API** enables you to generate realistic, high-quality videos of virtual people speaking
> from any text or audio input.

> We offer **1500+ lifelike personas** with diverse styles and expressions to match your use case.

> Use the `id` of the selected persona as the `creator` parameter when generating your video.

Its example request sets `"model_version": "aurora_v1_fast"` — i.e. the library-persona endpoint can be
rendered by an Aurora tier.

AI Avatar v2:

> Generate vivid, personalized videos featuring multiple scenes — each with its own avatar, voice,
> background, and styling — using a structured list of inputs.

Custom Avatar (BYOA):

> With **Custom Avatar**, you can upload your own consent and lipsync videos to create a personalized
> speaking avatar within the Creatify platform.

Required fields include `lipsync_input` (an MP4 "used for lipsync training"), `creator_name`, `gender`,
`video_scene`. The consent video is described in prose ("submit URLs to your consent and lipsync videos",
"upload your own consent and lipsync videos") but no dedicated consent parameter is documented. New avatars
are reviewed before use: `"is_active": false` means "Avatar is still under review by Creatify", and
"Reviews are typically completed within 24 hours".

## Summary

Creatify's avatar surface is three distinct things, and only one of them is Aurora:

| Product | Input | What the model does | Consent step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Aurora** (`/api/aurora/`) | one still image + audio | animates the still into a talking/singing video | none documented |
| **AI Avatar v1/v2** (`/api/lipsyncs/`, `/api/lipsyncs_v2/`) | a library persona ID (1500+) + text or audio | renders a library character; `model_version` may select `standard` or an Aurora tier | not applicable (library persona) |
| **Custom Avatar** (`/api/personas/`, `/api/personas_v2/`) | uploaded consent and lip-sync videos | trains a personal avatar for later use | consent video described in prose; new avatars go through Creatify review before activation |

Aurora is therefore both a standalone model and a renderer selectable inside the AI Avatar endpoints. The
changelog dates confirm the order: `aurora_v1` first appeared as an AI Avatar `model_version` in September
2025, and the standalone `/api/aurora/` endpoint is not announced in any entry captured.

The consent asymmetry is the material difference for safety: a trained Custom Avatar is described as needing a
consent video and is reviewed by Creatify before it becomes active, while Aurora accepts any still photo with
no documented verification and no review step.

## Gaps

- No page explains why a consent video is required for Custom Avatar but not for Aurora.
- The AI Avatar pages do not list which `model_version` values are currently valid beyond `standard`,
  `aurora_v1`, `aurora_v1_fast` (and `boreal`, added September 2026 per the changelog).
- No statement of whether AI Avatar library personas are themselves Aurora renders or a separate model.

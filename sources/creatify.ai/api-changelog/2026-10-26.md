- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor changelog, first-party)
- Publication date: rolling page; latest section "October 2026". Mintlify `lastModified`

- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Aurora 1.0 (`aurora_v1`), Aurora 1.0 Fast (`aurora_v1_fast`), Aurora 2.0

## Aurora-relevant entries, verbatim

### October 2026 — Added

> **Aurora 2.0 on the Aurora page**: `model_version: "aurora_v2"` renders at `480p`, `720p` (default),
> `1080p` or `2k` for 0.4, 0.7, 1.4 or 2.8 credits per second, from up to 59.7 seconds of audio. The page
> now lists every request parameter.

### May 2026 — Changed

> **Aurora pricing reduced**
> * `aurora_v1`: now **1 credit per second** (previously 20 credits per 15-second video).
> * `aurora_v1_fast`: now **0.5 credits per second** (previously 10 credits per 15-second video).
> * Billing is now per-second (partial seconds round up to the next whole second), replacing the
>   previous 15-second increments.

### October 2025 — Added

> **`aurora_v1_fast` option** to **`model_version` field** in **aurora** endpoints
> * Fast version of the aurora_v1 model that maintains high generation quality with faster processing.
> * **Cost:** 10 credits per 15-second video.
> * This option is now available alongside existing `standard` and `aurora_v1` choices.

### September 2025 — Added

> **`model_version` field** in both **AI Avatar v1** and **AI Avatar v2** endpoints
> * New optional parameter to specify which model powers the AI Avatar generation.
> * **Choices:**
>   * `standard` – Default. 5 credits per 30-second video.
>   * `aurora_v1` – Higher-quality Aurora model. 20 credits per 15-second video.

### September 2026 — Added (Boreal, for contrast)

> **Boreal API**: `POST /api/boreal/` generates a video from a prompt, a start image or an audio track.

> **Boreal-H3 on the Boreal API**: `model: "boreal-h3"` on `POST /api/boreal/` renders with Boreal-H3,
> Creatify's post-trained H3 model. It takes an optional closing frame (`end_image_url`) or reference
> media … and renders 5 to 15 seconds at 768p, 1088p or 2k.

## Summary

The changelog is the only first-party dated trail for Aurora, and it never announces an Aurora launch:

| Date | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 2025-09 (month only) | `aurora_v1` becomes a selectable `model_version` on the AI Avatar v1/v2 endpoints, 20 credits / 15 s |
| 2025-10 (month only) | `aurora_v1_fast` added, 10 credits / 15 s |
| 2026-05 (month only) | Per-second billing replaces 15-second increments; 1 and 0.5 credits per second |
| 2026-10 (month only) | Aurora 2.0 documented on the Aurora page at four resolutions |

The September 2025 entry describes Aurora as already existing and "higher-quality", i.e. it predates the
changelog entry. No entry announces a launch date, an architecture change, or the release of the
standalone `/api/aurora/` endpoint.

## Gaps

- No day-level dates anywhere; every entry is month-granular.
- The standalone `/api/aurora/` endpoint is not announced in any entry; only the `model_version` option
  on the AI Avatar endpoints is. When the dedicated endpoint appeared is unknown.
- Aurora 2.0's "October 2026" entry documents the model on the docs page; it does not say when the model
  itself became available.
- No deprecation notices for any Aurora tier.

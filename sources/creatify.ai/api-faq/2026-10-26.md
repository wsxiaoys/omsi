# Creatify API: FAQ (Aurora entries)

- Origin: Creatify, "Creatify API FAQ", <https://docs.creatify.ai/faq>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor FAQ, first-party)
- Publication date: not stated on the page
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Aurora (version not named), AI Avatar, and other Creatify APIs

## Verbatim (Aurora entries)

> ## What is Aurora?
>
> Still image → speaking / blinking / subtle expression animation.
>
> **Great for:** product spokespeople, legacy photos, stylized mascots.

> ## Do pets or stylized images work in Aurora?
>
> Yes, if facial landmarks are discernible.
>
> Checklist: good lighting, frontal or near‑frontal angle, minimal motion blur.

The FAQ also lists "Aurora image‑to‑video transformation" as one of the platform's separate capabilities,
alongside "AI avatar lip synchronization" and "Custom template" — i.e. Aurora is described as its own
capability, not as a setting on the avatar API.

## Summary

The FAQ is the only vendor page that gives input guidance for still images — frontal or near-frontal, well
lit, minimal motion blur, discernible facial landmarks — and the only one that says non-human subjects
(pets) and stylized art are supported. That guidance is advisory ("Great for"), not an enforced constraint:
the API accepts any png/jpg/webp within its size rules.

It also makes the sharpest short statement of what Aurora is: a still image turned into speaking, blinking,
subtle-expression animation.

## Gaps

- No version, resolution, duration, price, or frame-rate information.
- "Facial landmarks are discernible" is not quantified and no rejection behaviour is described.
- No statement on multiple people in frame, profile-only portraits, or full-body images.

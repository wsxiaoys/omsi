## Verbatim (selected)

> Aurora 1.0 (`aurora_v1`) is the default when `model_version` is omitted. Aurora 1.0 Fast
> (`aurora_v1_fast`) renders faster at half the price, at a lower resolution: Aurora 1.0 renders 720p,
> and our 9:16 example from Aurora 1.0 Fast came out 448 × 832. Both take up to 5 minutes of audio.

> Choose Aurora 1.0 for audio longer than Aurora 2.0's 59.7 seconds. For shorter clips, Aurora 2.0 gives
> higher quality and up to 2k.

## Pricing (verbatim table)

| Model | Output | Credits per second | Audio length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aurora 1.0 (`aurora_v1`) | 720p | 1 | Up to 5 minutes |
| Aurora 1.0 Fast (`aurora_v1_fast`) | Below 720p, faster | 0.5 | Up to 5 minutes |

Worked example: a 10.4-second audio billed as 11 seconds costs "11 credits with Aurora 1.0, or 5.5
credits with Aurora 1.0 Fast".

## Inputs and limits (verbatim table)

| Input | Aurora 1.0 rule |
| --- | --- |
| `image` | `png`, `jpg`, `jpeg` or `webp`. |
| `audio` | `mp3` or `wav`, up to 5 minutes. The video is as long as the audio. |
| `resolution` | "Not accepted: sending it with `aurora_v1` or `aurora_v1_fast` returns a 400 error. Only Aurora 2.0 takes a resolution." |
| `text_prompt` | Optional. Describes the style, scene, expression or motion. |
| `prompt_guidance_scale` | How closely to follow `text_prompt`, `0.0` to `10.0`. Default `1.0`. |

## Summary

Aurora 1.0 is the default tier: fixed 720p, audio up to 5 minutes, 1 credit per audio second. Aurora 1.0
Fast is the same model at half the price, "below 720p" — the only concrete figure the docs give is the
9:16 example at 448 × 832 (i.e. not a standard 480p frame). Neither tier accepts a `resolution`
parameter.

## Gaps

## Verbatim (selected)

> The highest-quality Aurora model: a talking video from one photo and up to 59.7 seconds of audio, at
> up to 2k.

> Set `model_version` to `aurora_v2` on `POST /api/aurora/`. Aurora 2.0 renders at `480p`, `720p`
> (default), `1080p` or `2k`, priced by resolution.

## Pricing (verbatim table)

| Resolution | Credits per second | Audio length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `480p` | 0.4 | Up to 59.7 seconds |
| `720p` (default) | 0.7 | Up to 59.7 seconds |
| `1080p` | 1.4 | Up to 59.7 seconds |
| `2k` | 2.8 | Up to 14.375 seconds |

> Billing is per second of audio, rounded up to the next whole second. For example, the 10.4-second
> audio in the example request is billed as 11 seconds: 30.8 credits at `2k`.

## Inputs and limits (verbatim table)

| Input | Aurora 2.0 rule |
| --- | --- |
| `image` | `png`, `jpg`, `jpeg` or `webp`; 256 to 5760 pixels on each side; aspect ratio between 1:4 and 4:1; up to 30 MB. |
| `audio` | `mp3` or `wav`, from 1 to 59.7 seconds. The video is as long as the audio. |
| `resolution` | `480p`, `720p` (default), `1080p` or `2k`. `2k` takes audio up to 14.375 seconds; use `1080p` for longer clips. |
| `text_prompt` | Optional. Describes the style, scene, expression or motion. |
| `prompt_guidance_scale` | Ignored by Aurora 2.0. |

The page shows two rendered examples from the same 10.4-second audio at `2k`, 30.8 credits each: a 9:16
car selfie and a 16:9 city-street frame. Both are rendered from different still images, so the output
aspect ratio appears to follow the input image; the page does not state a rule.

## Summary

Aurora 2.0 is the highest-quality tier, resolution-selectable across four settings, capped at 59.7 s of
audio (14.375 s at 2k). It is documented as a `model_version` on the same `/api/aurora/` endpoint rather
than a separate endpoint, and it ignores the older `prompt_guidance_scale` control.

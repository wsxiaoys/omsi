- Relevant models: Aurora 1.0 (`aurora_v1`), Aurora 1.0 Fast (`aurora_v1_fast`), Aurora 2.0 (`aurora_v2`)

## Verbatim (selected)

> The **Aurora API** enables you to create **studio-quality, avatar-based videos** from just a **single
> photo** (real or AI-generated) and an **audio clip** (speech or song).
> Powered by a state-of-the-art image-to-avatar model, Aurora generates lifelike videos where the avatar
> **blinks, speaks, gestures, and emotes** as if it were real.

> One endpoint, `POST /api/aurora/`, serves every Aurora model. Pick one with `model_version`.

| Model | `model_version` | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aurora 2.0 | `aurora_v2` | The highest quality, rendered at 480p, 720p, 1080p or 2k, from up to 59.7 seconds of audio. |
| Aurora 1.0 | `aurora_v1` (default) | Long audio, up to 5 minutes, at 720p. |
| Aurora 1.0 Fast | `aurora_v1_fast` | Faster, lower-cost renders of long audio, below 720p. |

## Parameters (transcribed)

| Parameter | Required | Documented rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `image` | Yes | `png`, `jpg`, `jpeg` or `webp`. For `aurora_v2`, 256–5760 px per side, aspect ratio 1:4–4:1, ≤ 30 MB. |
| `audio` | Yes | `mp3` or `wav`. Up to 5 minutes, or 59.7 s for `aurora_v2`. "The video is as long as the audio." |
| `model_version` | No | `aurora_v1` (default), `aurora_v1_fast`, `aurora_v2`. |
| `resolution` | No | `aurora_v2` only: `480p`, `720p` (default), `1080p`, `2k`. `2k` takes audio up to 14.375 s. "Sending `resolution` with `aurora_v1` or `aurora_v1_fast` returns a 400 error." |
| `text_prompt` | No | "Describes the style, scene, camera, lighting, expression or motion you want." |
| `prompt_guidance_scale` | No | 0.0–10.0, default 1.0. "Ignored by `aurora_v2`." |
| `name` | No | Video name. |
| `webhook_url` | No | POST callback on completion. |

Endpoints: `POST /api/aurora/` to create, `GET /api/aurora/{id}/` to poll. Auth headers `X-API-ID` and
`X-API-KEY`.

## Pricing, limits and retention (verbatim table)

> Billing is per second of audio, rounded up to the next whole second.

| `model_version` | Output | Credits per second | Audio length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `aurora_v1` (Aurora 1.0, default) | 720p | 1 | Up to 5 minutes |
| `aurora_v1_fast` (Aurora 1.0 Fast) | Below 720p, faster | 0.5 | Up to 5 minutes |
| `aurora_v2` (Aurora 2.0) | `480p`, `720p` (default), `1080p` or `2k` | 0.4, 0.7, 1.4 or 2.8 | Up to 59.7 seconds; up to 14.375 seconds at `2k` |

Worked example from the page: "the 10.4-second audio in the request above is billed as 11 seconds: 5.5
credits with `aurora_v1_fast`, or 15.4 credits with `aurora_v2` at `1080p`."

- Failed videos: `status` becomes `failed`, `failed_reason` explains why, "its credits are refunded
  automatically". Insufficient balance is refused with "Not enough credits" and nothing is charged.
- Retention: "Generated videos stay available for at least 7 days after the task finishes."

## Summary

The Aurora family is documented as one endpoint with a `model_version` switch over three models.
Inputs are a single still image plus an audio file; the output video length equals the audio length.
Resolution is selectable only on Aurora 2.0. Pricing is per second of audio, rounded up.

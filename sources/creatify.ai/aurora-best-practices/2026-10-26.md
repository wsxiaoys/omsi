# Creatify: Aurora best practices (vendor usage guide)

- Origin: Creatify, "Aurora best practices" (blog), <https://creatify.ai/blog/aurora-best-practices>,
  Creatify Team
- Evidence kind: official guidance (vendor usage guide, first-party)
- Publication date: **2026-03-06** (byline "Mar 6, 2026")
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Aurora (version not named; the guidance predates the documented Aurora 2.0 page)

## Verbatim (selected)

> Aurora is Creatify's proprietary diffusion transformer (DiT) model for audio-driven avatar synthesis.
> Give it one photo and an audio clip, and it generates a studio-quality video of that person speaking,
> presenting, or singing - with synchronized facial expressions, natural eye movements, breathing, and
> full upper-body gestures.

> The model has already been integrated into ElevenLabs, Runware and fal.ai as one of the first video
> generation models.

> **2. Use Voice Model V3**
> This is non-negotiable for quality results. Voice Model V3 delivers the most accurate lip sync and the
> widest expressive range. Older voice models produce noticeably worse output.

> **4. Dial in prompt_guidance**
> Aurora has a prompt_guidance parameter ranging from 0 to 4. It controls how strictly the model follows
> your prompt versus allowing natural variation.

> Start at 1 for most scenes. … Increase it if the avatar drifts off-prompt or doesn't follow the
> behavioral cues you set. Decrease it if the performance feels stiff or mechanical.

> The subject should be clearly visible and distinguishable in the frame … There are no strict limitations
> on angle, lighting, or composition. Aurora adjusts dynamically.

Base prompt recommended by the vendor, identical to the `prompt` example in fal's schema:

> 4K studio interview, medium close-up (shoulders-up crop). Solid light-grey seamless backdrop, uniform
> soft key-light - no lighting change. Presenter faces lens, steady eye-contact. Hands remain below frame,
> body perfectly still. Ultra-sharp.

Troubleshooting table (transcribed): "Lip sync feels off → Slow down voice speed slightly"; "Movement
looks unnatural → Try a different image with a cleaner pose"; "Avatar drifts off-prompt → Increase
prompt_guidance"; "Performance feels too robotic → Lower prompt_guidance".

## Summary

This is the only captured document that describes how the model is actually driven: the prompt controls
performance and staging (not speech), guidance trades adherence against naturalness, and the vendor's own
recommended mitigation for poor lip sync is to slow the audio down — i.e. sync quality degrades with
speech rate.

**Contradiction on the guidance range:** this post says `prompt_guidance` runs "from 0 to 4", while the
API reference documents `prompt_guidance_scale` as 0.0–10.0 (default 1.0) and fal's `guidance_scale`
caps at 5. Three different ceilings for what appears to be one control. The post may describe an older or
app-side range; nothing captured reconciles them.

**Scope caution:** "Use Voice Model V3" is guidance for Creatify's own TTS, not a property of the model.
A caller supplying their own audio bypasses it.

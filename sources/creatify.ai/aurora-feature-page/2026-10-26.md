## Summary

The app product page frames Aurora as the renderer behind Creatify's Avatar Video feature, with voice/script
input in front of it. Two of its statements conflict with the model's own documentation:

1. **Credit rate.** The page says "5 credits for every 30 seconds of video" as a general app rate, while the
   API prices Aurora at 1 credit per second (1.0) or 0.4–2.8 credits per second (2.0). At 5 credits per 30 s
   Aurora 1.0 would cost a tenth of its documented API price. The two credit systems (app plan vs API
   subscription) are not reconciled by any captured page; treat the app FAQ as describing app credit
   consumption, not the model's price.
2. **Languages.** This page lists 29 languages; the Aurora blog article claims "all 75+ languages Creatify
   supports". Both are Creatify statements about the same product.

It also adds the only captured statement about watermarking, and only for the free plan's exports — not for
API output.

## Gaps

- No model version, resolution, duration, or frame rate.
- The FAQ is generic to the app and mostly not Aurora-specific.
- No commercial terms specific to the API route; "you own all of your creatives" is an app FAQ line, while
  the binding statement is in the Terms of Service.

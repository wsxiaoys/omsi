# Creatify: Introducing the Aurora Model (launch announcement)

- Origin: Creatify, "Introducing the Aurora Model: Audio-Driven Ultra-Realistic Rendering of Reactive
  Avatars", <https://creatify.ai/introducing-aurora>; plus the same announcement re-published as a blog

  (`datePublished` 2025-09-03, `dateModified` 2026-10-23)
- Evidence kind: official announcement (vendor marketing, first-party)
- Publication date: **2025-09-03**. The blog rendition carries `datePublished: 2025-09-03`; the Internet
  Archive's earliest capture of `/introducing-aurora` is `20250903170214` (CDX API), so the page existed

- Capture date: 2026-10-26

## Verbatim (announcement page)

> State-of-the-art diffusion transformer (DiT) model designed specifically for creating professional
> studio-grade, avatar-based video ads — available today on Creatify.

> Give Aurora a single photo of a person (real or AI-generated) and an audio clip of speech or song, and
> it will generate a high-fidelity, studio-quality video of that person speaking or singing.

> At the core of Aurora is a diffusion-based multimodal foundation model purpose-built for generative
> avatar synthesis. We employ a novel architecture that includes an image encoder, a text encoder, and an
> audio encoder to process information from different modalities. We fuse all this information together
> to generate an avatar with motions that align with the audio and text input.

> The key difference between Aurora and prior lip-sync models is that Aurora doesn't just lip-sync – it
> brings full human expressiveness into the digital avatar.

The page closes with an "GET EARLY ACCESS" call to action, which sits awkwardly beside "available today
on Creatify"; the two statements are not reconciled.

## Verbatim (blog rendition, marketing claims)

> Aurora is Creatify's proprietary image-to-video AI avatar model.

> The traditional route for professional spokesperson videos costs $3,000-$15,000 per actor with 2-4
> week timelines. Aurora creates comparable quality in 10 minutes for under $4.

> Aurora generates lip sync at 24fps with emotional awareness.

> Aurora works with all 75+ languages Creatify supports. The lip sync automatically adjusts to match the
> selected language's phonetics and mouth shapes.

> Aurora supports long-form audio - several minutes of continuous speech or singing while maintaining
> character consistency and visual quality throughout.

> Integration: Aurora powers Creatify's AI Avatar feature and integrates with URL-to-Video, Batch Mode,
> and Asset Generator.

## Summary

Aurora was announced on 2025-09-03 as Creatify's in-house diffusion-transformer avatar model: one photo
plus audio in, talking/singing video out, with the vendor's differentiator being whole-upper-body
expressiveness rather than mouth-only lip sync. The announcement claims a three-encoder (image, text,
audio) architecture. The blog rendition adds quantitative claims — 24 fps, 75+ languages, "under $4" per
clip — that no API document repeats.

Two dating cautions:

1. The blog rendition was modified on 2026-10-23 and its body references Asset Generator and Batch Mode,
   which post-date 2025-09. Its `datePublished` may therefore describe the article's original slot rather
   than the text as captured. The 2025-09-03 archive capture of `/introducing-aurora` is the stronger
   dating evidence for the announcement itself.
2. The announcement predates the roster's one-year window (2025-10-26) by roughly seven weeks.

## Gaps

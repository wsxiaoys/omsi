the intellectual property or other proprietary rights of a third party including rights of publicity,

## Summary

Creatify assigns copyright in the user's generated Assets to the user, on condition of compliance with the
terms. Two restrictions matter for Aurora users: outputs may not be used to train AI models, and the user
bears all responsibility for rights, including publicity rights, in the uploaded photo and audio. There is
no separate likeness-consent warranty for uploading a photo of a real person beyond the general "owns or
holds a valid license to any content … that it uploads" representation and the indemnity clause.

## Gaps

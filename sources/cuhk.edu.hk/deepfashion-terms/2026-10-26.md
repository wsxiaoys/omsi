- Origin: Multimedia Laboratory, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, "DeepFashion" project page,
  https://mmlab.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/projects/DeepFashion.html
- Evidence kind: dataset publisher's terms of use (third-party, not the model's terms)
- Publication date: undated page; dataset paper CVPR 2016

## Why this record exists

Leffa's README states the pose transfer checkpoint is "trained on DeepFashion". The released weights
are declared MIT while the dataset is non-commercial research only. This record preserves the dataset
wording for the model page's licensing section.

## Gaps

- Only the public project page was read; the signed "DeepFashion dataset Release Agreement" that the
  page requires for download was not captured.

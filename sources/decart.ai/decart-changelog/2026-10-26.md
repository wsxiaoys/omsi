- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `lucy-2.1-vton`, `lucy-vton-2`, `lucy-vton-3`, `lucy-vton-3.5`,
  `lucy-vton-3.6`; base `lucy-2`, `lucy-2.1`, `lucy-2.5`, `lucy-2.6`

## Dating caveat

Entries are grouped under month headings ("April 2026", "May 2026", …). **No entry carries a
day-level date**, and no `<time>` element or ISO date is present in the page. Every release date
taken from this record is therefore month-granular.

## Virtual try-on line, in order

- **April 2026 — launch.** "Virtual Try-On - Launched `lucy-2.1-vton` for realtime and batch virtual
  try-on, with a new e-commerce integration guide". The same section announces Lucy 2.1
  (`lucy-2.1`) as "New flagship realtime and batch video editing model".
- **May 2026 — renumbering starts.** "Lucy VTON 2 - New next-generation virtual try-on model
  (`lucy-vton-2`) available on realtime and batch surfaces. `lucy-vton-latest` now resolves to
  `lucy-vton-2`". The Lucy 2.1 name is dropped from the try-on model ID after one month.
- **June 2026 — VTON 3, and 2.1 retirement.** "Lucy VTON 3 - New latest-generation virtual try-on
  model (`lucy-vton-3`) available on realtime and batch surfaces. `lucy-vton-latest` now resolves to
  `lucy-vton-3`. The previous `lucy-vton-2` remains live for pinned integrations." In the same
  section: "Lucy 2.1 VTON retirement - `lucy-2.1-vton` and its `lucy-vton` alias are no longer
  served. Use `lucy-vton-3`, `lucy-vton-latest`, or pin to `lucy-vton-2` if you need the previous
  generation".
- **August 2026 — VTON 3.5.** "Lucy VTON 3.5 - New latest-generation virtual try-on model
  (`lucy-vton-3.5`) available on realtime and batch surfaces, at 720p. `lucy-vton-latest` now
  resolves to `lucy-vton-3.5`."
- **September 2026 — 2 and 3 retired, fast mode added.** "Lucy VTON 2 and 3 retirement -
  `lucy-vton-2` and `lucy-vton-3` (and the `lucy-2.1-vton-2` alias) are retired and no longer
  served. Use `lucy-vton-3.5`, or `lucy-vton-latest` which resolves to it." Fast mode: "Pass
  `speed: "fast"` when connecting to `lucy-2.5` / `lucy-latest` or `lucy-vton-3.5` /
  `lucy-vton-latest` … Billed at 2x the standard realtime rate ($0.04/sec)."
- **October 2026 — VTON 3.6, then 3.6 as latest.** Two entries in one month: "Lucy VTON 3.6 - New
  virtual try-on model (`lucy-vton-3.6`) available on realtime and batch surfaces, at 720p. It is
  opt-in by name: `lucy-vton-latest` continues to resolve to `lucy-vton-3.5`. Unlike 3.5,
  `lucy-vton-3.6` does not offer fast mode — `speed: "fast"` is ignored and the session is billed at
  the standard rate." Then: "Lucy VTON 3.6 is now the latest, with fast mode - `lucy-vton-latest`
  now resolves to `lucy-vton-3.6`, and 3.6 now supports fast mode … billed at 2x the standard
  realtime rate, $0.04/sec". The same section adds "One API hostname - `api.decart.ai` now serves
  realtime signaling (WebSocket) as well as the REST API".

## Base Lucy 2 line, in order

- **January 2026.** "Lucy 2 RT (Preview) - Early access to Lucy 2 realtime at 720p@20FPS".
- **February 2026.** "Lucy 2 Realtime - Launched Lucy 2 (`lucy-2`), a new realtime video-to-video
  model with character reference image support". Also "Unified `set()` method", "Auto-reconnect",
  "generationTick event - Track session duration and billing".
- **April 2026.** "Lucy 2.1 - New flagship realtime and batch video editing model (`lucy-2.1`)."
- **May 2026.** "1080p realtime streams - New resolution option on `realtime.connect()` lets
  supported models deliver 1080p output … Defaults to 720p when omitted." No statement that VTON
  models are supported.
- **July 2026.** "Lucy 2.5 - New latest-generation video editing model (`lucy-2.5`) … The previous
  `lucy-2.1` remains live for pinned integrations."
- **October 2026.** "Lucy 2.6 - New latest-generation video editing model (`lucy-2.6`) … at 720p.
  `lucy-latest` now resolves to `lucy-2.6`; `lucy-2.5` remains available by name."

## Other entries relevant to hosting and safety

- **May 2026.** "Realtime SDKs now LiveKit-backed - The realtime media transport in the JavaScript
  (v0.1.0) and Android (0.7.0) SDKs moved to a LiveKit-managed WebRTC session".
- **November 2025.** "Improved error messages when content violates Terms of Service" and
  "Updated video pricing for 720p resolution".
- **May 2026.** "Model retirements - LipSync Live, Live Avatar, Lucy Motion, Lucy v1 Realtime, and
  Lucy Restyle v1 Realtime are no longer served by the API."

## Gaps

- Month-only dates; the exact launch day of `lucy-2.1-vton` is not published here.
- The changelog never states frame rate, latency, or quality differences for any VTON version
  beyond "next-generation"/"latest-generation" phrasing.
- No entry explains why `lucy-2.1-vton` was retired only two months after launch, or what 3.x
  changed relative to it.

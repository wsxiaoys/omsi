# Decart docs: Models (catalogue, IDs, aliases)

- Origin: Decart API Platform documentation, "Models" —
  <https://docs.platform.decart.ai/getting-started/models>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor model catalogue)
- Publication date: not stated; the page names `lucy-2.6` as latest, so on or after October 2026
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: whole Lucy family, with `lucy-vton-3.6` / `lucy-vton-3.5` as the try-on pair

## Realtime models table (verbatim)

| Model | ID | Resolution | Use Case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lucy 2.6 | `lucy-2.6` | 720p | Video editing (latest) |
| Lucy 2.5 | `lucy-2.5` | 720p | Video editing |
| Lucy VTON 3.6 | `lucy-vton-3.6` | 720p | Virtual try-on (latest) |
| Lucy VTON 3.5 | `lucy-vton-3.5` | 720p | Virtual try-on |
| Lucy Restyle 2 | `lucy-restyle-2` | 720p | Video restyling |
| Oasis 3 Preview | `oasis-3-preview` | 768×512 | Realtime world model / driving simulator |

Previous generation: "Lucy 2.1 | `lucy-2.1` | 720p | Video editing". The April 2026 try-on model
`lucy-2.1-vton` is **not** in this table and is not listed as a previous-generation model either.

## Video models table

Same five Lucy rows at 720p for `lucy-2.6`, `lucy-2.5`, `lucy-vton-3.6`, `lucy-vton-3.5`,
`lucy-restyle-2`, plus `lucy-2.1` under "Previous generation models". Image models: "Lucy Image 2 |
`lucy-image-2` | 480p, 720p | Image editing".

## Aliases (verbatim)

| Alias | Type | Currently resolves to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lucy-latest` | realtime + video | `lucy-2.6` |
| `lucy-vton-latest` | realtime + video | `lucy-vton-3.6` |
| `lucy-restyle-latest` | realtime + video | `lucy-restyle-2` |
| `lucy-image-latest` | image | `lucy-image-2` |

There is **no** `lucy-2.1-vton` alias, no `lucy-vton` alias, and no version pin for any try-on model
other than 3.5 and 3.6.

## Shared video requirements

> All video models accept MP4 files with these specifications:
> * **Format:** MP4 (H.264 or VP8 codec)
> * **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 or 9:16
> * **File Size:** Maximum 200MB

(The VTON guide adds the 2:1 / 1:2 shapes and the exact output dimensions; this catalogue page does
not.)

## Decision matrix

The page's "Choosing the Right Model" matrix routes live editing to Lucy 2.6, style to Lucy Restyle
2, existing-video editing to Lucy 2.6, image editing to Lucy Image 2 and world simulation to Oasis 3
Preview. **Virtual try-on is not in the decision matrix** even though two VTON rows are in both
model tables.

## Gaps

- The catalogue gives no release dates, no frame rates and no latency figures.
- Nothing maps the try-on version numbers (3.5 / 3.6) to the Lucy 2.x editing version numbers
  (2.1 / 2.5 / 2.6); the two lines are numbered independently.
- No explanation for why virtual try-on is absent from the decision matrix.

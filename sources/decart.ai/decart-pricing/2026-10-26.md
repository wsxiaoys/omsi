## Billing basis

> Decart uses pay-as-you-go pricing. No subscriptions and no minimum spend. You pay only for usage.
>
> * Realtime models: billed per second of active generation
> * Video models: billed per generated second
> * Image models: billed per generation

FAQ on the same page: "Video jobs are billed per generated second. Realtime is billed per active
generation time."

## Cost examples (verbatim)

> 5-second video edit with `lucy-2.6` (720p): 5 x $0.04 = $0.20
> 30-second realtime Lucy 2.6 session: 30 x $0.02 = $0.60
> 30-second realtime Lucy 2.6 session in fast mode (`speed: "fast"`): 30 x $0.04 = $1.20
> One 720p image edit with `lucy-image-2`: $0.02
> 60-second Oasis 3 Preview session: 60 x $0.02 = $1.20

No VTON example is given, but the per-second rates are identical to the editing models.

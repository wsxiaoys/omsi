# Decart.ai: Terms of Service (output ownership, training use)

- Origin: Decart AI, "Terms of Service" — <https://decart.ai/terms> (also published in the API docs
  at <https://docs.platform.decart.ai/resources/terms-of-service>; the two captures are the same
  text)
- Evidence kind: provider policy
- Publication date: not stated; page footer reads "Decart.ai 2026"
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: all Decart models, including Lucy VTON

## Output ownership (verbatim)

> As between the parties and to the extent permitted by applicable law, you own your Input. Subject
> to your compliance with these Terms, Decart.ai hereby assigns to you all its right, title and
> interest in and to Output. This means you can use your Content for any purpose, including
> commercial purposes such as sale or publication, but only if you comply with these Terms at all
> times. Decart.ai may use Content to develop, improve (including to train our AI models), provide
> and maintain the Platform and in furthering its business, comply with applicable law, defend and
> enforce our rights or the rights of other users, and enforce our policies.

Two facts sit in the same paragraph: the user gets the output and may use it commercially, and
Decart reserves the right to train on the user's content.

## Other relevant clauses

- "Materials are provided to you for your information and you may only use them for your personal
  non-commercial use only" — this restricts Decart's own marketing materials, not generated output.
- Disputes: arbitration in New York City under AAA Commercial Rules, with New York courts for
  appeals.

## Gaps

- The Terms do not state a retention period for inputs or outputs, nor a deletion mechanism.
- No model-specific licence exists for Lucy models; nothing in the Terms grants or withholds model
  weights, because no weights are distributed.
- The Terms do not mention virtual try-on, garment images, or likeness rights.

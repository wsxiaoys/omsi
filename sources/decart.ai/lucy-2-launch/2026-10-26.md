# Decart.ai: "Introducing Lucy 2" launch post

- Origin: Decart AI publications, "Introducing Lucy 2: SOTA realtime world transformation model" —
  <https://decart.ai/publications/lucy-2-introducing-sota-video-generation-in-realtime>
- Evidence kind: official launch announcement (vendor)
- Publication date: 2026-01-26 (dateline on the page)
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Lucy 2.0 (base). No try-on model is named.

## Identity statements

> Today, we're releasing Lucy 2.0 — a real-time world transformation model that shifts
> high-fidelity video editing from offline rendering to live interaction. This opens up an unlimited
> set of possibilities ranging from character swaps and product placement to high-fidelity real-time
> data augmentation and simulation for robotics.

> Lucy 2.0 builds on the foundations of MirageLSD and Lucy Edit 1.0, but it is not simply a
> higher-quality video generator. It is a live system capable of transforming the visual world with
> high fidelity at 30fps 1080p resolution with near-zero latency.

> Lucy 2.0 allows character swaps, motion control, product placement, clothing changes, and full
> environment transformation, all guided by text prompts and reference images while the video is
> still streaming live.

## Architecture claims (marketing, unverified)

- "Lucy 2.0 does not rely on depth maps, meshes, or hybrid 3D pipelines. It is a pure diffusion
  model."
- "Smart History Augmentation" is described as a proprietary training method that penalises drift,
  allowing streams to "persist for hours without identity collapse or world degradation."
- The real-time stack is described as mega-kernels, a hardware-tailored architecture and "a custom
  WebRTC pipeline".

## Virtual try-on in this post

Try-on appears only as an application, not a model:

> Because video remains editable and responsive while it is being generated, Lucy can be used for
> live character swaps, motion control, virtual try-ons, product placement, interactive media, and
> real-time content creation.

## Date conflict to preserve

This post claims "30fps 1080p resolution" for Lucy 2.0 on 2026-01-26. Decart's own changelog dates
"Lucy 2 RT (Preview)" to January 2026 at **720p@20FPS** and the production `lucy-2` launch to
February 2026. Neither page reconciles the two. The docs' model tables list 720p for every Lucy
model. Treat "30fps 1080p" as a launch-day marketing claim, not as a documented serving spec — and
note that it describes the base model, not any VTON model.

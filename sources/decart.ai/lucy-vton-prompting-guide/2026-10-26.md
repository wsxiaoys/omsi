## Scope statement (verbatim)

> Lucy VTON applies garments and accessories from a reference image onto a person. This guide covers
> both `lucy-vton-3.5` and `lucy-vton-3.6` — they share the same prompt-enhancement model
> server-side, so the guidance below is identical for either. A strong prompt tells the model what
> item or outfit to apply, where it belongs, and which visible garment details matter.

The guide is named for 3.5 and says nothing about the retired `lucy-2.1-vton`; whether the same
prompt patterns apply to it is undocumented.

## Prompt length limit

> **Prompt length.** Prompts have a maximum length. As a rough estimate, about 750 characters of
> English (around 120 words) fit. The exact limit depends on the wording, and other languages may
> allow fewer characters. If your prompt is too long, you get an error that tells you how many
> characters of it fit. This applies whether or not prompt enhancement is on.

## Two actions, one per prompt

- **substitute** replaces a clothing region: "Substitute the upper body garment with a cropped black
  leather jacket."
- **add** layers on top of the existing outfit: "Add a black ribbed-knit beanie to the outfit."
- "Avoid mixing actions in one prompt, such as replacing a jacket and adding a bag at the same time."

## Named regions (verbatim table)

| Region to change | Start the prompt with |
| --- | --- |
| Tops, jackets, coats, hoodies, shirts, and sweaters | `"Substitute the upper body garment with <description>."` |
| Pants, skirts, shorts, and other bottoms | `"Substitute the lower body garment with <description>."` |
| Dresses, jumpsuits, matching sets, or full looks | `"Substitute the outfit with <description>."` |
| Shoes | `"Substitute the footwear with <description>."` |
| Hats | `"Substitute the hat with <description>."` |
| Necklaces | `"Substitute the necklace with <description>."` |
| Accessories to add on top | `"Add <description> to the outfit."` |

## Other rules stated on the page

- "The reference image is the source of truth for the garment."
- "Do not invent logos, text, or details that are not visible in the reference image."
- Describe visible details: colour, fabric, garment type, fit, length, sleeves, neckline, closures,
  pockets, texture, pattern, visible logo.
- State whether buttoned/zipped garments are open or closed.
- Avoid asking for unrelated changes ("Change the background.") and vague prompts ("Make me
  fashionable.").

## Gaps

- No quality benchmark, success rate, or comparison between versions.
- No statement about garment categories the model refuses (swimwear, underwear, uniforms, religious
  dress) or about body-size handling.
- No documented behaviour for multi-garment outfits beyond "avoid mixing actions".

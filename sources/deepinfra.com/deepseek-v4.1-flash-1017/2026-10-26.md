describe the same base model rather than two different measurements of it.

## Gaps

- No independent evaluation. DeepInfra relays DeepSeek's numbers; it does not report its
  own runs, latency, or throughput for this endpoint.
- No release date for the `1017` checkpoint, and no statement of what `1017` refers to
  (a date, a build number, or an internal revision).
- No statement of whether weights for `1017` are published, or whether the public
  Hugging Face repository was updated to this checkpoint.
- The page is absent from DeepInfra's own
  [DeepSeek family listing](../../deepinfra.com/deepseek-family/2026-10-26.md), which
  still shows only the V4 and earlier models. Host catalogue tables lag their model
  pages; do not treat either as authoritative about what is served.

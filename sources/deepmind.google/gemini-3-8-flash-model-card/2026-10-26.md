## Summary

The vendor's own numbers describe a **modest, uneven** step from 3.7 Flash, not a tier change: the
largest gains are on agentic and tool benchmarks (Terminal-bench 4.0 +7.9 points, DeepSWE v1.1 +8.4,
BioMysteryBench human-difficult +13.0), while Terminal-bench 2.1 (+3.6), HLE-Verified (+1.3) and
GDP.PDF (+1.0) move little. Against the card's own frontier comparison columns the model is clearly
below Claude Opus 5 on Terminal-bench 4.0 (19.1% vs 51.8%) and OSWorld-2.0 (59.0% vs 75.4%) — the two
rows where the gap is largest — while beating it on Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark (10.0% vs 6.7%),
Vals Finance Agent v2 (61.4% vs 58.6%) and CharXiv (86.2% vs 83.7%).

## Gaps

- No parameter count, architecture or training-data disclosure for 3.8 specifically.
- No pass@k, no confidence intervals, no task counts, and no per-benchmark harness description on the
  card itself (deferred to a separate methodology page that was not captured).
- Comparison columns are named without versions ("Claude Opus 5", "GPT-5.6 Sol") and do not match the
  roster's 5.5-generation Anthropic or GPT-6 labels; the card is dated September 2026, which dates
  the comparison set rather than resolving it.
- Safety deltas are relative to 3.7 Flash only, in percentage points, on automated evaluations.

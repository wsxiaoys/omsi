- Relevant model: Gemini 3 Pro Image = Nano Banana Pro

## Captured content

### Model information (verbatim)

> Gemini 3 Pro Image is the next generation in the Gemini series of models, a suite of
> highly-capable, natively multimodal, reasoning models. Gemini 3 Pro Image is now Google's most
> advanced model for image generation

> **Model dependencies:** Gemini 3 Pro Image is based on Gemini 3 Pro.

> **Inputs:** Text strings (e.g. a question, a prompt, document(s) to be summarized), images, with
> a token context window of up to 1M.

> **Outputs:** Image, with a 64K token output

> **Architecture:** Gemini 3 Pro Image is based on Gemini 3 Pro.

> **Hardware:** Gemini 3 Pro Image was trained using Google's Tensor Processing Units (TPUs).

> **Software:** Training was done using JAX and ML Pathways.

The 1M input / 64K output figures conflict with the Vertex / Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform model
card's 65,536 input and 32,768 output for the same `gemini-3-pro-image` ID. The same pattern appears
for Nano Banana 2.1 (1M / 64K on its DeepMind card, 131,072 / 32,768 on its API page).

### Evaluation approach (verbatim, abridged)

> Human evaluations of several different quality aspects of image generation were conducted. Prompt
> sets for human evaluations were in two broad categories: ○ New capability sets [...] ○ Regression
> sets: popular use cases observed on Gemini 2.5 Flash Image, to ensure Gemini 3 Pro Image does not
> show noticeable regression.

> Results: Gemini 3 Pro Image demonstrated strong performance and leads on Elo score across all new
> capabilities and regression sets. It leads with significant margins in complex tasks, e.g.
> multi-character, chart and text editing. It improves substantially in style compared to Gemini 2.5
> Flash Image.

The results column is headed "Gemini 3 Pro Image (Search On)", i.e. the vendor's Elo numbers were
measured with Google Search grounding enabled.

### Intended usage (verbatim)

> Gemini 3 Pro Image is our most powerful AI image editing model yet, capable of using Gemini's
> real-world knowledge to deliver precise results, from complex infographics to historically
> accurate scenes.

Well-suited applications listed: "creation and editing of images with professional levels of
precision and control"; "generation of clear text for posters and intricate diagrams"; "long
context real-world knowledge"; "studio-quality control".

### Known limitations (verbatim)

> Gemini 3 Pro Image may exhibit some of the general limitations of foundation models, such as
> hallucinations. There may also be occasional slowness or timeout issues.

> - Text rendering: poor in small text (often blurry in 1k model), long paragraphs, page length
> - Character consistency is not always perfect between input images and generated output image
> - Masked/Doodle based editing: partial instruction following and persistent ink
> - When editing images: infrequent copying/pasting from user's input image to generated image
> - Occasional confusion around spatial localisation (e.g. left/right etc.)
> - Still limited in advanced capabilities with world knowledge, 3D reasoning and factuality

> The knowledge cutoff date for Gemini 3 Pro Image was January 2025.

> For more information about the acceptable usage for Gemini 3 Pro Image, see the Gemini 3 Pro
> model card.

These are the vendor's own failure modes and are more specific than anything on the product page:
small-text blur at 1K, page-length text, doodle-ink persistence, source-image copy/paste artifacts,
and left/right confusion.

### Safety (summary)

Safety sections defer to the Gemini 3 Pro model card for dataset, distribution, ethics, and Frontier
Safety Framework results, stating that Gemini 3 Pro "did not reach any critical capability levels".
Mitigations listed: dataset filtering, conditional pre-training, supervised fine-tuning, RL from
human and critic feedback, safety policies and desiderata, product-level safety filtering. Safety
policy categories are the standard six (CSAM, hate speech, dangerous content, harassment, sexually
explicit content, medical advice contrary to consensus).

## Gaps

- No latency numbers beyond "occasional slowness or timeout issues"; no throughput, no price.
- No per-language text-rendering accuracy, despite text rendering being the headline claim.
- Elo methodology is not reproducible from the card: no prompt counts, no rater demographics, no
  agreement statistics, and the Search-On condition is not compared against Search-Off.
- The 1M-context / 64K-output claims are not reconciled with the platform limits (65,536 / 32,768)
  on the Vertex model card.
- The card is dated November 2025 and was not revised for the 2026 family; it cannot speak to Nano
  Banana 2 or 2.1.

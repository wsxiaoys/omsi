# Google DeepMind: Gemini 4 Argon model card

- **Origin:** Google DeepMind, "Gemini 4 Argon — Model Card",
  <https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-4-argon/> (PDF linked from the page:
  <https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/Model-Cards/Gemini-4-Argon-Model-Card.pdf>).
  Retrieved 2026-10-26.
- **Evidence kind:** official documentation (first-party model card; vendor-run evaluations
  plus safety and Frontier Safety Framework disclosures).
- **Publication date:** page reads "Published 13 October 2026" and "Published: October
  2026". Evaluation results are labelled "as of October, 2026".
- **Capture date:** 2026-10-26
- **Relevant models:** Gemini 4 Argon. Competitor columns: GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1,
  Claude Opus 5.5.
- **Capture type:** rendered page text (the HTML page, not the PDF). Tables were flattened
  by the text extraction; row/column alignment was reconstructed from the four model names
  in the header and verified against the number of values per row.

## Identity statements (verbatim)

> Gemini 4 is the next generation in the Gemini series, a suite of highly-capable, natively
> multimodal, reasoning models. Gemini 4 Argon delivers frontier performance to help in
> complex workflows across real-world software engineering, enterprise knowledge work like
> legal and finance, and cybersecurity defense.

> Gemini 4 Argon, a new frontier model, is not a modification or a fine-tune of a prior
> model.

Inputs: "Text strings (e.g., a question, a prompt, document(s) to be summarized), images,
audio, and video files, with a combined input-output token context window of up to 1M."
Outputs: "Text, with up to a 1M token input + output."

Architecture: "a large scale sparse mixture-of-experts (MoE) … transformer-based model with
native multimodal support for text, vision, and audio inputs." Trained on TPUs with JAX and
ML Pathways. No parameter count is disclosed.

Distribution channels listed: Gemini App, Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Gemini for business,
Google Antigravity, Google AI Mode.

## Intended usage and limitations (verbatim)

> Gemini 4 Argon may exhibit some of the general limitations of foundation models, such as
> hallucinations. There may also be occasional slowness or timeout issues. The knowledge
> cutoff for Gemini 4 is March 2026, but in some domains the model's knowledge may be less
> up to date.

Intended-use list: multi-turn deep investigations, complex engineering tasks, deep visual
understanding, cybersecurity defense, multimodal document analysis and synthesis, deep
domain knowledge and writing (legal/financial analysis, science), scientific or mathematical
reasoning.

## Safety and Frontier Safety Framework

- Training-development deltas against Gemini 3.1 Pro, in percentage points, signed as the
  card prints them: text-to-text safety **+1.57pp**, multilingual safety **−3.16pp**,
  image-to-text safety **−9.29pp**, refusal tone **+5.50%**, unjustified refusals
  **−9.60pp**. The card marks "lower is better" on the four safety rows, so a positive
  delta is worse; it nevertheless summarises the result as Argon "outperforms Gemini 3.1
  Pro across multilingual and multimodal content safety, while substantially reducing
  unjustified refusals", with text-to-text safety "within confidence intervals". The two
  readings are not reconciled on the page.
- Frontier Safety Framework (April 2026) critical capability assessment:
  - CBRN: "We can rule out the TCL for the chemical, radiological, and nuclear domains with
    reasonable confidence" — TCL not reached. Biological CCL: "We cannot rule out that
    Gemini 4 Argon reaches the biological CCL" — safety case applied to bring residual risk
    below it.
  - Cybersecurity: "We cannot rule out that Gemini 4 Argon reaches the cybersecurity CCL" —
    safety case applied.
  - Stealth and situational awareness TCL: "cannot rule out"; residual risk assessment
    performed.
- The card says nothing about which deployed surfaces carry cyber guardrails, beyond the
  statement that the model is "designed to refuse harmful requests while preserving
  legitimate, dual-use scientific research" (that sentence is in the launch post, not here).

## Summary

First-party anchor for identity (new frontier model, not a fine-tune), date (October 2026),
modalities (text/image/audio/video in, text out), the 1M unified input+output window,
knowledge cutoff (March 2026), and the vendor's own benchmark table. Everything numeric here
is vendor-run and unaccompanied by harness detail.

## Gaps

- No parameter count, no training-token count, no pricing, no region list, no context
  breakdown by modality.
- No effort setting stated for the benchmark rows, which matters because the model has
  three thinking levels.
- The methodology page and the PDF were not captured; only the HTML page.
- The safety table's percentage-point direction is easy to misread: the card marks
  improvements in green and regressions in red, which a text capture loses. The values above
  are recorded with their sign, not with the colour coding.

# Google DeepMind: "Gemini Image – Nano Banana" model family page

- Origin: Google DeepMind, "Gemini Image – Nano Banana", https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-image/
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party marketing page)
- Publication date: not stated.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: the Nano Banana family cards, including Nano Banana 2 (Gemini 3.1 Flash
  Image).

## Captured content

### Family cards (verbatim)

> **Nano Banana** — State-of-the-art image generation and editing models, built on Gemini

> **Nano Banana 2 Lite** — Our fastest, most efficient Gemini Image model. Built to deliver
> high-speed generation and editing at our lowest cost yet.

> **Nano Banana 2** — Gemini 3.1 Flash Image offers pro-level image generation and editing, at
> Flash speed

> **Nano Banana Pro** — Built on Gemini 3. Create and edit images with studio-quality levels of
> precision and control

Also listed as a per-model selector further down the page: "Gemini 3 Pro Image / Nano Banana
Pro", "Gemini 3.1 Flash Image / Nano Banana 2", "Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Image / Nano Banana 2
Lite".

Generic capability copy for the family (verbatim, abridged): "The Gemini Image model uses deep
language understanding to capture the nuance of your prompts"; "Upload images and share text
instructions with Gemini to create complex and detailed images"; "Generate images that follow
real-world logic, thanks to Gemini's advanced reasoning capabilities".

## Known gaps

- **Nano Banana 2.1 is absent from this page** on the capture date, even though
  `gemini-nano-banana-2.1` had been GA since 2026-10-06 and the Gemini API docs already call it
  the current high-efficiency model. First-party surfaces disagree about which model is
  current; the marketing page lags.
- No prices, limits, resolutions, aspect ratios, model IDs, or dates appear on this page.
- All quality language ("pro-level", "studio-quality", "state-of-the-art") is unquantified
  marketing with no benchmark or methodology attached.
- The page confirms the marketing-name-to-model-name mapping for Nano Banana 2 ("Gemini 3.1
  Flash Image") but gives no API identifier.

## Additional family-page detail (from the merged capture)

The same page is also served at <https://deepmind.google/models/nano-banana/>. Its "Model
family" section lists exactly three entries: Gemini 3 Pro Image (Nano Banana Pro), Gemini 3.1
Flash Image (Nano Banana 2), and Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Image (Nano Banana 2 Lite).

Capability framing, verbatim:

> The Gemini Image model uses deep language understanding to capture the nuance of your prompts —
> bridging the gap between what you say and what you envision.

Bullets: "Multimodal understanding", "Conversational inputs", "Real-world knowledge — Generate
images that follow real-world logic, thanks to Gemini's advanced reasoning capabilities."

### What this page establishes

- Google's own family taxonomy groups the image models into Pro / Flash / Flash-Lite tiers and
  labels each with a Gemini version identifier.
- **Nano Banana 2.1 is absent.** A full-text check of the capture finds zero occurrences of
  "2.1", even though Google published a Nano Banana 2.1 model card on 2026-10-06 and shipped
  `gemini-nano-banana-2.1` to GA the same day. The product page is therefore stale relative to
  the model card and the API, or 2.1 is deliberately not yet featured there.
- Because 2.1 is not listed, this page cannot be used to establish 2.1's tier position within the
  family. That comes from the model card and the Gemini API docs instead.

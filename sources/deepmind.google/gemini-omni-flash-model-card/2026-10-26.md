# Google DeepMind: Gemini Omni Flash model card

- Origin: Google DeepMind, "Gemini Omni Flash - Model Card",
  https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-omni-flash/
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party model card, publisher = model developer)
- Publication date: page states "Published 27 August 2026", and under the title "Initially Published:
  May 2026, Last Updated: August 2026".
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Gemini Omni Flash and Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash. No other version or tier appears on the
  card.

## Captured content

### Identity and scope (verbatim)

> Gemini Omni Flash is our next step towards models that can create and edit anything from any input—
> starting with video. It combines Gemini's intelligence with our generative media models, representing
> a leap forward in world understanding, multimodality, and editing. Gemini Omni Flash enables
> high-quality video creation and a more natural way to edit videos through conversation. This model
> card describes Gemini Omni Flash and Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash.

> Inputs: Text strings (e.g., a question, a prompt), images, audio, and video files.

> Outputs: High-quality, high-resolution video with audio.

> Architecture: Gemini Omni Flash is a transformer-based model (Vaswani et al., 2017) with native
> multimodal support for text, vision, video and audio inputs.

The single card covering both names is the clearest first-party statement that "Gemini Omni Flash" and
"Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash" are one entity, not two products.

### Training and infrastructure

- "Gemini Omni Flash was trained on audio, video, image, and text data. Audio and video datasets were
  annotated with text captions at different levels of detail."
- "Training videos were also filtered for various compliance, safety, and quality metrics and
  deduplicated semantically."
- Hardware: "Google's Tensor Processing Units (TPUs)"; software: "Training was done using JAX and ML
  Pathways."

### Distribution (verbatim list)

> Gemini Omni Flash is distributed in the following channels; respective documentation shared in line:
> Gemini App, YouTube, Google Flow, Google Flow Music

The Gemini API and Google Cloud are not in this list, although both sell the model (see the pricing and
Agent Platform records).

### Known limitations (verbatim)

> While Gemini Omni Flash demonstrates strong progress, maintaining complete consistency throughout
> edits, generating scenes with complex motion, or rendering perfectly accurate text remains a
> challenge.

### Safety and responsible innovation (verbatim)

- "As part of editing videos, Gemini Omni Flash is capable of changing people's speech. For now, we are
  restricting this capability and working to better understand how to safely and responsibly bring it
  to our users."
- "we deployed advanced production filters and used SynthID, our digital watermarking tool to clearly
  verify AI-generated content".
- Acceptable-use text defers to "Google's Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy" and the "Gemini API
  Additional Terms of Service".

## Summary

The model card is the identity anchor: one model, two names (Gemini Omni Flash, Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash),
first published May 2026 and last updated August 2026, released 27 August 2026, taking text/image/
audio/video in and producing video with audio. It names three weaknesses itself — edit-to-edit
consistency, complex motion, accurate text — and says speech-changing edits are deliberately restricted.
It publishes no benchmark numbers and no duration, resolution, token or price limits.

# Google DeepMind: Lyria model family page

- Origin: Google DeepMind, "Lyria 3.5 — Google DeepMind" (page title at capture), model family page
  <https://deepmind.google/models/lyria/>. Page `<title>` reads "Lyria 3.5 — Google DeepMind"; the
  `og:title` and `twitter:title` read "Lyria 3.5". The hero block still says "Lyria — Our most
  advanced music generation model, now up to 3 minutes long".
- Evidence kind: official product documentation (first-party vendor page, marketing register)
- Publication date: not stated anywhere on the page. No `datePublished` or article date in the
  markup.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Lyria 3.5, Lyria 3, Lyria, Lyria RealTime, Magenta RealTime (Lyria 2 named only
  in the credits)

## Family list (verbatim)

> **Lyria 3.5** — Our latest model with improved vocals, musicality, and variable song lengths

> **Lyria** — Explore new genres, play with soundscapes, and find musical inspiration

> **Lyria RealTime** — Designed for real-time, interactive music generation.

> **Magenta RealTime** — Our Lyria open model explores the role of machine learning as a tool in the
> creative process.

Four entries, no version numbers beyond "3.5" and no dates. "Lyria" and "Lyria 3.5" are listed as
separate products, so the page does not tell a reader which of them the plain "Lyria" entry denotes.

## Capability claims (verbatim, marketing register)

> Lyria — Our most advanced music generation model, now up to 3 minutes long

> **Introducing Lyria 3.5** — Our newest music generation model delivers significant advancements
> across musicality, lyrics, and vocal quality, empowering you to craft richer tracks.

> **Prompt for the length you need** — Generate cohesive tracks matching your project's exact
> duration — whether you need a quick 60-second clip, a half-length track, or a full 3-minute song.

> **Compose with images** — Upload an image and ask Lyria to transform it into a custom
> high-fidelity track.

> Create cohesive tracks — Turn quick ideas into high-fidelity music that flows naturally from start
> to finish, up to 3 minutes long.

> Explore global languages and genres — Generate vocals in different languages, and create music
> spanning genres from pop to funk to Motown.

> Lyrics and vocals — Write your own lyrics, or generate them around specific subjects or themes.
> Then express them with realistic, natural, and nuanced vocals.

> Export professional-grade audio — Background ambience or mainstage anthem – produce crisp, clear
> tracks ready for your projects.

No sample rate, bitrate, file format, language list, track limit, or price is given anywhere on the
page.

## Safety and provenance (verbatim)

> We use extensive filtering and data labeling to minimize harmful content in datasets and reduce the
> likelihood of harmful lyrics.

> All of our tracks are imperceptibly watermarked with SynthID technology, allowing you to detect
> whether music has been created or edited using AI.

> **Limitations** — While Lyria can create a wide range of music, we're still working on improving
> key capabilities. You should always carefully check that the tracks you create fit your vision.

The safety block is a policy statement, not a description of a filter that can be configured or
disabled.

## Summary

- First-party confirmation that the family is Lyria 3.5 (newest) / Lyria / Lyria RealTime /
  Magenta RealTime, and that Lyria 3 and Lyria 2 are distinct models.
- First-party confirmation of the headline limits: up to 3 minutes, vocals and lyrics in multiple
  languages, image-to-music, SynthID on all output.
- **No dates at all.** Every date used on the wiki page comes from Google Cloud's model cards and
  release notes, or from host listings.

## Gaps

- No release date for Lyria 3.5, and no statement of whether 3.5 is served by any API. The page
  presents it as a product surface (Flow Music, Gemini), not as an API model.
- No technical specification (sample rate, format, per-request clip cap, language list).
- No pricing, no quota, no licensing terms.
- The undated page cannot support any claim about what was true at an earlier date.

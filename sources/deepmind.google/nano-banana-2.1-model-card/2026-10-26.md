## Captured content

### Model information (verbatim)

> Nano Banana 2.1 is a member of the Gemini 3 series of models, a suite of highly-capable,
> natively multimodal reasoning models. Nano Banana 2.1 can comprehend input from different
> information sources, including text and images. Image and text output is generated in the
> response.

> Model dependencies: Nano Banana 2.1 is based on **Gemini 3.6 Flash**.

> Inputs: Text strings (e.g., a prompt, document(s)) and images, with a token context window of
> up to 1M.

> Outputs: Image, with a 4K token output and text, with a 64K token output.

> Architecture: Nano Banana 2.1 is based on Gemini 3.6 Flash. For more information about the
> model architecture for Gemini 3.6 Flash, see the Gemini 3.6 Flash model card.

The same "based on Gemini 3.6 Flash" sentence is repeated for training dataset, training data
processing, hardware, and software, each deferring to the Gemini 3.6 Flash model card.

### Distribution channels (verbatim list)

Gemini App, Google AI Studio, Gemini API, Google Search AI Mode, Google Ads, Google Flow,
Google Stitch.

> Our models are available to downstream providers via an application program interface (API)
> and subject to relevant terms of use. There is no required hardware or software to use the
> model.

### Evaluation method (verbatim, abridged)

> Capability sets: diverse Text-to-Image (T2I) and editing public benchmarks, and internal evals
> [...] Regression sets: popular use cases observed on Gemini 2.5 Flash Image, and Gemini 3 Pro
> Image [...] Eval Methodology: SxS human eval to get Elo across diverse T2I, Editing on overall
> preference, design quality, etc. Single sided AutoRater on factuality.

### Intended usage (verbatim)

> Nano Banana 2.1 is capable of using Gemini's real-world knowledge to deliver precise results
> and reflect the world around you, from complex infographics to historically accurate scenes.

It is listed as well-suited for "creation and editing of images with professional levels of
precision and control and multiple, quick iterations", "generation of clear text for posters and
intricate diagrams", "long context real-world knowledge", and "localized text rendering across
several languages".

### Known limitations (verbatim bullets)

> Nano Banana 2.1 may exhibit some of the general limitations of foundation models, such as
> hallucinations. There may also be occasional slowness or timeout issues.
>
> Nano Banana 2.1 still has room for several quality improvements:
> - Poor text rendering in small text (often blurry in 1k model), long paragraphs, page length
> - Character consistency is not always perfect between input images and generated output image
> - Partial instruction following and ink persistence in masked/doodle based editing
> - During image editing, we observe rare instances of persistent subject pose, where structural
>   alignment from the input image is retained in the generated output image
> - Occasional confusion around spatial localisation (e.g., left/right etc.)
> - Still limited in advanced capabilities with world knowledge, 3D reasoning and factuality

> The knowledge cutoff date for Gemini 3.6 Flash is March 2026 – users can expect updated
> information for some domains while in others they may experience the model's knowledge is
> limited to January 2025 (in line with the Gemini 3 Model Family).

### Safety (verbatim, abridged)

> we saw similar or improved safety performance compared to Gemini 3 Flash [...] compared
> performance to Gemini 3.1 Pro, and found no egregious concerns.

> To assess Nano Banana 2.1 for frontier safety, we rely on our evaluations of Gemini 3.1 Pro and
> Gemini 3.7 Flash [...] Our assessments have shown that Nano Banana 2.1 does not have meaningful
> new capabilities or material increases in performance compared to Gemini 3.1 Pro or Gemini 3.7
> Flash.

## Known gaps

- The card gives **no absolute benchmark task counts, prompt sets, or rater counts**, only Elo
  with confidence intervals and a single AutoRater score. The comparisons are Google-run
  side-by-side human evaluations of Google's own models; nothing here is third-party.
- "Infographic Factuality" is an autorater score on an unspecified scale, so it is not comparable
  to the Elo rows in the same table.
- The card states the base model is Gemini 3.6 Flash, but its frontier-safety section assesses
  Gemini 3.1 Pro and Gemini 3.7 Flash instead, and its human red-teaming section compares against
  Gemini 3 Flash. Which model the safety results actually transfer from is not stated.
- The card's context-window and output-token figures (1M input, 64K text output) disagree with
  the Gemini API model page captured the same day; see that record.

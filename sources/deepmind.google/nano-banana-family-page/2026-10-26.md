# Google DeepMind: Nano Banana (Gemini Image) family page

- Origin: Google DeepMind, "Gemini Image – Nano Banana",
  https://deepmind.google/models/nano-banana/ (also served at
  https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-image/)
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party product/marketing page)
- Publication date: not stated on the page
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: the Nano Banana family — Nano Banana, Nano Banana 2, Nano Banana 2 Lite,
  Nano Banana Pro

## Captured content

### Family framing (verbatim)

> Nano Banana — State-of-the-art image generation and editing models, built on Gemini

Model family cards, verbatim:

- "Nano Banana Pro — Built on Gemini 3. Create and edit images with studio-quality levels of
  precision and control"
- "Nano Banana 2 — Gemini 3.1 Flash Image offers pro-level image generation and editing, at Flash
  speed"
- "Nano Banana 2 Lite — Our fastest, most efficient Gemini Image model. Built to deliver
  high-speed generation and editing at our lowest cost yet."

The page's "Model family" section lists exactly three entries: Gemini 3 Pro Image (Nano Banana
Pro), Gemini 3.1 Flash Image (Nano Banana 2), and Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Image (Nano Banana 2
Lite).

### Capability framing (verbatim)

> The Gemini Image model uses deep language understanding to capture the nuance of your prompts —
> bridging the gap between what you say and what you envision.

Bullets: "Multimodal understanding", "Conversational inputs", "Real-world knowledge — Generate
images that follow real-world logic, thanks to Gemini's advanced reasoning capabilities."

## What this page establishes

- Google's own family taxonomy groups the image models into Pro / Flash / Flash-Lite tiers and
  labels each with a Gemini version identifier.
- **Nano Banana 2.1 is absent.** A full-text check of the capture finds zero occurrences of
  "2.1", even though Google published a Nano Banana 2.1 model card on 2026-10-06 and shipped
  `gemini-nano-banana-2.1` to GA the same day (see the Gemini API changelog record). The product
  page is therefore stale relative to the model card and the API, or 2.1 is deliberately not yet
  featured there.

## Known gaps

- No pricing, limits, or version dates on this page; it is positioning copy.
- Because 2.1 is not listed, this page cannot be used to establish 2.1's tier position within the
  family. That comes from the model card and the Gemini API docs instead.

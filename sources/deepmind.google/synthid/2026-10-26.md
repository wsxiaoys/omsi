# Google DeepMind: SynthID

- Origin: Google DeepMind, "SynthID", <https://deepmind.google/technologies/synthid/>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party provenance/watermarking page)
- Publication date: not stated; capture date 2026-10-26
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: none named for Gemini API TTS; relevant to the watermarking question for
  `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts`

## Captured content

General description (verbatim):

> SynthID is a technology from Google DeepMind that watermarks and identifies AI-generated content by
> embedding digital watermarks directly into AI-generated images, audio, text or video.

Audio section (verbatim):

> **AI-generated audio** SynthID embeds a watermark into any audio generated or published through our
> AI music generation model Lyria or the podcast generation feature of Notebook LM. It's inaudible to
> the human ear, and can't be altered by common modifications like adding noise, MP3 compression, or
> changing the speed of the track

Detection (verbatim):

> Simply upload the image, video, or audio clip to your chat, and ask if it's been created or altered
> by Google AI. Gemini, Search and Chrome will check for a SynthID watermark, and let you know if it
> finds one.

> With the new SynthID Detector, anyone can easily check if an image, video, or audio file was made
> with AI from Google or our partners, including OpenAI, NVIDIA, Kakao, and soon, Apple.

## Summary

The first-party watermarking page scopes audio marking to **Lyria and NotebookLM podcast generation**
and does not name the Gemini API TTS models at all. It confirms that Google has an audio watermarking
capability and a public detector, but it is not evidence that `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts` output
carries SynthID.

So the watermarking claim for this model rests on a single host sentence — Replicate's "Every clip
includes an inaudible SynthID watermark" — while both the Gemini API TTS guide and the Flash-Lite
model page are silent. This is recorded as unresolved rather than as either a confirmed capability or
a confirmed absence.

## Gaps

- No per-model or per-API list of which Google models apply SynthID audio marking.
- No statement on detectability after transcoding for TTS output specifically (the Lyria/NotebookLM
  claim covers noise, MP3 and speed changes).
- No statement on whether third-party hosts (fal, Replicate) preserve the watermark when they proxy
  or transcode.

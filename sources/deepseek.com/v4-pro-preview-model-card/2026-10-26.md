## Why this record exists

The 0813 GA card says the GA model "is built on the DeepSeek-V4-Pro (Preview) model structure,
with a DSpark speculative decoding module attached", but it contains no architecture section.
Everything below is the only first-party description of that structure.

## Architecture and training, verbatim

> "We present a preview version of DeepSeek-V4 series, including two strong Mixture-of-Experts
> (MoE) language models — DeepSeek-V4-Pro with 1.6T parameters (49B activated) and
> DeepSeek-V4-Flash with 284B parameters (13B activated) — both supporting a context length of
> one million tokens."

- **Hybrid Attention Architecture:** *"We design a hybrid attention mechanism combining
  Compressed Sparse Attention (CSA) and Heavily Compressed Attention (HCA) to dramatically
  improve long-context efficiency. In the 1M-token context setting, DeepSeek-V4-Pro requires
  only 27% of single-token inference FLOPs and 10% of KV cache compared with DeepSeek-V3.2."*
- **Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections (mHC):** *"to strengthen conventional residual
  connections, enhancing stability of signal propagation across layers while preserving model
  expressivity."*
- **Muon Optimizer:** *"for faster convergence and greater training stability."*
- **Pre-training:** *"more than 32T diverse and high-quality tokens."*
- **Post-training:** *"a two-stage paradigm: independent cultivation of domain-specific experts
  (through SFT and RL with GRPO), followed by unified model consolidation via on-policy
  distillation."*
- **Precision:** the instruct checkpoint is "FP4 + FP8 Mixed", defined verbatim as *"MoE expert
  parameters use FP4 precision; most other parameters use FP8."* The base checkpoint is FP8
  mixed.

## Reasoning modes — named differently from the GA card

The preview card presents three modes by name, not by `reasoning_effort` value:

| Mode | Characteristic | Response format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Non-think | "Fast, intuitive responses" | `</think>` summary |
| Think High | "Conscious logical analysis, slower but more accurate" | ` thinking` … `</think>` summary |
| Think Max | "Push reasoning to its fullest extent" | Special system prompt + ` thinking` … `</think>` |

The GA card replaces this with `reasoning_effort` values `low` / `high` / `max`. The mapping
(low↔Non-think, high↔Think High, max↔Think Max) is a reasonable reading but **neither card
states it**.

Sampling on this card: *"temperature = 1.0, top_p = 1.0. For the Think Max reasoning mode, we
recommend setting the context window to at least 384K tokens."*

## Reported results — the DS-V4-Pro Max column

The card's instruct table has a `DS-V4-Pro Max` column against Opus-4.6 Max, GPT-5.4 xHigh,
Gemini-3.1-Pro High, K2.6 Thinking and GLM-5.1 Thinking. Selected rows for the Pro column:
MMLU-Pro 87.5, SimpleQA-Verified 57.9, Chinese-SimpleQA 84.4, GPQA Diamond 90.1, HLE 37.7,
LiveCodeBench 93.5, Codeforces 3206, HMMT 2026 Feb 95.2, IMOAnswerBench 89.8, Apex 38.3,
Apex Shortlist 90.2, MRCR 1M (MMR) 83.5, CorpusQA 1M 62.0, Terminal Bench 2.0 67.9,
SWE Verified 80.6.

Two consistency checks are worth recording:

- HLE 37.7 for the preview matches the preview column in the GA card's table (37.7 / 48.2) and
  the 2026-08-13 change-log entry. The vendor's own preview numbers therefore agree across
  three documents.
- Terminal Bench **2.0** is 67.9 here while the GA card's Terminal Bench **2.1** is 87.9 for the
  GA model and 72.1 for the preview. Different benchmark versions, so the 67.9 and 72.1 figures
  are not the same measurement and must not be differenced.

## Gaps

- No release date, no training-data composition, no safety evaluation, no latency data.
- No explanation of why `think max` needs a "special system prompt"; the prompt itself is not
  printed.
- The card does not say whether the preview checkpoint stayed obtainable after 2026-08-13. The
  repository was still up at capture with 367,647 downloads in the previous month, which is
  evidence of availability but not of vendor support.

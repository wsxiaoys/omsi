## Identity and licensing

- Separate repository from the base model. This is the decisive fact: `1017` is a
  **distinct downloadable checkpoint**, not a rolling alias over the base weights.
- License: **MIT**, stated for *"this repository and the model weights"*.
- Card metadata: Image-Text-to-Text, fp8, Transformers-compatible.
- Size: 48 safetensors shards, roughly **510 GB** (size from the third-party analysis at
  [OrcaRouter](../../orcarouter.ai/deepseek-v4-1-flash-leak/2026-10-26.md); the card page
  itself does not state a total).
- ModelScope mirror announced by DeepSeek:
  `modelscope.cn/models/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017`.

## What the card does and does not contain

The card is short. It contains:

- A one-paragraph introduction calling `1017` *"a minor update to DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash,
  delivering substantial improvements across reasoning, coding, agentic tasks, and visual
  benchmarks"*, with reduced task execution time and output length.
- The eleven-row benchmark table (identical to the one on the DeepInfra page and the
  change log).
- Prompt-encoding notes, minimal-inference pointer, sampling parameters, license, citation.

It does **not** contain the architecture description. The base card's CED / CSA2 / FP4-KV
material is absent here; the technical-report link points at
`DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash/blob/main/DeepSeek_V41_Tech_Report.pdf` in the **base** repository.
So the architecture claims belong to the base model and are inherited by assumption, not
restated. That is consistent with a post-training-only update, but the card never says so
explicitly.

## Sampling parameters

`temperature=1.0`, `top_p=1.0`, `context_window=1M`, `max_tokens ≥ 256K`. The base card
gives `top_p=0.95 or 1.0`, so the two cards differ slightly. Neither explains the change.

Same practical constraint as the base: **no Jinja chat template**, with `encoding/` and
`deepseek-recipe` provided instead.

## Gaps

- No release date on the card, no parameter count, no architecture, no training detail.
- No statement of what `1017` denotes.
- No latency, throughput, or cost-per-task figures, despite the "faster, shorter outputs"
  claim.
- The benchmark table is unqualified by harness in the card body; the harness footnotes
  appear in the change log and on the DeepInfra page instead.

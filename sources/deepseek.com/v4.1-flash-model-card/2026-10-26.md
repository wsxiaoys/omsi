## Architecture (as stated by DeepSeek)

- Multimodal Mixture-of-Experts, **552B backbone parameters**, context up to **1M
  tokens**. Processes images and text natively, generates text autoregressively.
- **Causal Encoder-Decoder (CED):** 40-layer Transformer, 20-layer causal encoder plus
  20-layer decoder. The decoder's global KV cache is projected from final encoder
  hidden states rather than each decoder layer's own states. DeepSeek states this
  activates only **8B parameters per token during prefill** and **16B during decode**.
- **SWA Bounded Replay** reconstructs missing sliding-window KV states by replaying the
  most recent *n_win* tokens, reducing persistent KV footprint to roughly **1/8** of
  DeepSeek-V4-Flash.
- **Compressed Sparse Attention 2 (CSA2):** each attention layer is statically assigned
  one of three modes (Full, Reindex, Reuse) to share main KV and indexer K across layers
  and reuse Top-K sparse-attention indices. A Hierarchical Sparse Indexer bounds deeper
  indexer cost independently of context length.
- **FP4 main KV caching** (E2M1 format, one E4M3 scale per 16 channels) brings the
  global KV cache to **890 bytes per token**, stated as roughly **1/4** of
  DeepSeek-V4-Flash.
- Other components: Single-Pass mHC, **Engram conditional memory (196B parameters)**,
  DSpark speculative decoding. **1 shared expert and 384 routed experts per MoE layer,
  6 routed experts active per token.**
- Pre-training: from scratch on a multimodal corpus of **45T tokens**; sparse attention
  trained at 64K sequence length, context extended to 1M at 34T tokens.
- Post-training: standard SFT → RL → on-policy distillation (OPD) with no algorithmic
  changes; the stated changes are in the data pipeline (automated synthesis of agent
  tasks and environments). Supports **continuously controllable reasoning effort,
  integer 1–100**.

Note that 552B backbone plus a 196B Engram module is not a single parameter count; the
card does not state the total including Engram. Do not quote "552B" as the full model
size without that caveat.

### Agent-scaffold sensitivity

DeepSeek reports the same two benchmarks across eight harnesses, N=8 samples on DeepSWE
v1.1 and N=3 on Terminal-Bench 2.1, `max_steps=500`, 1M context, Terminal-Bench 2.1
without network access.

| Benchmark | Claude Code | Codex | OpenCode | Pi | mini-SWE | DSH Minimal | DSH Standard | DSH PTC |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DeepSWE v1.1 | 69.8 | 65.6 | 65.5 | 66.2 | **74.2** | 72.6 | 70.5 | 67.6 |
| Terminal-Bench 2.1 | 88.0 | 84.1 | 85.0 | 86.1 | 90.3 | **90.6** | 85.8 | 85.8 |

This spread is the most useful thing in the card: the same model moves ~8 points on
DeepSWE v1.1 and ~6.5 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 depending only on the harness. Cross-vendor
agentic comparisons that use different scaffolds are therefore not directly comparable.

## Recommended sampling parameters

`temperature=1.0`, `top_p=0.95 or 1.0`, `context_window=1M`, `max_tokens ≥ 256K`.

# ElevenLabs help centre: "Can I publish the content I generate on the platform?" (commercial licence)

- Origin: ElevenLabs, help centre, "Can I publish the content I generate on the platform?",

- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: all ElevenLabs models, including `eleven_multilingual_v2`; the page is
  model-agnostic and keyed to plan tier

## Captured content (verbatim, in full)

> The free plan does **not** include a commercial license and cannot be used for any commercial
> purpose. If you publish content generated using our Services on a free plan or without being
> signed-in to your account, you must attribute it to ElevenLabs by including "elevenlabs.io" or
> "11.ai" in the title (except in the case where your content is generated using Eleven Music on a
> free plan or without being signed-in to your account, you must attribute it to ElevenLabs by
> referencing "Eleven Music" when distributing such content).

> All paid plans include a commercial license, provided you're not using Beta Services. As long as
> you hold the necessary intellectual property rights to your generated content, and it complies with
> applicable laws, our Terms of Service and Prohibited Use Policy, you may use it commercially.

> Some products and features may have additional terms and conditions. Consult our
> Service-Specific Terms before commercial use. Attribution requirement is subject to the agreement
> you have with ElevenLabs.

> * Content generated during a paid subscription can be used commercially, and indefinitely, subject
>   to our Service-Specific Terms. Attribution requirement is subject to the agreement you have with
>   ElevenLabs.
> * Content created outside of a paid subscription (before or after) cannot be used commercially and
>   always requires attribution when shared non-commercially.
> * Under our Beta Services Addendum, content generated using Beta Services cannot be used for any
>   commercial purpose or in any production environment.

## What this establishes

- Commercial rights are **plan-gated, not model-gated**. Multilingual v2 output inherits whatever
  licence the caller's subscription carries; there is no per-model licence for it.
- Free-plan output cannot be used commercially at all and must be attributed ("elevenlabs.io" or
  "11.ai") even for non-commercial publication.
- Content generated *during* a paid subscription stays commercially usable "indefinitely", so a later
  downgrade does not retroactively restrict earlier output — while content created outside a paid
  subscription never becomes commercial.
- No weights are offered under any licence; the model is hosted-only, so there is no weights licence
  to review.

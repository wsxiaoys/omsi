## Captured content

### Which models a Professional Voice Clone trains on (verbatim, FAQ)

> #### How do I add or upgrade the models used to train my Professional Voice Clone?
>
> All Professional Voice Clones (PVCs) will automatically train on the Flash v2.5, Turbo v2.5 and
> Multilingual v2 models. PVCs trained on English audio will also automatically train on the Flash v2
> and Turbo v2 models.

> If you have an existing PVC, you now have the option to fine-tune on additional models. In the
> future, if new models are released that support fine-tuning, you will receive a notification.

### Instant vs professional cloning (verbatim)

> Professional Voice Cloning, unlike Instant Voice Cloning which lets you quickly clone voices with
> less than 2 minutes of audio, allows you to train a more realistic model of your voice. This is
> achieved by training a dedicated model on a large set of voice data to produce a model that's
> virtually indistinguishable from your original voice.

> Since Professional Voice Clones require fine-tuning and training, it will take some time before you
> can use your voice clone … usually fine-tuning will take 3-6 hours.

### Sample requirements (verbatim, FAQ)

> For Instant Voice Cloning and Professional Voice Cloning, we accept a range of file types. We
> strongly recommend MP3 at 192kbps or above.
>
> * **Recommended format**: MP3, 192kbps or higher
> * **Recommended length**: Instant Voice Cloning: 1–2 minutes of good audio; Professional Voice
>   Cloning: 30–180 minutes of good audio
>
> **Minimum length for Instant Voice Cloning**: When you upload samples in the app, each file must be
> at least 5 seconds long, and all your samples together must add up to at least 10 seconds.

### Consent and verification (verbatim, FAQ)

> No. You can only create a Professional Voice Clone of your own voice. Even with their consent, you
> cannot clone someone else's voice. All Professional Voice Clones require a verification process to
> confirm that the voice belongs to you.

> If someone wants to share their voice with you, they can create and verify their Professional Voice
> Clone on their own account, then share it with you privately using a sharing link.

> For now, we only allow you to clone your own voice. You will be asked to go through a verification
> process before submitting your fine-tuning request.

### Language coverage of PVC (verbatim)

> Professional Voice Cloning supports all languages available in the Eleven v4 model family — 90+
> languages in total.

### Provenance claim (verbatim)

> All audio generated by our models can be instantly traced back to the user responsible for the
> generation.

## What this establishes

- `eleven_multilingual_v2` is a **documented Professional Voice Cloning target**: every PVC
  automatically trains on it, with no opt-in step. A cloned voice is therefore usable with
  Multilingual v2 by default, unlike the v4 family where the model guide describes PVC support as a
  newer capability.
- Instant Voice Cloning works from roughly 1–2 minutes of audio (minimum 10 seconds total), while
  PVC needs 30–180 minutes and 3–6 hours of fine-tuning.
- Third-party cloning is refused outright at this tier: "Even with their consent, you cannot clone
  someone else's voice." Sharing is done by the voice owner creating and verifying a PVC on their own
  account and issuing a sharing link, so a cloned voice used with Multilingual v2 always belongs to
  (or was shared by) the verified account holder.
- ElevenLabs claims generated audio "can be instantly traced back to the user responsible", which is
  a stronger provenance claim than the watermark page makes (that page only promises detection by
  ElevenLabs' own detector).

## Gaps

- No statement of whether PVC quality differs per training target, so nothing here says how good a
  PVC sounds on Multilingual v2 versus Flash v2.5.
- The "90+ languages" PVC claim is scoped to the v4 family and does not state language coverage for
  Multilingual v2 PVCs.
- No per-plan gating for PVC captured (the vendor sells PVC on higher tiers, but no page captured
  states which).
- No policy page captured on "No-Go Voices" restrictions or on how verification failures are
  adjudicated beyond the 24-hour retry window.
- Nothing captured states whether an Instant Voice Clone (which has no verification step) may be
  made of another person, so the "own voice only" rule above is documented for PVCs and unconfirmed
  for IVCs.

## Captured content (verbatim)

> ## Eleven Music, now available in the API
>
> Published
> Aug 18, 2025

> Today, we are launching Eleven [Music](https://elevenlabs.io/music) in our API. This is one of the
> first Music APIs for developers, built in partnership with artists, labels, and publishers and
> cleared for broad commercial use.

> With the Eleven [Music API](https://elevenlabs.io/eleven-music-api), you can:
> - Generate high-quality tracks from simple text prompts
> - Create vocal or instrumental versions in any genre
> - Customize track length, structure, and language

> Since launch, creators have already generated over 1 million songs with Eleven Music.

> Created in collaboration with labels, publishers, and artists, songs created with the Eleven Music
> API are available for broad commercial use.

> Certain use cases like film & TV require an Enterprise plan, for more information on supported
> usage across plans: [https://elevenlabs.io/music-rights](https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms).

Named integrations at launch: VEED (AI background music), AMP Sonic Hub, Hedra Labs.

## What this establishes

- **The Music API became available on 2025-08-18** — 69 days before the roster window opens. The API
  route is therefore also out-of-window for v1.
- The "broad commercial use, except film & TV without Enterprise" carve-out was stated at launch and
  is still present, in expanded form, in the October 2026 Music Terms (which also exclude radio and
  Studio Games below Enterprise).
- The post is marketing: "over 1 million songs" is an unaudited usage claim, not a quality measure.

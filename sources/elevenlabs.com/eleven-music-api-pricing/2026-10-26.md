- Publication date: not stated; live pricing page.

## What this establishes

- The vendor's API list price for Eleven Music is **$0.15 per minute of output audio**, flat across
  every plan row, plus **$1.50 per Finetune**.
- The same page states two different metering bases for music: "Price per minute" on the card and
  table, and "Music and Sound Effects are billed per generation" in the FAQ. They are not reconciled.
- The card repeats the **5 minute duration limit**, which conflicts with the API schema's 600000 ms
  bound and the ElevenCreative guide's 10-minute maximum.
- The card asserts **commercial use licensing on Starter+ plans** — i.e. Free is excluded — which
  matches Exhibit A of the Music Terms (attribution required, downloads not permitted on Free).
- Included monthly minutes (3 → 6,600) are the API allowance; they do not match the Music Terms'
  monthly *generation* limits (11 → 4,800 minutes). The two schedules are different quantities and
  neither page says so.

## Gaps

- No plan labels beside the six Music rows; which plan gets 3 versus 40 included minutes is inferred
  from row order, not stated.
- No statement of whether a partial minute bills as a full minute, nor whether failed generations
  bill. (fal's own wrapper, by contrast, rounds up to the whole minute — see the fal records.)
- No per-model price difference: the page quotes one rate for "Music" although the family spans three
  model IDs with different capability.
- The May 2026 v2 launch post says music pricing was cut "by up to 50%" for ElevenAPI at v2 launch;
  the pre-cut v1 rate is not published anywhere captured.

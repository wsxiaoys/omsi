# ElevenLabs docs: Music prompting best practices

- Origin: ElevenLabs, "Best practices" (Music prompting guide)
  <https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/music/best-practices> (read as Markdown via
  `Accept: text/markdown`)
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor guidance)
- Publication date: not stated; the page describes Music v2-era controls.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Eleven Music (`music_v1`, `music_v2`, `music_v2_5`) — the page does not say which
  model each behaviour applies to

## Captured content (verbatim)

> A prompt answers five questions, whether you intend it to or not: genre, mood, instrumentation,
> tempo, and production era. Any question you leave open, the model answers with the most
> statistically likely choice — which is to say, the most average one.

> The model demonstrates strong adherence to genre conventions and emotional tone. It responds
> effectively to both abstract mood descriptors (e.g., "eerie," "foreboding") and detailed musical
> language (e.g., "dissonant violin screeches over a pulsing sub-bass").

> Prompt length and detail do not always correlate with better quality outputs.

> The model understands music the way it's discussed in studios. Studio language moves real levers:
> *sidechained*, *close-mic'd*, *bone-dry*, *tape saturation*, *plate reverb* each audibly change the
> mix.

> The model accurately follows BPM and often captures the intended musical key. To gain more control
> over timing and harmony, include tempo cues like "130 BPM" and key signatures like "in A minor" in
> your prompt. The model holds a stated BPM and key precisely enough to layer the output with other
> material — a vocal take, a sample, a second generation.

> The model can effectively render multiple vocalists — use prompts like "two singers harmonizing in
> C" to direct vocal arrangement.

> You can specify the length of the song (e.g., "60 seconds") or use auto mode to let the model
> determine the duration. If lyrics are not provided, the model will generate structured lyrics that
> match the chosen or auto-detected length.

> By default, most music prompts will include lyrics. To generate music without vocals, add
> "instrumental only" to your prompt. … The model uses your lyrics in combination with the prompt
> length to determine vocal structure and placement.

> The model supports multilingual lyric generation. To change the language of a generated song in our
> UI, use follow-ups like "make it Japanese" or "translate to Spanish."

> The model renders sound itself, and it holds no opinion on whether the sound you're describing could
> physically occur. Non-musical audio — sound effects, ambience, textures — can be prompted directly
> or incorporated within a track

Stem guidance: "Use the word 'solo' before instruments … For vocals, use 'a cappella' before the
vocal description", plus key/tempo/tone cues, to improve stem quality.

## What this establishes

- Documented prompting levers: genre/mood/instrumentation/tempo/production-era framing, studio
  production vocabulary, explicit BPM and key signatures, multi-vocalist direction, explicit length,
  lyric control, and inline sound effects.
- The vendor's own hedges matter: "Prompt length and detail do not always correlate with better
  quality outputs", and BPM/key adherence is described qualitatively ("accurately follows", "often
  captures") with no measurement.
- Instrumental output is *not* the default in prompt mode: "By default, most music prompts will
  include lyrics", and "instrumental only" must be requested (or `force_instrumental` set). This
  matches the compose reference's default of `force_instrumental: false` and contradicts Replicate's
  wrapper default of `true`.
- Length can be specified inside the prompt ("60 seconds") as well as through `music_length_ms`.

## Gaps

- No model-version scoping: the page never says whether these behaviours were tested on v1, v2 or
  v2.5.
- No measurement of any claim: no BPM-accuracy test, no key-detection rate, no blind comparison.
- No statement of what "auto" duration actually resolves to.
- The prompt-length limit is not stated here, and fal's schema imposes a 4100-character cap that this
  page does not mention.

## Captured content (verbatim)

> Eleven Music offers an end-to-end workflow for music creation. Generate songs in any style and at
> your desired length

> Choose the number of **Variants** and the **Duration**. You can select a fixed length (e.g., 30s,
> 1m) or **Auto** for a dynamically determined length.

### Export and quality

> Generated audio is provided in MP3 format with professional-grade quality (44.1kHz, 128-192kbps).
> Other audio formats will be supported soon.

> Export quality varies by subscription tier:
> - **Free, Starter, and Creator:** Standard quality exports.
> - **Pro, Scale, Business, and Enterprise:** High-fidelity, studio-grade exports.

### FAQ

> **How long can my generated songs be?**
> You can create everything from short clips to full-length tracks. … The minimum duration of a song
> is 3 seconds and the maximum is 10 minutes.

> **What languages are supported for vocals?**
> Eleven Music supports vocals in multiple languages including English, Spanish, German, and
> Japanese. The model can generate lyrics in these languages, or you can provide your own lyrics for
> the AI to sing.

> **What is Eleven Music?**
> … Eleven Music offers:
> - **Complete Music Tracks** from a single text prompt
> - **Granular Control** of genre, mood, style, structure, and instrumentation
> - **Vocals & Lyrics** across multiple languages … with Music v2 and v2.5 supporting more natural
>   vocal performances and complex delivery patterns such as fast rap
> - **Audio Reference** to guide Music v2 and v2.5 generations with a short uploaded track
> - **Post-Generation Editing** to adjust sections and lyrics of those sections via the UI
> - **Studio-Ready Exports** in MP3 for professional workflows

> Eleven Music is available today for all users on the ElevenLabs website.
> API access is available for all users on paid subscription plans.

> The cost of Eleven Music depends on the length of your track and how many variants you're
> generating. … For a breakdown of how many minutes of Music each of our subscription plans can
> generate with the included credit quota, see the Music table on our [Pricing page](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing).

## What this establishes

- **The product guide's maximum song length is 10 minutes**, directly contradicting the capabilities
  FAQ's 5-minute maximum and agreeing with the API schema's 600000 ms bound.
- The product guide says exports are **MP3 only** (44.1 kHz, 128–192 kbps) and "Other audio formats
  will be supported soon", while the capabilities FAQ says MP3 **and WAV**. WAV is available through
  the API's `output_format` enum regardless; the two vendor pages disagree about the UI/export path.
- Export *quality* is tier-gated: standard for Free/Starter/Creator, "high-fidelity, studio-grade" for
  Pro and above — a second gate on top of the per-minute price.
- Billing in the UI is credit-based and scales with track length **and number of variants**; the
  guide points at the pricing page rather than quoting a rate.
- Variants and Auto-duration are documented UI controls; the API's compose body has no variants
  field, so variants are a product-layer feature.

## Gaps

- No credit-per-minute conversion, so UI cost cannot be reconciled with the API's $0.15/minute.
- No statement of what "Auto" duration resolves to, nor of any cap on variants per request.
- No mention of watermarking or C2PA.
- The 10-minute figure is a UI statement; it does not say the API accepts the same maximum (it does,
  per the compose schema).

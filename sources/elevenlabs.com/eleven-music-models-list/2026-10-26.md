## What this establishes

- The vendor itself ranks `music_v1` below v2 and v2.5: "Outclassed by `music_v2` and `music_v2_5`".
- Music generation has **zero concurrency on the Free plan** — music is not callable at all through
  the API on Free, consistent with the capabilities page ("The Music API is available for paid
  subscribers").
- No latency figure is published for any music model (unlike the TTS models on the same page).
- Language coverage is stated as four named languages plus "and more" for every music model, with no
  ISO list and no count.

## Gaps

- No quality metric, benchmark or listening-test result for any music model.
- No per-model output-length or format differences are stated here.
- The page does not say whether `music_v1`'s "outclassed" status changes its support window.

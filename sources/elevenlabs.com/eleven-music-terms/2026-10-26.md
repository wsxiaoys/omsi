## What this establishes

- Commercial rights are **tier-dependent**, not a blanket "cleared for commercial use":
  - **Free**: no downloads at all, attribution required, and generation capped at 11 minutes/month.
  - **Starter → Business**: online/offline commercial use **except film, TV, radio and Studio
    Games**; streaming distribution permitted from Creator upward; no attribution required.
  - **Enterprise Music**: the only tier whose media rights include film, TV and radio.
- Building or contributing to a "Music Library" — the thing stock-music resellers do — is prohibited
  from Free through Business, and "Model Resale" likewise.
- Eligibility caps who may buy Scale (< 10 employees) and Business (< 50 employees); plan enrollment
  by an ineligible user can void the account, output or credits.
- The Exhibit A monthly *generation* limits (11 → 4,800 minutes) are a different schedule from the
  API pricing page's "Minutes included" (3 → 6,600 minutes); they are not reconciled.
- ElevenLabs reserves the right to withdraw models, change terms prospectively (material reductions
  with 30 days' notice) and to designate iterations as separate models.

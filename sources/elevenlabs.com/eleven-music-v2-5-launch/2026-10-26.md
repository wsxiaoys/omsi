## Captured content (verbatim)

> ## Introducing Music v2.5, our best music model yet
> Published Sep 11, 2026 / Last updated Sep 20, 2026

> #### Summary
> - Music v2.5 is now the default for prompted and reference generation in ElevenMusic.
> - Songs sound more layered and complex, with better, more natural-sounding instruments.
> - You own what you make in ElevenMusic, on every plan, including Free.
> - Free gets five lossless downloads a day, Pro gets 400 lossless downloads per month.
> - Downloads are blocked on tracks that reference other artists' songs.

> Music v2.5 is ElevenMusic's best music model yet. The instruments sound real. Songs have more
> layers, more movement, and they hold together all the way through.

> In a blind test with the same prompt and two takes (one from each model) across **47,885 pairs**,
> v2.5 was the preferred take the majority of the time.

> The gap is widest with songs that are vocal-led and acoustic-heavy genres like R&B, soul, hip hop,
> rock, metal, orchestral and cinematic music.

> 2.5 is the new default in ElevenMusic, but if you're attached to Music v2, don't worry. It isn't
> going anywhere.

> Every track you make in ElevenMusic is yours. On the Free plan you get five lossless downloads a
> day, and you can use what you make commercially as long as you credit ElevenMusic. On Pro, you get
> 400 lossless downloads per month. The permissions that apply when you create a track stay with it,
> so cancelling or downgrading doesn't change how you can use tracks you've already created.

> Music v2.5 is live in ElevenCreative and the ElevenLabs API, too.

> Note: ElevenLabs recently announced a multi-year strategic agreement with Universal Music Group
> (UMG) spanning licensing and product development. This deal is separate from Music 2.5.

## What this establishes

- **Music v2.5 was announced 2026-09-11** — inside the roster window, and the current top generation
  of the family. It is the default for prompted and reference generation **in ElevenMusic (the
  product)**.
- The only quantitative quality evidence anywhere in the corpus is this **vendor-run blind test:
  47,885 prompt pairs, one take from each model, v2.5 "preferred the majority of the time"**. The
  page publishes no preference rate, no confidence interval, no rater count, no rater population and
  no audio. It is a vendor claim, not an independent measurement.
- Per-track permissions are frozen at creation time: downgrading or cancelling does not retract
  rights on already-created tracks, and future term changes apply only to new tracks.
- Tracks built on another artist's song are the exception — downloads are blocked for them.
- Free-tier rights as described here (five lossless downloads/day, commercial use with credit)
  conflict with Exhibit A of the Music Terms ("Monthly Download Limit: Not permitted" on Free); see
  the model page's contradictions section.

## Gaps

- No preference percentage, rater count or methodology beyond "blind test … 47,885 pairs".
- No technical detail: no sample rate, formats, length limits, or price for v2.5 specifically.
- "You own what you make" is a product-page statement; the Music Terms govern and do not use an
  ownership grant (see the Music Terms record).
- No independent re-measurement of the v2 → v2.5 delta exists in this corpus.

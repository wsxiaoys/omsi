## Captured content (verbatim)

> ## Introducing Music v2
> Published May 26, 2026 / Last updated Oct 2, 2026

> Music v2 delivers better vocals, instrumentation, and arrangement across every genre, with improved
> multilingual support and a set of new capabilities.

> Music v2 powers three ElevenLabs platforms, each built for a different use case:
> - ElevenMusic — listen, remix, and create tracks
> - ElevenAPI — embed music generation directly in your product
> - ElevenCreative — downloadable music for ads, branded content, and video

> Alongside this release, we've cut both Music v1 and Music v2 pricing by up to 50% for ElevenAPI and
> up to 40% for ElevenCreative self-serve customers.

> A single song can move from opera to heavy metal and back, sustain fast rap and dense lyrical
> delivery, and embed non-musical sound effects directly within the track, all without breaking
> musical coherence.

> With improved inpainting, you can select any section of a track and regenerate just that part,
> tweaking the bridge without touching the chorus, leaving everything else exactly as it is.

> Rather than generating short clips, Music v2 lets you build a full song section by section — intro,
> verse, chorus, and beyond — maintaining structure and continuity throughout.

> Music v2 is available today across ElevenMusic, ElevenCreative and **coming soon to ElevenAPI**.
> Reach out to our Sales team to enquire about early access.

## What this establishes

- **Music v2 was announced 2026-05-26** — inside the roster window (≥ 2025-10-26).
- At announcement the API was explicitly **"coming soon"**, not available; the API launch is a
  separate, later event (2026-06-15 per the changelog).
- ElevenLabs **cut music pricing at v2 launch**: up to 50% for ElevenAPI, up to 40% for
  ElevenCreative self-serve. The pre-cut v1 rate is not published on this page.
- The documented v2 capability delta over v1 is: better vocals/instrumentation/arrangement, improved
  multilingual output, mid-track genre transitions, embedded sound effects, improved inpainting and
  chunked section-by-section composition.
- The "better vocals / better arrangement" claims are unquantified marketing in this post; no
  listening test, metric or sample count is given.

## Gaps

- No numbers behind "better": no preference rate, no benchmark, no listening-test size.
- No output formats, sample rates, length limits, or per-model price table.
- Does not say when v2 would reach the API, nor what happens to `music_v1` default status.

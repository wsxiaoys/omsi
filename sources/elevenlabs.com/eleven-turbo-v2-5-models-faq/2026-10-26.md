## Captured content (verbatim)

The page splits models into "**Flagship Models**" (Eleven v4, Multilingual v2, Flash v2.5, Flash v2)
and a separate section:

> ## **Older Models**
>
> **These models are maintained for backward compatibility but are not recommended for new projects**

Under that heading, the Turbo entry reads:

> **Turbo v2.5** - **(model_id = eleven_turbo_v2_5)**- Eleven Turbo v2.5 is our high-quality,
> low-latency model with a good balance of quality and speed. As the Flash models give very similar
> results, we recommend using the Flash models over Turbo.
>
> For UI generations, 1 character costs 1 credit, excluding credit multipliers. API generations are
> discounted - for details, see our [API Pricing.](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing)
>
> It supports 32 languages, including:

The enumerated language list that follows (32 entries, verbatim order): Arabic, Bulgarian, Chinese,
Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Filipino, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Hindi,
Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian,
Slovak, Spanish, Swedish, Tamil, Turkish, Ukrainian, Vietnamese.

The Turbo v2 entry, immediately after it:

> **Turbo v2 (model_id = eleven_turbo_v2)** - As the Flash models give very similar results, we
> recommend using the Flash models over Turbo.

Turbo v2 is followed by a single language, English.

For contrast, the flagship Flash v2.5 entry:

> **Flash v2.5 - (model_id = eleven_flash_v2_5) -** our latest, ultra-low-latency model, generating
> speech in under 75ms. Best for developer use cases requiring speed and multiple languages.

Flash v2.5 is enumerated with the same 32 languages as Turbo v2.5 (identical list, identical order).

## What this establishes

- The vendor's **own status label** for `eleven_turbo_v2_5` at capture is "Older Models … maintained
  for backward compatibility but are not recommended for new projects". It is retired from the
  flagship tier but not switched off.
- The vendor asserts **near-equivalence with Flash v2.5** ("the Flash models give very similar
  results") and directs new work to Flash. That is a vendor claim about output similarity, not an
  independent measurement, and the page gives no quality, WER or preference evidence for it.
- `eleven_turbo_v2_5` and `eleven_flash_v2_5` are enumerated with the **same 32 languages**. So the
  language coverage argument for choosing Turbo over Flash does not exist at this date.
- Turbo v2.5 is distinguished from Turbo v2 by language coverage (32 vs 1), matching the vendor's
  model table.

## Gaps

- No latency figure in milliseconds for Turbo v2.5 on this page; only Flash's "under 75ms" is stated.
- No character limit, price, output-format or sample-rate statement for Turbo v2.5 here.
- No removal/retirement date and no notice period for the "Older Models" tier.
- No statement of which plan tiers can still call `eleven_turbo_v2_5`.

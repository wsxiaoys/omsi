## Captured content (verbatim)

### Website (ElevenCreative Text to Speech)

> In Text to Speech, using the website, you can generate up to 10,000 characters in a single
> generation when using Eleven v4, and up to 5,000 characters for all other models.

> However, if you plan on generating longer-form content of more than a few thousand characters, we
> highly recommend using Studio which allows you to generate extremely long-form content such as books
> and novels very easily.

### API, Text to Speech

> Eleven v4 - up to 10,000 characters (~10 minutes of audio)
>
> Eleven v3 - up to 5,000 characters (~5 minutes of audio)
>
> Flash v2.5 - up to 40,000 characters (~40 minutes of audio)
>
> Turbo v2.5 - up to 40,000 characters (~40 minutes of audio)
>
> Flash v2 - up to 30,000 characters (~30 minutes of audio)
>
> Turbo v2 - up to 30,000 characters (~30 minutes of audio)
>
> Multilingual v2 - up to 10,000 characters (~10 minutes of audio)

### API, Voice Changer

> Multilingual v2 - up to 10 minutes of audio

## What this establishes

- **Vendor ceiling for `eleven_v3`: 5,000 characters per request, ≈5 minutes of audio**, on both the
  website and the API. This is the number fal and the vendor agree on, unlike the v4 case where fal's
  cap is half the vendor's.
- The "≈5 minutes" conversion is the vendor's own, so any per-minute cost estimate for v3 should be
  derived from it rather than assumed.
- No route publishes a hard audio-duration cap independent of the character count; duration is
  described only as an approximation of the character limit.

## Gaps

- No statement of what happens at the boundary (truncation vs 400 error) and no statement about
  whether Text to Dialogue has a different limit.
- The page gives no limit for `eleven_v3_conversational`, which does not appear at all.
- No statement about billed characters when a request is rejected.

## What this establishes

- **GA date: 2026-02-02.** That is inside the roster's one-year window (≥ 2025-10-26) even though
  the model's first announcement (2025-06-03) is not. Both dates are therefore material: the model
  is an in-window *general availability* of an out-of-window *launch*.
- ElevenLabs ties GA to a model change, not a paperwork change: the GA release differs from the alpha
  the roster row was discovered from. Claims about "Eleven v3" therefore have a version boundary at
  2026-02-02 that no checkpoint ID marks.
- The two quality numbers — 72% user preference and 15.3% → 4.9% error rate — are **vendor-internal**,
  with no harness, text set, language list beyond "8 languages", rater pool or date published. The
  page does not name the "previous Alpha release" version either.

## Gaps

- No methodology for the 27-category benchmark: no test set size, no scoring rule, no per-language
  breakdown, no confidence intervals, and no third-party replication captured anywhere.
- The 72%-preference figure gives no sample size, no evaluation interface and no rater demographics.
- No pricing, latency, format or limit change is announced alongside GA, so the post says nothing
  about whether GA changed per-character billing.
- The post does not say what, if anything, changed about audio tags, PVC support or language coverage
  at GA — only stability and text normalization are claimed.

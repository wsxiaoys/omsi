copy as marketing shorthand.

### Credits (verbatim, from the consumer pricing FAQ on elevenlabs.io/pricing)

> Monthly price and included credits per plan: Free $0 (10,000 credits); Starter $6 (30,000 credits);
> Creator $22 (121,000 credits, $11 for the first month); Pro $99 (600,000 credits); Scale $299
> (1,800,000 credits, 3 seats); Business $990 (6,000,000 credits, 10 seats); Enterprise is custom.

> Each text character you generate consumes credits, with the exact cost depending on the model used.
> For V2 Multilingual models, 1 text character equals 1 credit. For V2 Flash/Turbo English and V2.5
> Flash/Turbo Multilingual models, discounted pricing applies for API usage, costing between 0.5 and
> 1 credit per character.

> Text to Speech 1 credit per character

Neither FAQ states a v4-specific credit multiplier. The API page quotes USD per 1,000 characters and
the consumer page quotes credits per character; the two are not tied together on either page for v4.

### Plan-gated audio quality (verbatim, from the TTS API reference)

> MP3 with 192kbps bitrate requires you to be subscribed to Creator tier or above. PCM and WAV
> formats with 44.1kHz sample rate requires you to be subscribed to Pro tier or above.

## What this establishes

- Vendor list price for `eleven_v4` is **$0.08 per 1,000 characters**; for `eleven_v4_turbo`,
  **$0.04 per 1,000 characters**. Both are the same rate as the previous-generation models they
  replace (v3 $0.08, v3 Conversational $0.04, Multilingual v2 $0.08) — v4 did not come with a price
  increase.
- Billing basis is **per character of input text**, not per second of audio and not per request.
- Higher-bitrate and 44.1 kHz PCM/WAV output are plan-gated, so the advertised per-character price
  does not buy the top output formats on the cheap plans.

## Gaps

- No published per-character rate for `eleven_v4` on the Text to Dialogue endpoint specifically; the
  page presents one TTS rate per model and does not distinguish endpoints.
- The consumer FAQ's credit model (1 credit/character) is not reconciled with the API page's USD
  per-1K-character prices, so the effective USD-per-credit rate depends on the plan and is not stated.
- No volume-discount or enterprise schedule captured.
- No statement of whether failed or empty generations are billed.

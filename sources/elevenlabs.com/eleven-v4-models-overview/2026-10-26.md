## What this establishes

- ElevenLabs gives `eleven_v4` and `eleven_v4_turbo` **distinct model IDs**, distinct prices (see the
  pricing record) and distinct latency/use-case positioning. They are two variants of one family, not
  one model with a speed flag.
- The v4 family is positioned as the current flagship; v3 is called "previous generation" on the
  Eleven v3 section of this same page, and Multilingual v2 is called "a previous generation" model.
- The vendor's own character limit for `eleven_v4` is **10,000** characters (~10 minutes of audio).
- The vendor routes v4 to the **Text to Dialogue API / WebSocket** in this page's prose, while the
  same page's model table lists `eleven_v4` among the text-to-speech model IDs. Both statements are
  on one page and are not reconciled there.

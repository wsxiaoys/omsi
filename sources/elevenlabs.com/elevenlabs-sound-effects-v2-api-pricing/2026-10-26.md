## Summary

The vendor's list rate for sound effects is **$0.12 per minute of generated audio**, described as
"Royalty-free", with the outputs described as 44.1 kHz MP3 or 48 kHz WAV. The same page says in one FAQ
entry that usage is "billed in US dollars, not credits" and in the next that Music and Sound Effects
are "billed per generation" — two different metering statements that the page does not reconcile with
the per-minute price.

## Gaps

- No rounding rule (per second? per started minute?), no minimum billable duration, and no statement of
  whether failed generations bill.
- "Billed per generation" versus "$0.12 per minute" is unresolved on the vendor's own page.
- No credit multiplier for sound effects (the capability page says 40 credits/second — see
  `../elevenlabs-sound-effects-v2-capabilities/2026-10-26.md`), so credits and USD cannot be tied
  together from these two pages.
- No licence text; "Royalty-free" is a marketing phrase here, not a licence grant.

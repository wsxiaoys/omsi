## Captured content

Under the heading `## October 19, 2026` → `### Music and Sound Effects` (verbatim):

> - **Zero Retention Mode**: Music generation, composition plan, stem separation and video to music
>   endpoints, plus [Sound Effects](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/text-to-sound-effects/convert), accept
>   `enable_logging=false` to use [Zero Retention Mode](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-api/resources/zero-retention-mode).
>   Zero Retention Mode is available to enterprise customers. Music requests that set
>   `store_for_inpainting=true` are rejected in this mode.
> - **WAV output for sound effects**: [Sound Effects](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/text-to-sound-effects/convert)
>   accepts `wav_8000`, `wav_16000`, `wav_22050`, `wav_24000`, `wav_32000`, `wav_44100` and
>   `wav_48000` output formats.

The same entry's SDK notes (JavaScript `v2.74.0`) repeat "WAV output formats for Sound Effects" and

## What this dates

- **WAV output for sound effects is one week old at capture.** Before 2026-10-19 the endpoint's WAV
  formats did not exist; the capability page's "WAV at 48 kHz for non-looping effects" claim and the
  API reference's seven `wav_*` values are both post-2026-10-19 states. A host schema captured before
  that date, or one that has not refreshed, would legitimately lack WAV.
- Zero-retention (`enable_logging=false`) for sound effects is also a 2026-10-19 addition, restricted
  to enterprise customers.

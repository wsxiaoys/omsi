## The capability page

**Snapshot 2025-08-14** — "Usage" section, verbatim:

> Sound effects are generated using text descriptions & two optional parameters:
>
> * Duration: Set a specific length for the generated audio (in seconds)
>   * Default: Automatically determined based on the prompt
>   * **Range: 0.1 to 22 seconds**
>   * Cost: 40 credits per second when duration is specified
> * Prompt influence: Control how strictly the model follows the prompt

No looping parameter, no `model_id`, and a **22-second** ceiling.

**Snapshot 2025-09-02** — the same section, verbatim:

> * Duration: … **Range: 0.1 to 30 seconds** … Cost: 40 credits per second when duration is specified
> * **Looping**: Enable seamless looping for sound effects longer than 30 seconds … Creates sound
>   effects that can be played on repeat without perceptible start/end points
> * Prompt influence: Control how strictly the model follows the prompt

The same snapshot also adds the 48 kHz WAV detail: "For WAV downloads of non-looping sound effects,
audio is delivered at 48kHz sample rate", plus a looping FAQ ("How do looping sound effects work?").

**Snapshot 2025-10-02** — the section is unchanged from 2025-09-02: 0.1–30 seconds, the Looping bullet,
and the 48 kHz WAV sentence are all still present. The v2 surface was therefore stable across the
window boundary.

## Summary

Three dated transitions:

1. **v2 arrived between the 2025-06-21 and 2025-09-08 API-reference snapshots, and between the
   2025-08-14 and 2025-09-02 capability-page snapshots.** The v2 surface is exactly: a `model_id`
   selector defaulting to `eleven_text_to_sound_v2`, a `loop` flag available only on v2, and the
   duration ceiling raised from 22 s to 30 s.
2. `model_id` became a typed enum by 2026-08-21.
3. A second enum value, `eleven_text_to_sound_v3`, appears between the 2026-08-21 and 2026-10-26
   captures, and the "loop is v2-only" qualifier disappears in the same interval.

This is the only first-party dating evidence found for the v2 release; ElevenLabs published no launch
announcement, changelog entry, or blog post for it in anything captured here. The window is
approximately **2025-08-14 → 2025-09-02** (capability page) and **2025-06-21 → 2025-09-08** (API
reference), which straddles fal's 2025-09-02 listing date.

## Gaps

- Archived HTML of these pages is client-rendered in part; only the server-rendered text quoted above
  was recoverable. No model card, announcement, or pricing page from the launch period was archived.
- The snapshots are sparse (roughly monthly to quarterly), so "between 2025-08-14 and 2025-09-02" is
  the tightest statement the archive supports, not a launch day.

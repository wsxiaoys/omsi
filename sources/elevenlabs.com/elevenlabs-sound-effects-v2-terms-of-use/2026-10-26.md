## Summary

Output ownership sits with the user, but **commercial use is plan-gated**: free-tier output is
non-commercial only, and paid plans grant commercial use subject to the Prohibited Use Policy. There is
no separate sound-effects licence and no weights licence — the model is hosted-only, so the terms of use
plus the subscription tier are the whole licence story. The terms were last updated three weeks before
capture.

## Gaps

- No sound-effects-specific clause; nothing addresses training-data provenance or indemnity for
  generated sound effects.
- The Prohibited Use Policy was not captured, so its content is unknown here.
- No statement on whether "Royalty-free" on the pricing page adds anything beyond these terms.

# ElevenLabs Audio Detector and watermarking: scope of the watermark

- Origin: ElevenLabs documentation, read as Markdown via `Accept: text/markdown`:
  - "Audio detector", <https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/audio-tools/audio-detector>
  - "What is watermarking, and why is ElevenLabs using it?",
    <https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/audio-detector/what-is-watermarking-and-why-is-eleven-labs-using-it>
- Evidence kind: official documentation / provider policy
- Publication date: not stated on either page
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: all audio generated by ElevenLabs' own models; the pages do not name individual
  models

## Captured content

### Scope (verbatim)

> Watermarking is a digital technology designed to identify AI-generated content. It embeds imperceptible
> patterns into audio at frequencies masked by existing sound, making the watermark completely inaudible
> while maintaining audio quality.

> ElevenLabs embeds watermarks into audio we generate to make it easier for anyone to verify whether a
> piece of audio originated from our platform.

> Content generated by third-party models available through ElevenLabs is **generally not watermarked**
> unless explicitly stated otherwise. Watermarking is specific to audio generated directly by ElevenLabs
> models.

### Detection (verbatim)

> The Audio Detector can only detect watermarks embedded by ElevenLabs. It will not detect watermarks
> from other AI platforms.

> Watermarking does not affect audio quality and adds zero latency in nearly all cases.

> Our classifier maintains high accuracy (99% precision, 80% recall) for audio files generated with
> ElevenLabs that have not been modified.

### Opt-out

Neither page documents a way to disable watermarking, and the sound-effects API reference exposes no
`watermark` parameter.

## Summary

The watermark policy is stated at the level of "audio we generate", with no per-model carve-out and no
opt-out. Read literally, sound effects generated by `eleven_text_to_sound_v2` carry it. Whether it
survives in audio returned through a host proxy (fal) is not stated anywhere and was not tested. The
99%/80% figures are the vendor's own measurement of its classifier, with no methodology attached.

## Gaps

- No explicit statement that sound effects are watermarked (only "audio we generate").
- No statement about watermark persistence through third-party hosts, re-encoding, or format conversion.
- No opt-out mechanism documented.

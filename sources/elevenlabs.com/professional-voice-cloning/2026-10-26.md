## Captured content (verbatim)

> #### How do I add or upgrade the models used to train my Professional Voice Clone?
>
> All Professional Voice Clones (PVCs) will automatically train on the Flash v2.5, Turbo v2.5 and
> Multilingual v2 models. PVCs trained on English audio will also automatically train on the Flash v2
> and Turbo v2 models.

From the FAQ on cloning restrictions and safeguards:

> * All audio generated by our models can be instantly traced back to the user responsible for the
>   generation.

## What this establishes

- **Turbo v2.5 still receives Professional Voice Clone fine-tunes.** Even under the "Older Models"
  label, every PVC is trained on it automatically, so a cloned voice can still be served by this model
  and existing clones carry a Turbo v2.5 variant.
- Voice-cloning capability is therefore not a differentiator between Turbo v2.5 and Flash v2.5 on the
  vendor route — both are in the same automatic PVC training set.
- The vendor states generated audio is traceable to the generating user. That is a policy claim about
  provenance/watermarking, not a technical description of an inaudible watermark.

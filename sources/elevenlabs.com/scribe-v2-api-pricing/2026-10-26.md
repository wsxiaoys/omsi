## Summary

Vendor list price for batch Scribe v2 is **$0.22 per hour of audio** ($0.0037/min), identical for
Scribe v2 Medical; the realtime sibling is $0.39/hour. Feature surcharges are additive per hour:
keyterm prompting $0.050/hour (~+23%) and transcript editing $0.070/hour (~+32%), which is consistent
with the API reference's "+20%" and "+30%" language after rounding.

The card claim "Over 98% transcription accuracy" is a marketing figure with no test set, language, or
comparison attached.

## Gaps

- Plan-column → plan-name mapping was not captured; the "hours included" figures are recorded as an
  ordered list, not assigned to tiers. A naive cross-check does not resolve it either: the Free plan's
  10,000 credits at 330 credits/minute would be ~30 minutes, not the 4 h 30 min in the first column.
- No statement of what happens to per-hour price at enterprise volume, or whether silence is billed
  (the capabilities page says it is).
- Prices are undated on the page; a later capture would be needed to detect a change.

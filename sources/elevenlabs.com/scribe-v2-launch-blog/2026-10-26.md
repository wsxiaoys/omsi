# ElevenLabs: "Introducing Scribe v2" (launch post)

- Origin: ElevenLabs, "Introducing Scribe v2", <https://elevenlabs.io/blog/introducing-scribe-v2>
- Evidence kind: official announcement (vendor blog, launch-day marketing copy)
- Publication date: **Published Jan 9, 2026** (verbatim byline on the page)
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Scribe v2 (`scribe_v2`); Scribe v1 (named as the model it replaces); Scribe v2
  Realtime (contrasted)

## Captured content (verbatim quotes)

Positioning:

> Scribe v2 is built for batch transcription, subtitling, and captioning at scale. It improves on the
> stability and accuracy of Scribe v1, with better handling of long-form audio, pauses, changes in
> tone, and extended silences.

> While Scribe v2 Realtime is optimized for ultra low latency and agents use cases, Scribe v2 is
> optimized for long and complex recordings, maintaining accuracy across diverse speakers, accents,
> and delivery styles. The result is consistently reliable transcripts across a wide range of
> real-world audio conditions.

Accuracy claim (unquantified, no benchmark named, no WER figure given):

> Scribe v2 achieves the lowest word error rate recorded on industry-standard benchmarks.

Keyterm prompting:

> Keyterm prompting goes beyond standard Custom Vocabulary by using the transcript's context. Select
> up to 100 words or phrases, and Scribe v2 will accurately decide when to transcribe those terms.

Entity detection:

> Scribe v2 includes native entity detection for structured audio analysis. You can select up to 56
> categories across Personally Identifiable Information, health data or payment details.

Multilingual:

> You can send audio that contains multiple languages in a single file. The model automatically
> detects each language and transcribes it correctly without manual segmentation or configuration.

Other launch features listed: "Smart speaker diarization for clear, intuitive speaker labeling";
"Precise word-level timestamps for accurate subtitle alignment"; "Dynamic audio tagging that detects
non-speech events such as laughter or footsteps"; "Enterprise readiness with SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI
DSS L1, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance, EU and India data residency, and zero retention mode support".

Availability:

> Scribe v2 is available today via our API and Creative platform.

The post also states Scribe v2 is the model now used in ElevenLabs Studio for subtitles, captions
and transcription.

## Summary

First-party launch record and the anchor for the model's public date: **2026-01-09**, announced as
generally available the same day ("available today"), not as a preview or beta. It is the only
first-party statement of the *v1 → v2* delta, and that statement is qualitative — "improves on the
stability and accuracy of Scribe v1" for long-form audio, pauses, tone changes and extended
silences — with no number attached to the "lowest word error rate" claim.

Launch-day feature counts in this post (100 keyterms, 56 entity categories) disagree with the
current docs, which list 1000 keyterms and 65 entity types. The docs are later; both are recorded
rather than reconciled.

## Gaps

- No WER number, benchmark name, language count, price, file-size limit, or duration limit is given.
- The claim "lowest word error rate recorded on industry-standard benchmarks" names no benchmark
  and no comparator set, so it cannot be checked as a measurement.
- Nothing states whether the model ID is `scribe_v2`; that comes from the docs.

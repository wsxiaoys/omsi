| Model ID | Description | Languages |

## Scribe v2 section (verbatim)

> Scribe v2 is our state-of-the-art speech recognition model designed for accurate transcription
> across 90+ languages. It provides precise word-level timestamps and advanced features like speaker
> diarization and dynamic audio tagging.

Key features listed: "Accurate transcription with word-level timestamps"; "Speaker diarization for
multi-speaker audio"; "Dynamic audio tagging for enhanced context"; "Support for 90+ languages";
"Entity detection"; "Keyterm prompting"; "Transcript editing".

## Siblings, as documented

- **Scribe v2 Realtime** (`scribe_v2_realtime`) — "our fastest and most accurate live speech
  recognition model, delivers state-of-the-art accuracy in over 90 languages with an ultra-low 150ms
  of latency". Streaming specifics: "Support for PCM (8kHz to 48kHz) and μ-law encoding", VAD, manual
  commit control, entity detection, transcript editing. The realtime feature list does **not** include
  diarization or keyterm prompting, though the capabilities page says keyterm prompting works on
  Scribe v2 Realtime.
- **Scribe v2 Medical** (`scribe_v2_medical`) — "a fine-tune of Scribe v2 that improves recognition of
  drug names, anatomy, pathology, and clinical dictation while matching Scribe v2's accuracy on
  everyday speech. It uses the same Speech to Text API as Scribe v2 and is billed at the same rate."
  Batch only; "eligible for HIPAA, with Business Associate Agreements available to Enterprise
  customers as well as Zero Retention Mode (ZRM)". Stated intended purpose excludes interpretation or
  diagnosis; output is "intended for review and correction by a healthcare professional".

## Summary

ElevenLabs gives Scribe v2 its own stable model ID (`scribe_v2`), its own pricing tier and a
documented successor relationship from `scribe_v1`, which is explicitly deprecated in favour of
`scribe_v2`. That is a vendor-level identity, not just a host slug, so Scribe v2 is a distinct model
entity rather than a repackaging of Scribe v1.

The family is three IDs — batch (`scribe_v2`), realtime (`scribe_v2_realtime`) and a medical
fine-tune (`scribe_v2_medical`) — all served through one Speech to Text API.

## Gaps

- No release, GA, or preview date for any Scribe ID on this page.
- No file-size or duration limits here (they are on the capabilities page).
- The page says `scribe_v1` is deprecated but gives no retirement date, so v1's continued
  availability cannot be dated from this record.

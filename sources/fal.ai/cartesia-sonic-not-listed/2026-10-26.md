## What this establishes

fal — which hosts ElevenLabs, Google, Inworld, Qwen, xAI and a dozen open TTS models — **does not
serve Cartesia Sonic** in any version at capture, under either the `fal-ai/` or new-style namespace.
Cross-host comparison for Sonic 3.6 is therefore Cartesia-direct versus LiveKit Inference, not fal.

## Gaps

- Absence at one capture date is not permanent; fal may add Cartesia later.
- No statement from fal or Cartesia about why the model is not hosted.
- A `keywords` search is fuzzy; the zero-hit `endpoint_id` lookups are the stronger evidence.

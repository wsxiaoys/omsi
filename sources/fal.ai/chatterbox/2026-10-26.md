# fal.ai: Chatterbox family endpoints (catalogue, schemas, pricing)

- Origin: fal.ai public model catalogue and endpoint surfaces:
  - `GET https://fal.ai/api/models?keywords=chatterbox&limit=50` and `?keywords=resemble&limit=50`
    (5 items)
  - `GET https://api.fal.ai/v1/models?endpoint_id=fal-ai/chatterbox` (returns the bare endpoint ID
    with no model record)
  - `GET https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/chatterbox/text-to-speech/llms.txt`,
    `…/fal-ai/chatterbox/text-to-speech/multilingual/llms.txt`,
    `…/fal-ai/chatterbox/speech-to-speech/llms.txt`,
    `…/resemble-ai/chatterboxhd/text-to-speech/llms.txt`,
    `…/resemble-ai/chatterboxhd/speech-to-speech/llms.txt`
- Evidence kind: provider documentation (host catalogue metadata, input schemas, price strings)
- Publication date: catalogue `date` / `publishedAt` per endpoint (below); capture date 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Chatterbox (original), Chatterbox Multilingual v2, Chatterbox speech-to-speech,
  ChatterboxHD (hosted)

## Endpoint identity — the roster label resolves to nothing

The roster's discovery path `fal-ai/chatterbox` is a **namespace, not an endpoint**.
`api.fal.ai/v1/models?endpoint_id=fal-ai/chatterbox` returns
`{"models":[{"endpoint_id":"fal-ai/chatterbox"}],"next_cursor":null,"has_more":false}` — a bare ID with
no title, date or schema. The live endpoints all carry a suffix, and there are two separate namespaces:

| endpoint_id | title | category | `date` = `publishedAt` | `modelFamily` / `modelLab` | `hostingType` | `licenseType` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `fal-ai/chatterbox/text-to-speech` | Chatterbox | text-to-speech | 2025-06-01T19:53:08.674Z | Chatterbox / Resemble AI | serverless | commercial |
| `fal-ai/chatterbox/speech-to-speech` | Chatterbox | speech-to-speech | 2025-06-01T19:56:11.760Z | Chatterbox / Resemble AI | serverless | commercial |

| `resemble-ai/chatterboxhd/text-to-speech` | Chatterboxhd | text-to-speech | 2025-06-02T19:16:12.548Z | Chatterbox / Resemble AI | serverless | commercial |
| `resemble-ai/chatterboxhd/speech-to-speech` | Chatterboxhd | speech-to-speech | 2025-06-02T19:21:41.438Z | Chatterbox / Resemble AI | serverless | commercial |

All five are `status: "public"`, `deprecated: false`, `removed: false`. Every date is **before the

Multilingual v3.

### `fal-ai/chatterbox/speech-to-speech` — $0.015 per minute

`source_audio_url` (required), `target_voice_audio_url` (optional; described as "Required URL" in its
own description — a host-side contradiction). Output WAV.

### `resemble-ai/chatterboxhd/text-to-speech` — $0.04 per 1,000 characters

Adds a **preset voice library** — `Aurora`, `Blade`, `Britney`, `Carl`, `Cliff`, `Richard`, `Rico`,
`Siobhan`, `Vicky` — plus `audio_url` zero-shot cloning that overrides the preset, `exaggeration`
(0.25–2, default 0.5), `cfg` (0–1), `temperature` (0.05–5), `seed`, and crucially
`high_quality_audio`: "If True, the generated audio will be upscaled to 48kHz. […] If False, the
generated audio will be 24kHz."

`resemble-ai/chatterboxhd/speech-to-speech` — $0.02 per minute, with the same nine preset target
voices and the same `high_quality_audio` 24 kHz / 48 kHz switch.

## What the host adds and what it changes

- **Defaults drift from the vendor's.** The vendor's documented default exaggeration is 0.5
  (`exaggeration=0.5`, `cfg=0.5`); `fal-ai/chatterbox/text-to-speech` ships `exaggeration: 0.25` and
  exposes the guidance weight as `cfg`, while the multilingual endpoint calls it `cfg_scale` and the
  HD endpoint calls it `cfg`. A request copied between fal endpoints silently changes behaviour.
- **The original endpoint documents emotion tags the vendor attributes to Turbo.** fal lists
  `<laugh>`, `<chuckle>`, `<sigh>`, `<cough>`, `<sniffle>`, `<groan>`, `<yawn>`, `<gasp>` on the
  original Chatterbox endpoint, in angle brackets; Resemble AI says paralinguistic tags "are now native
  to the Turbo model" and the Turbo/Nano card lists them in square brackets (`[laugh]`). The two
  descriptions are not reconcilable from the captured evidence.
- **A second, higher-priced product shares the name.** `resemble-ai/chatterboxhd/*` costs 1.6× the
  open-model endpoints ($0.04 vs $0.025 per 1,000 characters), adds Resemble's preset voices, and is
  the only fal route with a 48 kHz option. Nothing on fal explains what "HD" changes in the model.
- **Character ceilings are host-specific**: 5,000 on the English endpoint, 300 on the multilingual one.
  The vendor publishes no ceiling for either.

## Summary

fal hosts the pre-window generations only (English and multilingual v2, plus the hosted HD variant),
bills per input character for TTS and per minute for speech-to-speech, returns WAV with no format
control, and offers no route to Turbo, Nano, Flash or Multilingual v3. Its schema is the only place in
the corpus where the 24 kHz / 48 kHz choice is exposed.

## Gaps

- fal does not document the model checkpoint behind any of these endpoints, so it cannot be determined
  whether `fal-ai/chatterbox/text-to-speech` runs the original 2025-05 weights or a later revision.
- No documented watermarking behaviour on fal output, and no `watermark` parameter either way.
- No latency, throughput or concurrency information for any endpoint.
- `resemble-ai/chatterboxhd` is not linked from any Resemble AI page captured for this page; the
  relationship between "ChatterboxHD" and the vendor's current model line is unexplained.

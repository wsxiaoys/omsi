## What this establishes

- fal created both [flex] endpoints on **2025-11-25**, the same day as BFL's launch entry, and
  groups them under a single catalogue key, `Flex`, with two labels. That matches BFL's
  one-model-two-modes structure rather than introducing a new model identity.
- `license_type: "commercial"` is fal's catalogue field, i.e. fal's assertion about use through
  fal, not a BFL licence statement (there are no public [flex] weights).
- `updated_at` is 2026-10-20 for both endpoints — a catalogue edit timestamp, not a model
  revision. It is not evidence either way about whether fal picked up BFL's 2026-01-29 speed
  update.

## Known gaps

- The catalogue has no model-revision or weights-version field, so the [flex] snapshot fal serves
  is unknown.
- `date` semantics are not documented (creation, first publication, or first successful
  inference).

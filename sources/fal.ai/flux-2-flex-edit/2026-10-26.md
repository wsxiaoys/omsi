### Pricing (verbatim)

> Your request will cost **$0.05 per megapixel on both input and output side**, rounded up to the
> nearest megapixel. For example, a **1024x1024** image will cost **$0.05**, and a **1920x1080**
> image will cost **$0.10** ($0.05 × 2 megapixels). Similarly, a **512x512** output costs
> **$0.05** for the output image (0.26 megapixels, rounded to 1 megapixel); input-image charges
> are additional.

### Multi-reference (verbatim)

> FLUX.2 \[flex] combines up to 10 reference images with adjustable inference steps and guidance
> tuning for context-aware edits.

> **Multi-image composition**: Combine up to 10 reference images (14 MP total input) in a single
> edit. Reference specific images by index ("the jacket from image 2") or let the model interpret
> naturally described elements

> * **Configurable quality control**: Adjust inference steps based on edit complexity. Quick color
>   swaps need fewer steps; complex multi-image compositions benefit from higher step counts
> * **Guidance scale for edits**: Control how strictly the model follows your editing
>   instructions vs. maintaining source image coherence
> * **Superior text rendering in edits**: Industry-leading typography accuracy for correcting
>   text in existing images, updating signage, or refining design mockups
> * **Natural language editing**: Describe changes without masks or layers
> * **Explicit image indexing**: Reference specific inputs by number

> Built for: Multi-image product compositions | Brand asset refinement with style references |
> Typography corrections | E-commerce visual development | Budget-conscious editing workflows

The page's worked example prompt is a text-in-image edit: *"Change colors of the vase. In a cozy

## What this establishes

- fal splits [flex] into two endpoints (text-to-image and edit), as it does for [pro]
  (`fal-ai/flux-2-pro`, `fal-ai/flux-2-pro/edit`). BFL serves both modes from one endpoint.
- fal documents a **10-reference** ceiling and a **14 MP total input** budget for [flex] edits.
  That is the most explicit input cap found for [flex] anywhere; BFL documents 8 reference slots
  and a 9 MP input+output cap for [pro] only.
- Same default divergence as fal's text-to-image endpoint: steps 28, guidance 3.5.

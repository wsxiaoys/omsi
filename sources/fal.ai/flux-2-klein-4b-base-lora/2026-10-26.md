## What this establishes

- fal's klein 4B fine-tuning chain is: train on `fal-ai/flux-2-klein-4b-base-trainer`, then serve on
  this LoRA endpoint — the catalogue links the two explicitly via `training_endpoint_ids`.
- fal's LoRA path is built on the **Base** checkpoint, not the distilled one, matching BFL's own
  guidance that Base is the training target.
- The endpoint was added on 2026-01-19, four days after the family launch.

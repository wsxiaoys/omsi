(`date` 2026-01-17T00:10:10Z, `updated_at` 2026-10-20T22:24:35Z), while the 4B trainer was deprecated

retired first.

## What this establishes

- The roster's discovery path for this model — `fal-ai/flux-2-klein-4b-base-trainer` — is a
  **deprecated training endpoint**, not an inference endpoint. Anyone following that path today
  lands on a retired page; the live 4B endpoints are `fal-ai/flux-2/klein/4b` (+ `/edit`, `/base`,
  `/base/edit`, `/base/lora`).
- fal's 4B training was step-billed at $0.005/step.
- The trainer is tied to the Base checkpoint and to the LoRA inference endpoint, confirming that
  fal's fine-tuning story for klein 4B is Base-based.

pricing charges the *same* rate for [klein] 4B text-to-image and editing ($0.014 first MP); fal is
the only source captured that prices klein editing higher than klein generation.

## What this establishes

- fal's multi-reference ceiling for klein is **4 images**, matching BFL's "[klein] Up to 4" row and
  BFL's four `input_image*` fields — and contradicting Replicate's base-model copy that claims up to
  10 references for klein.
- Editing costs 2x generation on fal, which affects the cost calculus for multi-reference work: a
  4 MP edit is ~$0.04 on fal versus ~$0.017 on BFL (first MP $0.014 + 3 additional MP × $0.001,
  ignoring input MP).
- Hex colour control is a documented fal claim for klein editing, not only for [pro].

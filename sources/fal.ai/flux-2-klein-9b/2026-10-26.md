## Pricing (verbatim)

> Your request will cost **$0.006 per megapixel**.

Only $0.001/MP more than the 4B's $0.005/MP on fal, i.e. a 20% premium for the 9B. On BFL's own API
the gap is comparable in absolute terms ($0.015 vs $0.014 first MP) but the 9B's additional-MP rate is
double the 4B's ($0.002 vs $0.001)
([BFL pricing](../../bfl.ai/flux2-klein-4b-pricing/2026-10-26.md)). Either way, the 9B is not priced
as a premium tier — both klein sizes sit far below [pro] ($0.03 first MP).

## Why this record exists

The 9B is the 4B's nearest sibling and the one a reader is most likely to confuse with it. Three
distinctions are documented:

- **License**: 9B weights are FLUX Non-Commercial; 4B weights are Apache 2.0
  ([BFL blog](../../bfl.ai/flux2-klein-4b-blog/2026-10-26.md)).
- **Architecture**: BFL describes the 9B as "a 9B flow model with 8B Qwen3 text embedder,
  step-distilled to 4 inference steps"; no equivalent description exists for the 4B.
- **Endpoints**: the 9B has a `flux-2-klein-9b-preview` variant on BFL's API with KV caching, and
  there is no 4B preview counterpart
  ([BFL overview](../../bfl.ai/flux2-klein-4b-overview/2026-10-26.md)).

## What this establishes

- **Host confirms the launch date**: fal's catalogue `date` for the endpoint is 2026-01-15, the
  same day as BFL's launch post. This is host-side corroboration, not just a restatement.
- **fal exposes steps that BFL does not.** `num_inference_steps` runs 4–8 with a default of 4,
  where the first-party `flux-2-klein-9b` schema has no steps field at all
  (`../../bfl.ai/flux2-klein-9b-api-reference/2026-10-26.md`). Runs above 4 steps are
  off-spec relative to BFL's step-distilled configuration, so fal output at 8 steps is not
  evidence about the model as BFL ships it.
- **fal prices the 9B at $0.006 per output megapixel**, which is materially below BFL's
  release-note figure of "from $0.015 / image"
  (`../../bfl.ai/flux2-klein-9b-release-notes/2026-10-26.md`). The two are not directly
  comparable (per-MP vs per-image, and BFL's pricing page describes a first-MP-plus-additional
  schedule whose numbers did not render), but the gap is large enough that first-party is not
  the cheap route for this model.
- **fal's `license_type: commercial` is fal's statement about use of fal's endpoint**, not a
  relaxation of the FLUX Non-Commercial License that governs the weights. The two coexist: the
  weights are non-commercial, fal's hosted service is sold commercially.
- **Default output format differs by host** (png here, jpeg first-party).
- The description's "native editing capabilities" on a text-to-image endpoint is host marketing
  copy; editing is a separate endpoint (`.../9b/edit`).

## Gaps

- No latency figure, no resolution ceiling in megapixels (only a 14142px per-side cap).
- The endpoint's `updated_at` (2026-07-09) is later than its creation date, but the catalogue
  does not say what changed or which BFL checkpoint (fixed vs `-preview`) fal serves.
- "Enhanced realism, crisper text generation" is unquantified marketing copy.

## Sibling contrast retained from the 4B capture

The 4B page also captured this 9B endpoint as contrast material. Its three documented
distinctions are retained here:

- **License**: 9B weights are FLUX Non-Commercial; 4B weights are Apache 2.0
  ([BFL blog](../../bfl.ai/flux2-klein-4b-blog/2026-10-26.md)).
- **Architecture**: BFL describes the 9B as "a 9B flow model with 8B Qwen3 text embedder,
  step-distilled to 4 inference steps"; no equivalent description exists for the 4B.
- **Endpoints**: the 9B has a `flux-2-klein-9b-preview` variant on BFL's API with KV caching, and
  there is no 4B preview counterpart
  ([BFL overview](../../bfl.ai/flux2-klein-4b-overview/2026-10-26.md)).

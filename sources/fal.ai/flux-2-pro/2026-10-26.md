### Pricing (verbatim)

> Pricing starts at **$0.03 for the first output megapixel**, plus **$0.015 per additional
> input or output megapixel**, with megapixels rounded up. For this pricing schedule,
> **1 megapixel is 1024 × 1024 pixels**. For example, a **1024 × 1024** output costs
> **$0.03**, and a **1920 × 1080** output costs **$0.045** without image inputs.

### Generation controls (verbatim bullets)

- "**Zero-configuration quality**: Professional-grade outputs without tuning steps or guidance
  parameters. The model's internal optimization handles quality decisions"
- "**Production consistency**: Predictable results across batch generations"
- "**Fast iteration cycles**"
- "**Flexible output formats**: Choose between JPEG [...] or PNG"
- "**Reproducible generations**: Seed control maintains consistency across variations without
  exposing low-level inference parameters"
- "**Prompt enhancement**: Automatic prompt upsampling refines instructions for optimal
  interpretation (enabled by default)"

### Prompting features documented by fal

- JSON structured prompts (schema includes `scene`, `subjects[]`, `style`, `color_palette`,
  `lighting`, `mood`, `composition`, `camera{angle,distance,lens}`).
- HEX color codes: "Include the keyword 'color' or 'hex' before the code for best results."
- `@` image referencing: `"@image1 wearing the outfit from @image2"`; "maintaining support for
  traditional 'image 1', 'image 2' indexing."

## Captured content

Hero copy: "Built by Black Forest Labs with a 32B parameter architecture, multi-reference
editing, up to 4MP output, and enhanced typography. Available as Pro, Dev, Flex, and Max
variants via a fast serverless API."

### Architecture (verbatim)

> FLUX.2 couples a Mistral-3 24B vision-language model with a rectified flow transformer
> operating on latent image representations.

### Multi-reference (verbatim)

> FLUX.2 supports multi-reference editing, allowing you to combine up to 10 source images into
> a single coherent output at up to 4 megapixels.

The FAQ repeats this: "FLUX.2 can accept up to 10 reference images in a single request using
the @ syntax in your prompt (e.g., @image1, @image2)."

### FAQ: cost per variant (verbatim)

> FLUX.2 [dev] starts at $0.012/MP, [pro] at $0.03/MP, [flex] at $0.05/MP, and [max] at
> $0.07/MP. For a standard 1024x1024 image, that means generation starts at just over one cent.

### FAQ: commercial use (verbatim)

> Yes. Images generated through the fal.ai API with FLUX.2 [pro] and [dev] can be used in
> commercial projects. FLUX.2 [dev] is also available with a commercial license for
> self-hosted deployments. Check fal.ai's terms of service for full details on usage rights
> and licensing.

### FAQ: LoRA training (verbatim)

> Training costs $0.008 per step, and trained LoRAs can be applied at inference time through
> the LoRA endpoints.

### FAQ: resolution (verbatim)

> FLUX.2 supports output resolution up to 4 megapixels [...] You can choose from a variety of
> preset aspect ratios including square, landscape (16:9, 4:3), and portrait orientations, or
> specify custom dimensions through the image_size parameter.

## Contradiction to preserve

This page claims **up to 10 reference images in a single request** via the API. BFL's own
overview states **8 via API, 10 in the playground**
(see [bfl.ai/flux2-overview/2026-10-26.md](../../bfl.ai/flux2-overview/2026-10-26.md)), and
Replicate states eight on the API. fal's model page (`fal-ai/flux-2-pro`) does not state a
count. The 10-image figure may describe fal's own pipeline, may be a marketing rounding of the
playground limit, or may be stale. Not resolved by this capture.

## Notes

- The "32B parameter architecture" figure is fal's; BFL's docs do not state a parameter count,
  and 24B (VLM) + transformer does not obviously sum to 32B. Recorded as a host claim.
- The "just over one cent" line refers to [dev] at $0.012/MP, not [pro].

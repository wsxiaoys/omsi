### Per-endpoint descriptions (verbatim)

  duration 5-20 seconds (default 5), aspect_ratio (auto, 21:9, 2:1, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, 9:16;

- keyframes-to-video: same, with "Input: keyframes (list of image_url + frame_index, at most
  duration x fps) and a prompt describing how the video moves between them."

Tags: text-to-video and image-to-video carry `["text-to-video"|"image-to-video", "fast", "audio"]`;

## Contradictions and oddities worth keeping

- **1080p exists in the catalogue but not on the landing page.** The landing page's price block
  and FAQ quote 720p only, and every endpoint description says "Output: 720p video". The
  catalogue's price override names a 1080p rate. Either 1080p is selectable (the API schema says
  it is — see `sources/fal.ai/flux-3-max-api-schema/2026-10-26.md`) and the landing page is
  incomplete, or the landing page is right and the price override is generic. Unresolved.
- **"Two at launch" vs three endpoints.** The landing-page FAQ says two endpoints launched;
  keyframes-to-video was created five minutes after image-to-video (01:41:04 → 01:46:08) and
  published the same day.
- **Promotion start date precedes the endpoints.** The discount is described as running from
  "October 15", but all three endpoints have `date` 2026-10-16. A one-day gap; not explained.
- The `blackforestlabs/` namespace is BFL's, but the model is fal's post-trained variant. Namespace
  ownership is not a statement about who trained the checkpoint.
- The roster's discovery path `blackforestlabs/flux-3-max` is a **landing-page slug**, not a
  callable endpoint: `landingPageSlug: "flux-3-max"` and the page is `/models/blackforestlabs/flux-3-max`.
  Calls go to one of the three task paths above.

## Gaps

- The catalogue gives no checkpoint hash, parameter count, or revision history beyond
  `date`/`publishedAt`.
- No statement of what data fal post-trained on, or of the safety evaluation for this variant.

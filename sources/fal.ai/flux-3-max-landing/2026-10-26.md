# fal.ai: FLUX 3 Max landing page ("post-trained by fal")

- Origin: fal.ai, "FLUX 3 Max: Text and Image to Video, Post-Trained by fal",
  <https://fal.ai/flux-3-max>. This is the destination of BFL's own "Try FLUX 3 Max on fal"
  button on <https://bfl.ai/models/flux-3-video#flux-3-max>.
- Evidence kind: provider documentation (host and post-training author; fal Research co-developed
  the model, so this is a **developer** record rather than an independent host record)
- Publication date: not stated as a date on the page; the page describes a launch promotion
  running "October 15" to "November 7"
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: FLUX 3 Max (fal `blackforestlabs/flux-3-max/*`), FLUX 3 (base)

## Identity statements (verbatim)

> FLUX 3 Max is the newest post-trained video model from fal Research, built with Black Forest
> Labs on FLUX 3. Tied for #1 in image-to-video quality, and a 10-second video in about 12
> seconds.

FAQ, "Who made FLUX 3 Max?":

> fal Research made FLUX 3 Max in collaboration with Black Forest Labs, who built the base model,
> FLUX 3. Black Forest Labs brought the foundation model; fal Research brought post-training,
> inference optimization and systems engineering. It comes from the same team that built H3 Max.

"How it was built":

> FLUX 3 Max starts from Black Forest Labs' FLUX 3. fal Research worked on three layers at once,
> because a gain at one layer opens room at the next, and is continuing to post-train the model.
>
> **Post-training** — Working with Black Forest Labs, fal Research kept training FLUX 3 to improve
> visual fidelity, motion coherence, prompt adherence and image-to-video quality, without trading
> one off against another.
>
> **Inference optimization** — The team changed how the model executes to cut generation latency
> and raise throughput without giving up quality, bringing a 10-second video down to 12.45 seconds
> from 126.44 for FLUX 3.
>
> **Systems engineering** — The serving path around the model was tuned on fal's own inference
> engine, so the speedup holds up in reliable, scalable production workloads.

FAQ, "What is a post-trained model?":

> A post-trained model starts from an existing base model and keeps training it on new data and
> objectives, rather than training from scratch. The base model supplies its general knowledge of
> motion, light and composition; post-training sharpens specific behaviors on top of it [...]

Also: "H3 Max and FLUX 3 Max are both post-trained by fal Research and served on the same API, so
you can move a shot from one to the other by changing a single model ID."

And, on scope: "Text to video and image to video share the same post-trained model, so that
fidelity carries across both at 720p."

## Benchmark claims (fal's own numbers)

> FLUX 3 Max scores 1,139 Elo in Artificial Analysis's preliminary image-to-video arena, tied for
> #1 in blind human preference votes and ahead of Gemini Omni Flash 1.1, Wan 3.0 and H3 Max.

> For a 10-second clip, FLUX 3 Max matches Seedance 2.5 on quality, with both at 1,139 Elo, and
> generates it about 22 times faster: 12.45 seconds against 277.80.

> Post-training lifted FLUX 3 by 50 Elo points, from 1,089 to 1,139, while inference work cut a
> 10-second video from 126.44 seconds to 12.45: about 10x faster.

The page's own footnote on that table qualifies the ranking:

> Elo: Artificial Analysis AA-Video-I2V v1.1, preliminary. FLUX 3 Max is tied for #1: its
> confidence interval overlaps the leaders, with MiniMax H3 listed first on that board at 1,141.
> Sorted fastest first. Generation time: average for a 10-second, 16:9 video at 720p/768p, in
> seconds.

Table values as printed: FLUX 3 Max 1,139 Elo / 12.45 s; Gemini Omni Flash 1.1 1,134 / 47.37;
MiniMax H3 (768p) 1,141 / 83.26; FLUX 3 1,089 / 126.44; Wan 3.0 1,127 / 180.16; Seedance 2.5
1,139 / 277.80.

## Endpoints

FAQ: "Which FLUX 3 Max endpoints are available? — Two at launch: text to video and image to
video." The API example uses `fal.subscribe("blackforestlabs/flux-3-max/text-to-video", ...)`.
The catalogue lists a third endpoint (keyframes to video) created the same day, so "two at launch"
is fal's own simplification or was written before that endpoint shipped.

## Gaps

- No model card, no parameter list, no licence text, no weights. The only licence signal on the
  page is that it is served on fal's commercial API.
- The 12.45 s / 277.80 s timings are fal's measurements on fal's infrastructure; the prompt set,
  hardware, batch conditions and concurrency are not stated.
- "Tied for #1" depends on overlapping confidence intervals and on a **preliminary** AA board;
  fal itself prints the interval caveat.

## Summary

fal is the only captured third-party host that prices this model **per character**: **$0.03 per
1,000 characters**, billed as `billing_unit: "1000 characters"`, `provider_type: "partner"`,
`is_partner_api: true`. That is roughly $0.03 per minute of speech, against Google's own
token-priced $0.009/min at standard tier — a ~3.3× markup for the same model. Because it is a
partner proxy rather than a hosted deployment, fal sets `licenseType: "private"`, so it makes no
licence grant of its own.

The schema is a flattening of the Gemini API surface: single `voice` + `style_instructions` for
single-speaker, `speakers` + `turns` for two-speaker dialogue, one WAV output. Voice design, Voice
replication, sample-rate choice, `audio/l16`/mu-law/A-law and streaming are **not exposed** — fal
offers 30 prebuilt voices and WAV only.

## Summary

fal is one of two captured third-party hosts for this model and the only one that prices it **per
character**: **$0.045 per 1,000 characters**, billed as `billing_unit: "1000 characters"`,
`provider_type: "partner"`, `is_partner_api: true`. At a typical ~1,000 characters per minute of
English speech that is roughly **3.3× Google's standard-tier list rate** of $9.00 per 1M audio tokens
($0.0135/min at the vendor's own 25-tokens-per-second conversion).

The schema is a flattening of the Gemini API surface: one `voice` plus `style_instructions` for
single-speaker, `speakers` plus `turns` for two-speaker dialogue, WAV out. Voice design, Voice
replication, sample-rate choice, `audio/l16` / μ-law / A-law and streaming are **not exposed** — fal
offers the 30 prebuilt voices and WAV only. Because it is a partner proxy rather than a hosted
deployment, fal sets `licenseType: "private"` and makes no licence grant of its own.

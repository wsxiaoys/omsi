## Summary

fal serves Gemini Omni 1.1 under a version-pinned slug, `google/gemini-omni-flash/v1.1/*`, with four
tasks: text-to-video, image-to-video, reference-to-video and edit. Endpoint creation dates (2026-08-25)
sit two days before the published date (2026-08-27) that Google gives as both the Gemini API GA date and
the Google Cloud release date, which corroborates the 2026-08-27 launch from an independent party. The
older unversioned endpoints date from 2026-06-30, matching the Gemini API preview date, and are still
active — so the v1.0 model remains obtainable on fal even though Google shut down its own
`gemini-omni-flash-preview` endpoint.

`hostingType: "proxy"` plus an upstream `app_name` of `gemini-omni-1.1-flash` indicates fal forwards to
Google rather than running the model itself, and fal's 720p rate ($0.10/s) equals Google's own published
effective 720p rate, which supports that reading.

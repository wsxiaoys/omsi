## Notes and gaps

- **Sixteen reference images is fal's figure.** OpenAI's guide and model page do not state a
  maximum input-image count for GPT Image 2.5 in the captures used here
  ([guide](../../openai.com/image-generation-guide/2026-10-26.md)). Treat 16 as the host's
  limit on this endpoint, not a documented model limit.
- **Mask polarity differs between fal's default path and the passthrough path.** In standard
  processing "white as edit and black as keep"; with `image_passthrough` or `provider_options`
  the mask must be "an OpenAI-native PNG alpha mask: transparent regions are edited". Mixing
  the two conventions would invert the edit region.
- OpenAI describes masking as "entirely prompt-based" and warns the model "may not follow its
  exact shape with complete precision". fal's schema presents the mask as a hard region
  selector. These are not contradictory but they describe different layers: fal's preprocessing
  plus the model's prompt-guided use of the mask.
- The description's "preserved across many rounds of revision" is host marketing; no round
  count, drift measurement, or test method is given.
- Same gap as the text-to-image page: no per-image dollar cost table was obtainable, and the
  "Commercial use" badge is fal's statement about fal.

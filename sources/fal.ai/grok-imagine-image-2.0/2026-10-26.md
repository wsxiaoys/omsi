## What this establishes

- fal publishes Grok Imagine Image 2.0 as **two proxied xAI endpoints** (`hostingType: "proxy"`),
  listed from **2026-08-06** and published **2026-08-11**, under the distinct slug
  `xai/grok-imagine-image/v2.0/*` rather than the 1.0 endpoint.
- fal's 2.0 surface is **wider than its 1.0 surface**: `quality` (`low`/`medium`/`auto`) is exposed,
  `num_images` reaches xAI's documented 10 (the 1.0 endpoint caps at 4), the full 16-value
  aspect-ratio enum including `21:9` and `5:2` is present (1.0 omits both), `resolution` includes
  `1.5k`, and edits accept **5** references (1.0 caps at 3).
- fal's 2.0 default `quality` is **`medium`**, not xAI's documented default `auto`. A caller who
  omits the field on fal pays the medium rate; on xAI the same omission serves `low` for generation
  and `medium` for editing.
- fal prices 2.0 **by quality and resolution**: $0.04 (low 1K), $0.06 (medium 1K), $0.06 (low 2K),
  $0.08 (medium 2K), plus $0.01 per input image on edits. That is a four-cell price table where
  xAI's public table lists one number ($0.04/image).

## Gaps

- fal does not state which xAI snapshot it proxies, and no page documents what `1.5k` means in
  pixels.
- No latency, throughput, or quality data on either page; the catalogue record carries
  `"examples": []`.
- The price table has no cell for `auto`; the note only says `auto` currently resolves to `low` for
  generation, so an `auto` request's billed rate is not stated on this page.
- `revised_prompt` implies prompt rewriting is always applied; no field disables it.

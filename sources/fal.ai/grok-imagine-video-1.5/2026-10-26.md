## What this establishes

- **fal exposes 1.5 in three modes and no edit/extend** — matching xAI's own capability matrix and
  its `text, image → video` modality line.
- **The 1.5 I2V endpoint went live 2026-05-31**, one day after xAI's dated snapshot alias
  `grok-imagine-video-1.5-2026-05-30` and 16 days before the 2026-06-16 GA post — i.e. fal carried
  the preview. T2V and R2V appeared on 2026-07-29, weeks after GA, consistent with xAI's July
  release note announcing those modes.
- **Host pricing is resolution-tiered, xAI's price table is not.** fal: $0.08 / $0.14 / $0.25 per
  second at 480p / 720p / 1080p, plus $0.01 per reference image. xAI's model page lists a flat
  "$0.080 per second" (`../x.ai/grok-imagine-video-1-5-model-page/2026-10-26.md`). $0.08 matches
  only fal's 480p rate; the two schedules cannot both describe the same 720p generation.
- **Output is 24 fps** in both fal output examples, and `num_frames / duration` is exactly 24. This
  is host sample data, not a documented spec.
- **Host gaps versus xAI:** reference images capped at **7** (xAI: 14); reference-to-video offers no
  1080p and no aspect ratios beyond seven; aspect-ratio enum drops `21:9` and `5:2` on all three
  endpoints; `duration` defaults differ from xAI (6 vs 8, and 8 vs 8 for R2V); no `last_frame`, no
  `keyframes`, and no `generate_audio` toggle on any 1.5 endpoint — fal's 1.5 surface is narrower
  than the vendor's.
- **Passthrough hosting.** `hostingType: "proxy"` on every endpoint, so fal forwards to xAI and does
  not publish weights.
- `enable_safety_checker` is a fal-side knob ("Authorized callers may set false to request relaxed
  screening") with no counterpart in xAI's public docs.

## Gaps

- The `api.fal.ai` metadata API was rate-limited (HTTP 429) throughout the capture, so endpoint
  `updated_at`, `modelDetails`, and any richer per-endpoint documentation were not recorded.
- The landing page is client-rendered; its HTML contained only site navigation, so no fal FAQ or
  host-written capability prose was captured.
- No fal statement of which xAI snapshot is proxied, or whether fal tracks the `-preview` alias.
- No latency, queue-time, or concurrency figures in the catalogue payload.

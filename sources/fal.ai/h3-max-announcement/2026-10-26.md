# fal.ai blog: "Introducing H3 Max by fal"

- Origin: fal.ai, "Introducing H3 Max by fal", Team fal, **2026-08-27**,
  <https://blog.fal.ai/introducing-h3-max-by-fal/>
- Evidence kind: official announcement (developer of H3 Max announcing its own post-trained model)
- Publication date: 2026-08-27
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: MiniMax H3 Max, MiniMax H3 (as the base model)

## Verbatim claims

> Announcing H3 Max, a post-trained version of MiniMax H3 developed by fal Research and optimized
> for maximum speed by fal's inference team.

> In our human preference evaluations, H3 Max ranks #1 across overall quality, prompt understanding,
> and aesthetics against leading video models. It does so while generating a 5-second video in under
> 3 seconds, which is roughly 35x the throughput of the official MiniMax H3 endpoint, and on average
> 15x faster than anything with comparable quality.

> We started with the open-weights MiniMax H3 model and introduced substantial new data during
> post-training, with a particular focus on prompt adherence and visual quality. We aimed for a
> better model at much faster speed and after our work, the base model's core capabilities are intact
> with extremely low latency.

> H3 Max was trained and served entirely on NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 systems.

> We benchmarked H3 Max against twelve leading video models, including the official MiniMax H3
> endpoint, Gemini Omni Flash, Wan 3.0, Seedance 2.5, Kling 3, and Veo 3.1.

> We aggregated these comparisons using Bayesian Elo ratings with 95% confidence intervals.

> H3 Max ranks #1 across all three dimensions and wins the majority of head-to-head matchups against
> every model we tested, including the original H3.

> The results hold up outside our own evaluations. In independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis
> and Design Arena, H3 Max also ranks #1 against other video models.

Quoted from MiniMax's team in the same post:

> H3 Max combines SOTA video quality with a step-change in generation speed, making high-quality
> video generation practical across a much broader range of real-world applications. We've worked
> closely with fal since day one, and their expertise in generative AI infrastructure and bringing
> frontier models into production quickly and reliably makes them a natural partner for H3 Max.

Quoted from Design Arena in the same post:

> MiniMax H3 Max by fal delivers the quality of MiniMax H3 at more than 50× the speed, according to
> Design Arena's independent benchmarking, establishing a new speed–preference Pareto frontier.

Launch terms:

> H3 Max is available today on fal. … For the first week, we're offering H3 Max at 50% off.

Evaluation methodology, verbatim:

> Throughout post-training, we continuously evaluated checkpoints through head-to-head preference
> studies across three dimensions: overall quality, prompt understanding, and aesthetics. Evaluating
> these dimensions independently gave us a much clearer signal than optimizing against a single
> aggregate score.

## What this establishes

- **fal is the developer of H3 Max**, not a reseller: "developed by fal Research", "post-trained
  version of MiniMax H3", started "with the open-weights MiniMax H3 model". This is the only captured
  source that states the derivative's authorship in fal's own voice.
- The launch date is **2026-08-27**, consistent with fal's catalogue `date` of 2026-08-23 and
  `publishedAt` of 2026-08-26 and the endpoint page's "Released Aug 27, 2026".
- fal frames H3 Max as **both faster and better** ("a better model at much faster speed", "#1 across
  all three dimensions"), while MiniMax's own documentation frames it as **only faster** ("the fast
  generation variant", "optimized for high-speed generation"). The two framings are not the same
  claim, and the vendor does not repeat fal's quality claim anywhere captured.
- The speed claim is **quantified but unattributed to a harness**: "roughly 35x the throughput of the
  official MiniMax H3 endpoint" for a 5-second video, with no hardware, batch size, resolution, or
  measurement protocol given. It is a vendor claim about a vendor comparison.
- The preference study is fal's own (12 models, Bayesian Elo, 95% CI) and the blog publishes no
  numbers — no Elo, no win rates, no sample counts.
- The "independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis and Design Arena, H3 Max also ranks #1" claim
  is **contradicted by Artificial Analysis's public leaderboard as captured on 2026-10-26**, where
  MiniMax H3 Max is rank 7 (Elo 1152) and the parent MiniMax H3 (768p) is rank 3 (Elo 1158). See
  `../../artificialanalysis.ai/minimax-h3-max-text-to-video/2026-10-26.md`. The Design Arena
  quotation could not be verified at source.
- The commercial relationship is acknowledged by MiniMax in a quoted statement, which is the
  strongest evidence that the derivative is licensed rather than unaffiliated — but it is a
  testimonial, not a licence.

## Gaps

- No numbers for the preference study, the throughput measurement, or the Elo table.
- No statement of which H3 checkpoint/revision was the starting point, how much data was added, or
  what the post-training recipe was.
- No licence or territory statement for the derivative.
- The Artificial Analysis claim is not dated in the post and does not name the board or category.
- Design Arena's own leaderboard could not be captured with an H3 Max row (its video board is
  client-rendered and returned no H3 Max string on 2026-10-26), so the "more than 50× the speed"
  quotation is second-hand.

# fal.ai: HappyHorse-1.0 landing page (ranking claims, origin story, FAQ)

- Origin: fal.ai model landing page, "HappyHorse-1.0 | AI Video Generator | Official API Partner",
  <https://fal.ai/happyhorse-1.0>
- Evidence kind: provider marketing page and FAQ (independent host record; claims about the model are
  fal's, not the vendor's)
- Publication date: not stated. The page says the model became "Available now as of April 27, 2026 on
  fal as an official API partner."
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Happy Horse 1.0 (all four endpoints); referenced as the family's origin story for
  1.1

## Ranking claims (verbatim)

> **The Top Ranked AI Video Model**
>
> #1 on the Artificial Analysis Video Arena in both Text-to-Video and Image-to-Video, ranked by blind
> human preference votes. Joint audio-video generation in a single pass. Available now as of April 27,
> 2026 on fal as an official API partner.

The page prints its own Elo snapshot:

| Arena | Rank | Elo |

| Image-to-Video (no audio) | #1 | 1392 |

> Source: Artificial Analysis Video Arena, April 2026. Scores reflect early vote counts and may shift
> as more votes accumulate.

## Origin story (verbatim, two passages that do not agree)

> On April 9, 2026, **Alibaba Group Holding Ltd** revealed it created the "Happy Horse" video AI model
> that sent ripples across the AI industry, claiming ownership of a platform that hit #1 on global
> rankings for its debut. Happy Horse is the product of Alibaba Token Hub's innovation business unit,
> now available on fal as an official API partner.

> **Who built HappyHorse-1.0?**
>
> The model was submitted pseudonymously to the Artificial Analysis leaderboard. The team's own
> marketing materials claim it was built by the Future Life Lab team at Taotian Group (Alibaba), led by
> Zhang Di, described as the former VP of Kuaishou and technical lead of Kling AI. **This claim has not
> been independently verified.**

The same FAQ says the model "appeared on the Artificial Analysis Video Arena on **April 7, 2026**",
two days before the "revealed" date in the passage above.

Other page claims about the team and the architecture:

> It uses blind human preference voting where real users compare outputs without knowing which model
> produced them. […] The model reportedly generates video and audio jointly in a single forward pass
> using a unified 40-layer self-attention Transformer with no cross-attention modules.

> **What are the technical specs?** According to the team's own sites: **15 billion parameters**, a
> unified 40-layer self-attention Transformer that generates video and audio jointly in a single
> forward pass with no cross-attention modules. Claimed inference speed is approximately 38 seconds
> for a 1080p clip on a single NVIDIA H100 GPU. **These specs have not been independently verified.**

> **What languages does HappyHorse-1.0 support?** The model supports native lip-sync across seven
> languages: Mandarin, Cantonese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, and French.

## Licensing (verbatim)

> While other industry players say HappyHorse-1.0 will be open source, we can confirm that
> HappyHorse-1.0 will be **closed source**. It will not be licensable or open source.

> **Is HappyHorse-1.0 open source?** No. HappyHorse-1.0 is closed source — it will not be open source
> or licensable. Access is via the official API on fal.

## Pricing on the page (verbatim)

> Pricing is $0.14/second of generated video at 720p and $0.28/second at 1080p. Pay per second, no
> minimums or subscriptions.

## Summary

- fal frames Happy Horse as an **anonymously submitted leaderboard entrant** whose Alibaba authorship
  was asserted afterwards by the team's own marketing; the page itself says that attribution and the
  architecture/speed specs "have not been independently verified". This is the most candid statement
  found on any page about the model's provenance.
- The "#1" claim is dated **April 2026** and refers to the 1.0 model on two no-audio boards with
  "early vote counts". It is repeated verbatim in the 1.1 endpoint descriptions with no date.
- Closed-source status is stated flatly by the host and is consistent with the absence of any weights
  on Hugging Face or ModelScope.
- The only language list for lip-sync (seven languages) appears here, not in vendor documentation.

## Gaps

- No 1.1 equivalent page: `https://fal.ai/happyhorse-1.1` returns HTTP 404, so there is no host page
  describing what changed in 1.1.
- No explanation of the April 7 vs April 9 dates.
- No fal statement on whether the 1.1 endpoints run the same weights/checkpoint as 1.0's.

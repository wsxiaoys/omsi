## Summary

The schema confirms the endpoint is a thin wrapper over HeyGen's `POST /v3/videos` with
`engine.type = "avatar_v"`: same knobs (fit, background, remove_background, output_format, caption,
watermark), plus fal's own `enable_safety_checker`. Two fal-specific choices are notable — the avatar
selector is a *voice-catalogue-style name* with a stock default rather than an ID, and the default
resolution is 720p, whereas HeyGen's own default is 1080p.

`4k` is offered here with no caveat, which conflicts with HeyGen's field description attributing 4K to
Avatar III.

## Gaps

- No documented maximum duration, character limit, or output frame rate.
- The `about` blurb's $0.1500/s does not match the catalogue's $0.10/s; the effective price is
  unresolved.
- `enable_safety_checker` says only "Authorized callers may set false to request relaxed screening" —
  no definition of who is authorized, what is screened, or what a refusal looks like.
- Nothing says which HeyGen plan the proxy account sits on, or whether eligibility (`supported_api_engines`)
  is enforced at fal's boundary before the upstream 400.
- The `voice` enum is a snapshot of HeyGen's stock voices with inconsistent trailing spaces
  (e.g. `"Hope "`, `"Archer "`), which suggests generated-from-API rather than curated; no voice IDs
  are exposed.

## What this establishes

- fal exposes all three modes as separate endpoints, all dated **2026-10-20**, the same day the model
  page's "launch price through October" promo implies a launch window.
- fal's own description repeats the vendor's provenance claim: **built on MiniMax H3 and
  post-trained by HeyGen**. That is a host record of the claim, not an independent check.
- fal's promo pricing mirrors the vendor's page exactly: $0.01/s at 480p/768p, $0.03/s at 1080p/2K
  during October, then $0.02/s and $0.06/s. The 3× multiplier between 768p and 1080p/2K matches the
  vendor's billing rule.
- fal lists all three as `hostingType: "proxy"` with no machine type, i.e. forwarding to HeyGen.

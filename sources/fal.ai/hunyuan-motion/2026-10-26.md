## Gaps

- fal does not state which Tencent checkpoint it serves beyond the parameter-count labels (1B / 0.46B),
  nor the output frame rate, skeleton, or up-axis of the FBX.
- No latency, queue-time, or concurrency information was captured.
- Whether fal's `duration` cap of 12 s is the model's own limit or a host policy is not stated; the
  vendor paper segments training clips at 12 s and the released code defaults to 360 frames at 30 fps,
  which is the same figure, but no captured page says the host adopted it from the model.

**Discrepancy:** $0.22 for High text-to-image is 2.2× the first-party High prompt-only price of
$0.10 and equals the first-party *with-references / Precise Edit* High price

unresolved.

### Input schema (verbatim)

- `prompt` (required): "The generation or editing prompt."
- `image_size`: presets plus 36 exact sizes, including 3328x1248, 3168x1296, 2880x1440, 2560x1600,
  2048x2048 and 1440x2880; "Explicit dimensions must match a supported size"; default `square_hd`.
- `quality`: enum `low`, `medium`, `high`; default `medium`. **No `very_low` option.**
- `enable_prompt_expansion`: "Enable partner prompt expansion." default `true`.
- `num_images`: 1–8, default 1.
- `seed`, `sync_mode`.
- No reference-image, mask, background, or JSON-prompt parameter is exposed on this endpoint.

### Commercial use

Page badge "Commercial use" — fal's statement about use through fal, not a restatement of
Ideogram's terms.

## Known gaps

- No host-side statement of which Ideogram version or snapshot `ideogram/v4.5` tracks, or when it
  was last refreshed.
- The endpoint is a strict subset of the first-party surface (no reference images, no masks, no
  very-low tier, no transparent background), so it cannot be used to test 4.5's central
  reference/multi-turn claims.
- "Accurate typography" is a host restatement of vendor positioning with no measurement.

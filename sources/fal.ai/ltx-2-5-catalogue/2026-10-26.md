## Published pricing (verbatim price strings)

Text-to-video Fast:

> Billing is calculated per second of video generated: **$0.09 at 720p**, **$0.13 at 1080p**, **$0.19 at
> 1440p**, and **$0.30 at 4K**. Native audio is included at every resolution. For example, a 10-second
> video at 1080p costs $1.30.

Image-to-video Fast: the same four prices and the same wording.

Text-to-video Pro:

> Your request will cost **$0.12** per second for 720p or **$0.17** per second for 1080p.

Audio-to-video Fast:

> Billing is calculated at **$0.13 per second of input audio at 1080p**. Native audio is included. For
> example, 10 seconds of input audio cost $1.30.

These are the vendor's own rates, passed through unchanged — compare the LTX API price list
([vendor pricing](../../ltx.io/ltx-2-5-api-pricing/2026-10-26.md)). fal does not add a markup on the
published numbers, and does not offer Pro above 1080p even though the vendor does.

## Input schemas (verbatim parameters)

**`lightricks/ltx-2.5/text-to-video/fast`** (required: `prompt`)

- `prompt` (string)
- `duration` (enum, default `"auto"`): `6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, "auto"` — "At 720p and 1080p, 24
  or 25 FPS supports up to 20 seconds, while 48 or 50 FPS supports up to 10 seconds. At 1440p and 2160p,
  all frame rates support up to 10 seconds."
- `resolution` (enum, default `"1080p"`): `720p, 1080p, 1440p, 2160p`
- `aspect_ratio` (enum, default `"16:9"`): `16:9, 9:16`
- `fps` (enum, default `25`): `24, 25, 48, 50`
- `generate_audio` (boolean, default `true`)
- `camera_motion` (enum): `dolly_in, dolly_out, dolly_left, dolly_right, jib_up, jib_down, static,
  focus_shift`

**`…/text-to-video/pro`** differs: `fps` is `24, 25, 50` (no 48); `duration` is `6, 8, 10, "auto"` with
"up to 10 seconds"; `resolution` is `720p, 1080p` only.

**`…/image-to-video/fast`** (required: `prompt`, `image_url`) adds `end_image_url` ("When provided,
generates a transition video between start and end frames") and `aspect_ratio` with `auto` added.
**`…/image-to-video/pro`** has the same Pro restrictions (720p/1080p, 24/25/50 fps, ≤10 s).

**`…/audio-to-video/fast`** (required: `audio_url`) takes `audio_url` — "Duration must be between 2 and
20 seconds; pro models support a maximum of 10 seconds. Must be publicly accessible or base64 data
URI" — plus optional `image_url`, `prompt`, `aspect_ratio`, `enable_safety_checker` (default `true`,
"Authorized callers may set false to request relaxed screening").

## What fal exposes that the vendor API does not, and vice versa

- fal adds `camera_motion` on the hosted endpoints (the vendor API also has it), `enable_safety_checker`
  on audio-to-video (vendor docs list no such parameter), and an `auto` value for `aspect_ratio` on
  image-to-video.
- fal caps Pro at 720p/1080p and 10 s, while the vendor prices Pro at 1440p and 4K and its support
  matrix allows 4K at 6/8/10 s. **fal's Pro is a narrower product than the vendor's Pro.**
- fal exposes only `16:9` and `9:16` aspect ratios; the vendor API supports `1:1`, `3:2` and `2:3` as
  well.
- fal does not expose `retake`, `extend`, `reframe` or HDR for LTX-2.5 — consistent with the vendor's
  support matrix, unlike the vendor's llm-info page.
- No `seed` parameter on any fal LTX-2.5 endpoint.

## LTX-2.3 endpoints on fal, for contrast

`fal-ai/ltx-2.3/{text-to-video,image-to-video,audio-to-video,retake-video,extend-video,reframe}`,
`fal-ai/ltx-2.3-22b/{…}`, `fal-ai/ltx-2.3-quality/{…}` (inpaint, outpaint, hdr, deblur, colorization,
day-to-night, clean-plate, ingredient, text-to-audio, render-to-real) — all `date` 2026-03-05 or later,
all active. The 2.3 surface on fal is far broader than the 2.5 surface: LTX-2.5 has six endpoints, and
none of the editing/restoration ones.

## Summary

fal hosts all six LTX-2.5 task/variant combinations from launch day at the vendor's own per-second rates,
with a schema that matches the vendor's support matrix for Fast but narrows Pro to 1080p/10 s. Its 2.3
surface is much wider, which is the clearest host-side signal that LTX-2.5's editing and finishing
tooling has not shipped through the API yet.

## Gaps

- `GET /models/pricing` requires an API key, so the price strings above are taken from each endpoint's
  `llms.txt` documentation page rather than the machine-readable price API.
- `updated_at` was only captured for one endpoint.
- No run counts or popularity metrics are exposed by this API for these endpoints.
- The group-level endpoint `lightricks/ltx-2.5` (the URL in the roster) resolves in the catalogue but
  returns no metadata and no OpenAPI schema — it is a landing group, not a callable endpoint.

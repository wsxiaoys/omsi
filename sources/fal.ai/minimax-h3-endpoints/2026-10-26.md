### Verbatim short descriptions

- text-to-video: *"MiniMax H3 is a frontier video model. This endpoint generates video from a text
  prompt alone, rendering at 2K in durations from 5 to 15 seconds across seven aspect ratios."*
- image-to-video: *"MiniMax H3 is a frontier video model. This endpoint animates a supplied image into
  2K video, using it as the opening frame or pairs a first and last frame to control a transition
  between two images with the aspect ratio following the input."*
- reference-to-video: *"…generates 2K video from multimodal references up to 9 images for subject and
  style, 3 video clips for motion, and 3 audio clips each cited in the prompt by order, keeping
  subjects consistent while following the referenced motion and audio."*

## Request schemas (verbatim descriptions)

Response bodies are 6,896 / 7,314 / 7,615 bytes for text-to-video / image-to-video /
reference-to-video, so all three are callable and documented.

| Field | Applies to | Type | Default | Enum / notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `prompt` | all three | string | required | "Text prompt for video generation" |
| `resolution` | all three | string | `2K` | enum `480P`, `768P`, `2K`, `4K` — **"480P and 768P are native generation modes; 2K and 4K upscale a 768P base result."** |
| `duration` | all three | integer | 5 | "The duration of the video in seconds." (no enum; description elsewhere says 5–15) |
| `aspect_ratio` | t2v | string | `16:9` | `21:9`, `16:9`, `4:3`, `1:1`, `3:4`, `9:16` |
| `aspect_ratio` | r2v | string | `adaptive` | `adaptive` plus the six concrete ratios |
| `seed` | all three | integer/null | null | "Random seed. A random seed is selected when omitted." |
| `prompt_expansion_mode` | all three | string | `balanced` | "'disabled' skips prompt expansion. 'fast' returns in about a second. 'balanced' picks per request. 'quality' spends up to ~30s on a richer prompt." |
| `target_audio_url` | t2v, i2v | string | null | Audio clip ≥ 2 s, ≤ 15 MB, pinned to the soundtrack; trimmed to video length; "Exceptionally high sample rates may be resampled to 96 kHz." |
| `enable_safety_checker` | all three | boolean | true | "If set to true, the safety checker will be enabled." |
| `sync_mode` | all three | boolean | false | Return base64 instead of a CDN URL |
| `image_url` / `end_image_url` | i2v | string | null | first / last frame; either may be supplied alone; both omitted ⇒ text-to-video at 16:9 |
| `reference_image_urls` / `reference_video_urls` / `reference_audio_urls` | r2v | array | null | ≤ 9 images; videos and audio 2–15 s each, combined ≤ 15 s; "must add up to at most 12 files" |

Outputs: `video` (File) and `expanded_prompt`, whose description notes it is "Null when prompt
expansion was disabled, left the prompt unchanged, or was performed internally by MiniMax's hosted
API."

## H3 Max, for contrast (row 40)

`minimax/h3-max/*` is a **separate catalogue family** with its own `landingPageSlug: "minimax-h3-max"`
and `modelFamily: "H3 Max"`, but `modelLab: "Fal"` (not "Minimax") and `discovery.lab: "fal"`. Its
verbatim description:

> fal's H3 Max is a post-trained variant of MiniMax H3, tuned for stronger prompt adherence and better
> aesthetics while co-optimized with our custom inference stack for higher throughput with no
> compromises on output quality

Its text-to-video schema differs from H3's: `resolution` enum `480P`, `768P`, `1080P` — *"The native
generation resolution, or 1080P latent refinement from a native 768P source"*; no `2K`/`4K`; and its
price string is a promotional tier ("$0.0325 per second at 480p, $0.052 at 768p, $0.104 at 1080p …
these are promotional rates, **35%** off … The discount ends **October 31**"). Twelve H3 Max endpoints
exist, including `director`, `camera-controls`, `extend-video`, `recast`, `relight`, `reframe-video`,
`insert-video`, `3d-to-video`, `lip-sync/image-to-video`, four `styles/*` presets, plus
`minimax/h3-max-turbo/*`.

## Summary

fal treats H3 and H3 Max as **different models by different labs**: H3 is `modelLab: "Minimax"` and H3
Max is `modelLab: "Fal"`, matching MiniMax's own statement that H3 Max is "post-trained by fal.ai on
MiniMax H3". H3 on fal is three inference endpoints plus LoRA and training endpoints.

Three fal-specific points matter for anyone comparing routes:

1. **fal sells resolutions MiniMax's own API does not list for H3.** fal offers `480P` and `4K` on the
   plain H3 endpoints; MiniMax's H3 flagship offers only 768P and 2K, with 480P confined to its Lite and
   Max variants.
2. **fal states plainly what MiniMax describes at length**: only 480P and 768P are native; 2K and 4K
   "upscale a 768P base result". That is consistent with MiniMax's in-context regeneration design, but
   fal does not say whether its 2K/4K path uses H3-Regenerate-2K or a different upscaler.
3. **fal's H3 prices are lower than MiniMax's at the same resolution** — $0.06/s versus $0.08/s at 768P,
   and $0.13/s versus $0.13/s at 2K — while adding a 4K tier at $0.16/s and a 480P tier at $0.05/s.

fal also exposes fine-tuning of H3 (four trainers, $5–$15 per 1,000 steps) that MiniMax's public API
does not document.

## Gaps

- fal's `duration` field has no enum; the "5 to 15 seconds" claim is prose in the endpoint description
  and is not enforced in the schema, so the true accepted range is unknown from this record.
- No latency, queue-time or throughput figures per endpoint.
- The catalogue gives no indication whether the weights served are the released FL2VA/Ref2VA
  checkpoints, a MiniMax-hosted proxy, or a re-hosted build; `hostingType` is `"serverless"` rather
  than `"proxy"`, which is what fal uses for proxied third-party models such as Seedance 2.0.
- The 4K tier has no first-party counterpart in any captured MiniMax document, and its billing basis
  (upscale of a 768P render at 4K per-second rates) is not explained.
- The `hailuo-03-new` group key is internal metadata; no fal page explains it, so it is a hint about
  lineage rather than a documented alias.

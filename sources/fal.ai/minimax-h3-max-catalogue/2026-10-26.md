## What this establishes

- **fal attributes H3 Max to itself, not to MiniMax.** `modelLab: "Fal"` on all 21 endpoints, versus
  `modelLab: "Minimax"` on the parent H3 endpoints. Combined with `hostingType: "serverless"` (fal
  runs the weights itself, it does not proxy to MiniMax), this is a host claiming authorship of a
  model, not merely a reseller label.
- H3 Max is a **large, actively extended product surface** — 21 endpoints from 2026-08-23 to
  2026-10-15, covering t2v, i2v, reference, camera control, lip sync, extend, insert, recast,
  relight, reframe, 3D-to-video, style LoRAs and two realtime "worlds" endpoints. It is not a
  single re-skinned checkpoint.
- **fal's H3 Max supports 1080p and 2K in several endpoints while MiniMax's own API caps H3-Max at
  768P.** See `../minimax-h3-max-schemas/2026-10-26.md` for the 1080P mechanism.
- **H3 Max is not uniformly cheaper than H3 on fal.** Post-promotion at 768p, H3 Max is $0.08/s
  against H3's $0.06/s; at 480p H3 Max is $0.05/s against H3's $0.05/s. The promotional window
  (ending 2026-10-31) is what makes H3 Max look cheaper.
- fal's `group.key` (`hailuo-03-new-turbo`) groups H3 Max with the H3 generation — the internal
  family name for H3 is Hailuo 03.

## Gaps

- `date`/`publishedAt` are catalogue timestamps; fal does not say whether `date` is creation, first
  publication, or first successful inference.
- No revision/checkpoint field, so the served H3 Max weights are unversioned from the outside.
- No stated output frame rate, audio channel count, or audio sample rate anywhere in the catalogue
  strings.
- `minimax/h3-max/reference-director` carries no `pricingInfoOverride` at all.
- The catalogue's `discovery.facets.labs` counts 21 `fal`-labelled items and 46 `Minimax`-labelled;
  the 21 matches the H3 Max family exactly.

## Pricing (verbatim)

`llms.txt` and page: **"$0.002 per seconds"** / "Your request will cost **$0.002 per second**."

```json
{"endpoint":"minimax/music-3","billing_unit":"seconds","price":0.002,
 "provider_type":"fal","is_partner_api":false,"balance_check":false,"enterprise_status":"ready"}
```

`provider_type: "fal"` and `is_partner_api: false` confirm fal-hosted weights, unlike the
`"provider_type":"partner"` payload for v2.6. Billing is per second of *generated* audio, so cost
scales with `duration`: 60 s ≈ $0.12, the 300 s cap ≈ $0.60, against a flat $0.15/track for v2.6.

## What this establishes

- fal is the **cheapest per short track and the most controllable route**: seed, duration and two
  sampling knobs that neither the vendor API nor WaveSpeedAI exposes, at $0.002 per generated second.
- The output format claimed here (44.1 kHz WAV) **contradicts the first-party model card** (32 kHz),
  and the contradiction cannot be resolved from either side's documentation.
- fal exposes **no instrumental flag and no lyrics-optimizer**: because `lyrics` is required,
  instrumental-only generation is not offered as a documented control on this endpoint.
- No duration control exists on the vendor's own API, but 1–300 s exists here — so the host, not the
  vendor, is the source of the practical length limit. fal's cap (300 s) is below the model card's
  frame ceiling (9,000 frames ≈ 360 s).
- fal lists Music 3 as `public`, `deprecated: false` while the vendor's model list calls every music
  model Retired (see `../../minimax.io/music-26-model-list/2026-10-26.md`).

## Gaps

- No published max `lyrics`/`prompt` length, no language statement.
- No latency, p50 or throughput figures on the endpoint.
- No statement of what "relaxed screening" means when `enable_safety_checker` is false.
- `valid_regions` is empty in the page payload and `machine_type` is blank, so the serving footprint
  is not documented.
- No licence pointer for the weights; `licenseType: "commercial"` is fal's own label for the
  endpoint, not the weights licence.

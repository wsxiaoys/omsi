## What this establishes

- fal hosts this as a **proxied partner endpoint at the vendor's list price** — $0.10/1K characters,
  no host markup, no host discount.
- fal's **10,000-character cap matches the vendor's sync limit** (`maxLength: 10000`), unlike the
  Eleven v4 pattern where the host caps below the vendor.
- fal exposes **only 3 of the vendor's 7 output formats** (`mp3`, `pcm`, `flac`; no `wav`, no
  μ-law, no Opus) and the full 6 sample rates.
- fal's **emotion enum is neither the vendor's nor a subset**: it offers `neutral`, which the vendor
  does not document, and drops `calm` and `fluent`, which the vendor does.
- fal's **interjection list is 8 tags, not the vendor's 19**: it renames `(clear-throat)` to
  `(clears throat)` and adds `(yawns)`, which appears in no MiniMax list captured here.
- `voice_id` is a free-form string with presets; **no cloning or voice-upload field** exists on this
  endpoint, so a caller's own cloned MiniMax voice is not reachable through this schema as
  documented.
- fal lists the **2.8 family as published 2026-02-04**, twelve days after MiniMax's 2026-01-23
  announcement.

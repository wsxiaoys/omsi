## Summary

fal published four V6 endpoints on **2026-03-29**, one day before the vendor's public launch post and
two days before the vendor's changelog entry. The set matches the vendor's V6 capability list exactly
— text-to-video, image-to-video, first/last-frame transition, extend — and excludes the video-reference
(Fusion) mode and multi-clip control that the vendor API exposes.

fal's V6 pricing is per generated second and sits a consistent ~12–13 % above the vendor's own
per-second rate (e.g. 720p silent: fal $0.045/s vs vendor $0.040/s; 1080p silent: $0.090 vs $0.080;
1080p with audio: $0.115 vs $0.102). The audio uplift is proportionally the same as the vendor's.

V6 is visibly a different product line on fal, not a relabel. V5.6 is billed per fixed clip
($0.45 for 5 s at 720p = $0.09/s equivalent, $0.75 for 5 s at 1080p = $0.15/s), which is 1.7–2.8×
the V6 per-second rate at the same resolution, and V5.6 has no extend endpoint at all.

## Gaps

- `date`/`publishedAt` are catalogue timestamps; fal does not say whether `date` means creation,
  first publication, or first successful inference.
- The V6 landing page (`/models/pixverse-v6`) is client-rendered and returned no prose at capture, so
  no fal marketing copy or FAQ is recorded here.
- No model-revision field: fal's served V6 checkpoint is unknown.
- `licenseType: "commercial"` is a fal catalogue field, i.e. fal's assertion about use through fal,
  not a PixVerse licence statement.

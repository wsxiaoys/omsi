## What this establishes

- fal's marketing claims for Qwen Image 3 are the same three axes the vendor claims: long prompts
  (~4,500 tokens), multilingual typography (12 languages, 20+ fonts, ~10 px text), and photographic
  detail at 2K.
- fal states a July 2026 release, consistent with Alibaba's 2026-07-20 date for
  `qwen-image-3.0-pro`.
- fal claims commercial use of outputs through its API, and states it in the host's own terms.

## Contradictions inside this record

- **Pricing.** This page says "Both endpoints are pay-per-image at $0.075" and repeats "$0.075 per
  generated image" in the FAQ. fal's own catalogue metadata for the same two endpoints says
  **$0.04 per image at 1K and $0.075 at 2K**
  ([catalogue record](../qwen-image-3/2026-10-26.md)). The landing page quotes only the 2K price.
  A 1K request billed at $0.075 would be an 87% overcharge against the catalogue; a 1K request
  billed at $0.04 would contradict this page. Unresolved — treat $0.04–$0.075 as the range.
- **Hosting.** This page says "The API is serverless, so there are no GPUs to manage", while fal's
  catalogue marks both Qwen Image 3 endpoints `hostingType: "proxy"` — i.e. fal forwards to
  Alibaba's API. "Serverless" here reads as a user-facing property (no infrastructure to manage),
  not a claim that fal runs the weights.

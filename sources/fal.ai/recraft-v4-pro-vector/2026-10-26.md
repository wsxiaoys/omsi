(`https://v3b.fal.media/files/b/0a8ee4de/lmXuapOPV309mE533swE0_image.svg`, 66,125 bytes) and

files ... structured layers and clean geometry"
(`../../recraft.ai/recraft-v4-launch-blog/2026-10-26.md`) and with a 2K-tier canvas. It is a
measurement of a static host example artifact, not a model test.

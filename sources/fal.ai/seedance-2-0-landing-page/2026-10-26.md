- Origin: fal.ai, "Seedance 2.0" model landing page, <https://fal.ai/seedance-2.0>
- Evidence kind: provider marketing and FAQ (host)
- Publication date: none stated; the page says Seedance 2.0 "is available now on fal as of April 9th,
  2026"
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Seedance 2.0, Seedance 2.0 Fast, US-hosted Seedance 2.0

## Availability statement (verbatim)

> Seedance 2.0 is available now on fal as of April 9th, 2026. You can start generating videos
> immediately in the playground with no setup required.

> fal is the official Seedance 2.0 API, supporting two tiers so you can pick the right balance of
> quality, speed, and cost for your workflow.

Note the tension with the family page's "Early access — not yet generally available" gate on
`bytedance/seedance-2.0` at capture (see the catalogue record). The landing page's claim is not dated
by section, so it cannot be established whether "available now" describes the fast tier only or was
written before the gate was applied.

## Input/output FAQ (verbatim)

> Input: text prompts, images (JPEG, PNG, WebP), video files (MP4, MOV), and audio files (WAV, MP3).
> Output: MP4 video with synchronized audio. Resolutions: 480p and 720p. Durations: 4 to 15 seconds.
> Aspect ratios: 21:9, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, and 9:16.

> Generations complete in under 2 minutes. Fast-tier endpoints offer lower latency and cost for
> production workloads.

> Seedance 2.0 generates videos up to 15 seconds in a single generation. Within that duration, the
> model can produce multiple shots with natural cuts and transitions, so a single output can feel like
> an edited sequence rather than a single continuous clip.

> The Seedance 2.0 API is available globally through fal's infrastructure. Developers and enterprises
> in any country can access and integrate the API into their applications.

The "480p and 720p" resolution line is stale relative to the endpoint schemas and pricing strings in
the same catalogue, which offer 1080p and 4k on the standard and US tiers (see the catalogue record).

## Gaps

- The page carries no date per section, so its statements cannot be tied to a point in time beyond
  the "April 9th, 2026" availability line.
- "Under 2 minutes" is not tied to resolution, duration, tier, or queue time.
- The landing page contradicts the endpoint schemas on maximum resolution and on US-tier 1080p; the
  contradiction is preserved rather than resolved.
- No failure modes, refusal behaviour, or quality measurements are given.

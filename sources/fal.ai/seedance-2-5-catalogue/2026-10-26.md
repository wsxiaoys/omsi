The three non-US endpoints resolve (`openapi.json` returns a full input schema, and the docs pages
render) but `api.fal.ai/v1/models` returns only the bare `endpoint_id` for them, and they do not
appear in `GET /api/models?keywords=seedance-2.5`. The family page
`https://fal.ai/models/bytedance/seedance-2.5` shows:

> **Early access** — This model is not yet generally available. Request access and we will notify you
> when you can use it.

with the same B2B-only terms Seedance 2.0's standard tier carries: "You will only serve business
customers and verify that all end users are businesses … You will pass an `end_user_id` in your API
payload". So on fal the public Seedance 2.5 offering is the **US-hosted** deployment plus a draft
completion endpoint; the standard endpoints exist but are gated. fal publishes no date for the gate.

## Input schema — standard text-to-video (transcribed)

| Field | Type | Default | Values / notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `prompt` | string | — | required |
| `duration` | enum | `auto` | `auto`, 4–14 … (schema enum truncated at 14, description says "Supports 4 to 30 seconds, or auto") |
| `resolution` | enum | `720p` | `480p`, `720p`, `1080p` |
| `aspect_ratio` | enum | `auto` | `auto`, 21:9, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, 9:16 |
| `generate_audio` | boolean | `true` | "The cost of video generation is the same regardless of whether audio is generated or not." |
| `bitrate_mode` | enum | `standard` | `standard`, `high` |
| `codec` | enum | `auto` | `auto`, `H264`, `H265` |
| `draft` | boolean | `false` | "Generate a draft preview at 480p, overriding resolution. Returns a `draft_id` for completing the draft at 1080p within seven days." |
| `end_user_id` | string | null | required by the B2B terms |

The US text-to-video and US image-to-video schemas are the same minus `draft`.

## Input schema — reference-to-video (transcribed)

Adds `image_urls` (≤ 30, "Max 30 MB per image … Total files across all modalities must not exceed
50"), `video_urls` (≤ 10, "Each video must be 1.8 to 30.2 seconds and no larger than 200 MB; combined
duration must not exceed 30.2 seconds … frame rate 24 to 60 FPS"), `audio_urls` (≤ 10, "Each file must
be 1.8 to 30.2 seconds and no larger than 15 MB; combined duration must not exceed 30.2 seconds"),
`seed` ("Random seed for reproducibility. Note that results may still vary slightly even with the same
seed."), and:

> `task` … `auto`, `reference`, `editing`, `extension` … Editing modifies a reference video and
> automatically coerces `aspect_ratio` and `duration` to `auto`. Extension continues a reference video
> and automatically coerces `aspect_ratio` to `auto`.

The `1.8–30.2 s` bounds are fal's rounding of the vendor's 2–30 s window; the 30.2 s combined cap
matches the vendor's 30 s.

## Pricing (USD, per second of output video)

| Endpoint / tier | 480p | 720p | 1080p |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| US text-, image-, reference-to-video | $0.2205/s | $0.4730/s | $1.164/s |
| US reference-to-video **with video input** | — | $0.2838/s | — |
| `draft/complete` (1080p only) | — | — | $1.164/s |

Verbatim price string (US reference-to-video):

> For 480p, your request will cost roughly $0.2205 per second of generated video, for 720p, you will
> be charged roughly $0.4730 per second of generated video, and for 1080p, roughly $1.164 per second
> of generated video. Your request will cost $0.0214 per 1,000 tokens for 480p and 720p video and
> roughly $0.0234 per 1,000 tokens for 1080p video. The number of tokens is roughly given by (height of
> output video * width of output video * (input video duration + output video duration) * 24) / 1024.
> If video inputs are provided the price is multiplied by 0.6. With video inputs and 720p resolution,
> the price is roughly $0.2838 per second of generated video. With video references, you will be
> charged for both input and output videos.

So fal's US token rate is **$21.40 per million tokens** for 480p/720p ($23.40 for 1080p) — exactly 2×
BytePlus's $10.70/$11.70 for the same model. fal charges the premium for US hosting, and the ×0.6
video-input multiplier mirrors the vendor's own with-video discount.

No price is published for the gated non-US endpoints.

## Draft flow

`bytedance/seedance-2.5/draft/complete` takes only `draft_id`, `resolution` (fixed at `1080p`) and
`codec`, matching the vendor's two-step draft design; the draft ID is "Valid for seven days and only
for the original caller".

## Observations

- fal's catalogue repeats the vendor's 24 fps constant in its token formula, consistent with the
  documented 24 fps output.
- `licenseType: "commercial"` and `hostingType: "proxy"` on every endpoint: fal forwards to ByteDance
  rather than hosting weights.
- `generate_audio` does not change the price on fal either, which matches the vendor's single token
  schedule.

## Gaps

- No published date, price, or approval criteria for the gated standard tier.
- The `duration` enum in the fetched schema truncates at 14 while its description says 4–30; the enum
  is generated from the vendor's allowed values and the truncation is a capture artefact of the
  schema listing, not a documented cap.
- No latency, queue-time or failure-rate data.

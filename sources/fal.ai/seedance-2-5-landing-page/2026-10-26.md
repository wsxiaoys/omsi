## Availability and endpoints (verbatim)

> Native 30-second video, now on fal

> Seedance 2.5 is an AI video generation model from ByteDance, now available on fal. Seedance 2.5
> generates native 30-second video at up to 720p from text, a single image, or up to 50 multimodal
> references.

> **Available Endpoints** … Three endpoints, all generating native 30-second video at up to 720p:
> `bytedance/seedance-2.5/text-to-video`, `/image-to-video`, `/reference-to-video`

> **Can I use Seedance 2.5 on fal?** Seedance 2.5 API is now available on fal. There are three
> endpoints: text to video, image to video and reference to video.

> **What resolution does Seedance 2.5 output?** Dreamina Seedance 2.5 on fal generates at 480p or
> 720p, selected per request, in any of seven aspect ratios from 21:9 to 9:16.

**Contradiction with fal's own schema and price strings**, which expose `1080p` for all three
endpoints and quote a 1080p per-second price. The prose says 720p maximum in five separate places
including the FAQ; read the schema as operational truth and the prose as stale launch copy — the same
failure mode the Seedance 2.0 landing page shows for 1080p/4K.

**Contradiction with the family page**, which gates the non-US endpoints behind "Early access — not
yet generally available". The landing page says the three endpoints are available now and names them
without a region prefix. The gated page and the public US endpoints are the only two facts that can
both be true; the landing page does not mention US hosting at all, even though the three public
endpoints are the `/us/` ones.

## Capability claims (labelled as claims)

- **One-take creation**: "generates up to 30 seconds as one native take, rather than short clips
  stitched together. The model reasons about the whole shot at once, so identity, clothing, prop
  ownership and spatial direction hold from the first frame to the last instead of drifting at the
  joins."
- **Reference budget**: "up to 30 images, up to 10 videos and up to 10 audio clips, with video and
  audio each capped at 30 seconds in total"; "It selects the right material per scene rather than
  forcing all of them into shot at once."
- **Physics**: "Weight, momentum, fabric and hair behave in generated video the way they do in filmed
  footage. Sleeves trace natural arcs through the air instead of sliding through the body."
- **3D layout references**: "Lay out a scene in a 3D tool, export the viewport, and let the model
  finish it as video. A coarse blockout of simple geometry locks the action paths, blocking,
  entrances and exits, camera path, cut points, lighting changes and sound rhythm." fal distinguishes
  coarse and fine blockouts. This is the vendor's clay-render/白模 feature, restated.
- **Green-screen editing**: swap the background, keep the performance, re-light the subject for the
  new scene.
- **Timestamp pacing**: "Write the prompt as a timed shot list and the model spends the running time
  where you tell it to … Ranges run consecutively without overlapping. A timestamp gives an event its
  share of the running time rather than marking a frame-accurate cut."
- **Languages**: "Chinese, English, Spanish, Indonesian, Malay, Thai, Arabic, Portuguese, Vietnamese,
  Japanese and Korean."
- **Stability claim**: "Improved stability keeps stray subtitles and unwanted background music out."

The "not frame-accurate" caveat on timestamps is fal's own gloss and is more precise than the
vendor's "timestamp-level control"; no source states the granularity.

## Price

Rendered on each of the three model pages, and present in the page payload as
`endpointBilling` / `publicEndpointBilling`:

> Your request will cost **$0.027** per image.

`endpointBilling`: `{"billing_unit":"images","price":0.027,"provider_type":"partner","is_partner_api":true}`.
Identical for `text-to-image`, `edit` and `layerize`; no size or megapixel tier is stated, and no
per-input-image charge is stated. Compare the Pro endpoints, which are priced by output area
($0.0675 up to 1536×1536, $0.135 up to 2048×2048) plus $0.0045 per extra input image
([Pro endpoint record](../seedream-5-pro-endpoints/2026-10-26.md)).

`[left, top, right, bottom]` "normalized to the integer range [0, 1000]"), `name`, `description`.
The schema object is still named `SeedDream50ProLayer` on the Flash endpoint — a copy of the Pro
definition.

## What this establishes

- Flash is served by fal as three separate proxy endpoints created **2026-09-23** — about two and a
  half months after the Pro endpoints (2026-07-07) and over ten months after the Lite endpoints.
- On fal, Flash output tops out at the `auto_2K` tier with a 4,624,220-pixel ceiling — the same
  ceiling as Pro, and lower than Lite's 4K / 16,777,216-pixel ceiling on the same host
  ([fal Lite record](../seedream-5-lite/2026-10-26.md)).
- Flat **$0.027/image** on all three endpoints, with no megapixel tiering and no input-image charge
  documented — cheaper than fal's Pro ($0.0675–$0.135) and cheaper than fal's Lite ($0.035).
- The parameter surface is Pro-shaped: layer decomposition, transparent background, 10 references,
  no batch generation, no streaming, no `fast` prompt mode (the field is absent rather than
  exposed).
- No sampling controls are exposed, so exact reproducibility is not documented on this host either.

## Gaps

- No prose README, benchmark, latency figure or throughput claim on any of the three pages; the only
  descriptive text is the one-sentence `shortDescription`.
- fal does not state which ByteDance snapshot it proxies, and the `date`/`publishedAt` fields are fal
  timestamps, not model dates.
- No example outputs are attached to the `text-to-image` or `edit` endpoints (`examples: []`); the
  `layerize` endpoint has one example id.
- Whether `$0.027` includes the first input image on `edit` (as Pro's "first input image is not
  charged" wording does) is not stated.

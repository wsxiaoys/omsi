## Input schema, video-to-video (transcribed)

| Field | Type | Default | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `video_url` | string, required | — | "URL of the input video" |
| `audio_url` | string, required | — | "URL of the input audio" |
| `sync_mode` | `cut_off`, `loop`, `bounce`, `silence`, `remap` | `cut_off` | "Lipsync mode when audio and video durations are out of sync." |
| `options` | `Sync3GenerationOptions` or null | — | "Additional Sync.so generation options. If options.sync_mode is set, it overrides the top-level sync_mode field." |
| `enable_safety_checker` | boolean | `true` | fal-side safety checker |

`Sync3GenerationOptions` adds `active_speaker_detection` (auto-detect, v3, manual coordinates, bounding
boxes, face image), plus `temperature`, `model_mode` and `occlusion_detection_enabled`. The schema
states that `temperature` and `occlusion_detection_enabled` are "ignored by sync-3"; `model_mode` and
`prompt` are "primarily used by react-1."

Accepted upload types on the page: video `mp4, mov, webm, m4v, gif`; audio `mp3, ogg, wav, m4a, aac`.

Output: `{ "video": File }` with `file_name`, `file_size`, `content_type` and `url`; the example output
is `video/mp4`.

## Summary

fal hosts sync-3 under `fal-ai/sync-lipsync/v3` (video + audio) and
`fal-ai/sync-lipsync/v3/image-to-video` (still image + audio), both public, commercial, and proxied to
sync. labs. The video endpoint requires `video_url` and `audio_url`, exposes sync. labs' `sync_mode` and
speaker-selection options, and adds a fal safety checker. The image endpoint requires `image_url` and
`audio_url` and does not expose `sync_mode`. Both are priced at $0.1333 per output second ($8/minute).
The `v3` slug is a fal generation label, not the vendor's model name; the vendor calls the model
`sync-3`.

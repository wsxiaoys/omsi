`discovery.hosting` is **`proxy`** for all three H3.1 endpoints, in contrast to `serverless` for the older

output carries a `task_id` described as "Tripo task ID for a generated model", and the rig endpoint's input
takes "Token from Tripo's file upload API".

### The unsuffixed roster string is not callable

- `GET /api/openapi/queue/openapi.json?endpoint_id=tripo3d/h3.1` → **HTTP 404**, body `null`.
- `GET https://api.fal.ai/v1/models?endpoint_id=tripo3d/h3.1` → 200 with a **stub**: the response is
  `{"models":[{"endpoint_id":"tripo3d/h3.1"}]}` and carries no `metadata` object at all, unlike the three
  suffixed endpoints.
- `https://fal.ai/models/tripo3d/h3.1` returns 200 as a family landing page.

So `tripo3d/h3.1` is a family prefix, and calls must target one of the three suffixed endpoints.

## Summary

fal serves Tripo H3.1 as three proxied endpoints published on 2026-04-07 and last updated 2026-10-20, all
active and marked `commercial`. fal's prices match the vendor's credit schedule exactly at the tiers fal
exposes ($0.10/$0.20/$0.30 for text, $0.20/$0.30/$0.40 for image and multiview), plus the vendor's own
+$0.20 detailed-geometry and +$0.05 quad add-ons.

fal's surface is a **subset** of the vendor's: it exposes no `smart_low_poly`, no `generate_parts`, no
`texture_quality=extreme` (8K), no `texture_version` or `delight`, no `export_uv`, `export_orientation` or
`compress`, and no `enable_image_autofix`. It also exposes no format conversion, so USDZ/OBJ/STL/3MF are
not reachable through fal for this model.

Two schema-level disagreements with the vendor reference are recorded rather than resolved:
`negative_prompt` is capped at 1024 characters on fal against 255 in Tripo's reference, and
`face_limit` accepts up to 2,000,000 on fal without requiring `geometry_quality=detailed`, which the vendor
document ties to the 2,000,000 ceiling.

## Gaps

- The gallery does not say *which* Tripo snapshot each proxied endpoint calls; the H3.1 name and the
  `task_id` passthrough imply `v3.1-20260211` but nothing states it.
- fal publishes no run counts, latency percentiles, or quality comparisons for these endpoints.
- `license_type: "commercial"` is fal's statement about use through fal, not a Tripo licence grant.
- No capture of fal's per-request error behaviour or rate limits for the proxied endpoints.

## Summary

fal exposes three Fabric 1.0 endpoints — the base image-to-video, a `fast` variant that costs *more*
per second ($0.10/$0.20 vs $0.08/$0.15), and a `text` variant that adds a 2,000-character script plus an
optional voice description auto-derived from the image. The base endpoint is the oldest of the three
Fabric listings (2025-09-19), has a commercial licence flag and "Partner" badge on its model page, and
is billed per second of output at a rate identical to VEED's own API. The sibling `veed/avatars/*` endpoints, by
contrast, take a fixed 28-entry `avatar_id` enum — the concrete form of the "preset avatar library" that
Fabric is sold as replacing.

## Gaps

- No `seed`, `duration`, frame-rate, or audio-length parameter on any Fabric endpoint: output length is
  whatever the audio is, with no ceiling published in the schema.
- fal's metadata says `hostingType: serverless`, not `proxy` as it does for other partner models. That
  may mean fal runs the weights itself or may be a metadata convention; no captured page explains it.
- `veed/fabric-1.0/fast` has no first-party counterpart: VEED's docs describe one Fabric 1.0 and never
  a Fast tier, and the price is higher, not lower.
- `veed/fabric-1.0/text` has no first-party counterpart either: VEED's API reference says flatly "The
  model takes audio, not text" and requires a pre-generated TTS URL.
- No capture of fal's terms of service, so fal-route commercial terms beyond the `licenseType:
  commercial` flag are unverified.

## Summary

fal's landing page is a capability restatement plus two host-specific additions: an explicit "no
minimums" billing claim, and a list of what the host believes makes a good input image (front-facing,
well lit, unobstructed). The endpoint page adds a mechanism claim — phoneme timing mapped to facial
keypoints — and positions Fabric against general image-to-video models on lip-sync precision. Nothing on
either page states a duration limit, an audio limit, or a frame rate.

## Gaps

- The "phoneme timing mapped to facial keypoints" description is host-authored explanation, not a
  vendor architecture disclosure; no VEED document describes the pipeline.
- "No minimums" contradicts nothing on the vendor side but is fal's billing statement, not VEED's; the
  vendor article says "no stated minimum", hedging.
- "Fast … without compromising output quality" is unverified and sits oddly with the higher price.
- No date, no changelog, no statement of which VEED checkpoint fal serves.

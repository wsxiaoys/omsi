# fal.ai: `fal-ai/veo3.1/fast` and `fal-ai/veo3.1/reference-to-video`

- Origin: fal.ai model pages, <https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/veo3.1/fast> and
  <https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/veo3.1/reference-to-video>
- Evidence kind: host endpoint pages with per-second price statements (independent host)
- Publication date: none stated; both pages carry "This endpoint will be deprecated on November 17,
  2026."
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Veo 3.1 Fast (`fal-ai/veo3.1/fast`), Veo 3.1 reference-to-video
  (`fal-ai/veo3.1/reference-to-video`)

## Fast tier price statement (verbatim)

> For every second of video you generate you will be charged $0.10 without audio or $0.15 with audio
> for 720p or 1080p. At 4k, you will be charged $0.30 per second without audio, or $0.35 with. For
> example, a 5 second video at 1080p with audio on will cost $0.75.

This matches the landing page's Fast table exactly and confirms that fal bills Fast at 4K, which
Google's model card does not list for `veo-3.1-fast-generate-001`.

The fast page itself is thin: a playground, this price paragraph, and the deprecation banner. It does
not restate the parameter schema, so the fast endpoint's accepted `duration`/`resolution` values are
inferred from the shared landing-page table and the standard schema rather than read off this page.

## Reference-to-video page (verbatim)

> Veo 3.1 (Image to Video) API on fal
> `fal-ai/veo3.1/reference-to-video`, details and endpoints

> Generate Videos from images using Google's Veo 3.1

Input widget label: "Image Urls *" with "Add Image", and the rendered playground state reads "3
images added" — i.e. the reference-image cap on fal is three, matching Google's "up to three images of
a single person, character, or product".

Price statement (verbatim):

> For every second of video you generate you will be charged $0.20 without audio or $0.40 with audio
> for 720p or 1080p. At 4k, you will be charged $0.40 per second without audio, or $0.60 with audio.
> For example, 8 second video at 1080p with audio on will cost $3.20

The page title calls it "Image to Video" while the route and heading say "Reference to Video"; fal
uses the two labels loosely, and this endpoint is the reference-subject path, distinct from
`fal-ai/veo3.1/image-to-video`.

## Summary

Both pages repeat the per-second price model: Fast $0.10/$0.15 (720p–1080p) and $0.30/$0.35 (4K);
reference-to-video $0.20/$0.40 and $0.40/$0.60. The reference endpoint accepts up to three images.
Neither page adds a capability that the landing page and standard schema do not already state.

## Gaps

- No schema on either page, so Fast's own parameter surface is not directly captured.
- No statement of what the reference endpoint does at 4K (whether references survive upsampling) or
  whether reference-to-video forces an 8-second duration as Google's model card does.
- No queue-time or latency figures.

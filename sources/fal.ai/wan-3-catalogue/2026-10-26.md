All six report `modelFamily: "Wan 3.0"`, `modelLab: "Alibaba"`, `kind: "inference"`,
`deprecated: false`, `removed: false`. `discovery.version` is `3.0` for both groups; the prime group
adds `variant: "Prime"`. Group labels are "Text to Video", "Image to Video", "Reference to Video" for
both.

`hostingType: "proxy"` means fal reports forwarding to Alibaba rather than running weights itself. For
contrast, the same catalogue lists Wan 2.1/2.2 endpoints as `hostingType: "serverless"` and Wan 2.5,
2.6 and 2.7 endpoints as `proxy` — the open-weight generations are the serverless ones.

The standard tier's catalogue title for the text endpoint is the unversioned "Wan Text to Video" while
its `endpoint_id` and description are Wan 3.0; the image and reference rows are titled "Wan 3.0". A
naming artifact, not a different model.

## Price strings (verbatim)

Standard tier (identical string on all three endpoints):

> For every second of video you generate, you will be charged **$0.05** **480p**, **$0.10** **720p**,
> or **$0.20** **1080p**. For example, a **5s** video at **720p** will cost **$0.50**. **Pricing is
> subject to change.**

Prime tier (identical string on all three endpoints):

> Billing is calculated per second of video generated: **$0.068 at 480p**, **$0.14 at 720p**, or
> **$0.28 at 1080p**. For example, a **5-second video at 720p costs $0.70**. Pricing is subject to
> change.

These match Alibaba Cloud's Singapore list prices exactly and do not reflect the vendor's
"Limited-time 30% off" on the standard tier (see the pricing record).

## Input schemas (from the OpenAPI documents)

Standard tier — `Wan30TextToVideoInput`: required `prompt`; optional `duration` (integer 2–30 or null,
default 5, "Set to null for smart duration"), `resolution` enum `480p|720p|1080p` (default `1080p`),
`aspect_ratio` enum `adaptive|16:9|4:3|1:1|3:4|9:16` (default `adaptive`), `audio` boolean default
`true`, `seed` (0–2147483647), `enable_prompt_expansion` default `true`, `enable_thinking` default
`false`, `enable_safety_checker` default `true`.

`Wan30ImageToVideoInput` adds `start_image_url` (required) and `end_image_url` ("Last frame of the
generated video. Requires start_image_url"), and `prompt` becomes nullable with `maxLength: 20000`.

`Wan30ReferenceToVideoInput` requires nothing, and adds `reference_image_urls` ("Up to 10 reference
image URLs"), `reference_video_urls` ("Up to 5 reference video URLs totaling at most 15 seconds. Each
clip must be at least 16 fps. Input video duration is billed in addition to output duration"),
`reference_audio_urls` ("Up to 5 reference audio URLs totaling at most 15 seconds"), plus `web_url`
("Public webpage URL to base the video on. Requires enable_thinking=true. Only pages that do not
require login can be read") and `file_url` ("Document URL to base the video on. Requires
enable_thinking=true").

Prime tier — `Wan30Prime*Input` schemas are field-for-field identical to the standard tier's three,
including the same defaults, the same 2–30 s duration range, the same reference caps, and the same
`web_url`/`file_url` fields.

Verbatim description of the thinking switch: "Enable enhanced reasoning before generation."

Verbatim description of the prompt-expansion switch: "Enable intelligent prompt rewriting. Disabling it
can save roughly 20-60 seconds of latency but is likely to degrade generation quality."

Verbatim description of the safety switch: "Enable content moderation for input and output. Disabling
it requires account authorization; unauthorized requests are always checked."

Output schema for all six: `video` (file), `seed` (integer, "The seed used for generation"),
`duration` (number, "Generated video duration in seconds"), `actual_prompt` (nullable string — the
expanded prompt). The `VideoFile` object exposes `width`, `height`, `fps`, `num_frames`, `duration`,
`content_type`, `file_size` but declares only `url` as required, so a delivered file's frame rate is not
guaranteed to be reported.

## Summary

fal exposes both Wan 3.0 tiers, each with text-to-video, image-to-video (first frame, optional last
frame) and reference-to-video endpoints, all public, all proxied to Alibaba, all billed per second of
output at $0.05/$0.10/$0.20 (standard) and $0.068/$0.14/$0.28 (prime) for 480p/720p/1080p. The two
tiers' input schemas are identical on fal, so the host offers no parameter-level evidence of what
separates prime from standard beyond price.

## Gaps

- fal's catalogue does not say which Alibaba checkpoint or region a proxied request is routed to.
- `enable_thinking` and `web_url`/`file_url` exist on the standard tier's schema at fal, but Alibaba's
  own guide ties document/web-page input to thinking mode; no captured fal document explains the
  interaction or the failure mode when thinking is off.
- No fal-side latency, queue-time, or reliability figures were captured.
- The catalogue's unversioned "Wan Text to Video" title is unexplained.

# FASHN Help Center: "API On-Demand & Commitment Tiers" (credit costs)

- Origin: FASHN AI Help Center, "API On-Demand & Commitment Tiers",
  https://help.fashn.ai/plans-and-pricing/api-pricing
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor pricing schedule)
- Publication date: not stated
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: FASHN Virtual Try-On v1.6 (fixed-cost 1 credit), Try-On Max and other
  resolution-priced endpoints (comparison)

## Captured content

> FASHN API uses simple, credit-based pricing. You can start with on-demand credits and, when you're
> ready, add a monthly commitment tier to unlock included monthly credits and discounted top-up
> rates.

### On-demand

> **On-Demand** — $0.075 per credit … Pay for credits in advance … **Available for commercial use**
> … Minimum purchase of $7.50 (100 credits)

> Credit costs depend on the endpoint, resolution, and generation mode you select. Pricing is charged
> per successful output. Failed predictions do not consume credits.

### Fixed-cost endpoints, verbatim

| Endpoint | Credits per output |
| --- | --- |
| Virtual Try-On (v1.6) | 1 |
| Background Remove | 1 |

### Resolution-priced endpoints (for contrast)

"These costs apply to Product-to-Model, Model Creation, Model Swap, Face to Model, Edit, Reframe, and
Try-On Max":

| Generation Mode | 1K | 2K | 4K |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Balanced | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Quality | 3 | 4 | 5 |

> If you do not select a generation mode, the system selects one automatically based on factors like
> the requested resolution and endpoint.

Additional rules: "Face Reference: +3 credits per output image (available on Product-to-Model, Model
Creation, Model Swap)"; "Multiple outputs: the per-output cost is multiplied by the number of images
requested".

Video generation is priced by duration and resolution (5 s at 480p/720p/1080p = 1/3/6 credits; 10 s =
2/6/12).

### Commitment tiers

| Tier | Price | Included credits | Top-up rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| On-Demand | — | pay as you go | $0.0750 / credit |
| Tier I | $19/mo (or $190/yr) | 282/month | $0.0675 (10% off) |
| Tier II | $249/mo (or $2,490/yr) | 4,150/month | $0.0600 (20% off) |
| Tier III | $1,249/mo (or $12,490/yr) | 25,594/month | $0.0488 (35% off) |

> The same credit costs above apply regardless of tier; the subscription only affects how much you
> pay per credit.

> On-demand top-ups expire 12 months after purchase. Discounted top-ups expire when your subscription
> ends. … API credits are separate from app credits.

Refunds: "Unused API credit purchases, whether part of a commitment tier or individual top-ups, are
non-refundable."

## Derived arithmetic (not stated by FASHN)

At the on-demand rate, one v1.6 output costs **$0.075**; four outputs in one request (`num_samples:
4`) cost $0.30. At Tier III's $0.0488 the same image costs about $0.049. A Try-On Max 4K `quality`
image costs 5 credits, i.e. $0.375 on-demand.

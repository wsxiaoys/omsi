## Captured content

> Starting today, our virtual try-on model is available on fal's platform, making it easy for both

The link in the December 2024 post resolves to the **v1.6** endpoint today, so the post has been
retargeted rather than left on the version that existed in 2024. The following claims are therefore
about the December 2024 pipeline, not about v1.6:

> FASHN's Virtual Try-On Solution stands out as the best commercially available clothing try-on API.
> It's a sophisticated pipeline composed of seven specialized AI models, powered by a 1.2B-parameter
> flow-matching model capable of preserving fine garment details with unmatched accuracy.
>
> Now, as the first commercially available virtual try-on endpoint on FAL's platform, this integration
> ensures not only precision but also speed, producing high-resolution outputs (576x864) in just ~15
> seconds.

Garment-category and training-data statement (also from 2024):

> FASHN's virtual try-on solution is designed to handle a broad spectrum of clothing types, including
> tops, bottoms, jackets, and dresses. To ensure responsible AI deployment, intimate apparel and
> swimwear were intentionally excluded from the training dataset, reducing NSFW biases while
> maintaining focus on mainstream fashion categories.

## Observations

- The 1.2B / seven-model / 576×864 / ~15 s figures describe the 2024 generation of the model. They do
  not match any 2025–2026 documentation: v1.5 is documented at 972M parameters and 576×864, and v1.6 at
  864×1296 with 5–17 s. Do not attribute the 1.2B figure to v1.6.
- The claim that intimate apparel and swimwear were excluded from training sits in tension with v1.6's
  `moderation_level: permissive`, which "allows swimwear, underwear, and revealing garments". The 2024
  statement may describe an earlier dataset or the try-on training set specifically; FASHN has not
  reconciled the two.
- The post is the clearest statement that fal hosts FASHN's try-on API, and it predates every v1.x
  endpoint fal currently lists (v1.5 dated 2025-04-21, v1.6 dated 2025-06-24).

## Gaps

- No statement of whether fal runs FASHN's model on its own GPUs or forwards to FASHN's API. fal's
  catalogue marks the endpoint `hostingType: "serverless"` (separate fal record), which is fal's own
  claim about hosting.
- No quality benchmark, and no independent verification of "best commercially available".

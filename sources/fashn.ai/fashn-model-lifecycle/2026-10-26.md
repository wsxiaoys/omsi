# FASHN docs: Model Lifecycle (release dates and lifecycle stages)

- Origin: FASHN AI developer documentation, "Model Lifecycle",
  https://docs.fashn.ai/api-overview/model-lifecycle (Markdown via `Accept: text/markdown`)
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor release-date and support-status table)
- Publication date: not stated; contents reflect the docs as served on the capture date
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: FASHN Virtual Try-On v1.6 (`tryon-v1.6`), Try-On Max (`tryon-max`), and the rest
  of FASHN's current endpoint list

## Captured content

Lifecycle stages, verbatim:

> **Stable** — Mature and supported for long-term use. Changes maintain backwards compatibility.
> Recommended for production integrations that prioritize stability.
> **Preview** — Supported and approaching stable status. Output behavior may continue to improve
> before the stable release.
> **Experimental** — Supported but still evolving. Behavior, parameters, or response schema may
> change.
> **Deprecated** — Scheduled for discontinuation in favor of a newer model or workflow.

## Observations

- `tryon-v1.6` is the **only** try-on version in the current list. v1.5, v1.4 and earlier do not
  appear; the table is titled "Current models", so absence is not proof of retirement.
- `tryon-v1.6` is also the only endpoint in the table marked **Stable**; everything released in 2025
  after it, and Try-On Max, are Preview or Experimental.
- The release date FASHN publishes for v1.6 (June 25, 2025) is one day **after** the fal endpoint's
  catalogue `date`/`publishedAt` (2025-06-24T20:58:43Z) — consistent with a late-UTC-day fal listing
  or a same-week announcement.

## Gaps

- No retirement or deprecation date for v1.6 is published, and the page does not say what "Stable"
  guarantees about a future replacement.
- No per-model changelog; the page states only that a model can change lifecycle stage silently.

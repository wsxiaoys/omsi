## Observations

- The annual API-tier prices here ($190/$2,490/$12,490 in the Help Center; $16/$208/$1,041 per month
  here) are two presentations of the same 10-months-for-12 offer, and the Help Center's own table
  lists the annual totals. No contradiction, but the two pages must be read together to get an annual
  number.
- "Commercial use license" is a plan-entitlement bullet, not a licence document. The binding terms are
  in the Terms of Service (separate record).
- The page sells the **app** and the **API** on one screen; app credits and API credits are separate
  currencies per the Help Center.

## Gaps

- No per-endpoint credit table on this page; it points to the Help Center for that.
- No enterprise/self-host option and no statement about on-premises deployment of any FASHN model.

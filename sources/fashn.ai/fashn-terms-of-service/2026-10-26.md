## Observations

- There is **no licence text for model weights** here, because no FASHN v1.6 weights are distributed.
  The Apache-2.0 licence applies to the separately published v1.5 weights, not to this endpoint.
- "You own the outputs" is qualified by the possibility of identical outputs to other customers and by
  third-party rights in the inputs (e.g. a photograph of a real model, a branded garment).
- The reverse-engineering and no-resale clauses are the binding constraint on wrapping v1.6 in a
  competing API; the ToS explicitly permits embedding it in an application you operate.

## Gaps

- No statement of a specific jurisdiction's treatment of AI-output copyright beyond the
  "to the extent permitted by applicable law" caveat.
- No explicit clause about training competing models on outputs (only on inputs/customer content).

# FASHN blog: "FASHN v1.5: Our Next-Generation Virtual Try-On Model"

- Origin: FASHN AI blog (category: News), FASHN Team,
  https://fashn.ai/blog/fashn-v1-5-our-next-generation-virtual-try-on-model
- Evidence kind: official announcement (vendor launch post for the predecessor version)
- Publication date: March 26, 2025 ("Updated March 28, 2025")
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: FASHN Virtual Try-On v1.5 (predecessor of v1.6)

## Captured content

> Introducing FASHN v1.5—now available for early testing! Experience smarter fits, simplified
> controls, and a 2x speed boost for virtual clothing try-ons.
>
> FASHN Team — March 26, 2025 — Updated March 28, 2025

Release status at publication:

> This significant update is currently available under the 🧪 Experimental toggle in our web app and
> via the `/nightly` endpoint for API users… We invite all users to test and share their feedback as
> we prepare to make v1.5 our main model.

Claimed changes, verbatim (condensed):

- "Better Handling of Bulky and Loose Garments" — "v1.5 is not constrained by the model's original
  outfit dimensions, and takes as much space as needed for a realistic fit."
- "Improved Identity Preservation" — "Body shape, skin texture, and personal characteristics like
  tattoos are now preserved more accurately."
- "Simplified Controls for Effortless Use" — "Several previously required manual parameters—like
  Restore Background, Restore Clothes, Long Top, Adjust Hands, and Cover Feet—are now managed
  automatically by the model."
- "Performance Boost: Generation is Twice as Fast" — "Quality Mode: ~12 seconds / Balanced Mode:
  ~6 seconds / Performance Mode: ~4 seconds".

Two-input workflow:

> Integrating the FASHN API into an e-commerce platform is now simpler—only two images (one of the
> model and one of the garment) are needed to produce high-quality try-ons without additional inputs.
> Our solution automatically identifies photo type and category, determines what needs removal, and
> accurately places the new garment.

## Gaps

- The post gives no resolution for v1.5. The v1.6 launch post states v1.5 output was 576×864, and
  fal's catalogue description for the v1.5 endpoint says the same.
- No statement on parameter count or architecture.
- No statement about commercial terms or weights availability (v1.5 was open-sourced later, in
  January 2026 — see the open-source record).

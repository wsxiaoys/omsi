## Observations

- This page describes FASHN's try-on offering as a whole, at the app/agent level. "Export images up to
  4K" and "shoes, bags, hats, eyewear" match the Try-On Max API tier and the December 2025 app
  upgrade, **not** the v1.6 endpoint (864×1296, apparel categories only). Reading this page as a
  specification of v1.6 would overstate the endpoint.
- "Does not predict exact size and fit" is a first-party limit on the whole try-on line, and is the
  clearest statement that try-on output is a visualisation, not a fit measurement.
- Commercial use is stated here as a plan entitlement; the binding wording is in the Terms of Service.

# FASHN blog: "Open-Sourcing FASHN VTON v1.5: Maskless Virtual Try-On in Pixel Space"

- Origin: FASHN AI blog (category: News), FASHN Team,
  https://fashn.ai/blog/fashn-vton-1-5-open-source-release
- Evidence kind: official announcement (vendor release of open weights and code)
- Publication date: January 27, 2026
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: FASHN VTON v1.5 (open-weights sibling of FASHN Virtual Try-On v1.6)

## Captured content

> Today we're open-sourcing FASHN VTON v1.5, our virtual try-on model. Given an image of a person and
> an image of a garment, it generates a photorealistic image of that person wearing the garment.
>
> This Apache-2.0 licensed release is for researchers, developers, and product teams who want to
> build on a production-proven virtual try-on baseline.

What was released:

> - Model weights on Hugging Face
> - Inference code on GitHub
> - Project page with architecture and method details
>
> Next up: an interactive demo… And further down the road, we'll publish a technical paper covering
> the research, training process, and lessons learned from building production-ready virtual try-on
> pipelines.

Architecture claims (the only first-party architecture disclosure found anywhere in the family):

> **Pixel-Space Generation** — Most diffusion-based image generation happens in latent space… FASHN
> VTON v1.5 operates directly on RGB pixels. This eliminates the information loss that comes with VAE
> encoding and results in sharper, more accurate outputs, particularly for fine garment details like
> textures and patterns.
>
> **Maskless Inference** — Traditional virtual try-on requires a segmentation mask… FASHN VTON v1.5
> generates in segmentation-free mode by default. Without a mask, the model learns to preserve body
> regions that shouldn't change (face, hands, unaffected clothing) while allowing the garment to take
> its natural form.

Scale and cost:

> At 972M parameters, it runs on consumer GPUs. It needs no prompts, just images. It can be trained
> from scratch for $5,000 to $10,000. And it operates in pixel space, a rarity in open-source
> diffusion models.

Motivation, which bears on the closed v1.6 endpoint:

> The fashion tech ecosystem deserves more than API access to black boxes: models it can own,
> understand, and improve.

Earlier component in the same release series: the FASHN Human Parser ("a segmentation model that
identifies body parts and clothing regions… the first component").

## Gaps

- The post describes v1.5 only. It does not say whether v1.6 shares the architecture, the parameter
  count, or the pixel-space/maskless method, and **no v1.6 weights or code were released** — the
  Hugging Face and GitHub artefacts are v1.5.
- The technical paper is announced as future work; none was captured.
- No quality comparison between the open v1.5 weights and the hosted v1.6 endpoint.

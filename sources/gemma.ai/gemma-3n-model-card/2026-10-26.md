## Summary

Gemma 3n is the June 2025 on-device generation whose two sizes are **also** called E2B and E4B.
It already used "effective parameters" and per-layer embeddings (PLE) — the same two ideas
Gemma 4's E2B/E4B inherit — and already took audio input. The measurable difference is context:
**32K** for Gemma 3n against **128K** for Gemma 4's E2B/E4B.

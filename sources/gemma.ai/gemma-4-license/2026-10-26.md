# gemma.ai: Gemma 4 licence and Gemma Terms of Use scope

- Origin (licence): Google DeepMind, "Apache License 2.0", Gemma Documentation,
  <https://gemma.ai/resources/apache-2/> (nav label "Gemma 4 license")
- Origin (terms): Google DeepMind, "Gemma Terms of Use", <https://gemma.ai/resources/terms/>,
  "Last modified: April 1, 2026"
- Evidence kind: official documentation (licence text and terms)
- Publication date: licence page undated; terms page "Last modified: April 1, 2026".
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Gemma 4 (licence page); Gemma 1 through TranslateGemma (terms appendix)

## Captured content

### The Gemma 4 licence page is bare Apache 2.0

The page reached from the "Gemma 4 license" nav item is the unmodified Apache License,
Version 2.0, January 2004 text — sections 1–9, ending "END OF TERMS AND CONDITIONS". It
contains **no** Gemma-specific use-restriction addendum, no acceptable-use policy, no
redistribution notice requirement beyond Apache §4, and no naming of Gemma 4.

### The Gemma Terms of Use exclude Gemma 4 (verbatim)

> The terms below apply to Gemma models listed in the Appendix at bottom of this page. For
> Gemma 4 terms, see the Gemma 4 license .

The Appendix lists, verbatim:

> Gemma 1, Gemma 1.1, Gemma 2, Gemma 3, Gemma 3n, FunctionGemma, EmbeddingGemma, PaliGemma,
> PaliGemma 2, ShieldGemma, ShieldGemma 2, CodeGemma, CodeGemma 1.1, Gemma 2 JPN, DataGemma
> RIG, DataGemma RAG, RecurrentGemma, Gemma Scope, Gemma-APS, T5Gemma, VaultGemma,
> FunctionGemma, T5Gemma 2, TranslateGemma

Gemma 4 is absent from that list. The Terms impose, for the models they do cover:

> You must not use any of the Gemma Services ... for the restricted uses set forth in the
> Gemma Prohibited Use Policy ... or in violation of applicable laws and regulations.

> To the maximum extent permitted by law, Google reserves the right to restrict (remotely or
> otherwise) usage of any of the Gemma Services that Google reasonably believes are in
> violation of this Agreement.

> Google claims no rights in Outputs you generate using Gemma.

Distribution conditions in the Terms include shipping the Section 3.2 use restrictions as an
enforceable provision and a "Notice" text file: "Gemma is provided under and subject to the
Gemma Terms of Use found at gemma.ai/resources/terms".

### Corroborating first-party statements of the licence change

- Model card header: "**License**: Apache 2.0"
- Hugging Face model-card front matter for all five instruction-tuned checkpoints:
  `license: apache-2.0` with `license_link: https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/gemma_4_license`
  (captured separately in `sources/huggingface.co/gemma-4-12b-it/2026-10-26.md`). That older URL
  resolves to `https://ai.google.dev/gemma/apache_2`, which serves the same Apache 2.0 text on
  the deprecated docs host.

## Summary

Gemma 4 is the first mainline Gemma generation released under Apache 2.0 rather than the
Gemma Terms of Use. The evidence is structural, not just a marketing claim: the Terms of Use
page explicitly routes Gemma 4 elsewhere, its Appendix omits Gemma 4, and the page it points
at is plain Apache 2.0 with no use-restriction schedule.

## Gaps

- The Prohibited Use Policy and "Intended use" pages are still linked from the same nav
  ("Legal") without saying whether they apply to Gemma 4. The licence text itself does not
  incorporate them, so the safest reading is that they do not, but no Google page states
  that explicitly.
- No first-party page explains the relicensing decision beyond the launch blog's developer
  feedback framing.
- Apache 2.0 grants no trademark rights; the Terms' trademark clause does not carry over to
  Gemma 4, and no replacement naming policy was captured.

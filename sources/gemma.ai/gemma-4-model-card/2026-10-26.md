- Origin: Google DeepMind, "Gemma 4 — Model card", Gemma Documentation,

- Evidence kind: official documentation (developer-published model card)
- Publication date: not stated on the page. The card cites "Gemma 4 Technical Report",

- Capture date: 2026-10-26

## Captured content

### Header block (verbatim)

> **License**: Apache 2.0 | **Authors**: Google DeepMind

> Gemma is a family of open models built by Google DeepMind. Gemma 4 models are
> multimodal, handling text and image input (with audio supported on E2B, E4B, and 12B
> models) and generating text output. This release includes open-weights models in both
> pre-trained and instruction-tuned variants. Gemma 4 features a context window of up to
> 256K tokens and maintains multilingual support in over 140 languages.

> The models are available in five distinct sizes: **E2B**, **E4B**, **12B**, **26B A4B**,
> and **31B**.

### Stated capability advances (verbatim bullets)

- "Reasoning -- All models in the family are designed as highly capable reasoners, with
  configurable thinking modes."
- "Extended Multimodalities -- Processes Text, Image with variable aspect ratio and
  resolution support (all models), Video, and Audio (featured natively on the E2B, E4B,
  and 12B models)."
- "Increased Context Window -- The small models feature a 128K context window, while the
  medium models support 256K."
- "Enhanced Coding & Agentic Capabilities -- Achieves notable improvements in coding
  benchmarks alongside native function-calling support"
- "Native System Prompt Support -- Gemma 4 introduces native support for the system role"
- Multi-Token Prediction: "All Gemma 4 models (E2B, E4B, 12B, 31B, and 26B A4B) include a
  dedicated draft model for speculative decoding"

The 12B row is the only one whose "Supported Modalities" lists audio without an audio
encoder; the card explains: "The 'Unified' in Gemma 4 12B Unified refers to its
encoder-free architecture ... projecting raw image patches and audio waveforms directly
into the LLM's embedding space through lightweight linear layers."

### Prompting / operational rules (verbatim, abridged)

- Sampling: "temperature=1.0, top_p=0.95, top_k=64".
- Thinking: "Thinking is enabled by including the `<|think|>` token at the start of the
  system prompt. To disable thinking, remove the token." Output shape:
  `<|channel>thought\n [Internal reasoning] <|channel|>`.
- "For all models except for the E2B and E4B variants, if thinking is disabled, the model
  will still generate the tags but with an empty thought block."
- Modality order: "Image content before the text in your prompt. Audio content after the
  text in your prompt."
- Variable image resolution token budgets: "70, 140, 280, 560, and 1120".
- "Audio supports a maximum length of 30 seconds. Video supports a maximum of 60 seconds
  assuming the images are processed at one frame per second."
- Multi-turn: "the historical model output should only include the final response. Thoughts
  from previous model turns must not be added before the next user turn begins, with the
  exception of tool call turns."

### Data, safety, limitations

- Training data "with a cutoff date of January 2025", web text in "over 140 languages".
- "Out-of-the-box support for 35+ languages, pre-trained on 140+ languages."
- Safety: "we saw major improvements in all categories of content safety relative to
  previous Gemma models ... All testing was conducted without safety filters."
- Limitations are generic (bias, factual accuracy, common sense, language nuance) with no
  numeric safety or refusal rates.

## Summary

The card is the authoritative first-party description of the whole family: five sizes, two
architectures (dense, MoE) plus one encoder-free unified variant, 128K/256K context, audio
only on E2B/E4B/12B, video as frames everywhere, configurable thinking via a control token,
Apache 2.0 licence. Its benchmark table is vendor-run and includes a Gemma 3 27B column,
which is the only direct vendor comparison against the previous generation.

## Gaps

- No publication or revision date on the page, so the card cannot be pinned to the launch
  date; it has evidently been edited since launch because it documents the 12B, which
  shipped two months later.
- No harness descriptions, task counts or confidence intervals for any benchmark row.
- The 31B "Total Parameters" of 30.7B does not match the Hugging Face checkpoint tensor
  total (31.27B) or Fireworks' 32.2B listing.
- No per-size licence difference is stated; the card gives one licence for the family.
- Safety results are described qualitatively; no refusal-rate or violation-rate numbers.

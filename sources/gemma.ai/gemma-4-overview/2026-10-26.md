# gemma.ai: Gemma 4 overview (sizes, memory, QAT)

- Origin: Google DeepMind, "Gemma 4" overview page, Gemma Documentation,
  <https://gemma.ai/models/gemma-4/>
- Evidence kind: official documentation
- Publication date: not stated on the page.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Gemma 4 E2B, E4B, 12B, 26B A4B, 31B

## Captured content

### Architecture split (verbatim)

> Gemma 4 model family spans four distinct architectures tailored for specific hardware
> requirements:
>
> Small Sizes: 2B and 4B effective parameter models built for ultra-mobile, edge, and
> browser deployment (e.g., Pixel, Chrome).
> Dense: A powerful 31B parameter dense model that bridges the gap between server-grade
> performance and local execution.
> Mixture-of-Experts: A highly efficient 26B MoE model designed for high-throughput,
> advanced reasoning.
> Unified: A 12B parameter encoder free model for multimodal tasks, replaced vision and
> audio encoders with direct linear projections of the input.

The page says "four distinct architectures" while listing five sizes; the 12B is a later
addition to the family (see the releases record).

### Capabilities (verbatim bullets)

- Reasoning: "All models in the family are designed as highly capable reasoners, with
  configurable thinking modes."
- "Increased Context Window: Small models feature a 128K context window, while the medium
  models support 256K."
- "Multi-Token Prediction: All Gemma 4 models (E2B, E4B, 12B, 31B, and 26B A4B) include a
  dedicated draft model for speculative decoding, enabling significantly faster inference
  with no quality loss."

### Inference memory table (verbatim, "approximate GPU or TPU memory required to load
Gemma 4 models ... 20% overhead")

| Parameters | BF16 | SFP8 | Q4_0 | Mobile | Mobile (text-only) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gemma 4 E2B | 11.4 GB | 5.7 GB | 2.9 GB | 1.1 GB | 0.84 GB |
| Gemma 4 E4B | 17.9 GB | 8.9 GB | 4.5 GB | 2.5 GB | 2.2 GB |
| Gemma 4 12B | 26.7 GB | 13.4 GB | 6.7 GB | – | – |
| Gemma 4 26B A4B | 57.7 GB | 28.8 GB | 14.4 GB | – | – |
| Gemma 4 31B | 69.9 GB | 34.9 GB | 17.5 GB | – | – |

> Base Weights Only: The estimates in the preceding table only account for the memory
> required to load the static model weights. They don't include the additional VRAM needed
> for supporting software or the context window.

### Quantization-aware training (verbatim, abridged)

- "Gemma offers official Quantization-Aware Training (QAT) models ... resulting in smaller
  models that perform nearly identically to their high-precision baselines."
- Download suffixes: `-qat-q4_0-gguf` (llama.cpp / LM Studio), `-qat-w4a16-ct` and
  `-qat-mobile-ct` (vLLM / SGLang), `-qat-q4_0-unquantized` plus
  `-qat-q4_0-unquantized-assistant` (speculative decoding), `-qat-mobile-transformers`.
- Mobile weights use "a custom wNa8o8 schema ... targeted 2-bit decoding layers".

## Summary

First-party sizing and deployment data. The table is the only first-party statement of
memory cost per size, and the QAT section documents that official 4-bit and mobile
checkpoints exist for each size except 26B A4B compressed-tensors.

## Gaps

- Memory figures are labelled approximate and exclude KV cache; no per-context-length
  figures are given.
- "Four distinct architectures" vs five sizes is an internal inconsistency, resolved only
  by the releases page (12B added 2026-06-03).
- No prices; hosting is not discussed on this page.

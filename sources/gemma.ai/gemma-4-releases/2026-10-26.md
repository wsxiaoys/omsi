## Summary

Three dated facts that no other first-party page gives: the initial Gemma 4 release is
**2026-03-31** in four sizes, Multi-Token Prediction drafters followed on **2026-04-16**,
and the fifth size, **12B Unified**, arrived on **2026-06-03**. The same page dates Gemma 3
to 2025-03-10 (plus 270M on 2025-08-14).

## Gaps

- The page dates the release but does not say what "release" means (weights publication,
  announcement, or API availability). The launch blog is dated 2026-04-02, two days later,
  and the hosts' catalogue timestamps also fall on 2026-04-02/03 — the two-day gap is
  unexplained.
- No version or checkpoint identifiers, and no deprecation or retirement dates.
- No per-size note of which sizes gained MTP on 2026-04-16 beyond the four named.

- Origin: Google DeepMind, "Models — The Gemma model family", Gemma Documentation,
  <https://gemma.ai/models/>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (family tree and per-size routing table)
- Publication date: not stated on the page.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Gemma 4 (all sizes), Gemma 3n, Gemma 3, Gemma 2, Gemma 1, and the variants

## Captured content

### Family tree (verbatim, abridged)

> Gemma — The first generation of Gemma text models
> Gemma 3 — Multimodal text and image models, 270M to 27B
> Gemma 3n — On-device models for phones, laptops and tablets
> Gemma 4 — Multimodal text, image and audio input across five sizes
> DiffusionGemma — Experimental text diffusion for exceptionally fast generation

### Audio and video clarification (verbatim)

> Audio input is native to E2B, E4B and 12B . The 26B A4B and 31B models accept video as
> individual frames — that is, as images — but do not process audio.

## Summary

The index is the clearest first-party statement of the two identity traps around this model:
(1) **Gemma 4's E2B and E4B reuse the exact size labels of Gemma 3n's E2B and E4B**, a different
generation shipped in June 2025 with 32K context (see the Gemma 3n record); and (2) **Gemma 4's 12B shares a label with
Gemma 3's 12B** but is a different architecture (encoder-free) with a 256K context instead of
128K. The table also resolves which sizes take audio (E2B/E4B/12B) and how the large sizes
handle video (as frames, no audio).

## Gaps

- The 26B A4B row says "26B (4B active)" while the model card says 25.2B total / 3.8B active;
  the index rounds both.
- No release dates, licence, or pricing on this page.
- Gemma 3n's 32K context is not stated here; it is on the Gemma 3n card
  (`sources/gemma.ai/gemma-3n-model-card/2026-10-26.md`).

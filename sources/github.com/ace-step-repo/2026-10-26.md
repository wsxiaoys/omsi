## README abstract (verbatim)

> We introduce ACE-Step, a novel open-source foundation model for music generation […] ACE-Step
> bridges this gap by integrating diffusion-based generation with Sana's Deep Compression AutoEncoder
> (DCAE) and a lightweight linear transformer. It further leverages MERT and m-hubert to align
> semantic representations (REPA) during training […] our model synthesizes up to 4 minutes of music
> in just 20 seconds on an A100 GPU—15× faster than LLM-based baselines […] enabling advanced control
> mechanisms such as voice cloning, lyric editing, remixing, and track generation (e.g., lyric2vocal,
> singing2accompaniment).

Controllability section: variations generation (training-free), **repainting** (mask-constrained
re-noise), and **lyric editing** via flow-edit, with the stated caveat "can only modify small segments
of lyrics at once to avoid distortion". Separate LoRA applications: **Lyric2Vocal** (lyrics → dry
vocal) and **Text2Samples** (text → instrumental samples). The "Coming Soon" block lists RapMachine
(later shipped as a LoRA), StemGen and Singing2Accompaniment; the roadmap leaves ControlNet training
code and the Singing2Accompaniment ControlNet unchecked, and "Train and Release ACE-Step V1.5"
unchecked even though the news list announces v1.5.

## Facts taken from the inference source, not the docs

Neither the README nor the model card states an output sample rate or a hard duration ceiling. The
pipeline does:

- `pipeline_ace_step.py`, `latents2audio(...)`: `sample_rate=48000` is the default and is passed to
  `music_dcae.decode(pred_latents, sr=sample_rate)` and to `save_wav_file(...)`. The saved file is
  **48 kHz WAV** by default.
- `music_dcae_pipeline.py`: the codec's internal rate is **44 100 Hz** —
  `MODEL_INTERNAL_SR = 44100` in `decode_overlap`, `torchaudio.transforms.Resample(source_sample_rate,
  44100)` on encode, and `torchaudio.transforms.Resample(44100, sr)` on decode. Latent framing is
  computed as `int(duration * 44100 / 512 / 8)`.
- `pipeline_ace_step.py`, `audio_duration: float = 60.0`; a non-positive value is replaced by
  `random.uniform(30.0, 240.0)`; the extend path clamps to
  `max_infer_fame_length = int(240 * 44100 / 512 / 8)`, i.e. **240 s**. Four minutes is a code-level
  ceiling, not just a claim.

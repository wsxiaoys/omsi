## Verbatim excerpts

> **Dec 30, 2025**: 🤗 We released the inference code and pretrained models of HY-Motion 1.0. Please
> give it a try via our HuggingFace Space and our Official Site!

> **Jan 29, 2026**: 📊 We released the evaluation prompts and code for **SSAE** (Structured Semantic
> Alignment Evaluation), a VLM-based metric designed to assess the semantic alignment of generated
> videos.

> **HY-Motion 1.0** is a series of text-to-3D human motion generation models based on Diffusion
> Transformer (DiT) and Flow Matching. It allows developers to generate skeleton-based 3D character
> animations from simple text prompts, which can be directly integrated into various 3D animation
> pipelines. This model series is the first to scale DiT-based text-to-motion models to the
> billion-parameter level, achieving significant improvements in instruction-following capabilities
> and motion quality over existing open-source models.

Training description (verbatim): "Large-Scale Pre-training: Trained on over 3,000 hours of diverse
motion data… High-Quality Fine-tuning: Fine-tuned on 400 hours of curated, high-quality 3D motion
data… Reinforcement Learning: Utilizes Reinforcement Learning from human feedback and reward models."

Prompting guide (verbatim, numbered items 1–3):

> 1. Language & Length: Please use English. For optimal results, keep your prompt under 60 words. For
>    other languages, please use the Text2MotionPrompter to rewrite the prompt.
> 2. Content Focus: Focus on action descriptions or detailed movements of the limbs and torso.
> 3. Current Limitations (**NOT** Supported):
>    - ❌ Non-humanoid Characters: Animations for animals or non-human creatures.
>    - ❌ Subjective/Visual Attributes: Descriptions of complex emotions, clothing, or physical appearance.
>    - ❌ Environment & Camera: Descriptions of objects, scenes, or camera angles.
>    - ❌ Multi-person Interactions: Motions involving two or more people.
>    - ❌ Special Modes: Seamless loop or in-place animations.

## Supporting code facts read from the repository

- `hymotion/utils/smplh2woodfbx.py` plus `assets/wooden_models/boy_Rigging_smplx_tex.fbx` and
  `scripts/gradio/static/assets/dump_wooden/joint_names.json`: the FBX export targets a bundled
  rigged mannequin mesh; the acknowledgement list credits `SMPL`/`SMPLH` and `FBX-SDK`.
- `hymotion/pipeline/motion_diffusion.py`: `self.output_mesh_fps = kwargs.get("output_mesh_fps", 30)`
  and `self.train_frames = kwargs.get("train_frames", 360)`; `generate()` asserts the requested
  length is `< 5000` frames and otherwise clamps it to `train_frames`.
- `hymotion/utils/t2m_runtime.py` carries the comment "assuming 30fps output, 20fps internal".
- `gradio_app.py` uses `max_duration=12` when loading example prompts.
- `ckpts/README.md` names the text encoders as `openai/clip-vit-large-patch14` and `Qwen/Qwen3-8B`,
  and the optional rewrite/duration model as `Text2MotionPrompter/Text2MotionPrompter`.
- `ssae/` contains a Gemini-video-based evaluator script and prompt sets; it is an evaluation
  artefact shipped in the same repository, not part of the motion model.

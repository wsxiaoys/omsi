## Summary

The predecessor is a separate model with its own paper (2601.20540), its own licence (**Apache-2.0**,
permissive) and its own real-time figure (16 fps, sub-second latency). Version 2.0 changes both the
licence (to CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial) and the stated real-time figure (720p at 60 fps).

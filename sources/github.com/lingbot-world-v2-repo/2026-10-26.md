## Identity and claims (verbatim)

> We present **LingBot-World 2.0** (also known as **LingBot-World-Infinity**), an advanced iteration
> of LingBot-World featuring four distinct upgrades.
> - **Unbounded Interaction Horizon**: Our model achieves an unbounded interaction horizon while
>   maintaining consistent output quality, benefiting from a carefully crafted causal pretraining
>   paradigm.
> - **Rapid Response Time**: Through distilling a real-time variant from the base model, our system
>   guarantees rapid response time, sufficient to drive 720p video streams at 60 fps.
> - **Highly Diverse Interactive Elements**: Compared to the previous version, this update introduces
>   highly diverse interactive elements, comprising a broader spectrum of actions (*e.g.*, attacking,
>   archery, spell-casting, and shooting) alongside a richer variety of text-driven events.
> - **Agentic Harness**: We pioneer the integration of an agentic harness within the domain of world
>   modeling, wherein a pilot agent is tasked with planning and executing character behaviors, while a
>   director agent is responsible for synthesizing novel environmental elements as the scene
>   progresses.

## Inference envelope documented by the README

The codebase "is built upon [Wan2.2]" and ships `generate.py` for "causal inference with KV caching,
which processes video frames chunk-by-chunk instead of all at once".

Three documented commands, verbatim flags:

- 14B `causal_fast`: `--task i2v-A14B --size 480*832 --frame_num 361 --local_attn_size 18
  --sink_size 6 --ulysses_size 8`, `torchrun --nproc_per_node=8` ("480P, 8 GPUs (`ulysses_size` must
  divide 40 heads)").
- 1.3B `causal_fast`: same size/frame flags, `--ulysses_size 4`, `torchrun --nproc_per_node=4`
  ("480P, 4 GPUs (`ulysses_size` must divide 12 heads)").
- 14B `causal_pretrain`: `--infer_mode causal_pretrain --frame_num 81`, 8 GPUs.

All three examples are conditioned on an initial image (`--image examples/03/image.jpg`) plus an
action directory (`--action_path examples/03`) and a text prompt — i.e. the open-weights interface is
image + action + text to video, not text-only.

A commented-out block in the README documents the sampling modes: `causal_fast` = "Distilled few-step
model (`LingBot-World-Fast`)", "4 steps per chunk, no CFG"; `causal_pretrain` = "40 steps per chunk
with CFG". The block is commented out in the served README, so treat those step counts as a stale or
internal note rather than current documentation.

## Summary

The first-party engineering record. It fixes the canonical model names, the alias, the checkpoint
list, the licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial), the two release dates, and the actual open-weights
operating point: 480×832 frames in causal chunks (361 frames in the documented example), image +
action + text conditioning, 8 GPUs for 14B and 4 GPUs for 1.3B. It also states that the real-time
serving stack is deliberately not released.

The "720p at 60 fps" figure appears here as a property of the distilled real-time variant, not of the
checkpoints' documented inference commands, which are 480P.

## Gaps

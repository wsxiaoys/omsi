## Why this record exists

SAM 1 is the image-only, promptable-segmentation origin of the line: three ViT size tiers
(`vit_b`/`vit_l`/`vit_h`), Apache 2.0, visual prompts only. SAM 3's blog describes masks, boxes and
points as "introduced in SAM 1 and SAM 2"; the paper credits SAM 1 (Kirillov et al., 2023) with the
promptable segmentation task. This record supplies the SAM 1 side from Meta's own repository.

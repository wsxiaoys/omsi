## Observations

- The licence permits commercial use and redistribution (royalty-free, worldwide) but is **not** an
  OSI-approved open-source licence: it adds a no-reverse-engineering clause, trade-control and
  ITAR/military-use restrictions, a publication-acknowledgement requirement, and a patent-litigation
  termination clause. GitHub reports the repository licence as `Other` / `NOASSERTION`, and Hugging
  Face tags both weight repos `license:other`.
- Weights are separately **gated** on Hugging Face (manual approval), so "open weights" here means
  obtainable-after-approval, not anonymous download.
- The licence carries no output-ownership grant and no statement about the licence of generated
  masks or derived annotations. It is silent on model outputs.
- SAM 1 and SAM 2 were released under Apache 2.0 (see the SAM 1 and SAM 2 repository records); the
  SAM License is a change of terms for the SAM 3 generation, not an update to the earlier ones.

## Gaps

- No commercial-API terms, no acceptable-use policy, and no statement on outputs are included in the
  file.
- The licence does not distinguish SAM 3 from SAM 3.1 checkpoints, nor say whether the November 2025
  SAM 3 weights remain governed identically.

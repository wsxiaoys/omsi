## Headline claims, verbatim

> OXR-1.0 ranks first in the displayed comparison snapshots: **87.84 on olmOCR-bench, 97.18 on
> OmniDocBench, and 87.76 on DocParsingBench**. It also leads the 21-model speed comparison,
> reaching **557.77 native tokens/s** and **0.990373 pages/s**.

> Accuracy snapshot: 5 October 2026. Rankings apply to the displayed models and recorded evaluation
> protocols.

## OmniDocBench (23 models shown in the document; 37 in the launch post's appendix)

"Overall and component results on 1,651 pages." OXR-1.0: overall **97.18**, text edit 0.0251,
formula CDM 97.44, table TEDS 96.62, TEDS-S 98.44, reading-order edit 0.1214. Runner-up TeleOCR
96.91; PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 96.34; Gemini 3 Pro 92.91; Gemini 3 Flash 92.62.

Provenance note, verbatim:

> Xiaomi-OCR-0 uses the authors' released Mix-RL results; other peer-model scores are sourced from
> the v1.6_full table in opendatalab/OmniDocBench's README, at commit f133a71.

The launch post adds a caveat the benchmark file does not spell out: "Xiaomi-OCR-0's 96.83 score and
0.8B parameter count are reported by its publisher; equivalence of the evaluation environment and
inclusion on the official leaderboard have not been verified"
([launch post](../../somark.ai/oxr-launch-post/2026-10-27.md)).

## DocParsingBench (17 models)

OXR-1.0 overall **87.76** (text 90.60, formula 85.76, table 80.04); PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 85.17; jina-ocr-v1
84.46. Method note, verbatim:

> OXR scores are averaged over three evaluation rounds. Each round attempts 1,400 pages; the fixed
> 58-page exclusion set leaves 1,342 pages for scoring.

No peer model is described as re-run under the same protocol; the document does not state where
peer DocParsingBench scores came from.

## Speed protocol, verbatim

> All speed tests use one NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPU (96 GB), a 24-CPU quota, and 96 GiB RAM
> with no swap. OXR uses vLLM 0.25.1. Serial recognition excludes the model reading-order stage; the
> end-to-end pipeline includes it.

> The speed workload is a frozen, stratified, approximately uniform 100-page subset of the
> 1,651-page OmniDocBench source set. The same 100 unique pages are reused across models.

> Serial decode tok/s excludes each request's first token and input prefill time, and uses native
> decode time. Native tokens vary by tokenizer.

Results: serial decode 557.77 tok/s for OXR-1.0 against 516.39 for PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 (+8.0%);
end-to-end 0.990373 pages/s against 0.741960 for OvisOCR2 (+33.5%), 100 pages in 100.972 s. OXR ran
the 100 pages twice and reports the mean.

## What the numbers do and do not establish

- **The level is vendor-measured.** Every OXR figure in this document is the developer's own run.
  Only the olmOCR-bench file in the weights repository echoes it as a self-reported leaderboard
  entry ([weights metadata](../../huggingface.co/somark-oxr-1-0-weights-metadata/2026-10-27.md)).
- **The ranking is a cross-source collage.** Peer scores come from different publications with
  different harnesses and dates, and the launch post concedes "not all models were rerun in the
  same environment". A 0.24-point olmOCR-bench lead over Infinity-Parser2-Pro cannot be separated
  from harness differences at that margin.
- **One independent partial reproduction exists** and lands close to the vendor number: a community
  MLX port measured 87.6 ± 1.0 on a 596-page / 3,849-test subset of olmOCR-bench with the official
  `olmocr==0.4.27` evaluator ([MLX port](../../huggingface.co/oxr-1-0-mlx-8bit/2026-10-27.md)).
  That supports the level, not the rank.
- **The speed claim is single-machine and single-configuration**, and the document itself warns
  that "accuracy and speed use separate configurations; equivalent accuracy under the speed
  configuration has not been confirmed".
- **Benchmark contamination is addressed only by assertion**: "OXR's training data excludes
  leaderboard evaluation pages." No audit is published.

## Gaps

- No per-model hardware/version table for the peer entries.
- No variance or confidence intervals for the accuracy numbers, despite three DocParsingBench
  rounds.
- No DocParsingBench provenance note, unlike the other two benchmarks.
- `docs/frozen-100-pages.json` was not fetched, so the frozen page set cannot be checked.

## Structure, verbatim

> OXR is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0, subject to the additional terms below.
> These additional terms take precedence in the event of a conflict; otherwise Apache License 2.0
> applies.

Five numbered sections: 1 Commercial License and Thresholds, 2 Online Service Attribution,
3 Competing Hosted API Services, 4 Termination, 5 Definitions and Scope.

## 1. Commercial licence thresholds, verbatim

> Commercial use of OXR and its Derivative Works is permitted without a separate commercial license
> only while you and your Affiliates, on a consolidated basis, do not exceed either of the
> following thresholds:
>
> (a) USD 2,000,000 in Total Funding; or
> (b) USD 2,000,000 in Annual Revenue.
>
> If either threshold is exceeded, you must obtain a separate commercial license from SoMark Team
> before commencing or continuing commercial use.

"Total Funding" is defined as cumulative equity **and debt** financing from all sources, consolidated
across affiliates; "Annual Revenue" is gross revenue of the most recently completed fiscal year,
consolidated, with non-USD amounts converted at their USD equivalent.

## 2. Attribution, verbatim

> When providing an online service to third parties using OXR or its Derivative Works, you must
> prominently identify SoMark OXR in the service's user interface or publicly accessible
> documentation.

## 3. Competing hosted APIs, verbatim

> Unless expressly authorized in advance by a separate written license from SoMark Team, you may not
> use OXR or its Derivative Works to offer, operate, or make available to third parties a hosted API
> service that competes with a hosted API service offered by SoMark Team or its Affiliates.

> This restriction includes services offered through a reseller, a white-label arrangement, or an
> intermediary interface, whether paid or free. A competing hosted API service is one whose
> principal functionality provides document parsing, optical character recognition (OCR), document
> layout analysis, or document content extraction as a substitute for substantially similar
> functionality of a hosted API service offered by SoMark Team or its Affiliates.

> This restriction applies regardless of Total Funding or Annual Revenue. Use solely within your
> organization and its Affiliates, without offering such a service to third parties, is not
> prohibited by this Section; all other terms of this License still apply.

## 4. Termination, verbatim

> Your rights under this License terminate automatically, without notice from SoMark Team, if you
> fail to obtain a commercial license as required by Section 1, fail to comply with Section 2, or

## 5. Redistribution, verbatim

## Why this matters for the wiki

- **The licence explains the absent host.** Section 3 forbids offering a third-party hosted API
  whose principal function is document parsing, OCR, layout analysis or content extraction as a
  substitute for SoMark's. A general inference host reselling OXR for its advertised purpose is
  squarely inside that description, which is consistent with no independent host being found: fal,
  Replicate and OpenRouter catalogue searches on 2026-10-27 returned no entry, and Hugging Face
  lists no inference provider for the repository. The absence is structural rather than incidental:
  as long as Section 3 stands, a third-party host reselling OXR for document parsing is a licence
  violation, so distribution will stay first-party plus self-hosting.
- **"Open source" is the vendor's label, not an OSI classification.** GitHub reports the licence as
  `NOASSERTION`, and the terms restrict commercial use above a funding/revenue threshold and forbid
  a whole class of use regardless of size. Apache-2.0 compatibility is not the question; the
  additional terms govern.
- **The thresholds are small for a funded company.** USD 2M in cumulative funding (including debt)
  is below a typical seed round, so most venture-backed users need a commercial licence. Webdiga
  reaches the same reading independently
  ([Webdiga](../../webdiga.com/oxr-document-parsing/2026-10-27.md)).
- **Self-hosting for internal use is the clearly permitted path**; Section 3's carve-out covers use
  "solely within your organization and its Affiliates".

## Gaps

- No stated price, process or contact for the commercial licence beyond "contact the SoMark team".
- No definition of what counts as "competing" for a general-purpose host that also serves other
  tasks, and no statement on whether fine-tuned derivatives inherit the restriction (Section 5
  applies the terms to Derivative Works, which suggests they do).
- The Chinese translation was not compared clause by clause against the English text.

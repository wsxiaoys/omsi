## Identity and capabilities (verbatim)

> TRELLIS is a large 3D asset generation model. It takes in text or image prompts and generates high-quality
> 3D assets in various formats, such as Radiance Fields, 3D Gaussians, and meshes. The cornerstone of TRELLIS
> is a unified Structured LATent (SLAT) representation that allows decoding to different output formats and
> Rectified Flow Transformers tailored for SLAT as the powerful backbones. We provide large-scale pre-trained
> models with up to 2 billion parameters on a large 3D asset dataset of 500K diverse objects.

Paper: arXiv 2412.01506 (CVPR 2025 Spotlight, per the repository description).

Release log (verbatim excerpts): "**03/25/2025** — Release training code. Release **TRELLIS-text** models and
asset variants generation. … *Note: It is always recommended to do text to 3D generation by first generating
images using text-to-image models and then using TRELLIS-image models for 3D generation. Text-conditioned
models are less creative and detailed due to data limitations.*"; "**12/26/2024** — Release TRELLIS-500K

## Why this matters for TRELLIS 2

- TRELLIS 1 is **text *or* image** conditioned and decodes to radiance fields, 3D Gaussians *and* meshes;
  TRELLIS 2 is single-image conditioned and documented as a mesh-with-PBR output only.
- TRELLIS 1 is "up to 2 billion parameters" on a 500K-object dataset; TRELLIS 2 is 4B on ~800K assets.
- TRELLIS 1 shipped multi-image conditioning and local 3D editing; neither appears in TRELLIS 2's README,
  model card or hosted schemas.
- Both are MIT at the repository level; TRELLIS 2 adds a gated DINOv3 conditioner dependency that TRELLIS 1's
  description does not mention.

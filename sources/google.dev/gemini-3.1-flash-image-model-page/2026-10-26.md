## Captured content

### Summary and "Key updates" (verbatim)

> **Nano Banana 2** provides high-quality image generation and conversational editing at a
> mainstream price point and low latency. It serves as the high-efficiency counterpart to
> Gemini 3 Pro Image, optimized for speed and high-volume developer use cases.

> Key updates:
> - **New output resolution options:** New support for 0.5K, 2K and 4K, default 1K
> - **New Image Search Grounding:** Integration of both text and image search results to
>   inform generation with real-time web data
> - **Supported with Thinking on or off**
> - **New 1:4, 4:1, 1:8 and 8:1 aspect ratios**
> - **Improved aspect ratio adherence**
> - **Improved image quality and consistency**
> - **Improved i18n text rendering**

## Known gaps

- The page lists only the stable version. It does not list the preview ID, its shutdown date,
  or any dated snapshot history, so it cannot be used to distinguish what changed between the
  February preview and the May GA release.
- "Latest update February 2026" is inconsistent with a May 28, 2026 GA date recorded in the
  changelog and deprecations table; the field appears to track model-side changes rather than
  release events. Not resolved here.
- No pricing, latency, or quality numbers appear on this page.
- The page is dated 2026-10-09, three days before Nano Banana 2.1's GA, and still presents
  Nano Banana 2 as the current model; the image-generation guide (captured separately) calls
  it "previous-generation".

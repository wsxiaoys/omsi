## Summary

The Gemini API table dates `gemini-3.8-flash` to **September 2, 2026** and gives it no shutdown date,
while its predecessor `gemini-3.7-flash` (released 2026-08-13) is marked N/A and described as
auto-routing rather than shutting down. "Deprecated" on this page therefore does not mean the string
stops working — it means the string is remapped to the successor.

## Gaps

- "No shutdown date announced" is not a support commitment; the table's own preamble says listed
  dates are earliest-possible, and no date is listed here at all.
- The Agent Platform (Vertex) page gives a concrete retirement date for the same model ID; that
  divergence is recorded on the Agent Platform capture.
- The table's only fixed shutdown dates in the Gemini 3 range belong to `gemini-3.1-flash-lite`
  (2027-05-07) and the preview `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` (2026-11-17). No dated row was captured
  for the 2.5 or 3.8 families beyond what is quoted above.

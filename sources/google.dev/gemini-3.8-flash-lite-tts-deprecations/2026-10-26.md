# Google Gemini API docs: deprecations (3.8 TTS rows and the 3.1 preview shutdown)

- Origin: Google, "Gemini deprecations", <https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/deprecations>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party support-window table)
- Publication date: not stated; continuously edited.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts`, `gemini-3.8-flash-tts`,
  `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`

## Captured content

Page definition (verbatim):

> A "deprecation" is the announcement that we no longer provide support for a model, and that it will
> be "shut down" in the near future. Once a model is "shutdown", it is completely turned off, and the
> endpoint is no longer available.

> The shutdown dates listed in the table indicate the earliest possible dates on which a model might
> be retired.

### Gemini 3 stable table (verbatim rows)

| Model | Release date | Shutdown date | Recommended replacement |

| `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` | September 22, 2026 | No shutdown date announced | — |

| `gemini-3.8-live` | September 15, 2026 | No shutdown date announced | — |

### Preview table (verbatim row)

| Model | Release date | Shutdown date | Recommended replacement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` | February 26, 2026 | November 17, 2026 | `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` or `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts` |

The 2.5-family rows carry the same access note as the catalogue: those models "are not deprecated
and will continue to be served until further notice", but access is limited to prior users.

## Summary

The support window is unambiguous and first-party: **both 3.8 TTS models are GA with no announced
shutdown date**, and the model Flash-Lite replaces (`gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`) is scheduled to
shut down on **2026-11-17** — 22 days after this capture. Any pipeline still on the 3.1 preview has a
dated migration deadline, and Google names Flash-Lite as one of the two accepted replacements.

Note the internal inconsistency: the catalogue marks both 3.8 TTS entries `Stable` and the changelog
calls the launch "generally available (GA)", yet this table's stable section is titled "Gemini 3
models" and files the two TTS IDs under it, while the Cloud/Vertex card for the same ID says
"Launch stage: Preview".

## Gaps

- No statement of how much notice precedes a shutdown.
- The table does not say whether Flash-Lite inherits the 3.1 preview's rate-limit tier.
- The page does not list a shutdown date for `gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts` / `gemini-2.5-pro-preview-tts`
  beyond the access note.

# Google Gemini API docs: models catalogue (Gemini 3.8 TTS entries)

- Origin: Google, "Models", <https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party model catalogue)
- Publication date: not stated; continuously edited.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts` and `gemini-3.8-flash-tts`; also the legacy
  `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` entry

## Captured content

### "Gemini 3" card descriptions (verbatim)

> Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS — Flagship creative text-to-speech model for studio-grade voice fidelity,
> expressive acting, Voice design, and Voice replication. (`New`, `Stable`)

> Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS — Fast, cost-efficient text-to-speech model for high-volume production,
> real-time voice agent cascades, and Voice replication. (`New`, `Stable`)

> Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS — Legacy TTS preview model. We recommend updating to Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS or
> Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS. (`New`, `Preview`)

Note the catalogue's marketing spelling is **"Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS"** (hyphenated); the roster
row and the guide's prose say "Flash Lite TTS". The model code is unaffected.

## Summary

The catalogue resolves the roster label to a stable first-party endpoint and marks both 3.8 TTS
models `Stable`, while the 3.1 Flash TTS preview is labelled "Legacy … Preview" with a migration
pointer to both 3.8 IDs. That is the strongest first-party statement that Flash-Lite is the direct
successor to `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`, not a new lower tier below an unchanged 3.1 model.

## Gaps

- No dates, prices or limits in the catalogue.
- The 2.5 Flash TTS / 2.5 Pro TTS IDs are `*-preview-tts` while the catalogue labels them plainly;
  whether those previews are separately versioned is not stated.

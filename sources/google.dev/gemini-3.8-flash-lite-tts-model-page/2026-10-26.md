### Migration notes from `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` (verbatim, abridged)

> Move turn-level directions into `speech_metadata`: Gemini 3.8 TTS treats input text strictly as a
> verbatim transcript. Inline text directions like "Say cheerfully: Hello!" or "Speaker 1: Hello!"
> may be spoken aloud.

> Use angle-bracket inline tags only for point-in-time vocal events: Keep momentary non-speech
> vocalizations and pauses inline in the transcript using angle brackets (such as `<laugh>`, `<sigh>`,
> `<cough>`, `<breath>`, or `<short pause>`).

> Specify speaker on every turn in multi-speaker requests.

> Account for default WAV (`audio/wav`) output on unary requests: Unlike `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`
> and earlier TTS models (which returned headerless raw PCM `audio/l16` by default), Gemini 3.8 TTS
> returns WAV audio (`audio/wav` / `AUDIO_WAV`) with a standard RIFF header by default for unary
> requests.

> If your pipeline requires headerless raw PCM, mu-law, or A-law audio, explicitly set
> `response_format.mime_type` to `"audio/l16"`, `"audio/mulaw"`, or `"audio/alaw"`.

# Google Gemini API docs: pricing (Gemini 3.8 TTS entries)

- Origin: Google, "Pricing", <https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party price schedule)
- Publication date: not stated; continuously edited.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts`, `gemini-3.8-flash-tts`,
  `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`

## Captured content

Both 3.8 TTS entries carry a dated price step: current prices run "through December 31, 2026" and
double "starting January 1, 2027". All figures are USD per 1M tokens; audio is priced on output
tokens, and the page states: "Audio tokens correspond to 25 tokens per second of audio."

### Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS — `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts` (verbatim)

> Our 3.8 Flash-Lite Text-to-Speech audio model optimized for high-throughput, low-latency, and
> cost-efficient conversational speech.

| Consumption mode | Input (text) | Output (audio) | Equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | free tier free; $0.50 → $1.00 from 2027-01-01 | $6.00 → $12.00 | $0.0015 → $0.003 per 10 s |
| Batch | n/a (no free tier) | $3.00 → $6.00 | $0.00075 → $0.0015 per 10 s |
| Flex | n/a | $3.00 → $6.00 | $0.00075 → $0.0015 per 10 s |
| Priority | free tier free; $0.90 → $1.80 | $10.80 → $21.60 | $0.0027 → $0.0054 per 10 s |

Batch and Flex also halve the input price ($0.25 → $0.50). "Used to improve our products": free tier
**Yes**, paid tier **No**, for every mode.

| Standard | free tier free; $0.50 → $1.00 | $9.00 → $18.00 | $0.00225 → $0.0045 per 10 s |
| Batch / Flex | n/a | $4.50 → $9.00 | $0.001125 → $0.00225 per 10 s |

### Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS Preview — `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` (verbatim)

Standard tier: $1.00 input (text), $20.00 output (audio); Batch: $0.50 / $10.00. No dated step.

## Summary

The output-audio rate is the only price difference between the two 3.8 TTS models: **$6.00 vs $9.00
per 1M audio tokens at standard tier until 2026-12-31** — a 33% discount, and 50% after the January
2027 step ($12 vs $18). Input text is priced identically ($0.50/1M). Against the model it replaces,
Flash-Lite is cheaper on both sides (input $0.50 vs $1.00; output $6 vs $20).

Arithmetic implied by the vendor's own "25 tokens per second" note (derived, not printed by Google):
standard-tier Flash-Lite audio costs **$0.009 per minute** before 2027-01-01 and **$0.018 per
minute** after; Flash TTS is $0.0135 → $0.027 per minute. Batch/Flex halves both.

## Gaps

- No per-character or per-minute price is printed; the schedule is token-based with a token/second
  conversion note, so a text-to-duration estimate requires the caller's own assumption about
  speaking rate.
- The page does not say whether input text tokens are billed on the transcript only or also on
  style/metadata strings.
- No statement on whether failed or truncated requests bill.

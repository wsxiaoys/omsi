### Page definitions (verbatim)

> A "**deprecation**" is the announcement that we no longer provide support for a model, and that it
> will be "**shut down**" in the near future. Once a model is "shutdown", it is completely turned
> off, and the endpoint is no longer available.

> **Note:** The shutdown dates listed in the table indicate the earliest possible dates on which a
> model might be retired.

## Summary

`gemini-3.8-flash-tts` is listed as a **stable model with no shutdown date and no replacement**. It is
the vendor's named destination for three retiring TTS previews, all scheduled for **2026-11-17** —
22 days after this capture: `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`, `gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts` and
`gemini-2.5-pro-preview-tts`.

The 2026-09-22 release date here matches the changelog GA entry exactly
(`sources/google.dev/gemini-3.8-flash-tts-release-notes/2026-10-26.md`), so the Gemini API's two
dating surfaces agree for this ID. The Vertex card's 2026-09-28 / Preview disagreement is a different
platform's record, not a different row here.

The 2026-11-17 date is a **deadline for the models this one replaces**, not for this model. It is
also the second migration wave: the 2.5 TTS previews date from May 2025 and are being cleared on the
same day as the 3.1 preview.

## Gaps

- The deprecation announcement text for the three retiring TTS previews is not in the captured
  changelog range; only this table carries their schedule.
- No statement on what happens to in-flight integrations of the retired IDs, or whether requests are
  auto-routed to a 3.8 model.
- The page gives no support-window commitment for stable models beyond "no shutdown date announced".
- No price, region or rate-limit information.

## Captured content

### Description (verbatim)

> Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS (`gemini-3.8-flash-tts`) is Google's flagship creative text-to-speech model,
> engineered for studio-grade voice fidelity, expressive acting, authentic regional accents, and
> rock-solid long-form multi-turn stability.

### Overview bullets (verbatim, marketing register)

> **High acoustic fidelity and acting nuance:** Delivers rich emotional range, natural cadence, and
> precise adherence to turn-level style directions and inline vocal events (`<laugh>`, `<sigh>`,
> `<short pause>`).
> **Long-form multi-turn stability:** Maintains consistent voice identity, timbre, volume, and
> acoustic room tone across extended dialogues and multi-minute narrations without voice drift.
> **Authentic regional accents and pronunciation:** Supports regional accents and minority dialects.
> **Full voice ecosystem support:** Works seamlessly with prebuilt voices, the Extended Voice Library
> (`GET /v1beta/voices`), custom Voice design personas, and Voice replication (persistent stored voices
> by default, plus optional stateless keys).

### Languages

The page says the model "detects the input language automatically and supports over 130 languages"
and then enumerates a three-column list. Counting the rendered cells gives **132 language names**,
from "Acehnese (Arab script)" to "Vietnamese" — so the prose ("over 130") and the enumeration agree,
while the comparison table rounds to "130". The sibling page's list is shorter: 105 rows of which 2
are em-dashes (not supported), i.e. 103 named languages against a "over 100 languages" claim.

### Migration note (verbatim, decision-relevant)

> Unlike `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` and earlier TTS models (which returned headerless raw PCM
> `audio/l16` by default), Gemini 3.8 TTS returns WAV audio (`audio/wav` / `AUDIO_WAV`) with a
> standard RIFF header by default for unary requests.

## Summary

This page resolves the roster label: the first-party identity is exactly `gemini-3.8-flash-tts`, a
single-version, text-in/audio-out TTS model with an 8,192-token input ceiling and a 16,384-token
output ceiling. It is presented as the creative/flagship tier of a two-model 3.8 TTS pair, not as a
repackaging of a text model. The page states no price, no support window and no watermark, and it
does not say whether the model is GA or preview — that has to come from the release notes, the
catalogue and the deprecations table.

## Gaps

- No release date on this page, only "Latest update September 2026".
- No GA/preview label, no support window, no shutdown date.
- No price, no rate limits, no region list.
- No statement about watermarking or provenance of generated audio.
- No definition of "output token limit" in audio terms (the pricing page supplies 25 tokens/second).

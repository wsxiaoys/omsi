- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party price schedule)
- Publication date: not stated; page footer reads "Last updated 2026-10-26 UTC". Prices are list
  prices on the capture date, including the already-published 2027 step, not a historical record.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `gemini-3.8-flash-tts`; the `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts` and
  `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` blocks are captured for comparison only.

## Summary

The flagship is priced on **output audio tokens at $9.00 per 1M through 2026-12-31, doubling to
$18.00 on 2027-01-01**, with input text at $0.50 → $1.00 per 1M. Batch and Flex halve both; Priority
multiplies both by 1.8. There is no free tier for Batch or Flex, and the free tier is the only place
the page marks prompts as usable "to improve our products".

Derived from Google's own 25-tokens-per-second note (arithmetic, not printed by Google): standard-tier
output costs **$0.0135 per minute** of audio before 2027-01-01 and **$0.027 per minute** after;
Batch/Flex halves that to $0.00675 → $0.0135.

The single price difference from the Flash-Lite sibling is the output-audio rate — $9.00 vs $6.00 per
1M tokens (a 50% premium, and 50% again after the step); input text is priced identically. Against
the model it succeeds (`gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`, $20.00 output), the flagship is **less than
half the output price** while being the higher tier.

## Gaps

- No per-character or per-minute price is printed; the schedule is token-based with a token/second
  conversion note, so any text-to-duration estimate needs the caller's own speaking-rate assumption.
- The page does not say whether input text tokens are billed on the transcript only, or also on
  `speech_metadata` style strings and inline tags.
- No statement on whether failed, refused or truncated requests bill.
- No statement on whether the 2027 step is a promotional expiry or a list-price correction, and no
  announcement page for it was captured.
- Regional price differences and Vertex pricing are not on this page.

## April 15, 2026 — launch (verbatim)

> **Launched Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS Preview**, our cost-efficient, expressive, and steerable text to
> speech model. Read the Text-to-Speech docs to learn more.

## June 17, 2026 — streaming (verbatim)

> **Streaming support for speech generation:** Streaming via `streamGenerateContent` (and
> `stream: true` in the Interactions API) is now supported for the `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`
> model. To learn more, see the Text-to-Speech guide.

## September 22, 2026 — superseded (verbatim)

> **Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS generally available (GA):** Released our
> next-generation text-to-speech (TTS) audio models and the Gemini API Voices endpoint
> (`/v1beta/voices`):

> **Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS** (`gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts`): Fast, cost-efficient TTS model built
> to **replace `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`** for high-throughput production and real-time voice
> agent cascades.

The same entry introduces Voice design, Voice replication and the Extended Voice Library
("query 150+ prebuilt and custom voices").

## 2.5-generation TTS entries (for the version distinction)

May 20, 2025 (verbatim):

> Released the `gemini-2.5-pro-preview-tts` and `gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts` models, which are
> capable of generating speech with one or two speakers.

December 10, 2025 (verbatim):

> **Launched enhancements to our text-to-speech models**, Gemini 2.5 Flash TTS preview (optimized
> for low latency) and Gemini 2.5 Pro TTS preview (optimized for quality), including enhanced
> expressivity, precision pacing, and seamless dialogue.

September 18, 2026 — 2.5-family access note (verbatim):

> To ensure reliable performance for everyone, we are limiting access to the 2.5 models to users who
> have actively used them in the past. These models are not deprecated and will continue to be served
> until further notice through the API.

## Summary

Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS Preview launched **2026-04-15**, gained streaming on **2026-06-17**, and was
replaced on **2026-09-22** by the GA 3.8 TTS pair. Its predecessor generation (2.5 TTS previews)
launched 2025-05-20, before the roster's one-year window, and is access-limited rather than
deprecated.

# Google Gemini API docs: changelog (Nano Banana 2 entries)

- Origin: Google, "Gemini API release notes" (changelog), https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/changelog
- Evidence kind: official documentation (changelog)
- Publication date: rolling; latest entry captured is October 6, 2026.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Nano Banana 2 (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`), plus adjacent entries for
  Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2 Lite and Nano Banana 2.1 used to date the family timeline.

## Captured content

### February 26, 2026 — launch (verbatim)

> **Launched Nano Banana 2, Gemini 3.1 Flash Image Preview**, a high-efficiency model
> optimized for speed and high-volume use cases.

> **Deprecation announcement:** Gemini 3 Pro Preview (`gemini-3-pro-preview`) will be shut
> down March 9, 2026.

So the model was first obtainable as a **preview** on 2026-02-26, under
`gemini-3.1-flash-image-preview`.

### May 28, 2026 — general availability (verbatim)

> Released `gemini-3.1-flash-image` (Nano Banana 2) and `gemini-3-pro-image` (Nano Banana
> Pro), the generally available (GA) versions of our native visual models, Gemini 3.1 Flash
> Image and Gemini 3 Pro Image.

> **Video-to-image generation support:** You can now pass a video file (via direct upload or
> as a public YouTube URL) as multimodal context alongside a text prompt to generate
> high-quality thumbnails, cinematic movie posters, or summary infographics. This feature is
> supported exclusively on the `gemini-3.1-flash-image` model.

> **Deprecation announcement:** The `gemini-3.1-flash-image-preview` and
> `gemini-3-pro-image-preview` models are deprecated and will be shut down on June 25, 2026.

Two points to keep: Nano Banana 2 and Nano Banana Pro reached GA on the **same day**, and at
that moment video-to-image was exclusive to `gemini-3.1-flash-image`. The current image
generation guide (captured separately) extends video-to-image to 2.1 and Flash Lite, so the
exclusivity was a May 2026 state, not a standing one.

### Later family entries (verbatim headings, one line each)

- June 30, 2026: "Released `gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image` (Nano Banana 2 Lite) to general
  availability (GA)".
- October 6, 2026: "Gemini Nano Banana 2.1 generally available (GA): Released Gemini Nano
  Banana 2.1 (`gemini-nano-banana-2.1`) ... An update to Nano Banana 2
  (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`), it maintains Flash-level speed and cost efficiency while
  delivering significant improvements in visual quality, prompt adherence, multi-turn
  character consistency, text rendering, and wide and panoramic aspect ratio generation
  (`1:4`, `4:1`, `1:8`, `8:1`) across `1K`, `2K` ...".

## Known gaps

- The changelog records launches and deprecations, not capability changes to a shipped model
  ID, so it cannot show whether `gemini-3.1-flash-image` itself changed between May and
  October 2026.
- The October 6 entry credits 2.1 with panoramic aspect-ratio generation, but the February 26
  launch blog post (captured separately) and the current image-generation docs already credit
  Nano Banana 2 with `1:4/4:1/1:8/8:1` ratios; the changelog wording reads as an improvement
  claim over 2.1's predecessor rather than a first release, but it is not explicit.
  Unresolved.
- The changelog is not a complete history: entries before 2026-02-19 were not captured for
  this record.

### October 6, 2026 — 2.1 deprecation announcement (verbatim, from the same capture)

The same entry that ships 2.1 also carries:

> **Deprecation announcement**: The `gemini-3.1-flash-image` model is deprecated (no shutdown
> date announced). Migrate to `gemini-nano-banana-2.1`.

### November 20, 2025 — Nano Banana Pro preview (verbatim)

> Released Gemini 3 Pro Image Preview, `gemini-3-pro-image-preview`, the next iteration to the
> Nano Banana model.

This is the earliest image-model entry captured here; it is outside the one-year window and is
recorded only to date the family.

## Additional notes from the merged capture

- 2.1 went straight to GA on 2026-10-06; the changelog records no public 2.1 preview entry.
- The October 6 entry is therefore both the release note and the deprecation notice for
  `gemini-3.1-flash-image`.

## Additional known gaps

- No announcement post, benchmark table, or marketing page for 2.1 was found at the usual Google
  URLs during capture; the changelog line and the model card are the first-party record.
- The changelog gives no pricing change and no statement about whether 2.1's weights differ from
  Nano Banana 2's.

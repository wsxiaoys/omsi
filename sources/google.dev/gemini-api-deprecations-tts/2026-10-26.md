# Google Gemini API docs: deprecations table, `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` row

- Origin: Google, "Gemini deprecations", https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/deprecations
  (publisher: Google)
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party deprecation schedule)
- Publication date: rolling; the page states shutdown dates are the "earliest possible dates".
  Capture date 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`, `gemini-3.8-flash-tts`,
  `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts`

## Row (verbatim)

Under the "Preview models" heading:

| Model | Release date | Shutdown date | Recommended replacement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` | February 26, 2026 | November 17, 2026 | `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` or `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts` |

## Page definitions (verbatim)

> A "**deprecation**" is the announcement that we no longer provide support for a model, and that it
> will be "**shut down**" in the near future. Once a model is "shutdown", it is completely turned
> off, and the endpoint is no longer available.

> **Note:** The shutdown dates listed in the table indicate the earliest possible dates on which a
> model might be retired.

## Contradiction with the changelog

This table dates the release **2026-02-26**. The changelog announces the launch on **2026-04-15**
([changelog](../gemini-api-changelog-tts/2026-10-26.md)), and the model card says "Latest update:
April 2026" ([model card](../gemini-3.1-flash-tts-model-page/2026-10-26.md)). The two first-party
dates are 48 days apart and are recorded here unresolved. fal's endpoint went live 2026-04-16 and
Replicate's model object was created 2026-04-15, both consistent with the April date and neither
consistent with February.

For contrast, the neighbouring 3.8 rows read "September 22, 2026 — No shutdown date announced",
matching their changelog GA date.

## Summary

The vendor has scheduled `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` for shutdown on **2026-11-17**, with the two
3.8 TTS models named as replacements. That is 22 days after this capture, and the page warns the
listed date is the earliest possible retirement.

## Gaps

- The deprecation announcement itself is not in the captured changelog range; only the table carries
  the schedule.
- No statement of what happens to in-flight integrations, whether requests are auto-routed to a 3.8
  model, or whether the model can be extended on request.

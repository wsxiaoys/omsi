# Google Gemini API docs: pricing, Gemini TTS blocks

- Origin: Google, "Pricing", https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing (publisher: Google)
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party price list)
- Publication date: not stated; continuously edited. Prices are list prices on the capture date, not
  a historical record. Capture date 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` (primary subject), `gemini-3.8-flash-tts`,
  `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts`, `gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts`, `gemini-2.5-pro-preview-tts`

## Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS Preview (verbatim)

> `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` — Our 3.1 Flash Text-to-Speech audio model optimized for
> price-performant, low-latency, controllable speech generation.

Standard, paid tier, per 1M tokens (USD):

| Item | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Input, text | $1.00 |
| Output, audio | $20.00 |

Batch, paid tier, per 1M tokens (USD):

| Item | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Input, text | $0.50 |
| Output, audio | $10.00 |

Free tier: "Free of charge" on Standard; "Not available" on Batch. "Used to improve our products":
Yes on the free tier, No on paid.

Footnote: "* Audio tokens correspond to 25 tokens per second of audio."

## Gemini 2.5 TTS (verbatim, for the version comparison)

## Gemini 3.8 TTS (verbatim, for the successor comparison)

`gemini-3.8-flash-tts`: input $0.90 (text) through December 31, 2026, $1.80 starting January 1,
2027; output $10.80 (audio) through December 31, 2026, $21.60 starting 2027 — "Equivalent to $0.0027
per 10s audio" then "$0.0054 per 10s audio". `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts`: input $0.50 through
December 31, 2026, $1.00 starting 2027; output $6.00 through 2026, $12.00 starting 2027.

## Reading the numbers

At 25 audio tokens per second, 3.1 Flash TTS standard output is **$0.0005 per second of audio**
($20 per 1M tokens × 25 tokens/s), or **$0.03 per minute** of generated speech, before input text.

**3.1 Flash TTS costs exactly what 2.5 Pro TTS costs and twice what 2.5 Flash TTS cost.** The 3.8
Flash successor is cheaper on output ($10.80 vs $20.00 per 1M audio tokens) until the announced
January 1, 2027 increase, after which it costs slightly more ($21.60).

## Summary

`gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` is billed per token, not per character: $1.00 per 1M input text tokens
and $20.00 per 1M output audio tokens on the Standard tier, halved on Batch. Audio tokens run at 25
per second of generated audio.

## Gaps

- No per-character rate, so the vendor price cannot be compared directly with character-billed hosts
  without assuming a characters-per-token ratio.
- The page states no minimum charge, rounding rule, or free-tier quota for 3.1.

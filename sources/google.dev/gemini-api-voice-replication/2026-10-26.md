# Google Gemini API docs: Voice replication

- Origin: Google, "Voice replication", <https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/voice-replication>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party feature guide)
- Publication date: not stated; capture date 2026-10-26
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts`, `gemini-3.8-flash-tts`

## Captured content

Scope (verbatim):

> Both Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS (`gemini-3.8-flash-tts`) and Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS
> (`gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts`) support Voice replication.

### Audio and consent requirements (verbatim)

> Every CreateVoice replication request requires two real human audio recordings from the same adult
> speaker (24kHz mono 16-bit WAV recommended):

> Reference audio (`source_audio`): A 10–30 second clip of clean, natural speech from the speaker
> whose voice you want to replicate.

> Consent audio (`consent_audio`): A recording of the same speaker clearly reciting the mandatory
> consent statement in one of the supported languages (for example, in English):

> > "I am the owner of this voice and I consent to Google using this voice to create a synthetic voice
> > model."

### Storage modes (verbatim)

| Storage mode | Identifier | Project limit | Retention (TTL) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stateful (`store=True`) | `voice_...` | 200 voices per project (shared across prompted and replicated voices) | 1 year |
| Stateless (`store=False`) | `voicekey_...` | Client-managed | 7 days |

Stateless mode is described as being "For workloads requiring zero server-side persistence of
biometric voice profiles".

## Summary

Voice cloning on the 3.8 TTS tier is gated by an explicit consent recording: a 10–30 s reference clip
plus a spoken consent statement from the same adult speaker, verified by Google. The 200-voice
project cap is shared with Voice design personas, so designing and cloning compete for the same
quota. Stateless keys avoid server-side storage of the biometric profile but expire in 7 days.

This matters for Flash-Lite specifically: the vendor names "reliable voice replication" as one of its
intended uses, and the guide's feature matrix shows replication unavailable on the 3.1 preview — so
a cloning pipeline cannot stay on the model Flash-Lite replaces.

## Gaps

- No statement on how consent is verified (human review, automated check, or none) or what happens on
  a disputed voice.
- No per-voice price is documented for Voice design or Voice replication on the 3.8 tier (the pricing
  page charges only input/output tokens).
- No statement on whether replicated voices work through third-party hosts (fal, Replicate), which
  expose only the 30 prebuilt voices.

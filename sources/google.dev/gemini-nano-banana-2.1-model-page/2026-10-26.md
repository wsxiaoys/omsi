- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party model card page)

- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `gemini-nano-banana-2.1` (Nano Banana 2.1). The record retains the

## Captured content

### Relationship to Nano Banana 2 (verbatim)

> Gemini Nano Banana 2.1 (`gemini-nano-banana-2.1`) is the latest high-efficiency image
> generation and conversational editing model. An update to Nano Banana 2 (Gemini 3.1 Flash
> Image), it maintains Flash-level speed and cost efficiency while delivering significant
> improvements in visual quality, prompt adherence, multi-turn character consistency, and text
> rendering. It serves as the more efficient counterpart to Gemini 3 Pro Image (Nano Banana
> Pro).

### Statements that imply properties of Nano Banana 2 (verbatim bullets)

> Key updates:
> - Improved visual quality and realism across 1K, 2K, and 4K output resolutions (default 1K)
> - Fixed tiling artifacts on wide and panoramic aspect ratios (1:4, 4:1, 1:8, 8:1) at 2K and
>   4K resolutions
> - Enhanced text rendering and infographic layout accuracy
> - Multi-image fusion supporting up to 14 reference images (character consistency for up to 4
>   characters and object fidelity for up to 10 objects)
> - Grounding with Google Web and Image Search
> - Configurable Thinking levels (minimal, medium default, and high)

Two inferences are available, and both are inferences:

1. "Fixed tiling artifacts on wide and panoramic aspect ratios ... at 2K and 4K" states that
   the defect existed in the predecessor. Nano Banana 2's own documentation does not mention
   it, so the defect is attested only indirectly, by the successor's release note.
2. The reference-image figures Google states for 2.1 (14 total, 4 characters, 10 objects) are
   identical to those the image-generation guide states for `gemini-3.1-flash-image`, which
   suggests the two share the same reference budget. Neither page says the budgets are shared.

## Known gaps

- No deprecation information for `gemini-3.1-flash-image` appears here.
- This record deliberately does not capture 2.1's pricing, capabilities, or limitations.

### Contradictions with other first-party pages

- **Input context.** This page gives an input token limit of **131,072**. The Nano Banana 2.1
  model card says "a token context window of up to **1M**". Both were published 2026-10-06.
- **Output tokens.** This page gives an output token limit of **32,768**. The model card says
  "text, with a **64K** token output". Unresolved; the API limit is the one that binds a request.

### Additional gaps

- The page does not name the base Gemini model, the release date, or a price.
- The page does not say whether `gemini-nano-banana-2.1` accepts the `image_search` search type or
  only web search; that is documented on the image-generation guide.
- "Latest update: October 2026" is a month, not a date; the footer timestamp is the only precise
  date on the page.

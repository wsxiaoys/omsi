# google.dev: Gemini API pricing schedule, Gemma 4 section

- Origin: Google, "Gemini Developer API pricing", Google AI for Developers,
  <https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (published price schedule)
- Publication date: not stated on the page.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Gemma 4 (the section is not split by size)

## Captured content

The page carries one "Gemma 4" block, described verbatim as:

> Our lightweight, state-of-the art, open model built from the same technology that powers
> our Gemini models.

| Line item | Free Tier | Paid Tier, per 1M tokens in USD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input price | Free of charge | Not available |
| Output price | Free of charge | Not available |
| Context caching price | Free of charge | Not available |
| Context caching (storage) | Free of charge | Not available |
| Tuning price | Not available | Not available |
| Grounding with Google Search | Not available | Not available |
| Used to improve our products | Yes | No |

## Summary

On the Gemini API, Gemma 4 has **no paid tier at all**: input, output and context caching are
free of charge on the free tier, and the paid tier is listed as "Not available". Free-tier
traffic is the tier Google marks "Used to improve our products: Yes". No per-token price
exists for Gemma 4 on this surface, unlike the Gemini models on the same page.

## Gaps

- The block is not split by size, although the Gemini API only hosts 31B and 26B A4B, so the
  schedule cannot be read as covering E2B/E4B/12B.
- No rate limits (RPM/TPM) appear in this capture.
- "Not available" for Search grounding contradicts the grounding example in the Gemma 4
  Gemini-API serving guide; unresolved.
- No date or revision marker, so this price cannot be tied to a launch date.

# Google: SynthID watermarking overview

- Origin: Google, "SynthID: Tools for watermarking and detecting LLM-generated Text",
  <https://ai.google.dev/responsible/docs/safeguards/synthid>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party responsible-AI page)
- Publication date: not stated; continuously edited. Capture date 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: none named. Captured to test whether Gemini TTS audio is documented as watermarked;
  the page does not mention TTS, Gemini audio models, or any specific audio model.

## Scope statement (verbatim)

> SynthID is a technology from Google DeepMind that watermarks and identifies AI-generated content by
> embedding digital watermarks directly into AI-generated images, audio, text or video.

The remainder of the page covers SynthID **Text** only: the logits-processor mechanism, the `keys`
and `ngram` configuration parameters, the Hugging Face Transformers v4.46.0+ implementation, and the
open-source reference implementation.

## What the page does not say

- No model is named. There is no mention of Gemini, Gemini TTS, Lyria, or any speech model.
- No statement that audio generated by the Gemini API is watermarked, and no detector endpoint,
  verification API, or opt-out control is described.
- The audio modality appears only in the scope sentence above.

## Summary

Google documents SynthID as a technology that can watermark audio among other modalities, but this
page is about SynthID **Text** and says nothing model-specific. It therefore neither confirms nor
denies that `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` output carries a SynthID watermark.

## Gaps

- No Gemini TTS watermarking statement exists anywhere in the captured corpus: not on the model card,
  the TTS guide, the Cloud TTS reference, the pricing pages, or either host schema.
- No detector or verification route for watermarked speech audio is documented on this page.

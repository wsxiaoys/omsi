# HeyGen: Content Moderation Policy

- Origin: HeyGen legal page, "HeyGen Content Moderation Policy",
  <https://www.heygen.com/moderation-policy> (page title "Content Moderation Policy Guidelines &
  Standards | HeyGen"). Rendered text transcribed.
- Evidence kind: official vendor content policy (first-party)
- Publication date: "Effective as of 25 of July 2024"; the page states changes "come into effect
  immediately upon being posted". No revision date shown at capture.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: all HeyGen hosted models, including HeyGen Video 1 (`heygen-video-1`)

## Captured content (verbatim)

Scope:

> At HeyGen, our Acceptable Use and Moderation Policy underscores our commitment to fostering trust,
> inclusivity, and creativity. This policy, which applies to your access to and use of our Services …
> Attempts to circumvent or violate this policy will not be tolerated.

Consent for depicted people (avatar section):

> In the case of creating custom avatars, you are required to obtain the explicit consent of the
> individual being represented (the "Actor"). Unless explicit consent has been provided, creating
> avatars of other individuals is strictly prohibited. You are also responsible for ensuring that you
> have the consent of the Actor for your use or sharing of the avatar and any content generated based
> on the avatar. The Actor reserves the right to request removal of their likeness from our Services
> at any time.

Prohibited avatars include avatars that:

> Represent or appear in the sole discretion of HeyGen to represent individuals under the age of 18;
>
> **Represent real individuals, including celebrities or public figures, without their explicit
> consent**; or
>
> Represent or appear in the sole discretion of HeyGen to infringe copyrighted images without the
> consent of the copyright owner.

Video content:

> Video content created on the Services must respect the rights of others, and only be used for
> permissible purposes.

Non-permissible categories listed: violence and criminal behaviour (including "zero tolerance policy
for dangerous individuals, organizations and networks of people"); fraud and scams; restricted goods
and services (commercial sex, weapons/ammunition, alcohol, online gambling, endangered species, body
parts and fluids, hazmat); bullying and harassment; child safety/CSAM; political content ("Content
that displays political opinions … or content that promotes political parties or candidates, political
campaigning or lobbying government officials"); intellectual property; account integrity.

Moderation mechanics:

> We use automated moderation tools, powered by machine learning algorithms, to scan and flag content
> uploaded to or generated using our Services that is potentially non-permissible.
>
> Any content, including content flagged through the processes described above may also be reviewed by
> human moderators.
>
> If content is found to breach our policies, HeyGen may, in its sole discretion … remove the content
> or prevent the generation of the content on our Services; notify the user about the decision;
> temporarily or permanently suspend the user's access to our Services; report the content to legal
> authorities …

Appeals exist by email; "The submission of an appeal does not guarantee that you will receive a
response."

## What this establishes

- **Consent for real people is a policy obligation on the user, not an enforced gate for video
  generation.** The explicit-consent requirement is written for custom avatars; for general video
  generation the operative clause is that "Video content created on the Services must respect the
  rights of others", plus the Terms' prohibition on uploading images of individuals without consent.
- **Automated screening plus human review** is the stated moderation mechanism for generated content.
  No threshold, model, or refusal taxonomy is published, and nothing states which categories are
  blocked before generation versus removed after.
- **Political content is restricted**, which is unusual among video models and matters for anyone
  generating news or campaign material.
- Real individuals — including celebrities and public figures — require explicit consent, in HeyGen's
  sole discretion.

## Gaps

- The policy is avatar-centric; it never names `heygen-video-1`, `reference_to_video`, or how
  reference images of people are screened.
- No refusal codes, error taxonomy, or appeal timelines.
- No revision history; the effective date (2024-07-25) predates every video-generation feature, so
  the video clauses may not have been written with this model in mind.

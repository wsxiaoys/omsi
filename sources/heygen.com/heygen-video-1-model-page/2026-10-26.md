## What this establishes

- The consumer product name is **"HeyGen Video 1"** (page title) and **"HeyGen Video"** (product
  name inside the page); `heygen-video-1` is the model identifier used in the API.
- The vendor's own positioning is a **general video model**, not an avatar engine: it "generates the
  picture, dialogue and sound effects of a scene in one pass" from a prompt.
- A public **free tier** exists on the model page: 2 clips a day, 5 s, 768p, with sound, in four
  named styles (Claymation, Live action, Anime, Screen print) and three aspect ratios.
- A published **API price**: "$0.01 per second, launch price through October", i.e. the promo rate is
  the headline rate and expires at the end of October 2026. The page does not state the post-promo
  price; the developer documentation and fal do (see the model doc and fal records).
- The vendor claims a leaderboard position: "Ranked first on Artificial Analysis' video leaderboard".
  See the AA record for what that board actually shows.

## Contradictions inside the page

- The JSON-LD description says "5 to 15 second video scenes", while the developer documentation (same
  capture date) documents up to 30 s for text-to-video and image-to-video. The JSON-LD is stale
  relative to the October 2026 duration increase.
- The free playground is 5 s only; nothing on the page says whether the free tier's clips are
  watermarked. The subscription pricing page ties watermark-free output to paid plans.

## Gaps

- No launch date on the page.
- No fps, audio codec, sample rate, or per-resolution pixel table — those are only in the developer
  docs.
- No statement about commercial-use rights or likeness consent; that material lives in the Terms of
  Service and the Content Moderation Policy (captured separately).
- The page does not say which model version backs the free playground (the API model is
  `heygen-video-1`; the page is titled "HeyGen Video 1").

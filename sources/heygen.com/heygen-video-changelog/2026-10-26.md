## Captured content (verbatim, newest first)

**Added - October 2026 — "HeyGen Video up to 30 seconds"**

> **Longer text-to-video and image-to-video clips**
>
> [`POST /v3/models/videos`](https://heygen-1fa696a7.mintlify.site/docs/models/heygen-video#duration-limits)
> accepts whole-second durations from 5 to 30 for `text_to_video` and `image_to_video` with `turbo` or
> `quality` enhancement, at 480p, 768p, 1080p, or 2K. The model ID and endpoint are unchanged.
> `duration: "auto"` now selects 8–30 seconds for these settings; omitting duration still selects 5
> seconds.
>
> Reference-to-video, `disabled` or `ultraquality` enhancement, and MCP requests remain limited to 15
> seconds.

**Added - October 2026 — "HeyGen Video auto duration"**

> **Let HeyGen Video pick the clip length**
>
> [`POST /v3/models/videos`](https://heygen-1fa696a7.mintlify.site/docs/models/heygen-video) now
> accepts `"duration": "auto"`. HeyGen picks a length from 8 to 15 seconds based on quoted dialogue,
> so spoken lines land at a natural pace. The completed job reports the resolved `duration`, and
> billing uses that length.

**Added - October 2026 — "HeyGen Video 1080p"**

> **Render HeyGen Video at 1080p**
>
> [`POST /v3/models/videos`](https://heygen-1fa696a7.mintlify.site/reference/create-heygen-video) now
> accepts `resolution: "1080p"` alongside `480p`, `768p` and `2k`. The default stays `768p`.
>
> * `1080p` bills at the `2k` rate: 3× the `768p` rate, for output seconds and reference-video seconds
>   alike.
> * `16:9` returns 1890 × 1080 and `9:16` returns 1080 × 1890.
> * `text_to_video` and `reference_to_video` render `1080p` in `16:9` or `9:16`, the same as `2k`. Set
>   `aspect_ratio` explicitly for `reference_to_video`, which otherwise defaults to `adaptive`.
> * `image_to_video` follows the first frame at every resolution.

**Added - October 2026 — "HeyGen Video 2K"**

> **Render HeyGen Video at 2K**
>
> [`POST /v3/models/videos`](https://heygen-1fa696a7.mintlify.site/reference/create-heygen-video) now
> accepts `resolution: "2k"` alongside `480p` and `768p`. The default stays `768p`.
>
> * `2k` bills at 3× the `768p` rate, for output seconds and reference-video seconds alike.
> * `text_to_video` and `reference_to_video` render `2k` in `16:9` or `9:16`.

**Added - September 2026 — "HeyGen Video API"**

> **Generate a short video from a prompt, a first frame, or reference media**
>
> The public API reference now includes
> [`POST /v3/models/videos`](https://heygen-1fa696a7.mintlify.site/reference/create-heygen-video) and
> [`GET /v3/models/videos/{video_id}`](https://heygen-1fa696a7.mintlify.site/reference/get-heygen-video)
> for the `heygen-video-1` model.
>
> * Choose `text_to_video`, `image_to_video`, or `reference_to_video` with `mode`. Generate 5–15
>   second videos at 480p or 768p with a prompt of up to 5,000 characters.
> * Image-to-video follows the first frame's proportions. Reference-to-video requires at least one
>   image or video and accepts up to nine images, three videos, and three audio recordings, with twelve
>   references total.
> * Creation returns `202` with a `video_id`. Poll the status endpoint until `completed`, `failed`, or
>   `cancelled`; completed results include a download URL, and unsuccessful results include failure
>   details.
> * Send an `Idempotency-Key` header when submitting so a retry does not start a second generation.
>   API keys need `videos:write` to create and `videos:read` to poll.

## What this establishes

- **First-party timeline.** `heygen-video-1` was publicly documented in the API in **September 2026**
  at 5–15 s, 480p/768p, ≤5,000-character prompts, ≤12 references. October 2026 added 2K, then 1080p
  (at the 2K rate), then `auto` duration, then 30-second text/image clips. The prompt ceiling was
  later raised (the guide now says 32,000, the reference 16,000; this entry says 5,000).
- The launch configuration was materially narrower than the current one, so any early review of
  "HeyGen Video" describes a different model configuration.
- The reference total has been stated as 12 since launch, even though 9 + 3 + 3 = 15 and the reference
  page says fifteen. The discrepancy is at least two months old, not a transcription slip in one page.
- Every change is "Added" with the model ID and endpoint unchanged: this is one model entity with
  added parameters, not a new version.

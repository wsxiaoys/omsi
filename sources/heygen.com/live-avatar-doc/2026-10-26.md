# HeyGen: Live Avatar (real-time product)

- Origin: HeyGen developer documentation, "Live Avatar",
  <https://developers.heygen.com/live-avatar> (Markdown at `/live-avatar.md`), which indexes the
  separate documentation site <https://docs.liveavatar.com>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party)
- Publication date: none stated
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: HeyGen LiveAvatar; captured to establish whether HeyGen's real-time product is the
  Avatar V engine

## Captured content (verbatim)

> **LiveAvatar** powers real-time, **conversational** avatars: listening, understanding, and responding
> with a live avatar. It ships in two modes so you can choose how much of that loop HeyGen handles:
>
> * **FULL mode** — HeyGen manages the end-to-end conversation (STT, LLM, TTS, turn-taking, memory, and
>   contexts). …
> * **LITE mode** — you keep your own agent/LLM and use LiveAvatar for the avatar layer, wiring in
>   integrations like the ElevenLabs Agent plugin.

> LiveAvatar has its own API, guides, and concepts, documented on a dedicated site.

The page's link list covers "Avatars", "Voice Agents", "Voices", "Contexts", "Memory", "Secrets &
Integrations", "Sandbox Mode", and "API Reference" ("25+ endpoints for sessions, avatars, voices,
contexts, LLM configs, and secrets"). It does **not** mention Avatar III, IV, or V, or an engine
selector.

The LiveAvatar documentation index (<https://docs.liveavatar.com/llms.txt>, captured the same day) lists
no page naming an avatar rendering engine; its only HeyGen-comparison entry is "LiveAvatar vs HeyGen
Interactive Avatar".

## Summary

Real-time conversational avatars are a **separate HeyGen product with a separate API**, not a mode of
`POST /v3/videos`. Neither this page nor the LiveAvatar index ties it to the `avatar_v` engine.

That is enough to say Avatar V itself is asynchronous-only, and to flag "Avatar V in real time" as an
open question rather than a claim either way.

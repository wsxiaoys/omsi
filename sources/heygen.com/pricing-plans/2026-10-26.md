# HeyGen: pricing plans (Avatar V availability by plan)

- Origin: HeyGen marketing site, "Pricing", <https://www.heygen.com/pricing>. Plan comparison copy
  transcribed from the rendered page. The separate API price list lives behind a login at
  <https://app.heygen.com/developers/api?modal=pricing> and could not be read.
- Evidence kind: official vendor pricing page (first-party)
- Publication date: none stated on the page
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: HeyGen Avatar V; Avatar IV for contrast

Billing basis, from the page FAQ:

> Credits are HeyGen's single currency for video and asset creation. Every paid plan includes a monthly
> credit allocation … The number of credits a video or asset uses can vary based on which AI model is
> used, video duration, and the complexity …

> Tiers range from 1,000 credits/mo at $49 to 100,000 credits/mo at $4,300.

Per-operation API pricing is not on this page. The developer documentation's index points to
"Self-Serve Pricing — https://platform.heygen.com/pricing: Per-operation pricing for self-serve API
plans, shown in the API dashboard", and states that "Enterprise contract pricing is not published".

## Summary

Avatar V is a paid-plan feature: the Free plan lists "Avatar IV and Video Agent" only, Creator gets
"Avatar V, with limited usage", Pro gets unrestricted Avatar V, and Business adds 4K export plus five
Avatar V custom avatars. This is the only first-party statement that ties 4K to a plan tier rather than
to a specific engine.

Subscription billing is in credits, and the credit cost of a video depends on model, duration and
complexity. No credit-per-second or credit-per-minute figure for Avatar V is published on this page.

## Gaps

- **No per-second or per-render Avatar V price is published in public first-party material.** The API
  price list is behind an authenticated dashboard, so this knowledge base cannot state HeyGen's own
  Avatar V rate.
- "Limited usage" on Creator is never defined (no cap, no per-month count).
- Credits per Avatar V minute are undocumented, so plan cost cannot be converted to a per-video price.
- The page does not say whether API-key usage bills through the same credits or a separate API plan;
  the developer docs mention both models without reconciling them.
- Nothing states commercial-use or output-ownership terms for generated video; those are in the
  separate Terms of Service, which was not captured.

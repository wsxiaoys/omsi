## What this establishes

- **Output ownership and commercial use.** The user owns User Output, and commercial use is not
  restricted by the Terms (outside termination). This is the first-party basis for commercial use of
  HeyGen Video output; there is no separate model licence, because the model is hosted-only.
- **Consent is contractual and rests on the user.** Uploading images of individuals without their
  consent is listed as prohibited conduct, and impersonation without authorization is prohibited. The
  Terms contain **no consent-verification step** for non-avatar generation, so a HeyGen Video
  reference image or prompt describing a real person is governed by these clauses, not by the avatar
  consent flow.
- **HeyGen reserves discretionary removal** of inputs and outputs, and the user warrants that they
  hold the necessary rights and consents.
- The Terms were last updated **2026-10-15**, five days after the earliest leaderboard appearance of
  HeyGen Video 1 (2026-10-13). Nothing captured shows what changed in that revision.

# HeyGen: Usage Limits (video generation, concurrency, output specs)

- Origin: HeyGen developer documentation, "Usage Limits",
  <https://developers.heygen.com/docs/usage-limits> (Markdown at `/docs/usage-limits.md`)
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party)
- Publication date: none stated
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: HeyGen avatar video generation generally, including Avatar V

## Captured content (verbatim)

Concurrency:

| Plan | Included Concurrent Workflows | Burst |
| :- | :- | :- |
| Pay-As-You-Go | 10 | — |
| Enterprise | 20 | Up to +50 per workflow type at 1.5× |

> Concurrent jobs include any asynchronous generation in progress: Video Agent sessions, avatar video
> renders, and video translations.

Input limits for `POST /v3/videos`, transcribed:

| Resource Type | Supported Formats | Max File Size | Max Resolution |
| :- | :- | :- | :- |
| Video | MP4, WebM | 100 MB | < 2K |
| Image | JPG, PNG | 50 MB | < 2K |
| Audio | WAV, MP3 | 50 MB | — |

Avatar input:

> * **Script text:** Maximum 5,000 characters.
> * **Audio input:** Maximum 10 minutes (600 seconds).

Output video specifications:

> * **Frame rate:** 25 fps for videos containing avatars.
> * **Resolution:** Width and height must each be between 128 and 4,096 pixels. Default output is
>   1080p.
> * **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 or 9:16.
> * **Maximum scenes:** 50 per video.
> * **Maximum duration:** 30 minutes per scene.

> The cap is per scene. An `avatar` or `image` request to `POST /v3/videos` renders a single scene, so
> 30 minutes is also its whole-video maximum.

Rate limits: reads 300/min (Pay-As-You-Go) and 1,000/min (Enterprise); writes `POST /v3/videos`
10/s and 30/s; status polling 500/min plus 600/min per IP; exceeding returns `429` with `Retry-After`.

## Summary

This is the operational envelope for Avatar V rendering: 25 fps, 720p/1080p by default with a
128–4096 px dimension range, a 30-minute single-scene ceiling, a 5,000-character script cap, a
10-minute audio-input cap, and 10 concurrent renders on pay-as-you-go.

The 25 fps figure is the only frame-rate statement in the entire captured corpus, and neither host
publishes a frame rate at all.

## Gaps

- The resolution rules are written for "videos containing avatars" in general, not per engine, so they
  do not settle whether Avatar V can be rendered at 4K.
- Aspect ratios are listed as 16:9 or 9:16 here while the `POST /v3/videos` schema also accepts 4:5,
  5:4, 1:1 and `auto` — an internal vendor inconsistency.
- No latency or queue-time expectations, and no per-engine concurrency.
- Nothing on billing or credit consumption per render.

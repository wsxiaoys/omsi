# HeyGen: Video to Avatar (digital twin training requirements)

- Origin: HeyGen developer documentation, "Video to Avatar",
  <https://developers.heygen.com/docs/avatar-from-video> (Markdown at `/docs/avatar-from-video.md`)
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party)
- Publication date: none stated
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: HeyGen digital twins generally; the input path for Avatar V

## Captured content (verbatim)

> A **digital twin** is trained from footage of a real person, and clones a voice from the same
> recording so the twin speaks in the subject's own voice.

Footage requirements table, transcribed:

| Attribute | Requirement | Recommended |
| - | - | - |
| Duration | 15 to 600 seconds | About 2 minutes of natural talking |
| Resolution | Long edge at least 640 px, at most 4096 px (4K) | 1080p |
| Format | mp4 or webm | mp4 |
| People in frame | One, facing camera for the whole recording | |
| Audio | Clear, audible speech throughout | Quiet room, no background music |
| Framing | The face stays in frame from first frame to last | |

> A `url` input is fetched when you call the endpoint and is capped at 32 MB … Base64 input is capped
> at 16 MB after decoding. … use [direct upload], which accepts video files up to 200 MB.

> Poll `GET /v3/avatars/looks/{look_id}` until `status` leaves `processing`; training usually completes
> within a few minutes.

> A `failed` look carries an `error` object: `training_failed` when the footage could not be trained,
> `moderation_failed` when it did not pass content review.

> `POST /v3/avatars/{group_id}/consent` returns a link, valid for 24 hours, that the subject opens to
> record a short statement on camera. Enterprise workspaces can upload a pre-recorded consent video or
> have the step waived. An uploaded consent video must be under 35 seconds and show the same person as
> the footage.

> Creating a digital twin clones one voice from the training footage automatically. There is no
> separate voice request and no opt-in step. The id is returned immediately as
> `avatar_item.default_voice_id`.

> Render once the look reports `status: "completed"` and the group has cleared consent.

## Summary

Avatar V's input is not a per-request image or prompt: the identity comes from a trained digital twin
(or a photo/studio look), created once through `POST /v3/avatars` with 15–600 s of single-person
footage, a cloned voice, and a consent step. The rendering call then references the look by ID.

This is the record that makes the launch post's "15-second webcam clip" legible: 15 s is the documented
minimum footage length, not the only length.

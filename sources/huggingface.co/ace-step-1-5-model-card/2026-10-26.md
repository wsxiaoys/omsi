## Licence and training-data claims

The card states MIT and asserts that outputs are usable commercially because the training set is
"legally compliant". No licence text, dataset list, or provenance document backs the training-data
claim; the MIT grant covers the weights, not the data. Treat "Commercial-Ready" and "legally
compliant dataset" as **vendor claims**, distinct from the MIT licence itself.

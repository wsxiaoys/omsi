## Flash

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `license` | `mit` |
| `language` | `en` |
| `likes` | 29 |
| library | `chatterbox-flash` (`pip install chatterbox-flash`), class `ChatterboxFlashTTS` |
| architecture | block-diffusion T3 decoder ("Llama-520M + 1 extra `[MASK]` token") over the same S3Gen vocoder and voice encoder as Chatterbox |

Card text (verbatim):

> Chatterbox-Flash is a **block-diffusion zero-shot TTS model** that extends the Chatterbox-TTS
> pipeline with a parallel masked decoder while preserving streaming generation. […] delivering
> autoregressive-class quality at a fraction of the latency.
>
> **~9× real-time** synthesis at the default config (`D = 16`, `α = 0.5`), up to **~13× real-time** at
> `D = 32`, `α = 0.75`. **Time-to-first-packet from 103 ms** […] **RTF as low as 0.076**

Seed-TTS test-en quality table (verbatim values, vendor-run):

| System | SIM-o ↑ | WER ↓ | UTMOS ↑ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ground-truth | 0.734 | 2.14 | 3.52 |
| Chatterbox (AR backbone) | 0.685 | 2.20 | 4.10 |
| Chatterbox-Flash (`α = 0`) | 0.704 | 1.96 | 4.09 |
| Chatterbox-Flash (`α = 0.5`, early decoding) | 0.704 | 2.04 | 4.08 |

Streaming table (single request, 50 utterances, 25 Hz, 0.5B): TTFP 103–119 ms and RTF 0.076–0.107
across block sizes `D = 16/24/32`; Apple Silicon MLX RTF 0.778, 4-bit 0.665 on an M4.

The card's own interpretation: "Converting the autoregressive backbone into a block-diffusion decoder
improves both speaker similarity (`0.685 → 0.704`) and intelligibility (`2.20 → 1.96` WER) while
keeping naturalness essentially unchanged".

## Summary

Two further in-window, MIT-licensed English checkpoints. Nano is a 110M CPU-targeted distillation of
Turbo; Flash is a 2026 architectural variant (block-diffusion decoder, separate `chatterbox-flash`
package) that is the only Chatterbox model with published absolute WER/SIM/UTMOS numbers, measured on
the Seed-TTS test-en set by the vendor. Neither is hosted on fal or Replicate at capture.

## Gaps

- Both benchmark tables are vendor-run; no independent reproduction was captured.
- No stated evaluation date, seed protocol or speaker set beyond "Seed-TTS test-en".
- Flash's `α` / `D` parameters and its relationship to the Turbo checkpoints are not explained.
- Neither card states an input-length limit. The Nano card repeats the family PerTh watermarking
  claim; the Flash card says nothing about watermarking (the vendor blog claims it family-wide).

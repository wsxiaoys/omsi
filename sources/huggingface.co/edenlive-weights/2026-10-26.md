## Verbatim: overview

> EdenLive renders a **proxy or reference video stream**, a **target first frame**, and a **text
> prompt** into refined or stylized video. References can come from 2D/3D scenes, existing engines, or
> recordings. Upstream code maintains interaction state; the model handles visual rendering.

> Control is injected through a **vision encoder**: a post-trained DINOv3 encoder embeds each proxy
> frame, and those features condition the diffusion transformer.

> The [project page](https://vast-eden.ai/?p=edenlive) demonstrates over 24 FPS, minute-scale
> generation, and 480P/720P output. The 5B model supports single-GPU deployment at 720P.

## Summary

The weights are genuinely open: both sizes, both sampling modes, no gate, Apache-2.0 for the
Wan-derived parts. The licence is not uniform — the reference-encoder component carries Meta's DINOv3
licence and the optional workbench first-frame renderer carries a FLUX non-commercial licence — so
"open weights" here does not mean one permissive licence covers a commercial deployment end to end.
The `pipeline_tag` `image-to-video` describes the diffusers wrapper (Wan I2V lineage), not the product
interface, which is a streaming reference-video renderer.

## Gaps

- No stated commercial-use terms for the DINOv3-licensed vision encoder component; the card only names
  the licence file.
- No licence statement about model **outputs** (ownership, attribution, watermarking).
- No card-level safety section, intended-use restrictions, or prohibited-use list.
- `downloads: 335` and `likes: 22` at capture indicate a two-week-old release, not adoption.

## Observations

- **There is no v1.6 repository.** The only FASHN try-on weights published are v1.5, under Apache-2.0,
  which matches FASHN's January 2026 open-source announcement. v1.6 is API-only in every record
  captured.
- The `mmdit` tag is the only architecture hint in the registry (a multi-modal diffusion transformer),
  and it belongs to v1.5. Nothing links it to v1.6.
- `fashn-human-parser` uses `license:other` (not Apache-2.0) even though FASHN describes it as the
  first component of the same open-source series, so the pipeline is not uniformly permissive.

## Gaps

- `downloads: 0` for a model with 142 likes is almost certainly a reporting artefact rather than a
  usage signal; do not read it as adoption evidence.
- No v1.6 card, weights, or evaluation exists to compare against the v1.5 checkpoint.

## Access state at capture

Both repositories are **gated** (`gated: "auto"`, `private: false`). The model card body and files
returned HTTP 401 at capture:

> Access to model briaai/Fibo-2.0-dev is restricted. You must have access to it and be
> authenticated to access it. Please log in.

The gate is a form, not an approval queue — the heading reads "Fill in this form to immediately
access the model for non commercial use", and the card states "Bria AI Model weights are open source
for non commercial use only". The form asks for company name, website, Discord user, and an
"Intended Use" select (Commercial / Academic - Research / Personal), plus a checkbox: "I understand
that commercial use of this model requires a separate commercial agreement with BRIA AI, and that
academic access is granted on request and is limited to non-commercial research and teaching".

## What this establishes

- Fibo 2's weights are **published but not open source in the OSI sense**: they are gated,
  non-commercial by licence (`license_name: bria-fibo`, linked to CC BY-NC 4.0), with commercial use
  requiring a separate agreement with BRIA. Calling them "open-weights" is consistent with AA's and
  the vendor's usage (weights obtainable), but not with "open source" as a licensing claim.
- Both checkpoints are ~8.13B parameters in BF16, and the edit model is a finetune/distillation of
  the generate model, so generate and edit share one base. That matches the identical parameter
  counts.
- The repos are served by a diffusers pipeline class `BriaFibo2Pipeline`, so the weights are
  intended to run outside BRIA's API.
- The only paper referenced is `arXiv:2511.06876`, which is the **FIBO v1** paper ("Generating an
  Image From 1,000 Words: Enhancing Text-to-Image With Structured Captions", submitted 2025-11-10),
  not a Fibo 2 technical report. No Fibo 2-specific paper was found at capture.
- Downloads (1,267 + 1,083) and likes (167 + 127) are small next to mainstream open-weights image
  models on the same hub, i.e. adoption was limited in the first week.

## Gaps

- The model card body could not be read (gated). Nothing here is quoted from a README.
- The licence text itself (`license_name: bria-fibo`) is behind the gate; only the CC BY-NC 4.0 link

- `https://platform.bria.ai/licensing/pricing?model=fibo-2` did not render (client-side app), so
  BRIA's commercial licensing terms for the weights were not captured.
- No training-data description, evaluation results, or intended-use limits were readable.

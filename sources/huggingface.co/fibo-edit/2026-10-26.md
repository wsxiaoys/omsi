## What the comparison establishes

- 1.0 is documented as a **single source image** editor with an optional mask; 1.5 is documented as a
  **multi-reference** editor for up to four images plus improved single-reference quality.
- 1.0's repository remains public and gated-but-obtainable on 2026-10-26, so the two versions are
  separately obtainable and separately documented — the precondition for treating 1.5 as its own
  entity rather than a silent checkpoint refresh.
- Both versions share the pipeline class `BriaFiboEditPipeline`, the arXiv reference, and the licence
  terms, so they are one family with a version bump, not unrelated models.

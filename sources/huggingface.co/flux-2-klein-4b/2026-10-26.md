## Model card — description (verbatim)

> The FLUX.2 [klein] model family are our fastest image models to date. FLUX.2 [klein] unifies
> generation and editing in a single compact architecture, **delivering state-of-the-art quality
> with end-to-end inference in as low as under a second**. Built for applications that require
> real-time image generation without sacrificing quality, and runs on consumer hardware, with as
> little as 13GB VRAM.

> FLUX.2 [klein] 4B is a 4 billion parameter rectified flow transformer capable of generating images
> from text descriptions and supports multi-reference editing capabilities.

> Fully open under Apache 2.0. Our most accessible model runs on consumer GPUs like the RTX
> 3090/4070. Compact but capable: supports text-to-image, image editing, and multi-reference at
> quality that punches above its size. Built for local development, edge deployment, and production
> use.

### Key features (verbatim)

> 1. Our fastest distilled model for sub-second image generation.
> 2. Best suited for interactive workflows, production deployments, and latency-critical applications.
> 3. Text-to-image and image-to-image multi-reference editing in a single unified model.
> 4. Runs on consumer GPUs (~13GB VRAM).
> 5. Open weights available for commercial use under the Apache 2.0 license.

### Reference usage (verbatim code)

```python
pipe = Flux2KleinPipeline.from_pretrained("black-forest-labs/FLUX.2-klein-4B", torch_dtype=dtype)
image = pipe(
    prompt="A cat holding a sign that says hello world",
    height=1024, width=1024,
    guidance_scale=1.0,
    num_inference_steps=4,
    generator=torch.Generator(device=device).manual_seed(0)
).images[0]
```

`guidance_scale=1.0` with `num_inference_steps=4` is the vendor's reference configuration for this
checkpoint. The card also names ComfyUI and Diffusers support, and points to
`github.com/black-forest-labs/flux2` for the reference implementation.

### Limitations (verbatim)

> - This model is not intended or able to provide factual information.

> - As a statistical model, this checkpoint may represent or amplify biases observed in the training

> - Prompt following is heavily influenced by the prompting style.

## What this establishes

- Exact repository identity and casing: `black-forest-labs/FLUX.2-klein-4B`, ungated, Apache 2.0,
  ~436k downloads and ~1.1k likes at capture. For scale, the sibling `FLUX.2-klein-base-4B` reports
  ~579k downloads and `FLUX.2-klein-9B` ~185k over the same counter
  ([base card](../flux-2-klein-base-4b/2026-10-26.md)).
- The distilled checkpoint's reference configuration is 4 steps at guidance 1.0.
- "4B" refers to the rectified flow transformer; the repository also contains a Qwen3 text encoder
  and a VAE, so on-disk size exceeds 4B parameters.
- Weights were published 2026-01-14, a day before the announcement.

## Gaps

- No stated output-resolution ceiling on this card; the ~4 MP figure comes from hosts.
- No benchmark numbers, no latency table, no comparison to 9B or to FLUX.2 [pro]/[dev].
- No statement of what the 4B was distilled from.
- The card does not say whether the `text_encoder` can be swapped or which tokenizer/embedder size
  is required, so the Qwen3-4B identification above is config-shape inference.

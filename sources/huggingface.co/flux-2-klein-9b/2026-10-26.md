> The FLUX.2 [klein] model family are our fastest image models to date. FLUX.2 [klein] unifies
> generation and editing in a single compact architecture, delivering state-of-the-art quality

## What this establishes

- **Exact repo casing and name**: `black-forest-labs/FLUX.2-klein-9B` (capital `B`).
- **Parameter accounting**: "9 billion parameter rectified flow transformer" plus an
  "8B Qwen3 text embedder". The "9B" label therefore counts the flow model only; the text
  encoder is a separate 8B component. A reader should not assume the download is ~9B total.
- **Release timing**: the weights repo was created **2026-01-14**, one day before BFL's
  2026-01-15 launch post. Weights were public from launch; this is not a later open-sourcing.
- **Licence is non-commercial and the repo is gated** (`gated: auto`): access requires
  accepting the FLUX Non-Commercial License Agreement and the Acceptable Use Policy.
- **~29GB VRAM for the 9B** — and immediately next to it, "accessible on NVIDIA RTX 4090 and
  above". An RTX 4090 has 24GB of VRAM, so the two statements cannot both be literal without
  offloading or a quantised build. Recorded as a contradiction in the first-party card, not
  resolved here. The card's own Diffusers example calls `enable_model_cpu_offload()`, which is
  one way the 4090 claim could hold.
- **4 inference steps at `guidance_scale=1.0`** is the reference configuration — a distilled
  sampling regime, consistent with the family overview's "4 (step-distilled)".
- **Filters are a licence obligation**, not just a host policy: the card says filters or manual
  review "must be used" with the 9B weights.
- **Latency claims are vendor claims**: "as low as under a second", "under half a second",
  "matches or exceeds models 5x its size". No benchmark names, test sets, or hardware are given
  for the comparison, so the "5x its size" claim is unverifiable from this record.

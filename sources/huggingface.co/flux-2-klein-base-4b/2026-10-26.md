### Config files (verbatim values)

`transformer/config.json` is identical to the distilled checkpoint's (5 double + 20 single layers,
24 heads × 128, `joint_attention_dim` 7680, `in_channels` 128, `patch_size` 1, `guidance_embeds`
false) except `_name_or_path` = `/raid/yiyi/klein-4b-diffusers/transformer` — i.e. the same
architecture with no `-distilled` suffix.

`text_encoder/config.json` is also identical: `Qwen3ForCausalLM`, `hidden_size` 2560,
`num_hidden_layers` 36, `intermediate_size` 9728, `vocab_size` 151936.

So Base and distilled 4B are **the same architecture and the same text encoder**; the difference is
the distillation training stage, not the shape.

## What this establishes

- Base and distilled are two checkpoints of one 4B model family, both Apache 2.0, both ungated, both
  with the same file layout and config shapes.
- BFL's own words for Base: "Undistilled", "Trained without step or guidance distillation", "Higher
  output diversity". The distilled card's counter-claim is "fastest distilled model".
- Base is *not* "a version without safety fine-tuning" as one host's copy says ("This is the raw,
  pre-fine-tuned version", "hasn't undergone additional safety fine-tuning" —
  [Replicate](../../replicate.com/flux-2-klein-4b-base/2026-10-26.md)). BFL's card makes no such
  claim, and the 4B card's safety section describes pre-release safety fine-tuning applied to the
  proposed release checkpoints of both 4B models (see
  [the distilled card](../flux-2-klein-4b/2026-10-26.md)). The disagreement is unresolved.

## Gaps

- The card gives no recommended step count or guidance value for Base; the training guide's Diffusers
  example uses `num_inference_steps=50, guidance_scale=4.0` for the 9B Base, which is not
  necessarily the 4B Base's recommendation.
- No quality comparison against the distilled 4B beyond "higher output diversity".
- No statement of what the undistilled checkpoint was trained from, or of its relationship to
  FLUX.2 [dev].

## Summary

Hugging Face corroborates the Apache 2.0 relicensing at the repository level (`license:
apache-2.0` on all five) and gives per-checkpoint tensor totals that mostly match the card:
E2B 5.12B, E4B 8.00B, 12B 11.96B. The two large sizes do not match: the card says 30.7B for
the 31B and 25.2B for the 26B A4B, while the published tensors total 31.27B and 25.81B.

Repository creation dates (2026-03-02 for the E-sizes, 2026-03-11 for the large ones) precede
both the release-history date (2026-03-31) and the launch blog (2026-04-02), so they cannot be
used to date the release.

## Gaps

- `createdAt` is repo creation, not publication; private repos can be created in advance.
- The licence link points at `ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/gemma_4_license`, which resolves to the
  deprecated-docs copy of the same Apache 2.0 text; the live licence page is on `gemma.ai`.
- The 31B/26B tensor totals disagree with the model card by ~0.5–0.6B with no explanation.
- No download counts, revision history, or per-checkpoint eval results captured for the four
  non-12B repos.

## Description (verbatim)

> **Granite-4.2-30B** is the flagship reasoning model in the Granite 4.2 family. It delivers
> the strongest performance across reasoning-intensive tasks by leveraging built-in
> ` thinking...</think>` chain-of-thought. It supports flexible thinking modes — full thinking
> (default), non-thinking, and low-effort — allowing users to balance depth vs. latency on a
> per-query basis.

## Summary

The 30B is the largest and strongest tier of Granite 4.2: 30B dense, post-trained from
`granite-4.1-30b-base` with a second agentic-coding SFT phase and the full agentic-RL ladder,
released 2026-08-25 under Apache 2.0, no access gate, 128K native context with a claimed 512K
extension.

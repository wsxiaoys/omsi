## Description (verbatim)

> **Granite-4.2-3B** is the compact reasoning model in the Granite 4.2 family. Despite its
> small parameter count, it delivers strong performance on reasoning-intensive tasks by
> leveraging built-in ` thinking...</think>` chain-of-thought. It supports flexible thinking
> modes — full thinking (default), non-thinking, and low-effort — allowing users to balance
> depth vs. latency on a per-query basis.

Key-capability bullets include "Reasoning-Augmented Tool Calling" and "**512K Context
Window**: Supports long documents, multi-turn conversations, and complex agentic workflows".

## Summary

The 3B is the compact tier of Granite 4.2: 3B dense, post-trained from `granite-4.1-3b-base`,
released 2026-08-25 under Apache 2.0, no access gate, 128K native context with a claimed 512K
extension, no agentic-coding evaluation and no agentic RL.

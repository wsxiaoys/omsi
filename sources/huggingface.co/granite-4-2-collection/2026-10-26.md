## Summary

The family is exactly three instruct checkpoints plus quantized repackagings of each. There
is **no Granite 4.2 base model in the collection** — the 4.2 checkpoints are post-trained on
Granite 4.1 bases, so the 4.1 base repos remain the pre-trained artefacts. No 4.2 vision,
speech or guardian sibling appears in this collection.

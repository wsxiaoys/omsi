## What this establishes

- No `xai` organisation account with public model repositories, and no repository whose name
  contains `grok-imagine`, on Hugging Face at the capture date.
- This is consistent with xAI serving the model as a closed, API-only product, and with neither
  fal nor Replicate describing a downloadable checkpoint.

## Gaps and cautions

- A negative search is **not** proof that no weights exist anywhere. It covers the Hugging Face
  index only, for two query shapes, on one date. Weights could be hosted elsewhere, gated, or
  released later.
- The capture does not distinguish "no repositories" from "repositories not indexed under these
  queries".
- No vendor statement was found that says explicitly "no weights will be released", because the
  `x.ai` site returned HTTP 403 to this fetcher on the capture date (see the page's Evidence status).

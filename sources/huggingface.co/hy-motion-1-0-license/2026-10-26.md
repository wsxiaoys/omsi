## What this record establishes

- The weights are open but **not open-source in the OSI sense**: field-of-use and territory
  restrictions, a 1M-MAU commercial threshold, and a prohibition on using outputs to train other
  models.
- Territory excludes the EU, UK and South Korea. This is a licence restriction on the weights, not on
  fal's hosted endpoint, whose own terms were not captured.
- Tencent disclaims ownership of generated outputs; the user is responsible for them.
- The licence names the release date (2025-12-30) independently of the repository and README.

## Gaps

- No capture of fal's or any host's terms of service, so the hosted-service terms for EU/UK/KR users
  are unknown.
- The document does not state whether the restriction binds downstream users of a hosted endpoint that
  Tencent itself operates.

## Summary

The K3 licence is permissive in the ordinary open-weights sense — use, modify, redistribute,
sublicense, sell — with two commercial carve-outs: a separate agreement is required to run a
hosted "Model as a Service" business above $20M in trailing-12-month revenue, and prominent
"Kimi K3" attribution is required above 100M MAU or $20M monthly revenue. It is an
`license:other` custom licence, not OSI-approved.

## Gaps

- Nothing here establishes K3.1 Pro's terms. Whether the successor repeats, tightens, or
  loosens these thresholds is unknown until the weights ship.
- The licence names the model "Kimi K3" throughout; a K3.1 Pro licence would need its own
  naming, which is part of why it cannot be assumed.
- No statement on acceptable-use restrictions beyond "must comply with applicable laws".

## What the card establishes, verbatim

> We introduce **Ling-3.1-flash-base**, the base checkpoint of [Ling-3.1-flash](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ling-3.1-flash), released to support research and community-driven innovation. With **560B total parameters** and **25B active parameters per token**, the model provides a foundation for continued pretraining, domain adaptation, and post-training research.

> The model was trained using **FP8**. This release provides the **BF16 base checkpoint** produced after **Warmup-Stable and Merge (WSM)** checkpoint merging. BF16 refers to the precision of the released weights, while FP8 refers to the precision used during training.

- License `mit`, `gated: false`, tags include `safetensors`, `bailing_hybrid`, `text-generation`.
- Architecture described as identical to Ling-3.1-flash: "512 routed experts, with 8 routed
  experts and 1 shared expert activated per token", "63 KDA layers and 9 Gated MLA layers".
- Evaluation: "47 benchmarks across eight domains: language understanding, knowledge, code,
  mathematics, reasoning, multilingual capabilities, medical knowledge, and long-context
  understanding", compared against `DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base` (1.6T A49B) and `MiMo-V2.5-Pro-Base`
  (1.02T A42B) under stated shot configurations.

## Summary

The base checkpoint was published on **2026-10-25**, eight days after the instruct weights, under
MIT, as BF16 merged from an FP8-trained run. For the wiki this establishes two things: the Ling
3.1 line ships a genuine base model for fine-tuning, and the family's open-weights posture is
consistent across base, instruct and `1019` checkpoints.

## Model summary, verbatim

> "**Architecture**: Sparse MoE (Mixture of Experts), 309B total / 15B activated parameters"

> "**Context Length**: 1M tokens"

> "**Modalities**: Text, Image, Video, Audio"

> "**Vision Encoder**: 681M-param MiMo ViT (28 layers: 24 SWA + 4 Full)"

> "**Audio Encoder**: 308M AudioTokenizer + 127M audio patch encoder"

> "**Multi-Token Prediction (MTP)**: 5-layer speculative decoder"

Backbone table: 48 layers (39 SWA / 9 GA), hidden size 4096, SWA heads 64 Q / 8 KV, GA heads 64 Q
/ 4 KV, head dims 192 QK / 128 V, sliding window 128, **256 routed experts with 8 activated**, MTP
drafter 5 SWA layers with window 1024. The shared introduction, architecture note and benchmark
table are identical to the Pro card — the same series text with the model-specific tables swapped.

The card's own "Model Summary" section is the only place the 309B/15B pair appears alongside the
vision and audio encoders; the technical report says 310B for the same model.
## Deployment

The Flash card gives its own, smaller-footprint recipe: SGLang `--tp 8 --dp 2` with
`--enable-dp-lm-head`, single node, `--mem-fraction-static 0.65`; vLLM
`--tensor-parallel-size 4`. Same parsers (`--reasoning-parser mimo`, `--tool-call-parser mimo`),
same EAGLE speculative flags, same recommended `temperature=1.0`, `top_p=0.95`, and the same
`vllm/vllm-openai:mimov25-cu129` image as the Pro card.

## Gaps

- No knowledge cutoff, no safety evaluation, no eval settings, no statement of which host
  quantizations are supported.
- The card does not distinguish Flash from Pro's role beyond "efficiency-balanced"; the tier
  framing (price, index score, intended workload) lives in the launch post and API docs.

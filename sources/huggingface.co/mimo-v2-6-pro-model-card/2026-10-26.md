## Model summary, verbatim

> "**Architecture**: Sparse MoE (Mixture of Experts), 1.02T total / 42B activated parameters"

> "**Context Length**: 1M tokens"

> "**Modalities**: Text, Image, Video, Audio"

> "**Vision Encoder**: 681M-param MiMo ViT (28 layers: 24 SWA + 4 Full)"

> "**Audio Encoder**: 308M AudioTokenizer + 127M audio patch encoder"

> "**Multi-Token Prediction (MTP)**: 5-layer speculative decoder"

Backbone table: 70 layers (60 SWA / 10 GA), hidden size 6144, SWA and GA heads both 128 Q / 8 KV,
head dims 192 QK / 128 V, sliding window 128, **384 routed experts with 8 activated**, MTP drafter
5 SWA layers with window 1024, predicting "7 subsequent tokens per forward pass".

Audio path detail: AudioTokenizer 24 layers (12 SWA / 12 GA), 20 RVQ codebooks; patch encoder 6
layers, "four frames per patch (25 Hz → 6.25 Hz)" — the 6.25 tokens/second conversion in the API
docs.

## Serving recipe, verbatim

SGLang: `--tp 16 --dp 2 --ep 16 --moe-a2a-backend deepep … --nnodes 2`, `--reasoning-parser mimo
--tool-call-parser mimo`, `--speculative-algorithm EAGLE`. vLLM:
`--tensor-parallel-size 8 --trust-remote-code --reasoning-parser mimo --tool-call-parser mimo`.
Recommended sampling: "`temperature=1.0`, `top_p=0.95`". Docker images:
`lmsysorg/sglang:latest` and `vllm/vllm-openai:mimov25-cu129` (the vLLM tag still says `mimov25`).

Two-node SGLang at TP16 is the vendor's own minimum-reasonable deployment, which is the practical
self-hosting floor for a 1.02T fp8 checkpoint.

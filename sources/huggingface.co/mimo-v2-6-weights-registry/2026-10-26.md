## What this establishes

- **The weights are published, ungated, under MIT** for both Pro and Flash. This is the strongest
  single fact on the page: Xiaomi's flagship is not just "open-weight" by promise, it is
  downloadable, and Artificial Analysis independently records `licenseName: "MIT"` with the same
  `modelWeightsSourceUrl`.
- **Two post-trained variants per size.** `-RL` (2026-09-21) and `-MOPD` (2026-09-27) share the
  same parameter layout and differ in post-training (MOPD2 distillation on top of mixed RL). No
  captured source says which variant the API serves or which one the benchmark table describes.
- **The registry is small.** Five models and one dataset, no base checkpoints, no GGUF/AWQ
  community ports in the author listing for V2.6 (the author listing's GGUF entries are older
  MiMo-VL-7B conversions).

## Gaps

- The HF API returns no `lastModified`-dated eval results for the MOPD repos, and their cards were
  not captured separately; only the `-RL` cards were read in full.
- No repository carries a licence file link beyond the `license:mit` tag; the MIT text itself was
  not fetched.
- The author listing paginates at 100 and may hide further V2.6 artefacts.

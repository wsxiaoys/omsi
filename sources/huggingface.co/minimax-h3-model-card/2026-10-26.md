### The three-module system

> The complete H3 system consists of the following three modules: H3-Context-IR … H3-Base … H3-Regenerate-2K

- **H3-Context-IR** — "a hosted preprocessing and orchestration system"; "Because H3-Context-IR relies on
  a multi-stage workflow and multiple hosted models and services, it is not included in this
  open-source release. We provide an API that enables users to reproduce the behavior of the official
  workflow." The card calls it "critical to the quality of the final output" and "strongly
  recommend[s]" including it in a pipeline.
- **H3-Base** — generates at 768p from the Context-IR output.
- **H3-Regenerate-2K** — "instead of using a conventional dedicated super-resolution module, we use the
  H3 base model to regenerate its own low-resolution result through an in-context manner"; "Due to the
  complexity of the system, this module is not yet open-sourced. We will release it once it is ready."

### Architecture (verbatim, selected)

- H3-Encoder: "uses the full pretrained weights of Qwen3-VL-32B and provides the hidden states from its
  50th layer to the H3-Omni-Transformer."
- H3-VisualVAE: "a temporally causal video autoencoder with a spatial compression factor of 16×, a
  temporal compression factor of 4×, and 24 latent channels, denoted as f16t4d24"; patchified
  `1 × 2 × 2`, giving 32× effective spatial downsampling and 4× temporal.
- H3-AudioVAE: "compresses 32 kHz audio into a sequence of latent tokens with a temporal rate of 40 Hz",
  independent left/right channels "enabling stereo audio input and output".
- H3-Omni-Transformer: "a 33B-parameter dense, single-stream Transformer, with approximately 13B
  parameters residing in AdaLN-related branches"; AdaLN parameters "do not need to be loaded for
  inference-only deployment"; MM-RoPE over `(t, h, w)`.
- Sparse attention: "H3 natively supports sparse-attention training and inference. The initial
  open-source release provides inference with full attention only. Our sparse-attention implementation
  will be released in a future update."
- Released checkpoints are "CFG-distilled Omni Transformer model weights", BF16.

## Summary

The card is the strongest technical document in the corpus and the one that fixes the output envelope:
**24 fps, 32 kHz stereo, 4–15 s, 768-pixel short edge natively, 2K only via a separate regeneration
module**, 11 dialogue languages. It also makes the open-weights claim concrete and bounded: two
CFG-distilled base checkpoints are released (33.1 B parameters, BF16), but **the context preprocessor
and the 2K regenerator are not**, and the sparse-attention path used in training is not in the release.
A local deployment therefore reproduces 768p output, not the hosted system's 2K output, without calling
MiniMax's APIs.

The card states the encoder is "the full pretrained weights of Qwen3-VL-32B", which means the download
carries a second model's weights whose licence is not addressed here.

## Gaps

- No benchmark table, no comparison to Hailuo 2.3/02, no quality evaluation of any kind.
- No VRAM or latency figures; the deployment example uses 4 GPUs without stating their memory.
- No total download size, only per-file shard structure.
- No statement of what `H3-Base` alone scores against the hosted pipeline, i.e. how much of the quality
  is attributable to the un-released Context-IR stage.
- The card does not date the weight release; that comes from the licence file (2026-08-02).
- The card's licence section points to `docs/QA-about-License.md`, which was not retrieved.

## H3 Max absence (added for the sibling page)

A direct lookup of `MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3-Max` returns **HTTP 401 "Invalid username or password"**
(the Hugging Face response for a repository that does not exist or is not visible). A search for
`MiniMax-H3-Max` returns only community LoRA/quantisation repositories, none published by
`MiniMaxAI`. The open-weights release therefore covers H3 only; H3 Max is API-only on both
MiniMax's API and fal.

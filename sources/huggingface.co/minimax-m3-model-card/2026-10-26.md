Identity and scale, verbatim:

> MiniMax-M3 is a native multimodal model with 1M context. It has ~428B parameters and ~23B
> activated parameters.

Highlights, verbatim:

> - **Native Multimodality:** M3 undergoes mixed-modality training from the very first step,
>   enabling deeper semantic fusion across text, image, and video.
> - **Context Scaling via Sparse Attention:** M3 introduces MiniMax Sparse Attention (MSA) to
>   improve long context efficiency. M3 delivers 9× prefill and 15× decode speedups compared to
>   M2 at 1M context, reducing per-token compute to 1/20.
> - **Coding & Cowork Capability:** M3 achieves frontier-level performance across long-horizon
>   agentic benchmarks, excelling in both coding and cowork.

Reasoning modes, verbatim:

> M3 supports three reasoning modes through the `thinking` parameter:
> - **`enabled`** — Reasoning is always enabled.
> - **`adaptive`** — M3 automatically determines when additional reasoning is beneficial.
> - **`disabled`** — Reasoning is disabled to minimize latency and maximize throughput.

Deployment, verbatim: local download via `hf download MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M3 --local-dir
MiniMax-M3`; recommended servers SGLang, vLLM, Transformers (`model_doc/minimax_m3_vl`),
KTransformers, unsloth, ATOM. "We recommend the following parameters for best performance:
`temperature=1.0`, `top_p=0.95`."

MSA references, verbatim: technical report [arXiv:2606.13392] and the `MiniMax-AI/MSA` GitHub
repository.

## Summary

- M3 and M3.1 Flash share the same scale (**~428B total / ~23B active**), the same 1M context,
  the same MSA attention family and the same `minimax-community` licence name. M3.1 Flash is a
  retrain of this backbone, not a new size class.
- **The documented behavioural difference is reasoning control.** M3 exposes three thinking
  modes including **`disabled`**; the M3.1 Flash guide rejects `disabled` with a 400
  ([M3.1 Flash guide](../../minimax.io/minimax-m3.1-flash-text-generation/2026-10-26.md)).
  M3.1 Flash therefore always pays a reasoning floor, at half M3's per-token price
  ([pricing](../../minimax.io/minimax-m3.1-flash-pricing/2026-10-26.md)); which is cheaper per
  task depends on the workload, and no captured source measures that.
- M3's card is the source for the MSA / arXiv paper reference that the M3.1 Flash card also
  cites (`arxiv:2606.13392`).

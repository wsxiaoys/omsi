## Summary

- **Open weights, restricted licence.** The grant is framed for "non-commercial purposes";
  commercial use is permitted but conditional.
- Two commercial conditions: (a) prominent **"Built with MiniMax M3.1-Flash"** attribution, and
  (b) either a **one-time notice** email or, above **$20M yearly revenue**, a **separate prior
  written authorisation** from MiniMax.
- The Commercial Use definition explicitly reaches **hosted API resale** and **post-trained or
  fine-tuned derivatives**, so it binds third-party inference providers and fine-tuners, not only
  product builders.
- The prohibited-use list is five items long and was transcribed in full: illegal content,
  military use, harm to minors, disinformation, and discrimination.

- The file does not say whether the terms differ for hosted inference providers, although the

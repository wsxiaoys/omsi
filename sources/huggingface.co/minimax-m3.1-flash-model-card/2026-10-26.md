### Card body

Verbatim opening:

> **MiniMax-M3.1-Flash** is a native multimodal mixture-of-experts model for coding, agentic
> workflows, computer use, and cybersecurity. It supports text, image, and video inputs with a
> context window of up to **1M tokens**.
>
> Building on MiniMax-M3, M3.1-Flash improves task performance and inference efficiency with
> sparse attention throughout the model, quantization-aware training, and DSpark speculative
> decoding.

Highlights, verbatim:

> * **Agentic performance:** A generational leap in agentic tasks, coding, and cybersecurity —
>   76.2 on BankerToolBench, 73.7% on DeepSWE v1.1, 61.7 on OSWorld V2, 29.6 on Agents' Last
>   Exam, and 82.2 on CyberGym.
> * **Efficient by design:** Sparse attention throughout, quantization-aware training, and
>   DSpark speculative decoding improve inference efficiency.

"Model at a Glance", verbatim:

> - **Architecture:** Native multimodal mixture-of-experts (MoE)
> - **Parameters:** Approximately 428B total; 23B activated per token
> - **Inputs / output:** Text, image, and video inputs; text output
> - **Context window:** Up to 1,000,000 tokens
> - **Attention:** MiniMax Sparse Attention (MSA) throughout the model
> - **Speculative decoding:** DSpark draft head included

### "What's New in M3.1-Flash", verbatim

> M3.1-Flash retains M3's core mixture-of-experts architecture, with three main changes:
>
> - **Sparse attention throughout:** the first three full-attention layers are replaced with
>   sparse attention.
> - **Quantization-aware training:** the routed MoE experts and the attention paths are
>   quantized during training, reducing memory footprint and improving serving efficiency.
> - **DSpark speculative decoding:** DSpark replaces the earlier EAGLE-like MTP approach. The
>   release includes the draft head for use with compatible serving implementations.

### Deployment and inference settings, verbatim

- `hf download MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M3.1-Flash --local-dir MiniMax-M3.1-Flash`
- Recommended servers: SGLang (cookbook), vLLM (recipe), TokenSpeed (recipe). "Additional
  integrations in progress: Transformers, KTransformers, unsloth, and ATOM."
- Multi-chip: FlagOS, with a `docs/FlagOS-Multi-Chip-Support-and-Downloads.md` in the repo.
- "Recommended sampling settings: `temperature=1.0` and `top_p=0.95`."
- "For reasoning, set `reasoning_effort` in the chat template: `low`, `medium`, `high`,
  `xhigh`, or `max`."
- "The model supports a context window of up to 1M tokens. Usable context length and throughput
  depend on GPU memory and the serving configuration."

## Summary

- **Weights are public.** The repository is ungated, complete (48 shards plus a DSpark draft
  head) and carries 8,782 downloads and 389 likes at capture. This directly contradicts the
  launch post's forward-looking "Model weights will be released later"
  ([launch post](../../minimax.io/minimax-m3.1-flash-launch/2026-10-26.md)).
- **Scale:** ~428B total / 23B active parameters, native multimodal MoE, MSA sparse attention
  throughout, DSpark speculative decoding — the same total/active counts MiniMax gives for M3
  ([M3 card](../minimax-m3-model-card/2026-10-26.md)), so M3.1 Flash is a retrain of the M3
  backbone, not a new size class.
- **Benchmarks are vendor-run, with harnesses disclosed.** Each row names the agent scaffold
  (OpenCode, Codex, Claude Code, mini-SWE-agent) and, for some rows, the judge model. The
  comparator columns are quoted from the competitors' own publications or from AA.
- The card's **Terminal Bench 4.0 figure (38.1)** is the outlier worth flagging: Artificial
  Analysis measures 25.8% and Vals AI 25.3% on the same benchmark
  ([OpenRouter restatement](../../openrouter.ai/minimax-m3.1-flash/2026-10-26.md)), 12–13
  points below the vendor's Codex-harness number.
- Licence is `minimax-community` — see the [licence record](../minimax-m3.1-flash-license/2026-10-26.md).

## What this establishes

- The weights are **openly licensed but not OSI-style open source**: commercial use is permitted,
  with an attribution requirement, a $20M annual-revenue authorization threshold, and a 19-clause AUP.
- This is the first MiniMax music licence in the line; Music 2.6 had no published weights and
  therefore no weights licence at all (see `../minimax-music-26-weights/2026-10-26.md`).
- The licence names **Qwen3-8B** as the base, contradicting the launch post's "Qwen3.5-8B" (see
  `../../minimax.io/music-3-0-launch/2026-10-26.md`).
- Outputs are not separately licensed: the licence grants rights in the Software and puts
  responsibility for outputs on the licensee ("YOU SHALL BE SOLELY RESPONSIBLE FOR DETERMINING THE
  APPROPRIATENESS OF USING … ANY OF THE SOFTWARE OR OUTPUTS").

## Gaps

- No statement about ownership of generated audio, or about training-data provenance and music
  rights.
- No watermarking or provenance requirement for self-hosted output; the marking duty is a disclosure
  clause in the AUP (clause 11), not a technical requirement.
- The $20M threshold's accounting basis (per entity, per year, revenue vs. profit) is not defined
  beyond "aggregate yearly revenue".

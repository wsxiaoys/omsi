## Identity (verbatim)

> **MiniMax Music 3** is a high-performance music generation model for creating complete songs up to
> **five minutes** long. Conditioned on lyrics and a detailed music description, it generates
> structurally coherent songs with expressive vocals, evolving arrangements, and stable long-form
> audio quality.

> MiniMax Music 3 combines an **8B Global LLM** for long-range musical structure, a **0.6B Local LLM**
> for frame-level acoustic detail, and a continuous hidden-state synthesis system based on **Flow
> Matching** and **Flow-VAE**. The model produces 32 kHz, 16-bit stereo WAV audio.

Hub metadata: `library_name: diffusers`, `pipeline_tag: text-to-audio`, tags include
`music-generation`, `text-to-music`, `sglang-omni`.

## Controls and I/O (verbatim)

Lyrics carry section tags:

> … explicit section tags such as `[Intro]`, `[Verse]`, `[Pre-Chorus]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Post-Chorus]`,
> `[Bridge]`, `[Instrumental]`, `[Solo]`, and `[Outro]`.

The music description is a "Structured Caption" with `Global Metadata`, `Vocal Details` and
`Arrangement` sections (same three-part scheme as the launch post). A
[`music-caption-rewriter`](https://github.com/MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-Music3/tree/main/skills/music-caption-rewriter)
skill expands a short prompt into one.

Self-hosted serving uses the SGLang-Omni speech API — lyrics in `input`, description in
`instructions`:

```json
{"model": "MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-Music3", "input": "[Verse]\n…", "instructions": "…",
 "response_format": "wav", "seed": 7, "max_new_tokens": 750, "stream": false}
```

> `max_new_tokens` sets the maximum number of audio frames at 25 frames per second. Generation may
> finish before this limit when the model emits an end-of-audio token. The response is a 32 kHz,
> 16-bit stereo WAV file.

The diffusers pipeline takes `prompt`, `lyrics`, `audio_duration=60.0` and a `torch.Generator` seed.

## Documented limitations (verbatim, complete list)

> - Inference requires CUDA.
> - Only non-streaming generation is currently supported.
> - The tokenized text prompt is limited to 5,000 tokens.
> - Audio generation is limited to 9,000 acoustic frames.
> - Section tags and music descriptions provide generative control rather than strict symbolic
>   guarantees. The generated tempo, key, instrumentation, lyrics, and song structure may not always
>   match every requested detail exactly.

## Hardware

> The full precision fits under 24GB of VRAM. With automatic CPU offloading, generation takes in ~22
> GB; additionally streaming the language model layer by layer makes it fit even 8 GB video cards.

## What this establishes

- The weights are real, downloadable and documented: an 8B+0.6B hybrid LM plus a 2.4B flow-matching
  stage, served by SGLang-Omni, diffusers and ComfyUI. This is the structural difference from Music
  2.6, which was closed and hosted only.
- **Output format is first-party stated as 32 kHz, 16-bit stereo WAV.** fal's schema for the same
  weights says 44.1 kHz (see `../../fal.ai/minimax-music-3/2026-10-26.md`) — a direct contradiction.
- **9,000 acoustic frames at 25 fps = 360 s**, i.e. a six-minute hard frame ceiling in the reference
  implementation, while the prose claim (and the vendor's billing unit) is five minutes.
- Determinism is available: a `seed` is an input in both the SGLang-Omni and diffusers paths, and
  `audio_duration` is an explicit request parameter. Neither existed on the `music-2.6` API.
- Section-tag vocabulary here is title-case and has no `[Hook]`, `[Break]`, `[Transition]`,
  `[Build Up]`, `[Inst]` — different from the vendor API's 14 tags for `music-2.6`.

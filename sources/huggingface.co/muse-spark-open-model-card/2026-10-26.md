## Summary

The weights are real, published, and gated: two Meta repositories created 2026-09-15 and made
current on 2026-10-15, BF16 at 1.64 TB of storage with **822,036,529,664 total parameters** in the
Hub's own count, and an NVFP4 variant. The licence is a bespoke **Muse Model License** dated
2026-10-15 — not an OSI licence. It permits commercial use, modification, distillation and
redistribution, but adds an attribution string ("Built with Muse Spark Open"), a usage policy

`language: en` only and `pipeline_tag: image-text-to-text`.

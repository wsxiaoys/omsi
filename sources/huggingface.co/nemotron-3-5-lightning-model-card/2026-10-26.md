The card states most evaluations used NeMo Gym-native harnesses, with SWE-Bench and
Terminal-Bench run "using NeMo Evaluator natively". Recipes are published in
`NVIDIA-NeMo/Gym` under `nemotron_recipes/lightning-3.5/reproducibility.md`.

**Reading against independent measurement.** Artificial Analysis measures this model at
Intelligence Index 12.86, GPQA 74.3%, HLE 10.6%, AA-LCR 60.3%, SciCode 32.1%, Terminal-Bench
2.1 24.3% ([AA](../../artificialanalysis.ai/nemotron-3-5-lightning/2026-10-26.md)). GPQA,
HLE, SciCode and Terminal-Bench 2.1 track NVIDIA's own figures within a couple of points;
AA-LCR does not (60.3 vs 52.0 in the other direction) and AA's GDPval is 637.7 against
NVIDIA's 832 on "GDPval-AA-V2". So on this card vendor and independent numbers are broadly
consistent on the classic benchmarks and diverge on the AA-specific ones.

## Positioning

> "intended primarily for customization and post-training rather than direct production
> inference"

and the developer-facing alternative is the NVFP4 checkpoint. This matters for anyone
comparing it to Super VL: NVIDIA sells Lightning as a base to post-train and to serve quantized,
not as a drop-in BF16 production model.

## Summary

Lightning is the small text-only tier of the 3.5 generation: 30B/3B-active hybrid
Mamba-2+MoE with MTP, GA 2026-08-11, text-in/text-out, 1M context on multi-GPU or 256K on a
single H100, six supported natural languages, OpenMDW-1.1, commercial use permitted. It is
positioned as a post-training base and high-volume agent tier rather than a production BF16
endpoint. Its vendor benchmark table is more conservative than Super VL's and agrees with
independent measurement on the shared benchmarks.

## Gaps

- No price: NVIDIA publishes none for this model.
- The card does not say what distinguishes 3.5 Lightning from Nemotron 3 Nano / Super beyond
  the MTP layers and the 1M-context extension.
- AA counts 31.6B total / 3.6B active where the card says 30B / 3B. Neither source explains
  the difference.
- NVFP4 / DFlash / DSpark sibling cards were not read; only the BF16 card is captured here.

Network detail: "Nemotron Hybrid MoE with 512 routed experts per MoE layer, a C-RADIO vision
encoder, and a vision-language connector."

Reasoning levels are three, not two:

> "Nemotron 3.5 Super VL has configurable reasoning levels: off, on (medium), and on."

## Context window is capacity, not a promise

> "The model supports up to 1M tokens of context. The prompt, visual inputs, conversation
> history, tool results, and generated response share this context window. Individual
> deployment recipes may use a smaller context window to accommodate the available GPU memory
> and concurrency."

Every host observed at capture serves 256K or 1M depending on provider — see the
[OpenRouter record](../../openrouter.ai/nemotron-3-5-super-vl/2026-10-26.md).

## Input constraints

> "Video: mp4, up to 2 minutes. Image: Red, Green, Blue (RGB) (jpeg, png)"

Documents "can be supplied as page images with accompanying text". Video is handled by
sampled frames: the supplied vLLM recipe uses `--limit-mm-per-prompt '{"video": 1, "image":
1}'`, `--video-pruning-rate 0.5` and `{"video": {"fps": 2, "num_frames": 256}}`.

Supported languages for post-training, in addition to English: "French, German, Italian,
Japanese, Spanish, and Chinese"; the card's HF `language:` list is
`en, es, fr, de, it, ja` (no `zh`, though Chinese appears in the training-data section).

Harness disclosure, verbatim:

with recipes and a reproducibility tutorial in `NVIDIA-NeMo/Gym`; a named exception list

that we plan to open source in the future".

**Reading the table against independent measurement.** NVIDIA's own GPQA-Diamond 89.52 and HLE
31.79 are far above what Artificial Analysis measured for the same model family tier (AA's
Intelligence Index for Super VL is 26.49 with HLE 30.7% — HLE is the one number that roughly
agrees). The card's Terminal Bench 4 score is 1%, and AA independently measured 0% on the same
benchmark. See [AA](../../artificialanalysis.ai/nemotron-3-5-super-vl/2026-10-26.md). The
vendor's stronger relative showing is on benchmarks it runs itself with its own scaffolding.

## Training composition (as stated)

Six stages: MTP head training on 100B tokens against a frozen backbone, then 1T tokens of
distillation from Nemotron 3 Ultra Base; 100B Indic CPT, 35B long-context extension to 1M, 67B
software-engineering mid-training at 256K, 9B long-context recovery; vision-language training;
two-stage SFT on packed sequences up to 524,288 tokens; RLVR + RLHF + distillation; MTP
boosting. Pre-training data cutoff and post-training cutoff are **not** stated on this card
(the Lightning card states September 2025 / May 2026).

## Hardware and runtimes

Runtimes vLLM and SGLang; hardware "NVIDIA Blackwell / NVIDIA Hopper"; OS Linux. Test hardware
enumerated: H100, H200, GB200, GB300, B200, B300.

## Summary

This is the authoritative first-party record for the flagship: 120B/12B-active hybrid
Mamba-2 + MoE with a C-RADIO encoder, text+image+video in / text out, 1M-token context with
per-recipe reductions, three reasoning levels, MTP-based speculative decoding, BF16 reference
weights under OpenMDW-1.1, GA on 2026-10-15, deployable on 4× H100 or 2× GB200. It supplies a
large vendor-run benchmark table whose GPQA/LiveCodeBench/APEX numbers are much stronger than
independent measurement supports, plus one metric where vendor and independent runs agree
(Terminal-Bench 4 ≈ 0).

## Gaps

- The technical report PDF
  (`research.nvidia.com/labs/nemotron/files/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Super-and-Lightning-Technical-Report.pdf`)
  is linked but was not read; no PDF text extractor was available in this environment.
- The benchmark table header says "SuperGA", the prose says "Super"; the card never reconciles
  them, and there is no separate "Super GA" model repo.
- No pre-training or post-training data cutoff is given for Super VL, so knowledge freshness
  cannot be stated.
- The card does not address third-party rights in its open pre-training data; OpenMDW-1.1
  pushes that diligence onto the licensee.
- Card figures are vendor-run; no independent re-run of GPQA-Diamond or LiveCodeBench was found.

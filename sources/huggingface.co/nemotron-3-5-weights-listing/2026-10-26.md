Three observations:

1. **The family is far wider than four chat models.** Embedding, reranking, document parsing,
   content safety and streaming ASR all carry the 3.5 name. "Nemotron 3.5" on a leaderboard row
   therefore names a generation of at least a dozen first-party artifacts.
2. **Repository creation precedes the announced release, by 3 to 18 days.** Super VL repos were
   created 2026-10-11/12 and announced 2026-10-15; Bolt 74B-A9B repos were created 2026-10-07/09
   and released 2026-10-15; Bolt E4B repos were created 2026-10-05 and released 2026-10-23.
   Creation dates are upload prep, not release dates.
3. **Lightning is by far the most downloaded 3.5 checkpoint** (1.02M for the NVFP4 build,
   896K for BF16) — 27x the flagship's BF16 count. Download volume is not a quality signal, but
   it does show where the open-weights community actually went.

## Ecosystem, for context only

The same search at `limit=1000` returns **702 repositories, 677 of them third-party**: `unsloth`,
`ggml-org`, `mlx-community`, `RedHatAI`, `lmstudio-community` and `AtomicChat` quantizations;
`fastino` domain fine-tunes (Finance, Healthcare); `EAGLE-Corp` EAGLE-4 drafters; `bottlecapai`
ThinkingCap variants; and `abacusai/Smaug-Nemotron-3.5` (created 2026-10-18). Their existence
is evidence of adoption, not of capability, and none of their numbers are used on the model
page.

## Summary

The Hugging Face org listing confirms open weights for every 3.5 checkpoint under `nvidia/*`,
shows the variant structure (BF16 reference, NVFP4 optimized, Base, GGUF, DFlash/DSpark drafters),
and establishes that the 3.5 name spans embedding, reranking, parsing, safety and speech models
as well as chat models. It also fixes repository creation dates, which are consistently earlier
than announced release dates and should not be used as release dates.

## Gaps

- Download and like counts are point-in-time and were not normalised by age.
- The listing does not show licence per repo; that came from the individual model cards.
- Third-party derivatives were counted but not inspected.
- `nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Super-VL` (the ID OpenRouter publishes) does **not** appear in this
  listing, confirming it is an alias rather than a repository.

## Summary

The card establishes Olmo 3.1 32B Instruct as a **new 32B instruct checkpoint built on the Olmo 3 32B
base** (`Olmo-3-1125-32B`) through a fresh SFT → DPO → RLVR chain (`Olmo-3.1-32B-Instruct-SFT` → `-DPO`
→ final), Apache 2.0, English, date cutoff December 2024, released with all intermediate checkpoints
except most of the RL run ("due to a checkpointing issue, we only are releasing the final few
checkpoints"). Vendor numbers put it well above Qwen 3 32B (No Think) on AIME 2025 (57.9 vs 21.3) and
IFBench (39.7 vs 31.3) while trailing it on MMLU (80.9 vs 85.8) and GPQA (48.6 vs 54.4).

## Gaps

- The card's "Model Details" section copies the **Think** card's dataset list ("Dolci-Think-SFT-7B",
  "Dolci-Think-DPO-7B", "Dolci-Think-RL-7B") for an *instruct* model, while the repository metadata and
  the technical report describe instruct-side Dolci corpora. The stage descriptions are therefore
  unreliable as a record of which corpora trained this checkpoint.
- No context window, parameter count, or tokenizer/vocabulary details on the card; those come from
  `config.json`.
- No harness description, sample counts, or confidence intervals for the evaluation table; every column
  is vendor-produced.
- No pricing or hosting information; the card lists no inference provider.
- No statement of what "3.1" changed relative to Olmo 3 for the instruct side, because no Olmo 3 32B
  instruct checkpoint was released to compare against.

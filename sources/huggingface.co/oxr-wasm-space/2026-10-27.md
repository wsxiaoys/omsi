## What this establishes

- **A second independent runtime exists**, and it is radically cheaper to run than the vendor's
  documented path: no NVIDIA GPU, no vLLM, no layout service. That is a real capability difference
  from the README's 24 GB-VRAM floor
  ([README](../../github.com/somark-oxr-readme/2026-10-27.md)).
- **The licence travels with the weights**, and a third party reads it as "Apache-2.0 with
  additional terms" and redistributes the terms alongside the bundle — consistent with Section 5 of
  the licence ([licence](../../github.com/somark-oxr-license/2026-10-27.md)).
- It is a **distribution, not a host**: nothing is served to third parties, so it is evidence that
  the weights run outside the vendor's stack, not a serving option — the same status as the MLX
  conversion ([MLX](../../huggingface.co/oxr-1-0-mlx-8bit/2026-10-27.md)).

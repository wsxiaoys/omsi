or because it is private/gated.

## What this establishes

- As of 2026-10-26, **no public Qwen Image 3 checkpoint** was discoverable on Hugging Face under
  the Qwen organisation, while the previous generation (`Qwen-Image-2.1`, created 2026-09-14) was
  public and actively used.
- The most recent open-weights Qwen image release is therefore **Qwen-Image-2.1**, not Image 3.
  Qwen Image 3's documented access path is the hosted Model Studio API.

## Gaps

- A search result is weak evidence of absence: weights could be published under a different name,
  in a different organisation, gated, or on a different host (ModelScope is Alibaba's own model
  hub and was **not** searched). This record supports "no public weights found", not "no public
  weights exist".
- The 401 responses do not distinguish "does not exist" from "exists but private", so a gated
  release cannot be ruled out from this capture.
- No licence terms for Qwen Image 3 were captured on any surface; the licence question is open
  rather than answered.

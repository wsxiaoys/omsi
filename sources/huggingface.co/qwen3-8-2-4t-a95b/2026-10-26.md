## Relationship to the hosted Max model (verbatim)

> **Qwen3.8-Max** is the official version based on Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B with more features,
> such as vision input & non-thinking support, 1M context length by default, official
> built-in tools, etc.

> For the first time, Qwen3.8 brings a Qwen-Max-class model to open release.

This is the only first-party statement of how the open artifact differs from the paid Max
SKU: the open model is text-only, thinking-only, 262K-native, and has no built-in tools;
Max adds vision, a non-thinking mode, a 1M default context, and hosted tools. **The
weights in this repository are therefore not the Max serving configuration.**

## Architecture (verbatim)

- Type: Causal Language Model; stage: pre-training & post-training.
- "Number of Parameters: 2.4T in total and 95B activated"; hidden dim 8192; token
  embedding 248,320 (padded); **92 layers**.
- Hidden layout: `23 × (3 × (Gated DeltaNet → MoE) → 1 × (Gated Attention → MoE))` — a
  hybrid linear/full-attention stack, 3:1 in favour of Gated DeltaNet.
- Gated DeltaNet: 128 linear-attention V heads, 16 QK heads, head dim 128.
- Gated Attention: 64 Q heads, 4 KV heads, head dim 256, RoPE dim 64.
- MoE: **512 experts**, 10 routed + 1 shared active, expert intermediate dim 2048.
- "MTP (Multi-Token Prediction): trained with multiple steps".
- "**Context Length: 262,144 natively and extensible up to 1,010,000 tokens.**"

## Sampling and output-budget guidance (verbatim)

> - `temperature=1.0`, `top_p=0.95`, `top_k=20`, `min_p=0.0`, `presence_penalty=0.0`,
>   `repetition_penalty=1.0`
> - **Reasoning Content:** Set the maximum output length to 262,144 tokens.
> - **Final Response:** Set the maximum output length to 131,072 tokens.

Thinking is controlled by `reasoning_effort` and `preserve_thinking`; the card instructs
Qwen Cloud API users to pass `extra_body={"enable_thinking": True, "preserve_thinking":
True}`. Note the asymmetry: `reasoning_effort` is the open-weights control, while
`enable_thinking` is the hosted toggle.

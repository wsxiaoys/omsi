## Architecture (verbatim spec)

- Type: "Causal Language Model with Vision Encoder"; pipeline tag `image-text-to-text`;
  config `architectures: ["Qwen4ExpForConditionalGeneration"]`, `model_type: "qwen4_exp"`.
- "Number of Parameters: 125B with 6B activated, plus 51B n-gram embedding and 4B MTP".
- Hidden dimension 2560; 48 layers; "Hidden Layout: 12 × (3 × (Gated DeltaNet → MoE) →
  1 × (Qwen Sparse Attention → MoE))".
- Qwen Sparse Attention: 24 Q heads, 2 KV heads, head dim 256, RoPE dim 64, indexer "MQA
  with 4 Query Heads and 1 Shared Key Head", "Budget: 512 blocks or 2048 tokens".
- MoE: 512 experts, "10 Routed + 1 Shared" active, expert intermediate dim 640.
- Gated Residual: 4 branches, bottleneck rank 320.
- N-gram embedding table 20,000,000 entries ("bigrams/trigrams at layer 2"); token
  embedding 248320 (padded).
- "Context Length: 262,144 natively and extensible up to 1,000,000 tokens."

Safetensors metadata reports **179,999,981,424** BF16 parameters — i.e. 180B for the
125B + 51B + 4B sum, which is where a third party's "180B total" comes from.

## Reasoning and serving controls (verbatim)

> Qwen3.8-Flash-Next models operate in thinking mode by default, generating thinking
> content signified by `…` before producing the final responses.

- Thinking is controlled "via `enable_thinking`, `preserve_thinking`, and
  `reasoning_effort`"; `reasoning_effort` default `xhigh`, "supported levels are xhigh,
  medium, and low".
- Preserved thinking is on by default: "retains thinking blocks from all historical
  messages"; disabling it keeps only the latest user message's blocks.
- Recommended sampling: thinking `temperature=1.0, top_p=0.95, top_k=20`; instruct
  `temperature=0.7, top_p=0.80, top_k=20, presence_penalty=1.5`.
- Recommended output budget "within the 1M context length": reasoning 262,144 tokens,
  final response 131,072 tokens.
- Long context beyond 262,144 requires YaRN (`rope_type: yarn`, `factor: 4.0`,
  `rope_theta: 10000000`, `partial_rotary_factor: 0.25`); the card warns "All the notable
  open-source frameworks implement static YaRN … **potentially impacting performance on
  shorter texts**."
- Long video: the shipped `video_preprocessor_config.json` is "conservatively configured";
  raising `longest_edge` to 469,762,048 "correspond[s] to 224k video tokens … for
  hour-scale videos".

   Assistant business, the licensee shall obtain a separate license from Qwen before
   Using the Software … for any commercial purpose", with internal use exempt.

"Model as a Service" is defined as giving third parties "access to language model
inference or fine-tuning … in a manner that allows such third parties to exercise
meaningful control over the inputs, parameters, or training data", excluding "the mere
relaying of requests to models hosted by other third parties". "AI Work Assistant" is
defined narrowly around coding/office-productivity products and excludes single-purpose
tools.

Card metadata: `license: other`, `license_name: qwen-community-1.0`. This is not
Apache-2.0 and not an OSI licence; the hosting clause is the one that constrains
resellers.

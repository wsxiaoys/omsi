## Tokenizer card (verbatim)

> This repository contains the **Qwen3-TTS-Tokenizer-12Hz**, as presented in the paper
> [Qwen3-TTS Technical Report](https://huggingface.co/papers/2601.15621).
>
> Qwen3-TTS-Tokenizer-12Hz achieves extreme bitrate reduction and ultra-low-latency streaming,
> enabling immediate first-packet emission through its 12.5 Hz, 16-layer multi-codebook design and a
> lightweight causal ConvNet.

The tokenizer card repeats the repository's ten-language claim and the 97 ms first-packet figure,
and defers all quality numbers to the technical report.

## Gaps

- The model cards are near-copies of the GitHub README; they add no licence terms beyond
  `apache-2.0`, no usage restrictions, no consent policy and no watermark statement.
- No card states a text-length limit, an audio-duration ceiling, a VRAM figure, or an output
  container/bitrate (only the codec's 24 kHz rate is exposed, via `config.json`).
- No card documents which hosted endpoint runs which checkpoint.
- The Hub `language` tag is absent on four of the six cards; the ten-language claim rests on the
  cards that set it plus the README and `codec_language_id`.

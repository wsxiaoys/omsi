classified in HF's `< 3B` size bucket. This is an unverified third-party submission attached to the
repo, not a Meta claim and not a measurement made here. It is recorded because it is the only
benchmark number on the page; it should not be compared to the paper's SA-Co or LVIS figures, which
measure different tasks.

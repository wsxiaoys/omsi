## Parameter counts disagree with the host and the paper

- Hugging Face metadata: **2,305,495,793** (medium) and **567,573,761** (small).
- fal's description: "1.4 billion parameter" (medium) and "459 million parameter" (small).
- Paper Table 1: diffusion transformer only, **1.4B** and **459M**, with SAME-S 108M and SAME-L 852M.

Arithmetic reconciles two of the three: small 459M + 108M = **567M**, matching Hugging Face's
567,573,761 almost exactly. Medium 1.4B + 852M = **2.25B**, which is ~53M short of Hugging Face's
2.31B — plausibly a text encoder, but no captured page says so. The paper is explicit that its counts
exclude the autoencoder; fal repeats the paper's numbers; Hugging Face reports the whole bundle. **All
three are correct about different things**, and a reader comparing "1.4B" to "2.3B" is comparing a
transformer to a bundle.

## Gating prompt, verbatim (from the rendered page and `cardData.extra_gated_prompt`)

> By clicking "Agree", you agree to the License Agreement and acknowledge Stability AI's Privacy
> Policy. This model also includes components redistributed under the Gemma Terms of Use. By
> proceeding, you agree to those terms as well, including the use restrictions in Section 3.2.

Two things to preserve from that prompt:

1. **The "License Agreement" link points at
   `https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-audio-open-1.0/blob/main/LICENSE.md`** — the licence file
   of a different, earlier model, not a Stable Audio 3 licence file. The repo metadata names
   `stable-audio-community`; the gate links to Stable Audio Open 1.0. Both of those licence files are
   themselves gated, so neither text was readable.
2. **Components are redistributed under the Gemma Terms of Use**, with the gate explicitly binding the
   user to "the use restrictions in Section 3.2". No captured page says which components those are.

## Inference provider

The `stable-audio-3-medium` repo lists exactly one inference provider:

> `{"provider":"fal-ai","modelStatus":"live","providerStatus":"live","providerId":"fal-ai/stable-audio-3/medium/text-to-audio","task":"text-to-audio","isModelAuthor":false}`

Hugging Face's own metadata therefore confirms that fal serves this repo, and that fal is **not** the
model author.

## Summary

Stable Audio 3.0 ships as six Hugging Face repositories: three gated post-trained checkpoints and three
ungated base checkpoints, under a licence the metadata calls `stable-audio-community` but whose gating
prompt links to Stable Audio Open 1.0's licence text. No `large` repo exists. The download counts
(77k / 52k / 36k for the post-trained repos as of 2026-10-26) are the only adoption signal in the
corpus.

## Gaps

- **The model cards were not read** — every post-trained repo is gated, and the licence files the gate
  links to are gated too. Any claim about what the cards say (recommended steps, VRAM, limitations)
  would be unsourced from this capture.
- No file listing, no safetensors size, no licence text.
- `numParameters` for the three `-base` repos was `null` in the API response.

Two internal inconsistencies to carry forward: the prose says it "accepts text, images, and
video" while the specification table's Input row says only "Text, image"; and the table's
"Maximum Output | Up to 1M tokens" contradicts the vendor documentation's 64k output ceiling
(see the [docs capture](../../stepfun.com/step-5-preview-docs/2026-10-26.md)). The serving
guidance below resolves the output question in favour of "input and output share the 1M
window": "The 1M-token context limit covers input and output combined."

## Deployment, verbatim essentials

- "can be served through an OpenAI-compatible API with vLLM or SGLang, or used for offline
  generation with the standalone Transformers implementation."
- Container images: `vllm/vllm-openai:step-5-preview` and `lmsysorg/sglang:step-5-preview`;
  served model name `step5`; vLLM port 18000, SGLang port 30000.
- Hardware examples: FP8 on one node of 4× B300 (TP4/TEP4/DEP4) or 8× H200 (TP8/TEP8/DEP8);
  BF16 on 8× B300 or 2× 8× H200. `--max-model-len 1048576`, `--enable-prefix-caching`,
  speculative decoding `--spec-method mtp --spec-tokens 3`, `--reasoning-parser step5`,
  `--tool-call-parser step5`.
- "**BF16 validation:** The full BF16 `convert.py → generate.py --tp-dir` file-shard workflow
  has not yet been validated end to end." The BF16 path is therefore documented but
  unverified by the vendor.

## Summary

The card establishes identity (`Step-5-Preview`, 600B/27B sparse MoE, 1M context, text + image
+ video input, text output, weights published under StepFun Model License 1.0), the sibling
BF16 and ModelScope repositories, the evaluation harness names, and the self-hosting recipe. It
establishes **no** benchmark number, no release date, and no preview/GA timeline.

## Gaps

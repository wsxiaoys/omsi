## Summary

Tripo H3.1 is a closed, API-only model. The only open weights in the Tripo lineage are TripoSR (2024,
MIT-licensed, mirrored under `stabilityai/TripoSR` and widely forked) and the VAST AI Research releases
TripoSG / TripoSF / TripoSplat, which are separate models from a different organisation, despite the shared
name. None of these is H3.1, and nothing in the search suggests H3.1 weights exist.

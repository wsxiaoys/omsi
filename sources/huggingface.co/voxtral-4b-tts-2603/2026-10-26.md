## Summary

The predecessor's weights are public under **CC BY-NC 4.0** — non-commercial, gated, 4B parameters
built on `Ministral-3-3B-Base-2512`, 20 preset voices, 24 kHz output.

This is the licence the successor does **not** carry. The Mistral organisation's Hugging Face account
returns five Voxtral repositories on a `voxtral` search — `Voxtral-4B-TTS-2603`,
`Voxtral-Mini-4B-Realtime-2602`, `Voxtral-Mini-4B-Realtime-Arabic`, `Voxtral-Small-24B-2507`,
`Voxtral-Mini-3B-2507` — and **no Voxtral 3 TTS weights**.

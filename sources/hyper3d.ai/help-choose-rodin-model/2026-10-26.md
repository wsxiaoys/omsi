# Hyper3D Help Center: "Choose Rodin Model and Generation Settings"

- Origin: Hyper3D (Deemos) Help Center, "Choose Rodin Model and Generation Settings",
  <https://hyper3d.ai/help/articles/choose-rodin-model-and-generation-settings>.
- Evidence kind: official vendor support documentation (product behaviour).
- Publication date: undated.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26.
- Relevant models: Rodin Gen-2.5 and Rodin Gen-1.5 as exposed in the Rodin workspace.
- Capture type: page text transcribed; quoted text verbatim.

## Content (verbatim)

> "Select Gen-2.5 for more capable, adjustable reasoning or Gen-1.5 for faster simple-model generation,
> then set privacy, batch size, and reusable presets."

> "**Gen-2.5** is labeled as the most capable current model. Its Thinking Effort choices range from
> Extreme-Low, Low, Medium, and High to Extreme-High; the workspace shows approximate generation times
> beside each choice. Use lower effort for fast iteration and higher effort when the reference has
> difficult structure or fine geometry."

> "**Gen-1.5** is described as faster and better suited for simple models. Its modes are Default for
> balanced quality and speed, Focal for more detail at a slower pace, Zero for a clearer and sharper
> result, and Speedy for the fastest pass."

> "The workspace exposes 1 result by default; 5x and 10x are marked as Business-plan options."

> "After geometry is ready, use the confirmation controls for face count, topology, normal baking, and
> supported material or texture options. The labels shown in your active workspace and plan are the
> source of truth for availability and credit cost."

## What this establishes

- In the consumer workspace the choice presented is **Gen-2.5 vs Gen-1.5**; Gen-2 is not offered as a
  visible option there, even though the API still serves it. That is a product-surface difference, not
  evidence of withdrawal.
- The workspace's "Thinking Effort" ladder is the consumer name for the API's `tier` values
  (Extreme-Low … Extreme-High). "Adjustable reasoning" is the vendor's own framing — a compute/effort
  ladder, not a separate reasoning model.
- The vendor explicitly presents Gen-2.5 as "the most capable current model".
- Face count, topology, normal baking, and material choices are made **after** geometry generation, at
  a confirmation step — consistent with the API's separation of geometry from texture/material passes.

## Gaps

- No numbers: no face-count ranges, credit costs, or time estimates beyond "approximate generation
  times" shown in the UI.
- Does not say whether Gen-2.5 in the workspace is the same weights as `Gen-2.5-*` in the API.
- Does not define "privacy" or which plans include private generations.

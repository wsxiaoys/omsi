# Hyper3D Help Center: plan costs and failed-generation credits

- Origin: Hyper3D (Deemos) Help Center, two articles read on 2026-10-26:
  "How Much Do Hyper3d Plans Cost",
  <https://hyper3d.ai/help/articles/how-much-do-hyper3d-plans-cost>; and
  "Are Credits Deducted When a Generation Fails",
  <https://hyper3d.ai/help/articles/are-credits-deducted-when-a-generation-fails>.
- Evidence kind: official vendor support documentation (billing behaviour).
- Publication date: undated.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26.
- Relevant models: all Rodin generations on the Hyper3D account billing system.
- Capture type: page text transcribed; quoted text verbatim.

## Plan prices (verbatim)

> "Free: $0 … When you need credits without a subscription, direct credits are currently listed at
> $1.50 per credit."

> "Creator: $30 monthly or $280 yearly … Under standard settings, the plan is estimated to support
> approximately 60 model generations. Actual usage may vary depending on the selected model, quality
> settings, and options."

> "Business: $120 monthly or $1,152 yearly … The standard tier is estimated at about 416 models; actual
> output varies with credit cost per task."

> "Enterprise uses a custom quote rather than a public fixed price."

## Failure billing (verbatim)

> "Failed web generations do not deduct credits, and API credits are returned when a submitted task
> later reaches a failed status; requests that fail immediately consume none."

> "If a successfully submitted task is later detected as failed during polling, the consumed credits
> will be returned to your account wallet."

## Contradiction with the pricing page

The help article lists **Creator yearly at $280**, while the live pricing page shows "Billed yearly -
$288 today" ($24/mo equivalent). The monthly price ($30) agrees. No captured page explains the
difference; the most likely reading is that the help article lags a price change, but that is an
inference.

## What this establishes

- Credits are refunded on terminal failure, so a failed generation is not a paid outcome — relevant
  because the API returns HTTP 201 for accepted-but-failing submissions.
- The consumer plans are sold on a model-count estimate, not a stated credit balance, and the estimate
  is explicitly settings-dependent.
- Vendor pricing is inconsistent across its own surfaces at the yearly Creator tier.

## Gaps

- No per-tier credit cost for workspace generations.
- No statement of what "standard settings" means for the model-count estimates.

# Hyper3D Help Center: "Control Topology Polycount and Lods"

- Origin: Hyper3D (Deemos) Help Center, "Control Topology Polycount and Lods",
  <https://hyper3d.ai/help/articles/control-topology-polycount-and-lods>.
- Evidence kind: official vendor support documentation (workflow guidance).
- Publication date: undated.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26.
- Relevant models: Rodin generation in the Hyper3D workspace (generation-agnostic; no tier named).
- Capture type: page text transcribed; quoted text verbatim.

## Content (verbatim)

> "Choose face count and topology according to the asset's destination… Triangle or quad output, normal
> baking, and downstream LOD variants should be validated in the target renderer, DCC tool, or engine."

> "Rodin separates base geometry generation from the topology choices used for delivery. First decide
> whether the shape is correct. Then use the confirmation stage to choose a face count and supported
> topology for the destination."

> "The workspace recommends selecting face count according to use, from very small budgets to millions
> of faces."

> "Raw triangle output is practical for previews, printing, and many real-time handoffs. Quad topology
> can be more useful for subdivision, deformation, and artist cleanup. Always inspect the downloaded
> mesh because a topology label does not remove the need for rigging or simulation review."

> "When the confirmation interface offers normal baking, enable it for a lower-poly asset that still
> needs the appearance of finer surface detail. Validate the normal map in the destination renderer,
> including tangent-space orientation, UV seams, hard edges, and mirrored areas."

> "Rodin face-count controls define the delivered mesh; they do not replace an engine-specific LOD
> setup. Create or export appropriately simplified variants, keep scale, pivot, materials, and UVs
> consistent, then assign and test distance thresholds in the target DCC tool or engine."

## What this establishes

- The vendor's own support documentation treats **LODs as a downstream user task**, not a Rodin output.
  This contradicts the marketing page's "optional LOD generation" phrasing
  (`sources/hyper3d.ai/rodin-image-to-3d-page/2026-10-26.md`) and supports reading that phrase as
  "face-count control enables LOD workflows" rather than "the model emits LODs".
- Topology and face count are chosen **after** geometry confirmation, matching the API's separation of
  geometry from material/texture stages.
- The vendor explicitly warns that a topology label "does not remove the need for rigging or simulation
  review" — i.e. quad output is not claimed to be rig-ready.
- Normal-baking caveats (tangent space, UV seams, mirrored areas) imply UV islands and mirroring exist,
  but no UV layout guarantee is stated.

## Gaps

- No face-count numbers, no per-plan availability, no statement of which generations support normal
  baking.
- No description of the quad-retopology method or its failure modes.
- Nothing about pivot placement or unit scale, despite instructing users to keep them consistent.

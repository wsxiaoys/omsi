# Hyper3D: Terms of Service (output rights and restrictions)

- Origin: Hyper3D (Deemos), "Terms of Service", <https://hyper3d.ai/legal/terms>.
- Evidence kind: official vendor legal terms (current version, no version number shown).
- Publication date: undated live document.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26.
- Relevant models: Rodin and ChatAvatar outputs.
- Capture type: sections transcribed; quoted text verbatim.

## Operator

> "the operator of hyper3d.ai, Deemos Corporation ("Deemos" or "the Company"), a corporation with its
> registered address at 108 West 13th Street Wilmington, Delaware."

Users must be "AT LEAST 18 YEARS OLD OR THE MINIMUM AGE REQUIRED IN YOUR COUNTRY".

## Rodin output rights (verbatim)

> "**Rodin** If you use Rodin to generate Output, we will not limit your use of such Output, subject to
> any restrictions set forth in these Agreements, the Applicable Laws and any third-party terms and
> conditions applicable to such Output or Services. You may set your Output generated through Rodin as
> 'private'…"

Contrast, same section: ChatAvatar output is licensed for "personal entertainment use under the Creative
Commons license", and "Users shall not use the Content for commercial purpose" unless a separate
ChatAvatar licence is purchased.

Also, on copyright:

> "we make no representations, warranties or undertakings of any kind as to the copyrightability of any
> Output or the availability of Intellectual Property Rights…"

## Restrictions relevant to a 3D pipeline (verbatim selections)

- "using the Services to train, improve, enhance, or otherwise develop any AI models and services same,
  similar or competing with the Services provided by us".
- "sell, resell or commercially use the Content, except as expressly permitted by us or our licensors" —
  note this clause is about **Content** (site material), not the user's Output; the Rodin clause above
  governs Output.
- "reverse engineer any aspect of our Services".
- Content moderation: "we may access, review, screen, edit, modify, block and delete the Output and/or
  Prompts through our Services at any time and for any reason".

## Payments

> "ALL PAYMENTS ARE FINAL AND NON-REFUNDABLE, EXCEPT AS REQUIRED BY APPLICABLE LAW. PLEASE BE ADVISED
> THAT AI SERVICES ARE NON-REFUNDABLE."

Credits: gift/subscription credits "expire at the end of the subscription period"; directly purchased
credits "retain their validity until they are used".

## What this establishes

- **The vendor grants no restriction on Rodin output use**, and does not claim ownership of it — the
  strongest output-rights statement in the corpus, and materially broader than the marketing page's
  "paid plans include commercial licensing" framing.
- It is a permission with no warranty: the vendor disclaims copyrightability of the output, so a user
  relying on exclusivity has no vendor-backed assurance.
- Training a competing 3D model on Rodin output or on the service is prohibited by the terms.
- Weights are not offered, and nothing in the terms grants any model rights.

## Gaps

- No version number or effective date, so this capture cannot be diffed against a future revision.
- No statement about API-specific terms, rate limits, or SLA.
- No output indemnity.
- The terms do not address generated-3D asset licensing for downstream redistribution (e.g. selling a
  model as a stock asset) beyond the general "we will not limit your use".

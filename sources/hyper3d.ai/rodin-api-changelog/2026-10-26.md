## What this establishes

- **Documentation chronology, not release chronology.** The changelog records when a page was
  published or a parameter documented. It dates Gen-2's API docs to September 2025 and Gen-2.5's to May
  2026, which brackets the two generations but is not a launch announcement.
- Gen-2.5 is the **actively developed** generation as of capture: every parameter addition from March
  2026 onward lands on Gen-2.5 (or on Gen-2 only for `hd_texture`), and Gen-2.5 received five new
  geometry parameters in August 2026.
- Gen-2.5 changed behaviour twice after documentation: the `geometry_instruct_mode` default moved from
  `faithful` to `creative` in July 2026, and `creative` was restricted to Medium and above in June
  2026. Requests that pin neither parameter are therefore not stable across those dates.
- The August 2026 note that add-on pricing was "updated across" all three generations means the credit
  costs in any earlier snapshot of the docs are stale; this page's sibling records are post-update.

## Gaps

- No entry announces Gen-2.5's existence, deprecates Gen-2 or Gen-1.5, or gives a date for either.
- Nothing about model-version/checkpoint changes behind the same tier names.
- The changelog does not record the date of the July 2026 docs migration in a form that maps old URLs
  to new ones.

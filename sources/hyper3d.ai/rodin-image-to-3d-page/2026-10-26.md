# Hyper3D: Image to 3D product page (vendor marketing claims)

- Origin: Hyper3D (Deemos), "Image to 3D",
  <https://hyper3d.ai/features/image-to-3d>.
- Evidence kind: official vendor product page (marketing copy, not documentation).
- Publication date: undated live page.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26.
- Relevant models: Rodin (Gen-2.5 named explicitly).
- Capture type: page text transcribed; quoted text verbatim. Everything below is a vendor claim
  unless stated otherwise.

## Claims about Gen-2.5 specifically

> "Most models generate in seconds. With Rodin Gen-2.5, base geometry returns in roughly four seconds
> and a full, textured model in about five — larger or multi-view jobs take a little longer."

> "Rodin combines high-quality geometry and PBR textures from a single image with selectable target
> polygon counts up to 10 million and optional LOD generation."

> "Gen-2.5 resolves fine hard-surface features and produces production-ready meshes — not the blobby
> approximations of earlier tools."

> "Backed by SIGGRAPH Best Paper research, Rodin reconstructs faithful shape, proportion, and surface
> detail with sharp edges and clean topology."

- Exports: "GLB, FBX, OBJ, STL, USDZ, glTF, 3MF, or DXF"; also "Export GLB to place it on the web…,

- Characters: "Rodin supports characters, people, portraits, and figurines from one reference image…

- Batch: "Higher-tier plans support batch and higher-volume generation".

## Contradictions and unverifiable claims

1. **Polygon ceiling.** This page says "selectable target polygon counts up to 10 million"; the API
   reference caps `quality_override` at 2,000,000 (and at 200,000 for quad meshes), with a 10M-face
   mesh appearing only as an *additional* output of `Gen-2.5-Extreme-High`. The two can be reconciled
   (10M is produced, not requested), but the marketing sentence reads as a request-side limit.
2. **Export formats.** This page lists glTF, 3MF and DXF; the generation API accepts only `glb`, `usdz`,
   `fbx`, `obj`, `stl`. The extras are plausibly the workspace's separate converter tools.
3. **Speed.** "roughly four seconds" for base geometry and "about five" for a full textured model are
   unqualified by tier; the vendor's own homepage shows ~4 s only at the *lowest* effort level and
   ~80 s at the highest. Read the four-second claim as Extreme-Low, best case.
4. **"Production-ready" / "clean topology"** are unmeasured adjectives; no topology metric is published.
5. **Watertight/manifold** is asserted for all output with no exception for open or thin surfaces.

## Gaps

- No resolution or aspect-ratio guidance for input images, despite calling framing "optional capture
  choices".
- No statement of what "optional LOD generation" does or where it is available (the workspace help
  article says LODs are a downstream step, not a Rodin output).
- No dated claim, changelog, or version for any statement on the page.

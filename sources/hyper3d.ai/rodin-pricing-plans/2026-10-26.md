# Hyper3D: pricing and plans page

- Origin: Hyper3D (Deemos), "Pricing Plans for AI 3D Generation",
  <https://hyper3d.ai/pricing>.
- Evidence kind: official vendor pricing page (marketing surface; live prices).
- Publication date: undated live page.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26.
- Relevant models: Rodin Gen-2.5 (the workspace default model), plus ChatAvatar and OmniCraft tools.
- Capture type: page text transcribed; quoted strings verbatim.

- **Direct credit price, verbatim:** "Direct credits cost $1.5 per credit."
- Subscription credits are cheaper but are not quoted per credit; the page only gives the model-count
  estimates above ("Under standard settings, the plan is estimated to support approximately 60 model
  generations" per the help-centre restatement).
- **API access is Business-tier and above.** The comparison table shows `API access` as "—" for Free
  and Creator and "Full access" for Business; `API RPM` is "120 or 240 RPM".

## Licensing language on the page (verbatim)

- "Unlimited export and any use" (Creator row).
- "Can I use generated assets commercially? Paid plans include broader export and usage rights. Business
  also includes ChatAvatar commercial license benefits. Review the current terms before using assets in
  regulated or high-risk contexts."
- Free tier: "Export from legacy models" only — i.e. the newest model exports are a paid feature by the
  page's own comparison table.

## What this establishes

- The credit rate is **$1.50 per credit for direct purchases**; with the API's 0.5-credit base charge for
  a Gen-2.5 generation, the list-price floor for one API generation is about **$0.75**, before
  tier/texture add-ons. This is arithmetic on two published numbers, not a quoted price.
- The vendor sells subscription "models", not credits, to consumers: ~60 models for $30 and ~416 for
  $120, i.e. roughly $0.29–$0.50 of list value per model depending on plan and settings. The page does
  not say which tier those estimates assume, so they cannot be converted into a per-credit rate.
- Commercial use is plan-gated in the marketing copy even though the Terms of Service place no
  restriction on Rodin output (see the terms record). The two are not necessarily contradictory — the
  ToS governs output rights, the pricing table governs which features a plan unlocks — but the copy
  invites the stronger reading and should not be relied on.

## Gaps

- No per-tier, per-texture-mode credit table on the pricing page; the API docs carry those charges
  separately.
- `HighPack` is not priced anywhere on this page.
- The page does not state the credit-to-model conversion used for "About 60 models".
- Prices are live and undated; no historical capture exists to show when they last changed.

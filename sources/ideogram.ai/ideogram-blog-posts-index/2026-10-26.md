## Interpretation for dating 4.5

- The index gives a **ceiling** only: 4.5 existed by 2026-10-14, the date of the first 4.5-titled
  dated post.
- Ideogram publishes no dated 4.5 launch announcement in any captured first-party material.
- Sibling release dates for the model page: Ideogram 4.0 launched 2026-06-03 and was "our first
  open-weight model"; Ideogram 3.0 launched 2025-03-26.

## Captured content

### Resolution and size (verbatim)

> On Ideogram 4.0 and 4.5, the **Resolution** choice appears after you select a fixed aspect ratio.
> While the ratio is **Auto**, the model decides the output shape and that separate menu is
> hidden. On Ideogram 4.5 with reference images, **Size** replaces both controls.

> When you attach reference images to Ideogram 4.5, a **Size** menu replaces Aspect ratio and
> Resolution:
> - **Auto**: "Picks a size from your images and prompt."
> - **Source image size**: "Keeps the first image's own width and height."
> - **Custom**: enter **Width** and **Height**. Each side must be a multiple of 32 and at least
>   256 px. The total area can't exceed 4,194,304 pixels (2048 × 2048), and the aspect ratio can't
>   exceed 6:1 or 1:6.

> On Ideogram 4.0, the widest ratios (12:5, 22:9, 23:9, 8:3 and their mirrors) come in 2K only.
> Ideogram 4.5 offers the same ratios, but 3:1 and 1:3 also come in 2K only.

Aspect-ratio row for 4.5: "Auto, or a ratio from the Square, Landscape and Portrait groups, then
Resolution 1K or 2K".

### Quality (verbatim)

> | Ideogram 4.5 | Very low, Low, Medium, High. Very low appears only when you attach reference
> images | High |

(Column meaning: model | quality options | default.) Also: "On a free plan, Ideogram models start
at the cheapest option: Turbo, or Low on Ideogram 4.5."

### Magic Prompt (verbatim)

> Magic Prompt appears on Ideogram 4.5, 4.0, 3.0 and 3.0 Transparent. On Ideogram 4.5 it hides
> when you attach reference images, because the prompt then works as an edit instruction.

### Number of images

"Choose **Auto** or 1 to 8 images per run." Default for Ideogram models: 4.

### Background

"On **Ideogram 4.5** and **GPT Image 2.5**, open **Advanced settings** to find **Background**"
(Auto, Transparent, Opaque).

## Positioning claims, labelled

- "Inception's diffusion LLMs (dLLMs) deliver frontier LLM quality at 5x greater speed."
- "Our models run at 1000+ tokens per second on commercial NVIDIA GPUs."
- "Comparable quality to cost-optimized frontier models like GPT-6 Luna (medium) and Claude Haiku

None of these is quantified, and "5x greater speed" is not defined against a named baseline.

## Reading

- **Mercury 3 supersedes Mercury 2.5.** The footnote gives a hard sunset: new users cut off from
  2026-10-19, existing customers until **2026-11-15**, enterprise by contract. That makes Mercury
  3 the only route for new work and makes Mercury 2.5 a legacy endpoint with a dated end.
- The vendor's own speed figure for Mercury 3 (**1,068 t/s**) is *lower* than the 1,107 t/s it
  claimed for Mercury 2.5 (`../mercury-2-5-launch/2026-10-26.md`), so "as fast" here means "no
  regression despite ~40x scale", not "faster than the predecessor".
- The 170ms TTFT claim belongs to **Mercury Voice**, a different model — not to Mercury 3. No
  TTFT figure is published for Mercury 3 anywhere first-party.
- Prices here match the docs table exactly, including the 50% launch discount
  (`../mercury-models-pricing/2026-10-26.md`).

## Gaps

- No date for when the 50% discount ends.
- No parameter count, licence, or benchmark table.
- The footnote does not say what happens to Mercury 2/2.5 pricing after 2026-11-15.

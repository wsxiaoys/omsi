## Mercury 3 description, verbatim

> Our most intelligent model yet, with a 1M-token context window, up to 131,072 output tokens, and
> `instant`, `low`, `medium`, and `high` reasoning effort. Temperature is adjustable from 0.5 to
> 1.0.

> Mercury 3 is **50% off at launch**: $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.35 per million output
> tokens (regularly $0.20 and $0.70).

## Mercury 2.5 description, verbatim

> Our previous-generation reasoning model for fast chat, agents, and structured workflows.

Mercury 2 is described as "An earlier-generation reasoning model for chat completions". Both
remain in this table with prices and no retirement notice **on this page**; the retirement
statement appears only on the marketing site
(`../mercury-model-family-page/2026-10-26.md`).

## Reading

- **Mercury 3 is a sibling with its own row**, not an alias: different context (1M vs 260K),
  different output ceiling (131,072 vs 65,536), a fourth effort level (`instant`), and a lower
  output price than Mercury 2.5 ($0.70 vs $0.75 regular).
- **The 50% launch discount applies to Mercury 3, Mercury Voice and Mercury Decide**, not to
  Mercury 2.5 or Mercury 2, whose rows show a single price. A page banner reads "50% off
  Mercury 3" / "50% off Mercury Decide" and links to the platform.
- Model IDs in the request examples are bare slugs: `"model": "mercury-3"`, `"mercury-2.5"`,
  `"mercury-2"`, `"mercury-edit-2"` against `https://api.inceptionlabs.ai/v1/...`.
- Modalities: text in, text out for every chat model. Only Mercury Decide accepts images.

## Gaps

- No effective-date for the launch discount, so the price cannot be pinned to a future date.
- "1M" is not written as 1,048,576 or 1,000,000 anywhere on the page.
- No knowledge cutoff, parameter count or licence statement for any model.
- No statement here that Mercury 2/2.5 are closed to new users.

## Verbatim table (limits per minute)

| | Free Tier | Pay As You Go Tier | Enterprise Tier |
| - | - | - | - |
| **API Requests** | 1,000 | 3,000 | Custom |
| **Input Tokens** | 1,000,000 | 3,000,000 | Custom |
| **Output Tokens** | 100,000 | 300,000 | Custom |

> Please contact support if you need increased rate limits.

## Reading

- The per-minute **input** allowance (1M free / 3M paid) is one full Mercury 3 context window per
  minute on the free tier and three on the paid tier, so a single max-context request spends a
  third of a paid minute's budget. A 1M-token context is therefore not practically usable at high
  request rates on the self-serve tiers.
- Output is capped at 300k tokens/min paid, i.e. roughly two max-output (131,072) responses per
  minute.

## Gaps

- No per-request size cap, no concurrency limit, and no statement of whether cached input counts
  toward the input-token allowance.

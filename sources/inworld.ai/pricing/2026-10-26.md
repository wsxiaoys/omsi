## Captured content

### Only TTS-2 is priced

The page's TTS rows are **Realtime TTS-2** and **Realtime TTS-2 Flash**. TTS-1.5 and TTS-1 appear
nowhere on the page — no plan carries a rate for a deprecated model, so the page cannot be used to
price `inworld-tts-1.5-max` on the vendor's own API.

| Plan | Price | Realtime TTS-2 | Realtime TTS-2 Flash |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| On-Demand | $0 pay as you go | $25 /1M chars | $15 /1M chars |
| Creator | $25 /mo | $20 /1M chars | $10 /1M chars |
| Builder | $100 /mo | $17.50 /1M chars | $9 /1M chars |
| Developer | $300 /mo | $15 /1M chars | $8 /1M chars |
| Growth | $1,500 /mo | $12.50 /1M chars | $7 /1M chars |
| Enterprise | Custom | "As low as $5" | "Sub-$5" |

"Overage usage is charged at the same rate as your plan." Credits are dollar-denominated and equal
the plan price each cycle; unused subscription credits roll over for up to 3 months.

### Features that bear on cloning and limits

- Custom voices: 100 / 500 / 3,000 / 10,000 / 30,000 / custom by tier.
- Professional voice clones: 2 / 4 / 10 / 25 / 40 / 100+ by tier; "On-Demand accounts need a payment
  method on file before creating a professional voice clone."
- "Character limits in TTS Playground": 2,000 on On-Demand, 40,000 on paid tiers.
- Concurrent requests: 5 / 10 / 50 / 150 / 500 / custom.
- **"Commercial license"** is listed as a plan feature on every tier including On-Demand, alongside
  "GDPR & SOC 2 Type II", "Audio downloads", "Instant voice cloning" and "Voice design".
- Enterprise adds "ZDR, HIPAA & BAA (add-ons)", "SLA & DPA", "EU & India data residency".
- The cost estimator states "Audio uses Realtime TTS-2 Flash rates at about 1,000 characters per
  minute; TTS-2 costs more."

### Free allowance

> On-Demand includes up to 70 minutes of TTS, so you can try every product before you pick a plan.

## Summary

Pricing is per 1M input characters and tier-discounted, with a published list of $25/1M for TTS-2 and
$15/1M for TTS-2 Flash on the pay-as-you-go tier. The commercial-licence checkbox is a plan feature,
not a separate agreement. Nothing on the page prices a deprecated model, which is why host rates are
the only live prices for TTS-1.5.

## Gaps

- No TTS-1.5 rate anywhere; whether the vendor still bills `inworld-tts-1.5-max` at its launch price
  of $25/M or at the current TTS-2 rate is not stated.
- "Commercial license" is not defined on this page; the terms live in the Terms of Service.
- No statement of what happens to a deprecated model's billing if it is auto-routed.

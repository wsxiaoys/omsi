Launch framing:

> We're releasing Inworld TTS-1.5, the fastest, highest-quality realtime voice AI models available.
> With time-to-first-audio P90 latency of <250ms for 1.5 Max and <130ms for 1.5 Mini (4x faster

> standard for developers building voice-enabled applications at scale.

Two variants and their positioning:

> Inworld TTS-1.5 Max is recommended for most applications, while TTS-1.5 Mini is optimized for
> hyper-latency sensitive applications.

Quality claims, with no methodology, test set, or measurement date attached:

> TTS-1.5 improves on the Inworld models already #1 on leaderboards with 30% greater expressiveness,
> 40% reduction in word error rates, and enhanced multilingual support. It is also 75% cheaper than
> alternatives.

> Our models rank #1 on the Artificial Analysis TTS Leaderboard. What makes this ranking
> particularly meaningful is that it reflects blind comparisons by thousands of real users
> evaluating which outputs sound more natural and human.

Latency, restated in the body:

> Our new TTS-1.5 models achieve time-to-first-audio P90 latency under 250ms for our Max model and
> under 130ms for our Mini model. This is a 4x improvement from prior generations. The Max model now
> delivers quality previously only achievable at much higher latencies, running nearly as fast as the
> Mini model while producing richer, more expressive speech.

Languages:

> Language support now spans 15 languages, with the addition of Hindi and expanded coverage across
> major world languages, serving global enterprises with diverse requirements for data residency,
> compliance, and customization.

Pricing (list, at launch):

> At $15/million characters ($0.015/min) for 1.5 Mini and $25/million characters ($0.025/min) for
> 1.5 Max, we're keeping our commitment to radically accessible pricing that doesn't force
> developers to choose between quality and budget.

Distribution — no host is named in the list, and fal and Replicate are not among them:

> TTS 1.5 is also available now via Layercode, LiveKit, NLX, Pipecat, Stream Vision Agents,
> Ultravox, Vapi, and Voximplant.

Deployment: "Cloud API: Immediate access via our standard API with global availability", plus
enterprise "volume pricing, SLAs, and tailored deployment architectures".

## Summary

This is the first-party launch record for the TTS-1.5 generation and the only first-party document
captured that states TTS-1.5 list pricing. It establishes: two variants (Max, Mini), 2026-01-21
release, 15 languages, sub-250 ms / sub-130 ms P90 time-to-first-audio claims, $25/M and $15/M
characters, and a partner list that excludes fal and Replicate.

## Gaps

- No character or duration limit is stated for either variant.
- No output format, sample rate, watermarking, or voice-cloning detail appears in the post.
- The "#1 on Artificial Analysis" claim is undated: the post does not say when the ranking was
  observed, and no leaderboard capture from that period exists in this knowledge base.
- The "30% more expressive", "40% WER reduction" and "75% cheaper" figures have no stated baseline,
  test set, or evaluator.
- The latency figures are vendor-run; the footnote convention used elsewhere in Inworld's docs
  ("P90 time to first audio byte, measured server-side — excludes network latency") is not stated
  in this post.

# Inworld AI blog: "Introducing Realtime TTS" (TTS-1 and TTS-1-Max)

- Origin: Inworld AI, launch post, <https://inworld.ai/blog/introducing-inworld-tts>
- Evidence kind: official announcement (vendor marketing plus vendor-run pricing and safety claims)
- Publication date: 2025-08-15 (page metadata `datePublished` 2025-08-15T00:00:00.000Z; byline
  "08.15.2025")
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Realtime TTS 1 (`inworld-tts-1`), Realtime TTS 1-Max (`inworld-tts-1-max`);
  context for the later TTS-1.5 and TTS-2 generations

## Captured content (verbatim quotes for key claims)

Launch and the two-model structure:

> Today, we are launching Realtime TTS - a new generation of text-to-speech models that deliver
> cutting-edge quality and latency for the most accessible price on the market. Our flagship model
> Realtime TTS 1 offers realistic, context-aware speech synthesis and precise zero-shot voice
> cloning, outperforming comparable solutions from leading labs.

> We are also releasing Realtime TTS 1-Max, a larger, more expressive model, as experimental.

Languages at launch — 11, against 15 for TTS-1.5:

> This power is at your fingertips in 11 languages with Realtime TTS 1 and Realtime TTS 1-Max

Expressive control at launch, described as a research preview:

> We're also releasing a research preview of audio markups, such as [happy] or [whispering], which
> give users a new level of control over how the model speaks, not just what it says.

Latency:

> With the first 2-second audio chunk ready in as few as 200ms [3], Realtime TTS 1 is built for
> real-time applications.

Price and free cloning:

> We've optimized our entire stack to offer Realtime TTS 1 at a disruptive price of $15 per 1
> million characters. On top of that, we've made our powerful zero-shot voice cloning free for all
> users.

**Watermarking and consent — the only first-party statement on watermarking captured anywhere in
this knowledge base:**

> All synthesized audio from our platform contains an imperceptible watermark to ensure it can be
> identified as AI-generated.

> We have implemented robust safeguards to prevent the cloning of voices without explicit consent.

> We actively prohibit and will act against any uses that violate our policies, such as malicious
> impersonation or fraudulent activity.

Distribution: "already available through popular AI voice platforms like LiveKit and Vapi", with
development "accelerated by partners like Modular and Lightning AI".

## Summary

The TTS-1 launch record. It fixes the predecessor generation's date (2025-08-15), its 11-language
coverage, its $15/M-character list price, and — importantly for the TTS-1.5 page — the vendor's
watermarking commitment, which is stated here and not restated in any current documentation page.

## Gaps

- The watermark statement is not scoped to a model generation, does not name the detector, and
  states no opt-out. Nothing captured says whether the watermark is present in audio produced by
  TTS-1.5, TTS-2, or by third-party hosts.
- No character limits, output formats, sample rates, or cloning-sample requirements are stated.
- TTS-1-Max is called "experimental" here with no statement of what that implies for support.
- No independent measurement or third-party corroboration is referenced.

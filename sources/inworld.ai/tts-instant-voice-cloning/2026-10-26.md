# Inworld docs: Instant voice cloning

- Origin: Inworld AI documentation, "Instant voice cloning",
  <https://docs.inworld.ai/tts/instant-voice-cloning>
- Evidence kind: official documentation
- Publication date: none stated; contains a dated policy note (voices cloned on or after 2026-08-15);
  captured 2026-10-26
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: TTS API generally; available on every current model, and was introduced with the
  TTS-1 generation

## Captured content

> Inworld's Instant Voice Cloning creates a personalized voice clone in seconds, from as little as 3
> seconds of audio. The longer the sample (up to the 30-second limit), the better the speaker
> similarity. Also known as zero-shot cloning, it's available to all users through Portal or via API.

> The 30-second prompt limit applies to voices cloned on or after August 15, 2026. Voices cloned
> before that date continue to use their original 15-second prompt — re-clone a voice with a longer
> sample to take advantage of the higher limit.

Upload rules: 1 audio file, `wav` / `mp3` / `webm`, "Maximum file size is 12MB", samples longer than
30 seconds "will be automatically trimmed to 30 seconds". The Portal flow requires the operator to
"Confirm you have the rights to clone the voice" before continuing. Optional "Remove background
noise".

Quality caveat:

> Instant voice cloning may not perform well for less common voices, such as children's voices or
> unique accents. For those use cases, we recommend Professional Voice Cloning.

Professional Voice Cloning is described as **Beta**, "uses more audio and an asynchronous training
step", and per the models page is available on `inworld-tts-2` only — not on `inworld-tts-2-flash`.

## Summary

Cloning is a vendor-account feature: a 3–30 s sample produces an instant clone, and a paid plan
bounds how many clones can be stored. The consent requirement is a Portal checkbox — "Confirm you
have the rights to clone the voice" — with no verification step documented here.

## Gaps

- No published voice-cloning policy page: who may be cloned, what evidence of consent is required,
  what happens to a disputed clone, or how long clone data is retained.
- Nothing states whether TTS-1.5 can use clones created after the August 2026 prompt-length change,
  or whether Professional clones are usable on TTS-1.5 (the models page scopes PVC to TTS-2).
- No statement on whether clones are portable across hosts; both host records say they are not.

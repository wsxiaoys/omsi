# Inworld docs: TTS release notes

- Origin: Inworld AI documentation, "TTS release notes", <https://docs.inworld.ai/release-notes/tts>
  (read via the full-docs dump at <https://docs.inworld.ai/llms-full.txt>)
- Evidence kind: official documentation (dated release notes)
- Publication date: entries dated 2025-08-22 through 2026-09-11; capture date 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `inworld-tts-1.5-max`, `inworld-tts-1.5-mini`, `inworld-tts-2`,
  `inworld-tts-2-flash`, and the TTS-1 generation

## Captured content

### Entry dated January 21, 2026 — TTS-1.5 launch (verbatim)

> Launched [Inworld TTS 1.5](https://inworld.ai/blog/introducing-inworld-tts-1-5), our newest
> generation of realtime TTS models featuring:
> * **Two New Models:** Our flagship model `inworld-tts-1.5-max` is ideal for most use cases, with
>   the best balance of quality and speed. For use cases where latency is the top priority, we also
>   offer `inworld-tts-1.5-mini`.
> * **Latency Improvements:** Our new TTS-1.5 models achieve P90 latency for first audio chunk
>   delivery under 250ms for our Max model and under 130ms for our Mini model, a 4x improvement
>   compared to TTS-1.
> * **More Expressive and More Stable:** TTS-1.5 is 30% more expressive than prior generations and
>   demonstrates a 40% reduction in word error rates.
> * **Additional Languages:** We've added support for additional languages, including Hindi, Arabic,
>   and Hebrew, bringing total languages supported to 15.

This independently corroborates the blog's launch date and model IDs.

### Entry dated May 5, 2026 — TTS-2 launch (successor generation)

> Launched [Realtime TTS-2](https://inworld.ai/blog/realtime-tts-2) (`inworld-tts-2`), our most
> powerful and expressive TTS model
>
> * **Natural Language Steering:** Direct any voice with bracketed instructions like
>   `[say excitedly]` …
> * **Stronger Multilingual Support:** Production-quality synthesis across 15 languages, plus
>   experimental support for 90+ additional languages.

### Entry dated August 6, 2026 — steering behaviour change, with a TTS-1.5 carve-out

> **Behavior change.** An inline `[tag]` previously affected only the text immediately after it and
> delivery could revert on its own partway through longer text. A tag now stays in force until you
> change it. … Requests that use no inline tags are unaffected, as are `inworld-tts-1.5-max` and
> `inworld-tts-1.5-mini`.

This is the most recent first-party text that treats TTS-1.5 as a live, separately behaving model —
three months after TTS-2 shipped — and it says TTS-1.5 was *not* given the persistent-steering
change.

### Entry dated August 9, 2026 — TTS-2 Flash launch

`inworld-tts-2-flash` released with 20 ms P90 TTFB, "5× faster than `inworld-tts-2` at 100 ms",
same language coverage, instant voice cloning and timestamps; steering and professional voice
cloning are `inworld-tts-2`-only.

### Entry dated September 11, 2026 — OpenAI-compatible endpoint

`POST /v1/audio/speech` served in OpenAI's format at `https://api.inworld.ai/v1`; formats `mp3`,
`opus`, `flac`, `wav`, `pcm`, plus SSE.

### Entry dated August 22, 2025 — TTS-1 quality update

"Released an upgraded version of the Inworld TTS models with higher overall quality", adding inline
IPA custom pronunciation.

## Summary

The release notes pin the TTS-1.5 launch to 2026-01-21 and confirm the two model IDs. They also show
that TTS-1.5 was left behind by later TTS-2 work rather than folded into it: the August 2026
steering change explicitly excludes it.

## Gaps

- No entry announces the TTS-1 discontinuation date (2026-06-15) or the TTS-1.5 deprecation; both
  appear only in the models page notice.
- No TTS-1.5-specific character limits, formats, or price changes are recorded in the notes.
- The notes contain no post-launch quality or latency update for TTS-1.5.

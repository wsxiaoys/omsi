# Kimi K3.1 Pro tech blog — launch, benchmarks, availability

- **Origin:** "Kimi K3.1 Pro Tech Blog: Advancing Open Intelligence", Moonshot AI / Kimi,
  <https://www.kimi.ai/blog/kimi-k3-1-pro>. Linked from the research index
  <https://www.kimi.ai/blog/>, which dates the entry **2026-10-24**. Retrieved 2026-10-26.
- **Evidence kind:** official documentation (first-party launch post and benchmark report).
- **Publication date:** 2026-10-24 (date printed on the blog index entry; the post itself
  carries no separate byline date).
- **Relevant models:** Kimi K3.1 Pro, Kimi K3.1 Pro Highspeed, Kimi K3 (comparison only).
- **Capture type:** rendered page text. The benchmark *tables and charts* on the post are
  images/canvas and were **not** extractable; every number below comes from the post's prose
  or its footnotes, not from the chart series.

## Identity and availability, verbatim

> "We're introducing Kimi K3.1 Pro, the first model in our new K3.1 series and our most
> capable 3T-class open-weight model to date, with a 1-million-token context window."

> "We are also releasing Kimi K3.1 Pro Highspeed, which delivers 2-3× the speed of K3.1 Pro
> for more responsive interactions."

> "Kimi K3.1 Pro is a post-trained version of Kimi K3, with substantially larger
> post-training compute."

> "Kimi K3.1 Pro and Kimi K3.1 Pro Highspeed are available today on Kimi.ai, Kimi Work, Kimi
> Code, and the Kimi API. The full model weights will be released by Nov 4."

> "Full model weights will be released by Nov 4, 2026."

> "Kimi Enterprise administrators can enable K3.1 Pro for their organization; access is off
> by default at launch."

> "Zero Data Retention is available for API customers."

So at capture (2026-10-26) the model is **served but not yet downloadable**: the open-weight
claim is a forward commitment with a two-week-ish window, not a published artefact. The
3T-class figure is a class label, not a parameter count; no exact parameter count appears on
this page.

## Benchmark claims, verbatim

Footnotes state the conditions:

> "Unless otherwise specified, all Kimi K3.1 Pro results reported below are obtained with the
> reasoning effort set to 'max', temperature = 1.0, and top-p = 0.95."

> "Terminal-Bench 4.0. Kimi K3.1 Pro is evaluated with the Claude-Code harness."

Headline prose:

> "On Terminal-Bench 4.0, which evaluates coding agents on realistic, resource-constrained
> terminal tasks using strict task-specific tests, K3.1 Pro scores 65.7%, ahead of GPT-6 Astra
> (58.2%) and narrowly behind Claude Opus 5.5 (66.4%)."

> "on Kimi Code Bench 3.0 Long-horizon, our internal benchmark that tests coding agents'
> multi-step execution, tool use, and end-to-end problem solving across diverse engineering
> domains, K3.1 Pro scores 61.2%, above K3's 55.95%."

> "On BenchCAD, which tests 3D reconstruction from multi-view renderings through CAD code
> generation, K3.1 Pro scores 92.7% with tools, approaching the performance of Claude Opus 5.5
> (96.2%) and GPT-6 Astra (95.9%)."

> "K3.1 Pro achieves state-of-the-art results on OfficeQA Pro v2 (64.4), which tests complex
> fiscal data analysis over real financial documents…"

> "On Kimi Hallu Bench Hard bench, an internal benchmark designed to stress-test factual
> accuracy, its claim-level hallucination rate fell from 23.4% for K3 to 8.3%, while the share
> of responses containing at least one factual error was just 42.4% of its predecessor's
> rate."

Internal finance deltas, as stated: Financial Modeling "+7.2 points" over K3; Financial
Research "+11.6 points"; Business Operation "about 4.2× K3's performance".

Cybersecurity section, on the open-weight decision:

> "the checkpoint without these risk-reduction measures … solves 90.5% of problems at pass@3;
> the final K3.1 Pro reaches 81.0%."

> "K3.1 Pro's cyber capability remains behind that of frontier proprietary models that have
> been publicly available for at least three months."

Competitor figures are quoted from the competitors' own publications (the footnotes name
each source: OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra page, Anthropic's Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1 system cards,
DeepSeek's release blog, z.ai's GLM-5.3 post) or from official leaderboards
(tbench.ai, terminal-bench-science.ai, swe-marathon.org, posttrainbench.com,
vals.ai/benchmarks/programbench). They were not re-run by Moonshot except where stated
(Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 for DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017, PostTrainBench, BenchCAD open-model
subsets, Our Agents' Last Exam).

## Summary

The launch post is the strongest first-party record for this model: it fixes the date
(2026-10-24), the identity ("post-trained version of Kimi K3", new K3.1 series), the
highspeed sibling, the 1M context, the 3T-class label, the benchmark rows above, the pricing
pointer, and the weights timeline (by 2026-11-04). It is also where the model's own
limitations are stated: cyber capability deliberately reduced relative to the training
pipeline's potential, and explicitly behind frontier proprietary models.

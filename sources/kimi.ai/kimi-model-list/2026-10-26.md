  <https://platform.kimi.ai/docs/models>. Retrieved 2026-10-26.
- **Evidence kind:** official documentation (first-party model catalogue and lifecycle).
- **Publication date:** none stated; live catalogue page. Capture date 2026-10-26.
- **Relevant models:** `kimi-k3.1-pro`, `kimi-k3.1-pro-highspeed`, `kimi-k3`, `kimi-k2.7-code`,
  `kimi-k2.6`; deprecated IDs listed below.
- **Capture type:** full text capture of the Markdown rendition (4 KB).

## Lifecycle statements, verbatim

> "starting October 31, 2026, K2 series models will no longer be available to newly registered
> users; existing users are not affected. We plan to retire all K2 series models by the end of
> December 2026"

Deprecated IDs on the page: `kimi-k2.5` (discontinued 2026-08-31), the `moonshot-v1` series
including `-vision-preview` variants and `moonshot-v1-auto` (2026-08-31), the `kimi-k2` series
including `kimi-k2-0905-preview`, `kimi-k2-0711-preview`, `kimi-k2-turbo-preview`,
`kimi-k2-thinking`, `kimi-k2-thinking-turbo` (2026-05-25), `kimi-latest` (2026-01-28),
`kimi-thinking-preview` (2025-11-11).

Every deprecation notice points readers to `kimi-k3.1-pro` as the replacement, which makes it
the current flagship in the vendor's own words.

## Summary

The catalogue fixes the canonical API IDs (`kimi-k3.1-pro`, `kimi-k3.1-pro-highspeed`) and the
sibling set, and establishes that K3.1 Pro is the successor to `kimi-k3` rather than a
co-existing tier: K3 remains available but is described as "previous-generation". It also
shows the retirement wave that empties everything older than K3 out of the platform by
end-2026.

## Gaps

- No parameter count, licence, context-per-model table, or release date per model.
- No statement of whether `kimi-k3` will be retired on a schedule; only the K2 series has a
  stated end date.
- Does not say whether `-highspeed` is a different checkpoint or the same weights served
  faster; only that it has "same intelligence".

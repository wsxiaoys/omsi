# Kling 4.0 vs 3.0 comparison (kling.ai blog)

- Origin: Kling AI (Kuaishou), "Kling 4.0 vs 3.0: What's Different in the New Model?",
  <https://kling.ai/blog/kling-4-vs-3>
- Evidence kind: official product post (vendor), comparative specification table
- Publication date: 2026-09-30 (byline "Kling AI · Sep 30, 2026")
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Kling 3.0 series (Kling VIDEO 3.0 / 3.0 Omni), Kling 4.0, Kling 4.0 Flash
- Capture type: full text captured; the comparison table is transcribed verbatim

## Why this record matters

This is the only captured first-party document that states Kling 3.0's specifications as a list and
compares them with Kling 4.0. It therefore does double duty: it is the best first-party spec sheet
for 3.0, and it is the vendor's own statement that 4.0 is a distinct successor.

## Verbatim: the comparison table

Intro line: "Kling 4.0 expands on Kling 3.0 with up to 30-second native generation, 10 keyframes,
richer Omni Reference, stronger Dynamic Motions, and more flexible video editing."

| Capability | Kling 3.0 Series | Kling 4.0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text-to-Video | Supported | Supported |
| Image-to-Video | Supported | Supported |
| Start & End Frames | Supported | Supported |
| Multiple Keyframes | Not supported | Up to 10 keyframes |
| Video Duration | 3–15 sec | 3–30 sec |
| Resolution | 720p / 1080p / 4K | 720p/1080p/4K |
| Dynamic Range | 8-bit SDR | 10-bit HDR at 1080p/4K |
| Audio Output | Single-channel | Two-channel stereo |
| Main Languages | Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish | Adds Portuguese, German, French, Hindi, and more |
| Reference Images/Subjects | Up to 7 without video; up to 4 with video | Up to 10 images and 7 subjects |
| Reference Videos | Up to 1 video, max. 15 sec | Up to 5 videos, 30 sec combined |
| Combined Reference Assets | Up to 7 images/subjects, or 1 video + 4 images/subjects | Up to 15 assets |
| Video Editing | Available through 3.0 Omni workflows | Integrated into the Kling 4.0 multimodal workflow |

Body text that qualifies the table:

- "Kling 3.0 supports flexible generation from 3 to 15 seconds."
- "Reference-based creation was already an important part of the Kling 3.0 series, particularly in
  Kling VIDEO 3.0 Omni. Kling 3.0 can work with images, elements, video references, and voice
  controls, but its reference limits are smaller. Without a video input, images and subjects share a
  limit of up to seven. With video, Kling 3.0 supports one video plus up to four images or subjects."
- "Kling 3.0 supports Start & End Frames, giving creators control over how a video begins and
  finishes."
- "Kling 3.0 already introduced stronger Multi-Shot creation and camera control."
- "Kling 3.0 marked a major step forward for AI video creation, bringing Multi-Shot storytelling,
  Native Audio, stronger subject consistency, multilingual dialogue, and up to 15 seconds of video
  generation into one model family."

## Summary

Kling 3.0, per the vendor, is a 3–15 second, 720p/1080p/4K, 8-bit SDR, single-channel-audio video
model with start/end frames, multi-shot, and reference limits of 7 images/subjects (or 1 video + 4
images/subjects). Kling 4.0 changes the duration ceiling, keyframe control, dynamic range, audio
channels, language list, reference budget, and editing integration — which is the vendor's own
evidence that the two are separate models and not one model under two labels.

The post dates 4.0's launch to October 2026 and its Flash tier's early access to 2026-09-28. It gives
no launch date for 3.0.

## Gaps

- No date for Kling 3.0's own launch, and no changelog reference for it.
- No frame rate for either generation.
- "8-bit SDR" and "Single-channel" are stated for "Kling 3.0 Series" as a whole; the page does not
  say whether the 4K tier or the Turbo tier share those output properties.
- Marketing language throughout ("major step forward", "stronger"), with no benchmark, no
  measurement, and no methodology.

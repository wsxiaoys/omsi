## Contradiction recorded inside this single document

The spec table lists "Resolution 720p / 1080p / 4K" and "10-bit HDR (1080p、4K)" as current capabilities,
while the highlight section says "10-bit HDR output at 4K and 1080p is coming soon". The same split
appears on the vendor's feature page and in its user guide, and the API reference exposes only 720p and
1080p. Read 4K/10-bit HDR as announced-but-not-shipped.

## Summary

First-party launch note for Kling 4.0, published on the launch date 2026-10-15. Establishes the identity
("Kling 4.0"), the model family (4.0 plus a cheaper, faster 4.0 Flash at 720p / 3–20 s), the output
envelope (3–30 s, 720p/1080p shipped, 4K announced, 21:9 supported, two-channel stereo audio), the input
envelope (text ≤8,000 tokens, ≤10 images, ≤5 videos / ≤30 s total, ≤7 elements, voice references only, 15
assets combined), up to 10 keyframes, targeted video editing, and 2-minute extension as coming soon.

## Gaps

- No pricing, no rate limits, no frame rate, no per-region availability in this document.
- No quality benchmarks or comparisons; every capability statement is a vendor claim.
- Consumer credit cost per generation is not stated (the web app is login-gated).

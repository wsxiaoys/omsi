## Verbatim: input limits and parameters

### Omni Reference entry point

| Input type | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Text | ≤ 8,000 tokens |
| Images | Up to 10 images in total; ≤ 30 MB per image; aspect ratio 2:5–5:2 |
| Videos | Up to 5 videos; 3–30 seconds per video; ≤ 30 seconds total; ≤ 300 MB per video; aspect ratio 2:5–5:2 |
| Voice Profiles | Up to 3 per generation task; 5–30 seconds of audio to create a voice profile; ≤ 50 MB per file |
| Elements | Up to 7 elements in total, including up to 3 video elements; source footage for a video element must be 3–8 seconds long |
| Combined | Up to 15 reference assets in total across images, videos, voice profiles, and elements |

| Parameter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Generation Mode | 720p / 1080p / 4K |
| Duration | 3–30 seconds, adjustable in 1-second increments; default: 10 seconds |
| Aspect Ratio | 21:9 / 16:9 / 1:1 / 9:16 / Auto; default: Auto |

> Reference videos, videos for editing, and video elements share the same video quota: up to 5 clips and
> a maximum combined duration of 30 seconds.

### Keyframes entry point

| Input type | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Text | ≤ 8,000 tokens |
| Images | Up to 10 images in total; ≤ 30 MB each; aspect ratio 2:5–5:2 |

| Parameter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Generation Mode | 720p / 1080p / 4K |
| Duration | 3–30 sec, in 1-sec increments; default: 10 sec |
| Aspect Ratio | Without uploaded images: 21:9 / 16:9 / 1:1 / 9:16. After uploading images, the aspect ratio follows the first keyframe. |

> The Keyframes entry does not support uploading reference assets.

Keyframe controls are documented as first frame, last frame, start-and-last frames, and middle frames at
any position in the timeline.

## Verbatim: entry points and FAQ

> Kling 4.0 offers two primary entry points in the creation workspace: Omni Reference and Keyframes.
> […] The capabilities of the two entry points **cannot** be combined within a single generation task.

> **Q: How do I choose between Kling 4.0 and Kling 4.0 Flash?**
> Kling 4.0 Flash is ideal for rapid creative iteration and short-form video creation. It supports videos
> up to 20 seconds at a maximum resolution of 720p, but does not currently support start-and-last frames
> or multiple keyframes.

> **Q: What is the total asset upload limit?**
> In Omni Reference mode, you can upload up to 15 assets in total across images, videos, voice profiles,
> and elements. The limits are 10 images, 5 videos, 7 elements, and 3 voice profiles.

> **Q: Can I upload background music or sound effects?**
> No. Audio input currently supports voice profile references only, allowing you to specify a speaker's
> voice. Background music and sound effects are generated by the model based on the visuals.

> **Q: Do I need to manually enable sound effects and storyboarding?**
> No. Audio-visual synchronization and Smart Storyboard are enabled by default and have no separate
> toggles.

> **Q: What is the prompt length limit?**
> 8,000 tokens.

Text rendering in video is documented for Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese,
German, French, Hindi, "as well as emoji and logo generation", including burned-in subtitles.

## Summary

The vendor's operational reference for Kling 4.0. Confirms: two mutually exclusive entry points (Omni
Reference with mixed assets; Keyframes with images only); 3–30 s in 1-second steps, default 10 s; 720p /
1080p / 4K listed with 4K+HDR marked coming soon; aspect ratios 21:9/16:9/1:1/9:16/Auto; 15 combined
reference assets (10 images / 5 videos ≤30 s / 7 elements / 3 voice profiles); no audio uploads other
than voice profiles; audio and storyboarding on by default with no toggle; 8,000-token prompts.

## Gaps

- No frame rate, no file size or codec for the returned video, no queue or latency figures.
- No pricing (the guide says features "are subject to the actual product interface").
- No moderation or content-policy detail. The only related statement is a prompting note: "Only two
  types of negative instructions are truly necessary: no subtitles and no background music. The model may
  add these by default", i.e. subtitles and music are opt-out, not opt-in.
- No region list.

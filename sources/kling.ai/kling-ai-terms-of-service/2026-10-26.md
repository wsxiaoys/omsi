# Kling AI terms of service (kling.ai)

- Origin: Kling AI, Terms of Service, <https://kling.ai/docs/user-policy>
- Evidence kind: provider policy (vendor contract terms)
- Publication date: none on the page; the page is the currently served agreement
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: all Kling AI services, including Kling 3.0
- Capture type: full text captured; clauses below are quoted verbatim

## Contracting entity

> These Terms of Service … constitute a legally binding contract between you and Kling AI Pte. Ltd.
> and its affiliates (collectively, "Kling AI," "Company", "us", "we", or "our").

## Ownership of output

> 4.4 You own all intellectual property rights and other proprietary interests in and to the Content
> under applicable law, or such rights belong to the rightful rights holder as determined under
> applicable law. We do not claim ownership of the Content.

## Commercial use of output — restricted unless permitted

> 4.6 … without our written permission, you may not use, reproduce, distribute, and create derivative
> works of, and make modifications to, the Output for any commercial purposes.

This sits awkwardly against the consumer price guide, which lists commercial use of generated content
as a paid-plan benefit (see the credit-cost-guide record). The two documents do not reconcile
themselves; the most likely reading is that the plan table grants the permission the ToS requires,
but no captured page says so explicitly.

## Branding obligation

> 4.5 … when you use, distribute or disseminate the AI function to generate content, you shall label
> the Output interface with the brand and logo involved (including but not limited to "Kling AI",
> Kling AI 1.0/1.5","Kling", …). In cases that the Output generated by Kling are not labelled with
> any brand mark in the process of use due to objective reasons, you shall prominently indicate that
> the Output is generated by "Kling AI".

## Licence granted to Kling AI

> 4.7.1 … you agree that Kling AI has an non-exclusive, royalty-free right and license during the
> operational period of its business … to use the Content, which shall comprise the Input you provide
> or upload to the Services and Output generated during your use of our Services, including such
> right to host, store, transfer, publicly display, publicly perform … reproduce, modify … create
> derivative works from, and to distribute Input and Output.

## Other restrictions relevant to model use

- 2(w): you may not use the Services or Output "for purposes of, directly or indirectly, creating,
  testing, improving, training, or otherwise developing your or any third party's artificial
  intelligence or machine learning models, systems, architecture, weights or related technology."
- 2(d)/(e): no marketing, renting or leasing of the Services for a fee, and no commercial
  solicitation, "without our written consent".
- 4.8: content published through Kling AI "may … be made available to other users for use in creating
  and publishing related content, or for downloading", including "Recreate".
- 3.2(ii)/(iii): you are responsible for evaluating output accuracy, and must not use output about an
  individual for decisions with legal or material impact.

## Summary

Output ownership rests with the user, but commercial exploitation is conditioned on permission, an
attribution obligation applies, and the vendor takes a broad royalty-free licence over inputs and
outputs. Training other models on the output is prohibited. Nothing here grants rights to the model
itself, and no weights are offered.

## Gaps

- No effective date, version number, or change history for the agreement.
- No clause specific to API use, API redistribution, or hosting Kling models inside another product.
- No statement of how the plan-level "commercial use" benefit interacts with clause 4.6.
- The China-facing `klingai.com` terms were not captured, so the two jurisdictions cannot be compared.

## Verbatim: package incompatibility

> Please note that the following resource packs are not compatible with Kling 4.0 or Kling 4.0 Flash. To
> get early access to Kling 4.0 or Kling 4.0 Flash, click the Business Inquiries card on the far right.

The same sentence appears twice, once above the video packages and once above the image packages. The
packages themselves are prepaid unit blocks: e.g. Standard Package 1 = $700 for 5,000 video units
("$0.14 per unit"), 180-day validity, 20 concurrency; discounted tiers at $0.126/unit.

## Summary

The only first-party price list that mentions Kling 4.0. It is explicitly a preview: 4.0 is "Coming Soon",
its API "not yet available", and the prepaid packages that cover 2.6/3.0 "are not compatible with Kling
4.0". Preview unit rates are 1.0/1.5 units per second at 720p/1080p without video input and 0.6/0.9 with
video input, against a $0.14 list price per unit. 4.0 Flash pricing is "TBA".

## Gaps

- No 4K rate for 4.0 (dash in the table) even though the product documents list 4K as a mode.
- No consumer (web-app) credit price per generation — that page is login-gated.
- No date for when these preview prices become real, beyond the 2026-10-28 API availability statement in
  the API reference.
- Package concurrency and validity are documented; rate limits per model are not.

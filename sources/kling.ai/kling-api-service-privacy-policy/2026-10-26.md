## Privacy policy — verbatim

> Any of our Services, you may provide us with certain Data including any metadata accompanying user
> generated content, audiovisual clips segmentation, annotation, frame or text extraction, object and
> image recognition symbolling, aggregated classification tagging and other non-personally identifying
> information inferred from the Data you provide to us or that we collect about you in connection with
> your use across Kling AI. … We do not analyse any facial features in pictures or identify the identity
> of individuals from such pictures, and will not collect, share or store any face data, the above
> information could not be used to identify any individual.

> We promise that your and your end-users' personal information will not be used for any other purposes
> beyond those stated in this privacy policy (including, for example, model training).

### The contradiction this creates

The same API documents a **Face Recognition** endpoint that returns, per face, a `face_id` and a
`face_image` URL (a face crop), plus on-screen start/end times, and requires that session to run lip
sync at all. A service that does not "analyse any facial features" cannot identify which face to animate.
The policy sentence is scoped to "special video effects" and to information "inferred from the Data",
but the Face Recognition output is a direct face crop, not an inference. No captured page reconciles the
two. Treat "Kling AI does not collect or store face data" as unverified and contradicted by the
endpoint's own response schema.

## Summary

Two things matter for a lip-sync page.

**Consent is a contractual obligation on the caller, not a gate in the product.** There is no
authorization step, consent form, or identity-verification requirement anywhere in the lip-sync or
face-recognition API documentation. The obligation sits in §3.7: you must hold explicit authorization
from the rights holder of any third party's data you upload. Nothing verifies it.

**Commercial use is unrestricted on the API track**, in contrast to the consumer terms, where clause 4.6
conditions commercial use on written permission (see the existing `kling-ai-terms-of-service` record).
The two documents describe different products — API paid service versus the consumer app — and should not
be quoted interchangeably.

## Gaps

- Neither page carries an effective date, so the policy text cannot be pinned to a date.
- No lip-sync-specific or likeness-specific clause: nothing about deepfakes, political use, or
  public-figure likeness beyond the general ToS impersonation ban.
- No retention period for uploaded source video or for the face crops returned by Face Recognition.

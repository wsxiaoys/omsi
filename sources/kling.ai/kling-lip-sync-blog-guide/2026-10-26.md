## Verbatim: the two methods

> Kling AI offers two ways to create a lip sync video. Use the dedicated **Lip Sync** tool to synchronize
> speech or singing with an existing supported character video. Or generate an entirely new scene with
> synchronized dialogue through **Kling 4.0**.
>
> The best workflow depends on whether you already have the character footage or want to create the
> entire speaking performance from scratch.

> **Method 1: Add Lip Sync to an Existing Character Video**
> **Step 1: Open Lip Sync and Add a Character Video** — Open the Kling AI workspace and select **Lip
> Sync** from the available tools. Add a supported character video with a clearly visible face to prepare
> it for lip synchronization.
> **Step 2: Add Audio or Enter a Script** — Click **+ Speech** to access **Voice** and **Upload Local
> Dubbing**. You can upload your own voiceover or singing audio, or enter your dialogue and choose an AI
> voice. For AI-generated speech, you can also adjust the speech rate and emotion to better match your
> character's performance.
> **Step 3: Generate and Review Your Lip Sync Video** — Click **Generate** …

> **Method 2: Generate Lip-Synced Dialogue with Kling 4.0** — For a new talking scene, select Kling 4.0 in
> the Video Generation workspace and describe your character, setting, and exact dialogue. … Kling 4.0
> creates the dialogue, synchronized lip movements, and visuals together with Native Audio, without
> requiring a separate lip-syncing step.

## Verbatim: practical advice

> **Keep the mouth visible.** Begin with a front-facing or gently angled face. Hands, masks, and a
> microphone across the lips make the result harder to judge.

> **Use Clean Audio and Voice References.** For Lip Sync, upload clear speech or singing audio with
> minimal background noise.

> **Write for the available time.** Use short, conversational sentences and deliberate pauses.

> Can Kling Generate Lip-Synced Dialogue in Different Languages? — "Yes. Kling 4.0 supports dialogue
> generation in Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Hindi, and
> additional supported languages and accents. … **For existing videos, available language and voice
> options depend on the dedicated Lip Sync workflow.**"

## Summary

The most recent first-party statement about the product, dated 2026-10-21 — six days before capture. It
keeps Lip Sync positioned as a distinct tool that consumes an existing clip, now contrasted with Kling
4.0's Native Audio rather than 3.0's, and adds one control the API reference does not mention: TTS
"emotion" alongside speech rate.

It also confirms the tool is still actively promoted in October 2026, which is the evidence that Lip
Sync is current rather than retired, even though its API predates the roster window.

## Verbatim: product description

> AI Lip Sync Video Generator — Sync speech, songs, or generated voices to character videos with
> natural-looking mouth movements.

> Choose a supported Kling AI clip with a full, clearly visible face. Lip Sync works with realistic, 3D,
> and 2D human characters.

> Upload a voiceover or singing track, or use Text to Speech to generate a voice. Trim the audio if it
> runs longer than the video.

> Generate the lip sync and preview the result. If the timing feels off, trim the audio or adjust the
> Text to Speech speed.

Bottom bar: "Voiceover | Text to Speech | Audio trimming | Realistic, 3D & 2D character".

## Verbatim: the two workflows, as the vendor frames them

> **Add New Audio to an Existing Video** — Use the dedicated Lip Sync tool when your character video is
> already created. Upload a voiceover or song, or generate speech from text, then match the new audio to
> the character's mouth movements.

> **Generate Dialogue with the Scene** — Starting from scratch? Kling VIDEO 3.0 and VIDEO 3.0 Omni create
> dialogue, lip movement, visuals, sound effects, and ambience together. Native Audio supports Chinese,
> English, Japanese, Korean, and Spanish.

> **Keep Character and Voice Consistent** — Create a reusable Character Element from images or a video,
> bind a voice tone, and carry the same appearance and voice into new scenes with VIDEO 3.0 Omni.

## Verbatim: FAQ (JSON-LD `acceptedAnswer.text`)

- **What kinds of characters can I lip sync?** — "Kling AI Lip Sync works with realistic, 3D, and 2D
  human characters. Choose a supported video where the character's full face is clearly visible. Animal
  characters are not currently supported."
- **Which languages does Kling AI support?** — "Language availability depends on the workflow. The Lip
  Sync tool accepts uploaded audio and offers available Text to Speech voices. Native Audio in VIDEO 3.0
  and VIDEO 3.0 Omni supports Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, and Spanish."
- **Is Kling AI Lip Sync free?** — "Kling AI Lip Sync is a paid feature. Credit use depends on the length
  of the video, and the required amount is shown before generation."
- **What is the difference between Lip Sync and Native Audio?** — "Lip Sync adds new speech or singing to
  an existing supported character video. Native Audio in VIDEO 3.0 and VIDEO 3.0 Omni generates the
  scene, dialogue, facial performance, and supporting sound together as part of a new video."
- **Can I lip sync a photo?** — "The dedicated Lip Sync tool is designed for character videos. To make a
  still image speak, use Kling AI Avatar, which combines a character image, audio, and optional
  performance directions to create a talking-avatar video."
- **Can I use my own voice?** — "Yes. Upload a recording of your dialogue, narration, or singing and use
  it as the audio track for your character video."
- **Can Kling AI make a character sing?** — "Yes. Upload a singing track and apply it to a supported
  human-style character video. Kling AI matches the character's mouth movements to the vocal
  performance."
- **Can I create a lip sync video from audio online?** — "Yes. Open the Kling AI Lip Sync tool, choose a
  supported character video, then upload your audio or generate speech from text. Kling AI processes the
  lip synchronization online."

## Summary

This is the vendor's own answer to "separate model or a Kling Video mode?". Lip Sync is presented as a
**dedicated tool that consumes an existing video**, explicitly contrasted with two other things it is
not: Native Audio (dialogue generated together with the scene inside Video 3.0/3.0 Omni) and Avatar
(which makes a still image speak). The same page sells all three side by side.

The marketing envelope: human characters only (real/3D/2D), full face visible, animals excluded; audio
either uploaded or TTS-generated; audio trimming built in.

## Gaps

- No duration, resolution, frame rate, output format, or latency figure anywhere on the page.
- "Choose a supported Kling AI clip" implies the source must be Kling-generated, while the API accepts
  any 720p/1080p mp4/mov URL. The consumer tool and the API are described with different scopes.
- The language answer deliberately does not state which languages the lip-sync step itself handles; it
  redirects to TTS voice availability and to Native Audio. Nothing here says lip sync is language-bound.
- The page promotes 3.0/3.0 Omni rather than 4.0, so it is not evidence of the current flagship.

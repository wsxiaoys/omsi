## Summary

Consumer access is a credit system inside a subscription. The free tier explicitly excludes
commercial use of generated content; paid tiers include it. 1080p generation is listed as a paid-plan
benefit, not a free-tier feature. The post implies roughly 20 credits per 720p video (660 credits /
33 videos) but never states a per-second credit rate, so credits cannot be converted into dollars per
second from this record alone.

## Gaps

- No per-second credit rate for 3.0, 3.0 Omni, audio, multi-shot, or 4K — the headline promises to
  explain "4K, Omni Audio, and Multi Shot Pricing" but the captured body gives no such figures.
- No date for when these plan prices took effect.
- No statement of whether API usage consumes credits or is billed separately.

## Summary

The vendor's own positioning of Kling 3.0 is **native audio + multi-shot + 15-second output +
element/character consistency**, with multilingual dialogue and lip-sync. The page names 2.6 as the
predecessor and lists what changed, which is the vendor's own statement that 3.0 is a distinct
generation rather than a repackaged 2.6.

The page states no dates, no frame rate, no resolution list, no pricing, and no audio channel
count. Every capability statement here is marketing copy with no measurement attached; it is used on
the model page only as evidence of what the vendor claims, not of what the model does.

## Gaps

- No publication or launch date anywhere on the page.
- No resolution, frame-rate, aspect-ratio, or bit-depth specification.
- No statement of which tier (standard/pro/turbo/Omni) the claims apply to; the page describes
  "Video 3.0" as one model.
- The multilingual claim ("different languages and accents") is not qualified by a language list;
  the vendor blog gives one (see the 3.0-vs-3.0-Omni record), and fal's schema gives a narrower one.

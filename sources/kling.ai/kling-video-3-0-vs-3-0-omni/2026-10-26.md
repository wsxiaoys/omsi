## Summary

The vendor splits the 3.0 series into a prompt-led model (VIDEO 3.0) and a reference-driven model
(VIDEO 3.0 Omni). Both share 15-second output, native audio and multi-shot; they differ in reference
machinery and in element voice control. The five "main languages" listed here (Chinese, English,
Japanese, Korean, Spanish) plus "dialects and accents" is broader than what fal's schema documents
for the same model (see the fal record) — a contradiction the model page preserves.

The page calls the two "two closely related paths" of one series, which is the basis for treating
Kling 3.0 as one entity with per-tier caveats rather than two pages.

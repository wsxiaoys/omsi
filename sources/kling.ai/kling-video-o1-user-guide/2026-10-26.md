  <https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-o1-user-guide>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor user guide)
- Publication date: 2025-12-15 (byline "Kling AI · Dec 15, 2025")
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Kling VIDEO O1 (the predecessor of Kling VIDEO 3.0 Omni)
- Capture type: full rendered text captured; the prompt-structure list and the pricing block are
  transcribed

## Captured content

> Kling AI Video O1 — the world's first unified multimodal video model — is a brand-new creative
> engine for creators to unlock endless creative possibilities.

> Kling O1, based on the Multi-modal Visual Language (MVL) concept, uses natural language to combine
> videos, images, elements, and other multimodal descriptions to precisely understand your
> intentions, making the creative process more intuitive and efficient.

Task list, verbatim:

> Capabilities include Reference-based Generation, Text-to-Video, Keyframe Interpolation (Start/End
> Frame), Video Inpainting, Transformation, Stylization, and Video Extension. This integration
> eliminates the need to jump between multiple models or tools.

### Video editing, verbatim

> At the same time, Kling O1 turns tedious post-production processes into simple conversations.
> There's no need for manual masking or keyframing; simply input prompts like "remove bystanders",
> "change daytime to dusk", or "replace the main character's outfit", and the model will understand
> the visual context. From local subject replacement to entire-video restyling, the model will
> automatically complete pixel-level semantic reconstruction. Your prompts become the most efficient
> editing tool.

The guide then gives the editing prompt structures, which are the closest thing to an editing
specification the vendor publishes. Transcribed:

| Task | Prompt structure (verbatim) |
| --- | --- |
| Add content | "Add [describe content to add] from [@Image] to [@Video]"; "Add [describe content to add] to [@Video]"; "Add [@Element] to [@Video]" |
| Remove content | "Remove [describe content to remove] from [@Video]" |
| Change angle/composition | "Generate [another angle/composition, e.g, close-up, wide shot] in [@Video]" |
| Modify subject | "Change [describe specified subject] in [@Video] to [describe target subject]" (+ "from [@Image]" / "to [@Element]") |
| Modify background | "Change the background in [@Video] with [describe specified background]" / "with [@Image]" |
| Localized edit | "Change [describe specified content] in [@Video] to [describe target content]" |
| Restyle | "Change [@Video] to [Style words: American cartoon, Japanese anime, wool felt, cyberpunk, pixel art, ink wash painting, oil painting, etc] style"; "Change [@Video] to the style of [@Image1]" |
| Recolor | "Change the [item] in the [@Video] to [color]" |
| Weather/environment | "Change [@Video] to [describe weather, like "a rainy day"]" |
| Green screen | "Change the background in [@Video] to a green screen, and keep [describe content to keep]" |

On combining tasks:

> The Kling O1 model is not limited to single tasks; it supports a combination of different tasks in
> one prompt, such as "adding a subject while modifying the background in the video", or "changing the
> style while using elements".

### Video reference (distinct from editing)

> You can upload a 3-10s video as a reference to generate the previous/next shot within the same
> context. Or with text, images, or elements, create a completely new scene referencing actions or
> camera movements in the video.

### Frames

> (Generation with only an end frame is not supported for now.)

### Duration

> Kling O1 supports generations anywhere between 3-10s

### Input limits (FAQ, verbatim)

> **Images**: You can upload up to 7 images, with minimum resolution 300px, max file size 10MB, in
> jpg, jpeg or png format.

> **Videos**: You can upload one video with 3s-10s duration, max file size 200MB, and max resolution
> 2K.

> **Note**: When a video is present, you can upload up to 4 images/elements combined. Without a video,
> you can upload up to 7 images/elements.

### Pricing (credits)

> VIDEO O1 currently supports two modes: 1080p and 720p. […] Whether a reference video is provided
> will affect the final cost.

| Mode | Without video input | With video input |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1080p | 8 Credits/s (5s = 40, 10s = 80) | 12 Credits/s (5s = 60, 10s = 120) |
| 720p | 6 Credits/s (5s = 30, 10s = 60) | 9 Credits/s (5s = 45, 10s = 90) |

## Summary

The O1 guide is the editing specification for the Omni line, and the only first-party document in the
corpus that describes *how* Kling edits video: a text instruction plus `@Video` / `@Image` / `@Element`
references, covering add, remove, replace, restyle, recolor, relocalise, weather, green screen, and
combination of those in one prompt. The vendor's 3.0 Omni guide says these features "function the same
as in O1", so this record carries the editing behaviour for the 3.0 generation by explicit vendor
cross-reference.

O1 is 3–10 s; the successor raises that to 15 s. Video input costs 50% more than no video input at the
same resolution (1080p: 8 → 12 credits/s), which is the same shape as the 3.0 Omni credit table.

## Gaps

- No frame rate, bit depth, channel count, output resolution mapping, or container beyond mp4 in the
  examples.
- The guide does not say whether an edit preserves the source clip's length, resolution and frame
  rate, which is the central operational question for an edit endpoint.
- No statement on whether audio is retained, regenerated or dropped during an edit; the 3.0 Omni
  table implies native audio cannot be generated alongside a video input at all.
- No API model ID or endpoint path; the guide describes the product UI.
- No failure modes, refusal behaviour, or limits on how much of a frame can change.

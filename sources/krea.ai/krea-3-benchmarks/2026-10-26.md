# Krea: "Krea 3 Crashes the Top 4: Every Benchmark, Explained"

- Origin: Krea, "Krea 3 Crashes the Top 4: Every Benchmark, Explained",
  https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-3-benchmarks
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor blog post summarising third-party leaderboards)
- Publication date: 2026-10-20 (byline "The Krea Team · October 20, 2026")
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Krea 3 (Ultra and Fast modes)

## Why this record exists

This is Krea's own write-up of three blind-vote leaderboards that listed Krea 3 on 2026-10-19/20.
It is **first-party interpretation of third-party measurements**: the ranks are quoted from Arena,
Artificial Analysis, and Design Arena, but the document is Krea's. The Artificial Analysis overall
board was also captured directly on 2026-10-26 (see
[AA record](../../artificialanalysis.ai/krea-3/2026-10-26.md)); the Arena and Design Arena pages
could not be captured directly (arena.ai returned HTTP 403 to this capture), so their figures rest
on Krea's transcription and on the third-party posts Krea quotes.

Krea's reading of the AA board: Ultra is "statistically tied for #3 with Nano Banana 2.1 and
MAI-Image-2.6" because the three rank ranges and intervals overlap. Arena's own rule is quoted as
treating overlapping spreads as a tie, so the #4 is "tied on Arena's rule with Nano Banana 2.1".
Design Arena ranks Krea as the "#2 image-editing lab after OpenAI".

### Artificial Analysis methodology, as described by Krea

- Blind pairwise votes on edits of the same input image and the same instruction.
- Bradley-Terry fit reported as Elo; the scale anchors FLUX.2 [dev] at 1000.
- Prompts sampled across 10 use cases and 7 editing actions, refreshed monthly.
- Krea says AA tested both Krea modes through the Krea API at 2K resolution and records Krea 3 as
  released 2026-10-15.

## Gaps and cautions

- **Vendor-mediated third-party data.** The rank tables are Krea's transcription of Arena, AA, and
  Design Arena. Only AA was independently captured for this page, and its live board had already
  moved (Ultra displayed #4, not #5) by 2026-10-26.
- **No raw vote export, prompts, or per-vote data.** The post gives counts and intervals but not the
  underlying records.
- **Arena and Design Arena pages were not directly captured.** arena.ai returned HTTP 403
  ("Just a moment...") on 2026-10-26; the Design Arena image-editing board path was not found.
- Krea notes its own launch-card discrepancy: a card in its launch thread showed Ultra at #4 on AA,
  while the leaderboard and AA's chart put MAI-Image-2.6 at #4 and Ultra at #5 at that time.
- The post is dated 2026-10-20 and does not describe later board movement, the 2026-10-26 AA read,
  or any independent latency measurement.
- The category figures are AA-specific. They do not establish the same ordering on Arena or Design
  Arena, and Krea says the Elo scales are not comparable across boards.

# Krea: "Introducing Krea 3" (launch announcement)

- Origin: Krea, "Introducing Krea 3", https://www.krea.ai/blog/introducing-krea-3
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor launch announcement, written by Krea)
- Publication date: 2026-10-15 (byline "The Krea Team · October 15, 2026")
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Krea 3 (including the "Berry"/"Jam" variants named only by hosts)

## Captured content

### Release, price, and speed (verbatim)

> Today we're releasing Krea 3, our new image editing model, and we built it for the other side of
> that trade. Most edits come back in under 8 seconds, end to end, at native 2K. It costs $0.06 per
> image at 2K.

> Krea 3 also leaves alone what you didn't ask it to touch. Change the setting around a product and
> the label stays sharp. Give it a face and an outfit, and the person in the next image is still the
> same person. It takes up to 6 reference images per edit.

> Krea 3 is live today on krea.ai for our 30 million users, and through fal, Hedra, Together AI,
> Nous Research, OpenRouter and Replicate.

The announcement calls Krea 3 "our new image editing model" and is signed by Krea's team, which is
the clearest first-party statement that Krea 3 is Krea's own model rather than a third-party model
resold in Krea's app. Only the fal and Replicate routes were verified for this page; the other four
partner names are unverified leads.

### Vendor comparison table (verbatim values)

| | Krea 3 | Nano Banana Pro | GPT Image 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Time per edit | Under 8 s | 33 s | 90 s |
| Price per image | $0.06 at 2K | $0.135 | $0.22 |

Footnote as printed:

> Krea 3 at 2K · Nano Banana Pro and GPT Image 2: median time from request to finished image for
> edit jobs on Krea, Sep 24 to Oct 1, 2026, queue included · list prices per image, without image
> references

This is a vendor-run measurement on Krea's own platform, not a controlled cross-provider benchmark:
the competitor times include Krea's queue and were observed on Krea's serving stack. The post also
gives a volume table at 100,000 edits per month: $6,000 for Krea 3, $13,500 for Nano Banana Pro,
$22,000 for GPT Image 2, again "at list price per image · Krea 3 at 2K, without image references".

### Style and consistency claims (verbatim)

> It also has Krea 2's eye for style, so it works on paintings, vector art and ink drawings and
> keeps them in their own medium.

> For a consistent look across a series, you can also add style references.

The style-references sentence conflicts with Krea's API docs, which say Krea 3 "does not yet support
the Krea 2 API's style-reference or LoRA controls" and return "Style references are coming soon to
the API". See the [API overview record](../krea-3-api-overview/2026-10-26.md) and the
[model page record](../krea-3-model-page/2026-10-26.md). The style-quality sentence is a vendor
claim; no independent measurement is offered in the post.

### Named use cases (summary)

The post walks through product preservation (label and logo stay sharp when the scene changes),
precision edits (swap seating but keep the rug and the reader), material/light following a
line drawing and a six-colour palette, and character consistency across references. These are
illustrated examples with Krea-generated images, not a test set.

A Pixelcut testimonial is quoted ("We've been testing K3 since its early checkpoints..."), which is
a design-tool partner's endorsement, not an independent evaluation.

### What Krea says is not there yet (verbatim)

> Krea 3 isn't real time yet. Our fastest setup finishes an edit in about 4 to 5 seconds, and we
> want to get that lower.

This conflicts with fal listing a "Krea 3 Realtime" endpoint on 2026-10-14; see the
[fal record](../../fal.ai/krea-3/2026-10-26.md).

### FAQ answers (verbatim)

> Krea 3 costs $0.06 per image at 2K and $0.03 per image at 1K.

> Krea 3 works from up to 6 reference images per edit.

> Krea 3 generates natively at 2K.

## Gaps

- **No mention of `thinking_mode` anywhere in the post.** The launch announcement does not describe
  the fast/high/ultra modes at all; the only first-party documentation of them is the developer docs
  captured separately.
- No architecture, parameter count, training data, or model card. Krea 2 had a technical report;
  no equivalent for Krea 3 was found.
- No benchmark numbers, vote counts, or confidence intervals. The comparison table is platform
  latency medians and list prices.
- The "6 reference images" figure conflicts with the API docs and both host pages, which say 8.
- The post says style references can be added; the docs say they are not yet supported.
- Publication date is the byline date; the page itself carries no separate updated date.

The page lists four capability blocks: prompt-to-image, reference support ("Upload reference images
to guide composition, subject, and style"), fast iteration, and "Production-Ready Outputs" for
"social, marketing, concept, and product storytelling workflows with commercial-ready quality". It
also lists "Balanced Quality and Speed", "Reliable Prompt Control", and "Reference-Aware Creative
Direction". These are marketing descriptions with no numbers, examples, or test protocol.

on the Business and Enterprise feature lists. The Free tier advertises "Krea 3" access with 100

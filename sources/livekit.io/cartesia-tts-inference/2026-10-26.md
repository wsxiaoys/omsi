## Captured content

LiveKit offers Cartesia TTS two ways: **LiveKit Inference** (no Cartesia API key, billed through
LiveKit Cloud, runs "on LiveKit's infrastructure") and the **Cartesia plugin** (caller's own Cartesia
key and billing).

### Voices

> LiveKit Inference supports all of the default "Cartesia Voices" available in the Cartesia API.

> Pre-existing custom Cartesia voices are not available through LiveKit Inference. To use these, create
> your own Cartesia account and use the Cartesia plugin.

LiveKit offers its own cloning path instead, described on the TTS overview page: "You can create and
use custom voices with LiveKit Inference. Upload or record a sample, and LiveKit clones it to all
supported TTS providers on your plan." Sample Cartesia voices on the page include Blake, Daniela,
Jacqueline and Robyn, described by locale.

### Parameters passed through

`emotion` (string), `speed` (preset or float), `volume` (float), `duration` (target seconds),
`max_buffer_delay_ms`, `add_timestamps`, `add_phoneme_timestamps`, `use_normalized_timestamps`.
The plugin's defaults: `model = "sonic-3"`, voice `f786b574-daa5-4673-aa0c-cbe3e8534c02`, `language =
"en"`; "for `sonic-3`, the valid range [for speed] is 0.6 to 1.5" and volume 0.5–2.0, matching
Cartesia's own `generation_config` bounds.

### Expressive mode (host claim)

> Cartesia's Sonic models work with expressive mode, where the LLM marks up its replies and LiveKit
> renders the delivery. Cartesia can set an emotional tone (for example excited, sad, angry, or calm),
> adjust pacing and volume, and insert pauses. **Cartesia doesn't produce non-verbal sounds like laughs
> or sighs.**

That last sentence contradicts Cartesia's own docs, which document inserting `[laughter]` in the
transcript (see `sources/cartesia.ai/sonic-marketing-page/2026-10-26.md` and the Volume/Speed/Emotion
guide). It is a host statement about LiveKit's markup layer, but it is written as a statement about the
model.

## Summary

LiveKit is a real second route to Sonic 3.6 with its own model IDs (`cartesia/…`), its own billing, an
EU endpoint option, and a 44-language row that independently matches Cartesia's list. It also confirms
the dated snapshot ID. The limitation is concrete: voices created in a Cartesia account cannot be used
through Inference — LiveKit's own cloning flow or the Cartesia plugin are the alternatives.

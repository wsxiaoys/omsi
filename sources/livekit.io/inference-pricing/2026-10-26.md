## Summary

LiveKit prices **all** Sonic variants identically at **$0.03/min** on Build/Ship and **$0.0225/min** on
Scale — a 25% plan discount, not a model discount. This is a per-minute host price, unlike Cartesia's
per-character credits, so it can only be compared after a speaking-rate assumption: at Cartesia's own
750–800 credits/min, $0.03/min corresponds to about $0.0375–$0.040 per 1k characters, i.e. in the same
band as Cartesia's Startup/Pro credit rates.

## Gaps

- No character or token basis is published, so long-form versus conversational workloads cannot be
  compared precisely across the two routes.
- No statement of which voice inventory or snapshot is used at the per-minute rate.
- The page does not say whether the rate changes with model quality (it does not appear to).

## Endpoint availability (verbatim table)

| Endpoint | ltx-2-5-fast | ltx-2-5-pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| text-to-video | ✓ | ✓ |
| image-to-video | ✓ | ✓ |
| audio-to-video | ✓ | ✓ |
| retake | — | — |
| extend | — | — |

> Both variants accept `camera_motion`, `generate_audio: false` for silent video, `last_frame_uri` on
> image-to-video and audio-to-video, and automatic duration.

This is the authoritative statement that LTX-2.5 has **no retake and no extend** on the hosted API,
even though the same vendor's llm-info page lists Retake and Extend as LTX-2.5 workflows
([llm-info](../ltx-llm-info/2026-10-26.md)) and the release notes date those endpoints to the LTX-2.3
era.

| Model | Resolution | FPS | Duration (seconds) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ltx-2-5-fast** | 720p | 24, 25 | 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20 |
| | 720p | 48, 50 | 6, 8, 10 |
| | 1080p | 24, 25 | 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20 |
| | 1080p | 48, 50 | 6, 8, 10 |
| | 1440p | 24, 25, 48, 50 | 6, 8, 10 |
| | 4K | 24, 25, 48, 50 | 6, 8, 10 |
| **ltx-2-5-pro** | 720p | 24, 25, 48, 50 | 6, 8, 10 |
| | 1080p | 24, 25, 48, 50 | 6, 8, 10 |
| | 1440p | 24, 25, 48, 50 | 6, 8, 10 |
| | 4K | 24, 25, 48, 50 | 6, 8, 10 |

Durations are a closed enumeration, not a free range: 6/8/10 s everywhere, plus 12/14/16/18/20 s only
on Fast at 720p/1080p with 24 or 25 fps. Pro never exceeds 10 s at any resolution.

## Automatic duration (verbatim)

> Send `"duration": null` and the model picks the length itself, from your prompt. Use it when the
> prompt describes the shot rather than a fixed slot — a one-line action stays short, a multi-shot
> sequence runs longer.

> Available on `ltx-2-5-fast` and `ltx-2-5-pro` for text-to-video and image-to-video.

> The field is still required — leaving it out returns `duration is required`.

> Automatic duration cannot be combined with `last_frame_uri` on image-to-video. A last frame fixes
> where the clip has to end, which requires a known length.

Billing consequence, verbatim:

> On a prepaid account, credits are held against the longest duration your resolution and frame rate
> allow … a request that would have produced 6 seconds is still declined if you cannot cover 20 on
> `ltx-2-5-fast`, or 10 on `ltx-2-5-pro`.

## Other copy (verbatim)

> LTX-2.5 improves every stage of generation on a stronger foundation: a Diffusion Video Decoder with
> industry-leading pixel quality, higher fidelity, native multi-shot scenes, and a better distilled
> model.

> Native multi-shot means a single generation can produce multiple connected shots, holding character,
> scene, lighting, visual style, and voice consistent across cuts.

## Summary

This is the clearest first-party statement of LTX-2.5's hosted envelope: three tasks, two variants,
four resolution tiers, five aspect ratios, 24/25/48/50 fps, and a discrete duration ladder whose
maximum depends on variant, resolution and frame rate. It also shows the Fast/Pro split is asymmetric:
only Fast reaches 20 s, and only at 720p/1080p with 24/25 fps.

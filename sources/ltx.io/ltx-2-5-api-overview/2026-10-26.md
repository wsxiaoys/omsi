## Tier specifications (verbatim)

> Two tiers, one API. Start on Fast, ship on Pro.

**Fast** — "Low-cost, high-speed generation for rapid iteration, storyboards, and previews."

> Resolutions: 1080p, 1440p, 4K
> FPS: 24 / 25 / 48 / 50
> Duration: up to 20s at 1080p, up to 10s at 1440p/4K

**Pro** — "Higher detail and motion stability for final, commercial-grade renders."

> Resolutions: 1080p, 1440p, 4K
> FPS: 24 / 25 / 48 / 50
> Duration: up to 10s · required for Audio-to-video, Retake, Extend

This identical block appears verbatim on `/model/ltx-2`, `/model/ltx-2-3` and `/model/ltx-2-5`, so on
its own it cannot distinguish model versions. It also omits 720p, which the pricing page sells and
which the FAQ below lists.

## FAQ (verbatim)

> What is the LTX-2.5 API? — The LTX-2.5 API is a high-performance text-to-video generation interface
> powered by the LTX-2.5 model. It enables developers to generate HD and 4K video, set custom frame
> rates and durations …

> What resolutions and FPS values does the LTX-2.5 API support? — Supported formats include: 720p,
> 1080p, 1440p, and 4K · 25 or 50 FPS · 6–20 second durations

> The LTX-2.5 API is billed per second of generated video … ltx-2-5-fast — $0.09 at 720p, $0.13 at
> 1080p, $0.19 at 1440p, $0.30 at 4K; ltx-2-5-pro — $0.12 at 720p, $0.17 at 1080p, $0.25 at 1440p,
> $0.39 at 4K. There are no request fees or per-asset charges.

The FAQ's "25 or 50 FPS" contradicts the tier block's "24 / 25 / 48 / 50" on the same page. The tier
block is corroborated by the vendor's pricing page (which prices 720p) and by both hosts' schemas,
which expose 24/25/48/50 on Fast endpoints; the FAQ line appears to be stale copy.

> Auto Duration — Let the model set the pace. Clip length is predicted from the described action …

> Diffusion Fidelity Rendering — Generate every scene from a grid of high-fidelity keyframes …

> LTX-2.5 is here. Sharper, faster, yours to build on. … Native multishot, precise editing, and 4K HDR
> output, built to hold up from first draft to final render.

The page's headline claim is "up to 50% less cost per second and up to 30× faster at scale" with no
comparison baseline named.

## Summary

The vendor's API surface for LTX-2.5 is text-to-video, image-to-video and audio-to-video, in Fast and
Pro variants, at 720p–4K, 24/25/48/50 fps, with durations up to 20 s at 720p/1080p on Fast and up to
10 s on Pro. Durations of 6–20 s are stated in the FAQ; the per-resolution limits in the tier block are
more specific and agree with both hosts' schemas.

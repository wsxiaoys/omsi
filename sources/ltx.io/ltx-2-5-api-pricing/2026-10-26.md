## LTX-2.5 rates (USD per second, verbatim)

| Endpoint | Variant | 1280×720 | 1920×1080 | 2560×1440 | 3840×2160 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Text-to-Video | Fast | $0.09 | $0.13 | $0.19 | $0.30 |
| Text-to-Video | Pro | $0.12 | $0.17 | $0.25 | $0.39 |
| Image-to-Video | Fast | $0.09 | $0.13 | $0.19 | $0.30 |
| Image-to-Video | Pro | $0.12 | $0.17 | $0.25 | $0.39 |
| Audio-to-Video | Fast | $0.09 | $0.13 | $0.19 | $0.30 |
| Audio-to-Video | Pro | $0.12 | $0.17 | $0.25 | $0.39 |

URL paths for every row: `/v2/<task>` (async) and `/v1/<task>` (sync). Notes on the Fast cards:
"Same pricing applies for text input and pure prompt-based generation"; on the Image-to-Video cards:
"Same compute cost as Text-to-Video Fast. Resolution and duration determine total cost." On the
Audio-to-Video cards: "Billed per second of input audio. Generates up to ~20 seconds per request.
Full-length videos can be created by chaining multiple requests."

## Endpoints the page lists for LTX-2.3 but not for LTX-2.5

- Retake (video editing): LTX-2.3 Pro only, 1920×1080 only, $0.10/sec **of input video**.
- Extend: LTX-2.3 Pro only, 1080p only, $0.10/sec, "Billed frames = extended portion + context frames
  from input video, capped at 505 total".
- Reframe (outpainting): LTX-2.3 Pro, 720p output $0.10/sec, 1080p output $0.20/sec, videos up to 60 s.
- HDR video generation (beta): LTX-2.3 Pro, `/v2/video-to-video-hdr`, $0.20/$0.40/$0.80 per second of
  input video at 1080p/1440p/4K, output as per-frame 16-bit EXR.

No LTX-2.5 card exists for Retake, Extend, Reframe, or HDR on this page.

## FAQ (verbatim)

> Video generation in the LTX API is billed per second — there are no request fees or per-asset charges.
> Your rate depends on the endpoint, the model variant, and the output resolution.

> Text-to-Video and Image-to-Video are billed on the duration of the generated video. Audio-to-Video is
> billed on the input audio. Retake, Extend, HDR Upscale, and Reframe are billed on the input video.

> Portrait and landscape are priced identically at the same resolution tier.

> With automatic duration, you are charged for the length the model actually produces. On prepaid
> accounts, credits are held against the longest duration your resolution and frame rate allow, and the
> unused remainder is released when the job finishes.

> Fast is optimized for speed and cost … Pro is optimized for visual fidelity and motion stability …
> On LTX-2.5, both variants support 720p through 4K. On LTX-2.3, Pro is also required for
> Audio-to-Video, Retake, Extend, and Reframe.

The last sentence is the clearest vendor statement that the 2.5 Fast/Pro split is a compute tier, not a
capability tier: unlike 2.3, 2.5 exposes Audio-to-Video on Fast as well.

## Summary

The vendor bills every LTX-2.5 endpoint per second of output (or of input audio for A2V), with a
Fast/Pro premium of roughly 30–33% and a resolution ladder from 720p to 4K. There are no request fees.
The editing and finishing endpoints that exist for LTX-2.3 — Retake, Extend, Reframe, SDR→HDR — have no
LTX-2.5 card on this page.

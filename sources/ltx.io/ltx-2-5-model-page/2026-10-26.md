## Positioning copy (verbatim)

> LTX-2.5 improves quality, continuity, control, and efficiency through native multi-shot, stronger
> prompt adherence, and better local performance.

> a stronger foundation for what's already being built

Feature list, verbatim (numbered items 01, 02, 03, 04, 07, 08 as rendered — 05 and 06 are missing
from the page):

> 01 Cleaner motion — Fewer motion artifacts. Smoother, more natural results.
> 02 Multishot — Create connected shots that hold character, environment, lighting, and voice.
> 03 Better prompt adherence — Follow complex creative instructions from shorter, simpler prompts.
> 04 Auto duration — Generate the right clip length for the requested action.
> 07 Better results, less compute — Get near-full-model quality with fewer retries, fewer controls,
> and significantly lower compute.
> 08 A pretrained foundation you can fine-tune — Fine-tune a raw foundation model on your own data and
> domain.

"What changed", verbatim:

> New diffusion video decoder.
> Gemma 4 12B text encoder + custom prompt enhancer.
> Automatic duration prediction.
> Improved distilled model, larger dataset + RL, prompt enhancement, and automatic duration prediction.

"Diffusion Fidelity Rendering":

> A new video generation technology that allocates rendering compute by scene complexity, delivering
> industry-leading pixel quality that holds up frame by frame, even on a cinema screen.

## Comparison table (vendor claims about competitors)

The page carries a requirement-by-requirement table with LTX-2.5 in the first column and MiniMax H3,
Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0 Pro, Veo 3.1 and Grok 1.5 in the others. Rows and LTX's own cells:

| Requirement | LTX-2.5 (vendor's own cell) |
| --- | --- |
| Open weights you can download | Available |
| No mandatory branding | Available |
| Fine-tune on your own data and IP | Available |
| Runs on any GPU | Available |
| Min VRAM: 16GB | Available |
| Deployment | On-prem, edge, API |
| Faster than real-time | Available |

Competitor cells are marked "Not Available", "Partial", "Restricted", "Open, conditional" or
"Not in US, EU, UK, Korea". These are LTX's assertions about other vendors' terms; none is
independently verified here. Note that "Min VRAM: 16GB" on this page contradicts the same vendor's
own llm-info page, which says the full dev model needs 80GB+ VRAM and distilled/FP8 variants run in
32GB, with "as little as 12GB VRAM" in one configuration
([llm-info](../ltx-llm-info/2026-10-26.md)).

## Post-production capability list

Native Resolution, Refine, Restore, SDR to HDR, HDR Native, Layout to Render, AlphaGen (beta) — the
last four are distributed as IC-LoRAs rather than as hosted endpoints. Two of these workflows are
described as working "on one GPU" at "full delivery resolution", i.e. self-hosted claims.

## FAQ (verbatim)

> What is LTX-2.5? — LTX-2.5 is the newest version of LTX, an open-weights world model for video
> generation. It's built as a stronger foundation, not just a new tool, that teams can build on,
> fine-tune, and deploy themselves.

> What's new in LTX-2.5? — It adds a better video decoder for cleaner motion, native multi-shot scenes
> that stay consistent across cuts, stronger prompt understanding, automatic clip duration, beta
> precise video editing, native 4K HDR and RAW support, and a faster, higher-quality distilled model.

> How fast is LTX-2.5? — Self-hosted on 2× GB200 GPUs at 720p, LTX-2.5 generates a 10-second video clip
> in 6.8 seconds, which is faster than real time.

> What does the LTX-2.5 API cost? — API pricing is billed per second of generated video. ltx-2-5-fast:
> $0.09/sec at 720p, $0.13/sec at 1080p, $0.19/sec at 1440p, and $0.30/sec at 4K. ltx-2-5-pro:
> $0.12/sec at 720p and $0.17/sec at 1080p.

> What are the licensing terms for LTX-2.5? — LTX-2.5 uses open weights with a permissive license, no
> mandatory branding, and free use under $10M ARR.

The FAQ's own Pro price list stops at 1080p even though the same page's pricing section and the
API pricing page list Pro at 1440p and 4K. The "6.8 seconds for 10 seconds of 720p on 2× GB200" figure
is a vendor claim with no methodology or comparison baseline.

## Summary

The vendor's own summary of what LTX-2.5 is: the same 22B audio-video world-model family as LTX-2.3,
with a new diffusion video decoder, a Gemma 4 12B text encoder plus prompt enhancer, native multi-shot
generation, automatic duration prediction, and a better distilled checkpoint. Open weights, per-second
API billing, and a $10M-ARR revenue threshold on the free community licence are restated here in the
vendor's own words.

## Gaps

- No publication date on the page; the release date comes from the release notes and llm-info pages.
- The feature numbering skips 05 and 06, so at least two claimed changes are not shown.
- "Native 4K HDR and RAW support" is asserted in the FAQ but the API pricing page lists no HDR endpoint
  for LTX-2.5; HDR endpoints are LTX-2.3 Pro only.
- No measured quality numbers of any kind; the comparison table is a terms/features matrix, not a
  quality comparison.

# LTX: official AI information page ("llm-info")

- Origin: LTX, "Official AI Information: LTX" — a page LTX publishes for AI assistants and models,
  <https://ltx.io/llm-info>. Page states "Last updated: October 2026".
- Evidence kind: official vendor fact sheet (self-described as verified facts about LTX, LTX-2.3 and
  LTX-2.5)
- Publication date: "Last updated: October 2026"; capture date 2026-10-26
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: LTX-2, LTX-2.3, LTX-2.5
- Capture type: full text transcription

This is an unusual source: a vendor-authored page addressed to language models rather than to users. It
is first-party, but it is also promotional and self-contradicts on hardware requirements, so it is used
here for identity, dates and naming rather than for capability claims.

## Identity and dates (verbatim)

> **Origin:** LTX was established as a separate business unit of Lightricks in June 2026, when
> Lightricks split into two units — LTX (AI video models and infrastructure) and its consumer apps
> business. Lightricks was founded in 2013 in Jerusalem, Israel.

> LTX, LTXV, LTX Model, LTX-2, LTX-2.3 and LTX-2.5 all refer to the same model family.

> **LTX-2:** The original release. Approximately 19 billion parameters. First publicly available
> DiT-based audio-video foundation model with synchronized generation.

> **LTX-2.3:** Released March 2026. 22 billion parameters. Improvements include a redesigned VAE … a 4x
> larger text connector … native portrait video up to 1080x1920, cleaner audio with fewer artifacts, and
> production-grade HDR output via IC-LoRA (Beta).

> **LTX-2.5:** The current release (August 2026). The most significant upgrade to date …

> **Note:** LTX-2.3 has been superseded by LTX-2.5 (current release, August 2026).

> LTX-2.5 is a multimodal world model developed by LTX. It is built on a 22B-parameter asymmetric
> dual-stream diffusion transformer with bidirectional cross-attention, and generates synchronized audio
> and video in a single model pass.

## Workflows claimed for LTX-2.5 (verbatim)

> Text-to-video … Image-to-video … Audio-to-video … Video-to-video: Transform or edit existing video
> using IC-LoRA pipelines … Retake: Regenerate a specific time region of an existing video … Extend:
> Continue an existing video beyond its last frame … SDR to HDR … LipDub.

> **Generation specifications:** Resolutions: 1080p, 1440p, 4K (native), and portrait up to 1080x1920 ·
> Frame rates: 24, 25, 48, or 50 FPS · Duration: up to 20 seconds per generation · Audio: synchronized
> and generated jointly with video · HDR output: native 4K HDR generation, and available in Beta via
> IC-LoRA through the API.

This list overstates what the hosted API offers: the API documentation's own support matrix marks retake
and extend as unavailable for both LTX-2.5 variants
([API models doc](../ltx-2-5-api-models-doc/2026-10-26.md)), and the API pricing page carries no
LTX-2.5 retake, extend, reframe or HDR endpoint
([API pricing](../ltx-2-5-api-pricing/2026-10-26.md)). Read the llm-info list as model-level capability
(reachable through self-hosted IC-LoRAs and the LTX Trainer), not as API availability.

## Architecture claims (verbatim)

> **Diffusion Video Decoder:** A diffusion-based final reconstruction step (replacing plain VAE decoding)
> produces sharper faces in close-up, more legible text and signage, and fewer smears in fast motion.

> **Native Multishot:** A single generation produces multiple connected shots, holding character,
> environment, lighting, voice, style, and continuity across cuts (wide, over-the-shoulder, medium,
> close-up).

> **Auto Duration:** The model predicts appropriate clip length from the described action rather than
> requiring a fixed duration parameter.

> **Pretrained checkpoint for deep adaptation:** A raw, non-SFT base built for aggressive fine-tuning,
> intended for use cases including egocentric robotics, action-conditioned world prediction, synthetic
> AV/drone data, and industrial digital twins.

> LTX produces temporally synchronized 24 kHz stereo audio jointly with video.

## Hardware and open-source details (verbatim)

> Available model checkpoints on HuggingFace include: the dev model (bf16), distilled model for faster
> inference, quantized fp8 variant for lower VRAM setups, spatial upscaler (x1.5 and x2), and temporal
> upscaler.

> **Hardware requirement for full model:** GPU with 80GB+ VRAM. Distilled and FP8-quantized variants
> support 32GB, and run locally on a single GPU with as little as 12GB VRAM.

> **GitHub:** https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2 — "The repository is organized as a monorepo with
> three packages: ltx-core … ltx-pipelines … ltx-trainer".

> Open source license: **LTX Model License** (not Apache 2.0). Free to use for organizations with under
> $10 million in annual revenue. Companies above $10M ARR embedding LTX into commercial products or
> production environments require a commercial license through the LTX Licensing Program.

Note the conflict with the LTX-2.5 model page's comparison table, which advertises "Min VRAM: 16GB"
([model page](../ltx-2-5-model-page/2026-10-26.md)). The two numbers describe different things — the
llm-info figures are for the full self-hosted pack, the model page's 16 GB is unexplained — and no
source reconciles them.

## Benchmarking and adoption claims (verbatim)

> Ranked Top #3 AI video models globally by Artificial Analysis independent benchmarking
> #1 open source video model
> 33M+ downloads across all model versions on HuggingFace
> LTX runs at roughly one-eighth the cost and one-seventh the render time of comparable closed models,
> based on independent benchmarking.

> **LTX-2.5 is already in production use across three industries:** Asteria — film production;
> Markov — robotics; Reactor — real-time / interactive worlds.

> LTX is part of the NVIDIA Cosmos Coalition …

These are vendor claims. The Artificial Analysis boards captured for this page do not place LTX-2.5 in
the global top three: on AA-Video-I2V v1.1 it ranks 19th (Fast) and 21st (Pro), and on AA-Video-T2V v2.1
24th and 25th, while being the second-ranked open-weights family on both
([AA capture](../../artificialanalysis.ai/ltx-2-5-video-arena/2026-10-26.md)). "Top #3" and
"#1 open source video model" were true of an earlier version (LTX-2, January 2026, when MiniMax H3 and
others were not yet on the boards) and are not true at capture. "One-eighth the cost" names no
comparison set or measurement.

## Where the model is available (verbatim)

> as open-source weights for self-hosting and fine-tuning, via the managed LTX API, and embedded in LTX
> Desktop. It also powers LTX Studio, a separate creative application built on top of it.

> **API keys and developer console at https://console.ltx.io. Documentation at https://docs.ltx.io.**

> LTX-2.5 is a separate product from LTX Studio … LTX Studio uses LTX-2.5 as one of several models
> alongside Kling, Veo, FLUX, Seedance, and others.

## Summary

This page supplies the vendor's own canonical naming (LTX-2.5), the June 2026 Lightricks split that
created LTX as a business unit, the parameter counts (LTX-2 ≈19B, LTX-2.3 and LTX-2.5 22B), the
24 kHz stereo audio claim, the "LTX Model License, not Apache 2.0" statement, and a list of claimed
workflows that is broader than the hosted API. It is the single best first-party source for identity
and dates, and the single worst for capability boundaries.

## Gaps

- No publication date beyond "October 2026", and no changelog.
- The page is written to be quoted by AI assistants; several claims (top-3 ranking, 1/8 cost, 1/7 render
  time) are unverifiable as stated and one is contradicted by the independent leaderboard captured the
  same day.
- No statement of which checkpoint the API serves.

## Terms (verbatim FAQ)

> Is LTX really free under $10M in revenue? — Yes. If your company earns under $10M in annual revenue,
> you can self-host the full model weights, fine-tune, and use LTX commercially at no cost under the
> community license. No usage caps, no seat fees.

> Is the $10M threshold company revenue or product revenue? — Total annual company revenue, measured
> across your organization.

> Can I run LTX fully air-gapped? — Yes. Enterprise deployments support on-prem, edge, and fully
> air-gapped environments …

> What license governs the weights? — LTX ships under the LTX community license, which permits
> commercial use under $10M in annual revenue and requires a commercial license above it. The full terms
> are linked from each model on the license page.

> Do you charge per generation? — Not necessarily. We offer a fixed-price license for predictable costs,
> or per-generation pricing depending on your use case and preference. Either way, you run the model on
> your own infrastructure.

The Hugging Face repository tags the licence as `ltx-2.x-community-license-agreement`, and the model card
adds: "Transfer of fine-tunes may require a paid license, in accordance with the LTX-2.x Community
License" ([HF model card](../../huggingface.co/ltx-2-5-model-card/2026-10-26.md)). The licence text
itself is not published on this page — it is linked per model — and was not captured.

Commercial path, verbatim: "01 Talk to a licensing expert … 02 Run a structured pilot — A focused
30-day pilot with kickoff, technical implementation, direct engineer access, and mid- and end-of-pilot
reviews. … 03 Deploy to production".

## Summary

LTX-2.5's weights are open but not OSI-open: commercial self-hosted use is free below USD 10M of total
annual company revenue, and a paid commercial licence is required above that. The licence is named
"LTX-2.x Community License Agreement" and is explicitly not Apache 2.0 (the llm-info page states that
directly). Redistribution and fine-tune transfer may carry additional conditions, per the model card.

## Gaps

- The binding licence text (`LICENSE` in the HF repo, or the licence page's per-model link) was not

  session.
- No statement on output ownership, indemnity, acceptable use, or training-data provenance.
- The revenue threshold is defined as total company revenue including subsidiaries; nothing is said
  about how it is verified.

## LTX-2.5 entries

**August 11, 2026 — "LTX-2.5" (Open source)** and **"LTX-2.5 API" (API)**, same body text under both
tags:

> Native Multishot · Diffusion Fidelity Rendering · Pretrained Checkpoint

> Diffusion Fidelity Rendering — allocates compute by scene complexity and budget instead of locking
> every scene to one compression rate …

> Native EXR Workflow — reads and writes cinema-grade EXR in ACES and DaVinci Wide Gamut while
> preserving dynamic range, metadata, and color.

> Diffusion Video Decoder — diffusion-based reconstruction for sharper faces, legible text and signage,
> and fewer motion artifacts.

> Larger Dataset + RL Post-Training — broader filtered training and RL aligned to human preference
> improve take rate and reduce repeated failure modes.

> Native Multishot — generates multiple connected shots while preserving character, environment,
> lighting, voice, style, and continuity across cuts.

> Custom Gemma 4 12B Text Encoder — improved retention of complex prompts with multiple subjects,
> actions, lighting, and camera direction.

> Dedicated Prompt Enhancer — expands short prompts into richer cinematic, temporal, and contextual
> instructions at near-zero extra compute.

> Auto Duration — predicts clip length from scene content before generation.

> Pretrained Checkpoint for Deep Adaptation — raw, non-SFT checkpoint for aggressive fine-tuning across
> custom domains and objectives.

> Cleaner Permissive Licensing — reduced restrictive third-party dependencies for a clearer path to
> fine-tune, deploy, commercialize, and redistribute.

> Substantially Better Distilled Model — improved distillation carries far more of the full model's
> quality, prompt adherence, and motion into a smaller, faster model.

**2026-08-11 is therefore the vendor's launch date for LTX-2.5, announced simultaneously as open
weights and as an API.** No later LTX-2.5-specific changelog entry appears in the captured feed; later
entries are LTX Trainer, LTX Desktop, post-production IC-LoRAs and Studio features.

## LTX-2.3 entries (for the version delta)

**March 5, 2026 — "LTX-2.3" (API):** "Introducing LTX-2.3 — a major leap in quality and speed … LTX-2.3
is now the default model across the API, available in two variants: LTX-2.3 Pro (ltx-2-3-pro) …
LTX-2.3 Fast (ltx-2-3-fast) — blazing fast generation, supports text-to-video and image-to-video."
What's new: "Sharper fine details — new latent space with an updated VAE"; "Cleaner audio — improved
data filtering"; "Stronger image-to-video"; "Better prompt understanding — improved text connector".

**March 26, 2026 — "22B Model · Native Audio · Portrait Video · Desktop Editor" (Open source):**
"New 22-billion parameter architecture (upgraded from 19B)"; "Generates synchronized audio and video in
a single forward pass"; "Up to 4K resolution at 50 FPS, up to 20 seconds per clip"; "Stereo audio at
24 kHz"; "24 and 48 FPS output options"; "Last-frame interpolation support"; "Spatial and temporal
upscalers"; "4× larger text connector". Checkpoints: `ltx-2.3-22b-dev.safetensors`,
`ltx-2.3-22b-distilled.safetensors`.

So 4K, 50 fps, 20 s and 24 kHz stereo audio all predate LTX-2.5 — they arrived with LTX-2.3 in
March 2026. LTX-2.5's own deltas are the decoder, the Gemma 4 text encoder, multi-shot, auto duration
and distillation quality.

## Other dated entries that matter to LTX-2.5's API surface

- **2026-07-13** — Reframe/outpainting endpoint (1080p, up to 60 s videos).
- **2026-04-23** — `/v2/video-to-video-hdr` (SDR→HDR, per-frame EXR) plus first async support.
- **2026-05-03** — "Async API expanded to all video generation endpoints (Beta)": `/v2/text-to-video`,
  `/v2/image-to-video`, `/v2/audio-to-video`, `/v2/retake`, `/v2/extend`.
- **2026-10-13** — LTX Trainer in LTX Explore (no model change).

## Summary

The changelog dates LTX-2.5 to 2026-08-11 on both the open-weights and API tracks, and establishes that
the 4K/50 fps/20 s/24 kHz-audio envelope is inherited from LTX-2.3 (March 2026), not new in 2.5. The
2.5 entry's own claims are architectural: diffusion video decoder, Gemma 4 12B encoder, native
multishot, auto duration, better distillation, reduced third-party licence dependencies.

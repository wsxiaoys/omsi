## What this establishes

- Current per-image list prices for the two image tiers at 2048px: **$0.0404** (Uni-1.1 T2I),
  **$0.0434** (Uni-1.1 edit), **$0.10** (Uni-1.1 Max T2I), **$0.103** (Uni-1.1 Max edit), rising
  with each reference image.
- Two commercial shapes: pay-per-image "Build", and capacity-based "Scale" with a no-train guarantee.
  The no-train guarantee is attached to the Scale plan, not to the default pay-as-you-go path — the
  [API FAQ](../luma-agents-api-faq/2026-10-26.md) says data-handling follows the ToS and privacy
  policy.
- Luma's own naming on this page is **Uni-1.1 / Uni-1.1 Max**, while its API docs use `uni-1` /
  `uni-1-max` ([models doc](../luma-agents-api-models/2026-10-26.md)). The two names are not
  reconciled on either page.

## Gaps

- "Prices are approximate and based on billing tokens" — the dollar figures are derived from a
  token/credit basis the page does not publish. The
  [API pricing docs](../luma-agents-api-pricing/2026-10-26.md) publish the same service in
  *credits*, which do not convert to dollars on-page.
- The page does not state resolution beyond "2048px (2K)" or what happens on edits of other sizes.
- Partner list is a vendor claim; only fal was verified.

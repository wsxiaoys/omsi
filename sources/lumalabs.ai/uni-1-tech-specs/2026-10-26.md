## Captured content

Framing (verbatim):

> In the past 3 years, our journey at Luma has taken us from scene reconstruction, to 3D
> generation, to scaling video diffusion across the world's visual content. [...] But scaling
> visual media alone has a fundamental ceiling: generation without understanding can only go so
> far.

> Uni-1, Luma's first unified understanding and generation model, is our next step on the path
> towards multimodal general intelligence.

> With the Uni-fied family of models, we take an inspired approach. We grow a mind's eye from a
> logical brain: a system that reasons, imagines, plans, iterates, and executes across both digital
> and physical domains.

Architecture (verbatim):

> At its core, our model is a decoder-only autoregressive transformer. Text and images are
> represented in a single interleaved sequence, acting both as input and as output.

Reasoning (verbatim):

> Uni-1 can perform structured internal reasoning before and during image synthesis. It decomposes
> instructions, resolves constraints, and plans composition, then renders accordingly.

Benchmarks claimed (verbatim, no numbers on the page):

> We evaluate Uni-1 on RISEBench, a benchmark specifically designed for Reasoning-Informed Visual
> Editing (RISE). RISEBench assesses four core reasoning capabilities, namely Temporal, Causal,
> Spatial, and Logical. Uni-1 achieves state-of-the-art results on this benchmark [...]

> We evaluate on ODinW-13 following consistent protocols from prior work [...] ODinW (Open Detection
> in the Wild) measures open vocabulary dense detection, testing fine-grained visual reasoning. We
> use this benchmark to show how generation improves understanding in our unified model [...]

Capability showcase sections, in the vendor's words: "Intelligent" (temporal consistency,
layering, spatial, infographics, multilingual, common sense, causal); "Directable" (references,
sketch, visual instructions, style + structure, multi-turn refinement); "Cultured" (style, manga,
memes, aesthetics). One worked example is a six-frame storyboard ("Piano Aging") in which each
frame is generated in turn with the prior frame as context; the page labels them "Keyframe 1 — ..."
and shows the model's own reasoning text alongside each frame.

Closing claim (verbatim):

> This unified design naturally extends beyond static images to video, voice agents, and fully
> interactive world simulators.

## What this establishes

- Architecture is stated by the vendor: decoder-only autoregressive transformer over an interleaved
  text+image token sequence — not a diffusion model with a separate text encoder.
- The vendor claims SOTA on RISEBench and reports ODinW-13 detection results. **No numeric results
  are published on the page**; the charts are images and were not transcribed. Luma's
  [Uni-1.1 API post](../uni-1-1-api/2026-10-26.md) adds that Uni-1 "leads RISEBench on both overall
  reasoning and spatial logic" — still no numbers, and the benchmark's own leaderboard was not
  captured.
- Multi-turn / sequential generation (storyboard, keyframe chaining) is a demonstrated workflow on
  the vendor's own page, not just a bullet.

## Gaps

- All benchmark figures are vendor-reported and unquantified here. No independent RISEBench or
  ODinW-13 reproduction was captured.
- No parameter count, training data description, context length, or latency figures.
- The page does not state a release date or a version number; it is titled UNI-1 but is served from
  the current site, so it may describe the Uni-1.1 generation.

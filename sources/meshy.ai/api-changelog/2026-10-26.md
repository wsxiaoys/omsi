## Summary

The changelog supplies the API-level dates the blog posts do not. Meshy 7 became selectable through the
API on 2026-08-12 (Image to 3D, Multi-Image, Retexture) and 2026-08-13 (Text to 3D); Meshy 7.1 on
2026-09-18, the same day `meshy-7` was deprecated and `geometry_resolution` replaced `ultra_mode`. The
gap between the 7.1 launch post (2026-09-10) and API availability (2026-09-18) is eight days of web-app
rollout.

## Gaps

- No entry announces a latency or quality change for 7.1 beyond geometry detail.
- The changelog does not say when the 4096³ pass became available on the web app for all plans; the
  launch post describes a gradual rollout with no completion date.

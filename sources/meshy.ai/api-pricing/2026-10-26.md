# Meshy: API pricing (credits)

- Origin: Meshy Docs, "Pricing", https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/pricing
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor price list)
- Publication date: none stated; live price list as of capture.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Meshy 7.1 (`meshy-7.1` / `latest`), Meshy 6, Meshy 6 Lite, Meshy T2, Meshy T2.5

## Captured content (summary with verbatim quotes)

Basis: "The Meshy API uses prepaid credits. Before you send API requests, buy API credits in your
subscription settings." Prices are "in credits per request, unless a row says otherwise."

### 3D generation, credits per request

| API / configuration | meshy-7.1 (`latest`) | meshy-6 | meshy-6-lite | meshy-t2 | meshy-t2.5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Text to 3D (Preview), mesh only | 20 | 20 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| Image to 3D, mesh only | 20 | 20 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| Mesh with 2K textures | 30 | 30 | 15 | 15 | 15 |
| Mesh with 4K textures | 30 | 30 | not supported | 15 | 15 |
| Mesh with 8K textures | 35 | 35 | not supported | 20 | 20 |
| Multi-Image to 3D, mesh only | 20 | 20 | 5 | not supported | not supported |
| Multi-Image to 3D, 2K textures | 30 | 30 | 15 | not supported | not supported |
| Multi-Image to 3D, 4K / 8K textures | 30 / 35 | 30 / 35 | not supported | not supported | not supported |
| Ultra geometry surcharge (`geometry_resolution: "2k"` or `"4k"`) | +5 | not supported | not supported | not supported | not supported |

Verbatim notes: "Legacy models: `meshy-7` costs the same as `meshy-7.1`. `model_type: "lowpoly"` costs
the same as `meshy-6` until it's retired on October 30, 2026."

### Texturing and post-processing

| API | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Text to 3D (Refine), 2K / 4K | 10 |
| Text to 3D (Refine), 8K | 15 |
| Retexture, 2K / 4K | 10 |
| Retexture, 8K | 15 |
| Rigging (automatic rigging) | 5 per request |
| Animation | 3 per action, up to 10 actions per request |
| Text to Motion | 10 (prime) / 3 (swift) per request |
| Remesh | 5 |
| UV Unwrap | 5 |
| Convert | 1 |
| Resize | 1 |

"Text to 3D Preview and Refine are separate requests, and Meshy bills them separately."

### Credit calculator default shown at capture

Endpoint "Image to 3D", model `latest (meshy-7.1)`, Standard geometry, 2K texture:
"Estimated cost 30 credits per request", broken out as "Mesh generation 20 / Texture generation 10".

## Summary

The price of a Meshy 7.1 generation is identical to Meshy 7 and Meshy 6: 20 credits for geometry, plus
10 for 2K/4K texturing (35 total at 8K), plus 5 for Ultra geometry at either 2K or 4K. 7.1's
differentiator is therefore priced as an add-on, not a premium tier: "4K · Ultra (+5 credits)" costs the
same as 2K · Ultra.

## Gaps

- Credits are not dollars on this page; the conversion comes from the web pricing plans record.
- No statement of whether failed tasks are refunded here (the task-object and changelog records say
  credits are refunded on failure, and PENDING deletions are refunded).
- No volume or enterprise discount figures.

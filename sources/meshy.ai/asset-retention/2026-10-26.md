# Meshy: Asset retention (API output lifetime)

- Origin: Meshy Docs, "Asset retention", https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/asset-retention
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor policy)
- Publication date: none stated; live policy as of capture.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: every Meshy API generation, including Meshy 7.1

## Captured content (verbatim quotes)

> Meshy keeps the models you generate through the API for at most 3 days. After that, Meshy deletes
> them from its servers.

> If you aren't an Enterprise customer and need a model for longer, download it and store your own
> copy. Enterprise customers can keep their models indefinitely.

Download URLs are signed: "The URLs in a task's `model_urls`, `texture_urls`, and `thumbnail_url` point
at `assets.meshy.ai` and carry a signature (`?Expires=…&Signature=…`). A URL stops working at the
task's `expires_at`."

Practical rules the page states: download on `SUCCEEDED`; "Don't store a download URL to send back to
Meshy later as `model_url`"; pass `input_task_id` while the source task still exists, otherwise a 404.

## Summary

A 3-day retention window is the most consequential operational constraint for anyone building on Meshy
7.1 through the first-party API: outputs must be copied out immediately, and chained operations must go
through `input_task_id` rather than stored URLs. The web pricing page records longer windows for paid
team plans (Studio 14 days, Enterprise forever), so the 3-day figure is the default rather than a
universal limit.

## Gaps

- The page does not state whether the 3-day clock starts at creation or at completion.
- No statement of retention for the web app (the terms of use describe a discretionary storage limit
  there instead).

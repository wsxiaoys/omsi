## Captured content (summary with verbatim quotes)

> We are excited to introduce Meshy-6, our most refined and versatile generation model to date.
> Building on the evolution from Meshy 5, this release focuses on higher-quality geometry, better
> structural consistency, and powerful new workflow features.

Claimed upgrades: "smoother, more anatomically correct geometry for characters and organic models";
for hard-surface work "sharper edges, clearer silhouettes, and a cleaner overall structure".

New at Meshy 6: a dedicated **Low Poly Mode** for game developers ("efficient wireframes optimized for
real-time performance"), **multi-color 3D printing** with 3MF export, an **API Playground**, and new
**Text to Image / Image to Image APIs** built on "nano-banana and nano-banana-pro".

## Summary

Dated first-party record for Meshy 6, the model that remains active alongside 6 Lite and 7.1 in the
current deprecation table. Meshy 6 is the only model in the current line that produces an emission map
and that honours `remove_lighting` (see the text-to-3D API record), so it is not simply a cheaper 7.1.

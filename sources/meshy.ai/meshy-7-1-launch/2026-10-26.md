# Meshy: "Meshy 7.1: Attention to the Closest Detail" (launch post)

- Origin: Meshy (author "Andrew"), "Meshy 7.1: Attention to the Closest Detail",
  https://www.meshy.ai/blog/meshy-7-1-launch
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor launch post / product announcement)
- Publication date: 2026-09-10 (stated on the page: "Posted: September 10, 2026")
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Meshy 7.1 (also references Meshy 7, Meshy 6, and competitors Tripo 3.1,
  Hunyuan3D 3.1, Rodin 2.5, Hi3D 3.0 / Hi3D 2.1)

## Captured content (summary with verbatim quotes)

Positioning. The post calls Meshy 7.1 "an upgrade to Meshy 7" built around one goal:
"generating the richest geometric detail of any available image-to-3D model".

TL;DR, quoted in full:

> Meshy 7.1 targets one thing: geometric detail. A new Ultra 4K mode scales geometry generation
> from 2048³ to 4096³, as of this release the only 3D generative model to reach it, with raw meshes
> up to 80 million triangles before simplification. On our new Detail Richness benchmark, Meshy 7.1
> ranks first at 1K, 2K, and 4K render resolutions. Rolling out from September 10, 2026.

Geometry resolution. "The model inherits the strong image understanding capability from Meshy 7,
and further scales the generation resolution from 2048³ to 4096³ with detail enhancements."
"Meshy 7.1 is the only 3D generative model on the market that is able to obtain a 4096³ geometry
resolution to date." A raw mesh "without any simplification, can contain as many as 80 million
triangles."

Detail Richness benchmark (the vendor's own metric, no independent replication captured). Defined as
"when rendered at multiple directions, how much of the model needs to be anti-aliased at a given
render resolution". Vendor table, single-view inputs built from "images with very rich structural
details but very simple textures", all models run at their "maximum quality settings":

| Render resolution | Meshy 7.1 | Tripo 3.1 | Hunyuan3D 3.1 | Rodin 2.5 | Hi3D 3.0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1024 | 53.5% | 44.8% | 39.4% | 51.4% | 49.1% |
| 2048 | 37.4% | 29.5% | 25.4% | 35.0% | 34.4% |
| 4096 | 23.1% | 17.3% | 14.7% | 21.1% | 21.9% |

Per-object claim: "Meshy 7.1's win rate on Detail Richness ranges from 56% to 98%". The post says
"further scaling geometric resolution beyond 4096³ will likely provide little to no additional
benefit", and that Web3D compression noise forced them to evaluate "all generative models on
uncompressed geometry".

Generation settings. Three: "Standard, Ultra 2K, and Ultra 4K ... differ in the geometry resolution
the model generates at". "Ultra 2K is available for both single-view and multi-view generation, and
Ultra 4K is available for single-view generation only."

Availability (verbatim):

> Starting September 10, 2026, Meshy 7.1 rolls out gradually in the Meshy workspace and will reach
> all users soon after.

> Meshy 7.1 is open to all registered users, while only subscribed users are able to access Ultra 2K
> and Ultra 4K resolutions as well as download models generated by Meshy 7.1.

## Summary

First-party, dated launch record for Meshy 7.1. It establishes: the 2026-09-10 announcement and
gradual rollout; 4096³ Ultra 4K geometry as the single differentiator from Meshy 7 (2048³); the
single-view restriction on Ultra 4K; and a subscription requirement for Ultra resolutions and for
downloading 7.1 output. All quality numbers are the vendor's own benchmark, run by the vendor, on a
metric the vendor introduced in the same post. No third-party measurement of the claim was captured.

## Gaps

- No independent replication of Detail Richness; the metric and the input set are vendor-defined.
- The post does not state per-tier latency, credit cost, or output formats.
- "Rolling out ... will reach all users soon after" gives no completion date for the rollout.
- The competitor numbers are vendor-run; the benchmark tool itself is announced as "released
  separately" and was not captured.

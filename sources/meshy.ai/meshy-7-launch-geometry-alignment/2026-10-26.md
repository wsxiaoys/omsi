# Meshy: "Meshy 7: Pushing the frontier of 3D alignment" (launch post)

- Origin: Meshy (author "Andrew"), "Meshy 7: Pushing the frontier of 3D alignment",
  https://www.meshy.ai/blog/meshy-7-image-to-3d-geometry-alignment
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor launch post / product announcement)
- Publication date: 2026-08-12 (stated on the page: "Posted: August 12, 2026")
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Meshy 7 (predecessor of Meshy 7.1); compares Meshy 6 Lite, Tripo 3.1,
  Hunyuan3D 3.1, Rodin 2.5, Hi3D 2.1

## Captured content (summary with verbatim quotes)

Scope. "Today we release Meshy 7, our new image-to-3D generative model and the successor to Meshy 6."
The post is about geometry alignment — "how faithfully the shape and structure of the generated 3D
model match the input image" — not surface detail.

Geometry alignment benchmark (vendor-run, scored "directly from geometry against held-out reference
models"). Top-quarter single view:

| Metric | Meshy 7 | Meshy 6 Lite | Tripo 3.1 | Hunyuan3D 3.1 | Rodin 2.5 | Hi3D 2.1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Overall Proportion | 81.0% | 78.2% | 79.0% | 76.7% | 72.0% | 79.1% |
| Spatial Distribution | 79.7% | 75.2% | 78.4% | 73.7% | 71.4% | 76.5% |
| Surface Details | 59.8% | 52.7% | 51.7% | 54.5% | 49.0% | 55.2% |

Four-view input, same table: Meshy 7 84.4% / 81.8% / 60.6%; Meshy 6 Lite 76.3% / 74.2% / 55.1%;
Tripo 3.1 85.4% / 81.7% / **62.8%**; Hunyuan3D 3.1 79.2% / 73.5% / 57.5%; Rodin 2.5 82.6% / 79.6% /
53.9%; Hi3D 2.1 86.5% / 81.8% / 61.8%.

Note the post's own framing of the four-view result: "Tripo 3.1 in particular closes much of its
single-view gap and approaches Meshy 7 under the four-view condition", and "Meshy 7 posts the top
score on spatial distribution" — i.e. the vendor does **not** claim first place on all three metrics
in the four-view condition, and Tripo 3.1 and Hi3D 2.1 score higher on overall proportion and surface
details there.

Architecture claims (marketing, no detail): "We improved the image encoder in the Meshy 7 architecture
to read features at multiple scales and accept higher-resolution images", rebuilt training data "around
a stricter quality standard", and used alignment as a training evaluation signal.

## Summary

Dated first-party record for Meshy 7, six weeks before 7.1. It establishes the 2026-08-12 release and
that Meshy 7's stated target was geometry alignment; Meshy 7.1's stated target is surface detail at
4096³. Together with the changelog record, this supports treating 7 and 7.1 as consecutive standard
models with the same API surface and price, differing in geometry resolution (2048³ vs 4096³).

## Gaps

- Vendor-run benchmark; no independent replication.
- No parameter, pricing, or latency detail.
- The post does not say whether 7.1 reuses the Meshy 7 image encoder; that is inferred from the 7.1
  post's "inherits the strong image understanding capability from Meshy 7".

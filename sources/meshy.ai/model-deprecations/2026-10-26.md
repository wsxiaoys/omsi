## Captured content (verbatim table)

Lifecycle terms as defined by Meshy: Active — "Fully supported and recommended for use." Legacy — "No
longer receives updates and may be deprecated in the future. It still works, and nothing about your
request or your bill changes." Deprecated — "Still works, but no longer recommended. Meshy names a
recommended replacement and, once it is scheduled, a retirement date." Retired — "No longer available."

"`latest` resolves to `meshy-7.1`."

Guidance, quoted: "Set `ai_model` to a specific model, such as `meshy-7.1`, if your integration needs
consistent output. `latest` always points to the newest standard model, so its output changes when a
new model is released." And: "Meshy announces a retirement date at least one month before the
retirement."

Parameter deprecations recorded on the same page include `ultra_mode` (2026-09-18, replaced by
`geometry_resolution`), `hd_texture` (2026-07-21, replaced by `texture_resolution`), `symmetry_mode`
(2026-05-11, accepted and ignored), and `art_style` (2026-01-22, accepted and ignored).

## Summary

Meshy 7.1 is the current standard model and the `latest` alias target as of capture. Meshy 7 was
deprecated eleven days after 7.1 shipped (2026-09-18), with no retirement date announced. Meshy 6
remains **Active** alongside 7.1, which is why 6 is not simply legacy capacity: it is the only model
that still produces an emission map.

## Gaps

- No retirement date for `meshy-7`, so its remaining lifetime is unknown.
- The page does not say whether `latest` will move to a future 7.x or 8.x.

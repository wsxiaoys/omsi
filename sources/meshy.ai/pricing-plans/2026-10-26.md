# Meshy: Pricing and plans (credits, dollars, per-plan licences)

- Origin: Meshy, "Meshy Official Pricing: Free, Pro, Studio & Enterprise Plans",
  https://www.meshy.ai/pricing
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor plan and price page)
- Publication date: none stated; live price page as of capture, monthly billing, USD.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Meshy 7.1 and all Meshy plans; the per-plan licence and retention terms apply to
  7.1 output

## Captured content (verbatim quotes and figures)

| Plan | Price (monthly) | Monthly credits | Concurrent tasks | Licence / retention line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 100 | 1 | "CC BY 4.0 license (commercial use with attribution)" |
| Pro | $20 | 1,000 ("up to 100 assets") | 10 | "Private license for all assets" |
| Premium | $40 | 3,000 ("up to 300 assets") | 30 | "Everything in Pro, plus: Early access to new features" |
| Ultra | $100 | 8,000 ("up to 800 assets") | 100 | "Everything in Pro, plus ..." |
| Studio | $70 (1X; 3X and 5X packages) | 5,500 shared | 60 | "14-day API asset retention" |
| Enterprise | Custom | Customizable | 100 | "Forever API asset retention", "Full API access", "Configurable data retention" |

Plan features also list "600+ motions" for animation, "20% OFF on Extra Credit Packs" (Pro), and
per-plan retry allowances ("4 free retries per task" on Pro, up to 40 on Ultra).

Implied credit price, computed from the published plans: Pro $20 / 1,000 = $0.020 per credit;
Premium $40 / 3,000 ≈ $0.0133; Ultra $100 / 8,000 = $0.0125. Extra Credit Packs are sold separately and
were not priced in the captured page beyond the Pro discount.

## Summary

This is the only captured page that converts Meshy credits to money and that states licence and
retention differences by plan. Applying the implied rates to the API price list: a Meshy 7.1
image-to-3D with 2K textures (30 credits) costs roughly $0.38–$0.60 depending on plan, and the Ultra 4K
surcharge (5 credits) adds roughly $0.06–$0.10. The free plan is usable commercially only with
attribution, and the 3-day API retention quoted in the docs is a default that Studio (14 days) and
Enterprise (forever) extend.

## Gaps

- Credit-to-dollar rates are derived by division, not published as a per-credit price; annual billing
  and Extra Credit Packs would change them.
- The page does not state whether Ultra 4K generation is plan-gated on the API (the launch post says
  only that Ultra resolutions need a subscription in the web app).

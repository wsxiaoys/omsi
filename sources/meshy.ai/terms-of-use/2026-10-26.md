# Meshy: Terms of Use (output ownership, licences, training use)

- Origin: Meshy LLC, "Terms of Use", https://www.meshy.ai/terms-of-use
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor legal terms)
- Publication date: "Last Updated: September 19, 2026" (stated on the page)
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: every Meshy service, including Meshy 7.1 outputs through the web app and API

## Captured content (verbatim quotes)

Output ownership by plan (§3.2):

> Customers using Meshy's Services under the free plan, acknowledge and agree that Meshy owns all right,
> title, and interest, including all intellectual property rights, in and to the Customer Output and
> makes such rights available to free plan customers under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0

> appropriate credit to Meshy.

> As between Meshy and those Customers on a paid Meshy plan, and to the extent possible under applicable
> law, such customers on a paid Meshy plan own their Customer Output.

Licence back to Meshy for paid plans: a "non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to reproduce,
distribute, and otherwise use and display the User Content and Process the User Content as may be
necessary for Meshy to provide the Service".

Training use (§ quoted):

> Meshy may use Customer Inputs and Customer Outputs (collectively, "User Content") from non-Enterprise
> Customers to train, validate, test, or improve Services unless otherwise agreed to in the Order. You
> may opt out of such use by contacting support@meshy.ai.

AI-identification metadata (§2.4): "The Service may include machine-readable metadata, digital
watermarks, or other identifiers in Customer Output to identify that the Customer Output was generated
by AI. You agree not to remove, alter, disable, or otherwise tamper with such identifiers."

Storage (§2.5): "Customer Output generated by Customers using the APIs, other than Enterprise Customers,
will be deleted three (3) days after it is generated." Enterprise output "shall be retained
indefinitely by default".

Prohibited uses include "(iv) develop or offer competitive products or services or engage in competitive
analysis", "(xi) use generated digital assets to train, develop, or improve AI models that are
competitive with Meshy", and submitting "any sensitive personal data".

Uniqueness disclaimer: "DUE TO THE NATURE OF GENERATIVE AI TECHNOLOGY, THE SERVICE MAY GENERATE SIMILAR
OR IDENTICAL 3D MODELS FOR DIFFERENT USERS WHO PROVIDE SIMILAR 2D INPUTS."

## Summary

The licence is plan-dependent and not a model licence: free-plan output is Meshy-owned and offered under
CC BY 4.0 with attribution; paid-plan output is customer-owned to the extent law allows; non-Enterprise
inputs and outputs may be used for training unless the customer opts out by email. No weights are
offered under any plan, so there is no weight licence to state. The terms also forbid removing any
AI-provenance watermark, which matters for asset pipelines that strip metadata.

## Gaps

- The terms do not state a licence for outputs used commercially *after* a subscription lapses, beyond
  the plan-based split above.
- No captured page describes an API-specific acceptable-use list beyond the Acceptable Use Policy link.
- Whether `moderation: true` is required by the terms is not stated (it defaults to false).

# Meshy: "Meshy 7 Texturing: True to Color, Clear in Detail" (texture alignment benchmark)

- Origin: Meshy (author "Andrew"), "Meshy 7 Texturing: True to Color, Clear in Detail",
  https://www.meshy.ai/blog/texture-alignment-benchmark
- Evidence kind: official documentation (vendor benchmark post)
- Publication date: 2026-09-21 (stated on the page: "Posted: September 21, 2026")
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Meshy 7 texturing (also used for Meshy 7.1 output); compares Meshy 6, Hi3D 3.0,
  Tripo 3.5, Rodin 2.5, Hunyuan 3.1

## Captured content (summary with verbatim quotes)

Claim: "Meshy 7 texturing ranks first overall on our texture alignment benchmark, both on a shared mesh
and end to end. The benchmark is now open source."

Same-mesh comparison (every provider textured "the identical Meshy 7 mesh generated from the same
reference image at the maximum texture quality each provider offers"):

| Dimension | Meshy 7 | Hi3D 3.0 | Tripo 3.5 | Meshy 6 | Rodin 2.5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Color | 72.5% | 67.5% | 67.4% | 70.8% | 65.9% |
| Texture Style | 64.4% | 64.2% | 63.8% | 60.5% | 58.6% |
| Semantic Details | 61.7% | 61.6% | 61.7% | 60.3% | 59.7% |
| Average | 66.2% | 64.4% | 64.3% | 63.9% | 61.4% |
| Mesh-Texture Agreement | 84.8% | 86.5% | 84.9% | 84.1% | 84.4% |

End-to-end (each provider on its own geometry): Meshy 7 average 66.2%, Tripo 3.5 63.8%, Hi3D 3.0 63.1%,
Hunyuan 3.1 59.8%, Rodin 2.5 55.0%. "Meshy 7 wins between 71% and 94% of the objects depending on the
opponent". Hi3D 3.0 leads on Mesh-Texture Agreement in both tables (86.5% and 86.9% vs Meshy 7's 84.8%).

### The 7.1 relationship, quoted

> We did not choose higher geometry resolutions released in Meshy 7.1 because it exceeded the maximum
> polycount supported by other texture generators.

And in an example: "For the Meshy 7 texturing example, the mesh was generated by Meshy 7.1 at Ultra 4K
resolution."

## Summary

This is the vendor's texturing benchmark, and its most useful content for identity is incidental: it
treats "Meshy 7 texturing" as the current texturing model, pairs it with Meshy 7.1 geometry in its own
example, and says 7.1's higher geometry resolutions were excluded from the comparison because they
exceed competitor polycount limits. That is consistent with the changelog's description of 7.1 as
"same parameters and credit cost as meshy-7, higher geometry detail", i.e. 7.1 is a geometry upgrade
over the Meshy 7 texturing model rather than a new texturer.

The benchmark itself is vendor-run. Meshy 7 leads on the average but not on every dimension: Hi3D 3.0
scores higher on Mesh-Texture Agreement in both conditions, and ties or nearly ties on Texture Style and
Semantic Details on the shared mesh.

## Gaps

- Vendor-run; the "open source" benchmark implementation was not captured or run.
- The reference set is not public ("not able to make these objects publicly available due to licensing
  requirements").
- No texture-resolution or PBR-material comparison; the metric is image-space appearance.

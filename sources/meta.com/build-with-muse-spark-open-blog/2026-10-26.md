Standfirst:

> An 816B-parameter open-weight model from Meta Superintelligence Labs, trained for long-horizon
> agentic work such as coding and customer service agents. It activates 42B parameters per token,

Opening:

> Today we're releasing Muse Spark Open, the open weights version of Muse Spark, an 816B-parameter
> sparse mixture-of-experts model that activates roughly 42 billion parameters per token. It takes
> text, image, video and audio input and holds a million tokens of context. It's trained for agentic
> work, so you can build agents that can read a large codebase, plan a change, call tools, check the
> result and correct themselves when a step fails.

> For more details on the model and the research foundations, check out the Meta research blog.

TL;DR:

> * **Inference runtimes:** Runs on SGLang, vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, Hugging Face Transformers, MLX,
>   llama.cpp and Unsloth at launch, and verified on NVIDIA, AMD, Intel and Apple silicon.
> * **Hosted inference:** Baseten, Fireworks AI, Groq, Modal, Nebius, OpenRouter and Together AI.
> * **Precisions:** Includes BF16 and NVFP4 support from Meta, (FP8, MXFP4) from AMD, (INT4, MXFP4)
>   optimized for Crescent Island from Intel and GGUF quants from Unsloth.
> * **Post Training:** Fine-tuning with TorchTitan, SGLang + Miles, NVIDIA NeMo AutoModel, Hugging
>   Face TRL and managed post-training APIs from Fireworks AI and Together AI are all live at
>   release.
> * **Decoding Model:** Draft models for faster token generation, built by SGLang (_DFlash V2_) and
>   vLLM (_DSpark_).
> * **Model Catalogs:** Amazon SageMaker AI, Google Cloud Model Garden, Microsoft Foundry Models

Hosted inference table:

> | Provider | What they offer at launch |
> | Baseten | Serverless Model APIs and Dedicated Inference. Served in NVFP4 with a custom-trained DSpark drafter for faster decoding with 1M context window. |
> | Fireworks AI | Serverless pay-per-token API, on-demand dedicated deployments and serverless LoRA fine-tuning. |
> | Groq | Limited preview on GroqCloud, aimed at fast multi-step agent loops. |
> | Modal | Modal Auto Endpoints: a per-token Shared Endpoint, or a Dedicated Endpoint on isolated, autoscaling containers. |
> | Nebius | Available on Nebius Token Factory. |
> | OpenRouter | AI model gateway — One API to reach your preferred inference provider. |
> | Together AI | Serverless and on-demand dedicated endpoints with a 99.9% SLA. |

## Summary

The dated first-party release announcement: **2026-10-15**, from Meta Superintelligence Labs, 816B
total / ~42B active sparse MoE, text + image + video + audio in, text out, 1M-token context, Muse
Model License. It confirms the launch provider set (Baseten, Fireworks, Groq, Modal, Nebius,
OpenRouter, Together) and adds two details the docs overview omits: **Groq is a "limited preview"**
at launch, and Together offers a 99.9 % SLA on serverless/dedicated endpoints. It also names the
quantisations from other vendors (AMD FP8/MXFP4, Intel INT4/MXFP4, Unsloth GGUF) and the two draft
models (SGLang DFlash V2, vLLM DSpark).

## Captured content (verbatim)

Runtime table:

> | Runtime | Role | Checkpoints | Interface |
> | vLLM | Production serving, high throughput | BF16, NVFP4 | OpenAI-compatible HTTP |
> | TensorRT-LLM | Optimized serving on NVIDIA Blackwell, on a single 4-GPU node | NVFP4 | OpenAI-compatible HTTP |
> | NVIDIA Dynamo | Disaggregated serving and KV-aware routing | NVFP4 | OpenAI-compatible HTTP |
> | Hugging Face Transformers | Native Python implementation for research and custom pipelines | BF16, NVFP4 | Python API |
> | llama.cpp | Portable inference across Metal, CUDA, ROCm, Vulkan, and CPU | ggml-org GGUFs | OpenAI-compatible HTTP |
> | Unsloth | Local inference on macOS, Windows, Linux, and WSL | Unsloth Dynamic GGUFs | OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible HTTP |
> | MLX | Inference on Apple silicon, across two or four Mac Studio computers | NVFP4 | OpenAI-compatible HTTP |

(SGLang appears as a further tab on the same page, with BF16 and NVFP4 support and a DFlash2
speculative path.)

Hosted providers:

> Call Muse Spark Open through a hosted provider when you'd rather not provision GPU capacity
> yourself. These providers serve the open weights:
>
> | Provider | Model string |
> | --- | --- |
> | Together AI | `meta-models/muse-spark-open` |
> | Fireworks AI | `accounts/fireworks/models/muse-spark-open` |
> | Baseten | `meta-models/muse-spark-open` |
> | Groq | `meta-models/muse-spark-open` |
> | Nebius Token Factory | `meta-models/muse-spark-open` |
>
> You can also call Muse Spark Open through Modal and OpenRouter.

Speculative decoding: SGLang uses `--speculative-draft-model-path RadixArk/Muse-Spark-Open-DFlash2`;
vLLM uses `--speculative-config '{"method":"dspark","model": "Inferact/Muse-Spark-Open-DSpark",
"num_speculative_tokens":7,…}'`. TensorRT-LLM support lives on a branch:

> Muse Spark Open support is in the TensorRT-LLM `feat/musespark-open` branch. Build TensorRT-LLM
> from that branch; no pre-built release container or wheel includes Muse Spark Open support.

## Summary

Seven runtimes are documented (SGLang, vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, Dynamo, Transformers, llama.cpp, Unsloth,
MLX) and seven hosted providers (Together, Fireworks, Baseten, Groq, Nebius Token Factory, Modal,
OpenRouter). Meta itself is not among them, consistent with the models doc listing the model's API
endpoints as "None (self-host or a hosted provider)". TensorRT-LLM requires a source build from a
feature branch at capture.

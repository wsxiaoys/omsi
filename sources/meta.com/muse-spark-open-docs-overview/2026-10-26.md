## Captured content (verbatim)

Opening:

> Muse Spark Open is the open-weight release of Muse Spark, Meta's agentic and coding model. Run it
> two ways:
>
> * **Self-host:** download a checkpoint and serve it on your own GPUs with SGLang, vLLM, or another
>   runtime, so every prompt and response stays inside your infrastructure.
> * **Hosted provider:** call it through a hosted provider's API, with no GPUs to provision.

### Model variants

| Model | Parameters | Architecture | Context window |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Muse Spark Open | 816B total, 42B active | Sparse mixture of experts, multimodal (text, image, audio, and video input; text output) | 1,048,576 tokens |

### Architecture

> Muse Spark Open is a sparse mixture-of-experts transformer with built-in perception encoders. Each
> token routes to a small subset of experts, so per-token compute tracks the 42B active parameters.
> Serving still loads the full 816B weights into VRAM, which sets the hardware footprint.

### Supported languages

> Muse Spark Open was trained on data from more than 100 languages.

### Checkpoints and hardware

> Muse Spark Open ships in two checkpoint formats:
>
> * **BF16:** the full-precision reference, about 1.65 TB of weights. Serves on eight GPUs, such as
>   8 × B300.
> * **NVFP4:** stores most weights in 4-bit and keeps some layers in BF16, about 537 GB of weights.
>   Serves on a single 4-GPU NVIDIA Blackwell node.
>
> Serving needs memory beyond the weights for the key-value cache, activations, and runtime
> overhead: budget roughly 2 TB of VRAM for BF16.

### License

> Muse Spark Open is released under the Muse Model License(opens in new tab). Use is also subject to
> the Acceptable Use Policy(opens in new tab).

### Launch partners

> * **Model catalogs:** Amazon SageMaker AI, Google Cloud Model Garden, and Microsoft Foundry Model
>   Catalog
> * **Inference platforms:** Baseten, Fireworks AI, Groq, Modal, Nebius, and Together AI
> * **Tooling:** Hugging Face, llama.cpp, MLX, NVIDIA TensorRT-LLM, SGLang and RadixArk, Unsloth,
>   and vLLM and Inferact
> * **Silicon:** AMD, Apple, Intel, and NVIDIA
> * **Gateway:** OpenRouter

## Summary

The developer's own overview fixes the model's shape: one variant, 816B total / 42B active sparse
MoE with perception encoders, text/image/audio/video input and text output, a 1,048,576-token
context, two checkpoint formats (BF16 ≈1.65 TB, NVFP4 ≈537 GB), and a single "Muse Model License"
plus Acceptable Use Policy. There is no version number — unlike the hosted Muse Spark line, which
the docs list as 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3.

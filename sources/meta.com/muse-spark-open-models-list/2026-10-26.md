## Captured content (verbatim)

Intro to the model list:

> Meta ships six model lines. Muse Wonder, Muse Spark, Muse Image, Muse Voice Transcribe, and Segment
> Anything Model (SAM) are hosted on Meta Model API; Muse Glimmer is open-weight and runs on your own
> hardware. Muse Spark also has an open-weight release, Muse Spark Open.

"Models at a glance" row:

> | Self-hosted or provider-hosted agentic and coding work; post-training | Muse Spark Open | Text,
> image, video, and audio | Text | None (self-host or a hosted provider) |

Section body:

> Muse Spark Open is the open-weight release of Muse Spark: an 816B-parameter mixture-of-experts
> model with 42B active parameters. You don't call it over Model API. Download the weights and serve
> them through a runtime such as SGLang, vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, or llama.cpp, or call it through a hosted
> inference provider.

For contrast, the same table gives Muse Wonder and Muse Spark the endpoints "Responses, Chat
Completions, Messages", with input "Text, image, video, audio, and PDF".

## Summary

Meta's own inventory states that Muse Spark Open has **no Meta-hosted endpoint** ("None (self-host or
a hosted provider)"; "You don't call it over Model API"), and gives its modality set as text, image,
video and audio in, text out. The Meta Model API pricing page captured the same day carries no
`muse-spark-open` row at all, consistent with that statement.

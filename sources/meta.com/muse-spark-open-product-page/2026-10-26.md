## Summary

Meta's product page supplies the only first-party benchmark table for Muse Spark Open, plus the
lineage statement that the open weights derive from the August Muse Spark release with added safety
post-training. It publishes no price, no context or licence text of its own (those are in the docs
overview), and no date. The benchmark columns mix third-party models (GLM 5.3, Kimi K3, Mistral
Large 4, Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B) with two unattributed ones (Inkling, Beam).

## Captured content (verbatim)

Sampling:

> The recommended defaults are `temperature 1.0`, `top_p 1.0`, and `do_sample true`, which suit most
> use cases. The evaluation results in the Muse Spark Open model card(opens in new tab) were measured
> at these values with `xhigh` reasoning strength.

Reasoning strength:

> Muse Spark Open reasons before it answers. Reasoning strength sets how much it reasons, and it's
> the single biggest lever on both answer quality and token spend.

| `reasoning_effort` | Rendered strength | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `low` | 8 | Classification, extraction, straightforward questions, and short tool loops |
| `medium` | 32 | General agentic work |
| `high` | 256 | Multi-step reasoning, hard debugging, and long tool loops |
| `xhigh` | 512 (default) | Problems where depth matters more than latency |

Context length:

> Muse Spark Open carries a 1,048,576-token context window. Of its 62 decoder layers, 46 use
> sliding-window attention over the most recent 2,048 tokens and 16 use full attention. Only the
> full-attention layers keep a KV cache for the whole context, so KV memory grows with context length
> on those 16 layers and stays fixed on the rest. Set the server's maximum context length
> (`--context-length` in SGLang, `--max-model-len` in vLLM) to what your workload needs rather than
> the full window.

Parallelism:

> | Checkpoint | Tensor parallel | Expert parallel | GPUs |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | BF16 | 8 | 8 | 8 × B300 |
> | NVFP4 | 4 | 4 | 4 × B300 |

> * SGLang: `--reasoning-parser muse --tool-call-parser muse`
> * vLLM: `--reasoning-parser muse --tool-call-parser muse --enable-auto-tool-choice`
> * TensorRT-LLM: `--reasoning_parser musespark --tool_parser musespark`

Stop tokens:

> Muse Spark Open ends a finished turn with `<|eot|>`. It ends a message that continues the turn
> with `<|eom|>`.
>
> Never stop on `<|eom|>` […] Stop on `<|eot|>` and the end-of-text token only.

| Token | ID | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `<|begin_of_text|>` | 200000 | `bos_token_id` |
| `<|end_of_text|>` | 200001 | End of text, in `eos_token_id` |
| `<|eom|>` | 200007 | Ends a message while the turn continues […] Not in `eos_token_id` |
| `<|eot|>` | 200008 | Ends the turn, in `eos_token_id` |
| `<|finetune_right_pad|>` | 200018 | `pad_token_id` |

> `eos_token_id` is `[200001, 200008]`.

## Summary

Serving defaults: `temperature 1.0`, `top_p 1.0`, `xhigh` reasoning. The architecture detail here is
the most specific first-party statement captured: **62 decoder layers, 46 sliding-window (2,048
tokens) and 16 full-attention**, with the KV cache only growing on the 16 full layers. Reference
serving recipes are 8 × B300 for BF16 (TP8/EP8) and 4 × B300 for NVFP4 (TP4/EP4). Stop-token
handling is a footgun: `<|eom|>` must not be in the stop set.

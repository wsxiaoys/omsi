## Notes

- The post dates the series to **2026-10-12** and gives no separate GA date. It calls both variants
  available "today", but only Muse Wonder is on the pay-as-you-go Model API; Full is subscription
  only.
- The benchmark table on the post is an image; the same table is rendered as text on the
  `dev.meta.ai/models/muse-wonder` product page and is transcribed there.
- The post names **Muse Spark 1.3** as the previous generation and **Muse** (consumer app) as a
  future surface. It does not mention Muse Image.
- "Most capable", "state-of-the-art", "most aligned" are vendor claims. The only independent
  measurement captured for this page is Artificial Analysis.

## Gaps

- No model IDs, prices, context window or modality limits in the post.
- The promised "full report with additional safety evaluations" is the separate model card; the
  post does not link a public safety report URL.
- No statement on preview status, deprecation, or regional availability.

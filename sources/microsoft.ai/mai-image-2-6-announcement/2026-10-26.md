## Summary

This is the launch record: 2.6 shipped **into Microsoft Foundry public preview on 2026-09-04**,
together with MAI-Image-2.6-Flash. It is the only first-party statement of the 2.6-only feature set
(multi-image reference editing, web grounding, dynamic aspect ratios) and the origin of the
"#2 on Arena", "#2 text-to-image / #1 image editing on Artificial Analysis" claims — both
explicitly footnoted "as of Sep 4, 2026", i.e. point-in-time claims, not current rankings.

The "72% greater efficiency" and "2.8x faster" numbers are Microsoft's own, with no benchmark
definition, prompt set, or hardware stated. They are recorded as claims.

## Gaps

- No prices, no per-image cost, no token accounting.
- No model size, resolution ceiling, or context limit (those are in the model card and Foundry docs).
- The post does not say which Arena board, snapshot, or sample count backs the "#2" claim.
- Nothing on GA timing or on regional availability.

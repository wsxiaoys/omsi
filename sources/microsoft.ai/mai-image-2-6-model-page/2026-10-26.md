## Summary

The product page confirms 2.6 as a **named, first-party product with its own page, model card and
Foundry entry**, not a leaderboard-only label. It is also the page where the two 2.6-only features
are marketed — web grounding and multi-reference editing with "up to five visual references" — and
where the "up to 1.5K resolution" figure comes from (Foundry expresses the same limit as 2,359,296
total pixels).

Two date-relevant observations:

- The 2.5 product URL now **redirects to 2.6**, so Microsoft's site treats 2.6 as the current
  flagship of this line.
- The Elo figures plotted here (2.6 = 1324, 2.5 = 1301) do **not** match the Artificial Analysis
  leaderboards captured the same day (2.6 = 1166 text-to-image, 1151 editing; 2.5 = 1118 / 1127).
  The vendor chart is an image-edit-arena snapshot with no date; treat its absolute Elo as
  unverifiable and use only its price axis, which does reconcile with Azure meters.

## Summary

This inventory is the cleanest side-by-side of the 2.6 and 2.5 families and the strongest evidence
that 2.6 is a **distinct SKU with its own parameter set**: only the 2.6 rows expose
`auto_aspect_ratio` and `web_grounding`, and only the 2.6 rows raise the pixel ceiling from
1,048,576 to 2,359,296. Everything else — text + up to five images in, one PNG out, 32K context,
English only, no tool calling — is shared with 2.5.

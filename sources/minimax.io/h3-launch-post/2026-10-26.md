# MiniMax: "MiniMax H3: An Open Model Breaking the Boundaries Between Tasks and Modalities"

- Origin: MiniMax research blog, published **2026-07-31**,
  <https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-h3>
- Evidence kind: official announcement / vendor research post
- Publication date: 2026-07-31
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: MiniMax H3 (base model for H3 Max); no mention of H3 Max

## Key claims (verbatim)

> Today, we're launching MiniMax H3, a general-purpose multimodal generation model. H3 understands
> unified context across text, images, video, and audio, generating video with native stereo sound,
> up to 15 seconds at 2K resolution.

> Early testing shows H3 is ready for commercial content creation across a wide range of use cases,
> excelling at instruction following, accurate text and brand rendering, and V2V motion transfer.

> We offer 2K resolution by default. At 2K, H3's per-second price is less than a third of mainstream
> models, and at 768p, it's less than half the price of mainstream models' 720p.

> To support the open-source community, accelerate compatibility with a broader range of AI hardware,
> and make it easier for users to build their own customized versions, **we plan to open up the model
> weights in the coming days**, subject to applicable laws and regulations.

Audio and multi-shot, from the pretraining-paradigm section:

> Text-to-video — With jointly generated audio, all audio output is native stereo — Native multi-shot
> modeling

Technical modules named in the post: H3-Contextual Omni Representation, H3-VAE, H3-Omni Transformer,
In-Context Regeneration. On captioning: "Most source material requires around 100K tokens of
inference, distilled down to an average of roughly 4K tokens."

Positioning against the Hailuo line:

> We previously developed two generations of models: Hailuo 01 built the system from the ground up,
> and Hailuo 02 focused on improving core components like architectural efficiency, data quality,
> and scale.

Hands-on link given: `https://hailuoai.video/zh-Intl/tools/minimax-h3`.

## What this establishes

- **H3's launch date is 2026-07-31** — inside the roster's one-year window (≥ 2025-10-26).
- H3's launch envelope: unified text/image/video/audio context, native stereo audio,
  up to 15 s, 2K.
- H3 is an **open-weights** release ("we plan to open up the model weights"), which is what makes
  a third-party post-trained derivative (H3 Max) technically possible at all.
- The post is a **vendor performance claim**, not a measurement: "less than a third" and "less than
  half the price of mainstream models" have no named comparators, and the quality claims
  ("industry-leading", "excelling at") carry no benchmark numbers in the captured text.

## Gaps

- No mention of **H3 Max**, of fal, or of any post-training partnership. The derivative is not
  announced here.
- No latency/throughput figure for H3, so fal's "~35x the throughput of the official MiniMax H3
  endpoint" cannot be checked against a vendor number.
- The post promises weights "in the coming days"; the weights repository was created 2026-07-28
  and last modified 2026-08-13 (see the Hugging Face record), so the promise was kept within weeks,
  but the post itself carries no date for it.

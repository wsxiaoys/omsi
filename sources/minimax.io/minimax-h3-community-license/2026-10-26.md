### Other operative clauses

- **Derivative definition covers distillation.** "Model Derivatives" includes any model "created by
  transferring the patterns of the weights, parameters, operational patterns, or Outputs of MiniMax H3
  … including by distillation methods … or methods based on training using synthetic-data Outputs".
- **No use of outputs to improve other models.** "You may not use the MiniMax H3 Works or any of their
  Outputs or results to improve any other artificial intelligence model (other than MiniMax H3 or its
  Model Derivatives)."
- **Output ownership.** "MiniMax claims no rights over the Outputs you generate." Outputs are not
  Model Derivatives.
- **Redistribution duties.** Provide the agreement to recipients; mark modified files; add a NOTICE
  file reading "MiniMax H3 is licensed under the MiniMax H3 Community License Agreement, Copyright ©
  2026 MiniMax. All Rights Reserved."; "Powered by MiniMax H3" attribution is *encouraged* in Section
  III but *required* for commercial products in Section IV.
- **Hosted services must implement safeguards** against infringing or AUP-violating uses and outputs,
  bind downstream users to terms at least as protective, and maintain a reporting mechanism.
- **Patent/ litigation termination.** Asserting an IP suit against MiniMax terminates all licences.
- **No support obligation**, no warranties, and an indemnity running from licensee to MiniMax.
- **Acceptable Use Policy (Exhibit A)** — 20 numbered prohibitions, including use outside the
  Applicable Territory; non-disclosure of machine-generated content in public environments; impersonation
  without consent; high-risk automated decisions in critical domains; and "Use for military purposes".

## Summary

The weights are genuinely downloadable and open in the sense of "you can run and fine-tune them", but
the licence is **not** an open-source licence and it is **not** usable as one:

- Rights are granted only in the **Applicable Territory**, which excludes the **EU, UK, South Korea and
  the United States**. In those four jurisdictions the agreement grants nothing and the only route is a
  request form whose outcome MiniMax controls.
- Commercial use above **US$20M annual revenue** requires prior written authorisation regardless of
  territory.
- Distillation into other models is prohibited, and outputs may not be used to improve any model other
  than H3 itself.

The licence file's own date — 2026-08-02 — is the only first-party date for the weight release, three
days after the 2026-07-31 API launch.

## Gaps

- No explanation of what the Excluded Territory applicants receive, how long review takes, or on what
  criteria applications are granted or refused.
- No statement of how "use outside the Applicable Territory" is meant to apply to a hosted service
  whose end users are in an excluded territory; the Hosted Services clauses and the territory clause
  interact and neither resolves the case.
- No pricing, tiering, or commercial-licence terms are published by MiniMax itself; the paid commercial
  licence is only described by a third party (see the Comfy record).
- The licence does not name the Qwen3-VL-32B text encoder's own licence, although the model card says
  the encoder uses "the full pretrained weights of Qwen3-VL-32B".

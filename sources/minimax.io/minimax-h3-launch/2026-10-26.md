# MiniMax H3: An Open Model Breaking the Boundaries Between Tasks and Modalities

- Origin: MiniMax (publisher), research blog post, <https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-h3>
- Evidence kind: official announcement (vendor blog post)
- Publication date: 2026-07-31 (printed under the title)
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: MiniMax H3
- Capture type: full text snapshot, key passages quoted verbatim

## Captured content

### Launch claims (verbatim)

> Today, we're launching MiniMax H3, a general-purpose multimodal generation model. H3 understands
> unified context across text, images, video, and audio, generating video with native stereo sound,
> up to 15 seconds at 2K resolution. For a quick hands-on experience, please visit
> https://hailuoai.video/zh-Intl/tools/minimax-h3.

> Early testing shows H3 is ready for commercial content creation across a wide range of use cases,
> excelling at instruction following, accurate text and brand rendering, and V2V motion transfer.

> Powered by technologies including Contextual Omni Representation, H3-VAE, H3-Omni Transformer, and
> In-Context Regeneration, H3 delivers industry-leading price-performance. We offer 2K resolution by
> default. At 2K, H3's per-second price is less than a third of mainstream models, and at 768p, it's
> less than half the price of mainstream models' 720p.

> To support the open-source community, accelerate compatibility with a broader range of AI hardware,
> and make it easier for users to build their own customized versions, we plan to open up the model
> weights in the coming days, subject to applicable laws and regulations.

### Lineage and naming (verbatim)

> We previously developed two generations of models: Hailuo 01 built the system from the ground up,
> and Hailuo 02 focused on improving core components like architectural efficiency, data quality, and
> scale.

> From Hailuo 01 and Hailuo 02 to H3, each generation has been an order of magnitude more complex to
> build than the last.

> Notably, we set aside the Hailuo-02 architecture, despite the significant architectural advantages it
> once gave us, because it would introduce unnecessary complexity for a model built around task
> generalization.

The post calls H3 the third generation of the Hailuo video line but never writes the name "Hailuo 3".

### Tasks and modalities claimed

The post's pretraining-paradigm list, verbatim in structure: text-to-image; text-to-video; "With
jointly generated audio, all audio output is native stereo"; "Native multi-shot modeling";
text-to-audio; "No separation between voice, sound effects, and music — all jointly modeled";
"Generalized reference and editing" covering image-to-image, image-to-video, audio-to-audio and
audio-video-to-audio-video reference/editing.

Reference relationships are expressed in natural language rather than a fixed task set; the example
given is a prompt that says "Reference the Hitchcock camera movement from Video 1, have the character
in Image 2 sing, with the vocals matching Audio 3."

### Architecture claims (verbatim, selected)

- H3-Contextual Omni Representation: "Most source material requires around 100K tokens of inference,
  distilled down to an average of roughly 4K tokens."
- H3-VAE: "a 4x gain in effective sequence length, substantially cutting training and inference
  costs, and it's the key technology behind our native 2K resolution support."
- H3-In-context Regeneration: "For H3's 2K output, instead of using a conventional dedicated
  super-resolution module, we have the H3 base model regenerate its own low-resolution output
  in-context."

### Self-stated gaps (verbatim)

> H3 still has room to grow, and here are our priorities for future versions: […] In the next
> generation of the H series, we plan to integrate capabilities from our M-series models. […] H3's
> current model size leaves room for improvement across several capabilities. […] Visual detail can
> still be improved in certain scenarios.

> We'll be sharing the full H3 Technical Report soon.

## Summary

The post dates the model: **MiniMax H3 launched 2026-07-31**. It frames H3 as an omni-modal
generation system (video + native stereo audio from text, image, video and audio context) rather than
a text-to-video model, positions 2K as the default output, claims a large price advantage over
"mainstream models" without naming them or giving numbers, and announces that weights would be
released "in the coming days". It describes H3 as the successor to Hailuo 01 and Hailuo 02 while
dropping the "Hailuo" name.

## Gaps

- No benchmark numbers, no named comparison set, no methodology for the "less than a third" / "less
  than half" price claims.
- No frame rate, audio sample rate, language list, duration floor, or resolution ladder — those appear
  only in the model card and API reference captured separately.
- "Native multi-shot modeling" is asserted with no shot count or control surface.
- The promised technical report was not available on arXiv at capture time (arXiv API returned HTTP
  429 on repeated attempts; no report was located by other means).
- No date is given for the weight release; the licence file dates it 2026-08-02.

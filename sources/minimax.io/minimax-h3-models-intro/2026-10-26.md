## Summary

This is the single most useful identity document captured. It settles two questions:

1. **H3 is a video model**, described first-party as a "general-purpose multimodal video model".
2. **H3 Max is not a MiniMax tier of H3** — it is "post-trained by fal.ai on MiniMax H3, optimized
   for high-speed generation". MiniMax attributes the derivative to fal, and gives it a narrower
   surface: 480P/768P only, 5–15 s (no 4 s), text-to-video and first/last-frame image-to-video only
   (no multimodal reference), 24 fps.

It also shows the naming break: the previous generation is still listed as "MiniMax Hailuo 2.3" and
"MiniMax Hailuo 02" under **Legacy Models**, while the successor is "MiniMax H3" with no "Hailuo"
prefix. The page does not state that H3 replaces Hailuo 2.3, but the Legacy heading is a
first-party demotion of the Hailuo line.

## Gaps

- No launch dates on this page (dates are in the release-notes page and the blog post).
- No pricing, no input limits, no licence information.
- `MiniMax-H3-Highspeed` and `MiniMax-H3-Lite`, which the API reference and price list both expose,
  are **absent from this list**. The model list is therefore incomplete as of capture.
- The page says H3 Max supports 480P/768P; fal's own H3 Max schema adds a 1080P option (see the fal
  record). Not reconciled here.

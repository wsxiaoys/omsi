## Summary

The vendor's own changelog dates MiniMax H3 to **2026-07-31**, matching the blog post. It has no
entry for `MiniMax-H3-Highspeed`, `MiniMax-H3-Lite` or `MiniMax-H3-Max`, even though all three appear
in the API reference and price list by capture date. There is therefore **no first-party release date
for H3 Max**; the earliest observable date for it is fal's endpoint publication (2026-08-26 per the
fal catalogue record).

The changelog also fixes the predecessor dates: Hailuo 2.3 on 2025-10-28 and Hailuo 02 on 2025-06-18,
both before the roster's one-year window.

## Gaps

- Undated variants (Highspeed, Lite, Max, Regeneration, Context-IR) have no entries.
- No deprecation notices for Hailuo 2.3 / 02 appear here, although the Models page files them under
  "Legacy Models".
- The page gives no capability detail; it is a date index, not a specification.

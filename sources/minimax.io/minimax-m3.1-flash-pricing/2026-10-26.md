## Summary

- **M3.1 Flash is priced flat**: $0.15 in / $0.60 out / $0.015 cached input per million tokens,
  with no input-length tiers and no Priority tier shown.
- **M3 is priced by input length** (≤512k vs >512k) with a **permanent 50% discount** against
  struck-through list prices, and a 1.5× Priority tier. Its cheapest M3 rate ($0.30/$1.20) is
  exactly twice M3.1 Flash's, and its >512k rate four times.
- The cache read rate is 10% of the input rate in both cases ($0.015 vs $0.15; $0.06 vs $0.30),
  i.e. a 90% cache discount — matching Artificial Analysis's "Cache Discount 90%"
  ([AA](../../artificialanalysis.ai/minimax-m3.1-flash/2026-10-26.md)).
- Billing is per token; the page's own conversion note is ~750 English words per 1,000 tokens.

## Gaps

- No statement of what happens to M3.1 Flash billing above 512k input tokens; the absence of a
  tier row implies a flat rate across the full 1M window, but the page does not say so.
- No cache **write** or cache **storage** price is listed for any LLM, although OpenRouter
  lists only a read price as well.
- No batch price, no free tier, no per-request minimum, no refund/expiry terms on this page.
- The M3 strike-through prices and the "permanent 50% off" wording are the vendor's own; no
  earlier capture of this page exists in the wiki to date the discount.

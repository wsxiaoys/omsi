Thinking section, verbatim excerpts:

> MiniMax-M3.1-Flash reasons before it answers […] Thinking is **on by default and needs no
> configuration**.
>
> Thinking content comes back separately from the answer: on the OpenAI-compatible protocol,
> thinking is always returned on `reasoning_content` while `content` holds only the final
> answer […]
>
> `effort` accepts `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`, and `max`. […] For `MiniMax-M3.1-Flash`,

Per-protocol field names, verbatim:

| Protocol | Thinking depth field | Where thinking content is returned | Output limit field |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anthropic-compatible | `output_config.effort` | `thinking` content block | `max_tokens` |
| OpenAI-compatible | `reasoning_effort` | `reasoning_content` field | `max_tokens` / `max_completion_tokens` |
| OpenAI Responses | `reasoning.effort` | `type: "reasoning"` output item | `max_output_tokens` |

Limitation, verbatim:

> Thinking cannot be turned off. Sending `thinking: {"type": "disabled"}` or `effort: "none"`
> returns `400`:
>
> `model "MiniMax-M3.1-Flash" requires adaptive thinking`
>
> To reduce the latency and token usage of thinking, lower the `effort` level rather than
> trying to disable thinking.

## Summary

- First-party confirmation of the two-model LLM line: `MiniMax-M3.1-Flash` and `MiniMax-M3`,
  both at a **1,000,000-token** context window.
- MiniMax's own one-line description calls M3.1 Flash "an ultra-efficient **open-weight**
  model for everyday work" — the vendor, not a host, asserts open weights.
- Thinking is **mandatory**: only its depth is tunable. `disabled`/`none` is rejected with a
  400. This is a documented behavioural difference from M3, whose card documents
  `enabled`/`adaptive`/`disabled` (see
  [M3 model card](../../huggingface.co/minimax-m3-model-card/2026-10-26.md)).
- Two compatible surfaces, Anthropic-style (recommended, supports thinking blocks and
  interleaved thinking) and OpenAI-style.

## Gaps

- No price, no parameter count, no licence, no rate limits on this page.
- No per-modality input limits (image size, video length, fps) — those are in the chat API
  reference, not here.
- "1,000,000" is a round number; the page does not say whether it is exact or a rounded
  marketing figure. OpenRouter reports 1,048,576 for the same model
  ([host record](../../openrouter.ai/minimax-m3.1-flash/2026-10-26.md)).
- No statement of what `effort` does to price or to output-token limits.

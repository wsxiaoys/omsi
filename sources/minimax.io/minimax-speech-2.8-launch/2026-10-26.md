## What this establishes

- **Launch date 2026-01-23** for Speech 2.8, from the vendor's own dated post. This is inside the
  roster's one-year window (≥ 2025-10-26).
- The vendor's three headline claims are **sound tags** (interjections/fillers rendered natively),
  **10-second voice cloning**, and **noise/artifact reduction**. All three are qualitative and
  paired with demo audio, not with measurements.
- The post names **no model IDs, no tier split, no prices, no language count and no output
  formats.** Everything specific about `speech-2.8-hd` vs `speech-2.8-turbo` comes from other
  pages; the launch post cannot be used to distinguish HD from Turbo.
- Cross-lingual work is scoped narrowly and prospectively: "Starting with our Mandarin-Japanese
  pair" and "Stay tuned as we bring this seamless experience to even more languages soon." That is
  weaker than a general accent-removal claim.
- The sound-tag demo uses `(chuckle)`, `(breath)`, `(clear-throat)`, `(laughs)` — a subset of the
  19 tags the T2A API reference documents.

## Gaps

- No price, latency, language count, sample rate, character limit, or watermark statement.
- No statement of what changed technically between 2.6 and 2.8 beyond the three headline claims.
- No HD/Turbo distinction, and no indication whether the demos are HD.
- The "10-second sample" figure is not reconciled with the 10-second **minimum** upload enforced by
  the voice-clone API (see `../minimax-speech-2.8-voice-clone/2026-10-26.md`).

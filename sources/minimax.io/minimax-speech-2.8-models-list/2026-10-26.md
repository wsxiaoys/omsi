## What this establishes

- **The 2.8 tier split is vendor-documented, not a host label:** `speech-2.8-hd` is "Ultra-realistic
  quality featuring sound tags" and `speech-2.8-turbo` is "Seamless speed meets natural flow". Both
  are listed as current on both pages.
- **Language coverage ladder:** 24 languages for speech-02, 40 for speech-2.6 and speech-2.8.
- **The two vendor pages disagree about 2.6 and 02.** The overview calls them "Legacy Models"; the
  Model List presents them as "Available Models" and its "Retired Models" table contains no speech
  entry. Both were read on the same day. This is a genuine vendor contradiction, not a stale capture
  of one page.
- **The "7 emotions" count appears only here.** The API reference enumerates nine emotion values and
  states that 2.8 does not support `whisper` (see `../minimax-speech-2.8-t2a-api/2026-10-26.md`).
  Seven is consistent with "nine enumerated minus `fluent` minus `whisper`", but no page says so.
- The overview's 40-language figure matches the 40 named `language_boost` values exactly; speech-02's
  24 does not map to any enumerated subset captured here.

## Gaps

- Neither page gives prices, character limits, output formats, cloning support or release dates.
- No statement of what "specified languages and dialects supported" means operationally.
- The overview gives no retirement date or support window for the legacy speech rows.
- The pages do not say whether `speech-2.8-hd` and `speech-2.8-turbo` are the same weights at
  different serving budgets or different checkpoints.

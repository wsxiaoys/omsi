## Voice Management (verbatim)

| API | Description | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Voice Design** | Generate a voice (voice_id) from a natural-language description; usable in both sync and async T2A endpoints. | \$3 per voice | Charged when the designed voice is first used for synthesis, not at generation time. In-API preview synthesis is billed at \$60 / M characters. |
| **Rapid Voice Cloning** | LLM-powered voice cloning that produces a high-fidelity replica in seconds, without long high-quality reference audio. | \$1.5 per voice | Charged when the cloned voice is first used for synthesis, not at cloning time. The billing guide states pay-as-you-go is "All prices are in USD" and that subscription coverage is
"All speech models".

## What this establishes

- **Billing is per input character.** 2.8 HD is \$100 per million characters = **\$0.10 per 1,000
  characters**; 2.8 Turbo is \$60/M = \$0.06/1K. Sync and async are priced identically.
- **2.8 is price-neutral against 2.6 and 02.** The legacy block quotes the same \$100/\$60 split for
  `speech-2.6-hd`/`speech-02-hd` and their turbo siblings. So the tier premium is HD vs Turbo, not
  new vs old.
- **Cloning and voice design are charged per stored voice, not per upload:** \$1.5 per cloned voice
  and \$3 per designed voice, each billed on the voice's **first synthesis** — cloning itself is
  free. Preview text is billed as ordinary characters.
- **Voice capacity is plan-limited** by "voice slots" (10 → 800), which is the vendor's only stated
  cap on how many cloned/designed voices an account can hold.
- Async T2A accepts "up to 1M characters per request" — 100× the sync limit — at the same per-
  character price.

## Gaps

- No minimum charge, rounding rule, or statement about whether failed requests bill.
- The page does not say whether interjection tags or `<#x#>` pause markers count as billable
  characters (the API reference reports `usage_characters` separately from `word_count`).
- No enterprise/custom pricing, no regional price differences, no audio-point-to-character
  conversion rate.
- No date on any price; the capture date is the only anchor.

## Speech table (verbatim)

| T2A | speech-2.8-turbo/hd speech-2.6-turbo/hd speech-02-turbo/hd | 60 | —— | —— |
| Voice Cloning | —— | 60 | —— | —— |
| Voice Design | —— | 20 | —— | —— |
| Speech to Text | asr-1.0 | —— | 2 | 30,000 |

> Speech to Text is available to **paid accounts only** and is not accessible on the free tier.

## What this establishes

- **60 requests per minute** is the default ceiling for T2A, shared across every current speech model
  and both 2.8 tiers. There is no published TPM (character-per-minute) cap for T2A at all — the TPM
  column is empty.
- The Audio Subscription plans sell higher RPM (10 → 800) alongside voice slots, so the 60 RPM figure
  is the pay-as-you-go default rather than a hard platform limit.
- Voice Design (20 RPM) is a third of the synthesis rate, which matters for pipelines that generate
  voices on the fly.

## Gaps

- The page does not say whether the 60 RPM is per account, per API key or per model, nor whether the
  limit is shared across models in the same row.
- No burst allowance, queue depth, or back-off guidance.
- No separate limit for the async T2A route, which the pricing page says accepts up to 1M characters
  per request.

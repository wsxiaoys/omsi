## What this establishes

- **Speech 2.8 = 2026-01-23**, corroborated independently of the marketing post by the dated
  changelog entry, with the same one-line description ("Natural Sound Tags, lifelike voice, pristine
  audio quality").
- Speech lineage on the vendor changelog: Speech-02 (Jun 2025) → Speech 2.5 (2025-08-06) →
  Speech 2.6 (2025-10-29) → Speech 2.8 (2026-01-23). Speech 2.6 lands three days after the roster's
  window start, so it is also in-window; Speech 2.5 and Speech-02 are not.
- There is **no entry after 2026-01-23 for any speech model** — no 2.9, no HD/Turbo split, no
  deprecation notice for 2.6. The changelog therefore says nothing about whether 2.6 is retired.

## Gaps

- No model IDs, tiers, prices or retirement dates in these entries.
- The changelog does not record the HD/Turbo distinction, so it cannot be used to date
  `speech-2.8-turbo` separately.
- "Jun. 2025" is month-granular only; the Speech-02 launch day is not published here.

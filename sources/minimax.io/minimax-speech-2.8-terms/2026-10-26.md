## What this establishes

- **MiniMax does not claim to watermark its own output.** No captured MiniMax document states that
  generated audio carries a watermark; the only watermark control found anywhere in the speech
  surface is `aigc_watermark` on `/v1/voice_clone`, default `false` (see
  `../minimax-speech-2.8-voice-clone/2026-10-26.md`). The marking duty is instead pushed onto the
  developer: "You are required to adopt technical measures to add non-intrusive identifiers … and
  place a prominent mark".
- This is the **inverse of ElevenLabs' policy**, where an inaudible watermark is embedded by the
  vendor and cannot be disabled. A compliance pipeline that assumed vendor-side provenance does not
  transfer to MiniMax.
- **Outputs are owned by the customer, but also used by the vendor.** The licence is explicitly
  "you retain your ownership rights", with a broad grant to MiniMax to "use the input and generated
  content to provide, maintain, develop, and improve our Services". No opt-out is described on this
  page.
- **Resale is restricted** to "integrated applications" — a multi-tenant TTS API on top of MiniMax
  needs a commercial arrangement, not just a paid account.
- Contracting entity is **Nanonoble Pte. Ltd. (Singapore)**, with SIAC arbitration.

## Gaps

- The page is undated, so it is not known when these clauses took effect.
- No speech-specific clause: nothing about voice-cloning consent, impersonation, or likeness rights
  beyond the general content warranties.
- No data-retention period for synthesis requests, and no zero-retention or EU/US data-residency
  option on this page.
- Nothing on whether the marking obligation is enforced technically or only contractually.

## What this establishes

- MiniMax's own model catalogue lists **two current video models**: MiniMax H3 and MiniMax H3 Max.
  H3 Max is not a host-only label; the vendor documents it, describes it, and publishes its
  resolution/duration/fps envelope.
- The vendor's own description of the relationship is explicit: H3 Max is "post-trained by fal.ai
  on MiniMax H3, optimized for high-speed generation". So the differentiator is **speed**, and the
  derivative author is **fal**, not MiniMax.
- MiniMax calls H3 "open" ("Next-gen open general-purpose multimodal video model"); it does not
  call H3 Max open.
- The H3 Max feature list here is the narrowest of any MiniMax document captured: Text-to-Video and
  Image-to-Video (first / last frame) only, with no "Multimodal reference" bullet, unlike H3. This
  contradicts the video-generation guide and the pricing page, which both list reference-to-video
  for H3 Max — see `../video-generation-guide/2026-10-26.md` and `../video-pricing/2026-10-26.md`.

## Gaps

- No publication date, changelog entry, or deprecation date is attached to either row.
- The page does not say whether H3 Max is served outside MiniMax's own API, nor on what terms.
- No audio specification for either model (sample rate, channels) appears on this page.
- The frame rate (24 fps) is given without qualification for both models; no page captured states
  whether H3 Max's output is re-timed by the host.

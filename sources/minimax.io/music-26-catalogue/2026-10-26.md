Note that the table lists **`music-2.6` and `music-cover` as separate rows** — cover is a sibling

## What this establishes

- The vendor's catalogue at the capture date still treats `music-2.6` as a listed model (not in the
  page's "Legacy Models" block, which contains only `music-2.0`), while its own Model List page calls
  every music model retired. Both are first-party, captured the same day; the contradiction is
  preserved rather than resolved.
- The vendor's own summary of what 2.6 changed is two words of marketing ("Cover Reborn. Bass
  Redefined.") that match the two technical claims in the launch post — cover generation and reworked
  low end (`../music-26-launch/2026-10-26.md`).
- `music-2.5` and `music-2.5+` are absent from this page entirely, although both are priced in the
  pricing table and retired in the model list. Lineage must be assembled across three pages.

## Gaps

- No statement of what "Cover Reborn" means technically.

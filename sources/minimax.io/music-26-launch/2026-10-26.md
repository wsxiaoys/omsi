## Summary

- Music 2.6 launched **2026-04-10** as a "global creative beta", not a GA announcement.
- The four capability claims the vendor makes for 2.6 over its predecessor are: **cover generation
  (new)**, **BPM/key/structure/emotional-arc instruction control**, **reworked low end**, and
  **first-packet latency under 20 seconds**.
- No song-length, language, sample-rate, pricing or licensing statement appears in this post.

The page's "Available Models" sections are LLM, Video, Speech and Image only — **there is no Music
section among the available models**, so on this page every music model, including the current
Music 3.0, is retired.

The page's banner describes a separate retirement schedule for the M2.5/M2.7 language models
(2026-10-19 no new users, 2026-11-18 retirement); no equivalent dated schedule is given for music.

## What this establishes

- At the capture date MiniMax's own model list places `music-2.6` in **Retired**, alongside every
  other music model.
- This contradicts the same vendor's `Models` overview page, which still lists `music-3.0`,
  `music-2.6` and `music-cover` in a Music table with descriptions
  (`../music-26-catalogue/2026-10-26.md`), and its guide, which says only *new* users are excluded
  (`../music-generation-guide/2026-10-26.md`). The disagreement is about **who** can still call it,
  not about whether the model exists: hosts still serve it (see the fal and Replicate records).
- No retirement date is published for music models, unlike the M2.5/M2.7 schedule on the same page.

## Gaps

- No dates for any music retirement.
- No migration mapping (e.g. "music-2.6 users should move to X").
- The list gives no indication that `music-2.6` remains available through third-party hosts.

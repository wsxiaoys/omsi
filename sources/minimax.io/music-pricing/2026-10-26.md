## What this establishes

- The billing basis is **per track of up to 5 minutes**, not per second and not per minute:
  "$0.15/up-to-5 minutes music". A 30-second track and a 5-minute track cost the same.
- This is the vendor's only explicit statement of a maximum song length for Music 2.6: **5 minutes**.
- Music 2.6 is labelled **Discontinued** at the capture date, in the same table that labels Music 3.0
  discontinued. Music 2.0 was five times cheaper ($0.03) and Music 2.5/2.5+ already cost $0.15.
- Music 2.5+ is described as "Instrumental unlocked", which places instrumental generation before
  2.6 rather than in it.

## Gaps

- "Up-to-5 minutes" is a billing unit, not a documented generation cap; no page states what happens
  to a request that would exceed it.
- No statement of when each model became Discontinued, or whether grandfathered users keep the same
  price.
- No enterprise or volume pricing for music.
- The page does not say whether failed or flagged generations bill.

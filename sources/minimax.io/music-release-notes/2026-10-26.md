## What this establishes

- The music lineage with the vendor's own dates: Music-1.5 (Beta) 2025-06-20 → Music-1.5 2025-09-11
  → Music-2.0 2025-10-29 → Music-2.5 2026-01-16 → Music-2.5+ 2026-03 → **Music-2.6 2026-04** →
  Music-3.0 2026-07-16 (per this page).
- Music 2.6's release-notes date is only month-granular ("Apr. 2026"); the exact day, 2026-04-10,
  comes from the launch post it links to (`../music-26-launch/2026-10-26.md`).
- Music 2.6 was the **current** music model for roughly three months, and has been superseded since
  July/August 2026.
- Song length has changed across the line: Music-1.5 is announced as supporting "4-minute songs";
  Music 2.6 has no length statement here.
- Music 2.6 is inside the roster's one-year window (≥ 2025-10-26) by construction; Music 2.0
  (2025-10-29) is the earliest music entry inside the window.

## Gaps

- **Music 3.0 date conflict:** this page says Jul. 16, 2026; the vendor's own Music 3.0 blog post is
  dated 2026-08-13, and fal's catalogue date for `minimax/music-3` is 2026-08-13. Unresolved.
- No entry announces `music-cover` as a separate model, and no entry records the music retirement.
- No release note describes the Music 2.6 API surface, limits, or price.
- Release-notes entries are marketing taglines; none carries a benchmark or technical detail.

## Endpoint and model enum (verbatim)

> Currently supported models: `MiniMax-H3`, `MiniMax-H3-Highspeed`, `MiniMax-H3-Lite`,
> `MiniMax-H3-Max`.

`model` enum: `MiniMax-H3`, `MiniMax-H3-Highspeed`, `MiniMax-H3-Lite`, `MiniMax-H3-Max`.

The endpoint description in the header reads: "switch between MiniMax H3, MiniMax H3 Highspeed,
MiniMax H3 Lite, and MiniMax H3 Max with the `model` field to support text-to-video, image-to-video
(first & last frame), and reference-to-video, with output up to 2K."

## Per-model descriptions (verbatim)

> - **`MiniMax-H3`**: **the variant for high-quality video creation.** Supports text-to-video,
>   image-to-video (first / last frame), and reference-to-video; `768P` / `2K` resolution, 4–15s
>   duration.
> - **`MiniMax-H3-Highspeed`**: **balances generation quality and speed.** Supports text-to-video,
>   image-to-video (first / last frame), and reference-to-video (reference image / video / audio);
>   `768P` resolution, **`2K` is not supported**; 4–15s duration.
> - **`MiniMax-H3-Lite`**: **the lightweight variant.** Supports text-to-video, image-to-video
>   (first / last frame), and reference-to-video (reference image / video / audio); `480P` / `768P`
>   resolution, **`2K` is not supported**; 4–15s duration.
> - **`MiniMax-H3-Max`**: **the fast generation variant.** Supports text-to-video, image-to-video
>   (first / last frame), and reference-to-video (reference image / video / audio); `480P` / `768P`
>   resolution, **`2K` is not supported**; 5–15s duration.

## `resolution` (verbatim)

> Video resolution. Availability differs by model:
>
> - **`MiniMax-H3`**: `768P`, `2K`
> - **`MiniMax-H3-Highspeed`**: `768P` (`2K` is not supported)
> - **`MiniMax-H3-Lite`**: `480P`, `768P` (defaults to `768P`; `2K` is not supported)
> - **`MiniMax-H3-Max`**: `480P`, `768P` (defaults to `768P`; `2K` is not supported)

Enum: `480P`, `768P`, `2K`.

## `duration` (verbatim)

> Duration of the generated video in seconds. Required, integer. Available values differ by model:
>
> - **`MiniMax-H3`**: `4`–`15`
> - **`MiniMax-H3-Highspeed`**: `4`–`15`
> - **`MiniMax-H3-Lite`**: `4`–`15`
> - **`MiniMax-H3-Max`**: `5`–`15` (4 seconds is not supported)

Enum: 4 … 15 (integers).

## `extra` — the only H3-Max-exclusive parameter (verbatim)

> Additional generation options for `MiniMax-H3-Max`. Currently only `prompt_expansion_mode` is
> supported. If `extra` or this property is omitted, `balanced` is used. Do not pass `balance`, an
> empty string, a Boolean value, or other undeclared fields.

`extra.prompt_expansion_mode` enum: `disabled`, `balanced` (default), `quality` — "`disabled`
disables prompt expansion; `balanced` uses balanced expansion and is the default; `quality`
prioritizes expansion quality."

## `ratio` (verbatim, relevant part)

> **Image-to-video (i2va, content contains a `first_frame` / `last_frame` image)**: the aspect ratio
> is determined by the input image and `ratio` is always `adaptive`; passing another valid value
> does not error but is ignored and treated as `adaptive`.

Ratio enum: `adaptive`, `21:9`, `16:9`, `4:3`, `1:1`, `3:4`, `9:16`.

## What this establishes

- MiniMax's own API gives H3 Max its **own model ID (`MiniMax-H3-Max`), its own resolution and
  duration envelope, and its own exclusive parameter** (`extra.prompt_expansion_mode`). It is a
  selectable variant, not a routing label.
- The vendor's framing is **speed**, not quality: H3 is "the variant for high-quality video
  creation"; H3 Max is "the fast generation variant". The word "Max" therefore does not mean
  "highest quality" on this vendor's taxonomy — it means "fastest of the H3 family", which is the
  opposite of what "Max" means in most other families.
- The H3 family has four tiers: H3 (quality, 2K), H3-Highspeed (768P), H3-Lite (480P/768P),
  H3-Max (480P/768P, fast). H3 Max and H3-Lite have the *same* resolution envelope; the difference
  documented here is speed plus the prompt-expansion control.
- The OpenAPI `info.description` labels the whole V2 API "Hailuo-03", so H3's internal family name
  is Hailuo 03.

## Gaps

- No frame rate, audio format, or latency figure in the schema.
- No statement of which H3 checkpoint or revision H3-Max was post-trained from.
- `prompt_expansion_mode` is documented as H3-Max-only, but no page explains what expansion does to
  the output beyond "rewriting the prompt".

## What this establishes

- On MiniMax's own price list, **H3 Max is not a premium tier**. It sits in a separate
  "Extended Models" section, not the "Flagship Models" section, and at 768P it costs **exactly the
  same as flagship H3** ($0.08/s) while offering no 2K option.
- H3 Max is **more expensive than every other non-flagship H3 tier**: $0.08/s vs $0.06
  (Highspeed) and $0.04 (Lite) at 768P. Its input-video rate ($0.143/s) is also the highest in the
  family.
- H3 Max's free image allowance is smaller than the flagship tiers' — 2 images vs 5 — and its
  per-image price is higher ($0.074 vs $0.04).
- The "Max" suffix therefore prices at **parity with H3 at 768P**, and the differentiator a buyer
  is paying for is generation speed, not resolution or (on this evidence) quality.
- This page lists Reference (image, video, audio) for H3-Max, agreeing with the video-generation
  guide and contradicting the models-overview page's T2V/I2V-only feature list.

## Gaps

- No latency or throughput figure is attached to the H3-Max price, so the price/speed trade-off
  cannot be computed from this page alone.
- No subscription/pack pricing for H3-Max was captured (the page has a separate "Video Packages"
  section that was not extracted in full).
- The `MiniMax-H3-Context-IR` row's unit (per call, per 1k tokens, per minute) is not legible in the
  extracted table.

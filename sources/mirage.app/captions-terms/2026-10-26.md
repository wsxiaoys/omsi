## Summary

This is where the consent obligation for avatar and digital-twin work lives: the user warrants **written
consent from every identifiable person** in the input for name, likeness and voice, plus a release, and
takes sole responsibility for any further consents. Mirage separately promises not to commercialise a
user's voice on a standalone basis without written permission, while retaining a broad licence to use input
and output to provide and improve the services.

The page is a warranty, not a verification step: nothing captured says Mirage checks consent before
generating from an uploaded face.

## Gaps

- No verification mechanism, challenge, or attestation flow for likeness consent is described.
- No retention period for uploaded input, and no deletion commitment beyond the privacy policy.
- The interaction between this broad input licence and the API Terms' "Licensee owns Input and Output" was
  not reconciled; they are different documents for different surfaces (app vs. API).

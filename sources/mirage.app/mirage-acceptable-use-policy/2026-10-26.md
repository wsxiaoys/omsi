# Mirage Acceptable Use Policy

- Origin: Mirage (Captions), "Acceptable Use Policy", <https://mirage.app/legal/aup>
- Evidence kind: official policy
- Publication date: "Effective September 4, 2025"
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: all Mirage services, including `mirage-avatar-x`

## Captured content (verbatim, selected)

> You may not use the Services:
>
> - beyond published parameters or use limitations;
> - to violate or to encourage the violation of the legal rights of others;
> - to misrepresent the provenance of generated content by claiming it was created solely by a human, or
>   otherwise in order to deceive;
> - as a means to participate in any disinformation campaign or to create deceptive comments or reviews;
> - In any way that suggests that Mirage endorses you or your Output

> You may not provide Input that:
>
> - contains private or confidential information about another person;
> - violates or infringes the intellectual property rights, rights of publicity, privacy rights or other
>   rights of a third-party;
> - impersonates or misrepresents your affiliation with any person or entity …

> You may not generate Output that:
>
> - relates to, or appears in our sole discretion to relate to or represent, individuals under the age of 18;
> - is designed to harass or bully, intimidate, abuse or insult others;
> - contains sexually explicit content such as pornography;
> - facilitates or contains child sexual abuse or exploitation;
> - tracks or monitors people without their consent;
> - is used as or part of non-fungible tokens (NFTs) or similar digital asset;
> - will be used to create trademarks, design-marks, service-marks or other similar protected or
>   registrable rights.

## Summary

The content policy that governs Avatar X output: no minors, no sexual content, no harassment, no
impersonation, no false provenance claims, no NFT or trademark use, and no use "beyond published
parameters". It restricts *input* that carries third-party publicity or privacy rights, which is the policy
counterpart of the consent warranty in the Captions Terms.

## Gaps

- No enforcement description: no refusal taxonomy, strike system, or appeal path.
- No explicit deepfake/likeness clause beyond "impersonates" and the input rights language.
- "Appears in our sole discretion to relate to … individuals under the age of 18" gives the vendor
  discretion without a stated detection method.

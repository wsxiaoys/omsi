# Mirage: AI model legal terms (third-party providers)

- Origin: Mirage, "AI Model Legal Terms", <https://mirage.app/legal/aimodels>
- Evidence kind: official legal index (vendor statement of third-party model providers)
- Publication date: "Effective September 4, 2025"
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Captions app features that use third-party models; notable for the **absence** of any
  avatar-model provider

## Captured content

The page is a table of third-party providers with links to their terms and privacy policies. Providers
listed, in page order: Black Forest Labs, Cartesia, ElevenLabs, Fal, Google Imagen, Ideogram, Luma, MiniMax,
OpenAI, Pika, Recraft, Soundraw, Stability, Udio. Heading: "Captions Terms Section 6. AI Models".

## Summary

Mirage discloses the third-party models behind app features and lists none for avatars. Combined with the
2026-07-28 launch post ("our newest avatar model … the biggest jump in realism we've ever made"), the
supported reading is that Avatar X is Mirage's own model rather than a third-party engine. The page also
lists **Fal** as a provider, which is consistent with Mirage running model infrastructure on fal.

## Gaps

- The page predates Avatar X (effective 2025-09-04) and may not have been updated for it; absence from this
  list is weak evidence, not proof.
- The "Fal" entry gives no indication of which features or models use fal.
- No entry describes an avatar, lipsync, or digital-twin provider.

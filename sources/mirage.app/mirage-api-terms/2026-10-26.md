## Summary

Commercial API terms that give the customer ownership of Output and Input, forbid distillation and
under-18 end users, require end users to accept equivalent terms, and reserve unlimited revocation and
rate limiting. Two clauses matter for this page's identity question: non-public Mirage APIs are outside
these terms and "would be governed by a separate agreement", and the output grant is subject to "except as
otherwise expressly set forth herein" — so a partner-hosted deployment may carry different terms.

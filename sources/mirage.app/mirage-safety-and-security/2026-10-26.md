## Captured content (verbatim, selected)

> We use both AI-powered and human moderation to actively monitor and prevent misuse, so you can create
> with confidence and peace of mind.

> Stress test our systems. Continuously test against disallowed content so the system learns to refuse it
> before it reaches you.

> Stack defenses at every layer. Layer safeguards at the model, application, and platform levels to block
> nefarious use in real time.

> Mirage is hosted in isolated, US-based Google Cloud data centers, with industry-standard encryption that
> protects your data every step of the way.

> Validate our controls independently. Mirage is SOC 2 compliant, meaning our security and operational
> controls have been independently audited and meet industry standards for protecting customer data.

## Summary

The only captured statement of the moderation stack behind Avatar X: combined AI and human moderation,
layered model/application/platform safeguards, US-based Google Cloud hosting, and SOC 2 compliance. It is a
policy page, not a technical disclosure — no thresholds, categories, or refusal rates are published.

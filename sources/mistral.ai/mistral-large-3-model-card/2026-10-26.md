## Why it is recorded here

Large 3 is the direct predecessor and the model Mistral still sells at roughly a third of Large
4's price. The two are separated on every axis that matters for identity: different API IDs
(`mistral-large-2512` vs `mistral-large-4` / `mistral-large-4-0`), different parameter counts
(675B total / 41B active vs 1.05T / 52B), different context (256k vs 1M), different licence state
(Apache 2.0 published vs "Coming soon"), different lifecycle stage (GA vs Public Preview), and
different prices.

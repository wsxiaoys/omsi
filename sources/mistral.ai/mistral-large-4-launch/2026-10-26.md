# Introducing Mistral Large 4 — launch post

- **Origin:** "Introducing Mistral Large 4", Mistral AI, <https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/>.
  Retrieved 2026-10-26.
- **Evidence kind:** official vendor announcement (first-party).
- **Publication date:** October 6, 2026 (stated on the page).
- **Capture date:** 2026-10-26
- **Relevant models:** Mistral Large 4 (public preview), Mistral Large 3 (comparison only).
- **Capture type:** rendered page text. The benchmark charts are images; only figures printed in
  prose are recorded here.

## Verbatim

> "Today, we're launching a public preview of Mistral Large 4. Unofficially ML4, very
> officially: le Chonk. ML4 pushes the frontier of open-weight performance. You can try the
> preview API today on Mistral Studio. Weights drop end of this month."

> "ML4 is a 1 trillion-parameter natively multimodal model with 52 billion active parameters. It
> is our largest and most capable model to date, and it continues to improve rapidly as we refine
> it."

> "It already achieves performance competitive with the strongest open-source models globally,
> while significantly outperforming any open-weight model developed in the US or Europe."

> "ML4 was trained from scratch on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's own datacenters
> in Europe. The public preview is served on that same infrastructure."

> "We will release the weights by the end of the month. Until then, we are red-teaming the model
> in real-world settings with cybersecurity leaders, vetted partners, and state authorities, who
> will access the same model with reduced moderation and expanded cyber capabilities."

> "a significant share of ML4's training data was multilingual, spanning more than 160 languages,
> including every official language of the European Union."

> "As we work toward releasing the weights, we will share further details on the model
> architecture, additional benchmarks, and our post-training methodology."

> "This model will also serve as the foundation for a new generation of specialized and optimized
> Mistral models."

Model card footer on the same page: "Mistral Large 4 — New — Open-weight hybrid
instruct-and-reasoning MoE with multimodal input; unifies instruction, reasoning, and agentic
capabilities in a single model, state-of-the-art among open weights on cybersecurity, finance,
and manufacturing, natively fluent in 160+ languages." Input "/M tokens" $1.36, output
"/M tokens" $4.18.

## Vendor-reported results (prose only)

Coding and agents:

- "scoring 61.7% on DeepSWE v1.1, 59.4% on SWE-Atlas-QnA, and 28.3% on Terminal-Bench 4. Its
  combined Coding Agent Index score of 49.8% places it ahead of DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 and Qwen3.8
  Max."
- "On AutomationBench — 657 business workflows across apps like Gmail, Google Sheets, Slack, and
  Salesforce — it scores 59.9%, ahead of Kimi K3, MiMo-V2.6-Pro, and DeepSeek V4 Pro."
- "On AA-Briefcase, which evaluates long-horizon knowledge work, it reaches 1,393 Elo, ahead of
  DeepSeek V4 Pro."

Cyber:

- "On the Artificial Analysis Cyber Index … it ranks among the top five models globally and leads
  open-weight models developed outside China by a wide margin. On one of the index's tests, which
  asks a model to reproduce a real vulnerability in open-source software and then patch it, ML4
  scores 82%, the highest of any model. It also solves 93% of the challenges in Cybench."
- "Several leading closed models, including Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra, score near zero on
  the same test because they refuse to perform the task."

Multimodal and science:

- "surpassing GPT-6-Astra on Dense 200 (42% vs 41%)".
- "ML4 is state of the art on SciCode-Verified among open-weight models."

Knowledge work: "we evaluated ML4 through third party evaluators (vals.ai) on representative tasks
for both legal and financial tasks, finding the model exceeds GPT-6-Astra in both cases."

Safety (vendor-measured):

- "On Lakera's public B3 AI Security Benchmark, ML4 resists 93.3% of attacks – we see no higher
  scores among competitors."
- "KORA Benchmark, where ML4 again sits at our highest measured score among OSS models (1.691,
  with 2 being the maximum denoted as 'Exemplary')".
- "the average refusal rate of the model on cyber prompts from JailbreakBench, StrongREJECT, and
  AgentHarm is higher than all OSS models."

Human evaluation:

- "We also ran a blind human evaluation with Surge AI on coding quality … ML4 Preview ranked second
  of five models (3.74), ahead of Kimi K3 (3.59), GLM-5.3 (3.60) and GLM-5.2 (3.40), and behind
  only Claude Opus 5 (4.22)."
- "ML4 was preferred in CAD and STEM, while performing on par or close to GLM-5.3 in finance and
  coding." (internal annotator study, GLM-5.3 as the only comparator)

## Notes and gaps

- No model card, licence text, parameter table beyond "1 trillion / 52 billion active", or
  per-token price derivation appears in the post; those come from the docs model page.
- The post describes the preview as **not final**: "it continues to improve rapidly as we refine
  it", and the RL run "is still in flight".
- The claim of an "Artificial Analysis Cyber Index" could not be checked: no AA page at
  `/cyber`, `/models/cyber` or `/leaderboards/cyber` existed at capture (all 404). Treat the
  ranking as a vendor claim.
- Chart-only figures (CyberGym-E2E, GDP.pdf, ChartQA Pro, Finance Agent v2, Finch, Harvey Legal
  Agent, AA Cyber Index) were not readable as numbers and are not recorded.

# Mistral model lifecycle policy

- **Origin:** "Model lifecycle policy", Mistral AI documentation, <https://docs.mistral.ai/inference/model-lifecycle>.
  Retrieved 2026-10-26.
- **Evidence kind:** official vendor policy (first-party).
- **Publication date:** none stated; live page.
- **Capture date:** 2026-10-26
- **Relevant models:** all Mistral serverless models, including Mistral Large 4 (Public Preview).
- **Capture type:** rendered page text.

## Verbatim

Lifecycle stages table:

| Stage | Description | Silent updates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Labs | "Experimental. Not for production." | Yes |
| Public Preview | "Near-final before General Availability." | Yes |
| General Availability | "Production-ready and stable." | No |
| Deprecated | "Retirement announced. Still accessible until the retirement date." | No |
| Retired | "No longer accessible." | No |

Public Preview, verbatim:

> "Public Preview is the stage for general public launches that aren't final yet. These models
> are actively promoted, but may receive updates before reaching General Availability. They: Are
> priced at the same rate as General Availability models. Allow silent updates. Have no guaranteed
> path to General Availability, and may be retired before reaching it."

Naming convention, verbatim: "Model identifiers use a `model-name-major-minor` format, for example
`mistral-medium-3-5`." Aliases: `model-name-latest` (latest across generations),
`model-name-major` (latest minor of that generation), `model-name-major-minor` (fixed).

> "Public Preview models use the major-minor format."

Deprecation notice periods: Labs 1 month, Public Preview 1 month, General Availability 6 months,
Third-party models 1 month. "Once retired, requests to its identifiers fail with a 404 error."

## Consequence for Mistral Large 4

Large 4 ships under the fixed identifier `mistral-large-4-0` and the shorter `mistral-large-4`,
both of which the model card lists. Under this policy a preview model may change behaviour
without notice and may be retired without ever reaching GA, on one month's notice.

# Mistral regional inference (EU / US endpoints)

- **Origin:** "Regional inference", Mistral AI documentation, <https://docs.mistral.ai/inference/regional-inference>.
  Retrieved 2026-10-26.
- **Evidence kind:** official vendor documentation (first-party).
- **Publication date:** none stated; live page.
- **Capture date:** 2026-10-26
- **Relevant models:** Mistral-hosted models generally; used here to explain the price and
  context difference between the global and EU endpoints of Mistral Large 4.
- **Capture type:** rendered page text.

## Verbatim

> "Regional inference is billed at 1.1× standard list pricing (a 10% upcharge) for input tokens,
> output tokens, cached reads, and cache writes."

Endpoints: `api.mistral.ai` (global, no upcharge, "Mistral does not commit to a specific inference
location for requests sent to this endpoint"), `api.eu.mistral.ai` (EU, upcharge, "Multiple data
centers in EU and EFTA countries"), `api.us.mistral.ai` (US, upcharge, "Multiple data centers in
the United States").

Limitations, verbatim: "Not all tool calls are currently supported. Function calling is the only
supported regional tool. Stateful features, including Agents, Batch, and the Files API, are not
available on regional endpoints. Available models vary by region."

## Use in this page

The 1.1× upcharge reconciles the two Mistral Large 4 price points seen on OpenRouter: $1.36/$4.18
on the global endpoints and $1.496/$4.598 on the EU endpoint (1.36 × 1.1 = 1.496; 4.18 × 1.1 =
4.598).

## Gaps

- The page does not list which models are available on each regional endpoint, and the "Available
  models" section instructs the reader to call `models.list` against the regional base URL. No
  such call was made for this record.

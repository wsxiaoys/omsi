# Mistral docs — deprecated audio overview (Voxtral 2 generation)

- Origin: "Audio — Overview (legacy)", Mistral AI documentation,
  <https://docs.mistral.ai/studio/audio/deprecated/overview>. Retrieved 2026-10-26. It sits under the
  docs sidebar's "Deprecated" group, alongside `/studio/audio/deprecated/speech_to_text` and
  `/studio/audio/deprecated/text_to_speech`.
- Evidence kind: official vendor documentation (first-party).
- Publication date: none stated; live page.
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Voxtral Mini Transcribe 2 (`voxtral-mini-transcribe-26-02`), Voxtral Realtime
  (`voxtral-mini-transcribe-realtime-2602`), Voxtral TTS (`voxtral-mini-tts-2603`) — the Voxtral 2
  audio generation that Voxtral 3 replaces.
- Capture type: rendered page text.

## Verbatim

> "Transcribe recordings, meetings, or call archives" → Voxtral Mini Transcribe 2; "Transcribe live
> audio with sub-200ms latency" → Voxtral Realtime.

> "**Voxtral Mini Transcribe 2** for batch transcription — speaker diarization, context biasing (up to
> 100 custom terms), word-level timestamps, recordings up to 3 hours per request, and noise-robust
> accuracy across 13 languages."

> "**Voxtral Realtime** (`voxtral-mini-transcribe-realtime-2602`) for live streaming — transcribes
> audio as it arrives with latency configurable down to sub-200ms, a 4B footprint for edge
> deployment, and open weights under Apache 2.0."

> "Realtime transcription is not compatible with the `diarize` parameter. Use one or the other."

## Reading

- Voxtral 2 audio is **documented as deprecated but not retired**. The pages are still served under
  a "Deprecated" sidebar group and still link live model pages; no retirement date appears anywhere
  in this capture.
- The Voxtral 2 realtime model **could not diarize at all** ("not compatible with the `diarize`
  parameter"), which is the concrete capability gap Voxtral Mini Transcribe Realtime 3 fills.
- The 3-hour / 100-term / 13-language / word-timestamp feature list here is the last documented
  statement of Transcribe 2's limits, and it is the baseline Voxtral 3 is missing equivalents for.

## Gaps

- No retirement date, no notice period, no statement of which Voxtral 2 IDs remain callable.
- No file-size limit on this page (the 1 GB figure appears only in the Transcribe 2 launch post).

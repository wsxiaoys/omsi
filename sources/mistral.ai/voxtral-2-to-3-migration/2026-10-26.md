## Summary

This is the first-party statement that Voxtral TTS 3 **replaces** `voxtral-mini-tts-2603` (the March
2026 Voxtral TTS) under the same endpoint, and that the change is additive for existing TTS code:
every Voxtral 2 preset, `ref_audio` cloning and the `voice_id` contract keep working.

It also shows the alias policy conflict: `voxtral-mini-tts-latest` is documented here as switching to
Voxtral 3 only "within a month of GA", while the model card already lists it among the 3.0
identifiers — and the model is in Public Preview, not GA.

## Gaps

- The deprecation timeline is empty; the predecessor has no announced retirement date.
- No statement of whether Voxtral 2 presets will be retired with the model.
- No quality or price comparison between the two generations in the migration text.

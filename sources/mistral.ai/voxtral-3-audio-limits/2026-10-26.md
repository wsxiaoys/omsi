## Summary

**Mistral publishes no input-length limit for speech generation.** The limits page that gives exact
numbers for every transcription field lists only expressiveness, phonemes, tags and the reference
clip for TTS. Neither the model card nor the OpenAPI schema adds a `maxLength`.

This record also supplies the third pace-tag count: two values here, five on the reference table,
"2 pace tags" in the marketing copy.

## Gaps

- No maximum input characters, tokens or output duration.
- No documented error for over-long text; whether it truncates or fails is unknown.
- Quotas are per-workspace settings not published on the page.

## Context-bias limit, verbatim (from the linked page)

> "Context has no budget of its own: its entries count toward the model's context window, together
> with the audio. A long recording leaves less room for context, so keep the list to the terms and
> descriptions that matter."

Over the window: "A `400` error of type `invalid_request_prompt_too_long`, whose message gives the
size of the request and the maximum".

## Reading

- **No maximum audio duration or file size is published for Voxtral Transcribe 3.** The only size
  limit is a URL-length limit; the "Uploaded file" value cell is empty in the page source, so the
  documented limit is literally blank. This is a regression in published detail relative to Voxtral
  Transcribe 2, which documented 3 hours per request and 1 GB per file
  (`../../mistral.ai/voxtral-transcribe-2-launch/2026-10-26.md`).
- **65,536 tokens is the whole budget for audio plus vocabulary**, so long recordings leave little
  room for `context_bias`. No tokens-per-minute-of-audio conversion is given anywhere, so the
  effective maximum duration cannot be derived from the docs.
- Word timestamps are a hard `400`, not a silently degraded mode.

## Gaps

- The upload size limit cell is empty; audio-format cells on the sibling page are empty too
  (`../../mistral.ai/voxtral-3-audio-formats/2026-10-26.md`).
- No per-model differences: the table does not say whether Small and Mini share every limit.
- No rate limits on this page; they are workspace settings and were not captured.

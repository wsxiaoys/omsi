## Verbatim

> "Mistral audio covers three jobs: turning speech into text, turning text into speech, and running
> both in live conversations. Voxtral 3 models transcribe recorded files and live streams, with
> speaker labels, timestamps, and custom vocabulary."

> "The audio docs cover Voxtral 3. If your code still calls Voxtral 2 models, Migrate from Voxtral 2
> lists what to change."

> "Voxtral 3 has five models: three turn speech into text, and two turn text into speech."

Model table, verbatim:

| Model | Use it to | Key numbers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voxtral Mini Transcribe 3 (`voxtral-mini-transcribe-3-0`) | Transcribe recorded files, with speaker labels, segment timestamps, and custom vocabulary | 65,536-token context, shared by the audio and the vocabulary list |
| Voxtral Mini Transcribe Realtime 3 (`voxtral-mini-transcribe-realtime-3-0`) | Transcribe live audio for captions and voice agents, with turn detection | Streaming delay from 80 to 1,200 ms, or 2,400 ms, set per session |
| Voxtral Small Transcribe 3 (`voxtral-small-transcribe-3-0`) | Transcribe recorded files at higher quality | Transcription only at launch |
| Voxtral TTS 3 (`voxtral-mini-tts-3-0`) | Generate speech from text with preset or cloned voices | 5 output formats, WAV at 24 kHz; 34 emotion tags and 2 pace tags |
| Voxtral TTS Realtime 3 (`voxtral-mini-tts-realtime-3-0`) | Stream speech for live conversations | |

First-call example, verbatim: `client.audio.transcriptions.complete(model="voxtral-mini-transcribe-3", ...)`
with the tip "This example runs on Voxtral Mini Transcribe 3."

## Reading

- Two offline transcription IDs and one realtime ID are named with their full `-3-0` identifiers;
  "Voxtral 3" is the family label, not a single model.
- **The Small tier is documented as transcription-only at launch**, in the vendor's own model table.
  That contradicts the Small model card, which advertises translation, speech Q&A, summarization and
  function calling (see `../../mistral.ai/voxtral-small-transcribe-3-model-card/2026-10-26.md`).
- The overview gives the context window as **65,536 tokens shared by audio and vocabulary**, while
  both model cards display **32k** (see the card records). Unresolved; the docs page is the more
  specific statement and the limits page repeats 65,536.

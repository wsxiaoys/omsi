## Verbatim

> "Diarization labels each part of a transcript with the speaker who said it, in a recorded file or a
> live session."

> "Overlapping speech (two speakers talking at the same time) is not supported in this version."

> "If your application draws segments on a timeline, or shows them as a chat thread, don't assume that
> each segment ends before the next one starts: Order segments by `start`, and keep both segments when
> their times overlap."

> "The Voice Agents SDK does not send `diarize` or `speaker_references` in its `stt` config. For
> realtime diarization with speaker references, use a raw WebSocket session."

Label values, verbatim: "`speaker_1`, `speaker_2`, and so on, or the names of your reference clips".

Realtime event shape, verbatim:
`{"type": "transcription.segment", "text": " today?", "start": null, "end": null, "speaker_id": "speaker_1"}`

## Reading

- **Overlapping speech is not supported** — the docs still tell clients to tolerate overlapping
  segment times, which is a contradictory pair of statements worth keeping in view when reading the
  vendor's 18.4% cpWER diarization number on benchmarks that include overlapping-speech sets.
- Speaker references (naming known speakers from short clips) are realtime-only, capped at 8 clips of
  3–15 s (`../../mistral.ai/voxtral-3-audio-limits/2026-10-26.md`).
- Realtime segments carry a speaker label but null start/end times, so realtime diarization gives no
  timestamps at all.

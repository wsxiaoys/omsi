## Verbatim — scope and availability

> "Voxtral 3 introduces a cutting-edge voice agent SDK and state-of-the-art speech models designed for
> real-time, multi-step conversations across 50+ languages."

> "Available via Mistral's EU-hosted API or self-deployment, it ensures data sovereignty while
> delivering low-latency, human-like interactions for contact centers, virtual assistants, and
> hands-free copilots."

> "As with other Mistral models, the Voxtral 3 family is designed for sovereign deployment. Available
> today through Mistral's EU-hosted API or for self-deployment on your own infrastructure, it gives
> organizations control over where audio is processed, how data is handled, and whether conversations
> are retained."

## Verbatim — transcription claims

> "**Accurate transcription across 50+ languages**. The speech-to-text model, *Voxtral Small
> Transcribe 3*, is state-of-the-art in multilingual transcription, identifying who said what
> (diarization), using keyphrases to improve its accuracy (context-biasing). It detects when someone
> has finished speaking so agents can respond without delay (end-of-turn detection)."

> "The Small version achieves state-of-the-art results across diarization and multilingual
> transcription, including in English, French and Spanish. It outperforms Whisper, Scribe v2 and
> Gemini 3.5 Transcribe. The *Mini variant* is 4B parameters, enabling fast and accurate
> transcriptions at a price of $0.0035/min."

> "The supported list of languages is expanded to 50, including 24 official EU languages and many
> low-resource Asian and Indic languages."

> "Both models support context biasing, where key terms and free-form text can be given to bias the
> transcription and improve recall on technical terms. This enables them to be used in production
> settings with domain-specific vocabulary without the need for fine-tuning."

## Reading

- **Vendor benchmark numbers exist and are specific**: 4.1% / 4.0% FLEURS-45 WER for Small, 4.6% /
  4.5% for Mini, 18.4% cpWER diarization for Small. They are vendor-run, on the intersection of
  languages all seven compared models support, so they do not cover the 50-language claim.
- **The post names the Mini as 4B parameters** — the only parameter figure in any captured source.
- **"50+ languages" here vs 50 in the docs vs 45 realtime vs 35 in this post's realtime section**:
  the post, the languages page and the model cards do not agree on a single count. See
  `../../mistral.ai/voxtral-3-audio-languages/2026-10-26.md`.
- **Self-deployment is claimed, weights are not published**: no `mistralai` Hugging Face repository
  for any Voxtral 3 transcribe model exists at capture, and the cards name no licence. The claim is
  unsupported by any captured artefact.
- The post calls the Small model "Voxtral Transcribe 3" in one link label and "Voxtral Small
  Transcribe 3" everywhere else — the roster label comes from this umbrella usage.

## Gaps

- No latency methodology for the realtime figures, no per-language table, no audio duration limit,
  no licence, no deployment artefact, and no date on the rendered page other than the structured
  `datePublished` field.

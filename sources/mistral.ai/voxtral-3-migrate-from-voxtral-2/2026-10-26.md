## Speech-to-text changes, verbatim (breaking first)

| Change | Breaks existing code? | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Word timestamps are gone. `timestamp_granularities` accepts `segment` only, and `word` returns a `400` error. | Yes, if you request word timestamps. | Request segment timestamps. |
| A realtime session locks its settings when the first audio chunk arrives. A later `session.update` gets an `error` event. | | Send every setting in the first `session.update`, before any audio. |
| *(row label empty)* | Yes, if your display assumes that segments never overlap. | Keep both segments when they overlap. |
| `context_bias` entries can be phrases with spaces, such as a full product name, and a free-form context sentence works offline. | No. | See Bias context and vocabulary. |
| `context_bias` has no fixed number of entries. | No. | Keep the list short: the entries share the context window with the audio. |
| Transcription covers more languages. | No. | Omit `language` to detect the language automatically. |
| New: realtime diarization, speaker references, and turn detection in realtime sessions. | No. | Opt in when you need them. |

## Reading

- **`voxtral-small-2507` (Voxtral Small, the 24B open-weights audio-LLM from 2025-07-15) is mapped to
  Voxtral Small Transcribe 3.** So the Small Transcribe tier is positioned as the replacement for a
  model that did general audio understanding and chat, not just transcription — which is part of why
  the Small card advertises translation/Q&A (capability it does not, per the other docs, have at
  launch).
- **The mapping table's Model ID column is wrong for the Small row**: it gives
  `voxtral-small-transcribe-latest` (an alias) where every other row gives the fixed `-3-0` ID. The
  model card and the pricing page both use `voxtral-small-transcribe-3-0`
  (`../../mistral.ai/voxtral-small-transcribe-3-model-card/2026-10-26.md`).
- **No retirement date for Voxtral 2 is given.** The "Deprecation timeline" section exists as a
  heading with no body in the page HTML.
- Word timestamps and the 100-term context-bias cap are the two concrete capability regressions for
  anyone migrating from Transcribe 2.

## Gaps

- The "Deprecation timeline" section is empty; the deprecated overview for Voxtral 2 audio also gives
  no retirement date (`../../mistral.ai/voxtral-2-audio-deprecated/2026-10-26.md`).
- One change-table row has an empty label, so the breaking change about overlapping segments is
  described without naming what changed.

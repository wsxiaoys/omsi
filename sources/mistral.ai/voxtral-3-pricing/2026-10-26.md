## Reading

- **Billing basis is audio duration, per minute**, with a cached-input rate at one tenth of the input
  rate. No output rate is listed for any transcription model.
- **Transcribe 3 raises both tiers' prices over Transcribe 2**: Mini $0.003 → $0.0035 (+17%), realtime
  $0.006 → $0.008 (+33%).
- **Small Transcribe 3 costs exactly what Voxtral Small (the 2025 24B audio-LLM) cost per minute,
  $0.004** — the new tier replaces the old one at the same headline price, and the old model also
  charged $0.4/M output tokens for text generation the new one does not offer.
- Small is only 14% more per minute than Mini ($0.004 vs $0.0035), which makes the tier choice
  mostly a quality/latency decision rather than a cost one.

## Gaps

- Batch and Priority tab values were not captured; the audio section's Batch/Priority pricing is
  therefore unknown here.
- No statement of how cached input is triggered for audio, and no minimum billing increment (for
  example per-second rounding) is published.

## Summary

The predecessor's own documentation, kept live under the deprecated tree. It is what a reader needs
to separate the two models: **9 languages** (versus 35), **2–3 second zero-shot clone with no separate
tags** (versus 34 emotion and 2–5 pace tags), **no professional voice cloning**, **CC BY-NC 4.0 open
weights** (versus Premier, no weights), and the same ~90 ms processing-latency claim the successor
inherits.

Note that the ~90 ms figure appears in both generations. For the predecessor the legacy page
distinguishes it from end-to-end time-to-first-audio (~0.8 s PCM, ~3 s MP3); the Voxtral 3 card quotes
"~90 ms time-to-first-audio" without that distinction.

## Gaps

- The legacy pages carry no retirement date and no pointer to a migration deadline.
- The 9-language list here is shorter than the open-weights model card's own metadata, which lists
  nine languages in a different order (`en, fr, es, pt, it, nl, de, ar, hi`).
- No price on either legacy page.

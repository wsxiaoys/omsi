# Mistral docs: Text to Speech overview

- Origin: Mistral AI, "Text to Speech", Studio audio documentation, <https://docs.mistral.ai/studio/audio/text-to-speech/overview>
- Evidence kind: official vendor documentation (first-party)
- Publication date: none stated; live page
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Voxtral TTS 3 (`voxtral-mini-tts-3-0`), Voxtral TTS Realtime 3 (`voxtral-mini-tts-realtime-3-0`)
- Capture type: rendered page text

## Verbatim

Model table:

| Model | Model ID | Mode | Use it to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Voxtral TTS 3 | `voxtral-mini-tts-3-0` | Offline | "Generate speech from complete text, with preset or cloned voices, emotion and pace tags, and custom pronunciation. Stream the audio when the listener is waiting." |
| Voxtral TTS Realtime 3 | `voxtral-mini-tts-realtime-3-0` | Realtime | — |

Features by mode (offline column):

| Feature | Offline | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preset voices | Yes | "Pick a ready-made voice by its ID." |
| Zero-shot cloning | Yes | "Reproduce a voice from a short recording, and save it to reuse by ID." |
| Emotion and pace tags | "Yes: 34 emotions, and the slow and fast paces" | "Direct the delivery inline, word by word, in the text." |
| Style instructions | "No (not available at launch)" | "Describe the delivery of the whole request in plain words." |
| Custom pronunciation | Yes | "Spell out how a name or a term sounds, with phonemes." |
| Expressiveness | — | "A number >= 1.0 that controls how expressive the speech is. The default value is set by the service." |
| Streamed response | Yes | "Receive the audio in pieces and start playback before the file is complete." |
| Contextual TTS | Yes | "Condition the delivery of the response on prior conversation audio." |
| Output formats | — | "mp3, wav, pcm, flac, opus" |

Deprecated models section: "To move your requests to the new model, see Migrate from Voxtral 2."

The sibling audio overview page (<https://docs.mistral.ai/studio/audio/overview>) states:

> "Voxtral 3 has five models: three turn speech into text, and two turn text into speech."

and gives the key numbers for the TTS member: "Voxtral TTS 3 (`voxtral-mini-tts-3-0`) — Generate
speech from text with preset or cloned voices — 5 output formats, WAV at 24 kHz; 34 emotion tags and
2 pace tags".

## Summary

Confirms the offline/realtime split, the model IDs, and that style instructions are advertised but
switched off at launch. Establishes the headline counts — 34 emotions, 2 paces, 5 formats — that the
deeper pages then contradict on the pace count.

## Gaps

- No text length limit, latency figure or concurrency figure on this page.
- "Expressiveness … >= 1.0" here versus "1.0 to 5.0" on the Speech generation page.

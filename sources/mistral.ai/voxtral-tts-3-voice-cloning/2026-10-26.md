## Verbatim

> "Voice cloning generates speech in the voice of a recording that you provide, with no training.
> Clone a voice for a single request, or save it in your workspace and reuse it by its ID."

Reference clip rules:

> "Format: an MP3 or WAV file."
>
> "Encoding: the audio itself, base64-encoded. A URL isn't accepted."
>
> "Content:"

The "Content:" bullet list is **empty in the served render** — the page ships the label with no list
items. Nothing on the page states a length, sample-rate, noise or consent requirement for the clip.

Zero-shot cloning:

> "Pass the recording, base64-encoded, as ref_audio. The voice isn't saved."

Saved voice:

> "Create a voice from the recording, then pass its ID as voice_id. Saved voices carry a
> retention_notice field (default 30 days, min 1, max 365)."

Cross-lingual:

> "A cloned voice isn't limited to the language of its recording."

Professional voice cloning:

> "For best results with professional voice cloning, we recommend 30 minutes to 3 hours of clean,
> high-quality audio featuring only one speaker. Record in a quiet room with minimal echo, using the
> same microphone, distance, and setup throughout, because consistency matters more than length."
>
> "Avoid background noise, music, long pauses, or heavy audio processing such as noise reduction or
> effects."

Voice-creation call, verbatim from the example: `client.audio.voices.create(name=…,
sample_audio=…, sample_filename=…, languages=["en"])`.

## Summary

Cloning is the model's headline capability and has two documented modes: one-off zero-shot from a
clip sent with each request, and a saved workspace voice created from a clip and reused by ID. A
third, heavier workflow — professional voice cloning — is recommended at 30 minutes to 3 hours of
audio.

The only clip-length statement anywhere in the documentation set is the limits page's "a few seconds
to a few minutes", which covers the zero-shot path. The launch post's "voice cloning from 30+ minutes
of sampled audio" describes the professional path, not the zero-shot one.

## Gaps

- No consent, authorisation or identity-verification step is documented on this page.
- The reference-clip content rules render empty.
- No speaker-similarity metric, no failure modes, no cap on the number of saved voices.

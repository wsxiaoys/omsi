## Why this matters for the Dia page

The vendor's own commercial surface sells Qwen3-based speech APIs, not Dia. At capture, **there is
no first-party hosted Dia endpoint**: Dia is distributed as Apache-2.0 weights (Hugging Face +
GitHub), and every hosted Dia route in this knowledge base is a third party (fal runs it on its own
GPUs; Replicate carries a community Cog).

## Gaps

- No Dia version, price, latency, or availability detail — the site names Dia but sells nothing
  built on it.
- No statement that Dia is deprecated or superseded; no mention of Dia2 on the captured page.
- The "2M+ downloads" figure is not dated and is not reconcilable with the per-repo download counts
  Hugging Face reports (14,475 and 12,850 last-month downloads at capture).

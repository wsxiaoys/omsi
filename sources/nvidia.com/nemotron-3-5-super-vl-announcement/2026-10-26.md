# NVIDIA newsroom: Nemotron 3.5 Super VL and Bolt launch

- **Origin:** "Open From Data Center to Desktop: NVIDIA Expands Nemotron Family With Highest
  Efficiency Super VL and Bolt Models", Kari Briski, NVIDIA Blog (newsroom),
  <https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nemotron-3-5-super-vl/>. Published 2026-10-15, last modified
  2026-10-21. Retrieved 2026-10-26.
- **Evidence kind:** official announcement (vendor marketing; release dates, availability and
  headline efficiency claims — not independent measurement).
- **Publication date:** 2026-10-15.
- **Capture date:** 2026-10-26.
- **Relevant models:** Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB, Nemotron 3.5
  Bolt E4B 4GB; context for Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, Nemotron 3 Ultra.
- **Capture type:** text extraction of the article body. Quotes below are verbatim from that
  extraction.

## What launched

> "NVIDIA today released Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, an open 120-billion-parameter model that
> delivers the fastest task completion and highest accuracy for specialized always-on agents."

> "Joining it are two new edge models: Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB, optimized to balance
> intelligence and speed per GB for local agentic workloads, and Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B 4GB,
> optimized for intelligence per GB for fast conversational agents."

> "Super VL and Bolt 74B-A9B are available now, with Bolt E4B coming Oct. 23."

So the generation is dated **2026-10-15** on the vendor's own announcement, with Bolt E4B
landing **2026-10-23**. Lightning is older and is described as a predecessor release:

> "Super VL and Bolt join the Nemotron family alongside Lightning and Ultra."

> "Lightning, released in August, handles high-throughput workloads; Ultra handles the
> hardest reasoning tasks; and Bolt brings intelligence to the edge."

The post does **not** name a bare "Nemotron 3.5" model. Every product named carries a tier
suffix (Super VL, Bolt 74B-A9B, Bolt E4B, Lightning). This is the primary evidence that the
roster label "Nemotron 3.5" is a generation label rather than a single checkpoint.

## Claims about Super VL (vendor marketing, labelled)

> "comparable with NVIDIA's largest reasoning model at roughly one-fifth the parameter count."

> "As the fastest open agentic model in its class — 5x faster than the next best — it helps
> enterprise agents complete high-volume tasks faster…"

> "Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index leadboard, achieves up to
> 5x faster throughput compared to other models in its class."

The 5x figure is a throughput claim scoped to "in its class" and to AA agentic tasks; the
technical blog attributes it to a single-GPU, single-endpoint measurement (see
[technical blog](../nemotron-3-5-super-vl-technical-blog/2026-10-26.md)). No absolute
accuracy number appears in the announcement.

## Training scale, stated first-party

> "Nemotron 3.5 Super VL was trained on about 26 trillion tokens across pretraining and
> continued pretraining, then post-trained with roughly 3.8 million samples and reinforcement
> learning across dozens of environments."

## Availability and packaging

> "Nemotron 3.5 Super VL is available now on Hugging Face, Open Router, ModelScope community
> and as NVIDIA NIM."

> "NVIDIA NIM packages the model as a production-ready microservice with performance-optimized,
> validated configurations and enterprise-grade support, refreshed every two weeks with
> security patches and performance improvements."

> "Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB is available now on Hugging Face, with support for llama.cpp,
> Ollama, LM Studio, Unsloth, Canonical and vLLM."

> "Openness and customizability reinforce each other. The model ships with open weights, open
> datasets and training recipes."

**No price is stated anywhere in the announcement.** Pricing therefore has to come from hosts
(see the OpenRouter and DeepInfra records) — the vendor does not publish a per-token rate for
this model.

## Named adopters (vendor-supplied, unaudited)

Scale AI (Skydance vendor-credit finance agent, EY M&A due diligence), AfterQuery (Hebbia
credit-agreement analysis), AI21 and Alice (cybersecurity), Mercor (APEX-SWE coding),
ModelScope (ms-swift fine-tuning), Ramp, Dream, Lila Sciences, R1, TrainLoop/Nolla; platform
integrations named: Atlassian, CrowdStrike, Dassault Systèmes, Perplexity, Salesforce,
Synopsys. These are self-reported by NVIDIA and carry no measurement.

## Summary

The launch post fixes the dates (Super VL and Bolt 74B-A9B 2026-10-15; Bolt E4B 2026-10-23;
Lightning "in August"), establishes that "Nemotron 3.5" is a family rather than one model,
confirms open weights plus open datasets and recipes, and confirms Hugging Face / OpenRouter /
NIM distribution. Its quantitative content is limited to throughput and efficiency claims and
a 26T-token training figure; it publishes no accuracy benchmark and no price.

## Gaps

- Marketing copy only. The 5x throughput claim has no absolute TPS, no harness and no
  comparison set beyond "in its class".
- No price, no licence terms (the word "OpenMDW" does not appear), no context-window figure.
- The post is dated 2026-10-15 but was modified 2026-10-21; the capture cannot tell which
  sentences changed, so treat the quotes as of the 2026-10-21 revision.

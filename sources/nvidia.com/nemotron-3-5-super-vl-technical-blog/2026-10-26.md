## Where it sits in the family

> "Within a system of models, Nemotron 3.5 Super VL handles specialized, multistep work that
> needs reasoning, tool use, and text and visual understanding together. Smaller models, such
> as NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, handle high-volume text tasks, while frontier models such
> as NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra tackle complex planning and orchestration."

This is the clearest first-party statement of the 3.5 tier structure: Lightning = small /
high-volume, Super VL = mid-tier multimodal workhorse, Ultra (3.x, not 3.5) = frontier
orchestrator. Bolt is absent from this post.

## Architecture and training

> "Its 120B-parameter mixture-of-experts (MoE) architecture activates 12B parameters per token"

> "Nemotron 3.5 Super VL was distilled from Nemotron 3 Ultra."

> "Continued pretraining with high-quality natural and synthetic data improved Indic-language
> capabilities… This stage added 10B Indic tokens, drawn from a 12-language subset of
> Nemotron-pretraining-Indic-v1 and natural Indic text."

> "a two-stage SFT pipeline trains the language and vision components"

The post describes LiLiCorr speculative decoding (up to 2.3x faster token generation on DGX
Spark), NVFP4 post-training quantization with quantization-aware distillation, multi-token
prediction, and cross-turn context reuse preserving "both attention caches and Mamba recurrent
states."

## First-party measurements (vendor-run, harnesses named)

| Comparison | Nemotron 3.5 Super VL | Nemotron 3 Ultra |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Hermes Bench | 63.44% | 56.33% |
| LangChain Evals | 87.05% | 84.3% |

> "On CharXiv, a chart-reasoning benchmark, the model scored 80.8%, compared with 63% for
> Nemotron 3 Nano Omni. With Python code interpretation, it reached 84%."

> "On a single GPU, this model delivers up to 5x higher throughput for agentic tasks compared
> to other open models in its size category."

Figure 1 caption: "Nemotron 3.5 Super VL delivers over 600 TPS on Artificial Analysis
Intelligence Index agentic tasks on the Baseten endpoint". That is the only absolute
throughput number in the post, and it is scoped to one host endpoint.

The post also cites "an independent evaluation by EXO Labs" placing the model on the accuracy /
task-completion-time Pareto frontier on DGX Spark, without publishing the numbers.

## Claimed post-training outcomes (customer-reported, not measured here)

> "Scale AI… post-trained Nemotron 3.5 Super VL for automated vendor credit processing at
> Skydance Corporation… Inference cost for this workload will decrease by 40x and latency by
> 5x."

> "Ramp… expects up to 50% higher throughput and 10x lower latency."

> "AI21 and Alice post-trained Nemotron 3.5 Super VL for threat intelligence and vulnerability
> analysis, improving accuracy by 41%."

Note the tense: "will decrease", "expects". These are projections from partners, not
measurements, and they describe *post-trained derivatives*, not the released checkpoint.

## Summary

The technical post is the best first-party description of what Super VL is: a 120B/12B-active
hybrid Mamba-2 + MoE model with a C-RADIO vision encoder, distilled from Nemotron 3 Ultra,
extended with 1M context and Indic data, and shipped with an unusually specific inference stack
(LiLiCorr, NVFP4+QAD, MTP, prefix/Mamba-state reuse). It adds two vendor-run harness scores
(Hermes 63.44, LangChain 87.05) that beat Nemotron 3 Ultra, and one absolute throughput figure
(>600 TPS on Baseten). Its headline "5x faster" claim is scoped to "in its size category" and
single-GPU serving.

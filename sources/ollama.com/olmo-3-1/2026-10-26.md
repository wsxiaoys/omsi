## Summary

Ollama packages both 3.1 checkpoints as a 19 GB, 64K-context local model under one library name, with
`32b-instruct` and `32b-think` tags and a "thinking"/"tools" tag pair. This is the cheapest access path
(local, no per-token billing) but it is a quantised redistribution, not a hosted endpoint.

## Gaps

- The page does not state the quantisation of the 19 GB artifact. For reference, 32B parameters in
  BF16 would be on the order of 64 GB, so the published artifact is a compressed format — that is an
  inference from the size, not a statement by Ollama. The page does not say who produced the
  conversion.
- "64K context window" is rounded; the model config's ceiling is 65,536 tokens.
- The "Updated 10 months ago" label is relative and undated, so the artifact's revision cannot be pinned.
- No licence statement on the page beyond the Ai2 framing.

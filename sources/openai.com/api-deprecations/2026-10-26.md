## Captured content (verbatim)

### 2026-06-02: GPT Image model deprecations

> On June 2, 2026, we notified developers using older GPT Image models of their deprecation and
> removal from the API on December 1, 2026.

| Shutdown date | Model / system | Recommended replacement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dec 1, 2026 | `gpt-image-1-mini` | `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` or `gpt-image-2.5-flare` |
| Dec 1, 2026 | `gpt-image-1.5` \| `gpt-image-1.5-2025-12-16` | `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` or `gpt-image-2.5-flare` |
| Dec 1, 2026 | `chatgpt-image-latest` | `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` or `gpt-image-2.5-flare` |

### 2026-04-22: Legacy GPT model snapshots (image row only)

| Shutdown date | Model snapshot | Substitute model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| October 23, 2026 | `gpt-image-1` | `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` or `gpt-image-2.5-flare` |

The same notice lists DALL·E 2/3 as shut down on 2026-05-12 with the substitute
"`gpt-image-2`, `gpt-image-1`, or `gpt-image-1-mini`".

## What this record establishes, and what it does not

- **Establishes:** as of the 2026-10-26 capture, `gpt-image-2` does not appear on this page under
  any announced, upcoming, or past deprecation. Every retired or scheduled image model named
  here — `gpt-image-1`, `gpt-image-1.5`, `gpt-image-1-mini`, `chatgpt-image-latest` — has a
  stated shutdown date.
- **Does not establish:** that GPT Image 2 will not be deprecated. A page capture shows only
  what was published on that date, and a notice could be added later.
- Note the inconsistency across pages: the DALL·E replacement advice (May 2026) names
  `gpt-image-2` as the recommended image model, while the June 2026 and October 2026 notices
  name only the 2.5 models. Both are live on the same site at capture.

## Known gaps

- No statement of a support window, freeze policy, or minimum notice period for `gpt-image-2`
  specifically.

## Summary

As of 2026-10-26, GPT-6.1 Sol is **not deprecated and has no announced shutdown date**, and
it carries no `preview` suffix, so the 6-month notice period applies to it. Its predecessor
`gpt-6-sol` is likewise still current — it is the designated landing spot for older
retirements — which means the two Sol tiers coexist rather than one replacing the other
outright.

## Gaps

- An absence on this page is not proof of indefinite availability; it only bounds the
  claim to the capture date.
- The page does not say whether `gpt-6-sol` will itself be retired once migration to
  GPT-6.1 Sol completes.

## Summary

GPT-6.1 Sol is priced identically to GPT-6 Sol on input and output ($2/$10) and at half
its cached-input rate ($0.10 vs $0.20). It sits at one fifth of GPT-6 Astra's input and
output price. The five service tiers span 0.5× to 6× the Standard rate, and long-context
requests (>272K) double input and 1.5× output.

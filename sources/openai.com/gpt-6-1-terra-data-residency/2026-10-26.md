  and OpenAI, "Fast mode", <https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/fast-mode>
  (Markdown variants `…/guides/your-data.md`, `…/guides/fast-mode.md`). Retrieved
  2026-10-26. Both pages carry the same residency sentence for Terra; captured together
  because neither has Terra-specific content beyond it.
- **Evidence kind:** official documentation (first-party residency and processing rules).
- **Publication date:** none stated; live guides.
- **Capture date:** 2026-10-26
- **Relevant models:** `gpt-6.1-terra`, with `gpt-6-astra`, `gpt-6.1-sol`, `gpt-6-sol`,

## The Terra-specific limitation, verbatim

From the `/v1/responses`, `/v1/chat/completions`, and `/v1/batches` rows of the residency
table:

> GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6.1 Terra, GPT-6 Sol, and GPT-6 Luna support EU data residency with
> Standard, Flex, and Batch processing.

> GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6.1 Terra support only US and EU data residency.

That second sentence is the constraint: **Terra is limited to the United States and Europe
(EEA + Switzerland)**. It is the only sentence of its kind in the table — no comparable
"only US and EU" note is attached to Astra, GPT-6 Sol or GPT-6 Luna. But the UAE column does
not contradict it for those models either: on this capture the UAE column lists only
`gpt-5.6-luna`, `gpt-5.5-2026-04-23` and `gpt-5.2-2025-12-11` for Chat Completions and
`gpt-5.5-pro-2026-04-23`, `gpt-5.6-luna`, `gpt-5.5-2026-04-23` and `gpt-5.2-2025-12-11` for
Responses — **no GPT-6 model at all**.

So the accurate reading is: Terra is documented as US+EU only, and no GPT-6 model is served
from the UAE on this capture. Whether Astra, GPT-6 Sol or GPT-6 Luna have residency options
beyond US+EU is unresolved; only Terra and 6.1 Sol carry the explicit restriction.

## Fast mode, verbatim

> Fast mode supports EU data residency for GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6.1 Terra, and GPT-6 Luna. Fast
> mode is not available with EU data residency for GPT-6 Astra or GPT-6 Sol.

> Fast mode is compatible with data residency, Zero Data Retention, and a Business Associate
> Agreement (BAA), subject to model-specific availability.

So Terra's paid speed tier is available with EU residency — unlike Astra and GPT-6 Sol.

## Ultrafast mode

The Ultrafast guide describes Ultrafast availability for `gpt-6.1-sol` (US and EU residency
plus global processing) and `gpt-6-astra` (US residency and global processing only). **Terra
is not mentioned in the Ultrafast guide**, matching its absence from the Ultrafast price
table ([pricing](../gpt-6-1-terra-pricing/2026-10-26.md)).

## Summary

Terra is documented as US-and-EU-only. It gets EU residency on Standard, Flex, Batch and Fast,
which is better than Astra and GPT-6 Sol on Fast, and it has no Ultrafast option. No GPT-6
model appears in the UAE column, so Terra's geography is not visibly narrower than its siblings'
— it is only more explicitly stated.

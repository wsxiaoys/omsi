## Negative result

`gpt-6.1-terra` appears **nowhere** on the deprecations page on this capture: not in the
upcoming-deprecations tables, not in the retired-models tables, not as a shutdown target and
not as a recommended replacement. Terra was released the same day, so this establishes only
that no deprecation had been announced on 2026-10-26.

## Notice periods, verbatim

> - **Generally available models:** At least 6 months.
> - **Specialized variants of generally available models:** At least 3 months. Examples
>   include chat variants such as `gpt-5.1-chat-latest`, Codex variants such as
>   `gpt-5.3-codex`, and deep research variants such as `o3-deep-research`.
> - **Preview models:** Preview models, identified by `preview` in the model name, may be
>   retired with much shorter notice, such as 2 weeks.

`gpt-6.1-terra` carries no `preview` token, so the 6-month floor applies.

## `gpt-5.6-terra` as a retirement target, verbatim

Several retiring models are pointed at `gpt-5.6-terra`, including `gpt-5-mini` (Dec 11,
2026), `gpt-3.5-turbo-0125` (October 23, 2026), `o4-mini` (October 23, 2026), and
`computer-use-preview` (July 23, 2026). **`gpt-6.1-terra` is not used as a replacement target
anywhere on the page.**

That is a notable asymmetry with GPT-6.1 Sol, which OpenAI does steer users toward: the
deprecations page and the guide together treat Sol as the migration path
([family guide](../gpt-6-family-guide/2026-10-26.md)), while Terra's predecessor
`gpt-5.6-terra` is still the page's designated landing spot for small/cheap retirements.

## Shutdown-date field, verbatim

> To monitor model shutdowns programmatically, call the List models endpoint
> (`GET /v1/models`) and check each model's optional `shutdown_date` field.

No `shutdown_date` value for `gpt-6.1-terra` was observed in this capture.

## Summary

Terra is generally available, non-preview, and undeprecated as of 2026-10-26, so the 6-month
notice floor applies. The page provides no evidence that GPT-5.6 Terra is being retired, and
it still recommends `gpt-5.6-terra` for several legacy migrations.

## Gaps

- The page is a snapshot of announcements; it cannot show a retirement planned after
  2026-10-26.
- No statement of GPT-5.6 Terra's own status or support window.

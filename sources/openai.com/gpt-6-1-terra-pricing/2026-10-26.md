## Summary

Terra is the third of four current tiers: **$1.00 / $5.00** on Standard, exactly half of
GPT-6.1 Sol on both sides, a tenth of GPT-6 Astra, ten times GPT-6 Luna, and half the input /
58% off the output of its predecessor GPT-5.6 Terra ($2.00 / $12.00). Batch and Flex are 50%
of Standard; Fast is 2×; prompts over 272K input tokens bill the whole request at 2× input and
1.5× output.

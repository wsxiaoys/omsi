## What the entry establishes

1. **Release date: 2026-10-26.** That is inside the one-year window (≥ 2025-10-26) by
   construction, and it is the same day this capture was taken — the model is one day old at
   most relative to any later reading of this record.
2. **The surfaces named are Responses and Chat Completions.** No Batch entry is named in the
   changelog line even though the model page lists Batch as supported; the changelog names
   only the two request APIs.
3. **The launch copy is the tier description, not a capability claim.** "Fast, efficient …
   visual reasoning, precise computer use, clear, natural writing" is repeated verbatim on
   the model page, the guide, and the system-card addendum.

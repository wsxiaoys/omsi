## What this establishes

- Astra is **5× the input and 5× the output price of `gpt-6-sol`/`gpt-6.1-sol`** and 100×
  `gpt-6-luna`'s input price. It is the most expensive text model on the page apart from
  the legacy `gpt-5.4-pro`/`gpt-5.5-pro` rows.
- The **long-context premium is a cliff, not a taper**: crossing 272K input tokens doubles
  input and cache rates and multiplies output by 1.5 for the *whole* request.
- Tier multipliers on Astra are 1× standard, 0.5× batch/flex, 2× fast, 6× ultrafast.
- A 12-hour cache write costs 2.5× input ($25/M), twice the 30-minute write ($12.50/M).

## Gaps

- No effective date for any rate, and no statement of scheduled changes.
- No regional pricing, no per-tool-call fees for Astra (web search is priced separately
  elsewhere on the page but not captured here in the Astra context).
- The page does not say what Ultrafast mode does beyond token-rate multiplication.

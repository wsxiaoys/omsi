## Preparedness levels, verbatim

> Based on the findings described here, we determined that Astra reaches the Critical level
> in Cybersecurity capability, and the High level in the Biological and Chemical category.
> In AI Self-Improvement, Astra does not reach our High threshold.

> Today, we are releasing GPT-6 Astra, the most capable model we have ever broadly
> deployed. Astra is our first model to reach the Critical level of cybersecurity capability
> under our Preparedness Framework.

> We are treating Astra as High capability in the biological and chemical domain.

## Cyber capability, verbatim

> This means that, with the right tools and access, GPT-6 Astra can find previously unknown
> security flaws and develop new ways to exploit them across many well-protected systems
> without a person guiding each step. Accordingly, we significantly strengthened our
> protections against the model taking harmful cyber actions, whether that's due to misuse
> or misalignment.

> For automated evaluations of Astra's cybersecurity capabilities, we have adopted four
> public benchmarks: ExploitBench, ExploitGym, SEC-Bench Pro, and SRE-Bench. We have also
> supplemented these with two internal evaluations: Sandbox Bench, and ExploitBench -
> Internal Port (June – August 2026) … We have retired the evaluations we used for assessing
> High capability in cybersecurity – Capture the Flag (Internal) and CVE-Bench – because
> these evaluations have become saturated.

## Monitorability and sandbagging, verbatim

> GPT-6 Astra action-only aggregate g-mean² is consistently higher than GPT-5.6 Sol's.

> Overall, we find that GPT-6 Astra is capable of much higher covert sandbagging than
> GPT-5.6 Sol.

> In this view, GPT-6 Astra's lower CoT monitorability is especially stark because GPT-6
> Astra's medium reasoning effort CoT length is substantially shorter than GPT-5.6 Sol's
> medium reasoning effort CoT length … and GPT-6 Astra also has notably more very short or
> empty CoTs.

## What this establishes

- Astra is OpenAI's **first Critical-tier cybersecurity model** and is treated as High for
  biological and chemical risk. That is the reason its deployed behaviour is constrained.
- Monitoring is trained, not just bolted on: the model is *less* legible in its written
  reasoning than its predecessor, and more capable of covert sandbagging — OpenAI states
  both as findings, not as reassurance.
- The card is the authoritative place for "what the deployed model will refuse"; the launch
  post summarises it as refusing proof-of-concept exploit development.

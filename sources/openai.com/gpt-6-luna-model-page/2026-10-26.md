## Summary

First-party identity and limits for `gpt-6-luna`: one undated snapshot alias, 1,050,000-token
context with 922,000 max input and 128,000 max output, text+image in / text out, a May 18,
2026 knowledge cutoff, and $0.10 / $0.50 per 1M input / output with a 90% cache discount and a
272K long-context cliff. Unlike GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol, Luna accepts
`reasoning.effort: "none"`; unlike Astra, it has EU data residency in every processing tier
listed. Chat Completions function calling works only with effort `none`.

## What this establishes

- **Luna is the cheapest GPT-6 text tier**, 20× cheaper on input than `gpt-6-sol` /
  `gpt-6.1-sol`, 100× cheaper than `gpt-6-astra`, and 10× cheaper than `gpt-6.1-terra`.
- **The tier multipliers are uniform across the family**: Batch and Flex at 0.5×, Fast at
  2×, long context at 2× input / 1.5× output for the whole request.
- **The Luna tier has a direct predecessor**, `gpt-5.6-luna`, at 2× Luna's input and cache
  rates and 2.4× its output rate ($0.20/$1.20 against $0.10/$0.50) — the same order of
  discount OpenAI applied elsewhere in the GPT-6 generation.
- **Luna has the shallowest absolute cache economics in the family** ($0.01 cached vs $0.10
  input) but the same 10% cache-read ratio as Astra.

## Gaps

- No effective date and no statement of scheduled price changes.
- The page does not price tool calls per model; web search, computer use and other tools are
  billed separately elsewhere on the page and were not extracted here.
- No regional/`regional processing` premium row: the model page states a flat 10% premium
  "where available" and the pricing page has no per-region table in this capture.
- No Ultrafast row and no explanation for its absence.

## Family ladder, verbatim

> - GPT-6 Astra — **Highest intelligence**. For the most demanding reasoning, coding, and
>   professional work.
> - GPT-6.1 Sol — **Balanced speed, cost, and intelligence**. Near-Astra performance for
>   complex work at a lower cost.
> - GPT-6.1 Terra — **Fast and efficient**. For visual reasoning, precise computer use, and
>   clear, natural writing.
> - GPT-6 Luna — **Fastest and most cost-effective**. Strong performance for focused,
>   high-volume tasks.

## Astra positioning, verbatim

> GPT-6 Astra is our most intelligent model yet, with state-of-the-art performance in
> computer use, browsing, software engineering, science, and professional work. It can carry
> out multi-step workflows across code, browsers, and professional software. In several
> evaluations, Astra achieved stronger results using substantially fewer output tokens. Its
> estimated API cost per task was lower than earlier models despite its higher per-token
> pricing.

> GPT-6 Astra is also our most aligned model yet. It excels at exercising care, respecting
> task boundaries, and communicating transparently.

> All GPT-6 Astra users also have access to Fast mode and the new Ultrafast mode for our
> fastest API speeds.

## What's new with GPT-6, verbatim

- Async tool calling: "Set `async: true` on a function or custom tool and return its result
  when ready using the original `call_id`."
- Mid-turn steering: "Send additional user instructions while GPT-6 is working … Over a
  WebSocket connection, the Responses API preserves completed work."
- Change reasoning mid-conversation: "Add a `configuration_update` input item to increase
  reasoning effort … without rewriting the original prompt prefix."
- Misalignment monitoring: "our systems asynchronously monitor for misalignment and trigger
  alerts when necessary."

The guide also lists inherited GPT-5.6 capabilities: computer use, Structured Outputs,
streaming, Programmatic Tool Calling, multi-agent orchestration, prompt caching, persisted
reasoning, compaction, and pro mode.

## Limitations, verbatim

> - GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol do not support the `none` reasoning effort; GPT-6.1 Terra,
>   GPT-6 Sol, and GPT-6 Luna do.
> - Fast mode supports EU data residency for GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6.1 Terra, and GPT-6 Luna, but
>   not GPT-6 Astra or GPT-6 Sol. Ultrafast mode for GPT-6.1 Sol supports US and EU data
>   residency and global processing. GPT-6 Astra Ultrafast supports US data residency and
>   global processing only.

So Astra is the one GPT-6 tier with **no EU data-residency option in Fast or Ultrafast
mode** — a procurement-relevant constraint that has nothing to do with model quality.

## Documented behaviour, verbatim (prompting section)

> The model is designed to be a more effective collaborator and is thus more likely to ask
> the user a question when additional input could materially change the result. This can
> cause it to stop when the user may expect it to make reasonable assumptions and persist.

> It can be more sensitive to instructions contained in skills and other files, such as
> `AGENTS.md`. We **strongly recommend** auditing skills and other files accessible to your
> model for instructions that could influence its behavior.

> The model tends toward detailed, formatted responses and may use recurring phrases across
> sessions.

> The model may delegate less often than desired for your workflow.

> For coding tasks, the model tends to be thorough in testing before considering a task
> complete. For smaller tasks, this can result in broader tests than the task requires.

This is first-party behavioural guidance, not measurement. It matters because it is the
vendor describing a cost/verbosity profile that interacts with the $50/M output price.

## What this establishes

- Astra is the top rung of a four-model family (Astra > 6.1 Sol > 6.1 Terra > Luna) by
  intelligence, and the most expensive by token price.
- Vendor claims of token efficiency ("substantially fewer output tokens", lower cost per
  task) are stated as claims about evaluations, without numbers on this page.
- Astra has no `none` reasoning effort and no EU residency in fast tiers.

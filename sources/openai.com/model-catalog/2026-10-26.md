## Notes and gaps

- This is the clearest first-party statement that Sunburst and Flare are **two distinct
  first-party tiers**, not one model with host-specific serving names: they have separate model
  pages, separate model IDs, and different one-line positionings.
- The catalog does not say which tier backs the ChatGPT Images 2.5 product experience, and
  `chatgpt-image-latest` is labelled "Previous", with no successor alias listed on this page.
  That gap is why the ChatGPT↔API mapping is left open on the model page.
- No dates, prices, or benchmark figures appear in the catalog entries.

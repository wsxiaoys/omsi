## Grant

> "Subject to your compliance with this agreement, permission is hereby granted, free of
> charge, to deal in the Model Materials without restriction, including under all copyright,
> patent, database, and trade secret rights included or embodied therein."

"Model Materials" covers "one or more machine learning models (including architecture and
parameters); and … all related artifacts (including associated data, documentation and
software)".

## Outputs are explicitly unencumbered

> "This agreement does not impose any restrictions or obligations with respect to any use,
> modification, or sharing of any outputs generated by using the Model Materials."

This is the clause that distinguishes OpenMDW-1.1 from most open-weight licences: there is no
acceptable-use policy, no field-of-use restriction, no user threshold, and no output clause.
Nothing in the text restricts commercial use — NVIDIA's model cards assert "ready for
commercial or non-commercial use", and the licence text is consistent with that.

## Conditions

Two, both light:

1. **Notice retention on distribution:** "If you distribute any portion of the Model Materials,
   you shall retain in your distribution (1) a copy of this agreement, and (2) all copyright
   notices and other notices of origin included in the Model Materials".
2. **Patent/copyright retaliation:** "If you file, maintain, or voluntarily participate in a
   lawsuit against any person or entity asserting that the Model Materials directly or
   indirectly infringe any patent or copyright, then all rights and grants made to you hereunder
   are terminated, unless that lawsuit was in response to a corresponding lawsuit first brought
   against you."

## Disclaimers

"AS IS", no warranty of any kind; the licensee is "SOLELY RESPONSIBLE FOR (1) CLEARING RIGHTS
OF OTHER PERSONS … (2) OBTAINING ANY NECESSARY CONSENTS … OR (3) PERFORMING ANY DUE DILIGENCE";
no liability for the providers.

## Summary

OpenMDW-1.1 is a permissive, MIT-shaped licence applied to weights, data and documentation
together. For the Nemotron 3.5 family it means: commercial use allowed, modification and
redistribution allowed, outputs unrestricted, only obligations are retaining the licence and
notices and not suing over patents. The practical caveat is the disclaimer, which shifts all
third-party-rights clearance to the licensee — relevant because NVIDIA ships open pre-training
data alongside these weights.

## Summary

Host record confirming the model is served on five independent backends at an identical
$0.10/$0.50 (with a $0.11/$0.55 EU in-region premium), and carrying the only captured
per-benchmark AA numbers for the model. The two most useful facts here are the per-effort
index slope (43.4 → 29.5) and the fact that the same model reaches 86–87% on GPQA Diamond
while scoring 5.7% on Vals' SRE Bench and 2.5% on ProgramBench — the spread across task
families is far wider than the index suggests.

## Gaps

- The "Not used in Standard routing" block is unexplained; whether the $0.11/$0.55 endpoints
  are the EU ones or a separate capacity tier is not stated.
- Provider uptime is a 3-day window only.
- No statement of which endpoint produced the AA scores.
- No long-context (>100k) pricing is exposed anywhere on the page, even though Anthropic
  charges 5× above that boundary.

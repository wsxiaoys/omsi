### The number worth flagging

AA's Terminal-Bench 4.0 figure for Opus 5.5 at max effort is **59.6%**, against Anthropic's
own 66.4% (xhigh effort, Claude Code harness) and Vals AI's 65.15% on Vals' own board
([Vals AI](../../vals.ai/terminal-bench-4/2026-10-26.md)). The two independent evaluators
disagree with each other by 5.5 points, which is larger than Anthropic's stated ±2.6
standard error — so the vendor number is not the outlier here, the harness is the variable.
Note also that AA's max and xhigh rows are identical (59.6%) on this benchmark.

entries but Sail Research appears twice with identical terms, so the table has 24 distinct rows.

Three observations:

1. **The DeepSeek endpoint's $0.66 / $1.98 is the off-peak half of the first-party rate card**
   ([price list](../../deepseek.com/api-pricing/2026-10-26.md)). OpenRouter lists one number; DeepSeek's own
   price doubles during peak hours. A reader comparing on OpenRouter's figures will understate
   first-party peak cost by 2×.
2. **"The same model" is served at fp4, mxfp4 and fp8, and four endpoints declare no
   quantization at all.** Benchmark numbers attached to a slug are therefore not necessarily
   numbers for identical weights.
3. **DeepInfra caps completion at 16,384 tokens** against DeepSeek's 393,216 — a 24× gap on a
   model whose recommended output length at `high`/`max` effort is 384K. Venice's 32,768 is the
   next tightest.

The 0.0 and 1.9 are reproduced as printed. Nothing on the page explains them, and a 0.0 on a
benchmark where the vendor reports 0.0 nowhere is more likely a harness or submission failure
than a capability floor. Do not quote them as measurements without that caveat.

Also note **GPQA Diamond appears twice at different values** (92.8 AA, 92.4 Vals) and
**Terminal-Bench 4.0 appears twice at the same value** (14.1 both) — the second agreement is the
more informative fact, because two independent evaluators landing on 14.1% for the same
checkpoint is a real cross-check.

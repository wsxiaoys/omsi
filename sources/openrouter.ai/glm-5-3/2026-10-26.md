## Summary

OpenRouter establishes: a real multi-provider market for GLM-5.3 (37 providers), the vendor's
own endpoint at list price, a 40× spread in input pricing driven largely by quantization, a
Bedrock premium of 20%, and catalogue metadata (1M context, text-only, mandatory reasoning at
`max` by default) that agrees with the vendor except on maximum output length.

## Gaps

- No uptime history beyond `uptime_last_30m`; no latency data.
- The re-published AA numbers (44.8 / 74.8 / 53.1) are not dated and AA itself prints **45**
  ([AA](../../artificialanalysis.ai/glm-5-3/2026-10-26.md)); the 0.2 gap is unexplained rounding.
- Endpoint duplication (Fireworks, Mistral, BaseTen, Alibaba, Wafer, InferenceNet, Sail
  Research each appear more than once) makes "48 endpoints" a count of routes, not of
  independent operators.
- No statement of which endpoint the model page's default routing selects.

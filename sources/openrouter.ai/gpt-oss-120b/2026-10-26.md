What the table establishes:

- **Price spread is nearly 12× on input and 6× on output** for nominally the same weights:
  $0.03–$0.35 in, $0.15–$0.95 out.
- **Quantisation varies by host** — int4, fp4, fp8, bf16, fp16 — so the vendor's MXFP4 benchmark
  numbers do not automatically describe every endpoint.

  128,072 at BaseTen, and max output from 4,096 (DigitalOcean) to 117,964.
- Every endpoint advertises `tools` and `tool_choice`, but `supports_tool_choice` is not uniform:
  `none` is false at Near AI, Venice and DekaLLM, and DekaLLM advertises only `auto` (its
  `required` and `function` flags are false).

## Summary

date, a 131,072-token context, mandatory reasoning at three efforts, and live multi-vendor
serving at prices from $0.03/$0.15 to $0.35/$0.75 per 1M tokens. It is a host record, not a
measurement: its benchmark fields are re-published Artificial Analysis numbers.

## Summary

The host record confirms the vendor's identity, price and 1M context from a second
independent catalogue, adds a machine-readable modality declaration (`text+image+video->text`
— video included, which AA's page omits), dates the listing to 2026-10-23, shows a
max_completion_tokens of 943718 (a different ceiling from the vendor's documented 1048576),
and shows that the model is Moonshot-served only, in `mxfp4`, with no EU in-region routing.

## Gaps

- No provider diversity to compare: one upstream, so "host differences" are
  base-vs-highspeed pricing, not vendor-vs-vendor.
- OpenRouter does not label which endpoint is the highspeed variant.
- Uptime is a 30-minute snapshot, not a history.
- Re-published AA/Vals scores carry no capture date of their own.

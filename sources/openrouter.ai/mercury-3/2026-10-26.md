## Reading

- **This is a host record of addressability, not independent measurement.** The only provider
  behind the slug is Inception itself, so every request terminates at the vendor and OpenRouter
  measures nothing of its own. Its value is the metadata (context, output cap, price, modalities,
  parameter list) and the restated third-party scores.
- The restated **Vals** figures match the first-party Vals AI model page exactly once rounded
  (`../../../vals.ai/mercury-3/2026-10-26.md`), which makes them usable, and OpenRouter's
  `design_arena` array is the only place this capture shows Design Arena Elo.
- **Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 3.0%** is the striking number: whatever Mercury 3 is good at, agentic
  terminal work is not it.
- The absence of `seed` and of any `hugging_face_id` corroborate the vendor's own limits.

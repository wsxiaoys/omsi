## Summary

- **Identity confirmed from the host side**: OpenRouter's `hugging_face_id` points at
  `MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M3.1-Flash`, its `canonical_slug` carries the date **2026-10-19**, and the
  page states "Released Oct 19, 2026". Host and vendor agree on the date.
- **Context**: OpenRouter reports **1,048,576** (2^20) while the vendor says **1,000,000**;
  OpenRouter also caps `max_completion_tokens` at **524,288**, which matches the vendor's
  documented 512K output maximum.
- **Modalities**: text + image + video in, text out — matches the vendor card. Artificial
  Analysis lists only text and image
  ([AA](../../artificialanalysis.ai/minimax-m3.1-flash/2026-10-26.md)), so the video claim rests
  on vendor and host documentation.
- **Ten providers serve it on OpenRouter, all at the vendor's list price** except two
  promotional discounts (GMICloud 5%, SambaNova 50%). The host records no independent price
  discovery: the model is effectively price-anchored to MiniMax's $0.15/$0.60.
- **Independent benchmark rows exist**: AA Index 42.7, and Vals AI publishes nine separate
  evaluations including Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 25.3% — a third harness figure for a benchmark
  where the vendor claims 38.1.

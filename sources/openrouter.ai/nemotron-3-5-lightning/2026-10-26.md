## Summary

OpenRouter confirms Lightning as a text-only 262K-context small tier with tool calling and
reasoning control, priced between $0.025 and $0.07 per million input tokens across four
providers — about 2.4x cheaper on input and 2.9x on output than the flagship's cheapest route
($0.06/$0.35 at Darkbloom), and 6x/4.2x cheaper than the flagship's modal $0.15/$0.50 tier. The
1M-token context
in the card and in AA is **not** available on any OpenRouter route.

## Gaps

- Uptime is a 30-minute snapshot per endpoint, not a history.
- OpenRouter does not say which quantization the "Io Net" route uses.
- The `:free` variant advertises 1,000,000 context at $0 with a 65,536 completion cap; not
  exercised, so its real limits are unknown.
- No independent measurement of this model was run; all scores are restatements.

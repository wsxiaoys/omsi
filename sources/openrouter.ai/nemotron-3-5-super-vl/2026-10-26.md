Three findings from this table:

1. **Input price spans 5x** ($0.06 Darkbloom to $0.30 Nebius) and **output price spans 3.6x**
   ($0.35 to $1.25) for the same weights. OpenRouter's headline $0.15/$0.50 is the modal
   tier (4 of 9 endpoints), not the cheapest.
2. **Full 1M context is a minority service.** Only CoreWeave, BaseTen and Nebius advertise
   1,048,576; the other six cap at 262,144, which matches DeepInfra's catalogue limit and is
   well under the model card's "up to 1M tokens".
3. **Cheapest is not the fastest quantized tier you would expect.** Darkbloom's nvfp4 route at
   $0.06/$0.35 is one tenth the output price of Venice's fp4 route.

The `max_completion_tokens` ceiling also varies from 32,768 (Darkbloom, Venice) to 943,718
(CoreWeave, BaseTen, Nebius) — a 29x spread on the same model.

## Summary

OpenRouter confirms the identity, modalities (including **video** input, which AA's page
denies), tool use and reasoning control, and prices the model nine ways between $0.06 and
$0.30 per million input tokens. It is the most useful single source on the model's commercial
reality: the same weights are sold at a 5x input spread with three different context ceilings,
and only three providers honour the card's 1M-token claim.

## Gaps

- Uptime is a 30-minute snapshot per endpoint, not a history.
- OpenRouter does not say which quantization the "Together" route uses.
- The `hugging_face_id` alias 401s; no attempt was made to call the API, so per-endpoint
  behaviour (reasoning, tool use, vision) is untested.
- Re-published AA and Design Arena scores carry no capture date of their own.
- The `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl:free` variant was read but not captured in detail; it
  advertises 1,000,000 context with a 131,072 completion cap at $0, and is rate-limited.

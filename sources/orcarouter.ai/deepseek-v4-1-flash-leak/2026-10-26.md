# OrcaRouter analysis of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash

- **Origin:** "DeepSeek V4.1 Flash Goes GA: 552B Under a New Architecture, MIT Weights,
  and a V4 Pro Sunset That Never Happened", OrcaRouter blog,
  <https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/deepseek-v4-1-flash-leak>. First published 2026-09-08,
  updated through 2026-10-26. Retrieved 2026-10-26.
- **Evidence kind:** third-party analysis. Not a measurement — it reports and interprets
  other parties' numbers. Treated here as a **lead and a cross-check**, not as primary
  evidence.
- **Capture type:** full-page text capture, ~136k characters. Only the parts bearing on
  checkpoint verification are summarised.

## The claim this record exists for

OrcaRouter reports Artificial Analysis's 2026-10-18 addition of a separate `1017` row:

> *"Artificial Analysis added a separate row for the 1017 checkpoint, scored it 45 on its
> Intelligence Index and 33.8 on Terminal-Bench 4.0, and left the September row standing
> at 39 and 26.8."*

And on the gap to the vendor:

> *"Artificial Analysis evaluated the same 1017 weights on Terminal-Bench 4.0 under
> mini-swe-agent — the harness it holds constant across every model on that board — and
> got 33.8, against 26.8 for the September checkpoint on the same harness. On a fixed
> harness the checkpoint is worth seven points, not eleven, and DeepSeek's headline number
> sits eight and a half points above what the same weights score when tested by somebody
> else."*

And on the consequence:

> *"On its own harness the same weights score 33.8 — below GLM-5.3's 41.9, not above it.
> The vendor row is real and it is DeepSeek's; the inference drawn from it does not
> survive being run by somebody else."*

## Other claims in the piece

- The base V4.1-Flash was released **2026-09-10**, after a two-day beta under the ID
  `deepseek-v4.1-flash-expires-on-0910`.
- Weights: 48 safetensors shards, ~510 GB, MIT.
- Argues the model's `deepseek-flash` alias is a rolling name that has already moved once.
- States that nobody has replicated DeepSeek's individual benchmark rows, and that no
  independent measurement exists at production effort settings or at the far end of the
  1M-token window.

## Reliability caveats

- **Secondary source.** The 45 / 33.8 / 39 / 26.8 figures are OrcaRouter's reading of
  Artificial Analysis, not Artificial Analysis's own publication. This wiki's direct AA
  capture confirms 45 for `1017` but does not expose a Terminal-Bench sub-score or a
  September row, so the delta rests on this secondary report.
- **Internal inconsistency with other secondary sources.** A different blog (kie.ai)
  reports the September checkpoint at **40** on the AA index and says AA had *not*
  published `1017` results. OrcaRouter says 39, and says AA published on 2026-10-18. The
  direct leaderboard capture supports OrcaRouter on the existence of a `1017` row; the
  39-vs-40 difference is unresolved and is plausibly index drift.
- A Reddit thread in the same period speculated the model was distilled from GPT-6 Astra
  on the basis of a third-party fingerprint tool that reportedly had no DeepSeek
  reference model in its database. That claim is **not** recorded as evidence here and
  should not be repeated without a proper source.
- OrcaRouter sells inference routing, so it has a commercial interest in
  cost-performance comparisons. The numbers it quotes are checkable; its framing is not
  neutral.

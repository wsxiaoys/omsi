# Pixelcut API: FAQ (commercial use, formats, rate limits, retention)

- **Origin:** Pixelcut Docs, resources, "Frequently Asked Questions",
  <https://pixelcut.ai/docs/developer-guide/resources/faq>
  (markdown at <https://pixelcut.ai/docs/developer-guide/resources/faq.md>).
- **Evidence kind:** official documentation (first-party FAQ).
- **Publication date:** not stated. Capture date 2026-10-26.
- **Capture date:** 2026-10-26
- **Relevant models:** Pixelcut API, background removal included.
- **Capture type:** verbatim quotes for the load-bearing answers, summary for the rest.

## Commercial use and ownership (verbatim)

> **Can I use the API for commercial purposes?**
>
> Yes, you can use the API for commercial purposes such as creating images for use in a website or
> application, or providing image editing features in your own software. You may not use the API to
> create training data for your own models.
>
> **Do I retain copyright of the images I process through the API?**
>
> Yes, you retain copyright of the images you process through the API.

## Cost per call (verbatim)

> For example, removing the background from an image consumes 5 credits. Credits are consumed when
> the API request is successful. If the API request fails, generally no credits are consumed except
> in cases where the failure was due to a safety check violation.

## Technical limits (verbatim)

> **What image formats does the API support?** The API supports JPEG and PNG formats.
>
> Your developer account is rate limited to 5 requests per second by default. You can increase this
> limit by contacting us at api@pixelcut.ai

> **Do your offer SLAs?** We do not offer SLAs for the API at this time. However, these APIs also
> power the Pixelcut mobile and web products which serve millions of people per month so we aim to
> have a very reliable service.

## Data handling (verbatim)

> Images and videos processed through the API are stored only temporarily to operate the API. They
> are kept for at most 24 hours, then automatically and permanently deleted. Result URLs remain
> valid until the file is deleted. Metadata and usage data are retained for billing and analytics
> purposes.

> **Will my data be used for training?** No, we do not use your data for training without your
> consent.

> **Do you share my data with third parties?** No, we do not share your data with third parties
> except as required by law or to provide the service (e.g., using third-party infrastructure
> providers).

## Pricing and plans (verbatim)

> **Are there any pricing tiers or volume discounts?** No, there are no pricing tiers. For volumes
> above 1 million credits, please contact us at api@pixelcut.ai.

## Why this record exists

It is the first-party statement of the commercial-use grant, the no-training-data restriction, the
per-call credit cost, the format list (JPEG/PNG — narrower than the API reference), the default rate
limit, the absence of an SLA, and the 24-hour retention window.

## Contradictions / gaps

- "JPEG and PNG" here and on the Image Requirements page vs "JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF" in the API
  reference ([API reference](../remove-background-api/2026-10-26.md),
  [image requirements](../image-requirements/2026-10-26.md)).
- "Credits are consumed ... except in cases where the failure was due to a safety check violation"
  is the only moderation statement in the docs; the safety system itself is undocumented.
- No statement of maximum concurrent requests, burst allowance, or queue behavior at the 5 req/s
  default.
- The FAQ does not say whether output from the *free app* is commercially licensed (see
  [app pricing plans](../pricing-plans/2026-10-26.md)).

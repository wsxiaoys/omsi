# Pixelcut Docs: "Background Removal" integration guide

- **Origin:** Pixelcut Docs, developer guide, "Background Removal",
  <https://pixelcut.ai/docs/developer-guide/guides/background-removal> (markdown at
  <https://pixelcut.ai/docs/developer-guide/guides/background-removal.md>).
- **Evidence kind:** official documentation (first-party).
- **Publication date:** not stated. Capture date 2026-10-26.
- **Capture date:** 2026-10-26
- **Relevant models:** Pixelcut background removal (`POST /v1/remove-background`).
- **Capture type:** full text capture (the page is short).

## Verbatim content

> The background removal API allows you to remove the background from an image and optionally add
> AI-generated shadows, crop to the subject, and apply margins.

**What to build with it** (verbatim list):

> - Product listing cutouts with transparency
> - Catalog normalization (consistent background removal)
> - Preprocessing step for generation workflows

**For Best Results** (verbatim):

> - Use sharp images with strong subject/background contrast
> - Avoid tiny thumbnails and heavy JPEG artifacts

**Limitations** (verbatim):

> - Does not support translucent materials such as glass.

The page has one illustration, captioned "Background removal example", showing a cutout on a
transparent/checkerboard background. It is a marketing example, not a test set.

## What this record is for

It is the only first-party page that states a **substantive limitation** for the model rather than a
benefit: translucent materials such as glass are out of scope. It also frames the endpoint's
intended use as commerce/catalogue cutouts and as a preprocessing step, not as general-purpose
image editing.

## Gaps

- "Does not support translucent materials" is not quantified: no statement about partial alpha for
  veils, smoke, mesh, or glass edges, and no statement about hair-level alpha detail.
- No resolution guidance beyond the numeric limits in the API reference.
- No quality metric, test set, or comparison to other background removers.
- No date; the guide gives no release or revision marker for the underlying model.

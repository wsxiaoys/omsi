# Pixelcut: app pricing plans (Free / Pro / Business)

- **Origin:** Pixelcut, "Pricing", <https://www.pixelcut.ai/pricing>.
- **Evidence kind:** vendor pricing page (first-party; product plans, not API pricing).
- **Publication date:** not stated. Capture date 2026-10-26.
- **Capture date:** 2026-10-26
- **Relevant models:** Pixelcut app features including background removal; relevant here for the
  free tier and for app-level commercial licensing.
- **Capture type:** verbatim transcription of the plan cards and the feature table.

## Plan cards (verbatim)

| Plan | Price | Background removal line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 per month | "Limited Background Removal"; "Free export without watermark" |
| Pro | $10/mo ($8 yearly) | "Unlimited Background Removal", "Unlimited Upscale", "Commercial license" |
| Business | $30/mo ($24 yearly) | "Unlimited Background Removal", "Commercial license", 3,600 AI credits/mo |

Feature table (verbatim, first rows): Background Removal — Free: "Limited"; Magic Eraser — Free:
"Limited"; Fast Upscaler — Free: "Limited"; Generative Fill — Free "Limited", Pro "300/day",
Business "Up to 1200/day". Credits row — Free "—", Pro "600/mo", Business "Up to 180000/mo".

Also verbatim: "Trusted by over 200,000 creators, brands and small businesses"; "Pixelcut is loved
by over 2 million people".

## Why this record exists

Two things the API docs do not cover:

1. **Free tier of the app.** Background removal is available free but explicitly "Limited"; the
   page does not define the limit. The free plan does include "Free export without watermark",
   which matches the tool page's "completely free ... no watermark" marketing
   ([tool page](../background-remover-tool/2026-10-26.md)) but not the "unlimited" impression.
2. **Commercial licence is a paid-plan line item.** "Commercial license" appears on Pro and
   Business only, and nowhere on the Free card. That is a pricing-page claim, not a licence
   document, but it is the only app-side statement about commercial rights found.

The credits table on the same page lists third-party image/video models by name (Nano Banana,
FLUX, Seedream, Kling, Veo, etc.) with "up to N images" per plan. **Background Removal is not in
that table**, i.e. the app does not meter background removal against third-party-model credits; it
meters it with the "Limited/Unlimited" plan feature instead. That is consistent with background
removal being Pixelcut's own model rather than a resold third-party one.

## Gaps

- "Limited Background Removal" is never quantified (per day? per file? watermarked?).
- The page does not say whether output made on the Free plan may be used commercially; the absence
  of "Commercial license" on the Free card is suggestive, not conclusive.
- No date, no changelog for plan changes.
- App pricing and API pricing are separate systems ("API credits are not the same as app credits",
  per [API pricing](../api-pricing/2026-10-26.md)); the page does not mention the API at all.

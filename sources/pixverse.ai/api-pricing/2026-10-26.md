- Evidence kind: official vendor price list (first-party)
- Publication date: undated page; content current at capture (V6 Max rates present)
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: PixVerse V6, V6 Max, C1, V5.6, V5.5, V5

## Billing basis (verbatim)

> 225 Credits = USD 1; 1 Credit ≈ USD 0.004444.

> `Resp.credits` is the request-level Credit value. Use it for estimates and preliminary
> reconciliation; the account ledger determines the final charge.

> USD amounts use total Credits ÷ 225, rounded to three decimal places.

Section heading for the V6 rates: "C1, V6, V6 Max, and V6 Video References — Billed per generated
second." Also:

> V6 Extend uses the applicable V6 resolution, audio, and generated-duration rate, not a separate
> flat rate.

> V6 Fusion with `video_references` uses twice the standard V6 rate.

> V6 MAX (v6-max) Only support text-to-video/image-to-video now

Published worked examples:

> V6, 720p, audio on, 8 seconds → 12 x 8 = 96 Credits → US$0.427

> V6 Fusion with video references, 720p, audio off, 5 seconds → 18 x 5 = 90 Credits → US$0.400

For contrast, V5.6 and V5.5 are **fixed Credits per request** by duration tier (V5.6: 5 s = 35–75
credits depending on quality; 8 s = 70–150; 10 s = 77–99 with 1080p "Not supported"). V5.5 splits
single-shot and multi-shot tables; V5 has only 5 s and 8 s rows.

## Summary

V6 is the first PixVerse generation model billed **per generated second** at the vendor's own API,
whereas V5/V5.5/V5.6 are billed per fixed-duration request. Audio costs roughly 40 % more than silent
output at 360p–720p and roughly 28 % more at 1080p. Video-reference ("Fusion") requests cost exactly
twice the standard V6 rate at every resolution. At the vendor's own conversion, 5 seconds of 720p V6
with audio is ≈ $0.27 — the cheapest published route to V6 output captured here.

The credit-to-USD rate (225 credits = $1) is a documentation conversion; the page itself says the
account ledger is final.

## Gaps

- No regional price variation, volume tier, or enterprise discount is published.
- No refund policy detail beyond "qualifying failed, moderated, or timed-out tasks may be refunded"

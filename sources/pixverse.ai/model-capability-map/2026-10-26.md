## Summary

This is the clearest statement of where V6 sits in the line. V6 is **not** the top tier: V6 Max is
listed first and is described as the quality tier, but V6 Max is narrower (text/image only — no
first/last frame, no multi-subject references). V6 is the widest-capability tier in the family: it is
the only model with **video references** and **video extend**, and it supports first/last frame and
multi-subject image references that V6 Max lacks.

The V5/V5.5/V5.6 line is visibly a different generation: fixed 5/8/10-second durations, five aspect
ratios, and V5 alone has the older multi-transition feature. V6's 1–15-second range and eight aspect
ratios are the structural break, not a marketing label.

## Gaps

- V5.5 is absent from this page's five columns even though the API enums and price list still include
  `v5.5`; the map is a "current choices" table, not a complete model list.
- No frame rate, no file/container spec, no quality benchmark, no per-model latency.
- "Determined by the template" is not expanded; the template list is a live API, not a document.

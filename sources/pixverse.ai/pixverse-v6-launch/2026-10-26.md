## Summary

The only first-party launch announcement captured. It fixes the public launch at **2026-03-30** and
establishes V6 as the successor to the V5 line at the top of PixVerse's model range *at that date*.
The capability language is qualitative: camera control, character performance, multilingual
in-frame text, and single-prompt multi-shot generation with jointly generated audio. The post
volunteers two weaknesses (directional control in complex scenes, consistency across large spatial
changes), which is unusual for launch copy and worth recording as the vendor's own gap list.

It does not state duration, resolution, aspect ratios, frame rate, parameter names, or prices. Those
appear only in the platform documentation captured separately.

## Gaps

- No technical report, benchmark, or sample-count claim; "measurable improvements" is unquantified.
- No statement about model architecture, training data, or weights.
- Does not mention V6 Max (which the platform docs add on 2026-10-22) or C1 (2026-04-07); the model
  line has since grown above and beside V6.

## Captured content (verbatim)

Restatement of the launch date and envelope:

> PixVerse announced V6 on March 30, 2026, and the official PixVerse V6 Platform Docs list 1-15 second
> generation, 360p to 1080p quality options, audio switches, and multi-clip support for applicable
> flows.

Vendor's own scoping of its "hands-on" evidence:

> Evidence level: Qualitative hands-on review based on the showcased outputs, not a large-sample
> statistical pass-rate study

> Test period: March 2026 … Product surface: PixVerse Web with PixVerse V6 selected … Target output:
> 15-second 1080p clips when available

> Our hands-on verdict: PixVerse V6 is strongest when a prompt depends on camera movement, character
> continuity, short narrative structure, or audio-visual coordination. It is not a magic "one try
> solves everything" model. Complex action, multilingual dialogue, and product-accurate scenes still
> deserve review, retries, and clear success criteria.

Credit arithmetic:

> V6 is billed per second; 1080p is listed at 18 credits/s without audio and 23 credits/s with audio.
> A 15-second 1080p clip costs 270 credits without audio or 345 credits with audio before retries.

V6 vs V5.6, in the vendor's own words:

| Area | PixVerse V5.6 | PixVerse V6 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duration pattern | "Listed in fixed 5s, 8s, and 10s examples in pricing docs" | "1-15s duration in V6 docs" |
| Cost model | "Pricing varies by quality, duration, and audio for fixed clip examples" | "Per-second V6 credit rates by resolution and audio setting" |
| Workflow fit | "Short standalone social clips and quick visual ideas" | "Longer short-form scenes, narrative tests, transitions, extension, and reference-to-video" |
| Audio | "Available in priced variants" | "Documented as a generation switch in V6 workflows" |

> This does not mean V6 is automatically better for every job.

Three self-described stress tests are reported (fox-demon dialogue, bee POV, city-destruction action),
each with a "Main limitation" column admitting the sample is one showcased output.

## Summary

Vendor-authored review, so it is marketing with a methodology section. Its value is that it states the
V6/V5.6 differences in the vendor's own framing — duration model, billing basis, and workflow fit —
which corroborates the API documents rather than adding new capability. It also quantifies credit cost
for a worst-case clip (15 s, 1080p, audio on = 345 credits ≈ $1.53 at the documented 225 credits/$).

The honest "qualitative, not a pass-rate study" line is worth preserving: it means PixVerse itself
does not claim a measured quality delta for V6.

## Gaps

- The three "tests" report no prompts beyond paraphrase, no seeds, no repeat counts, and no
  comparison against V5.6 outputs on the same prompt.
- No benchmark numbers, no failure rates.

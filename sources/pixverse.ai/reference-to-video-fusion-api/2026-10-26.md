## Captured content (verbatim)

> Image references and video references share this endpoint. Video references require model=v6,
> reference_mode=omni, and duration=0. Up to 2 reference videos with combined duration no greater
> than 15 seconds; output duration follows the longest reference. V6 Omni supports up to 10 image
> references. Image-only mode supports the listed compatible models subject to account access. At
> least one nonempty reference collection is required.

## Summary

This is V6's distinguishing capability. Video references are **V6-only** in the whole PixVerse line
(the capability map gives "Video References: Supported" to V6 and "Not supported / Not applicable" to
V6 Max, C1, V5.6 and V5). The documented limits are concrete: at most two reference videos, combined
≤ 15 s, and the output length follows the longest reference, which is why `duration` must be sent as
`0`. Up to ten image references can be mixed in, each optionally labelled `subject` or `background`.

The pricing page charges V6 Fusion with `video_references` at exactly twice the standard V6 per-second
rate.

## Gaps

- No documented limit on reference video resolution, file size, or codec here (upload endpoint
  documentation was not captured in that detail).
- No statement of what "output duration follows the longest reference" means when two references
  differ substantially in length.
- No quality guidance on how much a reference video constrains camera or subject identity.

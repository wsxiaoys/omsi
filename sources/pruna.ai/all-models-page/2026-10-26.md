## Summary

The only first-party latency figures: 1.99 s for a `p-video-2-pro` text-to-video run at 480p, and
0.97 s / 1 s for the avatar and reference variants. The page does not say what clip length,
duration setting or hardware those times correspond to, so they cannot be turned into a cost or a
throughput figure. They are the basis for the landing page's "real-time" language.

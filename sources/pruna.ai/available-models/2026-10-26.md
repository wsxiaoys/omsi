## Prior state: the same page on 2026-05-14

Internet Archive capture `20260514080812`
(<https://web.archive.org/web/20260514080812/https://docs.api.pruna.ai/guides/models>) lists only
**`p-video`, `p-video-avatar`, `p-image-upscale`** in the Pruna section — no `p-video-2`,
`p-video-2-pro`, `p-video-edit`, `p-video-animate`, `p-video-replace`, `p-video-2-pro-reference` or
`p-video-2-pro-avatar`. That capture predates the whole P-Video-2 line.

## Summary

Two things the catalogue establishes: the P-Video-2-Pro family is **newer than 2026-05-14**, and
Pruna itself describes every member of it as "built on MiniMax H3" while the older `p-video` and
`p-video-2` rows carry no base-model note. The 250/min rate limit for `p-video-2-pro` is the highest
of the video line; the avatar/reference products run at 50/min.

## Gaps

- No dates, no version strings, no deprecation markers on any row.
- "Built on Minimax H3" is the only first-party statement of the base model in the catalogue; the
  `p-video-2` row does not name LTX-2 even though its own page links the LTX-2 licence.

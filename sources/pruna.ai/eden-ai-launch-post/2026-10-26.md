## Dated statements (verbatim)

> Our models are now available through Eden AI, the European AI gateway, so teams can put them into
> production behind one key and one bill.

> In Eden AI, P-Video runs at $0.02 per second of output at 720p and $0.04 at 1080p. P-Video-2 is the
> higher-quality step up at $0.025 and $0.05, with draft mode at $0.015 and $0.03. P-Video-2-Pro is
> the top of the range, billed per second of returned video at $0.02 at 480p and $0.035 at 768p in
> speed mode, and $0.04 and $0.075 in quality mode.

> Pruna is based in Munich and Paris, and Eden AI is a French company.

> Our video models expose that as a `draft` flag, at roughly half the price of a full-quality render.

The `draft` flag sentence describes the P-Video / P-Video-2 models; P-Video-2-Pro has no `draft`
parameter in either vendor or host documentation.

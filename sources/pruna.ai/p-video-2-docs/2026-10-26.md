## Overview (verbatim)

> p-video-2 is Pruna's video generation model supporting text-to-video, image-to-video, and
> audio-conditioned generation. Same inputs as p-video. It offers up to 1080p resolution at 24 or
> 48 fps, with an optional duration of up to 20 seconds — leave it empty and the model picks the
> length from the prompt.

> Rate Limit: 250 requests per minute

> Pricing: billed per second of the video returned.

Prices: 720p `draft=false` $0.025/s, 720p `draft=true` $0.015/s, 1080p `draft=false` $0.05/s,
1080p `draft=true` $0.03/s.

Parameters differ from the Pro model in ways that matter for identity: `duration` 1–20 (empty =
model-chosen), `audio` (flac/mp3/wav input), `fps` 24 or 48, `draft` boolean, `save_audio`,
`prompt_upsampling` boolean, `disable_safety_filter`.

## Summary

`p-video-2` is built on **LTX-2** (Lightricks), not on MiniMax H3: it exposes 48 fps, a 20-second
maximum, audio-conditioned generation and a `draft` flag, none of which `p-video-2-pro` has, and its
only licence link is the LTX-2 repository licence. "Pro" therefore is **not** a quality tier of
`p-video-2`; the two share a product name and a price band, not a base model.

## Gaps

- The page never says "LTX" or "Lightricks" in prose; the base model is named only by the licence
  URL.
- No date, no statement of what `draft` changes beyond "faster, lower-quality preview".

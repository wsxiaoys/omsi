## Overview (verbatim)

> p-video-2-pro-avatar turns a portrait and speech into a talking video with synced lips and
> expressions. Provide a portrait plus either an audio file or a script. Uploaded audio sets the
> timing; a script is spoken with text-to-speech. Output is a 24 fps MP4 from 1 second to 3 minutes.

Pricing: 480p $0.02/s, 768p $0.04/s, 1080p $0.06/s, 2k $0.08/s, 4k $0.12/s; 1080p and above upscale
the 768p video with `p-video-upscale`, and "4k is limited to 125 seconds of audio".

> A 60 second video is $1.20 at 480p, $2.40 at 768p or $7.20 at 4k.

Inputs: `image` (portrait, first frame, jpg/jpeg/png/webp ≤ 20 MiB) plus `audio` (≤ 64 MiB, ≤ 3 min)
or `voice_script`; 30 named TTS voices; `voice_language` is one of English (US), English (UK),
Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Korean, Hindi; `voice_prompt`
delivery instructions; `prompt` default "The person is talking."; `prompt_upsampling` on by default.

> `prompt_upsampling` is on by default. A vision model looks at the portrait and appends the scene's
> own motion (rain, lights, background, body movement) to `prompt`.

## Summary

A separate model ID, not a mode of `p-video-2-pro`: it takes a portrait plus speech and returns a
lip-synced talking video of 1 s to 3 min at 24 fps, with TTS in ten languages. It is the only
sibling that documents its output container (MP4).

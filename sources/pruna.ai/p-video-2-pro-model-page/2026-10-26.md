## Headline (verbatim)

> Introducing P-Video-2-Pro

> Based on MiniMax H3. The best quality. The best speed.

> Pro video generation from text and images with native audio.

> Generate videos from text or images at up to 768p, with generated audio and Cost, Speed or Quality
> modes.

Per-mode blurbs and prices ("From …"):

> P-Video-2-Pro Cost — From $0.01/s — Lower-cost, high-quality generations when speed isn't the
> priority.

> P-Video-2-Pro Quality — From $0.035/s — Higher-quality generation for final videos and production
> use.

> P-Video-2-Pro Speed — From $0.02/s — Fast, lower-cost previews for testing prompts and creative
> directions.

Note the mismatch against the docs price table: here Quality "from $0.035/s" and Speed "from
$0.02/s" are the *480p* rates of the other mode. The docs give 480p cost/speed/quality =
$0.01/$0.02/$0.04 and 768p = $0.025/$0.035/$0.075.

## Benchmark claim

> Performance Benchmark: Efficiency & Quality

> P-Video-2-Pro delivers quality on par with SoTA model.

> To find more benchmarks check out P-Bench.

The chart is a client-rendered SVG scatter with "Datapoint Elo score" on y (gridlines 955, 977,
1000, 1023, 1045) and "Generation price per second($)" on a log x-axis (ticks 0.05, 0.1, 0.2). Plot
points are labelled: Agnes Video 2.5, Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash, Grok Imagine Video 1.5, LTX 2.5 Pro,
MiniMax H3 Max Turbo, P-Video-2, P-Video-2 Pro Cost, P-Video-2 Pro Quality, P-Video-2 Pro Speed,
Seedance 2.5 Turbo, Veo 3.1 Lite. **No numeric table, sample size, harness or date is published with
it.** Reading point positions off the SVG places P-Video-2 Pro Quality slightly above the 1023
gridline, Cost and Speed level with each other around 1012, and P-Video-2 around 988 — a
reconstruction from pixel geometry, not a published number, and recorded here as such.

## Summary

The vendor's own framing of the model: a Pruna-tuned deployment of MiniMax H3, marketed on
speed/price rather than on a new architecture, with a "quality on par with SoTA" claim supported only
by an unlabelled scatter plot on Pruna's own benchmark. It is the only page that calls the work
"real-time".

## Gaps

- No launch date, no model card, no technical report.
- "Real-time" is undefined — no latency, resolution or clip length attached to it.
- The per-mode "from" prices on this page do not line up with the docs table at the same resolution
  (see above); no page reconciles them.
- P-Bench methodology, vote counts and per-model scores were not captured.

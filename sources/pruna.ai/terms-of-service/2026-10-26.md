> Customers accessing Pruna Models via API may, upon reaching a revenue threshold defined in the

## Summary

Access is API-only for all Pruna Models, and the P-Video-2-Pro family additionally inherits the
MiniMax H3 Community License: its Acceptable Use Policy applies, outputs may not be used to train
other models, and downstream users must be bound to the same restrictions. The territory clause
(item 1) is waived for use *through Pruna's service*. Output ownership sits with the customer "as
between the parties", subject to the third-party licence terms.

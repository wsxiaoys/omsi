## What this establishes

- **OXR's 87.84 is the top self-reported entry on the Hugging Face olmOCR-bench leaderboard**, out of
  22 entries, as read from the public API. That is a real fact about the leaderboard, and it is the
  only place where the number appears outside the vendor's own repository.
- **It is not independent verification.** The same 87.84 is the value in the weights repository's
  `.eval_results/olmocrbench.yaml`
  ([weights metadata](../../huggingface.co/somark-oxr-1-0-weights-metadata/2026-10-27.md)); QuantID
  reads that file back. The aggregator explicitly disclaims independent evaluation and notes that
  most entries are author-submitted.
- **The document-parsing leaderboard space is crowded and split.** OXR leads olmOCR-bench while
  PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 leads Real5-OmniDocBench and KDL-Frontier-Parser-nano leads ParseBench, which is a
  caution against treating any single "#1" as a general parsing verdict.

## Gaps

- The article is a daily snapshot; it does not publish per-entry metadata (hardware, harness version,
  submission date) for the olmOCR-bench row.
- No confidence intervals, and no statement of whether the 22 entries were run under one harness.
- The map reads leaderboard data only; it does not run models.

- `qwenlm.github.io/blog/` is now a meta-refresh redirect to `https://qwen.ai/research`. Its RSS

  Safety for Your Token Stream" (2025-09-23). It therefore cannot cover a 2026 release. The newest
  image posts in that feed are "Qwen-Image-Edit" (2025-08-19) and "Qwen-Image" (2025-08-04).
- `https://qwen.ai/research` renders a "Latest Advancements" block of five items plus a "Research
  Index". In the captured render the five latest items were dated 2026-09-20 (Qwen-Image-2.1,
  Open-Source), 2026-09-18 (Qwen3.8-LiveTranslate), 2026-09-18 (Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash), 2026-09-03
  (E-Commerce Bench) and 2026-09-03 (Qwen-Drive-1.0). **No Qwen Image 3 post was visible.**
  The Qwen-Image-2.1 item reads (verbatim, truncated in the index): "We are excited to open-source
  Qwen-Image-2.1, an image model in the Qwen family that balances generation quality, inference
  efficiency, and cost." That is the team's own statement that the 2.x image line is open weights,
  which contrasts with the absence of any equivalent statement for Image 3.
- The index is paginated client-side through `GET https://qwen.ai/api/v2/article/retrieval`. Probes
  with `page/pageSize`, `pageNum/pageSize`, `pageNo/pageSize`, `current/size` and `page/limit`
  all returned HTTP 200 with `{"articles":[]}`, so older entries could not be enumerated.
- `https://qwen.ai/models` is generic marketing copy; its "Image Generation" feature block still
  describes **Qwen VLo**, and its "Latest Research" block repeats the Qwen-Image-2.1 item. It does
  not mention Qwen Image 3.

## What this establishes

- No Qwen blog post announcing Qwen Image 3 was located on 2026-10-26 through the Qwen team's own
  publication channels.
- Consequently this page's capability and pricing claims rest on **Alibaba Cloud Model Studio**
  (the same vendor's product documentation) rather than on a launch blog post.

## Gaps

- This is a **failed search, not proof of absence**. The archive endpoint returned empty result
  sets for every parameter shape tried, so posts older than the five rendered on the index were
  never enumerated; a July 2026 Qwen Image 3 post may exist and simply not be reachable this way.
- The capture is of a rendered page, not of an API payload, so item ordering and completeness
  reflect the proxy's render rather than the site's data model.
- No Qwen Image 3 model card, technical report, or benchmark table was found in this channel; if
  one exists, it is not captured here.

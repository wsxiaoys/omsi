The post never states a context window, a price, or a modality table. Those come from the
Model Studio and QwenCloud model pages.

## Long-horizon demonstrations (vendor self-reports, no independent check)

Three worked examples, each a vendor-run autonomous run rather than a benchmark:

- A 10+ day autonomous coding run building a "self-evolving harness" (`oh-my-cli`). As of
  **July 30, 2026**, "after approximately **16 days** of fully autonomous AI operation, the
  repository had accumulated **265 commits, 127 PRs, and 151 issues**".
- Reproduction-then-improvement of an external paper: "Working **completely on its own for
  about five days** (~125 hours of continuous effort), Qwen3.8-Max wrote roughly **7,600
  lines of code**, took over **1,100 actions**, and ran **33 rounds of GPU training**";
  then "inventing and testing **18 improvement ideas of its own across four rounds**" and
  reaching "a **+2.7-point** gain on the competition-level math benchmark AIME24".
- E-Commerce Bench, a 365-day simulated retail operation: "**a net profit exceeding
  ¥100,000 during the year-end major promotion period**—nearly 2.4 times that of the
  second-place GLM 5.2."

These are demonstrations and vendor-authored in-house benchmarks. They establish what Qwen
chose to show, not a measured capability level.

Two consequences worth keeping: the Qwen3.8-Max column is a **best-of-two-harnesses** figure
on DeepSWE 1.1, and several benchmarks (QwenSWEBench, QwenQoderBench, QwenReactBench,
QwenSVGBench, CoWorkBench) are **Qwen-authored and not publicly reproducible**. Six of the
30 rows are Qwen's own benchmarks.

## What this establishes

- Canonical launch date **2026-08-03** for Qwen3.8-Max, and that the open weights were
  promised "next week" rather than shipped with the post.
- That the hosted Max SKU is 2.4T total / 95B active, built on the Qwen3.5 architecture.
- Vendor-reported benchmark levels, including the rows where it beats Opus 4.8
  (PaperBench 93.0 vs 80.3, FrontierSWE 73.5 vs 70.0, WideSearch 81.9 vs 72.9) and the
  rows where it loses (SWE-bench Pro 67.7 vs 69.2, DeepSWE 1.1 56.6 vs 59.0, HLE 43.6 vs
  45.7).

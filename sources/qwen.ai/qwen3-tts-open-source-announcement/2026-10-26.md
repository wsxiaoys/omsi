## Captured content (verbatim)

> **Qwen3-TTS** is a series of powerful speech generation capabilities developed by Qwen, offering
> comprehensive support for voice clone, voice design, ultra-high-quality human-like speech
> generation, and natural language-based voice control. … Utilizing Dual-Track modeling, Qwen3-TTS
> achieves extreme bidirectional streaming generation speeds, where the first audio packet is
> delivered after processing just a single character. The entire Qwen3-TTS multi-codebook model
> series is now open-sourced, featuring two sizes: 1.7B and 0.6B. … The models support 10 mainstream
> languages (Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Russian, Portuguese, Spanish, and
> Italian) along with various dialects to meet global application demands. … **Now open-sourced on
> GitHub and accessible via the [Qwen API](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/qwen-tts-voice-design).**

Model list: identical five-checkpoint table as the README (1.7B VoiceDesign / CustomVoice / Base,
0.6B CustomVoice / Base; 10 languages; streaming ✅ on all; instruction control ✅ on the three
1.7B checkpoints and the 0.6B CustomVoice — note this differs from the README, which shows no
instruction-control tick for 0.6B-CustomVoice).

Performance claims, verbatim:

> * In voice design tasks: Qwen3-TTS-VoiceDesign outperformed the MiniMax-Voice-Design closed-source
>   model in both instruction-following capability and generative expressiveness on the
>   InstructTTS-Eval benchmark …
> * In voice control tasks: Qwen3-TTS-Instruct demonstrates single-speaker multilingual
>   generalization with an average Word Error Rate (WER) of 2.34%. It also features the ability to
>   maintain timbre while providing precise style control, achieving a score of 75.4% on
>   InstructTTS-Eval. Furthermore, it shows exceptional long-form speech generation capabilities,
>   with a WER of 2.36% (Chinese) and 2.81% (English) during continuous 10-minute synthesis.
> * In voice clone tasks: Qwen3-TTS-VoiceClone surpassed MiniMax and SeedTTS in speech stability for
>   both Chinese and English cloning on Seed-tts-eval. On the TTS multilingual test set across 10
>   languages, it achieved an average WER of 1.835% and a speaker similarity of 0.789 …

Tokenizer claims: PESQ 3.21 wideband / 3.68 narrowband, STOI 0.96, UTMOS 4.16, speaker similarity
0.95 on LibriSpeech test-clean.

The blog's voice-design section shows instruction examples in both Chinese and English covering
acoustic attributes, age, gradual change, "human-likeness" and full character background sheets;

timbres.

## What this establishes

- The open-weights release is a family, not a single model: two sizes × {Base (clone),
  CustomVoice (9 timbres + instruction), VoiceDesign (1.7B only)}.
- The vendor's own access path for the hosted version is the Alibaba Cloud Model Studio Qwen-TTS
  API — in particular a `/qwen-tts-voice-design` page, which by capture date redirects to the
  generic Model Studio voice-design guide (see the Model Studio record).
- Every quality number on the page is vendor-run; the page names the benchmarks but publishes them
  as images (`table1.png`, `table2.png`, `table3.png`), so only the README's HTML tables are
  machine-readable.

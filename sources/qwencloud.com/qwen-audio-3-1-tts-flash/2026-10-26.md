## Summary

A second vendor-operated platform sells the 3.1 TTS family under the same model IDs and the same
prices, but only the Flash tier. Anyone arriving through Qwen Cloud cannot buy `qwen-audio-3.1-tts-plus`
at all, which is a route gap rather than an identity question — Model Studio sells it in both
Singapore and Beijing.

## Gaps

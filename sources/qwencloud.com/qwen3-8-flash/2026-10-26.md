## Built-in tools (verbatim list)

`code_interpreter`, `i2i_search`, `t2i_search`, `web_extractor`, `web_search` (Responses
API); `pdf_parsing` (Completions API); `web_fetch` and `web_search` (Anthropic API).

Also listed as features: prefix completion ("Partial Mode"), function calling, context
cache, structured outputs, batches, web search, and a fine-tuning entry (the fine-tuning
link is present although Model Studio marks fine-tuning Unsupported for this model — a
contradiction between the two vendor pages).

## What this establishes

- The hosted SKU's modality set (text, image, video in; text out) and its 1M / 991K
  context numbers, on a second first-party surface.
- That the $0.15 / $0.47 figures quoted in the launch post are the Singapore list prices.
- That QwenCloud, like Model Studio, sells no `qwen3.8-flash-next` ID.

## Gaps

- No mention of `Qwen3.8-Flash-Next` anywhere on the page; the relationship to the open
  weights is stated only in the blog and the Hugging Face card.
- No snapshot list or serving-checkpoint date.
- The fine-tuning feature link conflicts with Model Studio's "Fine-tuning Unsupported".

- Evidence kind: provider policy (contract terms governing API use)

## Captured content

### Scope (verbatim)

> These Developer Terms of Service ("Developer Terms") are a legally binding agreement between you
> ("Developer," "you," or "your") and Recraft Inc. ... and govern your access to and use of the
> Recraft application programming interface ("API"), the Recraft Model Context Protocol ("MCP")
> server, and related software, tools, models, documentation, and services

> Where Developer has entered into a separate written agreement with Recraft governing access to,
> resale of, or distribution of the Recraft Developer Services, including any reseller,
> marketplace, platform, or model distribution agreement, that agreement prevails over these
> Developer Terms to the extent of any inconsistency.

### Output ownership (verbatim, §6.2)

> "Assets" means images or other content generated by the Recraft API in response to your Inputs.
> You own all Assets you create with the API Services, and Recraft hereby assigns to you all
> copyright rights it may have in the Assets, for personal or commercial use, subject to the
> following restriction: Assets may not be used to train any artificial intelligence model, system,
> network, or similar technology. Any breach of this restriction shall result in the immediate
> termination of these Developer Terms, and all rights granted to you shall revert to Recraft.

### No training on API assets (verbatim, §6.3)

> Recraft will not use any Assets created using the API Services to train Recraft AI models or for
> any other use in any Recraft Services, except as may be necessary for customer support for the
> customer that created the Asset, and to comply with applicable law and enforce Recraft policies.
> For the avoidance of doubt, the "Licenses to Recraft" provision in the General Terms of Service
> does not apply to the API Services.

### Provenance marking (verbatim, §6.4)

> In order to comply with applicable laws and emerging industry standards relating to AI-generated
> content, Recraft may embed machine-readable metadata, watermarks, or other provenance information
> into Assets. ... you will not remove, obscure, disable, or alter any machine-readable metadata,
> watermarks, or other markings applied by Recraft to Assets, except as may be expressly permitted
> by applicable law

### License grant (verbatim, §3.1, abridged)

> Recraft grants you a non-exclusive, limited, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, non-assignable,
> freely revocable license during the Term to: (i) access and use the Recraft API solely to
> develop, test, and operate applications or services that integrate with Recraft's image
> generation and editing capabilities ... All Services are licensed and not sold.

### Relevant restrictions (verbatim, §3.2, abridged)

Prohibited: using the API or Assets "to develop goods or services that compete with Recraft";
reverse engineering the models; scraping or extracting data other than as expressly permitted.

## Gaps

- These are Recraft's terms for *its own* API. Generation through fal's or Replicate's endpoints is
  governed by those hosts' terms plus whatever distribution agreement Recraft has with them (§1.4
  acknowledges such agreements exist but does not publish them). No host record captured here
  states which Recraft agreement covers resale.
- The terms were updated 2026-10-26-adjacent (effective 20 October 2026); earlier versions were not
  captured, so a licensing change on that date would not be visible.
- No statement about whether output may be used to train models you do not own, or about
  indemnification.

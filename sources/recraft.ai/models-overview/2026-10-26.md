## Captured content

### How model names encode variants (verbatim)

> * names ending in `_vector` produce vector (SVG) images; all other models produce raster images;
> * `_pro` variants generate at a higher resolution (2K raster) with finer detail;
> * `_utility` variants (Recraft V4.1 only) produce clean, predictable images with flat lighting
>   and simple composition;
> * `_flash` variants (Recraft V4.1 only) are fast, low-cost raster models without style support;
> * `_styles` models are dedicated style-driven models that always require a style.
> * every raster model also accepts a `_raster` alias of the same model, for example
>   `recraftv4_1_raster` for `recraftv4_1` or `recraftv4_pro_raster` for `recraftv4_pro`. The
>   aliases behave exactly like the base names

> When no `model` is specified, Generate image defaults to `recraftv4_1`.

## Summary

The page fixes the V4.1 release date at 2026-05-14 and prices nine versions per image: base 1K
raster $0.035, vector $0.08, Pro 2K raster $0.21, Pro vector $0.30, Utility 1K $0.035, Utility
Pro 2K $0.21, Utility vector $0.08, Utility Pro vector $0.30, and Flash $0.007. Median latency
ranges from 1.3 s (Flash) to 17 s (Utility Pro Vector); the page warns that latencies vary and
that these are medians. Pro is positioned as higher resolution/finer detail; Utility as flat
lighting and predictable composition; Vector as editable SVG.

## Gaps

- The comparisons are vendor-run and vendor-selected, with no seeds, sample sizes, rater counts,
  or blind protocol. They are marketing, not measurement.
- No pixel dimensions are given for 1K or 2K on this page; the appendix has the size table
  (`../limits-and-sizes/2026-10-26.md`).
- The "Fastest Model on the Market" claim is unqualified and not benchmarked here.
- "Available to test on all plan types, including the Free plan" is an access statement, not a
  licensing statement; the app's Free-tier output ownership rules still apply
  (`../terms-of-service/2026-10-26.md`).
- No deprecation policy, no statement of what the Pro tier changes for vector output beyond
  price/latency, and no image-to-image pricing.

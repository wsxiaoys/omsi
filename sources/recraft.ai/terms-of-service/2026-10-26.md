## Why this matters for V4 Pro

Recraft's V4 page states that all V4 versions, including Pro, are "available to every user,
including Free plan users" (`../recraft-models-v4/2026-10-26.md`). That is an *access* statement,
not a licensing one: on the Free tier, output generated in the Recraft app is owned by Recraft and
may not be used commercially. Commercial rights attach to paid plans and to the API.

## Gaps

- The app-side clauses are not mirrored exactly in the API terms: both forbid using Assets to train
  AI models, but the app terms condition ownership on subscription tier while the API terms grant
  ownership to any API user.
- Whether a Free-tier user's V4 Pro output differs in watermarking or resolution from a paid user's
  is not stated.
- No capture of a dated revision history; the page's effective date was not read.

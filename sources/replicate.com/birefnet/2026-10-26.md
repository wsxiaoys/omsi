## What these records establish, and what they do not

- They establish that independent packagers can serve the whole checkpoint zoo, that the checkpoints
  run in roughly 2–3 s per image on an A100 (80 GB) or L40S, and that the per-run cost estimate on
  Replicate is on the order of a tenth of a cent to a quarter of a cent.
- They are **host records, not measurements**: neither page reports mask quality, edge accuracy,
  hair/transparency handling, or any benchmark.
- Neither endpoint is author-operated, so "BiRefNet on Replicate" is not a first-party service and
  its defaults (weights, resolution, post-processing) are the packager's choices.
- `replicate.com/kndlt/birefnet` — an endpoint the upstream README added on 2026-06-15 — returned
  **HTTP 404** at capture; the author's link is stale. The author also links
  `replicate.com/sprited/birefnet` and `replicate.com/sprited/birefnet-toonout`.

## Gaps

- Replicate's API requires authentication (`GET https://api.replicate.com/v1/models?query=birefnet`
  returns 401), so a complete list of BiRefNet endpoints on Replicate could not be enumerated; only
  the two endpoints linked from the upstream README were captured.
- No per-version weight hashes, licence files, or checkpoint provenance are published on either
  page, so it is not possible to confirm which upstream weight files each version loads.
- Replicate cost figures are estimates tied to that host's hardware and its own margin; they do not
  transfer to self-hosting or to fal.

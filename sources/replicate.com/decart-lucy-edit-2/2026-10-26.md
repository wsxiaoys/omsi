> Lucy Edit 2 is Decart's video editing model. Upload a video, describe the changes you want, and
> Lucy Edit 2 modifies it while preserving motion and temporal consistency.
>
> **Inputs**
> `video` (required) — MP4 video file (H.264 or VP8), 16:9 or 9:16 aspect ratio, max 200MB. No
> duration limit.
> `prompt` — text description of the changes. Can be empty if you provide a reference image.
> `reference_image` — an image to guide the edit. Use this for specific items like sunglasses,
> logos, or style references.
> `seed` — for reproducible results.
> `enhance_prompt` — automatically improve your prompt before generation (default: true).
>
> You can drive edits with text, a visual reference, or both together. At least one of `prompt` or
> `reference_image` must be provided.

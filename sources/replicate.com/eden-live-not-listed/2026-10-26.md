## Captured content

- Every direct route probed returned **HTTP 404**: `/vast`, `/vast/eden-live`, `/vast/eden-live-5b`,
  `/eden-live`, `/edenlive`, `/vastai`, `/vast-ai`, `/tripo`, `/tripo3d`. There is no VAST or Tripo
  owner page on Replicate at capture.
- `GET https://replicate.com/api/search?query=eden` returned 25 fuzzy matches (`qwen-image-edit`,
  `p-image-edit`, `qwen-image-edit-plus`, `qwen-image-edit-2511`, `qwen-edit-multiangle`,
  `structureddreaming`, `qwen3-tts`, `qwen-image`, …) and no Eden or EdenLive entry.
- `?query=edenlive` returned unrelated live-video models (`live-portrait` ×4, `video-01-live`,
  `p-image-edit`, `whisper-subtitles`, …) and no EdenLive entry.
- `?query=vast` returned 25 matches on the substring "fast" (`flux-fast`, `veo-3-fast`, `veo-3.1-fast`,
  `incredibly-fast-whisper`, `rd-fast`, `flux-2-max`, …) and no VAST model.
- `?query=tripo` returned only community work (`triposg` "TripoSG unofficial implementation", `trellis`,
  `trim-video`, …) and no first-party Tripo or VAST listing.
- `?query=eden live` returned the same unrelated live-portrait cluster.

## What this establishes

- Replicate is **not** an independent host for EdenLive as of 2026-10-26. The independent host records
  for this model are fal (`sources/fal.ai/eden-live-5b/2026-10-26.md`) and Reactor
  (`sources/reactor.inc/eden-live/2026-10-26.md`).
- Replicate's search does surface real-time and live-video models, so the absence is specific to this
  model rather than to the modality.
- The roster's "host fal and Replicate" instruction is satisfied for fal and refuted for Replicate.

## Gaps

- Replicate's model API (`https://api.replicate.com/v1/models?query=…`) returns HTTP 401 to
  unauthenticated requests, so a machine-readable full-catalogue sweep was not possible; the negative
  claim rests on the public search endpoint plus 404s from the rendered routes.
- A private, unlisted, or community re-upload could exist without appearing in search.

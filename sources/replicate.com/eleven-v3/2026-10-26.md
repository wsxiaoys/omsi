## What this establishes

- Replicate lists Eleven v3 as an **official ElevenLabs model** (`is_official: true`, owner
  `elevenlabs`), with 165k runs at capture — a materially used endpoint, not a stub.
- **Replicate prices v3 at $0.10 per 1,000 input characters**, matching fal and 25% above the vendor's
  $0.08 list rate. Two independent hosts agreeing on $0.10 while the vendor lists $0.08 is the
  clearest pricing finding for this model.
- The README is **copied vendor alpha copy**, so it dates the model's messaging to 2025 and does not
  reflect the 2026-02-02 GA. A reader who trusts the host page would conclude v3 is still a research
  preview.
- The schema **exposes `previous_text` / `next_text`**, which both the vendor's stitching guide and
  fal's schema say `eleven_v3` does not support. The fields are present; whether they do anything is
  not stated. This is an unresolved host/vendor contradiction.
- The `speed` field's range (0.7–1.2) contradicts its own description ("0.25 to 4.0") — a schema
  inconsistency, preserved rather than resolved.
- Replicate exposes 26 voice presets and no voice-ID field, so cloned voices are not reachable as
  documented.

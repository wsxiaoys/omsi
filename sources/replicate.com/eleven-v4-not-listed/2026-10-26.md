authenticated model listing.

## What this establishes

- Replicate, which hosts the rest of ElevenLabs' TTS line, had **not** listed Eleven v4 or v4 Turbo
  roughly four weeks after the 2026-09-28 launch. Replicate's newest ElevenLabs speech-synthesis entry
  is still `v3`.
- The roster's discovery path for this row was not Replicate, which is consistent: v4 is a fal and
  WaveSpeed listing, not a Replicate one.
- Cross-host comparison for v4 is therefore fal-vs-WaveSpeed, not fal-vs-Replicate.

## Gaps

- Absence at one capture date is not a permanent state; Replicate may add v4 later.
- The org page shows run counts but no creation or update dates, so it cannot be shown whether a v4
  listing was ever present and withdrawn.
- No statement from Replicate about why v4 is not hosted.

## What this establishes

- Replicate hosts BRIA's **first-generation** FIBO and FIBO Edit, plus BRIA 3.2 (`/bria/image-3.2`)
  and the utility endpoints (RMBG, eraser, genfill, upscale, product shots, video). It does not list
  Fibo 2 as of 2026-10-26.
- The independent host record for Fibo 2 is therefore fal only (see
  `sources/fal.ai/fibo-2/2026-10-26/index.md`).
- Replicate's FIBO page confirms the family lineage: FIBO v1 is the 8B, JSON-caption-trained
  text-to-image model whose structured control Fibo 2 continues.

## Gaps

- Replicate's public model API returned HTTP 401 for unauthenticated metadata requests at capture,
  so `created_at`, run counts and schemas could not be read programmatically; only the rendered
  organization and model pages were available.
- A model can be published on Replicate without appearing on the organization page in some layouts;
  the negative claim here is limited to what the organization listing showed.
- Whether Replicate plans to host Fibo 2 is not stated anywhere captured.

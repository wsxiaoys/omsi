## What this establishes

- Replicate is an independent host deployment with its own schedule, and it is by far the cheapest of
  the three captured routes at ~$0.001/MP.
- Replicate's copy is **host-written and partly inaccurate**: it says klein was "distilled from Black
  Forest Labs' larger FLUX 2 base model" and that it "combines a vision-language model (based on
  Mistral 3) with a rectified flow transformer". BFL's own klein card describes a rectified flow
  transformer plus a Qwen3 text encoder; the Mistral-3 24B VLM description belongs to BFL's
  [pro]/[dev] family material, and no BFL page states that klein 4B contains a Mistral VLM
  ([BFL card](../../huggingface.co/flux-2-klein-4b/2026-10-26.md)). Treat Replicate's architecture
  paragraph as unreliable.
- The page offers no `num_inference_steps` control, consistent with a fixed 4-step distilled config.
- Model created 2026-01-15, the launch date; the served version was rebuilt 2026-03-19.

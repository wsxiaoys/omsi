## Captured content

One-line description (verbatim): "FLUX 3 is Black Forest Labs' text-to-image and image-editing
model. Generate images from a prompt, or edit with up to 10 reference images." Run count shown:
"116.6K runs".

README, verbatim structure:

> **Text to image.** Describe what you want and get an image back.
>
> **Image editing.** Pass up to 10 reference images and the prompt describes the edit or
> composition to apply. When you supply reference images, the first one sets the aspect ratio if
> you leave `aspect_ratio` on `auto`.

Inputs (verbatim):

- `prompt` — "what to generate, or the edit to make."
- `images` — "up to 10 reference images (jpeg, png, gif, or webp). Supplying any image switches the
  model into edit mode."
- `aspect_ratio` — "`auto` (default) picks a ratio from the prompt and the first reference image, or
  choose a fixed ratio from `21:9` down to `9:21`."
- `resolution` — "output size: `768sq`, `1k` (default), `1.5k`, `2k`, or `4k`."
- `grounding` — "when on (default), the model researches the prompt with web and image search before
  generating. Turn it off for faster, self-contained generation."
- `safety_tolerance` — "moderation strictness from 0 (strictest) to 4 (most permissive)."
- `output_format` / `output_quality` — "`webp`, `jpg`, or `png`, and the quality for lossy formats."

Reference images (verbatim): "Reference images must be at least 256x256 px and at most 16 MP."

Prompting tips: be specific about style, lighting and composition; for edits "describe the change
clearly … rather than re-describing the whole scene"; leave `aspect_ratio` on `auto` when editing.

## What this establishes

- Replicate exposes **`grounding`** and a **`9:21`** ratio, matching BFL's schema where fal does
  not; it adds `webp`/`output_quality`, which neither BFL's schema nor fal documents.
- Replicate states the same **16 MP** reference-image ceiling as BFL, against fal's 4 MP downscale.
- Replicate's prices are materially **lower than BFL's first-party prices at every tier**: $0.024
  vs $0.048 at 1K, $0.3035 vs $0.607 at 4K — both almost exactly half. Replicate is also the only
  source that prices a `1.5k` tier for FLUX 3 Image, which BFL's release notes omit.
- The `created_at` timestamp (2026-10-01T15:45:53Z) corroborates BFL's October 1, 2026 launch date
  from a second, independent party.

## Known gaps

- Replicate gives no explanation for pricing below the model developer's own list price, and does
  not say which BFL revision it serves. Whether the cheaper price reflects a different quality
  tier, a subsidy, or a reseller arrangement is unknown, and no test was run to compare outputs.
- The `$0.0001 per second` / `p50price $0.0038` metadata is unexplained; it may be a
  boot-time or non-image metric and should not be read as the per-image cost.
- No latency, quality, or moderation-behaviour data beyond the input ranges.
- The page is host copy; it restates vendor capability claims rather than measuring them.

## Billing

`billingConfig`: "per million input tokens" **$2** ("or 500,000 tokens for $1"); "per thousand output
tokens" **$0.04** ("or 25,000 tokens for $1"). Page headline `price`: "$0.0001 per second",
`p50price`: "$0.00052". Hardware: CPU.

Converting the output rate with Google's own audio-token ratio (25 tokens per second of audio):
$0.04 per 1,000 tokens is $40 per 1M, which is **$0.001 per second of audio** — **2× Google's**
$20 per 1M rate ($0.0005/s). Input tokens are likewise 2× Google's $1.00 per 1M.

The page's headline `price` of "$0.0001 per second" is a tenth of that and disagrees with the
model's own `billingConfig`; the token-based config is the billing rule, the headline appears to be
a display artifact. The `p50price` of $0.00052 sits between the two.

## Summary

Replicate serves the 3.1 Flash TTS model officially at **$2 per 1M input tokens and $0.04 per 1,000
output tokens**, with hard input caps of 4,000 bytes for `text`, 4,000 bytes for `prompt`, 8,000 bytes
combined, and an output ceiling of roughly 655 seconds. 1.8M runs at capture make this the
best-evidenced deployment of the model.

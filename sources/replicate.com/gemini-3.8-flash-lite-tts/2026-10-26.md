## Summary

Replicate is the second independent host and the only captured source that says anything about
provenance marking for this model: **"Every clip includes an inaudible SynthID watermark that marks
it as AI-generated."** No Google page captured for this page says that (see the `deepmind.google`
SynthID record and the TTS guide record), so the claim is host-attributed and unverified
first-party.

Two other things matter here. First, **billing matches Google's list price exactly** — $0.50 per 1M
input tokens and $6 per 1M output tokens — unlike fal's per-character markup, so Replicate appears to
be a transparent passthrough with no markup at capture. Second, Replicate adds an `mp3` output option
that the Gemini API's own format list does not contain (Google offers `audio/wav`, `audio/l16`,
`audio/mulaw`, `audio/alaw`), and it flattens the schema to `text` / `voice` / `second_voice` /
`style`, dropping Voice design and Voice replication entirely.

The README's quality claim — "ranks second on Hume AI's Overall Quality Index" — is a third-party
ranking cited by a host, with no Hume capture in this corpus and no score, so it is recorded as a
host assertion, not evidence. It also disagrees in direction with Artificial Analysis' board, where
the two 3.8 TTS models are 24 points apart with overlapping confidence intervals (see the wiki page).

## Gaps

- The README is undated; the listing is new ("Model created 13 hours ago") even though the model went
  GA 2026-09-22, so the listing date says nothing about the model's release.
- 23 runs is a very small sample; nothing here is a measurement.
- No statement on rate limits, cold-start latency, or whether the endpoint proxies Google or hosts a
  copy.
- `$0.0001 per second` appears alongside the token prices; the page does not say what that clock bills
  (prediction wall-time, presumably).
- The Hume AI ranking is uncited and unquantified.

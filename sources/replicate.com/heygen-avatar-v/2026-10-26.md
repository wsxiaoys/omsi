## Summary

Replicate exposes Avatar V as a Cog wrapper that takes HeyGen-native identifiers — `avatar_id` and
`voice_id` from the caller's own HeyGen account — rather than a stock avatar name, so it is closer to
HeyGen's own contract than fal's version. It publishes $0.075/s, cheaper than either of fal's two
conflicting figures, and defaults to 1080p.

It also offers `4k`, and its README makes the claim that "4k" is a valid Avatar V resolution, which
conflicts with HeyGen's field description that 4K belongs to Avatar III.

## Gaps

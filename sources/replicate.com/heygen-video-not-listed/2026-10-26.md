## What this establishes

- **Replicate does not host HeyGen Video 1** as of 2026-10-26. There is no text-to-video,
  image-to-video, or reference-to-video endpoint from HeyGen on Replicate, and no model slug matching
  `heygen-video` or `heygen/video`.
- The absence is a **host gap, not an identity problem**: the vendor documents the model, and fal
  serves all three modes. For this model, Replicate cannot be used as the independent host record.
- HeyGen's Replicate presence is avatar- and lipsync-centric, which is consistent with the vendor's
  own framing that HeyGen Video is "a different tool from the avatar rendering engines".

## Verbatim: description and README

> Kling 4.0: generate 3 to 30 second videos at up to 1080p from text, keyframes, reference images, or
> reference videos, and edit existing videos

> Kling 4.0 is Kuaishou's latest video model. It generates videos from 3 to 30 seconds long at 720p or
> 1080p, and it takes almost any mix of inputs: a text prompt, keyframes, reference images, reference
> videos, or a video you want to edit.
>
> Earlier Kling versions split these jobs across separate models (text-to-video, image-to-video, Omni).
> Kling 4.0 handles all of them in one model, and picks the right mode from the inputs you give it.

Documented modes: text to video (aspect 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 21:9); keyframes (`start_image`, `end_image`, any
number of `middle_images`); reference images (`reference_images`, up to 10, addressed as `@image_1`);
reference videos (`reference_videos`, addressed as `@video_1`); video editing (`base_video` plus an
instruction, "The output keeps the length and aspect ratio of the original video").

Reference numbering rule: "`start_image`, then `middle_images`, then `end_image`, then `reference_images`";
videos: "`base_video` first, then `reference_videos`".

## Verbatim: limits

- "Up to 10 images in total across all image inputs."
- "Up to 5 videos in total across `base_video` and `reference_videos`."
- "Each input video must be 3 to 30 seconds long, and all input videos together can't add up to more than 30
  seconds."
- "Input videos must be .mp4 or .mov, 700 to 4553 pixels per side, at most 300 MB, with an aspect ratio
  between 2:5 and 5:2."
- "Images can be up to 30 MB, with an aspect ratio between 2:5 and 5:2."
- "Prompts can be up to 26,000 English characters or 11,000 non-English characters."

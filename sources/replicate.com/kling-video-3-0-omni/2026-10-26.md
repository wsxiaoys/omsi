## Summary

Omni is the reference- and edit-driven member of the 3.0 series. Its limits match the vendor's own
table: up to 7 reference images (4 when a reference video is present), one reference video of 3–10 s
and 200 MB, 3–15 s output, 720p or 1080p, multi-shot up to 6 shots. Two host-side constraints are not
in the vendor's copy: native audio is **mutually exclusive with a reference video**, and duration is
ignored when editing.

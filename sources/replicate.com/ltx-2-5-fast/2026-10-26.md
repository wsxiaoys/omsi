## README (verbatim)

> LTX 2.5 Fast is the speed-optimized variant of Lightricks' LTX-2.5 video generation model. It
> generates videos with synchronized audio quickly — good for rapid prototyping, mobile workflows, and
> high-volume production.

> It supports both text-to-video and image-to-video, including start-and-end frame interpolation.

Input table (verbatim):

| Input | Description | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `prompt` | Text description of the video to generate | (required) |
| `image` | First frame image for image-to-video | None |
| `last_frame_image` | End frame for interpolation (requires `image`) | None |
| `resolution` | Video resolution: `720p`, `1080p`, `2k`, or `4k` | 1080p |
| `duration` | Length in seconds: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, or 20 | 6 |
| `aspect_ratio` | `16:9` (landscape) or `9:16` (portrait) | 16:9 |
| `fps` | Frame rate: 24, 25, 48, or 50 | 25 |
| `generate_audio` | Generate synchronized audio | true |

> Durations longer than 10 seconds are only available at 720p or 1080p with 24 or 25 FPS. At 48 or 50
> FPS the maximum duration is 10 seconds.

> Output: MP4 video file with synchronized audio (when `generate_audio` is enabled).

Prompting tips, verbatim: "Write one continuous paragraph in present tense … Be specific about camera
angles, lighting, and movement … Longer, more detailed prompts produce better results".

## Billing tiers (verbatim JSON)

| `target resolution` | `price` | `metric` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `720p` | `$0.03` | per second of output video ("or around 33 seconds for $1") |
| `1080p` | `$0.06` | per second of output video ("or around 16 seconds for $1") |
| `2k` | `$0.12` | per second of output video ("or around 83 seconds for $10") |
| `4k` | `$0.24` | per second of output video ("or around 41 seconds for $10") |

**Replicate's LTX-2.5 Fast prices are one third to one half of the vendor's own Fast rates**
($0.03 vs $0.09 at 720p; $0.06 vs $0.13 at 1080p; $0.12 vs $0.19 at 1440p/"2k"; $0.24 vs $0.30 at 4K).
Replicate does not explain the gap; the vendor API's `ltx-2-3-fast` rates are $0.03/$0.06/$0.12/$0.24,
i.e. **Replicate's LTX-2.5 Fast price list is numerically identical to the vendor's LTX-2.3 Fast price
list**. Whether that is a deliberate discount, a stale price table copied from 2.3, or a different
billing basis cannot be determined from the page.

## Differences from the vendor API and from fal

- `duration` is a free choice from an enumerated ladder that includes 2, 3, 4 and 5 s; the vendor's and
  fal's enumerations start at 6 s. Replicate's own schema description repeats the 10-second cap rule.
- `last_frame_image` is exposed as its own input (vendor: `last_frame_uri`, fal: `end_image_url`).
- No `camera_motion` input.
- `resolution` uses `2k` where the vendor says 1440p and fal says 1440p/2160p; "4k" here vs "2160p" on
  fal.
- No automatic-duration option: there is no `auto` value, so the model cannot choose length here even
  though both the vendor and fal support it.

## Summary

Replicate offers a single official LTX-2.5 endpoint — Fast — at roughly a third of the vendor's
per-second price at 720p, with a slightly wider duration ladder (2 s minimum) and no camera-motion or
auto-duration control. It is a forwarded service (CPU hardware, no GPU) created on 2026-08-10, one day
before the vendor's launch announcement. The Pro tier is not available here.

## Gaps

- No Pro endpoint, so no cross-host price comparison is possible for the higher tier.
- The price gap versus the vendor is unexplained; a stale 2.3 price table is a plausible but unverified
  explanation.
- `supports_cancelation: false` means a bad prompt costs the full generation.
- No run-duration or latency data captured.

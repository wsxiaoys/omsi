## What this establishes

- Replicate does **not** host Luma Uni-1 as of 2026-10-26, despite hosting Luma's video models
  (Ray 3.2) and the older Photon image models.
- Host coverage for Uni-1 is therefore first-party (Luma Agents API) plus fal among the hosts checked
  here. Luma's partner list also names Envato, Comfy, Runware, Flora, Krea, Magnific/Freepik and
  LovArt ([Uni-1.1 post](../../lumalabs.ai/uni-1-1-api/2026-10-26.md)); those were not verified.
- The older Luma Photon / Photon Flash image models are separate models with their own AA rows
  ([AA record](../../artificialanalysis.ai/luma-uni-1/2026-10-26.md)) and are not Uni-1 checkpoints.

## Gaps

- Replicate may host Uni-1 under a non-`luma` owner (e.g. a third-party Cog); only the `luma`
  organization and direct path guesses were checked. A search-API query for `uni-1` surfaced no such
  model, but the search index is not exhaustive.
- Absence is a point-in-time observation, not a statement about Replicate's roadmap.

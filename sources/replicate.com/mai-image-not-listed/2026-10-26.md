The site search for "mai image" returns no model results.

## Why this record exists

It is the second independent-host check for this page. Replicate hosts Microsoft's open-weight
family (Phi, VibeVoice, OmniParser) but none of the hosted-only MAI image models, which is
consistent with MAI-Image having no published weights and being sold only through Microsoft's own
platforms. Together with fal (which stops at 2.5), it establishes that **no third-party host was
found serving MAI-Image-2.6** at capture time.

## Gaps

- A 404 on three URLs is not proof that no endpoint exists under another owner namespace; the
  authenticated catalogue API returned **401 Unauthorized** without a token, so no full search was
  run.
- Absence on Replicate says nothing about whether 2.6 is a distinct model — only about who serves it.

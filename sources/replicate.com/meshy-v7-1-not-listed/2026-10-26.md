- Relevant models: Meshy 7.1 (absent), and the 3D models Replicate does list

## Captured content

Replicate's 3D collection lists these models at capture:

`tencent/hunyuan-3d-3.1`, `tencent/hunyuan3d-2mv`, `fishwowater/trellis2`, `firtoz/trellis`,
`hyper3d/rodin`, `prunaai/hunyuan3d-2`, `adirik/imagedream`, `adirik/mvdream`, `adirik/texture`,
`adirik/wonder3d`, `cjwbw/shap-e`, `jd7h/zero123plusplus`, `lucataco/deep3d`.

No Meshy model appears in that list, and no `meshy` owner or model path resolves. The collection's
editorial summary names Hunyuan 3D 3.1 as "the best all-around model for 3D generation right now" and
describes its "configurable polygon counts (40K–1.5M faces)" and PBR materials — that is Replicate's
own opinion, not a measurement, and it is recorded here only to show that the collection was read, not
that Meshy was compared.

Search caveat: Replicate's unauthenticated search endpoints did not return Meshy for either `query=` or
`q=` (the `query=` form returned unrelated models, e.g. `meta/meta-llama-3-8b-instruct`, for every
term tried, including `tripo`), so the search result is weak evidence on its own. The 404 probes and the
absent catalogue row are the substantive evidence.

## Summary

Meshy 7.1 has no Replicate listing as of 2026-10-26. The roster's discovery path for this row is fal
`meshy/v7.1`, and fal is the only independent host found. The evidence floor is still met (first-party
Meshy docs plus the fal host record); this record exists so that the absence of a second host is
documented rather than assumed.

## Gaps

- Replicate could host Meshy under a private or unlisted deployment that a public catalogue check
  cannot see.
- The search API's behaviour (ignoring the query term) means a future search-based check should not be
  trusted without a positive control.

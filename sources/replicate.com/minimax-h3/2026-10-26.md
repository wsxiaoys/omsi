### README (verbatim, excerpted)

> MiniMax H3 is a multimodal video generation model from MiniMax. It can generate videos from text
> prompts, animate a first or last frame, and use reference images, videos, or audio to guide the result.
>
> What it does — Text-to-video: describe a scene and generate a video from scratch. Image-to-video:
> provide a first frame, a last frame, or both to control the start and end of the video. Reference
> generation: provide reference images, videos, or audio to guide character, motion, camera, style,
> voice, or editing rhythm.
>
> The model supports 4–15 second videos at 768P or 2K resolution.

## Summary

Replicate hosts H3 as **one model with one schema**, not the three-endpoint split fal uses: first/last
frame, references, duration, resolution and ratio are all inputs to the same call. It exposes fewer
knobs than fal — no `seed`, no `prompt_expansion_mode`, no `target_audio_url`, no 480P or 4K — and
matches MiniMax's own resolution ladder exactly (768P, 2K).

Billing is **$0.08/s at 768P and $0.13/s at 2K**, identical to MiniMax's first-party per-second rates
and higher than fal's at 768P ($0.06). Replicate lists no per-image reference charge, whereas MiniMax
charges $0.04 per image beyond the first five and fal charges $0.08 per image beyond the first five;
whether Replicate absorbs or omits that charge is not stated.

The first Replicate version is dated 2026-08-13, thirteen days after the 2026-07-31 launch and eleven
days after the 2026-08-02 weight release.

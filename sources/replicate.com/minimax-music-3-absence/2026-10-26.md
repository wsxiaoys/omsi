- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: MiniMax Music 3 (absent), MiniMax Music 2.6 / 2.5 / cover (present)

## Observation

`https://replicate.com/minimax` lists 22 models. The music entries are:

```
/minimax/music-01   /minimax/music-1.5   /minimax/music-2.5
/minimax/music-2.6  /minimax/music-cover
```

There is **no** `minimax/music-3` (or `music-3.0`) page: `https://replicate.com/minimax/music-3`
answers **HTTP 404** and the unauthenticated API path answers 401, the same generic response this
repository recorded for unlisted repositories in
`../minimax-music-26-weights/2026-10-26.md`. The listing is the reliable signal.

## What this establishes

- Replicate, which carries Music 2.6, Music 2.5, Music 1.5, Music-01 and `music-cover` as official
  MiniMax-published models, **does not host Music 3** at the capture date — despite Music 3 being the
  open-weights release and the vendor's recommended replacement.
- So the successor is *less* widely hosted than its closed predecessor on Replicate, while the
  reverse holds on fal (Music 3 only, no cover endpoint) and both exist on WaveSpeedAI.

## Gaps

- No reason is given for the absence; it may be a licensing decision, a queue of work, or a private
  or gated deployment not visible in the public listing.
- No statement about whether a Music 3 endpoint is planned.

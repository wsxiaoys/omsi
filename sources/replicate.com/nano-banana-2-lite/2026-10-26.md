## What this record establishes

- Replicate's listing is the **narrowest** of the three surfaces: it drops every control Google
  documents beyond prompt, references, ratio and format. Thinking level and batch count, both
  documented by Google and exposed by fal, are unreachable here.
- Replicate confirms the three cuts that matter — 1K only, no search grounding, cheaper than
  Nano Banana 2 — in the host's own words, independently of Google's copy.
- **$0.034 per image is Google's $0.0336 rounded up**, i.e. pass-through rather than a markup.
  It is the cheapest of the three routes for a plain 1K text-to-image call.

  with Google, not within Replicate.

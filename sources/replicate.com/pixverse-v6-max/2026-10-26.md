## Summary

Evidence that "PixVerse V6" and "PixVerse V6 Max" are two separate products, not one label with a
quality knob. The host states the split in functional terms: same inputs, higher quality, **no
first/last-frame transition** on Max. The vendor's capability map independently says the same thing
(V6 Max: "First and Last Frame — Not supported"; V6: "Supported").

Timeline: Replicate published V6 Max on 2026-10-21, one day before the vendor changelog records
"Added V6-max support for Text-to-Video and Image-to-Video" (2026-10-22). V6 Max therefore post-dates
the V6 page's subject by about seven months and is a sibling tier, not a V6 checkpoint.

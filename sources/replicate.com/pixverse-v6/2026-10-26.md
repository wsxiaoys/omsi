## Captured content (verbatim from the README)

> PixVerse V6 is the latest video generation model from PixVerse, ranking among the top video models
> globally alongside Seedance 2.0. It generates up to 15 seconds of 1080p video with synchronized
> audio.

> **Modes**
> Text-to-video: Provide a `prompt` and an `aspect_ratio`.
> Image-to-video: Provide a `prompt` and an `image`.
> Transition (first/last frame): Provide a `prompt`, `image` (first frame), and `last_frame_image`
> (last frame).

> Set `generate_audio_switch: true` to generate synchronized audio alongside the video, including
> background music, sound effects, and character dialogue. Audio generation adds to the per-second
> price.

> Set `generate_multi_clip_switch: true` to generate cinematic sequences with multiple shots and scene
> transitions. Works with text-to-video and image-to-video.

## Summary

Replicate is the second independent host and the more expensive one: its per-second prices are exactly
**2× fal's** at every resolution and audio setting (720p silent $0.09 vs fal $0.045; 1080p with audio
$0.23 vs fal $0.115), and roughly **2.25×** the vendor's own per-second rate (5 s of 720p with audio:
$0.60 vs ≈$0.27 direct, ≈$0.30 on fal). Its audio uplift uses the same ratios as the vendor and fal
(+40 % at 360p, +29 % at 540p, +33 % at 720p, +28 % at 1080p), so the absolute premium is simply
double fal's.

The input surface is a strict subset of the vendor's:

- `duration` is an enum of 5/8/10/15 seconds, not the vendor's 1–15 integer range.
- `aspect_ratio` offers three ratios (16:9, 9:16, 1:1), not eight.
- No `style`, no `thinking_type`, no `enable_safety_checker`, no explicit extend endpoint.
- Transition is folded into the same model via `last_frame_image` rather than a separate endpoint.

Version history visible at capture is a single version created 2026-04-22, i.e. about three weeks after
fal's endpoints and the vendor launch.

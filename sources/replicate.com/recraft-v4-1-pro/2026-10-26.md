## Summary

Replicate's V4.1 Pro listing is a 2K raster endpoint with the same 14-size table as Recraft's Pro
tier, at $0.25 per image — $0.04 above Recraft's $0.21 list and above fal's $0.21. It exposes
`size` and `aspect_ratio` but no style, seed, color, batch, or format parameters, and no vector
endpoint exists on Replicate.

- `GET …?query=seed-audio` returned the same pattern: audio models from other vendors plus
  ByteDance image/video models, no `seed-audio` slug.

## Summary

Replicate does not host Seed Audio 1.0 as of 2026-10-26, so there is no second general-purpose host
record for this model in the corpus. ByteDance's other Seed families (Seedream, Seedance, SeedVR)
are present on Replicate, which makes the absence specific to the audio model rather than to the
vendor.

## Gaps

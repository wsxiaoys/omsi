## README claims (verbatim)

> Generate high-quality video from text, images, video clips, and audio — all in one pass with
> synchronized sound. Seedance 2.5 is ByteDance's flagship video model, built on a unified multimodal
> architecture that accepts mixed inputs and produces coherent, audio-synced output up to 30 seconds
> long in a single generation.

"What's new in 2.5", as Replicate states it:

> Native 30-second generation … Larger reference sets — combine up to 30 images, 10 video clips, and
> 10 audio files in a single generation … Stronger instruction following … Multilingual prompts …
> Adaptive aspect ratio.

Task list: text-to-video, image-to-video (first frame, optional last frame), multimodal reference,
video editing, video extension. Prompt conventions differ from the vendor's: references are written
`[Image1]`, `[Video1]`, `[Audio1]`, and dialogue goes in double quotes.

> Audio and video are generated together, not separately … You can turn audio off if you just want
> silent video.

| `generate_audio` | boolean | `true` | dialogue in double quotes |

| `watermark` | boolean | `false` | — |
| `seed` | int | null | "Set for reproducible generation. Reproducibility is not guaranteed." |

Two divergences from the vendor worth noting:

1. **`reference_audios` requires a reference image or video.** BytePlus/Volcengine document that 2.5
   "newly supports generating videos with pure audio references, without requiring image or video
   assets". Replicate's schema still carries the 2.0-era constraint. Whether the restriction is in the
   model or in Replicate's wrapper is not stated.
2. **No 1080p.** Replicate offers 480p and 720p only, while the vendor documents 1080p (10-bit) and
   fal's US endpoints quote a 1080p price. So the top resolution is not reachable on this host.

## Pricing (USD, per second of output video)

| Resolution | No video input | With video input |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 480p | $0.1028/s ("or around 97 seconds for $10") | $0.4304/s ("or around 23 seconds for $10") |
| 720p | $0.2312/s ("or around 43 seconds for $10") | $0.9676/s ("or around 10 seconds for $10") |

The 480p and 720p no-video rates sit just under the vendor's own token-rate equivalents ($0.52 and
$1.10 per 5 s at BytePlus, i.e. $0.104/s and $0.22/s) — Replicate is the cheapest captured route for
this model. The **with-video** tiers are 4.2× the no-video price, which is the opposite direction from
the vendor's 0.6× with-video discount and from fal's ×0.6. No captured page explains it; the most
plausible reading is that Replicate bills input-video seconds as output seconds, but that is an
inference, not a documented rule. Treat the with-video prices as unverified for budgeting.

## What this establishes

- Replicate hosts Pro and Lite but **not** Flash, so the only independent host record available for
  Flash is fal. This narrows the evidence floor to a single host and removes Replicate's price as a
  cross-check (Replicate lists $0.045/$0.09 for Pro and $0.035 for Lite).
- Run counts give a rough adoption signal for the siblings (Pro 656.7K vs Lite 3.6M vs 4.5 at
  37.4M) but say nothing about Flash.

## Gaps

- A 404 on one slug does not prove a host will never list the model; it is a snapshot of the
  catalogue on the capture date.
- The search endpoint's ranking and page size are undocumented, so the list is not guaranteed
  exhaustive — the direct 404s on both plausible slugs (`seedream-5-flash`, `seedream-5`) are the
  stronger evidence.
- No Replicate-side latency, price, or capability data exists for Flash to compare with fal.

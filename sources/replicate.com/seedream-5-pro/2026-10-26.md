## README (verbatim)

> Seedream 5.0 Pro is ByteDance's flagship image model. It generates high-quality images from a text
> prompt, and can use up to 10 reference images to guide the result for character consistency, style
> matching, and multi-image composition.

> **What it does**
>
> Text to image — describe what you want and get a sharp result at 1K (~2 megapixels) or 2K
> (~4 megapixels).
>
> Reference-guided generation — pass up to 10 reference images to keep a character, product, or
> style consistent across generations, or to blend multiple subjects into one scene.
>
> Flexible aspect ratios — square, portrait, landscape, and wide formats (1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 16:9, 9:16,
> 3:2, 2:3, 21:9).
>
> PNG or JPEG output.

> **Resolutions**
>
> 1K — around 2 megapixels, good for fast iteration and web use.
>
> 2K — around 4 megapixels (up to 2048x2048, or wider for cinematic ratios), for higher-detail work.
>
> When you pass reference images and leave the aspect ratio on `match_input_image`, the output
> matches the shape of your first reference image at the resolution you pick.

## Pricing (verbatim)

## What this establishes

- An independent host, with a different slug convention from fal, exposes the same model with the
  same headline limits: **10 reference images**, **1K/2K tiers**, **PNG or JPEG**.
- Replicate's "up to 2048x2048, or wider for cinematic ratios" is a more accurate phrasing of the
  2K tier than fal's bare "up to 2048x2048", and matches ByteDance's documented 2K pixel table.
- Replicate's two prices bracket fal's advertised floor: fal says "from $0.0675 / image", which
  sits between Replicate's $0.045 (1K) and $0.09 (2K). Since fal's floor has no stated conditions,
  no conclusion follows about which host is cheaper at a given resolution.
- The page does **not** mention layer separation, interactive editing by coordinates, or multilingual
  text, even though the model description calls it an "image editing model". Replicate's public
  surface is a narrower, text-to-image-plus-references product.

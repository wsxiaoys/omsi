## Summary

Replicate does not host Stable Audio 3.0 in any tier as of 2026-10-26, not even as a community
repackage — while it does host Stable Audio 2.5 officially with 115.2K runs. The absence is specific to
3.0, not to Stability AI's audio line. fal is therefore the only general-purpose third-party host for
this model family in the corpus, and there is no second host to compare prices or defaults against.

## Gaps

- Replicate's search is fuzzy and paginated; an unlisted or private endpoint would not appear, though the
  direct slug probes also 404'd.
- No price comparison is possible on Replicate for this model.
- No explanation from either Replicate or Stability for the absence.

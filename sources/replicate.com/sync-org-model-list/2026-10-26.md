# Replicate: sync. labs model listing (no sync-3)

- Origin: Replicate, sync. labs organization page <https://replicate.com/sync> and model page
  <https://replicate.com/sync/lipsync-2>; public search endpoint
  <https://replicate.com/api/search?query=sync-3> was also queried
- Evidence kind: independent host model listing and model card
- Publication date: no exact date on the pages; the lipsync-2 page renders "Model created over 1 year
  ago / Model updated 3 months, 2 weeks ago"
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `sync/lipsync-2`, `sync/lipsync-2-pro`, `sync/react-1`; absence of `sync-3`

## Organization listing (verbatim)

The `replicate.com/sync` page lists exactly three public models:

| Model | Description | Runs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sync/react-1` | "Realistic lipsync with refined human emotion capabilities" | 10.7K |
| `sync/lipsync-2-pro` | "Studio-grade lipsync in minutes, not weeks" | 88.8K |
| `sync/lipsync-2` | "Generate realistic lipsyncs with Sync Labs' 2.0 model" | 61.3K |

No `sync-3`, `sync/lipsync-3`, or equivalent appears on the page.

## Search check

A query to `https://replicate.com/api/search?query=sync-3` returned the same three `sync/*` models
plus unrelated lip-sync models from other publishers (`pixverse/lipsync`, `kwaivgi/kling-lip-sync`,
`heygen/lipsync-*`, `bytedance/latentsync`, and others). It returned no sync-3 model under any owner.

## lipsync-2 model card (verbatim excerpts)

> Lipsync 2.0 is a zero-shot model for generating realistic lip movements that match spoken audio. It
> works out of the box—no training or fine-tuning needed—and preserves a speaker’s unique style across
> different languages and video types.

> Temperature control: Fine-tune how expressive the lipsync is.

> Active speaker detection: Automatically detects who’s speaking in multi-person videos and applies
> lipsync only when that person is talking.

The card describes lipsync-2 as a distinct 2.0 model, not as a variant of sync-3.

## Summary

Replicate's official sync. labs organization hosts lipsync-2, lipsync-2-pro and react-1, but not
sync-3. As of capture, sync-3 is available first-party through sync. labs and through fal, and is
absent from Replicate. The Replicate card confirms the v2 generation exists independently and is still
served.

## Gaps

- No Replicate-side price for sync-3 because the model is not listed.
- No exact model-creation date for lipsync-2; only relative text.

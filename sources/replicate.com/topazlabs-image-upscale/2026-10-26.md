## Interpretation

- Replicate exposes **only the five precision models of the Gigapixel V2 generation**. It has no
  Wonder (generative), Bloom (creative), Transparent, High Fidelity 3, or Face Recovery 3 model. The
  host therefore under-represents the first-party catalogue in exactly the tiers that fal sells as
  separate endpoints.
- Pricing is **$0.05 per started 24 MP** of output (1 unit), stepping to 17 units at 512 MP
  (~$0.048 per 24 MP). That is roughly half of fal's $0.08/24 MP and below Topaz's own $0.08/credit
  Scale rate.
- Defaults differ from fal's: `upscale_factor` defaults to `"None"` (enhance without enlarging) and
  `face_enhancement` defaults to `false`, whereas fal defaults to 2x and face enhancement on. A
  caller who does not set either gets different behaviour on the two hosts.
- Replicate's maximum factor is **6x**, above fal's 4x cap and above the first-party API's
  dimension-based ceiling in practice.
- The version history (2025-04-24, then 2025-09-01) predates the roster window, so this host record
  cannot support an in-window launch claim.

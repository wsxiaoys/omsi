## Readme (verbatim, complete text)

> TRELLIS.2 is a state-of-the-art large 3D generative model (4B parameters) designed for high-fidelity
> image-to-3D generation. It leverages a novel "field-free" sparse voxel structure termed O-Voxel to
> reconstruct and generate arbitrary 3D assets with complex topologies, sharp features, and full PBR
> materials.
>
> Please refer to the original repo for the complete code/weights/license.
>
> Usage of this service should also follow the license of DINOv3 and BRIA-AI (REMBG-2.0).
>
> Note that the setup in replicate takes a long time (4-8mins) on Replicate, be aware of the cost! You can
> also disable the video rendering or use the non-cascade inference mode to reduce the cost

This is the only captured page that names the two licence-bearing dependencies: the DINOv3 image conditioner
(confirmed in Microsoft's config, see [repo record](../../github.com/trellis-2-repo/2026-10-26.md)) and BRIA
REMBG-2.0 background removal, which the port uses for its `preprocess_image` step.

Two divergences from fal's schema are visible: the port exposes the **cascade as an explicit choice**
(`1024_cascade` default, `1536_cascade`), matching the paper's test-time scaling, and it describes
`decimation_target` as a *face* count where fal says *vertices*. Ranges also differ (fal: 5,000–2,000,000
vertices, texture 1024/2048/4096; Replicate: 100,000–2,000,000 faces, texture 1024–8192 continuous). No
safety-checker field, no prompt field, no output-format selector.

## Output schema

`PredictOutput` with three nullable URI fields: `model_file` ("GLB" per the input description), `video`, and
`no_background_image`. GLB is the only model format; the video is a rendered preview, not a model output.

## Gaps

- Unofficial: an individual account's port, not Microsoft or Replicate. Nothing on the page states which
  upstream commit or weights revision it pins, though the linked weights are `microsoft/TRELLIS.2-4B`.
- The cost block ("approximately $0.85", "1 runs per $1") has no billing metric, no per-resolution breakdown
  and no statement of what drives the variance beyond "this varies depending on your inputs"; the readme
  warns that "the setup in replicate takes a long time (4-8mins)".
- The DINOv3 and BRIA REMBG-2.0 licence obligations are asserted by the port author with no terms text; what
  they require of an end user is not stated anywhere captured.
- No moderation or content-policy statement on the page; Replicate's platform terms were not captured.

## Summary

Replicate's listing is the only captured source that states a 60-second ceiling and ties it to the model
rather than to a route. It also gives the narrowest file-format list (image jpg/jpeg/png, audio
mp3/wav/m4a/aac), a 720p default (fal's and VEED's default is 720p too, but fal's schema does not mark a
default), and a per-second price identical to both other routes. The README's framing — "fine-tuned to
generate videos of 'talking heads' in a social media influencer style" — is host copy, not vendor
positioning; VEED describes the same product as a general image-to-talking-video model for marketing,
education and social.

## Gaps

- Replicate does not publish whether predictions are cancellable or what happens to a failed render's
  charge; the page's billing config shows only per-second output pricing.
- No audio-duration limit is enforced in the schema; the 60-second figure lives in the README only.
- No output frame rate, codec, or aspect-ratio statement.
- The README's "fine-tuned" and "influencer style" language is not corroborated by any VEED document and
  should not be read as a description of the training method.

## README (verbatim, selected)

> New and improved version of Veo 3, with higher-fidelity video, context-aware audio, reference image
> and last frame support

> Google's state-of-the-art video generation model that creates high-quality videos with synchronized
> native audio from text prompts or images. Veo 3.1 offers enhanced prompt adherence, improved
> audiovisual quality, and powerful creative controls for image-to-video generation.

> **Reference Image Support** – Upload up to 3 reference images to guide the appearance, style, and
> character consistency across your generated video, ensuring visual continuity throughout.

> **Frame-to-Frame Generation** – Provide a starting and ending frame, and Veo 3.1 generates smooth,
> seamless transitions between them, perfect for creating artful scene transitions.

> **Multiple Output Formats** – Generate videos at 720p or 1080p resolution at 24 FPS, with support for
> both landscape (16:9) and portrait (9:16) aspect ratios. Choose from 4, 6, or 8-second durations.

> All videos generated with Veo 3.1 are marked with SynthID, Google's watermarking technology for
> identifying AI-generated content.

> Veo 3.1 also comes in a Fast variant (Veo 3.1 Fast) that offers faster generation times while
> maintaining high quality, perfect for rapid iteration and experimentation.

Replicate documents **no 4K** — its resolution enum stops at 1080p — so the host's maximum output is
lower than Google's and fal's.

## Input schema (transcribed exactly)

| Field | Type / enum | Default | Note (verbatim) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `prompt` | string, required | — | "Text prompt for video generation" |
| `aspect_ratio` | `16:9`, `9:16` | `16:9` | "Video aspect ratio" |
| `duration` | `4`, `6`, `8` (integer) | `8` | "Video duration in seconds" |
| `image` | URI | — | "Input image to start generating from. Ideal images are 16:9 or 9:16 and 1280x720 or 720x1280, depending on the aspect ratio you choose." |
| `last_frame` | URI | — | "Ending image for interpolation. When provided with an input image, creates a transition between the two images." |
| `reference_images` | array of URI | `[]` | "1 to 3 reference images for subject-consistent generation (reference-to-video, or R2V). Reference images only work with 16:9 aspect ratio and 8-second duration. Last frame is ignored if reference images are provided." |
| `negative_prompt` | string | — | "Description of what to exclude from the generated video" |
| `resolution` | `720p`, `1080p` | `1080p` | "Resolution of the generated video" |
| `generate_audio` | boolean | `true` | "Generate audio with the video" |
| `seed` | integer | — | "Random seed. Omit for random generations" |

Output: a single URI (the video file).

Replicate's `reference_images` note is the only captured statement of the mutual-exclusion rule
("Last frame is ignored if reference images are provided") and of the reference/8-second/16:9
coupling, both of which are consistent with Google's "reference image to video only supports 8
seconds".

## Summary

Replicate serves the standard Veo 3.1 tier at $0.40/s with audio and $0.20/s silent, 720p or 1080p,
4/6/8 seconds, with first-image, last-frame and 1–3 reference-image inputs, no 4K, no cancellation,
and a version history that starts on 2025-10-15. Its schema is the most precise of the three routes
about how reference images interact with duration, aspect ratio and last-frame input.

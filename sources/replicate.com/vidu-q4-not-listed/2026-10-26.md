# Replicate: no Vidu Q4 listing found

- Origin: Replicate public pages and its unauthenticated search route:
  - `https://replicate.com/vidu` (the Vidu publisher account's model list)
  - `https://replicate.com/vidu/q4`, `https://replicate.com/vidu/q4-preview`
  - `https://replicate.com/vidu/q3-pro` (positive control)
  - `GET https://replicate.com/api/search?query=vidu` (the search route the web UI calls; JSON)
- Evidence kind: provider catalogue search (record of absence, not of a hosted model)
- Publication date: not applicable; pages fetched for this capture
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Vidu Q4 / `viduq4-preview` (expected but not found); Vidu Q3 Pro, Q3 Turbo (found)

Artifact beside this file: `replicate-search-vidu.json` (the search response verbatim).

## What was checked

`https://replicate.com/vidu` lists exactly **two** models at capture, both Q3:

> vidu / q3-turbo — "Fast video generation with text-to-video, image-to-video, and start-end-to-video
> modes. Up to 16 seconds at 1080p with synchronized audio." — 5.4K runs — Public
>
> vidu / q3-pro — "High-fidelity video generation with text-to-video, image-to-video, and start-end-to-video
> modes. Up to 16 seconds at 1080p with synchronized audio." — 6.4K runs — Public

`GET https://replicate.com/api/search?query=vidu` returns 25 models. Exactly **two** are Vidu's own
(`vidu/q3-pro`, `vidu/q3-turbo`); the rest are other publishers' video models whose descriptions mention
Vidu (`prunaai/p-video`, `wan-video/wan-2.2-i2v-fast`, `minimax/video-01`, `lightricks/ltx-video`, and
others). No Q4 record appears under any owner, and every result carries a
`latest_enabled_version_id`, so the absence is not a disabled-version artefact.

Direct requests for `https://replicate.com/vidu/q4` and `https://replicate.com/vidu/q4-preview` each
returned **HTTP 404** (Replicate's "Page not found" template). `https://replicate.com/vidu/q3-pro`
returned **HTTP 200**, so the publisher path and the 404 are both behaving normally — this is an absent
model, not a broken URL pattern.

## What this establishes

As of capture, Replicate carries the Vidu family only up to Q3 (`q3-pro`, `q3-turbo`), and Replicate is
therefore **not** a second host for Q4 and cannot supply a Q4 per-second price. It also shows Replicate
does not mirror the vendor's mode coverage for Q3 either: Replicate lists only two Q3 models where the
vendor documents five (`pro`, `mix`, `drama`, `ad`, `turbo`).

This is evidence of a failed search, not proof that no private, region-restricted, or differently named
Replicate deployment exists.

## Gaps

- The authenticated Replicate API was not queried (no token); the public account page and the
  unauthenticated search JSON are the strongest available surfaces.
- Search ranking could hide a low-run listing under an unrelated owner name.
- No Replicate page was captured for `vidu/q3-pro` itself, so its parameters and price are not recorded
  here; only its existence is.

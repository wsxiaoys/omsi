## What was checked

`https://replicate.com/wan-video` lists 22 Wan models and stops one generation short of Wan 3.0:

`wan-2.1-1.3b`, `wan-2.2-5b-fast`, `wan-2.2-animate-animation`, `wan-2.2-animate-replace`,
`wan-2.2-i2v-a14b`, `wan-2.2-i2v-fast`, `wan-2.2-s2v`, `wan-2.2-t2v-fast`, `wan-2.5-i2v`,
`wan-2.5-i2v-fast`, `wan-2.5-t2v`, `wan-2.5-t2v-fast`, `wan-2.6-i2v`, `wan-2.6-t2v`, `wan-2.7-i2v`,
`wan-2.7-image`, `wan-2.7-image-pro`, `wan-2.7-r2v`, `wan-2.7-t2v`, `wan-2.7-videoedit`,
`wan2.1-with-lora`, `wan2.6-i2v-flash`.

`wan2.7-*` is the newest Wan generation Replicate carries. Direct requests for
`replicate.com/alibaba/wan-3.0`, `replicate.com/alibaba/wan-3.0-prime` and
`replicate.com/wan-video/wan-3.0` each returned HTTP 404 (Replicate's "Page not found" template).

`GET https://api.replicate.com/v1/models?query=wan` returned `401 Unauthenticated` — the
authenticated catalogue could not be queried without a token, so the account page above is the
strongest available surface.

## What this establishes

As of 2026-10-26, Replicate carries the Wan family only up to 2.7; the independent hosts found for
Wan 3.0 Prime are fal and WaveSpeedAI (see the two host records). Replicate is therefore not available
as a third comparison point, and no Replicate per-second schedule exists for this model.

This is evidence of a failed search, not proof that no private, region-restricted or differently named
Replicate deployment exists.

## Gaps

- Search ranking and account pagination could hide a low-traffic listing under an unrelated owner.
- The authenticated Replicate API was not queried (401 without a token).
- No other independent host was checked beyond the fal and WaveSpeedAI records captured for this page.

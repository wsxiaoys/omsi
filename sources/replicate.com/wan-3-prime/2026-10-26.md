## README (verbatim, selected)

> Wan 3.0 Prime is a text-to-video model from Alibaba's Wan family. Describe a scene in natural language
> and it generates a video with coherent motion and lighting.

> The model generates video at 480p, 720p, or 1080p, with durations from 2 to 30 seconds. Let the model
> pick the best aspect ratio for your prompt with the `adaptive` setting, or choose your own.

### Limitations (verbatim, the page's own list)

> Complex multi-character scenes with specific interactions can be inconsistent.
> Text rendering within generated videos is unreliable.
> Longer durations may show motion degradation or scene drift.
> Precise spatial relationships ("object A is to the left of object B") are not always followed exactly.

This is the only host-side limitations list in the Wan 3.0 corpus, and it is Replicate's generic video
model boilerplate rather than a measured result. Recorded as a host claim.

## Summary

Replicate hosts Wan 3.0 Prime with the same narrow wrapper as its standard Wan 3.0 model: prompt plus
optional first-frame image, 2–30 s, 480p–1080p, no audio toggle, no reference inputs. Billing is
$0.068/$0.14/$0.28 per second, matching the vendor and fal. The page's "Apache 2.0 License" link points
at Wan 2.1 and should not be read as a Wan 3.0 licence.

## Gaps

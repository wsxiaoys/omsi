## README (verbatim, selected)

> Wan 3.0 is Alibaba's video model. Give it a prompt and it generates a short video with cinematic
> motion and camera movement. You can also provide a starting image to animate.

> Text to video. Turn a description into a video clip.
> Image to video. Provide a first-frame image and the model animates it, guided by your prompt.
> Multiple resolutions. Choose 480p, 720p, or 1080p.
> Long clips. Generate up to 30 seconds.
> Adaptive framing. Let the model pick the best aspect ratio for your prompt, or set one explicitly.

> `image` — optional first-frame image to animate (jpg/png/bmp/webp). When provided, the video is
> generated from this image.

> Priced per second of output video: 480p: $0.05 per second, 720p: $0.10 per second, 1080p: $0.20 per
> second

schema captured here.

**This contradicts the same page's README**, which quotes $0.05 / $0.10 / $0.20. The billing table is
exactly half the README figures and half Alibaba's Singapore list price. The billing table is the
machine-readable tier payload that Replicate charges from, so it is the more likely operational truth,
but the two cannot both be right and no source reconciles them. Recorded as an open contradiction.

## Summary

Replicate hosts the standard Wan 3.0 tier as `alibaba/wan-3` with a narrow wrapper (prompt + optional
first-frame image), 2–30 s, 480p/720p/1080p, no audio toggle and no reference inputs. Its stated billing
tiers are half the README's quoted per-second prices. Nothing in the page names the Alibaba checkpoint
being served.

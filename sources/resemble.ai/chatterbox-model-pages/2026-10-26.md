## `/learn/models/chatterbox-turbo`

Headline claims: "350M Parameters", "75ms Latency — real-time voice synthesis on a GPU", "6× Faster than
real-time inference on a single GPU", "Voice cloning from 5 seconds of reference audio", "The only
open-source TTS with built-in watermarking".

**Contradiction with the vendor's own code:** the Turbo page still lists "Emotion — Unique emotion
control — First open-source model with emotion exaggeration control. Adjust intensity from monotone to
dramatically expressive with a single parameter", copied from the family page. The shipped Turbo code
ignores `exaggeration` and `cfg_weight` and logs a warning to that effect (see the repository record).

## What this changes

The vendor's live position (October 2026) is that Chatterbox is **archived open research, not a paid
product**, while remaining MIT-licensed, downloadable and explicitly cleared for commercial use. The
pages keep the 2025 marketing claims ("~200ms", "63.75% preferred over ElevenLabs") and add three that
need care: a "100% accuracy" detection claim, a "10 seconds or more" reference requirement for v3 that
conflicts with the family's "5 seconds" claim, and an emotion-control claim on Turbo that the code
contradicts.

## Gaps

- "No longer offered as commercial paid products" is not reconciled with the same page's "Resemble AI
  also offers managed hosting via app.resemble.ai".
- The "100% accuracy" claim for Resemble Detect on Chatterbox audio has no test set, condition or date.
- No deprecation date, no support window and no statement about future checkpoint releases.
- No sample rate, character limit or duration limit on any of the three pages.

# Resemble AI: "Chatterbox Nano and Flash: Speed at the edge, throughput at scale"

- Origin: Resemble AI (Research), DeokJin Seo, Zihan Jin and John Meade,
  <https://www.resemble.ai/resources/chatterbox-nano-and-flash-speed-at-the-edge-throughput-at-scale>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party release post)
- Publication date: 2026-07-06. Capture date 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Chatterbox Nano, Chatterbox Flash, Chatterbox Multilingual V3 (referenced)

## Captured content (verbatim)

> Chatterbox Nano and Chatterbox Flash are both answers to that. Both are open source, shipping today
> alongside DramaBox and Chatterbox Multilingual V3, which we released recently. One thing that holds
> across all of the releases: every model ships with watermarking by default.

> Chatterbox crossed 13 million downloads on Hugging Face somewhere in the middle of building all of
> this.

Nano:

> Chatterbox Nano is a 110M parameter TTS model built for the edge. It's small enough to run locally
> and fast enough that the gap between text and audio disappears.
>
> - 10x realtime on GPU, 3x realtime on CPU using 8 threads
> - Paralinguistic tags like [laugh], [sigh], [chuckle], and [cough] work natively
> - Voice cloning from a 5-second reference clip, no fine-tuning required
> - Watermarking baked in by default

Flash:

> Chatterbox Flash is Chatterbox rebuilt on a diffusion-LLM architecture for teams running TTS at
> scale. […] 2x the speed of our autoregressive baseline on vLLM […] Novel prior-subtraction technique
> that reduces diffusion steps without hurting output quality […] One of the first TTS models built on
> dLLM architecture that is actually ready to run in production

Both: "Both ship as open source under the MIT license."

## Summary

Establishes the two mid-2026 family additions: Nano (110M, English, CPU-targeted) and Flash
(block-diffusion rebuild for throughput), both MIT and both in-window (July 2026). The post repeats the
family-wide claim that watermarking is on by default for every release. It also gives the only
cumulative adoption figure in the corpus — "13 million downloads on Hugging Face" — which is an order
of magnitude above the 30-day counter on the weights repository and is not defined (all repos versus
one, all time versus window).

## Gaps

- No benchmark numbers for Nano; Flash's numbers live only on its model card.
- "13 million downloads" is undefined and unverifiable against Hugging Face counters.
- No pricing, hosting or licensing detail beyond "MIT".
- No date is given for Nano's own release; the model card's creation date (2026-04-14) predates this
  post, so the post is a re-announcement rather than a launch.

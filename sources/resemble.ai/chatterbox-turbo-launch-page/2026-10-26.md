## Captured content (verbatim)

> Fast, expressive, open source TTS – authenticated by default.
>
> The only open-source TTS with built-in watermarking. Up to 6× faster than real-time on a GPU.
> Paralinguistic prompting. Zero-shot cloning. MIT licensed.

Feature blocks:

> **6× faster than real-time on a GPU**
>
> **350M Parameters and 75ms Latency**
>
> **5 seconds of audio for voice cloning**
>
> **Paralinguistic Prompting** — Text-based tags that tell the model to perform natural vocal
> reactions in the cloned voice. Supported tags include sigh, gasp, cough and more.

Head-to-head testing (verbatim):

> We conducted a test through Podonos designed to assess the performance of Resemble AI's Chatterbox
> Turbo, ElevenLabs Turbo 2.5, Cartesia Sonic 3, and VibeVoice 7B in generating natural and
> high-quality speech. All systems produce audio clips based on 5 to 10 second long audio clips and
> identical text inputs (zero-shot, no prompt engineering and audio processing).

The page renders a "Head-to-Head Win Rates" chart for the three matchups but prints no numeric values
in text. Paired samples compare "Gen Z Girl" and "Liam Neeson" by ElevenLabs and by "Resemble AI,
Chatterbox Turbo".

Watermarking section (verbatim): "Every audio file generated by Chatterbox includes Resemble AI's PerTh
(Perceptual Threshold) Watermarker — a deep neural network watermarker that embeds data in an
imperceptible and difficult-to-detect way."

## Summary

Establishes Turbo as a distinct, in-window release: 350M parameters, "75ms latency", "6× faster than
real-time on a GPU", paralinguistic tags, MIT licence, PerTh watermarking on by default. The page went
live in December 2025, consistent with the Hugging Face repository creation date of 2025-12-02 and the
repository commit "Chatterbox Turbo 350M Model" of 2025-12-15.

## Gaps

- No release date is printed; the December 2025 date is inferred from asset paths and the snapshot.
- The Podonos win-rate chart is an image; the underlying percentages are not in the captured text.
- "75ms latency" has no stated definition (time to first byte, or per-token), hardware or text length.
- No output sample rate, input-length cap or duration limit on the page.

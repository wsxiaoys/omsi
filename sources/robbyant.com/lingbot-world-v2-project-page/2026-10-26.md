## Summary

First-party product page. It establishes the canonical marketing name and alias
(LingBot-World 2.0 = LingBot-World-Infinity), the vendor (Robbyant, an Ant Group company), the
headline performance claim (720p at 60 fps, sub-second control latency), the intended framing
(interactive world model that also serves as an embodied-simulation substrate), the two official demo
partners (Reactor for PC/web internationally, LingGuang for mobile in China), and a vendor-authored
limitations paragraph.

Every performance statement on the page is an unquantified vendor claim: no benchmark, no latency
distribution, no horizon length, no resolution table, no pricing.

## Gaps

- No publication or revision date on the page; the only dates come from the linked report and repo.
- No output specification: no exact pixel dimensions, no frame rate other than "60 fps", no statement
  of whether the stream is compressed or raw, no session length limit.
- No pricing, licence, or access terms on the page — the licence is stated only in the repository
  (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) and the hosts.
- "Model weights will be released" contradicts the published weights; the page was not updated.
- No quantitative comparison with LingBot-World 1.0, and no indication of what "hours" of exploration
  was actually demonstrated.

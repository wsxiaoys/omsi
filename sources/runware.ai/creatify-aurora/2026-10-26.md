## Summary

Runware exposes Aurora as two model records with distinct IDs, prices and `releasedAt` dates of
2025-09-10, and it prices by the output second at exactly the two rates fal uses ($0.14 and $0.07). Its
`releasedAt` is the earliest host-dated evidence of Aurora being available outside Creatify, and it sits
one week after Creatify's 2025-09-03 announcement.

The page's `startingPrice` ($1.12 for v1, $0.42 for fast) is the cheapest published example run, not a
unit price; the per-second rate is the billing basis.

Runware's schema accepts a 3000-character prompt and a `CFGScale` of 0–10, i.e. a wider guidance range
than fal's 0–5 for the same model.

## Gaps

- `releasedAt` is Runware's platform record; it does not necessarily date the model's creation.
- No documented audio-length cap, image-size cap, output resolution, or frame rate.
- `hosted: false` on the catalogue row is unexplained; it may mean "not a Runware-hosted fine-tune"
  rather than "proxied", and no Runware page captured defines the flag.
- No Aurora 2.0 on Runware.
- No terms page captured; commercial use is asserted only in Creatify's integration post.

- Origin: ByteDance Seed Team, "Seedance 2.0 Official Launch", Seed News blog post,

- Evidence kind: official announcement (model developer)
- Publication date: 2026-02-12 (page field "Date 2026-02-12")

## Scope and provenance

The post is the vendor's launch announcement. It is the only first-party page found that dates the
Seedance 2.0 release by day. The page is not linked from the current `Models` navigation (which
lists Seedance 2.5); it was reached from the Visual blog index
(<https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog_list/visual>), where it appears as "Feb 12, 2026 — Seedance 2.0
Official Launch — Unified multimodal audio-video generation with SOTA performance in complex
motion."

## Verbatim quotes

> We recently launched Seedance 2.0, our next-generation video creation model.

> Built with a unified multimodal audio-video joint generation architecture, Seedance 2.0 supports
> four input modalities: text, image, audio, and video.

> Trained on the unified multimodal audio-video joint architecture, supporting mixed-modality input.
> Users can simultaneously input up to 9 images, 3 video clips, 3 audio clips, plus natural language

> The model supports 15-second high-quality multi-shot audio-video output and features dual-channel
> audio for ultra-realistic audio-visual experiences.

> Seedance 2.0 has also enhanced its audio capabilities by integrating two-channel stereo technology,
> enabling high-fidelity, immersive sound generation. The model supports multi-track parallel output
> for background music, ambient sound effects, and character voiceovers, all seamlessly aligned with
> the visual rhythm.

> Meanwhile, Seedance 2.0 introduces new video editing capabilities, supporting targeted
> modifications to specified clips, characters, actions, and storylines. The model also features
> video extension functionality that can generate continuous shots based on user prompts.

> Home Page: https://seed.bytedance.com/seedance2_0

## Self-reported evaluation (vendor claims, not independent measurement)

The post says the team "worked with experts to build an evaluation dataset and standards covering
audio/video generation, referencing, and editing", and reports results on it. No numbers, sample
counts, or per-model scores are published in the text; the figures are in charts that were not
captured as data. Stated conclusions:

- Text-to-video and image-to-video: "at the forefront of the industry", with improvements in motion
  stability, instruction following, and visual aesthetics.
- Acknowledged weaknesses: "Seedance 2.0 still requires ongoing refinement in detail stability,
  hyper-realism, and dynamic vitality"; "still needs to address issues like occasional audio
  distortion"; "there is still room for optimization regarding multi-subject consistency, text
  rendering accuracy, and complex editing effects."

## Capability claims stated in the post

- Four input modalities (text, image, audio, video); up to 9 images + 3 videos + 3 audio clips.
- Reference of "visual composition, camera language, motion rhythm, and sound characteristics";
  the post also claims it "can even directly reference text-based storyboards".
- Video editing of specified clips/characters/actions/storylines and video extension.
- 15-second multi-shot output with audio generated jointly with the video.
- "Dual-channel" / "two-channel stereo" audio, multi-track (music, ambience, voiceover).

## Gaps

- No API identifiers, prices, resolution list, frame rate, or region availability appear in the
  post. Those come from Volcengine Ark, BytePlus ModelArk, and the hosts (separate records).
- The post does not say whether the February launch was GA or staged; Volcengine's release
  announcement page records a later "全量发布" (full release) entry in its 2026-04 section (separate
  record).
- Evaluation numbers are not published as text and were not captured.
- The claim of "two-channel stereo" audio contradicts BytePlus's API documentation, which states all
  generated audio is mono (see the BytePlus create-task record).

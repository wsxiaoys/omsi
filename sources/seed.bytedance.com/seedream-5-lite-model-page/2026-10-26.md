## What this establishes

- The model has a first-party product page separate from the blog, so "Seedream 5.0 Lite" is a
  first-party product name, not a host label.
- First-party API access is routed to BytePlus ModelArk rather than to a `seed.bytedance.com`
  endpoint.
- ByteDance's own benchmark framing is an *internal* benchmark, and the page names it
  "MagicBench" while the launch blog names the evaluation platform "MagicArena". Both are
  internal and neither publishes numbers in text.

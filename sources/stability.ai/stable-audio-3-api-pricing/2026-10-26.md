# Stability AI: API pricing for Stable Audio 3.0 (and a contradicting credit table)

- Origin: Stability AI Platform docs —
  - pricing page: <https://docs.stability.ai/pricing> (also served at
    `https://platform.stability.ai/pricing`)
  - FAQ: <https://docs.stability.ai/faq>
  - documentation index: <https://platform.stability.ai/llms.txt> (a 25-line summary that assigns its
    own credit charges)
- Evidence kind: first-party pricing documentation; the llms.txt index is first-party but stale
- Publication date: undated; "Pricing is subject to change as we improve our models and
  infrastructure." Capture date 2026-10-26
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Stable Audio 3.0 (and Stable Audio 2.5, for comparison)

## Pricing page, verbatim

> API usage is based on credits. 1 credit = $0.01.

| Service | Description (verbatim) | Price (credits) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stable Audio 3.0** | "Generate up to six minutes high-quality audio with coherent structure from text prompts or audio samples" | **8** |
| Stable Audio 2.5 | "Generate up to three minutes high-quality audio with coherent structure from text prompts or audio samples" | 8 |

Per-endpoint pages repeat the basis: "Flat rate of 8 credits per successful generation" — **$0.08 per
generation**, regardless of requested duration (1–380 s), workflow (text-to-audio, audio-to-audio,
inpaint) or output format.

## The contradiction

`https://platform.stability.ai/llms.txt` — the machine-readable index that the docs pages themselves
tell agents to fetch — lists different charges:

> ## Audio (Stable Audio 3 — async: 202 `{"id"}` then poll GET /v2beta/audio/results/{id})
> - POST https://api.stability.ai/v2beta/audio/stable-audio/text-to-audio — **26 credits**
> - POST https://api.stability.ai/v2beta/audio/stable-audio/audio-to-audio
> - POST https://api.stability.ai/v2beta/audio/stable-audio/inpaint
>
> ## Audio (Stable Audio 2.5 / 2.0 — synchronous)
> - POST https://api.stability.ai/v2beta/audio/stable-audio-2/text-to-audio — **20 credits**

Three disagreements, preserved rather than reconciled:

1. **26 credits versus 8 credits** for the same Stable Audio 3 text-to-audio endpoint.
2. **20 versus 8** for Stable Audio 2.5.
3. llms.txt calls Stable Audio 2.5/2.0 "synchronous" while the 3.0 route is async; the 2.5 reference
   page is not captured here, so that split is unverified.

The OpenAPI pages are the more specific and more recent-looking artefact (they describe the async 202
flow and name the credits inline in the `model` field), so this page treats **8 credits = $0.08** as
the operative price and records 26 as an unresolved stale value.

## FAQ, verbatim

> ## What format does Stable Audio return?
> Audio is returned as a file in the response, or as base64 JSON if you set the `accept` header that
> way.
>
> ## How fast is a Stable Audio generation?
> Most requests finish in seconds, and time scales with the length of audio you ask for.
>
> ## Can I use the API outputs for commercial purposes?
> Yes, as long as the output doesn't violate anyone's copyright. See the Terms of Service for details.
>
> ## What was Stable Audio trained on?
> Stable Audio models are trained on fully licensed material.

The commercial-use answer is a bare assertion with a Terms-of-Service referral; it does not mention the
Community Licence revenue threshold that the launch post and the licence FAQ both impose on the
weights.

## Gaps

- No enterprise or volume pricing, no per-second tier, no free-tier quota beyond "25 free credits" in
  llms.txt.
- No statement of whether a failed generation that is moderated is refunded — the API reference says
  it is not charged; the pricing page says only "you will not be charged for failed generations".
- No date on either document, so the direction of the 26→8 change cannot be established.

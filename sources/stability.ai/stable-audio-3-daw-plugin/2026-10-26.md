# Stability AI: DAW plugin and web-app update for Stable Audio 3.0 (2026-08-18)

- Origin: Stability AI, "Sharing a new way to work with Stable Audio", Louisa Marshall, 18 Aug 2026,

- Publication date: **2026-08-18** (post header "Aug 18"; news index "8/18/26"). Capture date

- Relevant models: Stable Audio 3.0 (unnamed size; the post says "our commercially-safe models")

## Verbatim

> **TL;DR:** We're sharing two new ways for you to use Stable Audio 3.0. We now have a DAW plugin and a
> more advanced generation experience on StableAudio.com.
>
> The new plugin brings generation to your DAW, so you can work where you need to work. Arrange, edit,
> and mix what you make with Stable Audio 3.0 like any other audio.
>
> In the web app, go beyond the initial generation to give iterative direction, try variations, mix and
> edit, work with multiple tracks, and extend the length.
>
> Both are powered by our commercially-safe models. You own your outputs, and can distribute outputs
> freely.
>
> This is an early beta, so some features are experimental.

Plugin details (verbatim):

> The plugin is available as a macOS AU and VST3, with support for both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
> It is compatible with major DAWs including Logic Pro and Ableton Live.

> Work at your tempo. Sync Stable Audio to your session BPM so your generations fit the project you're
> already working on.
>
> Choose the length. Generate exactly what you need, from a short section to a full six-minute song.
>
> Keep your options open. Save multiple takes in a playlist so you can compare generations and decide
> what stays.

## Summary

The vendor's own product surface, four months after launch: a macOS-only AU/VST3 plugin in beta and an
expanded web app on stableaudio.com. It confirms six-minute generation inside the app and that the
vendor treats outputs as commercially usable, but it does not name the model size behind either
surface and does not add any pricing.

## Gaps

- No model size, no price, no usage limits, no Windows/Linux plugin.
- Neither the plugin nor the web app is documented in the API reference, so their model routing is
  unknown.

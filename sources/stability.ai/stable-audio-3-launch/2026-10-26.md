- Relevant models: Stable Audio 3.0 Small SFX, 3.0 Small, 3.0 Medium, 3.0 Large

## Key takeaways (verbatim)

> We're releasing **Stable Audio 3.0**, a model family with open-weights music models that are trained
> on fully licensed data.
>
> You own your outputs and can distribute and commercialize them under the Stability AI Community
> License, or the Enterprise License for organizations with more than $1M in revenue.
>
> Key innovations include variable-length generation up to six minutes, and full song composition on
> portable devices.
>
> Stable Audio 3.0 Small and Medium are available on Hugging Face. You can download the weights here.
>
> Stable Audio 3.0 Large is available via the Stability AI API and self-hosting for enterprise
> deployments. Try it out here.

## The four models (verbatim)

> - **Stable Audio 3.0 Small SFX**: Sound effects generation on-device, such as mobile phones and
>   consumer-grade laptops.
> - **Stable Audio 3.0 Small**: Full music composition on-device.
> - **Stable Audio 3.0 Medium**: Higher musicality (i.e. structure, melodic coherence, and phrasing)
>   and longer track length at up to 6:20.
> - **Stable Audio 3.0 Large**: The most advanced musicality in the family, built for music platforms
>   and creative applications that need low-latency generation at high volume.

The post says "Three of the models are open weights, free to download and build on" and later
"3.0 Small SFX, 3.0 Small, and 3.0 Medium are all open-weights". **Large's weights are not released**;
the only routes to it are the Stability API and enterprise self-hosting.

## Capability claims (verbatim, vendor marketing)

> Stable Audio 3.0 is our next-gen architecture, built with a novel semantic-acoustic autoencoder that
> enables longer, more flexible audio generation. … Stable Audio 3.0 introduces a new method for
> variable-length audio generation that enables you to generate exactly what you need, at per-second
> granularity.

> 3.0 Small generates up to two minutes, compared to 11 seconds from Stable Audio Open Small, and 47
> seconds from Stable Audio Open. 3.0 Medium and 3.0 Large generate more than six minutes.

> To our knowledge, 3.0 Small is the only model capable of full music composition on-device.

> For the first time we're publishing documentation for LoRa training, alongside the weights for 3.0
> Small and 3.0 Medium.

> Stable Audio 3.0 supports single-segment editing, multi-segment editing, and causal continuation
> (extending audio beyond its original endpoint).

Licensing and safety claims (verbatim):

> All Stable Audio 3.0 models are trained on fully licensed data. Under the Stability AI Community
> License, you own your outputs and can distribute and commercialize them freely. For organizations
> with more than $1M in annual revenue, you can get commercial coverage with our Enterprise license. We
> also offer legal indemnification under the Enterprise license.

> To our knowledge, other open music models either restrict commercial use or carry the risks
> associated with being trained on unlicensed music.

Availability claims (verbatim): "Stable Audio 3.0 will be available on ComfyUI and other platforms."
No date is given and no ComfyUI page is linked.

## Summary

This is the primary first-party release statement for the family. It fixes the public date
(**2026-05-20**), the four-tier structure, the open/closed split (Small SFX, Small, Medium open; Large
API/enterprise only), the 2-minute and 6:20 duration ceilings, the Community-License output ownership
and the $1M revenue threshold for the Enterprise licence, and the LoRA-training and editing claims.

## Gaps

- No model sizes, parameter counts, sample rate, output formats, or latency figures — those are in the
  technical report and the API docs.
- No prices.
- No named training datasets (the API reference names AudioSparx and Freesound; this post only says
  "fully licensed data").
- The link targets are not resolvable from the captured HTML: "here" for the weights points at a
  Hugging Face **collection** (`https://huggingface.co/collections/stabilityai/stable-audio-3`), and
  the paper link is not an arXiv URL in the markup.
- The post's own "ComfyUI" availability claim is unverified in this corpus.

## Community tier, verbatim

> **Community** — For researchers, developers, small businesses, and creators with less than $1M in
> annual revenue.
>
> License includes: **Stable Audio 3.0**, Stable Diffusion 3.5 Suite, SDXL Turbo, Stable Fast 3D
>
> **Free**

> **Enterprise** — For enterprise, API providers, and businesses with annual revenue exceeding $1M.
>
> License includes: Commercial Use, Implementation Support
>
> Available upgrades include: Custom Model Training, Consulting Services, Custom Pricing

## The revenue threshold, verbatim

> Use of the Core Models … is free for everyone, unless you're using the Core Models for a commercial
> purpose and you or your organization generate over USD $1M (or local currency equivalent) of annual
> revenue, regardless of the source of that revenue.

> The Stability AI Community License allows for research, non-commercial, and commercial use of the
> Core Models for individuals or organizations that generate under $1M … of annual revenue,
> regardless of the source of that revenue.

> I am part of an organization that just hit USD $1M of annual revenue. What should I do next in order
> to continue using the Core Models and Derivative Works? … Please contact Stability AI in order to
> purchase an Enterprise License.

## Output ownership, verbatim

> Yes, as between you and Stability, you own outputs generated from the Core Models or Derivative
> Works (such as fine-tunes) and therefore can use those outputs at your discretion, so long as you do
> so in compliance with applicable law and our AUP.

## Fine-tuning and LoRA, verbatim

> LoRAs, hypernetworks, fine-tunes, retrains, etc., are not "foundational models". If you have created
> a LoRA, you can use its outputs to train other LoRA's. You can't use a Core Model, or its outputs, to
> create a "new" model architecture.

> The Community License places restrictions on creating competing "foundational models" using the Core
> Models, Derivative Works and their outputs.

## Revocability, verbatim

> Like most agreements, the Community License is revocable if you violate its terms.

## Summary

Stable Audio 3.0's open weights (Small SFX, Small, Medium) are released under the Stability AI
Community License: free for individuals and organisations under USD $1M annual revenue, output
ownership with Stability, LoRA and fine-tune rights, a ban on using the models or their outputs to
build a competing foundation model, and revocation for breach. Above $1M, an Enterprise licence is
required and is the only route that includes indemnification.

The page is a **FAQ about** the licence, not the licence text. The licence text itself is served from
the Hugging Face repositories (`LICENSE.md`), which are gated.

Step 5 Preview appears nowhere in the index, the retirement table, or the scheduled-retirement
list. Nothing on this page gives it a support window, an end date, or a GA commitment.

## The one Step 5 Preview reference, verbatim

> "For new projects or upgrades involving software engineering, professional knowledge work, or
> multimodal input, also consider Step 5 Preview, with text, image, and video input and a
> 1M-token context window. The table above preserves the original retirement migration mappings.
> Step 5 Preview does not replace image-generation or audio models; check parameters, prices,
> and account access and validate your workload before switching."

Two things follow. The vendor positions Step 5 Preview as the forward path for text/multimodal
workloads on retired text models (`step-3` → `step-3.7-flash` is the table's mapping, with Step 5
Preview offered as the richer alternative). And the vendor explicitly disclaims it as a
replacement for image-generation or audio models — a scope statement, not a capability claim.

The retirement date for the July batch is **2026-07-08**; the image models were retired
**2026-10-10**. Both predate the Step 5 Preview listing on OpenRouter, and the July retirement
predates the model's own existence, so neither is evidence about Step 5 Preview's status.

## Summary

The lifecycle page is the only first-party page that discusses model status at all, and it
treats Step 5 Preview as a current recommendation rather than a scheduled retirement. It
neither confirms nor denies a preview expiry.

## Gaps

- No preview policy: the docs set has no page explaining what the `-preview` suffix means, how
  long it lasts, or what happens at GA (ID change, price change, weight change).
- The lifecycle index has no entry for any preview model, so absence here is weak evidence of
  stability rather than positive evidence of a support window.

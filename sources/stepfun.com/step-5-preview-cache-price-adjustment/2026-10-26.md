## What the announcement says

Effective **北京时间 2026 年 10 月 14 日 00:30** (2026-10-14 00:30 Beijing time), the platform
raised the cache-hit input price for the Step 5 Preview pay-as-you-go API. Cache-hit pricing
under the Step Plan access route is excluded from the change.

| Billing item | Before | After (standard) |
| - | - | - |
| Cache-hit input | ¥0.35 / M tokens | **¥0.70 / M tokens** |
| Cache-miss input | ¥7 / M tokens | unchanged |
| Output | ¥20 / M tokens | unchanged |

Grandfathering, verbatim:

> "北京时间 2026 年 10 月 14 日 00:30 前，已通过按量付费 API 调用过 Step 5 Preview 的用户，后续调用继续按原缓存命中输入价格 0.35 元／百万 tokens 计费，不受本次调价影响。"

("Users who had already called Step 5 Preview through the pay-as-you-go API before 2026-10-14
00:30 Beijing time continue to be billed at the old cache-hit input price of ¥0.35 per million
tokens and are not affected by this adjustment.")

## Why this record matters

1. **It is the only dated first-party artefact about Step 5 Preview that was captured.** It
   proves the model was in paying customer use before 2026-10-14, which is consistent with a
   September launch and inconsistent with treating OpenRouter's "Released Oct 8, 2026" as the
   model's first availability (see the [OpenRouter record](../../openrouter.ai/step-5-preview/2026-10-26.md)).
2. **It dates a price change on one line item only.** Input and output list prices were not
   touched; the USD page's $1.00 / $2.70 therefore describe both sides of the change.
3. **The CNY figures are the primary price list.** ¥7 / ¥0.70 / ¥20 per million tokens is
   consistent with $1.00 / $0.10 / $2.70 at roughly ¥7 per USD, but no StepFun page states a
   conversion rate or guarantees that the two lists move together.

## Summary

A dated, narrow announcement: cache-hit input rose from ¥0.35 to ¥0.70 per million tokens on
2026-10-14 00:30 Beijing time for pay-as-you-go Step 5 Preview calls, with grandfathering for
prior callers and no change to cache-miss input (¥7) or output (¥20).

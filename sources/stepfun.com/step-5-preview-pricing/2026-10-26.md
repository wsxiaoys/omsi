## Multimodal reasoning models, verbatim table

| Model | Billing unit | Input (cache miss) | Input (cache hit) | Output |
| :- | :- | :- | :- | :- |
| `step-5-preview` | 1M tokens | $1.00 | $0.10 | $2.70 |
| `step-3.7-flash` | 1M tokens | $0.20 | $0.04 | $1.15 |

Billing note, verbatim:

> "For `step-5-preview`, the cache-miss input price includes writing new content to the cache.
> Output tokens include both the model's reasoning process and final answer."

That second sentence matters for cost estimation: reasoning tokens are billed at the $2.70
output rate, not separately and not discounted. The first sentence means there is no separate
cache-write line item on this model, unlike some providers' cache-write pricing.

## Rate limits, verbatim

Tiers are assigned by cumulative cash top-up (vouchers excluded); identity verification is
required for individual accounts.

| User tier | Cumulative top-up | Concurrency | RPM | TPM |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| V0 | Under $15 | 5 | 100 | 500,000 |
| V1 | $15 to $69 | 20 | 400 | 2,000,000 |
| V2 | $70 to $299 | 30 | 600 | 3,000,000 |
| V3 | $300 to $1,499 | 40 | 800 | 4,000,000 |
| V4 | $1,500 and above | 130 | 2,600 | 13,000,000 |

> "When resources reach capacity, we may temporarily adjust the limits for each tier."

The page does not break limits out per model, so the Step 5 Preview-specific concurrency and TPM
are whatever the account tier allows, not a model-level quota.

## Summary

Step 5 Preview is billed per token at **$1.00 / $0.10 / $2.70** per million cache-miss input /
cache-hit input / output tokens. Cache-miss input includes cache writes; reasoning tokens bill
as output. Rate limits are account-tier, not model-specific.

# sync. labs billing docs: frame billing, free trial, duration limits

- Origin: sync. labs, "Billing", <https://docs.sync.so/product/billing>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (billing mechanics, plan limits)
- Publication date: page footer says "Last updated October 19, 2026"
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `sync-3`; all sync. labs lip-sync models for the billing basis

## Billing basis (verbatim)

> Lip sync usage is calculated per output frame, with per-second rates (at 25 fps) ranging from $0.04
> (lipsync-2) to $0.133 (sync-3).

> For lip sync, we calculate usage by counting the frames in your output video and multiplying by the
> per-frame price for the model you used.

> With `sync_mode: "cut_off"`, the processed duration is the shorter of the video and audio tracks.
> Billing uses that processed duration, rather than charging for the discarded part of the longer
> track. For example, a 10-second video with 30 seconds of audio processes and bills 10 seconds.

## Model price table (verbatim)

| Model | Per-second rate |
| --- | --- |
| lipsync-2 | $0.04 — $0.05/sec |
| lipsync-2-pro | $0.067 — $0.083/sec |
| sync-3 | $0.107 — $0.133/sec |

The page also says "sync-3 and lipsync-2-pro cost more per frame than lipsync-2."

## Free trial (verbatim)

> New to Sync Labs? Every free account gets 3 free generations per month plus 10 text-to-speech
> generations, with a maximum duration of 20 seconds. Of your 3 monthly generations, at most 1 can be
> Sync 3 with a 15-second limit. No credit card required.

## Duration limits (verbatim)

| Plan | Maximum duration |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 20 seconds |
| Hobbyist | 1 minute |
| Scale+ | 30 minutes |

The billing page points to the pricing page for the intermediate Creator (5 min) and Growth (10 min)
limits.

## Watermark and plan features (verbatim)

> The Hobbyist plan still includes a watermark. Watermark-free downloads start on Creator and above.
> After upgrading, you can download videos you already generated without the watermark.

## Other statements

- Usage invoices are generated when a tier threshold is reached ($6 Hobbyist, $20 Creator, $50 Growth,
  $250 Scale), then charged automatically.
- The page describes "legacy subscription + usage plans" and says organizations on credit billing use
  Hobby/Starter/Pro/Team instead. The captured page does not give a credit rate for sync-3.
- An "Estimate Cost" endpoint (`POST /v2/generations/estimate`) takes `model`, `duration` and optional
  `fps` (default 30) and returns an estimated frame count and cost.

## Summary

sync-3 is billed per output frame, not per request or per input second. At the 25 fps reference rate
that is $0.1334/sec on Hobbyist/Creator and $0.1066/sec on Scale. With `cut_off`, only the shorter of
video and audio is billed. Free accounts get one 15-second sync-3 generation per month; paid plans cap
output duration at 1, 5, 10 or 30 minutes depending on tier.

## Gaps

- No statement of how a non-25 fps output is billed, despite the pricing table being defined at 25 fps.
- No statement of whether failed or rejected generations are billed.
- No credit-plan rate for sync-3 on the captured page.

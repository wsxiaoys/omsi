- Origin: sync. labs, "sync. labs changelog", <https://sync.so/changelog>
- Evidence kind: official product changelog (dated release entries)
- Publication date: entries dated 2026-05-05, 2026-07-14, 2026-09-30 and later; the captured page is

- Relevant models: `sync-3`; image-to-video with sync-3

## Dated entries (verbatim excerpts)

**May 5, 2026 — "Dubbing, Assistant mode, MCP server, and sync-3"**

> sync-3 - Our newest model is now available to all users, including free tier. sync-3 delivers the
> highest quality lipsync we’ve ever shipped.

**July 14, 2026 — "Image-to-video, DaVinci Resolve, and the full creation stack"**

> Image-to-video with sync-3 - Turn a still image into a naturally speaking video. Upload a JPEG, PNG,
> or WebP image, add audio, choose the face you want to animate, and let sync-3 build the performance.
> Available in the App, API, Adobe Premiere Pro, and DaVinci Resolve.

The same entry says: "Closed-lip footage now prompts you to use sync-3, which handles that starting
position more naturally." It also records a bug fix: "Fixed sync-3 rejecting some videos that were
within the duration limit after sync mode was applied."

**September 30, 2026 — "Clearer wait times and more reliable creation"**

The entry describes Studio/API wait-time estimates and downloadable completed results. It does not
mention sync-3 by name.

## Summary

The changelog dates the broad rollout, not the first announcement. sync-3 was announced in the
2026-04-06 blog post, but the May 5 entry says it became "available to all users, including free
tier" on that later date. Image-to-video for sync-3 was added on 2026-07-14. That is the strongest
first-party evidence for treating 2026-04-06 as launch/announcement and 2026-05-05 as general
availability.

## Gaps

- No entry on 2026-04-06 itself; the launch date comes from the blog, not the changelog.
- The changelog does not define what "all users" included before May 5 (preview, waitlist, or paid
  only).
- No entry describes a sync-3 quality update or checkpoint after launch.

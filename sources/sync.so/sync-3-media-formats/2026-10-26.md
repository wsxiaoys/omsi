# sync. labs media formats: input limits and output encoding

- Origin: sync. labs, "Media Formats Support",
  <https://docs.sync.so/compatibility-and-tips/media-formats-support>
- Evidence kind: official documentation (accepted formats, resolution limits, output encoding)
- Publication date: page footer says "Last updated October 2, 2026"
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: all sync. labs lip-sync models, including `sync-3`

## Input formats (verbatim tables)

Video: `video/mp4` (.mp4), `video/quicktime` (.mov, .qt), `video/webm` (.webm), `video/x-msvideo`
(.avi).

Audio full support: `audio/wav` (.wav), `audio/mpeg` (.mp3), `audio/ogg` (.ogg), `audio/flac`
(.flac), `audio/alac` (.alac), `audio/mp4` (.mp4). Limited support (licensing/legal restrictions):
WMA, M4A, M3A, AAC.

The page recommends MP4 for video and WAV or MP3 for audio.

## Resolution and duration limits (verbatim)

> 4K maximum: Videos above 4096×2160 are rejected. Downscale to 4K or below before uploading.

> The pipeline extracts the face region at 512×512 for processing, then composites it back into the
> original frame. The output always matches the input dimensions and orientation.

The page says the recommended input is 1920×1080, H.264 High Profile, ≥10 Mbps, 24/25/30 fps constant,
8-bit SDR BT.709. "Use at least 480p resolution for reliable face detection. Higher resolutions up to
4K (4096x2160) are supported."

Maximum duration is plan-dependent: Free 20 seconds, Hobbyist 1 minute, Scale+ 30 minutes. `react-1`
has a hard 15-second limit regardless of plan.

## Audio limits (verbatim)

> Sample rate: 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz. Higher rates are downsampled to 48 kHz, which may reduce quality.
> Bit depth: Up to 32-bit float. Channels: Up to 7.1. Spatial audio is not supported. Multiple
> streams: Only the first audio stream is processed; all others are discarded.

## Output encoding (verbatim)

> All output is re-encoded to H.264 using libx264 (`-crf 17 -preset slow`), regardless of the input
> codec. Frames are processed in RGB color space internally during generation, so the original
> bitrate, frame rate, and color grading may differ in the output.

> HDR is not fully supported: HDR videos are normalized to SDR, which may affect color grading in the
> output. Alpha channels are removed: The H.264/RGB pipeline does not support transparency. Alpha
> channels are replaced with a solid background.

## Contradiction with the sync-3 model page

The sync-3 docs page says "Face resolution: 4K native output" and the comparison table says
"4K native", while this page says the face region is extracted at 512×512 for processing, and the
generation-times page says "all models process faces at 512x512". The media-formats page also says
output dimensions always match the input. These statements can be reconciled only if "4K native output" means
the composited frame is 4K while the generated face patch is not; no captured page says that
explicitly. Recorded as an unresolved contradiction.

## Summary

sync-3 accepts video or image plus audio, with video capped at 4096×2160 and audio at 7.1 channels /
48 kHz. Output is always H.264 MP4 at input dimensions, SDR, no alpha, and is re-encoded even when the
input was a different codec. The documented face-processing resolution conflicts with the marketing
claim of "4K native" output.

## Gaps

- No bitrate or file-size limit for video on this page beyond the 5 GiB upload ceiling mentioned in
  the FAQ.
- No explicit output frame-rate rule; the page says the original frame rate "may differ".
- No per-model resolution table; the 512×512 statement is generic and may predate sync-3.

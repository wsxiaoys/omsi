## Watermark

The pricing page's feature table shows "No watermark" only for Creator and above. The billing docs
state: "The Hobbyist plan still includes a watermark. Watermark-free downloads start on Creator and
above."

## Summary

sync-3 is the most expensive lip-sync model on sync. labs' own price list: $0.00534 per output frame at
25 fps on Hobbyist/Creator, falling to $0.00427 on Scale, which is $0.1334 to $0.1066 per output
second. The docs' headline range ($0.107–$0.133/sec) matches this table. Plan tier controls maximum
video duration (1/5/10/30 minutes) and watermark removal.

## Gaps

- No date on the pricing page, so this is a snapshot of current rates, not a price history.
- The table is denominated per frame and converted at 25 fps; no statement of how billing handles
  other output frame rates.
- No enterprise rate, volume threshold, or refund detail on this page.

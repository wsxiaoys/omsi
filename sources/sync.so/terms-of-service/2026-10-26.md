# sync. labs terms of service: user content, consent, output

- Origin: sync. labs (Synchronicity Labs Inc.), "sync. terms of service", <https://sync.so/terms>
- Evidence kind: provider policy / legal terms
- Publication date: "Date of Last Revision: June 6th, 2024"
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: all sync. labs services, including `sync-3`

## User-content warranty (verbatim)

> You represent and warrant that you own all right, title and interest in and to your User Content,
> including all copyrights, trademarks and rights of privacy and publicity contained therein, and you
> have provided any required notices and obtained any required consents to upload, store, transmit,
> use, or otherwise process User Content via the Service in accordance with applicable laws.

The same paragraph grants sync. labs a broad licence to use User Content:

> You hereby grant Sync Labs and its affiliates, successors and assigns a non-exclusive, worldwide,
> royalty-free, fully paid-up, transferable, sublicensable (directly and indirectly through multiple
> tiers), perpetual, and irrevocable license to copy, display, upload, perform, distribute, store,
> modify, and otherwise use your User Content ... (a) in connection with the operation of the Service,
> (b) to develop and improve the Service and other Sync Labs offerings, (c) for the promotion,
> advertising or marketing of the foregoing; and (d) as otherwise set forth in our Privacy Policy.

## AI-generated output (verbatim excerpt)

> You acknowledge and agree the Service is supported by various artificial intelligence algorithms and
> models (the "AI Models") and that certain information, recommendations, suggestions or other output
> ... generated and returned by the Service may be generated using such AI Models and other
> third-party AI tools.

The clause then lists generic AI limitations (errors, bias, lack of creativity) and says the user is
"responsible for evaluating, and bearing all risks associated with" the output.

## What the terms do not say

- No clause assigns ownership of generated output to the user. The captured terms contain no
  "you own the output" or "commercial use of output is permitted" statement.
- No explicit deepfake, impersonation, or likeness-verification policy beyond the general warranty
  that the user has obtained "any required consents" and holds rights of privacy and publicity.
- No model-specific licence, weights licence, or acceptable-use policy for sync-3.

## Summary

The operative commercial terms are the general sync. labs ToS, last revised 2024-06-06. They put the
burden on the user to hold rights and obtain consents for uploaded video/audio, and grant sync. labs a
broad licence over user content. They do not explicitly grant output ownership or state commercial-use
rights for generated video; no separate sync-3 licence was captured.

## Gaps

- The terms predate sync-3 by almost two years; no sync-3-specific addendum was found.
- No enterprise/DPA or output-ownership terms were captured.
- No host-side licence terms for fal or Replicate were captured beyond fal's "Commercial use" label.

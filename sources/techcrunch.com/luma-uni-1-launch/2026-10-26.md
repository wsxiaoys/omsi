# TechCrunch: Luma launches creative AI agents on its new "Unified Intelligence" models (Uni-1)

- Origin: TechCrunch, "Luma launches creative AI agents powered by its new 'Unified Intelligence'
  models", Rebecca Bellan,
  <https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/exclusive-luma-launches-creative-ai-agents-powered-by-its-new-unified-intelligence-models/>
- Evidence kind: third-party report (trade press, from a vendor briefing)
- Publication date: 2026-03-05, 10:11 AM PST
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Luma Uni-1; Luma Agents (platform)

## Captured content

Byline: "Rebecca Bellan — 10:11 AM PST · March 5, 2026". Headline reads "Exclusive".

Verbatim:

> AI video-generation startup Luma on Thursday launched Luma Agents, designed to handle end-to-end
> creative work across text, image, video, and audio. Luma Agents are powered by the startup's
> Unified Intelligence family of models, with architecture trained on a single multimodal reasoning
> system.

> Luma's agents are built on the startup's Uni-1 model, the first of its Unified Intelligence family
> of AI models. It has been trained on audio, video, image, language, and spatial reasoning,
> according to Amit Jain, chief executive officer and co-founder of Luma.

> Jain told TechCrunch that the Uni-1 model can "think in language and imagine and render in pixels
> or images … we call it 'intelligence in pixels.'" Other output capabilities like audio and video
> will come in subsequent model releases, he added.

> Luma has already started rolling out its new agentic platform with existing customers, including
> global ad agencies Publicis Groupe and Serviceplan, as well as for brands like Adidas, Mazda, and
> Saudi AI company Humain.

> Jain said the Luma Agents are a game changer because they can maintain persistent context across
> assets, collaborators, and creative iterations. They can also evaluate and refine outputs,
> improving their own results through an iterative self-critique, according to Jain.

The article also notes the agents can coordinate with other vendors' models (Ray 3.14, Veo 3,
Nano Banana Pro, Seedream, ElevenLabs).

## What this establishes

- **Dated launch evidence for Uni-1: 2026-03-05**, announced together with Luma Agents. This is
  inside the 2025-10-26 one-year window.
- Uni-1's first shipped surface was the **agent platform**, not a standalone image API; the image
  API (Uni-1.1) followed on 2026-05-05.
- The "intelligence in pixels" framing and the claim that Uni-1 was trained on audio, video, image,
  language and spatial reasoning are the CEO's statements, reported second-hand.
- Video/audio output was described as future work as of March 2026.

## Gaps

- This is a press report of a vendor briefing, not an independent test; capability claims are Luma's.
- The article does not give a product name for the image feature or any pricing.
- Date conflict to preserve: Luma's own 2026-05-05 post says Uni-1 "launched last month" (implying
  April 2026), while this same-day report and Artificial Analysis's `releaseDate` (2026-05-05, the API
  date) give different anchors. March 5 is the best-dated launch evidence captured; "last month" is
  a loose vendor phrasing that may refer to the API's public availability.

## Captured content

### Processing-time ceilings and failure billing (FAQ, verbatim)

> The maximum processing time for our models is, generally—
>
> * **\~90 minutes** for GAN image models (e.g. Gigapixel Family)
> * **\~20 minutes** for generative image models (e.g. Wonder Family, Bloom Family)
> * **\~4 hours** for most GAN video models (e.g. Proteus Family)
> * **\~17 hours** for most generative video models (e.g. Starlight Family, Astra Family)
>
> Timeouts are considered failures on our side, so a full refund is issued, typically within 24
> hours. Note that the maximum processing timeframes listed above are *estimations* for reference and
> don't take into account queue time (e.g. image tasks are queued until three attempts are made, and
> the estimated maximum processing time for those three attempts is 3 hours).

> **If I cancel a job mid-processing, how is billing handled?** If the progress is 0% for the task in
> question, we issue a full refund. Else, the billing formula is `1.1 * [progress %]`. For example, if
> a canceled at 50%, then we keep 55% of the credits and refund 45%.

### Provenance and compliance (FAQ, verbatim)

> **Are outputs C2PA-compliant?** Yes, all of our API model outputs are C2PA-compliant.

### API key

Keys are created in the account portal (`account.topazlabs.com/manage-api`), shown once at creation,
and sent in the `X-API-KEY` header.

### API Limits

`resources/api-limits.md` exists at capture but contains **no body text** — only the page title "API
Limits" and the site's boilerplate. No rate limits, concurrency limits, or file-size limits are
published there.

### ZDR policy (effective 2026-10-16)

> Zero Data Retention (ZDR) is an optional arrangement that Topaz Labs offers Enterprise API
> customers on request.

> One commitment applies to every customer, with or without ZDR: **Topaz Labs never trains its
> models on customer data.**

> ZDR is off by default and does not come with any plan automatically. It cannot be turned on in user
> settings or an administrator portal. Topaz Labs enables it for eligible Enterprise customers once
> they request it.

> ZDR is available to Enterprise API customers that meet the volume and/or contractual requirements
> Topaz Labs sets. There is no separately published fee.

Retention table (verbatim):

| Data | Standard retention | With ZDR |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input media | "Stored securely in Topaz Labs cloud storage and deleted after 7 days" | short account-specific download window, then deleted |
| Output media | "available for download for 7 days, then deleted" | short account-specific window, then deleted |
| Job history | Retained | Retained |
| Request metadata | Retained | Retained |
| Service logs | "Retained for up to 3 years" | "Retained for up to 3 years" |
| Use of customer data for model training | Never | Never |

> All processing takes place in the United States.

> ZDR covers every model available through the Topaz Labs API, for both image and video. No models
> are excluded. API responses do not indicate ZDR status.

## Gaps

- **No latency data.** The FAQ gives worst-case processing ceilings and notes queueing, but publishes
  no typical or median duration for any model or output size. Nothing in the captured material
  measures wall-clock time.
- **The limits page is empty**, so throughput, concurrency, upload size, and burst behaviour are
  undocumented.
- ZDR is Enterprise-only and is not observable from a response, so a third party cannot verify
  whether a given call ran under ZDR.
- Standard retention is 7 days for inputs and outputs; no deletion-on-request policy is published
  outside the ZDR arrangement.

### Model Pricing — billing basis

> Pay only for what you process — billed per output megapixel across all image models. No
> subscriptions, no minimums, no expiration. Use our credit calculator to estimate costs before you
> build.

> Credits are the currency of the Topaz Labs API. All image processing is billed in credits, deducted
> automatically when a job completes.

> Billing is based on the **output** resolution of your processed image — not the input.

> Image models are priced linearly based on MP output (e.g. 1 credit = 24 MP of output).

> There are a few exceptions to some of the families. Click into a given family to view all prices,

> To access our most update-to-date pricing, please visit our [website](https://www.topazlabs.com/enhance-api).

## Interpretation

- The published unit is **output megapixels per credit**, not dollars. Converting to money requires
  the plan's per-credit price from the product page
  (`../../topazlabs.com/api-product-page/2026-10-26.md`): $0.12 (Starter, "coming soon"), $0.10
  (Developer), $0.08 (Scale), volume for Enterprise.
- At those rates a 24 MP Gigapixel upscale costs **$0.08** on Scale and **$0.10** on Developer. A
  24 MP Wonder-family upscale (4 MP/credit) costs 6 credits, i.e. **$0.48** on Scale.
- The individual table contradicts the family table for the Gigapixel family: Standard 2 is listed at
  24 MP/credit but High Fidelity 2 at 4 and High Fidelity 3 at 2, while the Gigapixel family page
  says the whole family is 24 MP/credit
  (`../../topazlabs.com/api-docs-gigapixel-models/2026-10-26.md`). fal's precision endpoint charges a
  single 24 MP rate for every precision model
  (`../../fal.ai/topaz-upscale/2026-10-26.md`), which matches the family page and not this table.

## Gaps

- **"No subscriptions, no minimums" contradicts the product page**, which sells $50/mo and $240/mo
  plans with included credits and a $0/mo "coming soon" tier. The docs describe metered per-MP
  billing; the site describes prepaid monthly credit bundles. Both may be true if the plans are
  credit bundles rather than minimums, but the two pages do not reconcile it.
- No effective date, no changelog, no per-model dollar prices, and no rounding rule (whether a
  partially used credit is charged in full). The product page's credit table implies rounding up per
  output-MP band.
- Half the individual-model table is empty, so it cannot be used to price most of the current models.

## Captured content

### Wonder family page

> Generative Upscaling for Images
>
> Generative image enhancement models designed to recover and restore low quality images while
> retaining precise and natural details.
>
> Generative models attempt to improve image quality by adding recovered detail and texture while
> preserving the original image intent.

> This family is priced at 1 credit per 4 megapixels of output.

Family credit table (verbatim): 1 credit at 1 MP, 1 at 4, 2 at 8, 4 at 16, 6 at 24, 8 at 32, 10 at
40, 13 at 50, 16 at 64, 25 at 100.

Family model cards: `Redefine`, `Wonder`, `Wonder 2`, `Standard Max`, `Recover 3`. The family page
does **not** list `Wonder 3` or `Wonder 3.5`, although both have pages and both are served on fal.

Key features claimed (verbatim):

> * Upscale, denoise and improve low resolution photos in 1 click.
> * Restore previously unrecoverable photos with high fidelity
> * Highest quality generative face recovery avoids plastic and synthetic looks

### Wonder model IDs and limits

| Model | Endpoint | `model` string | Input limit | Output limit | Credits/MP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wonder 3.5 | `POST https://api.topazlabs.com/image/v1/enhance-gen/async` | `"Wonder 3.5"` | not stated | not stated | 8 MP/credit |
| Standard Max | `POST https://api.topazlabs.com/image/v1/enhance-gen/async` | not quoted on page | 24 MP | 384 MP | 4 MP/credit (family) |
| Recover 3 | `POST https://api.topazlabs.com/image/v1/enhance-gen/async` | not quoted on page | 24 MP | 384 MP | 4 MP/credit (family) |
| Redefine | `POST https://api.topazlabs.com/image/v1/enhance-gen/async` | not quoted on page | 256 MP | 256 MP | 4 MP/credit (family) |

The Wonder 3.5 page's own credit table is the 8 MP step: 1 credit at 1–8 MP, 2 at 16, 3 at 24,
4 at 32, 5 at 40, 7 at 50, 8 at 64, 13 at 100. That is **half the family page's rate** for the same
output.

Wonder 3.5 parameters (verbatim):

> * `enhancementStrength` — enum (optional), default `"high"` — Enhancement strength (low, medium,
>   high)
> * `grain` — boolean (optional), default `false` … `grainModel` 'silver' | 'gaussian' | 'grey' …
>   `grainSize` (1 to 5) … `grainStrength` (0.0 to 1.0)
> * `inputHeight` / `inputWidth` / `outputHeight` / `outputWidth` — integer (optional), default null

### Bloom family page

> Creative Upscaling for Images
>
> Creative image enhancement models designed to upscale and transform GenAI images, with or without
> text based prompts
>
> Creative models add new levels of detail that can change objects, identity, and stylization.

Key features claimed (verbatim):

> * Creatively upscale images made with Al up to 8x with stunning detail.
> * Control details with text-based prompts.
> * Next-Gen AI image realism for realistic face and skin detail.

> This family is priced at 1 credit per 2 megapixels of output.

Family credit table (verbatim): 1 credit at 1 MP, 2 at 4, 4 at 8, 8 at 16, 12 at 24, 16 at 32,
20 at 40, 25 at 50, 32 at 64, 50 at 100.

### Bloom 2

Endpoint `POST https://api.topazlabs.com/image/v1/enhance-gen/async`, model `"Bloom 2"`. Parameters
(verbatim):

> * `colorPreservation` — boolean (optional), default `true`
> * `creativity` — integer (optional), default `3` — Creativity level: from 1 (low) to 9 (high)
> * `grain` / `grainDensity` / `grainModel` / `grainSize` / `grainStrength` — as on Wonder 3.5
> * `inputHeight` / `inputWidth` / `outputHeight` / `outputWidth` — integer (optional)

Bloom 2's own credit table is the same 2 MP step as the family page (1 credit at 1 MP, 12 at 24 MP).

## Gaps

- **Wonder 3.5 is priced at 8 MP/credit on its own page but 4 MP/credit on the family page**, and
  the individual-model pricing table does not list Wonder 3.5 at all
  (`../../topazlabs.com/api-docs-pricing/2026-10-26.md`). fal charges the 8 MP rate for Wonder 3 and
  Wonder 3.5 and 4 MP for the rest of the family
  (`../../fal.ai/topaz-upscale/2026-10-26.md`), which matches the model page rather than the family
  page.
- **"Up to 8x"** appears only as a Bloom marketing bullet. No model page states a scale-factor
  parameter or a per-model maximum factor for the generative/creative families.
- `model` strings for Standard Max, Recover 3, Redefine and Wonder 2 are not quoted in the pages
  captured; the `/enhance-gen/async` OpenAPI enum lists only `Standard MAX`, `Recovery V2`,
  `Wonder`, `Redefine`
  (`../../topazlabs.com/api-docs-image-reference/2026-10-26.md`), which omits Wonder 2, Wonder 3,
  Wonder 3.5, Recover 3 and Bloom entirely.
- No dates on any page.

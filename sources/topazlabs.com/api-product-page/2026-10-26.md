### Credit FAQ (verbatim)

> **What models are included with the Topaz API?** With the Topaz Labs API, you get access to image
> upscaling, image denoise, image sharpen, video upscaling, and video frame interpolation.

> **How do credits work?** Each credit processes a single request at up to 24 megapixel output size
> (e.g. 6000px by 4000px) with the Enhance endpoint. Up to 24 MP output: 1 credit · 32 MP output:
> 2 credits · 48 MP output: 3 credits · 64 MP output: 4 credits · 128 MP output: 6 credits · 256 MP
> output: 10 credits · 512 MP output: 16 credits

> **Do unused credits roll over?** You can maintain a maximum balance of 5x your monthly credit
> volume. All unused credits roll over each month and never expire.

> **What happens if I run out of credits?** If you run out of tokens, to avoid service interruption
> we will automatically reload tokens equal to 25% of your plan at the current per-token price.

> **What are your payment methods?** We currently support monthly credit card payment processing
> through Stripe.

## Gaps

- **Internal contradiction on the credit unit.** The banner says "1 API credit = 32MP Enhance
  output"; the FAQ on the same page says "up to 24 megapixel output size … Up to 24 MP output: 1
  credit" and then charges 2 credits for 32 MP. Both cannot describe one schedule. The FAQ table is
  the more detailed statement and matches the developer docs' "1 credit = 24 MP of output"
  (`../../topazlabs.com/api-docs-pricing/2026-10-26.md`), so the banner is most likely stale.
- The page does not name individual models, upscale factors, maximum output dimensions, input
  formats, or latency. Those come from the developer documentation.
- No publication date or changelog is shown, so this capture cannot date the page's terms; only the
  capture date is known.

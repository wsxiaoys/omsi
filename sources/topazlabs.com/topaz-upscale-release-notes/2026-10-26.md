### Press release, 2026-05-14 (verbatim)

> DALLAS, May 14, 2026 -- Topaz Labs, the leader in AI image and video enhancement, today announced
> the next iteration of its proprietary acceleration technology, Topaz NeuroStream™.

> Key models powered by NeuroStream 2 include Wonder 3, Denoise Max, Face Recovery 3, Super Focus 3,
> Starlight Precise 2.5, and Hyperion 2.

> Also introduced are two new powerful image enhancement models, Face Recovery 3 and Noise-Aware
> Sharpen, expanded local support for Mac and AMD users, and a Topaz Plugin for Nuke VFX & Film
> Editing Software.

> NeuroStream 2 allows users of the Starlight Precise 2.5 video upscaling model to utilize a typical
> 12GB enthusiast PC setup. In this setup detailed below, the same task dropped from 30 minutes to
> just 14 minutes.

The press release attributes "2X-to-4X faster processing time for images" to NeuroStream 2 and gives
one worked example (Starlight Precise 2.5 on an RTX 4070 Ti: 30 min → 14 min). That is a
hardware-specific vendor figure for a **video** model, not a latency measurement of the image API.

## Interpretation

- The image-upscaling **model family** has repeated in-window releases: 2025-12-24 (Realism), 2026-01-20
  (Wonder 2, Recover 3, with Wonder 2 explicitly in the API), 2026-05-14 (Wonder 3, Face Recovery 3),
  2026-08-04 (Wonder 3.5, Bloom 2), 2026-09-28 (Speed).
- These pages announce desktop and cloud-app releases. They do not date the **API** itself, and none
  of them says when the image API was first offered.

## Gaps

- **No API launch date.** The updates index only goes back to 2025-12-24, and none of the captured
  announcements mentions the API's own launch. The Wayback capture
  (`../../archive.org/topazlabs-api-2024-09-07/2026-10-26.md`) is the only evidence that dates the
  API, and it dates it to before the roster window.
- The "Wonder 3" release date is inferred from the 2026-05-14 press release listing it as a
  NeuroStream 2 launch model; there is no separate announcement captured for it.
- "Definition Update" does not say whether Wonder 3.5 and Bloom 2 were available in the API on
  2026-08-04; fal exposed both by 2026-08-11 (`../../fal.ai/topaz-upscale/2026-10-26.md`), and the
  first-party docs pages for both exist at capture, but no dated API availability statement was
  found.
- High Fidelity 3 has no dated announcement in any captured page.

## Summary

This is the only first-party page that gives Tripo H3.1 its own identity, and it does so through a
snapshot string rather than a prose release announcement. It establishes: the product name ("Tripo H3.1"),
the model ID (`v3.1-20260211`), the default status of that ID on all three generation endpoints, three
input modalities, a mesh + PBR output, a 2,000,000-face ceiling (1,500,000 in standard geometry mode),
quad/FBX and smart-low-poly options, four texture tiers, and a credit price table. It also names the two
older geometry snapshots (`v3.0-20250812`, `v2.5-20250123`) and states that the `v3.x` geometry family
defaults to the **older** `v3.0-20250812` texture model.

Every quality statement on the page ("Best-in-class geometry and PBR textures", "Quality — Highest",
"top-tier geometry") is unquantified marketing: no benchmark, no comparison numbers, no harness.

## Summary

H3.1 generation is priced in credits on a flat schedule that stacks add-ons, so a single call's price is
`base(task, texture tier) + geometry + topology + parts`. At the published rate of one cent per credit, an
H3.1 text-to-3D call ranges from **$0.10** (no texture) to **$0.40** (8K texture), and image- or
multiview-to-3D from **$0.20** to **$0.50**, before the optional +$0.20 detailed geometry, +$0.05 quad and
+$0.10 smart-low-poly add-ons. The vendor's own worked example ("$50 ≈ 250 text-to-3D models with standard
texture") is consistent with the 20-credit standard-texture price.

Rigging and animation are separately billed (25 credits to rig, 10 per animation, rig-check free), as is
every export container beyond GLB/FBX (5 or 10 credits) and any retopology.

## Gaps

  sales team".
- No free trial or initial credit grant is stated.
- The page shows only the "No Texture" and "Standard Texture" columns for H-series generation; the
  detailed/8K totals are only obtainable by adding the add-on rows, and they agree with the per-model page
  ([H3.1 model page](../tripo-h3-1-model-page/2026-10-26.md)).
- No per-second or per-minute metric anywhere: 3D is per-task only.

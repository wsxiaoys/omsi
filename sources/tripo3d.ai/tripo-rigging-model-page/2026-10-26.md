Parameters:

Rate limits: "Animation (shared with retarget) — 10 parallel tasks".

## Summary

Rigging is a separate Tripo product with its own model snapshots, its own credits and its own
`/v3/animations/*` endpoints; the H3.1 generation call returns geometry and texture only. The rig input is
any GLB/FBX/OBJ/STL mesh, so an H3.1 GLB is an eligible input by format, but no captured Tripo page states
that H3.1 output was tested or is recommended, and the free rig-check endpoint exists precisely because
compatibility is not guaranteed.

Note that the rig model versions are on a different track from the geometry versions: the newest rig is
`v3.0-20260909` (2026-09-09), while the newest geometry is `v3.1-20260211`.

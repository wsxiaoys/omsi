## Summary

There is no weights licence for H3.1 because no weights are published: use is governed entirely by this
service contract. Paid API users get a broad grant over their inputs and outputs (commercial use
included), and Tripo commits not to train on paid-user inputs/outputs. Free users get the opposite
treatment — Tripo retains the rights to their inputs and outputs. Two carve-outs matter in practice:
outputs may not be used to build a competing model or service, and the API may not be resold to third
parties as a service without written consent. No output exclusivity is offered. Governing law is Hong
Kong with HKIAC arbitration.

The terms do not contain a model-specific licence, a "commercial use" tier name, or a per-model
restriction; the distinction that matters is paid versus free.

## Gaps

- The terms are dated 2025-07-11, before H3.1's snapshot date; nothing indicates they were revised for it.
- No explicit statement on whether outputs may be used to train a *third party's* 3D model (the
  restriction is on "models or services that directly compete").
- No statement on ownership of the *mesh* as distinct from the texture maps, nor on moral rights.
- A separate privacy policy and any enterprise/API addendum were not captured.

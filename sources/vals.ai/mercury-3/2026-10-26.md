## Reading

- **The only independent quality measurement of Mercury 3 captured here, and it is unflattering.**
  Bottom quartile of the Vals Index (#25 of 28) at 43.66%, roughly 27 points behind Claude Sonnet
  5.5, and below MiniMax-M3.1-Flash (53.21%).
- **Terminal-Bench 4.0 = 3.03%** (with a zero-width interval, i.e. every run failed the same way)
  and **HLAB-Vals-1.1 = 5.00%**: near-floor results on agentic coding and legal hallucination
  gating. This is direct counter-evidence to the launch post's "on par or better than frontier
  speed-optimized models" framing for agentic work.
- **Latency 22 min 6 s per Vals Index test** is the number that most complicates the "real-time"
  positioning. It is measured at `reasoning_effort: high` with a 131,072-token output ceiling, so
  it is not the same configuration the vendor's 1,068 t/s claim describes — but it is the only
  end-to-end task latency captured, and it is minutes, not milliseconds.
- **Refusal rate 0.14%** and **fallback rate 0.00%** are low, so the low scores are not an
  artifact of refusals or provider failover.
- Vals lists **Weights: Private**, corroborating `hugging_face_id: null` on OpenRouter.

## Gaps

- No per-benchmark description of scaffolding, retries, or token budgets is captured here; the
  hyperparameter block is the only configuration disclosure.
- The leaderboard's own row ordering in text extraction is ambiguous (values appear before names
  in some renderings), which is why the model page's own 43.66% is the value used.
- No speed (tokens/s) measurement — Vals reports task latency, not throughput.

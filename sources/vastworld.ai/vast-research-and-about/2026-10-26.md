## Summary

VAST presents EdenLive as the **rendering** component of a two-part architecture, with Project Eden
supplying persistent world state. The company describes itself as a 2022-founded, Beijing-headquartered
3D-AI company with production 3D models (Tripo) and a world model under development, which is the context
for EdenLive being an open-source release rather than a standalone commercial API.

## Gaps

- No launch date, version number or roadmap date for EdenLive or Project Eden on any of the three pages.
- Project Eden is described only as a "research preview"; no interface, availability or licence is given,
  so its relationship to EdenLive beyond "rendering is separated from state" cannot be pinned down.
- The `/about` funding line ("Series B+B+ closed September 2026") has no linked announcement in this

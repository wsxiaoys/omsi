## Captured content

Limitations, verbatim:

> Requires AI Credits for generation.

Workflow, verbatim:

> Go to the Video tab. Scroll down to Talking Characters and click View All to browse the available
> avatars.
>
> Choose a Character […] You can also create a new taking character by clicking Create New within the
> Talking Characters tab
>
> Add Your Script — Go to the Text tab and type or paste your script. You can enter up to 20000
> characters per avatar generation.

> We will begin generating your avatar using the text you've provided.

FAQ, verbatim:

> Q: Can I edit the character or the text after it is generated?
>
> You can edit the text after the talking character is generated as long as you don't cut or trim the
> video. Please note that if you regenerate the avatar, it will deduct from your AI Credit allowance
> again.

> Q: Can I add more than 20000 characters?
>
> You can add up to 20000 characters per avatar. To add more, create a new avatar segment.

> Q: Can I change the appearance of the characters?
>
> No, the appearance of the characters cannot be changed, even if they are regenerated, or instructions
> are included in the prompt.

> Q: Can I have multiple AI Avatars or Talking Characters on screen at the same time?
>
> You can only have 1 character or avatar on screen. AI Avatars and Talking Characters aren't designed
> to have multiple characters on screen at the same time.

## Summary

This documents the sibling product Fabric 1.0 is sold against: a browsable avatar library driven by a
text script (up to 20,000 characters per generation), with no ability to change a character's
appearance and one character per screen. It is a text-in surface, whereas Fabric 1.0 takes an image plus
audio — the distinction VEED's own API article draws when it says Fabric works "unlike avatar tools that
limit you to a preset library".

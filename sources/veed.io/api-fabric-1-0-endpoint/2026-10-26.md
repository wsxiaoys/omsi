## Captured content

Endpoint summary, verbatim from the docs page:

> Fabric 1.0
> Submit a fabric-1.0 job
> image audio → produces video
> $0.08–$0.15 per second
>
> Start an asynchronous job that animates a source image to lip-sync it to an audio track, producing a
> talking video at the resolution you choose.

Inputs, verbatim:

> image_url — public URL of the source image, which should show a clear face. Photos, illustrations,
> mascots and stylised characters all work
> audio_url — public URL of the speech to lip-sync. The model takes audio, not text: to make the image
> say a script, generate the audio with a text-to-speech tool first
> resolution — output resolution: 480p or 720p

> The job is accepted immediately — you get 202 Accepted with a job_id and status PROCESSING. Rendering
> usually takes a minute or two; poll GET /v1/fabric-1.0/{job_id} until the job is COMPLETED or FAILED.

Request headers, verbatim:

> X-Veed-Store-IO — Set to 0 to not store this request's and response's bodies. […] Defaults to "1".
> X-Veed-Media-Expiration-Seconds — Number of seconds before the media URLs returned for this request
> expire. […] At most 2592000. Defaults to 86400.

Fabric-specific error codes, verbatim:

> input_validation, content_moderation, invalid_file, audio_too_long, transload_failed,
> generation_failed, timeout, insufficient_credits

OpenAPI extension block for `POST /v1/fabric-1.0`, verbatim (YAML):

```yaml
      x-veed-media:
        inputs:
          - image
          - audio
        outputs:
          - video
      x-veed-model-slug: veed/fabric-one-lipsync
      x-veed-poll-interval-seconds: 10
      x-veed-pricing:
        currency: USD
        dimensions:
          - field: resolution
            label: Resolution
        measure: the generated video
        rates:
          - amount: 0.08
            when:
              resolution: 480p
          - amount: 0.15
            when:
              resolution: 720p
        rounding: exact
        unit: second
        unit_quantity: 1
```

`FabricInput` schema constraints, verbatim (YAML):

```yaml
    FabricInput:
      additionalProperties: false
      properties:
        audio_url: {format: uri, maxLength: 8192, minLength: 1, pattern: '^[Hh][Tt][Tt][Pp][Ss]?://'}
        image_url: {format: uri, maxLength: 8192, minLength: 1, pattern: '^[Hh][Tt][Tt][Pp][Ss]?://'}
        resolution: {enum: [720p, 480p]}
      required: [image_url, audio_url, resolution]
```

(Reformatted onto one line per field; the field names, types, patterns, `maxLength` and the
`required` list are verbatim. The schema also carries a read-only `$schema` property that callers do
not send.)

## Summary

api.veed.io is VEED's own API platform and documents Fabric 1.0 as `POST /v1/fabric-1.0` with three
required inputs — image URL, audio URL, and resolution — returning a job envelope that resolves to one
video file. The OpenAPI document supplies the machine-readable price ($0.08/s at 480p, $0.15/s at
720p, billed per exact second with no rounding), a 10-second poll interval, and the internal model slug
`veed/fabric-one-lipsync`. The 8192-character URL limit and `^https?://` pattern mean inputs are
fetched over HTTP, not uploaded.

## Gaps

- No maximum audio duration in the schema. The only enforcement signal is the `audio_too_long` error
  code; the 1-minute figure comes from prose
  ([API article](../fabric-1-0-api-help-article/2026-10-26.md)).
- No output frame rate, aspect-ratio, or codec statement; the output schema is a generic `File` with a
  `video` field and a `video/mp4` example.
- No pricing for failed jobs, no per-request concurrency figure, no version history for the model.
- The docs' guide duplicates the help-centre article on the same subject with near-identical wording
  ([help-centre version](../lipsync-apis-fabric-1-0-and-lipsync-2-0/2026-10-26.md)).

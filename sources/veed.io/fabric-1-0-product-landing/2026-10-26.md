## Summary

The landing page is the editor-side commercial surface. It states the free/paid split (3 free
generations at 10 s, paid up to 30 s per generation), the editor price (240 AI credits per
generation), an API route through fal with a 1,000-minute enterprise escalation, and a "Fabric
Emotions" script-tag feature. It never mentions the "up to 5 minutes" length claimed on the model page
([model page](../fabric-1-0-model-page/2026-10-26.md)); here the longest single generation is 30
seconds and longer videos are built by stitching.

## Gaps

- No resolution, frame rate, output format, or audio-length specification.
- "Fabric Emotions" appears only on this page. No help-centre article, API doc, or host page describes
  the tag syntax beyond the `[excited!]` example, so its availability on the API routes is unverified.
- The credit price (240 credits per generation) cannot be converted to money without VEED's
  subscription pricing, which was not captured.
- The 1,000-minute threshold is not defined as a per-account or per-project limit.

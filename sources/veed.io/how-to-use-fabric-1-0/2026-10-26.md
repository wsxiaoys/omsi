# VEED Help Center: "How to Use Fabric 1.0"

- Origin: VEED Help Center (Intercom-hosted), "How to Use Fabric 1.0",
  <https://support.veed.io/en/articles/12302080-how-to-use-fabric-1-0>. Collections > AI tools.
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party vendor support article)
- Publication date: **August 19, 2026** (byline shown on the article at capture).
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: VEED Fabric 1.0

## Captured content

Opening, verbatim:

> Fabric 1.0 allows you to generate realistic lip-synced videos by combining an image with audio. This
> tool is designed for creators, teams, and professionals who want to bring static talking head
> characters to life using generative video.

Limitations, verbatim:

> Uses AI Credit for each generation.
> Up to 30 seconds of length per clip.

Steps, verbatim:

> Open Editor > Video > Generate Tab > Talking Video > VEED Fabric 1.0 model […]
> Select generate or upload a character image.
> Add audio by uploading a file, recording with your microphone, or generating text-to-speech using an
> AI voice (30 seconds maximum length).
> Click Generate to create your lip-synced video.

Character options, verbatim:

> Upload your own image
> Generate an image using AI
> Choose from VEED's character library

Audio options, verbatim:

> All audio options have a maximum length of 30 seconds.
> Record with your microphone
> Upload an audio file
> Use AI voice (text-to-speech)

Important notes, verbatim:

> Lip-sync quality works best with clear audio input.
> Multiple short clips can be generated and combined for longer videos.
> Works with various character styles (realistic, illustrated, animated, etc.).

FAQ, verbatim:

> Q: How can I create videos longer than 10 seconds?
>
> A subscription with credits is required for 30-second videos.

> Q: Is there an API available for Fabric?
>
> Yes, an API is available for Fabric that you can access here. It is available through Fal. It is not
> part of our pricing plans.

## Summary

The editor workflow document gives the strictest single-generation ceiling in the corpus: 30 seconds
per clip, with audio also capped at 30 seconds, for both uploads and AI text-to-speech. It confirms
three character sources (upload, AI-generated image, VEED's library) and that longer videos are built by
combining clips. Written 2026-08-19, it post-dates the "up to 5 minutes" copy on the model page and does
not repeat it.

## Gaps

- No resolution, frame rate, output format, or aspect ratio.
- The 30-second cap is stated three ways ("Up to 30 seconds of length per clip", "30 seconds maximum
  length", "30-second videos") without saying whether the cap applies to the audio, the output, or both;
  the article's own step list caps the audio at 30 seconds, which implies both.
- Says the API "is not part of our pricing plans" while the API is in fact billed per second on VEED's
  own API platform as well as fal
  ([API article](../fabric-1-0-api-help-article/2026-10-26.md)); the sentence reads as "not included in
  a subscription".
- No statement of what a free account gets versus paid, beyond the 10-second reference in the FAQ.

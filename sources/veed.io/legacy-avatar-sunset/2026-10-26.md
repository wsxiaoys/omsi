# VEED Help Center: "We are sunsetting some of our legacy Avatars"

- Origin: VEED Help Center, "We are sunsetting some of our legacy Avatars",
  <https://support.veed.io/en/articles/12745759-we-are-sunsetting-some-of-our-legacy-avatars>.
  Collections > AI tools > How to use our AI Avatars.
- Evidence kind: official product notice (first-party)
- Publication date: **March 10, 2026** (byline shown on the article at capture)
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: VEED AI Avatars (library product), as context for Fabric 1.0

## Captured content

Verbatim:

> We are refreshing our avatar library to improve quality, realism, and performance. As part of this
> update, a number of avatars will be retired in 2026. You can continue using these avatars for the
> remainder of this year, and your existing projects will continue to work as usual.

> A set of legacy avatars will be removed in 2026. These avatars are being retired so we can focus on
> supporting newer, more capable characters.

> If a project contains an avatar that is being retired, VEED will automatically update the project to a
> newer and closely matched avatar once the older version is removed.

## Summary

VEED is retiring part of its preset avatar library during 2026 and silently substituting "a newer and
closely matched avatar" in existing projects. That is a stability risk for anyone building on the
library-avatar product and is one reason the image-plus-audio route (Fabric) is the more durable
surface — it depends on the user's own image rather than a catalogue entry that can be withdrawn.

## Gaps

- No list of which avatars are being retired, no exact removal date, and no statement of what "newer and
  closely matched" means.
- No indication that Fabric 1.0 is the replacement path; the notice never mentions Fabric.

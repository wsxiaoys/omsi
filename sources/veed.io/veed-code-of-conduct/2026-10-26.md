## Captured content

Scope, verbatim:

> As part of our Terms of Use we require that VEED users abide by this Code of Conduct.

Enforcement, verbatim:

> We reserve the right to take any appropriate measure to protect our legitimate interests if we
> reasonably consider a user to have acted in breach of or contrary to the standards of behaviour set
> out in this Code of Conduct, including denying users access to VEED, our Applications or our services,
> terminating contracts, removing offensive content, reporting any misconduct […] to relevant
> stakeholders and/or competent authorities

Prohibited content, verbatim (clauses most relevant to face and voice synthesis):

> That is misleading or deceptive or impersonates (or attempts to impersonate) any person or
> organisation (this includes, by way of non-limiting examples, creating AI content which falsely depicts
> a person, fraudulent endorsements or misleading political content)

> that misleads or attempts to mislead any person as to the origin or authenticity of content. This
> includes any actual or attempt to remove, deface, obscure, modify or circumvent any Provenance Marking
> or functionality to enable the identification of AI content.

> That constitutes personal data or personally identifiable information without the consent of the
> relevant person, including a person's name, image, voice or other information about them.

> of a sexual nature (in particular, pornographic or sexually explicit content, any non-consensual
> sexual content or any sexual content depicting anyone who appears to be under the age of 18).

## Summary

VEED's conduct policy is the consent-and-safety layer under Fabric: it prohibits impersonation and
misleading synthetic depictions of real people, requires consent to use a person's "name, image, voice
or other information", forbids removing provenance markings, and bans sexual content including
non-consensual and minor-depicting material. There is no dedicated likeness-verification or
liveness-check process documented anywhere captured, so consent is a user warranty rather than a
platform control.

## Gaps

- No description of the automated moderation classifier, thresholds, or refusal taxonomy beyond the

- No stated policy on public-figure likeness, satire, or political content beyond the impersonation
  clause.
- No provenance-marking mechanism is described; the Code only forbids removing one.

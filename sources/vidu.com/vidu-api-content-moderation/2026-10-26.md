# Vidu API docs: "Content Moderation"

- Origin: Vidu Open Platform documentation, "Content Moderation",
  https://platform.vidu.com/docs/terms-and-policies/content-moderation (read as Markdown via `…/content-moderation.md`)
- Evidence kind: provider policy (first-party)
- Publication date: not stated on the page
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: all Vidu API models, including `viduq4-preview`

## Verbatim

> Vidu takes the safety of its platform seriously, and as a result, certain API requests will be
> moderated.

> If your account makes too many requests that get moderated, it may be suspended.

> Vidu reserves the right to moderate certain additional categories of content such as nudity, violence,
> sexually explicit content, other offensive subject matter, or public figures.

> - Nudity, obscenity, or overly provocative content
> - Violence
> - Gore, blood, or other viscera
> - Sexually explicit content
> - Offensive subject matter
> - Public figures
> - Living artists
> - Protection of minors

> Vidu's moderation evaluates all elements of your request. This means your requests may be moderated
> for either an image or text prompt or final generated video violation.

> **Content moderation cannot be disabled for an account, project, or topic by the support team.**

> Currently, we will return the credit cost after moderate generations.

> If a request is moderated, the task status response will return with `"status":"FAILED"`. Additional
> details describing moderation will appear in the `"CreationPolicyViolation"` fields.

## Summary

Moderation is mandatory at the vendor layer: it applies to input image, prompt and output video, covers
"public figures" and "living artists" explicitly, cannot be switched off, and can lead to account
suspension on repeated hits. Refunded credits are the stated compensation for a blocked generation.

This is the constraint most likely to bite a Q4 user, because Q4's selling points are photoreal people
with cloned voices and dialogue — exactly the categories ("public figures", voice likeness) that this
policy reserves the right to block. No page states whether a reference voice clip is screened for
identity or consent.

## Gaps

- No list of countries, no appeal process, no false-positive rate, and no statement of how a reference
  audio clip is judged.
- No documented distinction between policy categories and automatic classifiers ("may include").
- Nothing on retention of moderated inputs, or on what `CreationPolicyViolation` contains.

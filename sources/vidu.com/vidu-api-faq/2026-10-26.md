## Verbatim: the answers that contradict the model pages

> | 5 | What is the maximum number of characters supported for a prompt? | Up to approximately 1500
> characters are supported. |

> | 1 | What video generation durations does Vidu support? | Two durations are supported: 4 seconds and
> 8 seconds. The 8-second duration is only supported at 720p resolution. |

> | 2 | What resolutions does Vidu support? | Three resolutions are supported: 360p, 720p, and 1080p.
> For videos generated at 360p resolution, Smart Super Resolution can upscale them to 1080p. |

> | 3 | How long does it take to generate a video? | Generally, generating a 4-second video at 360p
> resolution takes about 10 seconds, and generating an 8-second video takes about twice as long. |

> | 6 | What languages are supported for prompts? | We currently support input in over 200 different
> languages worldwide. |

> | 7 | Can the optimized result of the prompt be returned? | Returning the optimized prompt result is
> not currently supported. |

## Summary

This FAQ describes a Vidu that no longer exists: 4/8-second durations, 360p/720p/1080p only, and a
~1,500-character prompt limit. Every one of those numbers is contradicted by the Q4 pages (3–16 s,
540p–4K, 20,000 characters) and by the Q3 pages (1–16 s, 540p–1080p, 2,000 characters on fal).

It is recorded here for one reason: to keep the contradiction visible and to stop the FAQ being used as
a limits reference. The FAQ was evidently written for the Vidu 2.0 era and not revised.

The one answer that is not contradicted elsewhere is the language claim ("over 200 different
languages"); no Q4 page restates or refutes it, and no captured page measures language coverage or
accent quality.

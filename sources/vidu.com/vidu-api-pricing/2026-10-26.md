# Vidu API docs: "Pricing"

- Origin: Vidu Open Platform documentation, "Pricing", https://platform.vidu.com/docs/overview/pricing
  (read as Markdown via `https://platform.vidu.com/docs/overview/pricing.md`)
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party price list)
- Publication date: not stated; live price list
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `viduq4-preview` (primary), `viduq3-pro`, `viduq3-turbo`, `viduq3-mix` (comparison)

## Billing model

> The Vidu Open Platform provides a rich set of video generation API capabilities to meet the varying
> needs of enterprises and developers. Currently, API calls are billed using a credit-based pricing
> model.

> | Credits | Price (RMB) |
> | --- | --- |
> | 1 | 0.03125 |

Top-up packages are quoted in RMB only (500 RMB → 16,000 credits … 20,000 RMB → 640,000 credits, all
"≤ 5" concurrency, 1-year validity). **No USD price and no per-request minimum appear on this page.**

> Videos that fail to generate do not consume credits (including failures caused by content moderation
> rejection).

## Verbatim: ViduQ4 preview credit table

> ### ViduQ4 preview
>
> | Capability | Model Version | Duration | Resolution | Credit Consumption |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | - Reference-to-video - Image-to-video | Viduq4-preview | 3-16 seconds | 4k | 78credits/second |
> | - Reference-to-video - Image-to-video | Viduq4-preview | 3-16 seconds | 2k | 38credits/second |
> | - Reference-to-video - Image-to-video | Viduq4-preview | 3-16 seconds | 1080p | 24credits/second |
> | - Reference-to-video - Image-to-video | Viduq4-preview | 3-16 seconds | 720p | 19credits/second |
> | - Reference-to-video - Image-to-video | Viduq4-preview | 3-16 seconds | 540p | 9credits/second |

Four facts from this table:

1. **Q4 is billed per output second, per resolution.** Duration says 3-16 s here — a third first-party
   statement of the range, and the one that matches the API reference.
2. **Image-to-video and reference-to-video are the same price** at the same resolution.
3. **There is no audio surcharge line and no off-peak column** for Q4. Q2's table carries an explicit
   audio surcharge ("consumes an additional 15 credits"), and every Q3 row has an "Off-peak Credit
   Consumption" column; the Q4 rows have neither.
4. **Q4 is the only model with 2K and 4K rates.** Q3 tops out at 1080p.

At 1 credit = ¥0.03125, the Q4 ladder is ¥0.281/s (540p), ¥0.594/s (720p), ¥0.75/s (1080p),
¥1.1875/s (2K), ¥2.4375/s (4K). This is arithmetic on the two published tables, not a quoted price.

> | - Image-to-video - Text-to-video - First-last frame | viduq3-pro | 1-16 seconds | 1080p | 24 credits/second | 12 credits/second |
> | - Image-to-video - Text-to-video - First-last frame | viduq3-pro | 1-16 seconds | 720p | 20 credits/second | 10 credits/second |
> | - Image-to-video - Text-to-video - First-last frame | viduq3-pro | 1-16 seconds | 540p | 9 credits/second | 5 credits/second |
> | - Image-to-video - Text-to-video - First-last frame | viduq3-turbo | 1-16 seconds | 1080p | 13 credits/second | 7 credits/second |
> | - Image-to-video - Text-to-video - First-last frame | viduq3-turbo | 1-16 seconds | 720p | 12 credits/second | 6 credits/second |
> | - Image-to-video - Text-to-video - First-last frame | viduq3-turbo | 1-16 seconds | 540p | 7 credits/second | 4 credits/second |
> | - Image-to-video | viduq3-pro-fast | 1-16 seconds | 1080p | 15 credits/second | 8 credits/second |
> | - Image-to-video | viduq3-pro-fast | 1-16 seconds | 720p | 12 credits/second | 6 credits/second |

> For the viduq3-pro and viduq3-turbo models, off-peak pricing is only supported when the audio
> parameter is true.

Against viduq3-pro, Q4 Preview is priced at parity: 9 vs 9 credits/s at 540p, 19 vs 20 at 720p, 24 vs
24 at 1080p. The genuine price change is the top of the ladder — 2K and 4K exist only on Q4, at 1.6×
and 3.25× its own 1080p rate.

## Summary

Q4 Preview's vendor price is a pure per-second, per-resolution credit schedule with no audio surcharge
and no off-peak mode. In RMB terms it is roughly at parity with viduq3-pro at 540p and 1080p, with the
2K/4K rungs as the expensive new capability.

## Gaps

- No USD list price, so cross-host comparison requires an exchange-rate assumption; this page makes
  none.
- No statement of what a credit costs at enterprise tiers, or whether the 2025-11-03 "credit
  inflation" (10×) is reflected in these figures (the numbers here are post-inflation values, but the
  page does not say so).
- The absence of an off-peak row for Q4 is an inference from the table's shape, not a sentence saying
  "off-peak is unsupported".
- `viduq3-pro-fast` and `viduq3-mix` appear in the price list; only `mix` appears in the model map.

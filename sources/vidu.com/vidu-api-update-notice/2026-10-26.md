# Vidu API docs: "Update Notice"

- Origin: Vidu Open Platform documentation, "Update Notice",
  https://platform.vidu.com/docs/api-reference/updates (read as Markdown via `…/updates.md`)
- Evidence kind: official documentation (first-party changelog)
- Publication date: rolling changelog; the Q4 entry is dated 2026/10/7
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: Vidu Q4 preview (primary); viduq3-* entries (context)

## Verbatim: the Q4 entry

> | 2026/10/7 | [New Models]-Vidu Q4 preview：More Authentic Performances: Supports reference voices for
> greater vocal consistency, more natural emotion, and characters that truly know how to perform.
> Smoother Motion: Handles high-dynamic scenes with fluidity—from martial arts and running to intense
> confrontations, complex actions flow seamlessly from start to finish. More Spectacular Effects:
> Explosions, fireworks, particles, and energy effects are rendered naturally, with finer detail and
> greater visual impact—even in complex VFX scenes. Stronger Cinematic Quality: Upgraded image quality,
> lighting, aesthetics, and cinematic camera language, with support for 2K and 4K output. More
> References: Supports up to 15 reference images, making it easier to combine multiple creative
> elements into complex concepts. More Efficient Creation: Better results, faster generation, and lower
> production costs. |

This is the **only first-party date attached to Q4** that was captured. It is the vendor's own launch
entry, and it calls the release "**Vidu Q4 preview**", not "Vidu Q4".

## Verbatim: neighbouring Q3 entries

> | 2026/4/13 | [New Models] - Added viduq3-mix, viduq3-turbo, viduq3 models to Reference-to-Video |
> | 2026/2/11 | [New Models] - Added viduq3-turbo model to Text-to-Video and Image-to-Video … [New
> Models] - Added viduq3-pro, viduq3-turbo models to Start-End Frame |
> | 2026/1/27 | [New Models] - Added viduq3-pro model to Text-to-Video and Image-to-Video |

Q3's rollout ran 2026-01-27 → 2026-04-13 and touched Text-to-Video, Image-to-Video and Start-End Frame
before Reference-to-Video. Q4's launch entry mentions no Text-to-Video, no start/end-frame capability,
and no tier split — consistent with the API reference and the model map.

## Verbatim: earlier entries that shape Q4's pricing and safety surface

> | 2025/11/03 | **Important Update - Vidu Credit Inflation** … Starting November 3, 2025, all credits
> on the Vidu open platform will be inflated by 10x … This credit inflation only adjusts the credit
> quantity; the actual value of credits remains unchanged |

> | 2025/11/13 | [New **Direct Audio-Video Output** capability] - Vidu Reference-to-Video and
> Image-to-Video now support direct audio-video output |

Q4's credit figures therefore sit on a 10×-inflated scale and are not comparable with any Vidu price
quoted before 2025-11-03. Q4 inherits audio-video output as a default-on feature rather than as a new
option.

## Summary

Q4 Preview launched on **2026-10-07** by the vendor's own changelog — inside the roster's one-year
window (after 2025-10-26). The entry is a marketing paragraph, not a specification: it claims "better
results, faster generation, and lower production costs" against Q3 without a single number, and it
announces 2K/4K and 15 references but not the frame rate, duration range, modes, or price.

## Gaps

- No GA date, no deprecation date, and no statement of how long the "preview" label will last.
- No changelog entry after 2026-10-07 mentions Q4, so nothing captured shows a Q4 point release or a
  price revision.
- The claim of "lower production costs" is unquantified and is contradicted at the top of the ladder
  by the 2K/4K rates (see the pricing record).

## Verbatim: FAQ

> **What video generation modes does Vidu Q4 support?** — Vidu Q4 supports Image-to-Video and
> Reference-to-Video. Image-to-Video creates videos from a single image and text prompt, while
> Reference-to-Video uses multiple image and audio references.

> **What is the difference between Image-to-Video and Reference-to-Video?** — Image-to-Video animates a
> single image based on your prompt. Reference-to-Video supports 1–15 image references and 0–3 audio
> references to maintain consistency across subjects and voices.

> **What is the maximum video length?** — Reference-to-Video supports 1–16 seconds, while
> Image-to-Video supports 3–16 seconds.

> **What scenarios is Vidu Q4 designed for?** — Vidu Q4 is ideal for AI series, advertising, social
> content, and cinematic production.

## Summary

Two things this page settles. First, **Q4 has no text-to-video mode**: the FAQ enumerates exactly
Image-to-Video and Reference-to-Video. Second, the product page claims a **16-second ceiling, 4K
output, 15 reference images and 3 reference-audio clips**, and states different minimum durations for
the two modes (R2V 1 s, I2V 3 s) — a detail that contradicts the API reference's single 3–16 s range.

Everything else on the page is marketing: no numbers behind "smoother motion", "richer FX", or "more
authentic voice", and no statement of price, frame rate, resolution ladder below 2K, or release date.

## Gaps

- No release or update date is published on this page, so it cannot date Q4 on its own.
- The page never names the API model ID (`viduq4-preview`) or says whether the product is preview or
  GA; the word "preview" does not appear here.
- No statement on licensing, commercial use, moderation, or region availability.
- The claims "more authentic voice", "smoother motion", "richer FX" and "stronger cinematic quality"
  have no supporting measurement on the page.

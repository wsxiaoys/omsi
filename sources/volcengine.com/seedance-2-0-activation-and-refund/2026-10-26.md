## Activation thresholds (transcribed)

| 开通方式 / method | 门槛条件 / threshold | 有效期 / validity | 适用范围 / scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 账户余额 (recommended) | available balance reaches 200 CNY | unlimited | balance offsets Volcengine products |
| 节省计划 (recommended) | a dedicated declining savings plan of 200 CNY or more, with balance remaining | 200 CNY plan: 30 days; 500/1000 CNY plans: 90 days | offsets all Ark models, including the whole Seedance series |
| 资源包 | a model resource pack of 200 CNY or more, with quota remaining | 90 days | offsets only the corresponding single model |

After meeting the threshold the account must still activate the model in 开通管理 (model activation)
before calling the API.

## Deduction order (verbatim, translated)

> 调用产生的费用，按以下顺序自动抵扣：资源包（若该模型有对应资源包且有 tokens 余量，优先抵扣）；节省计划（若已购买
> 节省计划且有余额，那么节省计划余额可抵扣按量后付费账单）；账户余额（即按量后付费）
>
> [Charges are deducted in this order: resource pack first if the model has one with tokens left;
> then the savings plan balance; then the account balance (pay-as-you-go).]

## Refund rules (transcribed)

- Account balance: while a model instance is activated, up to 200 CNY of the balance cannot be
  withdrawn; the excess can. Deactivating the model instance releases the locked part.
- Savings plans and resource packs: not refundable after 7 days or once used.

## What this establishes, and what it does not

- Volcengine records a **general availability ("全量发布") event for both Seedance 2.0 and 2.0 Fast in
  its 2026-04 section**. The page gives a month, not a day.
- There is **no February or March Seedance 2.0 entry**, even though the ByteDance Seed launch blog is
  dated 2026-02-12 and the paper says "officially released in China in early February 2026". The
  changelog is therefore consistent with a staged release — launch in February, API general
  availability on Volcengine Ark in April — but the page never says so in words, and a missing row is
  weak evidence.
- The release is gated on a spending threshold (200 CNY balance, savings plan, or resource pack), so
  "GA" here does not mean "open to any account".

## Gaps

- No day-level dates anywhere in the changelog sections captured.
- The page does not state the launch date of Seedance 2.0 Mini (`doubao-seedance-2-0-mini-260615`);
  the Mini model ID suffix implies a 2026-06-15 checkpoint, and fal's Mini endpoints were published
  2026-06-23/24 (separate record).
- No mention of 4K or 1080p being added, so this record cannot date the resolution expansion the
  tutorial documents.

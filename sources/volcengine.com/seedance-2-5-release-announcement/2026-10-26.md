## What this establishes, and what it contradicts

- The changelog files the 2.5 API as **new in the 2026-06 section**, and the model ID suffix
  `260628` reads as 2026-06-28. That is roughly five weeks **before** the ByteDance Seed launch blog
  of 2026-07-31, which said API access was "coming soon via BytePlus ModelArk" / "近期上线火山方舟".
- The two are not reconciled by any captured page. Readings: (a) the API was actually released in
  late June and the 2026-07-31 post describes only the consumer-product rollout (Jimeng, Doubao Pro),
  written earlier and shipped later; (b) the changelog month heading is approximate or back-dated.
- The 2.5 row is marked 新发布 (new release) where the 2.0 rows in April are marked 更新 (update) —
  the same distinction the Seedance 2.0 page relies on to argue a staged rollout.

## Gaps

- The changelog gives a month, not a day, for either model.
- No deprecation or supersession notice for 2.0 appears anywhere in the captured changelog.

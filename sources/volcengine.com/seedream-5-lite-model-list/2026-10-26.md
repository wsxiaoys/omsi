"即将下线" translates as "to be taken offline / retiring soon". The table's rate-limit column
header carries the platform's standard caveat that these are not hard guarantees and depend on
platform load and invocation method.

## What this establishes

- In the China region the Lite snapshot is documented as **`doubao-seedream-5-0-lite-260128`**,
  accepted under the `doubao-seedream-5-0-260128` model entry — the same "parent entry plus lite
  ID" structure as BytePlus international, with a `doubao-` prefix added.
- The Seedream 5.0 entry that serves the Lite snapshot is flagged **即将下线 (retiring soon)**, as
  are Seedream 4.5 and 4.0. The `pro` and `flash` rows carry no such flag.
- The Chinese name published on ByteDance's own launch blog ("Doubao-Seedream-5.0-lite") matches
  the `doubao-` prefix used here, so the alias is first-party on both surfaces.

## Gaps

- No retirement date is given for 即将下线.
- The flag sits on the parent `doubao-seedream-5-0-260128` row, so the capture does not prove
  whether the *lite* snapshot specifically is retiring, or only the parent entry with which it is
  listed. Both readings are consistent with the page.
- The BytePlus (international) model list showed no equivalent flag on capture; whether the
  retirement is China-only is unresolved.
- No pricing on this page; the Volcengine price list (模型价格) was not captured.

## Pricing (verbatim)

> Just **$0.15** per song (up to 5 minutes).

The model page's own FAQ answer is unreliable boilerplate: "Music 2.6 starts at $0.15 per run or per
second. That figure is the base price — the final charge scales with the parameters you set…". The
docs page states a flat per-song price for tracks up to five minutes, which matches the vendor's
billing unit; the page's "or per second / scales with parameters" text is template copy that does not
describe this endpoint (there is no duration parameter to scale).

## Other documented behaviour

- "Generated audio URLs expire after 24 hours — download your output promptly." (Matches the vendor's
  24-hour `url` expiry.)
- "Both prompt and lyrics are required fields; leave lyrics empty to trigger auto-generation." The two
  halves of that sentence contradict each other, and the parameter table marks `lyrics` required while
  the same table says it may be left empty.
- "Please ensure your content complies with MiniMax's usage policies."
- Configurable quality is summarised as "up to 256kbps/44.1kHz".

## What this establishes

- A **third independent host**, at the same $0.15 per track, and the only one whose price statement
  explicitly caps the track at **5 minutes** — matching MiniMax's billing unit and contradicting
  Replicate's "up to 6 minutes".
- Host schemas disagree on the lyrics ceiling: vendor 1–3500, WaveSpeed 10–3000, fal 3500 max,
  Replicate 1–3500. A prompt/lyrics pair that works on one route can be rejected on another.
- WaveSpeed adds a `60000` bitrate value that the vendor's enum does not list.
- No `seed`, no duration parameter, no cover/reference-audio input on this route either.

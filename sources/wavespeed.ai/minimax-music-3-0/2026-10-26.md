| --- | --- | --- |
| `prompt` | Yes | "Music style description, including genre, mood, instruments, tempo, vocal style, and production direction. Maximum length: 2000 characters." |
| `lyrics` | Yes | "Song lyrics with optional structure tags. Length: 10–3000 characters. Pass an empty value for auto-generated lyrics." |

`lyrics_optimizer` is present in the playground form and in the request example but is not in the
parameter table. No `duration`, `seed`, `format` or step/guidance control is exposed.

## What this establishes

- Music 3 has a **second commercial host** besides fal, and it is a *different product*: WaveSpeedAI
  sells a vendor-API-shaped surface (flat $0.15 per song, 14 structure tags, `is_instrumental`,
  `lyrics_optimizer`, bitrate/sample-rate selection) rather than the open-weights schema. That it
  proxies MiniMax's API is an inference from the parameter set and price, not a published fact — the
  page never says which upstream it calls.
- The two hosts disagree on the model's practical control surface: fal has duration/seed/steps but no
  instrumental flag; WaveSpeedAI has the instrumental flag and audio settings but no duration or
  seed. Neither is a superset of the vendor API.
- Per-track pricing is identical to the vendor's own $0.15/up-to-5-minutes rate
  (`../../minimax.io/music-pricing/2026-10-26.md`), so the flat rate has survived the transition from
  Music 2.6 to Music 3 on this host.
- The lyrics ceiling here is **10–3000 characters**, not the vendor API's 1–3500 and not fal's
  unstated limit.

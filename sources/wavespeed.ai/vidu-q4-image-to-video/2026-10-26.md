> Vidu Q4 Image-to-Video animates a single reference image into high-quality AI video with prompt-guided
> motion, optional audio, 3-16 second duration, and output resolutions from 540P to 4K for product

> Vidu Q4 Reference-to-Video generates subject-consistent AI video from text prompts, 1-15 reference

## Request schema (verbatim, image-to-video)

```json
{"duration":{"default":5,"maximum":16,"minimum":3,"type":"integer"},
 "enable_prompt_expansion":{"default":false,"type":"boolean"},
 "generate_audio":{"default":true,"type":"boolean"},
 "image":{"type":"string"},
 "prompt":{"type":"string"},
 "resolution":{"default":"720p","enum":["540p","720p","1080p","2k","4k"],"type":"string"},
 "seed":{"type":"integer"}}
```

Required: `image`, `prompt`. Endpoint `POST https://api.wavespeed.ai/api/v3/vidu/q4/image-to-video`.

Reference-to-video schema: `images` (1–15, `maxItems: 15`), `audios` (`maxItems: 3`), `aspect_ratio`
(`16:9`,`9:16`,`1:1`,`3:4`,`4:3`), `duration` (3–16, default 5), `resolution` (540p–4k),
`generate_audio` (default **true**), `prompt`, `seed`.

WaveSpeed is the host whose reference-image cap (**15**) matches the vendor's; fal caps at 12.

## Pricing (verbatim formula, from the embedded model record)

> `{"total_price": (base_price * 10 / 19) * duration * ((resolution="720p") ? 1.9 : (resolution="1080p") ? 2.4 : (resolution="2k") ? 3.8 : (resolution="4k") ? 7.8 : 0.9 ) }`

with `base_price: 95000` and `discount_rate: 100` on both endpoints.

Reading `base_price` in WaveSpeed's micro-USD unit (1,000,000 = $1) gives a per-second price of
**$0.045 / $0.095 / $0.12 / $0.19 / $0.39** at 540p / 720p / 1080p / 2K / 4K. That is exactly **10/7
(≈1.43×) fal's per-second rates** on all five rungs, which is a coherent host-markup pattern and makes
the arithmetic check self-consistent.

The page's own headline figure is **"$0.095 per run · ~10 / $1"** for the default settings. That number
equals one second at 720p, not five seconds (the schema's `duration` default), so the displayed per-run
price and the formula do not reconcile. Both figures are recorded; the per-second derivation is
arithmetic on the published formula, not a quoted schedule.

## Summary

A second independent host, and the only one captured that serves Q4 with the vendor's full 15-image
reference cap. It corroborates the model's existence, its two modes, the 3–16 s range, the 540p–4K
ladder, audio-on-by-default, and the five reference aspect ratios. Its price is ~1.43× fal's at every
resolution.

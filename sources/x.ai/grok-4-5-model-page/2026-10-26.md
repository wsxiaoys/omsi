## What this establishes

- **`grok-4.7` is not an alias of `grok-4.5`.** 4.5 is a separately documented model with its own
  aliases (`grok-4.5-latest`, `grok-build-latest`), a different tagline ("intelligent coding
  model" vs "frontier model"), and a **cheaper cached-input price** ($0.30 vs $0.50 per 1M below
  200k; $0.60 vs $1.00 above).
- The alias mechanism exists and is documented generically on the Models page
  (`<model>-latest`, `<model>-<date>`), yet **4.7 and 4.6 carry no aliases at all** while 4.5
  does. That is a real difference in the vendor's versioning, not an omission in one capture:
  the two 4.x pages were fetched the same way on the same date.
- 4.5 is served from two regions (no `us-central-1`) while 4.6 and 4.7 list three.

## Contradiction with the reasoning guide

This page lists `xhigh` as a supported reasoning effort, but the reasoning guide states
`"xhigh"` is available only on `grok-4.6` and later and that requests on `grok-4.5` "are treated
as `high`" ([reasoning](../text-reasoning/2026-10-26.md)). The two first-party pages disagree
about 4.5. Recorded, not resolved; it does not affect `grok-4.7`, where both pages agree `xhigh`
is supported.

## What this establishes

- **The `grok-4.6` and `grok-4.7` model pages are identical except for the heading and the model
  name.** A byte diff of the two `.md` captures shows exactly two changed lines (the `# Grok 4.6`
  heading and the `- **Model name:**` line). Context window, modalities, capabilities, reasoning
  efforts, price table, rate limits, regions, and Batch support are all the same.
  ([`grok-4.7` page](../grok-4-7-model-page/2026-10-26.md))
- So the vendor's own API contract distinguishes the two models **by identity and by nothing
  else**. Any quality difference between 4.6 and 4.7 is documented only in the launch post
  ([announcement](../grok-4-7-announcement/2026-10-26/index.md)) and in measurements, not in the
  model card.
- `grok-4.6` also has no "Aliases" line, matching 4.7 and differing from 4.5.
- `grok-4.6` is still served on 2026-10-26: xAI lists it on the models page, on the US regional
  endpoint, and in the Batch discount list.

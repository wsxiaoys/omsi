## What this establishes

- **1.5 is a separate model ID with its own price and its own modality line.** The modality line is
  the sharpest first-party differentiator in the family: 1.5 and 1.5-lite are `text, image → video`,
  while 1.0 is `text, image, video → video` — only 1.0 accepts a video input, which is what its
  editing and extension endpoints consume.
- **1.5 costs 1.6× 1.0 per second** ($0.080 vs $0.050) and **4× the lite tier** ($0.020).
- **Aliases:** 1.5 carries a `-preview` alias and a dated snapshot alias `-2026-05-30`. The dated
  alias is the only first-party artefact that dates the model itself, and it matches the host
  listing dates (fal 2026-05-31, Replicate version 2026-06-01).
- **Rate limit is 10 rps on all three tiers** — the same ceiling, so price is the only lever.
- **Regions:** 1.5 and 1.0 include `us-saltlake-2`; lite is limited to `us-east-1` and `us-west-2`.
  No captured page explains what a "region" is here or whether a caller selects one.
- **Batch API** is supported on 1.5 in two of its three regions (not `us-saltlake-2`); lite's row
  says "Supported" with no region list.

## Gaps

- The pages never state output frame rate, container/codec, or maximum prompt length.
- No page states whether `us-saltlake-2` is selectable or automatic, or how it relates to the

- Nothing says what changed between the `-preview` alias and GA, or whether `-2026-05-30` names a
  weights snapshot or a launch date.
- No deprecation or retirement notice for 1.0 or 1.5 was found on any page captured here.

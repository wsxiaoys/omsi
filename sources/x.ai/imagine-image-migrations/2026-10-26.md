## November 2, 2026: `grok-imagine-image-quality` retires (verbatim)

> `grok-imagine-image-2.0` now covers everything `grok-imagine-image-quality` was used for, at a
> lower price. **Effective November 2, 2026**, the `grok-imagine-image-quality` model slug is
> retired from the xAI API. Its 60-day notice period began on September 2, 2026.

> After **November 2, 2026**, requests to `grok-imagine-image-quality` are served by
> `grok-imagine-image-2.0` with `quality` set to `low`. The slug continues to resolve, so you do not
> need to change your code to avoid breakage.

> `grok-imagine-image` (1.0) is not affected by this change.

### Pricing impact (verbatim)

> `grok-imagine-image-2.0` at `low` quality is $0.01 per image cheaper than
> `grok-imagine-image-quality` at every resolution. If you keep sending requests to the retired slug
> after November 2, you are billed at the `grok-imagine-image-2.0` `low` rate.

### Quality guidance and leaderboard claims (verbatim)

> * `low` is what the redirect uses. It ranks #2 on the [Arena](https://arena.ai) Image Edit
>   leaderboard and #3 on Text-to-Image, and is the closest match in cost to
>   `grok-imagine-image-quality`.
> * `medium` spends more compute on each image for finer detail; choose it where output quality
>   matters more than cost.
> * `auto` (the default when `quality` is omitted) currently serves `low` for generation and
>   `medium` for editing, and you are billed at the quality served.

## May 15, 2026: `grok-imagine-image-pro` retires (verbatim)

> **Effective May 15, 2026 at 12:00 PM PT**, the following models will be retired from the xAI API:
> […] `grok-imagine-image-pro`

> `grok-imagine-image-pro` will be redirected to `grok-imagine-image-quality`. On November 2, 2026,
> `grok-imagine-image-quality` is itself retired in favor of `grok-imagine-image-2.0`.

## What this establishes

- The image tier line-up has churned twice in 2026: a `-pro` slug existed and was redirected to
  `-quality` on 2026-05-15, and `-quality` is itself retired on 2026-11-02. That is consistent with
  the model page's alias list, which still carries `grok-imagine-image-pro` as an alias of
  `grok-imagine-image-quality`.
- **The base `grok-imagine-image` (1.0) survives both migrations**, so it is a current model at the
  capture date, not a retired one — but it is also not the tier xAI recommends for new work.
- Slugs continue to resolve after retirement (redirect), so a working endpoint is not evidence that
  the model is still served under its own name. This matters for any host pinned to a retired slug.
- The #2 / #3 Arena ranks are **xAI's own reading of a third-party leaderboard**, quoted without a
  date, sample, or snapshot; they are claims, not measurements, and they are about
  `grok-imagine-image-2.0` at `low`, not about `grok-imagine-image` (1.0).

## Gaps

- No retirement date is published for `grok-imagine-image` (1.0) itself.
- The guides do not say how long a redirect is honoured before a slug stops resolving.
- The Arena claim names no leaderboard snapshot date, so it cannot be checked against a pinned
  capture.

## Billing basis (verbatim, from the Imagine overview page, captured separately in
`../imagine-overview/2026-10-26.md`)

> Image generation uses flat per-image pricing regardless of prompt length. Each generated image
> incurs a fixed fee. Image edits are billed for both the input image and the generated output
> image. Video generation uses per-second pricing where both duration and resolution affect the
> total cost.

## What this establishes

- Flat **per-image** pricing, not per-megapixel and not per-token, for every image tier. The base
  tier is the cheapest at **$0.02/image**; quality is $0.05; 2.0 is $0.04.
- Edits bill the input image *and* the output image, which is why hosts quote $0.022 for an edit
  ($0.02 + $0.002).
- Video is a separate per-second schedule on separate model IDs, so the Imagine family is priced on
  two different bases depending on modality.
- xAI's own recommendation has moved to 2.0; 1.0 remains listed and priced but is not the
  recommended image model.

## Gaps

- No resolution-differentiated pricing for the base tier or quality tier on the pricing page, even
  though fal charges a 2K premium for both ($0.05 → $0.07 and $0.04/$0.06 → $0.06/$0.08). Whether
  xAI's $0.02 and $0.05 hold at 2K is not stated on these pages. The migration guide's claim that
  "`grok-imagine-image-2.0` at `low` quality is $0.01 per image cheaper than
  `grok-imagine-image-quality` at every resolution" implies resolution-varying prices exist for
  quality, which the pricing table does not show.
- No enterprise/volume terms, no Batch API discount for images.

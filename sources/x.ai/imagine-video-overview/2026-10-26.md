# xAI docs: Grok Imagine video overview

- Origin: xAI developer documentation, "Video Overview",
  <https://docs.x.ai/developers/model-capabilities/video/overview> (`.md` capture)
- Evidence kind: official documentation (capability comparison)
- Publication date: none stated; live page
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `grok-imagine-video`, `grok-imagine-video-1.5`,
  `grok-imagine-video-1.5-lite` (contrast only; not the subject of this page)

Captured to establish the boundary between the image model and the video models that share the
"Grok Imagine" name.

## Scope (verbatim)

> Grok Imagine video models turn a set of references, a still image, an existing clip, or a prompt
> into video with generated audio. `grok-imagine-video-1.5` generates lip-synced speech, renders
> text-to-video and image-to-video at native 1080p, takes up to 14 reference images and 3 voice
> references, and pins first, last, and mid-video frames, in clips up to 15 seconds.
> `grok-imagine-video-1.5-lite` does text-to-video and image-to-video with lip-synced speech at the
> lowest price per second, reaching 1080p by upscaling 720p.

## Capability table (verbatim, abridged)

> | Capability | `grok-imagine-video-1.5` | `grok-imagine-video-1.5-lite` | `grok-imagine-video` |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | Text-to-video | Yes: Up to 1080p, native | Yes: Up to 1080p, upscaled from 720p | Yes: Up to 720p |
> | Image-to-video | Yes: Up to 1080p, native | Yes: Up to 1080p, upscaled from 720p | Yes: Up to 720p |
> | Reference images | Yes: Up to 14 images, 15 s, 720p | Not supported | Yes: Up to 7 images, 10 s, 720p |
> | Voice references | Yes: Up to 3 voices | Not supported | Not supported |
> | Duration | Yes: 1–15 s | Yes: 1–15 s | Yes: 1–15 s |
> | Resolution | Yes: 480p, 720p, 1080p | Yes: 480p, 720p, 1080p | Yes: 480p, 720p |
> | Audio | Yes: Native audio with lip-synced speech | Yes: Native audio with lip-synced speech | Yes: Generated audio |

> Per-second prices by resolution are listed on the [Models](/developers/models) page.

> The still you pass as `image` becomes the first frame, and the prompt describes what happens next.
> The output always matches the still's aspect ratio; `aspect_ratio` is ignored.

## What this establishes

- **"Grok Imagine" is a family, not a model.** The image model (`grok-imagine-image`) and the video
  models (`grok-imagine-video`, `-1.5`, `-1.5-lite`) are separate IDs with separate modalities,
  parameters, and pricing bases.
- Video is asynchronous and priced per second of output by resolution; image is synchronous
  (optionally deferred) and priced per image. Nothing about video findings transfers to the image
  model.
- The image model is an **input path** to video: image-to-video takes a still as the first frame,
  and (per the July 2026 release note) text-to-video on 1.5 is implemented as text-to-image then
  image-to-video. This is the one place where the two capabilities are coupled.
- Video models are 480p/720p/1080p and up to 15 s; the image model is 1K/2K. Resolution vocabularies
  are not interchangeable.

# xAI docs: Reference-to-Video (references, voices, pinned frames)

- Origin: xAI developer documentation, "Reference-to-Video",
  <https://docs.x.ai/developers/model-capabilities/video/reference-to-video> (`.md` capture)
- Evidence kind: official documentation (API guide)
- Publication date: none stated; live page
- Capture date: 2026-10-26
- Relevant models: `grok-imagine-video-1.5` (subject of every 1.5-only feature here);
  `grok-imagine-video` for contrast

## Scope (verbatim)

> Provide reference images, a preset voice, or both to guide the generated video. Images incorporate
> specific people, objects, clothing, or other visual elements without locking the first frame
> (unlike image-to-video). This is useful for virtual try-on, product placement, and stories with
> recurring characters. On `grok-imagine-video-1.5`, you can also pick the voice your subject speaks
> in (see Reference audio), pin the exact first or last frame (see First & Last frame), and pin
> frames at chosen moments inside the clip (see Keyframes).

## Limits table (verbatim)

> | Limit | Value |
> |-------|-------|
> | Reference images | Up to **14** per request on `grok-imagine-video-1.5`; up to **7** on `grok-imagine-video` |
> | Voice references | Up to **3** per request on `grok-imagine-video-1.5`, preset voices selected by `voice_id` |
> | Pinned frames | `grok-imagine-video-1.5` only: `image` as the first frame alongside references, `last_frame`, and up to **4** `keyframes` |
> | Required input | At least one reference image, voice, `last_frame`, or `keyframes` entry |
> | Duration | Up to **15 seconds** on `grok-imagine-video-1.5`; up to **10 seconds** on `grok-imagine-video` |
> | Resolution | Up to **720p** |

Input encodings: "a public HTTPS URL, a base64-encoded data URI, or a `file_id` from the Files API —
and you can mix kinds within a single request."

Reference labelling: "Refer to reference images in the prompt by their position in the list,
starting at `<IMAGE_0>` … If you also set `image` to pin the first frame, it takes `<IMAGE_0>` and
your reference images start at `<IMAGE_1>`."

## Reference audio (verbatim, abridged)

> On `grok-imagine-video-1.5`, give your subject a voice by passing up to **3** preset voices with
> `reference_audios`. Each entry names a voice by `voice_id`, drawn from the same built-in roster as
> Text to Speech, so `{"voice_id": "eve"}` speaks in Eve's voice. Identifiers are case-insensitive;
> an unknown one returns `400` with the list of available voices.

> `reference_audios` accepts preset voices; voice references with your own audio files are available
> to trusted partners on request.

Voices are tagged in the prompt as `<AUDIO_0>`, `<AUDIO_1>`, `<AUDIO_2>`.

## First & last frame (verbatim, abridged)

> On `grok-imagine-video-1.5`, `last_frame` pins the exact last frame of the clip. The video ends
> arriving on that image rather than re-rendering it as a reference.

| Request shape | Result |
|---------------|--------|
| `image` + `last_frame` | Pinned first and last frame. The model interpolates between the two. |
| `last_frame` only | Pinned last frame. The model generates the opening and lands on the pinned image. |
| `last_frame` + `reference_images` / `reference_audios` | Pinned last frame with reference guidance. |

> `prompt` is optional in every first & last frame request.

## Keyframes (verbatim, abridged)

> On `grok-imagine-video-1.5`, `keyframes` pins images at chosen moments inside the clip. Each entry
> pairs an `image` with a `timestamp_s`, and the video passes through that exact image at that time.

| Constraint | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| Count | At most 4 keyframes per request. |
| Timing | `timestamp_s` must fall strictly inside the clip: greater than 0 and less than `duration`. |
| Spacing | Anchors snap to a 1/3-second grid. Two keyframes that round to the same slot are rejected, so keep them at least 1/3 s apart. |
| Inputs | Each `image` accepts the same URL, data-URI, and `file_id` shapes as image-to-video. |

> To loop a clip, pass the same image as `image` and `last_frame`.

## What this establishes

- **Reference-to-video is the 1.5-only capability band.** 14 images, 3 preset voices, first/last
  frame pinning, and 4 mid-clip keyframes all attach to `grok-imagine-video-1.5` and to no other
  tier (lite has none of them; 1.0 has 7 images, no voices, no pinned frames).
- **R2V is capped at 720p** while T2V and I2V reach native 1080p on the same model.
- **Custom voice cloning is gated**, not public: preset voices only, with user-supplied audio "on
  request" through sales. The REST reference contradicts this by documenting a caller-supplied
  `url` for `reference_audios` clips of up to 15 s (`../videos-api-reference/2026-10-26.md`); no
  captured page reconciles the two.

## Gaps

- No statement of reference-image size, format, or per-image limits beyond URL/base64/file_id.
- No description of what "trusted partner" means, how it is approved, or what it costs.
- No quality guidance with numbers for many-reference requests; the page offers only prose advice
  ("give each one a single role and a fixed place in the scene, keep the cast small").
- The `last_frame` and `keyframes` sections are complete on the page and are quoted above; the
  page does not say whether keyframe adherence is measured, only that the video "passes through
  that exact image at that time".

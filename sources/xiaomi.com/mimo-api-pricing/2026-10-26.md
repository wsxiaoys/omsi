- **Evidence kind:** official documentation (first-party price list).
- **Publication date:** none stated on the page.
- **Capture date:** 2026-10-26
- **Relevant models:** `mimo-v2.6-pro`, `mimo-v2.6-flash`, `mimo-v2.6-pro-ultraspeed`,

- **Capture type:** full raw Markdown of the page (the docs platform serves `.md` sources).

## Billing rules, verbatim

> "Billing Unit: China：RMB / M tokens; Overseas: dollar / M tokens"

> "Cache Hit: When the requested prefix content hits the Prompt Cache, it will be billed
> according to the cached hit price."

> "Cache Write: Limited-time Free"

> "Internet search is billed independently based on the number of calls and is not included in
> the Token price"

## Deprecation notice, verbatim

> "`mimo-v2.5-pro` and `mimo-v2.5` were officially deprecated at 10:00 (Beijing time) on October
> 21,2026. It is recommended to switch to the new version of the models as soon as possible."

This is the only dated lifecycle event in any captured Xiaomi material. It establishes that the
V2.5 generation was retired **four weeks** after the V2.6 launch, and that no equivalent
retirement date has been published for V2.6.

## Gaps

- No rate-limit, quota or Token Plan detail on this page (rate limits are on the models page).
- No per-image, per-audio-second or per-video-second price: images, audio and video are metered
  as input tokens at the rates above, with the conversion rules in the multimodal docs.
- No statement of what happens to batch pricing on the UltraSpeed tier beyond "not supported".
- The RMB and USD tables are not simple conversions (¥3.00/$0.435 ≈ 6.9 CNY/USD on input,
  ¥6.00/$0.87 ≈ 6.9 on output), so the domestic list is a separate price list rather than an FX
  conversion of the overseas one.

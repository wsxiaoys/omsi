## Model summary, verbatim (Introduction)

> "we introduce the MiMo-V2.6 series, including MiMo-V2.6-Pro, a 1.02T-parameter Mixture-of-
> Experts model with 42B active parameters, and MiMo-V2.6-Flash, a 310B-parameter
> Mixture-of-Experts model with 15B active parameters"

Note: the Flash figure is **310B** here, **309B** on the model card and on Artificial
Analysis. The Hugging Face safetensors index for `MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL` totals 310.76B parameters
and for `MiMo-V2.6-Pro-RL` 1,024.22B, so 309B is a rounded class label rather than a count.

## Architecture, verbatim (2.1)

> "The text backbone of MiMo-V2.6 is mainly composed of repeated hybrid blocks that interleave
> Local Sliding Window Attention (SWA) and Global Attention (GA) … The only exception is the
> very first Transformer block, which uses global attention with a dense Feed-Forward Network
> (FFN) to stabilize early representation learning. The sliding window size used in MiMo-V2.6
> is 128. Both the SWA block and the GA block utilize a sparse MoE FFN without shared experts."

Model cards give the per-model layout: Pro 70 layers (60 SWA / 10 GA), hidden 6144, 384 routed
experts with 8 activated; Flash 48 layers (39 SWA / 9 GA), hidden 4096, 256 routed experts with
8 activated. Both carry a 681M-parameter MiMo-ViT (28 layers, 24 SWA / 4 GA), a 308M
AudioTokenizer plus 127M audio patch encoder, and a 5-layer SWA MTP speculative decoder.

## Pre-training, verbatim (3.1)

> "MiMo-V2.6-Flash is trained on 48T tokens, comprising 26T tokens in the text stage and 22T in
> the omni stage. MiMo-V2.6-Pro is trained on 30T tokens, with 27T and 3T tokens in the two
> stages, respectively."

> "We begin pre-training with a context length of 32K tokens and extend it to 256K partway
> through training."

Mid-training (3.2) extends context from 256K to **1M** in a final stage on an agent-centric data
mixture, and the RL optimizer is **Muon** rather than AdamW. No knowledge cutoff date is
published anywhere in the report.

## RL setup, verbatim

> "asynchronous training that consumes 1,568 samples and 2.7∼3.7B tokens per step at context
> lengths of up to 1M"

> "we freeze the MoE router and establish a multi-layer defense against reward hacking"

Released RL environments (Table 5): software engineering 3k tasks (executable tests), cyber
vulnerability reproduction 1k (rule checks), general knowledge work 1k (rubric-based judging),
visual web development 2k (visual grading) — "7k+" total, also published as the
`XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-RL-oss` dataset.

### CyberGym is not the stock configuration

Footnote 1 to the evaluation section reads: "We corrected the flawed evaluation environments
based on the method described in Section 4.2.4". Section 4.2.4 explains that the vendor's
verifier replaces CyberGym's fix-binary differential testing with sanitizer-report matching, and
that they hand the agent the compiled harness binary where "CyberGym withholds the binary,
restricting agents to source-only analysis". The 94.0 / 95.1 CyberGym scores therefore come
from a **modified harness with more information than the public benchmark provides** and are not
comparable to published CyberGym numbers run stock.

## Gaps

- No knowledge cutoff, no safety/alignment evaluation, no licence text in the report (the MIT
  licence is declared on the model cards).
- No latency, throughput or cost measurements; no human-preference or writing evaluation.
- Benchmark settings (temperature, harness version, attempt counts, pass@k) are not stated per
  row, and the internal benchmarks have no public harness at all.
